Easy Purāṇic Sanskrit

Ten short verses from the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, chosen for a gentle first read of Devanāgarī. Each line is one rhythmic part of an anuṣṭubh śloka. Read one line at a time; the IAST button lets you check the sounds.

The brief English senses are reading aids. Each chapter reference links to the source text.

A tiny sound key: ā = long “aa” · ī = long “ee” · ū = long “oo” · ś / ṣ ≈ “sh” · ḥ = a light breath · ṁ = a nasal sound.

  1. 1. Kṛṣṇa, the cowherd boy · Bhāgavatam 1.8.21

    Anuṣṭubh

    कृष्णाय वासुदेवायदेवकीनन्दनाय च ।नन्दगोपकुमारायगोविन्दाय नमो नमः ॥

    kṛṣṇāya vāsudevāyadevakī-nandanāya cananda-gopa-kumārāyagovindāya namo namaḥ

    Plain sense: Salutations to Kṛṣṇa, the son of Vasudeva and Devakī, and the cowherd boy of Nanda.

  2. 2. The lotus imagery · Bhāgavatam 1.8.22

    Anuṣṭubh

    नमः पङ्कजनाभायनमः पङ्कजमालिने ।नमः पङ्कजनेत्रायनमस्ते पङ्कजाङ्घ्रये ॥

    namaḥ paṅkaja-nābhāyanamaḥ paṅkaja-mālinenamaḥ paṅkaja-netrāyanamas te paṅkajāṅghraye

    Plain sense: Salutations to the Lord whose navel, garland, eyes, and feet are praised as lotus-like.

  3. 3. All paths point to Vāsudeva · Bhāgavatam 1.2.28

    Anuṣṭubh

    वासुदेवपरा वेदावासुदेवपरा मखाः ।वासुदेवपरा योगावासुदेवपराः क्रियाः ॥

    vāsudeva-parā vedāvāsudeva-parā makhāḥvāsudeva-parā yogāvāsudeva-parāḥ kriyāḥ

    Plain sense: Scripture, worship, yoga, and action all have Vāsudeva as their highest aim.

  4. 4. The highest goal · Bhāgavatam 1.2.29

    Anuṣṭubh

    वासुदेवपरं ज्ञानंवासुदेवपरं तपः ।वासुदेवपरो धर्मोवासुदेवपरा गतिः ॥

    vāsudeva-paraṁ jñānaṁvāsudeva-paraṁ tapaḥvāsudeva-paro dharmovāsudeva-parā gatiḥ

    Plain sense: Knowledge, spiritual effort, dharma, and life’s highest goal all lead to Vāsudeva.

  5. 5. Nine ways of devotion · Bhāgavatam 7.5.23

    Anuṣṭubh

    श्रवणं कीर्तनं विष्णोःस्मरणं पादसेवनम् ।अर्चनं वन्दनं दास्यंसख्यमात्मनिवेदनम् ॥

    śravaṇaṁ kīrtanaṁ viṣṇoḥsmaraṇaṁ pāda-sevanamarcanaṁ vandanaṁ dāsyaṁsakhyam ātma-nivedanam

    Plain sense: Hear and chant about Viṣṇu, remember and serve him, worship and pray, be a servant and friend, and offer yourself.

  6. 6. Begin with the name · Bhāgavatam 6.3.22

    Anuṣṭubh

    एतावानेव लोकेऽस्मिन्पुंसां धर्मः परः स्मृतः ।भक्तियोगो भगवतितन्नामग्रहणादिभिः ॥

    etāvān eva loke ’sminpuṁsāṁ dharmaḥ paraḥ smṛtaḥbhakti-yogo bhagavatitan-nāma-grahaṇādibhiḥ

    Plain sense: The highest path is devotion to the Lord, beginning with repeating his name.

  7. 7. Vraja’s good fortune · Bhāgavatam 10.14.32

    Anuṣṭubh

    अहो भाग्यमहो भाग्यंनन्दगोपव्रजौकसाम् ।यन्मित्रं परमानन्दंपूर्णं ब्रह्म सनातनम् ॥

    aho bhāgyam aho bhāgyaṁnanda-gopa-vrajaukasāmyan-mitraṁ paramānandaṁpūrṇaṁ brahma sanātanam

    Plain sense: How fortunate are Vraja’s people: the eternal source of joy is their friend.

  8. 8. A prayer in hard times · Bhāgavatam 1.8.25

    Anuṣṭubh

    विपदः सन्तु ताः शश्वत्तत्र तत्र जगद्गुरो ।भवतो दर्शनं यत्स्याद्अपुनर्भवदर्शनम् ॥

    vipadaḥ santu tāḥ śaśvattatra tatra jagad-gurobhavato darśanaṁ yat syādapunar bhava-darśanam

    Plain sense: Let troubles come again and again, if they mean seeing You and not returning to this cycle.

  9. 9. A joyful heart · Bhāgavatam 1.2.6

    Anuṣṭubh

    स वै पुंसां परो धर्मोयतो भक्तिरधोक्षजे ।अहैतुक्यप्रतिहताययात्मा सुप्रसीदति ॥

    sa vai puṁsāṁ paro dharmoyato bhaktir adhokṣajeahaituky apratihatāyayātmā suprasīdati

    Plain sense: The highest way of life is steady, wholehearted devotion, which brings deep contentment.

  10. 10. Devotion brings clarity · Bhāgavatam 1.2.7

    Anuṣṭubh

    वासुदेवे भगवतिभक्तियोगः प्रयोजितः ।जनयत्याशु वैराग्यंज्ञानं च यदहैतुकम् ॥

    vāsudeve bhagavatibhakti-yogaḥ prayojitaḥjanayaty āśu vairāgyaṁjñānaṁ ca yad ahaitukam

    Plain sense: Devotion to Vāsudeva soon brings understanding and a calm freedom from clinging.
