VP 3.15.1

अथ पञ्चदशोऽध्यायः और्वः- ब्राह्मणान्भोजयेच्छ्राद्धे यद्गुणांस्तान्निबोध मे ॥१॥

Aurva: What are the expected qualifications of the Brahmins, who are to be fed during a shraaddha.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

atha pañcadaśo'dhyāyaḥ aurvaḥ- brāhmaṇānbhojayecchrāddhe yadguṇāṃstānnibodha me — Aurva: What are the expected qualifications of the Brahmins, who are to be fed during a shraaddha.

Śrīdhara

iti kṛtyān vidhīnatra śrāddhīyān brāhmaṇānapi — thus kṛtyān vidhīnatra śrāddhīyān brāhmaṇānapi; bhoktuḥ kartṛśca varjyāni prāha pañcadaśe muniḥ — bhoktuḥ kartṛśca varjyāni prāha pañcadaśe muniḥ; atha pārvaṇaśrāddhaprayogān vakṣyan prathamaṃ śrāddhīyān brāhmaṇānāha—brāhmaṇān iti tribhiḥ — atha pārvaṇaśrāddhaprayogān vakṣyan prathamaṃ śrāddhīyān brāhmaṇānāhabrāhmaṇān thus tribhiḥ; yad-guṇān yair guṇair yuktān — yad + guṇān yair guṇair yuktān; dvitīyakaṭhikasthāstrayo'nuvākāstriṇāciketāḥ, tadadhyāyī tadanuṣṭhātā ca triṇāciketaḥ — dvitīyakaṭhikasthāstrayo'nuvākāstriṇāciketāḥ tadadhyāyī tadanuṣṭhātā and triṇāciketaḥ; madhuvātā iti tryṛcādhyāyī tadvṛtaśca trimadhuḥ — madhuvātā thus tryṛcādhyāyī tadvṛtaśca trimadhuḥ; brāhmaṇenmān ityādyanuvākatrayādhyāyī tadvṛtaśca trisuparṇaḥ — brāhmaṇenmān ityādyanuvākatrayādhyāyī tadvṛtaśca trisuparṇaḥ; ṣaṭ aṅgāni yasya taṃ vedamadhīte vetti vā ṣaḍaṅgavit — ṣaṭ aṅgāni yasya taṃ vedamadhīte vetti or ṣaḍaṅgavit

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इति कृत्यान् विधीनत्र श्राद्धीयान् ब्राह्मणानपि । भोक्तुः कर्तृश्च वर्ज्यानि प्राह पञ्चदशे मुनिः । अथ पार्वणश्राद्धप्रयोगान् वक्ष्यन् प्रथमं श्राद्धीयान् ब्राह्मणानाह—ब्राह्मणान् इति त्रिभिः । यद्-गुणान् यैर् गुणैर् युक्तान् । द्वितीयकठिकस्थास्त्रयोऽनुवाकास्त्रिणाचिकेताः, तदध्यायी तदनुष्ठाता च त्रिणाचिकेतः । मधुवाता इति त्र्यृचाध्यायी तद्वृतश्च त्रिमधुः । ब्राह्मणेन्मान् इत्याद्यनुवाकत्रयाध्यायी तद्वृतश्च त्रिसुपर्णः । षट् अङ्गानि यस्य तं वेदमधीते वेत्ति वा षडङ्गवित् ॥१-२॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1–2).

Direct lexical rendering in the source order: thus kṛtyān vidhīnatra śrāddhīyān brāhmaṇānapi bhoktuḥ kartṛśca varjyāni prāha pañcadaśe muniḥ atha pārvaṇaśrāddhaprayogān vakṣyan prathamaṃ śrāddhīyān brāhmaṇānāhabrāhmaṇān thus tribhiḥ yad + guṇān yair guṇair yuktān dvitīyakaṭhikasthāstrayo'nuvākāstriṇāciketāḥ tadadhyāyī tadanuṣṭhātā and triṇāciketaḥ madhuvātā thus tryṛcādhyāyī tadvṛtaśca trimadhuḥ brāhmaṇenmān ityādyanuvākatrayādhyāyī tadvṛtaśca. VP 3.15.2

त्रिणाचिकेतस्त्रिमधुस्त्रिसुपर्णष्षडङ्गवित् । वेदविच्छ्रोत्रियो योगी तथा वै ज्येष्ठसामगः ॥२॥

The first eligibility is trinaachiketa. The next is trimadhu. Next is trisuparna. Next is shadangavit - one who has knowledge of all the six angaas. Vedavit is one who has studied the Vedaas fully. A shrotriya. Vedavit and shrotriya. A yogi. A jyestha saamaga.
