VP 2.11.1

यदेतद्भगवानाह गणस्सप्तविधो रवेः ।
मण्डले हिमतापादेः कारणं तन्मया श्रुतम् ॥१॥

You have related to me, holy preceptor, the seven classes of beings who are ever present in the solar orb, and are the causes of heat and cold;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yad etat — what this; bhagavān āha — the blessed one has said; gaṇaḥ saptavidhaḥ — sevenfold group; raveḥ — of the sun; maṇḍale — in the orb; hima-tāpa-ādeḥ kāraṇam — cause of cold, heat, and the rest; śrutam — heard.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yadetadbhagavānāha gaṇassaptavidho raveḥ | maṇḍale himatāpādeḥ kāraṇaṃ tanmayā śrutam ||1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उक्तानुवादपूर्वकं रवेर्विशेषं पृच्छति—यदेतद् इति पञ्चभिः ॥१-३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara notes that Maitreya asks about the sun’s distinct function after repeating what has already been explained.

VP 2.11.2

व्यापारश्चापि कथितो गन्धर्वोरगरक्षसाम् ।
ऋषीणां वालखिल्यानां तथैवाप्सरसां गुरो ॥२॥

you have also described to me their individual functions, sustained by the energy of Vishnu:

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vyāpāraḥ — functions; kathitaḥ — described; gandharva-uraga-rakṣasām — of Gandharvas, serpents, and Rākṣasas; ṛṣīṇām — of sages; Vālakhilyānām — of Vālakhilyas; apsarasām — of nymphs; guro — O preceptor.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vyāpāraścāpi kathito gandharvoragarakṣasām | ṛṣīṇāṃ vālakhilyānāṃ tathaivāpsarasāṃ guro ||2 ||

उक्तानुवादपूर्वकं रवेर्विशेषं पृच्छति—यदेतद् इति पञ्चभिः ॥१-३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara notes that Maitreya asks about the sun’s distinct function after repeating what has already been explained.

VP 2.11.3

यक्षाणां च रथे भानोर्विष्णुशक्तिधृतात्मनाम् ।
किं चादित्यस्य यत्कर्म तन्नात्रोक्तं त्वया मुने ॥३॥

but you have not told me the duty of the sun himself;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yakṣāṇām — of Yakṣas; rathe bhānoḥ — in the sun’s chariot; viṣṇu-śakti-dhṛta-ātmanām — whose selves are upheld by Vishnu’s power; kiṃ ca — and what; ādityasya karma — the sun’s function; na uktam — not stated; tvayā — by you.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yakṣāṇāṃ ca rathe bhānorviṣṇuśaktidhṛtātmanām | kiṃ cādityasya yatkarma tannātroktaṃ tvayā mune ||3 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उक्तानुवादपूर्वकं रवेर्विशेषं पृच्छति—यदेतद् इति पञ्चभिः ॥१-३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara notes that Maitreya asks about the sun’s distinct function after repeating what has already been explained.

VP 2.11.4

यदि सप्तगणो वारि हिममुष्णं च वर्षति ।
तत्किमत्र रवेर्येन वृष्टिः सूर्यादितीर्यते ॥ ४ ॥

for if, as you say, the seven beings in his sphere are the causes of heat, cold, and rain, how can it be also true, as you have before mentioned, that rain proceeds from the sun?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yadi — if; sapta-gaṇaḥ — the sevenfold group; vāri, himam, uṣṇam — water, snow, and heat; varṣati — causes to fall; kim atra raveḥ — what here belongs to the sun; yena vṛṣṭiḥ — by which rain; sūryāt īryate — is said to proceed from the sun.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yadi saptagaṇo vāri himamuṣṇaṃ ca varṣati | tatkimatra raveryena vṛṣṭiḥ sūryāditīryate || 4 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नन्वादित्यस्यापि वृष्टिकर्मोक्तम् एव विवस्वानष्टभिर्मासैरित्यादिना अत आह—यदि इति ॥ सोऽयं सप्तगणो हिमोष्णवारिवृष्टीनां हेतुरिति साधारण्यस्योक्तत्वादित्यर्थः ॥४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Although the sun’s rain-producing function was already stated, the seven groups are also said generally to cause snow, heat, and rain; Maitreya asks how this is to be understood.

VP 2.11.5

विवस्वानुदितो मध्ये यात्यस्तमिति किं जनः ।
ब्रवीत्येतत्समं कर्म यदि सप्तगणस्य तत् ॥ ५॥

or how can it be asserted that the sun rises, reaches the meridian, or sets, if these situations be the act of the collective seven.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vivasvān — the sun; uditaḥ — risen; madhye — in the middle; yāti astam — goes to setting; kim janāḥ — why do people; bravīti — say; etat samam karma — this common function; yadi — if; sapta-gaṇasya — of the sevenfold group; tat — that.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vivasvānudito madhye yātyastamiti kiṃ janaḥ | bravītyetatsamaṃ karma yadi saptagaṇasya tat || 5 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ननु साधारणत्वात् रवेरपि तत् कर्म भवत्येव । तत्राह—विवस्वान् इति ॥५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Since the function is described as common, the sun too participates in it; this verse raises that point.

VP 2.11.6

मैत्रेय श्रूयतामेतद्यद्भवान्परिपृच्छति ।
यथा सप्तगणेऽप्येकः प्राधान्येनाधिको रविः ॥६॥ ..

I will explain to you, Maitreya, the subject of your inquiry. The sun, though identified with the seven beings in his orb, is distinct from them as their chief.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrūyatām — let it be heard; etat — this; yathā — how; sva-gaṇe api — even among his own group; ekaḥ — one; prādhānyena — by superiority; adhikaḥ — predominant; raviḥ — the sun.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreya śrūyatāmetadyadbhavānparipṛcchati | yathā saptagaṇe'pyekaḥ prādhānyenādhiko raviḥ ||6 || ..

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विष्णुशक्त्यधिष्ठानातिशयाद्रविप्राधान्येन सर्वोऽप्ययं व्यवहारो युक्त इति परिहारम् आह—मैत्रेय इत्यादिना ॥६॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Because Vishnu’s power rests in the sun in a special way, the ordinary activity of the whole group is properly attributed chiefly to the sun.

VP 2.11.7

सर्वशक्तिः परा विष्णोः ऋग्यजुस्सामसंज्ञिता ।
सैषा त्रयी तपत्यंहो जगतश्च हिनस्ति या ॥७॥

The entire and mighty energy of Vishnu, which is called the three Vedas, or Rich, Yajush, and Saman, is that which enlightens the world, and destroys its iniquity.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarva-śaktiḥ — entire power; parā — supreme; viṣṇoḥ — of Vishnu; ṛk-yajuḥ-sāma-saṃjñitā — called Rig, Yajur, and Saman; sā eṣā — this very power; trayī — threefold Vedic knowledge; tapati — burns; aṃhaḥ — sin; jagataḥ hinasti — destroys the world’s sin.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarvaśaktiḥ parā viṣṇoḥ ṛgyajussāmasaṃjñitā | saiṣā trayī tapatyaṃho jagataśca hinasti yā ||7 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यद् यस्माद् या परा सर्वार्थप्रकाशिका विष्णोस्त्रयीरूपा शक्तिः सैवैषा रविरूपेण तपति अहंश्च पापमुपासिता सती हिनस्ति, अतः प्राधान्येनाधिको रविः, सैषा त्रयेव विद्या तपति इति श्रुतेः । सर्वा शक्तिरिति पाठे सर्वा शक्तिः समग्रा शक्तिरित्यर्थः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The supreme power of Vishnu, illuminating every object in the form of the three Vedas, burns as the sun and destroys sin; therefore the sun is chief.

VP 2.11.8

सैषा विष्णुः स्थितः स्थित्यां जगतः पालनोद्यतः ।
ऋग्यजुस्सामभूतोऽन्तः सवितुर्द्विज तिष्ठति॥८॥

It is that also which, during the continuance of things, is present as Vishnu, actively engaged in the preservation of the universe,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saiṣa viṣṇuḥ — this is Vishnu; sthitaḥ sthityām — abiding in preservation; jagataḥ pālana-udyataḥ — intent on preserving the world; ṛk-yajuḥ-sāma-bhūtaḥ — consisting of the three Vedas; antaḥ savituḥ — within the sun; tiṣṭhati — abides.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saiṣā viṣṇuḥ sthitaḥ sthityāṃ jagataḥ pālanodyataḥ | ṛgyajussāmabhūto'ntaḥ saviturdvija tiṣṭhati ||8 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अत एव तस्य विष्णुना अभेदमेवाभिप्रेत्याह—सैव इति । जगतः स्थित्यां स्थितः सन् पालनायोद्यतो विष्णुर्नाम सा शक्तिरेवेत्यन्वयः ॥८-९॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Vishnu, intent on preserving the world, abides as this very power; because the possessor of power and the power are non-different, the verse identifies them.

VP 2.11.9

मासिमासि रविर्योयस्तत्र तत्र हि सा परा ।
त्रयीमयी विष्णुशक्तिरवस्थानं करोति वै ॥९॥

and abiding as the three Vedas within the sun. The solar luminary that appears in every month is nothing else than that very supreme energy of Vishnu which is composed of the three Vedas,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māsi māsi — in each month; raviḥ yaḥ yaḥ — whatever sun; tatra tatra — there and there; sā parā — that supreme power; trayīmayī viṣṇuśaktiḥ — Vishnu’s three-Veda power; avasthānam karoti — makes its station.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māsimāsi raviryoyastatra tatra hi sā parā | trayīmayī viṣṇuśaktiravasthānaṃ karoti vai ||9 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अत एव तस्य विष्णुना अभेदमेवाभिप्रेत्याह—सैव इति । जगतः स्थित्यां स्थितः सन् पालनायोद्यतो विष्णुर्नाम सा शक्तिरेवेत्यन्वयः ॥८-९॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The same supreme power takes its place in the sun of each month, and the three-Veda form of Vishnu remains within the solar orb.

VP 2.11.10

ऋचस्स्तुवन्ति पूर्वाह्णे मध्याह्नेऽथ यजूंषि वै ।
बृहद्रथन्तरादीनि सामान्यह्नःक्षये रविम् ॥१०॥

influencing the motions of the planet; for the Richas (the hymns of the Rig-veda) shine in the morning,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛcaḥ — Rig hymns; stuvanti — praise; pūrvāhne — in the morning; madhyāhne — at midday; yajūṃṣi — Yajur formulas; bṛhadrathantara-ādīni sāmāni — the Bṛhad-rathantara and other Saman chants; ahnaḥ kṣaye — at day’s end; ravim — the sun.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛcasstuvanti pūrvāhṇe madhyāhne'tha yajūṃṣi vai | bṛhadrathantarādīni sāmānyahnaḥkṣaye ravim ||10 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ऋचस्तपन्ति इत्यत्र श्रुतिः—ऋग्भिः पूर्वाह्णे दिवि देव ईयते यजुर्वेदन तिष्ठति मध्येऽह्नः । सामवेदेनास्तमये महीयते इत्यादि ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The Vedic revelation says that the Rig hymns glorify the sun in the morning, the Yajur at midday, and the Saman at sunset.

VP 2.11.11

अङ्गमेषा त्रयी विष्णो ऋग्यजुस्सामसंज्ञिता ।
विष्णुशक्तिरवस्थानं सदाऽऽदित्ये करोति सा ॥११॥

the prayers of the Yajush at noon,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aṅgam — limb or form; eṣā trayī — this threefold Vedic power; viṣṇoḥ — of Vishnu; ṛk-yajuḥ-sāma-saṃjñitā — called Rig, Yajur, and Saman; viṣṇuśaktiḥ — Vishnu’s power; avasthānam karoti — makes its station; sadā āditye — always in the sun.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aṅgameṣā trayī viṣṇo ṛgyajussāmasaṃjñitā | viṣṇuśaktiravasthānaṃ sadā''ditye karoti sā ||11 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अङ्गं मूर्तिः ॥११॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The Vedic revelation says that the Rig hymns glorify the sun in the morning, the Yajur at midday, and the Saman at sunset.

VP 2.11.12

न केवलं रवेः शक्तिर्वैष्णवी सा त्रयीमयी ।
ब्रह्माथ पुरुषो रुद्रस्त्रयमेतत्त्रयीमयम् ॥१२॥

and the Vrihadrathantara and other portions of the Saman in the afternoon.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na kevalam — not only; raveḥ — of the sun; śaktiḥ — the power; vaiṣṇavī — Vaishnava; brahmā — Brahma; puruṣaḥ — Purusha; rudraḥ — Rudra; trayam etat — this triad; trayīmayam — made of the threefold Veda.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na kevalaṃ raveḥ śaktirvaiṣṇavī sā trayīmayī | brahmātha puruṣo rudrastrayametattrayīmayam ||12 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

न केवलं रवेरेव सा शक्तिरधिष्ठात्री किन्तु ब्रह्मादीनामपीत्याह—न इति । पुरुषो विष्णुस्त्रयीमयं त्रयीप्रधानं, तदधिष्ठितमित्यर्थः ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Here “limb” means form or embodiment: the threefold Vedic power is the body of Vishnu and remains always in the sun.

VP 2.11.13

सर्गादौ ऋङ्मयो ब्रह्मा स्थितौ विष्णुर्यजुर्मयः ।
रुद्रस्साममयोऽन्ताय तस्मात्तस्याशुचिर्ध्वनिः ॥१३॥

This triple impersonation of Vishnu, distinguished by the titles of the three Vedas, is the energy of Vishnu, which influences the positions of the sun.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarga-ādau — at creation’s beginning; ṛṅmayaḥ brahmā — Brahma consists of the Rig; sthitau viṣṇuḥ yajuḥmayaḥ — in preservation Vishnu consists of the Yajur; rudraḥ sāmamayaḥ — Rudra consists of the Saman; antāya — for destruction; tasmāt — therefore; aśuciḥ dhvaniḥ — its sound is inauspicious.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sargādau ṛṅmayo brahmā sthitau viṣṇuryajurmayaḥ | rudrassāmamayo'ntāya tasmāttasyāśucirdhvaniḥ ||13 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यस्तस्मात् सामशक्त्वा रुद्रोऽन्तं करोति, तस्मान्नाशकरत्वात् तस्य साम्नो ध्वनिरशुचिः अशुचिदेशकालादिवद् वेदान्तरस्यानध्यायत्वापादक इत्यर्थः, न सामध्वनावृग्यजुषी इति गौतमस्मृतेः ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The power is not the presiding power of the sun alone; it also presides over Brahma and the others. Purusha means Vishnu, the principal reality of the threefold Vedic essence.

VP 2.11.14

एवं सा सात्त्विकी शक्तिर्वैष्णवी या त्रयीमयी ।
आत्मसप्तगणस्थं तं भास्वन्तमधितिष्ठति ॥ १४ ॥

But this triple energy of Vishnu is not limited to the sun alone, for Brahma, Purusha (Vishnu), and Rudra are also made up of the same triform essence.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evam — thus; sā sāttvikī śaktiḥ — that sattvic power; vaiṣṇavī — Vaishnava; yā trayīmayī — which is made of the three Vedas; ātma-sapta-gaṇa-stham — situated in its own sevenfold group; tam bhāsvantam — that radiant one; adhitiṣṭhati — presides over.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃ sā sāttvikī śaktirvaiṣṇavī yā trayīmayī | ātmasaptagaṇasthaṃ taṃ bhāsvantamadhitiṣṭhati || 14 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आत्मसप्तगणस्थं स्वाधिष्ठेये सप्तगणे स्थितं भास्वन्तं सूर्यमतिशयेनाधितिष्ठति ॥१४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Because Rudra brings about dissolution through the Saman power, its sound is called inauspicious in the sense that it brings destruction; this does not condemn the Saman sound together with the Rig and Yajur.

VP 2.11.15

तया चाधिष्ठितस्सोऽपि जाज्वलीति स्वरश्मिभिः ।
तमस्समस्तजगतां नाशं नयति चाखिलम् ॥१५॥

In creation it is Brahma, consisting of the Rig-veda;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tayā adhitiṣṭhitaḥ — presided over by it; saḥ api — he too; jājvalīti — shines intensely; sva-raśmibhiḥ — with his own rays; tamaḥ — darkness; samasta-jagatām — of all worlds; nāśam nayati — brings to destruction; akhilam — all.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tayā cādhiṣṭhitasso'pi jājvalīti svaraśmibhiḥ | tamassamastajagatāṃ nāśaṃ nayati cākhilam ||15 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जाज्वलीति अतिशयेन प्रकाशते ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The sattvic Vaishnava power, made of the three Vedas, presides intensely over the radiant sun situated among its sevenfold attendants.

VP 2.11.16

स्तुवन्ति चैनं मुनयो गन्धर्वैर्गीयते पुरः ।
नृत्यन्त्यप्सरसो यान्ति तस्य चानु निशाचराः ॥१६॥

in preservation it is Vishnu, composed of the Yajur-veda;
