Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 11 — Household Duties and Stores

BP 1.11.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
या पतिं दैवतं पश्येन्मनोवाक्कायकर्मभिः ।
तच्छरीरार्धजातेव सर्वदा हितमाचरेत् ॥

A wife who regards her husband as a deity in thought, speech, and bodily acts should always act for his good, as if born as half of his body.

ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
या पतिम् दैवतम् पश्येत् (yā patim daivatam paśyet) — wife who regards husband as deity;
मनः-वाक्-काय-कर्मभिः (manaḥ-vāk-kāya-karmabhiḥ) — through mind, speech, body and action;
तत्-शरीर-अर्ध-जाता इव (tat-śarīra-ardha-jātā iva) — as half born from his body;
सर्वदा हितम् आचरेत् (sarvadā hitam ācaret) — should always promote welfare

BP 1.11.2

तत्प्रियां प्रियवत्पश्येत्तद्द्वेष्या द्वेष्यवत्सदा ।
अधर्मानर्थयुक्तेभ्योऽयुक्ता चास्य निवर्तते ॥

She should regard what is dear to him as dear, and what he hates as hateful; she should turn him away from wrongdoing, harm, and what is improper.

तत्-प्रियान् (tat-priyān) — those dear to him;
प्रिय-वत् पश्येत् (priya-vat paśyet) — should regard as dear;
तत्-द्वेष्यान् (tat-dveṣyān) — those he opposes;
द्वेष्य-वत् सदा (dveṣya-vat sadā) — as adverse always;
अधर्म-अनर्थ-युक्तेभ्यः (adharma-anartha-yuktebhyaḥ) — from paths joined with adharma and loss;
अयुक्तात् च (ayuktāt ca) — and improper;
अस्य निवर्तते (asya nivartate) — turns him away

BP 1.11.3

प्रियं किमस्य किं पथ्यं साम्यं चास्य कथं भवेत् ।
ज्ञात्वैवं सर्वभृत्येषु न प्रमाद्येत वै द्विजाः ॥

Knowing what pleases him, what is wholesome for him, and how harmony may be kept, O twice-born, she should not be heedless regarding any of the servants.

प्रियम् किम् अस्य (priyam kim asya) — what pleases him;
किम् पथ्यम् (kim pathyam) — what is wholesome;
साम्यम् च अस्य कथम् भवेत् (sāmyam ca asya katham bhavet) — how balance may be;
ज्ञात्वा एवम् (jñātvā evam) — discerning thus;
सर्व-भृत्येषु (sarva-bhṛtyeṣu) — concerning all servants;
न प्रमाद्येत् (na pramādyet) — should not be negligent;
द्विजाः (dvijāḥ) — O twice-born

BP 1.11.4

देवतापितृकार्येषु भर्तुः स्नानाशनादिषु ।
सत्कारेऽभ्यागतानां च यथौचित्यं न हापयेत् ॥

In rites for the gods and ancestors, in her husband's bathing and eating and the like, and in honoring arriving guests, she should not fail to act as is fitting.

देवता-पितृ-कार्येषु (devatā-pitṛ-kāryeṣu) — in rites for gods and ancestors;
भर्तुः स्नान-अशन-आदिषु (bhartuḥ snāna-aśana-ādiṣu) — in husband’s bathing, meals and others;
सत्कारे अभ्यागतानाम् (satkāre abhyāgatānām) — in honouring arrivals;
यथा-औचित्यम् (yathā-aucityam) — what is appropriate;
न हापयेत् (na hāpayet) — should not omit

BP 1.11.5

वेश्मात्मा च शरीरं हि गृहिणीनां द्विधा कृतम् ।
संस्कर्तव्यं प्रयत्नेन प्रथमं पश्चिमादपि ॥

For housewives, the house and the body are indeed made into a twofold self. She should tend both with effort, the first before the latter.

वेश्म (veśma) — house;
आत्मा च शरीरम् हि (ātmā ca śarīram hi) — and body as self;
गृहिणीनाम् (gṛhiṇīnām) — for household women;
द्विधा कृतम् (dvidhā kṛtam) — made twofold;
संस्कर्तव्यम् प्रयत्नेन (saṃskartavyam prayatnena) — to be carefully prepared;
प्रथमम् (prathamam) — first;
पश्चिमात् अपि (paścimāt api) — before latter

BP 1.11.6

कृत्वा वेश्म सुसंमृष्टं त्रिकालविहितार्चनम् ।
वृत्तकर्मोपभोगानां संस्कर्तव्यं यथोचितम् ॥

Having thoroughly swept the house and arranged the worship prescribed for the three times, she should properly prepare what is needed for daily duties and enjoyment.

कृत्वा वेश्म सु-संमृष्टम् (kṛtvā veśma su-saṃmṛṣṭam) — making house well swept;
त्रि-काल-विहित-अर्चनम् (tri-kāla-vihita-arcanam) — prescribed worship at three times;
वृत्त-कर्म-उपभोगानाम् (vṛtta-karma-upabhogānām) — for regular duties and uses;
संस्कर्तव्यम् यथा-उचितम् (saṃskartavyam yathā-ucitam) — should prepare appropriately

BP 1.11.7

प्रातर्मध्यापराह्णेषु बहिर्मध्यान्तरेषु च ।
गृहसम्मार्जनं कृत्वा निष्कारान्न निशि क्षिपेत् ॥

Having swept the outer, middle, and inner parts of the house in the morning, midday, and afternoon, she should not throw refuse outside at night.

प्रातः-मध्य-अपराह्णेषु (prātaḥ-madhya-aparāhṇeṣu) — morning, midday and afternoon;
बहिः-मध्य-अन्तरेषु (bahiḥ-madhya-antareṣu) — outer, middle and inner areas;
गृह-संमार्जनम् कृत्वा (gṛha-saṃmārjanam kṛtvā) — sweeping house;
निष्कारान् (niṣkārān) — refuse;
न निशि क्षिपेत् (na niśi kṣipet) — should not throw at night

BP 1.11.8

गोमहिष्यादिशालानां तत्पुरीषादिमात्रकम् ।
व्यपनेयं तु यत्नेन सम्मार्जन्या प्रसाधनम् ॥

She should carefully remove dung and the like from the sheds of cows, buffaloes, and other animals, and sweep them clean.

गो-महिषी-आदि-शालानाम् (go-mahiṣī-ādi-śālānām) — sheds of cows, buffaloes and others;
तत्-पुरीष-आदि-मात्रकम् (tat-purīṣa-ādi-mātrakam) — their dung and similar waste;
व्यपनेयम् यत्नेन (vyapaneyam yatnena) — to be removed carefully;
संमार्जन्या प्रसाधनम् (saṃmārjanyā prasādhanam) — finishing with broom

BP 1.11.9

दासकर्मकरादीनां बाह्याभ्यन्तरचारिणाम् ।
पोषणादिविधिं विद्यादनुष्ठानं च कर्मसु ॥

She should know the provisions for servants, laborers, and others who move within and outside, and their performance of their work.

दास-कर्म-कर-आदीनाम् (dāsa-karma-kara-ādīnām) — servants, labourers and others;
बाह्य-आभ्यन्तर-चारिणाम् (bāhya-ābhyantara-cāriṇām) — working outside and inside;
पोषण-आदि-विधिम् विद्यात् (poṣaṇa-ādi-vidhim vidyāt) — should know rules for nourishment;
अनुष्ठानम् च कर्मसु (anuṣṭhānam ca karmasu) — and performance in tasks

BP 1.11.10

शाकमूलफलादीनां वल्लीनामौषधस्य च ।
सङ्ग्रहः सर्वबीजानां यथाकालं यथाबलम् ॥

She should gather vegetables, roots, fruits, creepers, medicinal plants, and all kinds of seeds at the proper time and according to her means.

शाक-मूल-फल-आदीनाम् (śāka-mūla-phala-ādīnām) — vegetables, roots, fruits and others;
वल्लीनाम् (vallīnām) — vines;
औषधस्य च (auṣadhasya ca) — and medicine;
सङ्ग्रहः (saṅgrahaḥ) — collection;
सर्व-बीजानाम् (sarva-bījānām) — of all seeds;
यथा-कालम् (yathā-kālam) — according to season;
यथा-बलम् (yathā-balam) — according to means

BP 1.11.11

ताम्रकांस्यायसादीनां काष्ठवेणुमयस्य च ।
मृन्मयानां च भाण्डानां विविधानां च सङ्ग्रहम् ॥

She should gather various vessels made of copper, bronze, iron, and other metals, of wood and bamboo, and of clay.

ताम्र-कांस्य-अयस-आदीनाम् (tāmra-kāṃsya-ayasa-ādīnām) — copper, bronze, iron and others;
काष्ठ-वेणु-मयस्य च (kāṣṭha-veṇu-mayasya ca) — made of wood and bamboo;
मृत्-मयानाम् च (mṛt-mayānām ca) — and made of clay;
भाण्डानाम् विविधानाम् (bhāṇḍānām vividhānām) — of various vessels;
सङ्ग्रहम् (saṅgraham) — collection

BP 1.11.12

कुण्डकादिजलद्रोण्या कलशोदञ्चतालुकाः ।
शाकपात्राण्यनेकानि स्नेहानां गोरसस्य च ॥

She should have water tubs, pitchers, water-drawing vessels and ladles, many vegetable dishes, and vessels for oils and dairy products.

कुण्डक-आदि (kuṇḍaka-ādi) — small basins and others;
जल-द्रोण्या (jala-droṇyā) — water trough;
कलश (kalaśa) — pitcher;
उदञ्च (udañca) — drawing vessel;
तालुकाः (tālukāḥ) — ladles or shallow vessels;
शाक-पात्राणि अनेकानि (śāka-pātrāṇi anekāni) — many vegetable dishes;
स्नेहानाम् (snehānām) — for oils;
गो-रसस्य च (go-rasasya ca) — and dairy products

BP 1.11.13

मुसलं कुण्डनीयं तु यन्त्रकं२ चूर्णचालनी ।
दोहन्यो नेत्रकं मन्था मण्डन्यः शृङ्खलानि च ॥

She should have a pestle, mortar, grinder, flour sieve, milking vessels, churning cord and churn, mixing vessels, and chains.

मुसलम् (musalam) — pestle;
कुण्डनीयम् (kuṇḍanīyam) — mortar;
यन्त्रकम् (yantrakam) — mechanical grinder;
चूर्ण-चालनी (cūrṇa-cālanī) — flour sieve;
दोहन्यः (dohanyaḥ) — milking vessels;
नेत्रकम् (netrakam) — churning cord;
मन्था (manthā) — churn;
मण्डन्यः (maṇḍanyaḥ) — mixing bowls;
शृङ्खलानि (śṛṅkhalāni) — chains

BP 1.11.14

संदंशः कुण्डिका शूलाः पट्टपिप्पलको दृषत् ।
डाविका हस्तको दर्वी भ्राष्टस्फुटलकानि च ॥

She should have tongs, small pots, spits, a board, a grinding stone, a scoop, hand tools, a ladle, and implements for roasting and frying.

संदंशः (saṃdaṃśaḥ) — tongs;
कुण्डिका (kuṇḍikā) — small pot;
शूलाः (śūlāḥ) — skewers;
पट्ट-पिप्पलकः (paṭṭa-pippalakaḥ) — grinding slab or board;
दृषत् (dṛṣat) — grinding stone;
डाविका (ḍāvikā) — small scoop;
हस्तकः (hastakaḥ) — hand tool;
दर्वी (darvī) — ladle;
भ्राष्ट-स्फुटलकानि (bhrāṣṭa-sphuṭalakāni) — roasting and frying implements

BP 1.11.15

तुलाप्रस्थादिमानानि मार्जन्यः पिटकानि च ।
सर्वमेतत्प्रकुर्वीत प्रयत्नेन च सर्वदा ॥

She should carefully provide and always keep scales, standard measures, brooms, and baskets.

तुला (tulā) — scales;
प्रस्थ-आदि-मानानि (prastha-ādi-mānāni) — measures such as prastha;
मार्जन्यः (mārjanyaḥ) — brooms;
पिटकानि च (piṭakāni ca) — and baskets;
सर्वम् एतत् प्रकुर्वीत (sarvam etat prakurvīta) — should procure all this;
प्रयत्नेन (prayatnena) — carefully;
सर्वदा (sarvadā) — always

BP 1.11.16

हिंग्वादिकमथो जाजी पिपल्यो मारिचानि च ।
राजिका धान्यकं शुण्ठी त्रिचतुर्जातकानि च ॥

She should have asafoetida and the like, cumin, long pepper, black pepper, mustard, coriander, dried ginger, and three-spice and four-spice mixtures.

हिङ्गु-आदिकम् (hiṅgu-ādikam) — asafoetida and others;
जाजी (jājī) — cumin;
पिप्पल्यः (pippalyaḥ) — long peppers;
मरिचानि (maricāni) — black pepper;
राजिका (rājikā) — mustard seed;
धान्यकम् (dhānyakam) — coriander;
शुण्ठी (śuṇṭhī) — dried ginger;
त्रि-चतुर्-जातकानि (tri-catur-jātakāni) — groups of three and four spices

BP 1.11.17

लवणं क्षारवर्गाश्च सौवीरकपरूषकौ ।
द्विदलामलकं चिंचा सर्वाश्च स्नेहजातयः ॥

She should have salt, alkaline substances, fermented foods and dried fruits, split pulses, Indian gooseberry, tamarind, and all kinds of oils.

लवणम् (lavaṇam) — salt;
क्षार-वर्गाः (kṣāra-vargāḥ) — alkaline seasonings;
सौवीरक-परूषकौ (sauvīraka-parūṣakau) — sauviraka and parūṣaka;
द्वि-दल (dvi-dala) — split pulses;
आमलकम् (āmalakam) — āmalaka;
चिञ्चा (ciñcā) — tamarind;
सर्वाः स्नेह-जातयः (sarvāḥ sneha-jātayaḥ) — all kinds of oils and fats

BP 1.11.18

शुष्ककाष्ठानि वल्लूरमरिष्टा पिष्टमाषयोः ।
विकाराः पयसश्चापि विविधाः कन्दजातयः ॥

She should have dry firewood, dried meat, fermented preparations, preparations of flour and black gram, various milk products, and kinds of tubers.

शुष्क-काष्ठानि (śuṣka-kāṣṭhāni) — dry firewood;
वल्लूरम् (vallūram) — dried meat;
अरिष्टाः (ariṣṭāḥ) — fermented preparations;
पिष्ट-माषयोः विकाराः (piṣṭa-māṣayoḥ vikārāḥ) — preparations of flour and black gram;
पयसः विविधाः (payasaḥ vividhāḥ) — various milk products;
कन्द-जातयः (kanda-jātayaḥ) — kinds of tubers

BP 1.11.19

नित्यनैमित्तिकानां हि कार्याणामुपयोगतः ।
सर्वमित्यादि संग्राह्यं यथावद्विभवोचितम् ॥

For the sake of daily and occasional duties, she should properly gather all these and similar things according to her means.

नित्य-नैमित्तिकानाम् (nitya-naimittikānām) — of daily and occasional;
कार्याणाम् (kāryāṇām) — duties;
उपयोगतः (upayogataḥ) — because of use;
सर्वम् इति-आदि संग्राह्यम् (sarvam iti-ādi saṃgrāhyam) — all this and similar to be gathered;
यथा-वत् (yathā-vat) — properly;
विभव-उचितम् (vibhava-ucitam) — suited to resources

BP 1.11.20

यत्कार्याणां समुत्पत्तावुपाहर्तुं न दृश्यते ।
तत्प्रागेव यथायोगं सङ्गृह्णीयात्प्रयत्नतः ॥

Whatever cannot be procured when the need arises, she should carefully gather beforehand, as appropriate.

यत् कार्याणाम् समुत्पत्तौ (yat kāryāṇām samutpattau) — what at arising of tasks;
उपाहर्तुम् न दृश्यते (upāhartum na dṛśyate) — is not found easy to procure;
तत् प्राक् एव (tat prāk eva) — that beforehand;
यथा-योगम् (yathā-yogam) — appropriately;
सङ्गृह्णीयात् प्रयत्नतः (saṅgṛhṇīyāt prayatnataḥ) — should gather carefully

BP 1.11.21

धान्यानां घृष्टपिष्टानां क्षुण्णोपहतयोरपि ।
भृशं शुष्कार्द्रसिद्धानां क्षयवृद्धी निरूपयेत् ॥

She should carefully determine the decrease and increase of grains that are pounded, ground, crushed, or damaged, and those that are dry, moist, or cooked.

धान्यानाम् (dhānyānām) — of grains;
घृष्ट-पिष्टानाम् (ghṛṣṭa-piṣṭānām) — rubbed and ground;
क्षुण्ण-उपहतयोः अपि (kṣuṇṇa-upahatayoḥ api) — crushed or damaged;
भृशम् (bhṛśam) — closely;
शुष्क-आर्द्र-सिद्धानाम् (śuṣka-ārdra-siddhānām) — dried, moist and cooked;
क्षय-वृद्धी निरूपयेत् (kṣaya-vṛddhī nirūpayet) — should monitor decrease and increase
