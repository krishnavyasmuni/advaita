VP 1.21.1

संह्रादपुत्र आयुष्मान् शिबिर् बाष्कल एव च । विरोचनस् तु प्राह्लादिर् बलिर् जज्ञे विरोचनात् ॥१॥

The sons of Saṃhrāda were Āyuṣmān, Śibi, and Bāṣkala; Virocana was Prahlāda’s son, and Bali was born from Virocana.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saṃhrāda-putrāḥ — sons of Saṃhrāda; āyuṣmān — Āyuṣmān; śibiḥ — Śibi; bāṣkalaḥ — Bāṣkala; eva ca — and; virocanaḥ tu — Virocana, however; prāhlādiḥ — son of Prahlāda; baliḥ — Bali; jajñe — was born; virocanāt — from Virocana.

Śrīdhara

prāhlādiḥ prahlādasya putraḥ — contextual sense: “Prāhlādi” means the son of Prahlāda.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saṃhrādaputra āyuṣmān śibir bāṣkala eva ca . virocanas tu prāhlādir balir jajñe virocanāt ||१||

Śrīdhara

prāhlādiḥ prahlādasya putraḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्राह्लादिः प्रह्लादस्य पुत्रः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Prāhlādi” means the son of Prahlāda.

VP 1.21.2

VP 1.21.5

एकचक्रो महाबाहुस् तारकश् च महाबलः । स्वर्भानुर् वृषपर्वा च पुलोमश् च महाबलः ॥५॥

The lineages of Aditi and Diti have now been stated. He now begins the lineage of the other wives of Kaśyapa, beginning Danu, in nine and a half verses.

VP 1.21.7

स्वर्भानोस् तु प्रभा कन्या शर्मिष्ठा वार्षपर्वणी । उपदानी हयशिराः प्रख्याता वरकन्यकाः ॥७॥

Svarbhānu’s daughter was Prabhā; Śarmiṣṭhā, Upadānī, and Hayaśirā were the celebrated daughters of Vṛṣaparvan.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

svarbhānoḥ — of Svarbhānu; tu — indeed; prabhā — Prabhā; kanyā — daughter; śarmiṣṭhā — Śarmiṣṭhā; vārṣaparvaṇī — daughter of Vṛṣaparvan; upadānī — Upadānī; hayaśirāḥ — Hayaśirā; prakhyātāḥ — celebrated; vara-kanyakāḥ — excellent maidens/daughters.

Śrīdhara

ete prakhyātā iti viśeṣaṇena harivaṃśamatsyādiṣu danoḥ sutaśate gaṇitā vaiśvānarādayaś ca sucitāḥ| śarmiṣṭhā vṛṣaparvaṇaḥ kanyā — contextual sense: The famous sons are counted among Danu’s hundred sons in the Harivaṃśa, Matsya and other texts; Vaishvānara and the rest are also indicated. Śarmiṣṭhā was the daughter of Vṛṣaparvan.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

svarbhānos tu prabhā kanyā śarmiṣṭhā vārṣaparvaṇī . upadānī hayaśirāḥ prakhyātā varakanyakāḥ ||७||

Śrīdhara

ete prakhyātā iti viśeṣaṇena harivaṃśamatsyādiṣu danoḥ sutaśate gaṇitā vaiśvānarādayaś ca sucitāḥ| śarmiṣṭhā vṛṣaparvaṇaḥ kanyā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एते प्रख्याता इति विशेषणेन हरिवंशमत्स्यादिषु दनोः सुतशते गणिता वैश्वानरादयश् च सुचिताः। शर्मिष्ठा वृषपर्वणः कन्या

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The famous sons are counted among Danu’s hundred sons in the Harivaṃśa, Matsya and other texts; Vaishvānara and the rest are also indicated. Śarmiṣṭhā was the daughter of Vṛṣaparvan.

VP 1.21.8

वैश्वानरसुते चोभे पुलोमा कालका तथा । उभे सुते महाभागे मारीचेस् तु परिग्रहः ॥८॥

Vaiśvānara had two daughters, Pulomā and Kālaka; both were received as wives by Kaśyapa, the son of Marīci.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vaiśvānara-sute — daughters of Vaiśvānara; ca ubhe — both; pulomā — Pulomā; kālakā — Kālaka; tathā — likewise; ubhe sute — both daughters; mahā-bhāge — greatly fortunate; mārīceḥ — of Marīci; tu — indeed; parigrahaḥ — wives/accepted in marriage.

Śrīdhara

upadānavī ca hayaśirāś ca tasyaiva kanyā iti kecit| anyadīye ity anye, vaiśvānarasutāyāstu catasraścārudarśanāḥ upadānavī hayaśirāḥ pulomā kālakā tathā iti bhāgavatādyukteḥ — contextual sense: Some hold Upadānavī and Hayaśirā to be his daughters; others say they belong to Vaishvānara, since the Bhāgavata lists four beautiful daughters: Upadānavī, Hayaśirā, Pulomā and Kālakā.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vaiśvānarasute cobhe pulomā kālakā tathā . ubhe sute mahābhāge mārīces tu parigrahaḥ ||८||

Śrīdhara

upadānavī ca hayaśirāś ca tasyaiva kanyā iti kecit| anyadīye ity anye, vaiśvānarasutāyāstu catasraścārudarśanāḥ upadānavī hayaśirāḥ pulomā kālakā tathā iti bhāgavatādyukteḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उपदानवी च हयशिराश् च तस्यैव कन्या इति केचित्। अन्यदीये इत्य् अन्ये, वैश्वानरसुतायास्तु चतस्रश्चारुदर्शनाः उपदानवी हयशिराः पुलोमा कालका तथा इति भागवताद्युक्तेः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Some hold Upadānavī and Hayaśirā to be his daughters; others say they belong to Vaishvānara, since the Bhāgavata lists four beautiful daughters: Upadānavī, Hayaśirā, Pulomā and Kālakā.

VP 1.21.9

ताभ्याम् पुत्रसहस्राणि षष्ठिर् दानवसत्तमाः । पौलोमाः कालकेयाश् च मारीचतनयाः स्मृताः ॥९॥

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

She was the wife accepted by Marīci’s son Kaśyapa.

VP 1.21.10

VP 1.21.13

एते वै दानवाः श्रेष्ठा दनुवंशविवर्धनाः । एतेषां पुत्रपौत्राश् च शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः ॥१३॥

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ete — these; vai — indeed; dānavāḥ — Dānavas; śreṣṭhāḥ — eminent; danu-vaṃśa-vivardhanāḥ — increasers of Danu’s lineage; eteṣām — of these; putra-pautrāḥ — sons and grandsons; ca — and; śataśaḥ — by hundreds; atha — then; sahasraśaḥ — by thousands.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ete vai dānavāḥ śreṣṭhā danuvaṃśavivardhanāḥ . eteṣāṃ putrapautrāś ca śataśo’tha sahasraśaḥ ||१३||

Śrīdhara

siṃhikāyāṃ hiraṇyakaśiporbhaginyāṃ vipracitterdanusutāt| siṃhāvalokananyāyena punarāha—prahlādasya iti| bhāvitātmanaḥ prahlādasyetyanvayaḥ 1013 ||

VP 1.21.15

षट् सुताः सुमहासत्वास्ताम्रायाः परिकीर्त्तिताः ।
