VP 1.21.1

संह्रादपुत्र आयुष्मान् शिबिर् बाष्कल एव च । विरोचनस् तु प्राह्लादिर् बलिर् जज्ञे विरोचनात् ॥१॥

The sons of Saṃhrāda were Āyuṣmān, Śibi, and Bāṣkala; Virocana was Prahlāda’s son, and Bali was born from Virocana.

VP 1.21.15

षट् सुताः सुमहासत्वास्ताम्रायाः परिकीर्त्तिताः ।
शुकी श्येनी च भासी च सुग्रीवी शुचिगृध्रका ॥१५॥

The six daughters of Tāmra were renowned as women of great power: Śukī, Śyenī, Bhāsī, Sugrīvī, Śucī, and Gṛdhrikā.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṣaṭ sutāḥ — six daughters; sumahā-sattvāḥ — of great power; tāmrayāḥ — of Tāmra; parikīrtitāḥ — are renowned; śukī — Śukī; śyenī — Śyenī; bhāsī — Bhāsī; sugrīvī — Sugrīvī; śucī — Śucī; śucigṛdhrikā — Śucigṛdhrikā

Śrīdhara

tā evāha—śuki iti — Śrīdhara explains the reading of the bird names and notes that Śukī, Śucī, or Sugrīvī is understood to have produced the owls and crows; the text identifies the daughters as Tāmra’s lineage.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṣaṭ sutāḥ sumahāsatvāstāmrāyāḥ parikīrttitāḥ | śukī śyenī ca bhāsī ca sugrīvī śucigṛdhrakā ||15||

Śrīdhara

tā evāha—śuki iti | sugrīvī vā śucirvāpi ulūkān pratyulūkāṃś ca saivājanayadity arthaḥ | na punarūlūkī sañjñā anyā anirddiṣṭatvāt | śukī śukānajanayadulūkī pratyulukakān iti mātsyokteś ca ||15||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ता एवाह—शुकि इति । सुग्रीवी वा शुचिर्वापि उलूकान् प्रत्युलूकांश् च सैवाजनयदित्य् अर्थः । न पुनरूलूकी सञ्ज्ञा अन्या अनिर्द्दिष्टत्वात् । शुकी शुकानजनयदुलूकी प्रत्युलुककान् इति मात्स्योक्तेश् च ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara explains the reading of the bird names and notes that Śukī, Śucī, or Sugrīvī is understood to have produced the owls and crows; the text identifies the daughters as Tāmra’s lineage.

VP 1.21.18
