VP 1.21.1

संह्रादपुत्र आयुष्मान् शिबिर् बाष्कल एव च । विरोचनस् तु प्राह्लादिर् बलिर् जज्ञे विरोचनात् ॥१॥

The sons of Saṃhrāda were Āyuṣmān, Śibi, and Bāṣkala; Virocana was Prahlāda’s son, and Bali was born from Virocana.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — saṃhrāda-putrāḥ — sons of Saṃhrāda; āyuṣmān — Āyuṣmān; śibiḥ — Śibi; bāṣkalaḥ — Bāṣkala; eva ca — and; virocanaḥ tu — Virocana, however; prāhlādiḥ — son of Prahlāda; baliḥ — Bali; jajñe — was born; virocanāt — from Virocana.

Śrīdhara

No commentary.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saṃhrādaputra āyuṣmān śibir bāṣkala eva ca . virocanas tu prāhlādir balir jajñe virocanāt ||१||

Śrīdhara

VP 1.21.2

बलेः पुत्रशतं त्व् आसीद् बाणज्येष्ठं महामुने । हिरण्याक्षसुताश् चासन् सर्व एव महाबलाः ॥२॥
