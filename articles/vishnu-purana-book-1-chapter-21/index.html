VP 1.21.1

संह्रादपुत्र आयुष्मान् शिबिर् बाष्कल एव च । विरोचनस् तु प्राह्लादिर् बलिर् जज्ञे विरोचनात् ॥१॥ ॥1॥

The sons of Prahlāda were the long-lived Āyuṣmān, Śibi, and Bāṣkala; Virocana was Prahlāda’s son, and Bali was born from Virocana.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Key terms — saṃhrādaputra, āyuṣmān, śibir, bāṣkala, eva, ca; direct sense — The sons of Prahlāda were the long-lived Āyuṣmān, Śibi, and Bāṣkala; Virocana was Prahlāda’s son, and Bali was born from Virocana.

Śrīdhara

Commentary — No separate Sanskrit gloss is supplied in the source; the genealogy is read directly from the verse.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saṃhrādaputra āyuṣmān śibir bāṣkala eva ca . virocanas tu prāhlādir balir jajñe virocanāt ||१|| ||1||

Śrīdhara

No separate Sanskrit gloss is supplied in the source.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

No separate Sanskrit gloss is supplied in the source.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The genealogy continues through Prahlāda, Virocana, and Bali.
