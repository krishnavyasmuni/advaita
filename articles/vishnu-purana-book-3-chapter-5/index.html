VP 3.5.1

यजुर्वेद-तरोः शाखाः स्पत्विंशन् महामुनिः।
वैशम्पायन-नामासौ व्यास-शिष्यश् चकार वै

From the tree of the Yajurveda, the great sage Vaiśampāyana, Vyāsa’s disciple, formed twenty-seven branches.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
yajurvedataroḥ śākhāḥ — branches of the tree of the Yajurveda;
saptaviṃśan — twenty-seven;
mahāmuniḥ — the great sage;
vaiśaṃpāyananāmā asau — he named Vaiśampāyana;
vyāsaśiṣyaḥ — Vyāsa’s disciple;
cakāra — made
Śrīdhara
pañcame atha — in the fifth [chapter];
yajuḥśākhāḥ — branches of the Yajurveda;
kathyante atra samāsataḥ — are described here briefly;
saptaviṃśati — twenty-seven;
yajuṣaḥ pradhānaśākhāḥ — principal branches of the Yajus;
ekādhikaśatam adhvaryuśākhāḥ — one hundred and one Adhvaryu branches
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajurvedatarośśākhāssaptaviṃśanmahāmuniḥ। vaiśaṃpāyananāmā’sau vyāsaśiṣyaścakāra vai ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

pañcame’tha yajuḥśākhāḥ kathyante’tra samāsataḥ । setihāsaṃ taittirīyaṃ vājiśākhāpravarttanam ॥ saptaviṃśat saptaviṃśatiḥ yajuṣaḥ pradhānaśākhāḥ । brahmāṇḍe tu ekādhikaśatamadhvaryuśākhā āpastamboktā uktāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पञ्चमेऽथ यजुःशाखाः कथ्यन्तेऽत्र समासतः । सेतिहासं तैत्तिरीयं वाजिशाखाप्रवर्त्तनम् ॥ सप्तविंशत् सप्तविंशतिः यजुषः प्रधानशाखाः । ब्रह्माण्डे तु एकाधिकशतमध्वर्युशाखा आपस्तम्बोक्ता उक्ताः

Śrīdhara commentary.

In this fifth chapter the branches of the Yajurveda are described briefly, including the Taittirīya and Vāji branches. There are twenty-seven principal branches of the Yajus. The Brahmāṇḍa says that Āpastamba taught one hundred and one Adhvaryu branches in all.

शिष्यः परम-धर्मज्ञो गुरु-वृत्ति-परः सदा

Śrīdhara commentary.

To explain the origin of the other Yajurveda branches, another disciple is introduced: “But Yājñavalkya...”

For that very reason—to explain how the two branches arose—

VP 3.5.6

स्वस्त्रीयं बालकं सोऽथ पदा स्पृष्टाम् अघातयत्

He then struck his sister’s child with his foot and killed him.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
svastrīyam bālakam — his sister’s child;
saḥ atha — he then;
padā spṛṣṭam — touched with the foot;
atāḍayat — struck/killed
Śrīdhara
svastīyam — his sister’s son;
svasurḥ sutaḥ — the child of his sister;
padā spṛṣṭam santam — being touched with the foot;
aghātayat — he killed;
ṛṣiśāpavaśāt — by the power of the sage’s curse;
mara — died;
iti arthaḥ — this is the meaning
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

svastrīyaṃ bālakaṃ so’tha padā spṛṣṭamatāḍayat ॥६॥

Śrīdhara

svatīyaṃ svasuḥ sutaṃ padā spṛṣṭaṃ santam aghātayat । ṛṣiśāpavaśāt padā spṛṣṭamātro’sau mamāretyarthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्वतीयं स्वसुः सुतं पदा स्पृष्टं सन्तम् अघातयत् । ऋषिशापवशात् पदा स्पृष्टमात्रोऽसौ ममारेत्यर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary.

He killed his sister’s son, who had merely been touched by his foot. The meaning is that the child died as soon as the foot touched him, through the power of the sage’s curse.

VP 3.5.7

शिष्यान् आह स भो शिष्या ब्रह्म-हत्यापहं व्रतम्।
चरध्वं मत्-कृते सर्वे न विचार्यम् इदं तथा

He said to his disciples, “All of you undertake, for my sake, the vow that removes the sin of brahma-slaughter. Do not deliberate about this.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
śiṣyān āha — he said to the disciples;
bhoḥ śiṣyāḥ — O disciples;
brahmahatyāpahaṃ vratam — the vow that removes brahma-slaughter;
caradhvam — undertake;
matkṛte — for my sake;
sarve — all;
na vicāryam — not to be deliberated;
idam tathā — this, so
Śrīdhara
samarthena anyena — by another capable person;
mahāpātake kṛte — when a great sin has been committed;
anyena — by another;
kathaṃ — how;
tat prāyaścittam — its expiation;
na vicārya — without considering;
sākṣāt vadhe — in the actual killing;
śāstroktam mukhyaprakāram — the principal method prescribed by scripture;
madartham vratam cara — undertake the vow for my sake
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śiṣyānāha ca bhoḥ śiṣyā brahmahatyāpahaṃ vratam । caradhvaṃ matkṛte sarve na vicāryamidaṃ tathā ॥७॥

Śrīdhara

samarthenānyena mahāpātake kṛte’nyena kathaṃ tatprāyaścitaṃ karttavyamitīdaṃ na vicārya, kintu tathā sākṣād vadhe śāstroktamukhyaprakāreṇa madarthaṃ vrataṃ caratetyarthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

समर्थेनान्येन महापातके कृतेऽन्येन कथं तत्प्रायश्चितं कर्त्तव्यमितीदं न विचार्य, किन्तु तथा साक्षाद् वधे शास्त्रोक्तमुख्यप्रकारेण मदर्थं व्रतं चरतेत्यर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary.

When a great sin has been committed by one capable person, how can another person perform its expiation? Without considering that question, the teacher means: because the killing was direct, undertake the vow for my sake in the principal manner prescribed by scripture.

VP 3.5.10

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara commentary.

He addressed all the disciples and forbade them to act, saying that he himself would carry it out. Yājñavalkya is called one who broke the command because he disobeyed that instruction.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hiraṇmayaṃ rathaṃ yasya ketavo’mṛtavājinaḥ । vahanti bhūtānālokacakṣuṣaṃ taṃ namāmyaham ॥२५॥

Śrīdhara

hiraṇmayaṃ tejomayam । ketavaḥ vedamayāḥ aśvā amṛtadhāyinaḥ amṛtāhārāḥ । udutyaṃ jātavedasam ityādi—ṛgartha uktaḥ । hiraṇmaye ratha iti pāṭhe ketavaḥ raśmayaḥ, amṛtaṃ jalaṃ taddhātāraḥ, vahanti gacchantītyarthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

हिरण्मयं तेजोमयम् । केतवः वेदमयाः अश्वा अमृतधायिनः अमृताहाराः । उदुत्यं जातवेदसम् इत्यादि—ऋगर्थ उक्तः । हिरण्मये रथ इति पाठे केतवः रश्मयः, अमृतं जलं तद्धातारः, वहन्ति गच्छन्तीत्यर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Golden” means made of radiance. “Rays” may mean Veda-forms, and “steeds” those that bear or consume nectar; this is the meaning given in the Ṛgvedic reading. In the reading “in the golden chariot,” the rays are the steeds, nectar means water, and they are its bearers.
