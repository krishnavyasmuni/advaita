VP 3.5.1

अथ पञ्चमोऽध्यायः श्रीपराशरः- यजुर्वेदतरोश्शाखास्सप्तविंशन्महामुनिः। वैशंपायननामाऽसौ व्यासशिष्यश्चकार वै ॥१॥

27 shaakhaas, branches of Yajurveda, were done by Vaishampaayana who was shishya of Vyaasa. There are 27 main branches in Yajurveda. The tree of Yajurveda was divided into main branches. Vyaasa's shishya, Vaishampaayana, who was taken as shishya for promulgation of Yajurveda, created 27 main shaakhaas.
