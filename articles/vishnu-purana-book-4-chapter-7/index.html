VP 4.7.1

तस्याप्यायुर्धीमानमावसुर्विश्वावसुःश्रुतायुश्शतायुरयुतायुरितिसंज्ञाः षट् पुत्रा अभवन्

Sri Paraasharar - Pururavas had 6 children - Aayu, who was a very wise and intelligent person, Amaavasu, Vishvaavasu, Shrunaayu, Shataayu, Ayutaayu.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - Pururavas had 6 children - Aayu, who was a very wise and intelligent person, Amaavasu, Vishvaavasu, Shrunaayu, Shataayu, Ayutaayu.

VP 4.7.4
