Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Book 2, Chapter 1

Descendants of Priyavrata, the eldest son of Swáyambhuva Manu: his ten sons: three adopt a religious life; the others become kings of the seven Dwípas, or isles, of the earth. Agnídhra, king of Jambu-dwípa, divides it into nine portions, which he distributes amongst his sons. Nábhi, king of the south, succeeded by Rishabha; and he by Bharata: India named after him Bhárata: his descendants reign during the Swáyambhuva Manwantara.

VP 2.1.43

एष स्वायम्भुवः सर्गो येनेदं पूरितं जगत्।
वाराहे तु मुने कल्पे पूर्व-मन्वन्तराधिपः

Under these princes, Bhárata-varsha (India) was divided into nine portions (to be hereafter particularized); and their descendants successively held possession of the country for seventy-one periods of the aggregate of the four ages (or for the reign of a Manu). This was the creation of Swáyambhuva Manu, by which the earth was peopled, when he presided over the first Manwantara, in the Kalpa of Varáha.

ननु त्वया पूर्वमुत्तानपादादयोऽपि सार्वभौमाः प्रोक्ताः इदानीं प्रियव्रतादयोऽपि सार्वभौमा एवोच्यन्ते, तच्च समकाले न सङ्गच्छते, अतः क्रमो वक्तव्य इत्यत्राह—तेषाम् इति । तेषां प्रियव्रतान्वयप्रसूतानां वंशे प्रसूतैः पुरा प्रथममियं भारती भूमिर्भुक्ता, पश्चादुत्तानपादादिभिः कियन्तं कालमियं भुक्तेत्यत आह—कृतत्रेतादीनां सर्गेण प्रवृत्त्या युगाख्या चतुर्युगैराख्यायते या एकसप्ततिमन्वन्तराख्यः कालः । तावन्तं कालमित्यर्थः । कृतत्रेतादिसञ्ज्ञेयम् इति पाठेऽप्येवम् एव योज्यम् । युगाख्यामेकसप्तति पाठे स्फुटोऽयमर्थः ॥ एतद् एवम् एव स्पष्टयति-एष इति । वाराहेऽस्मिन् कल्पे यदा स्वायम्भुवः पूर्वस्य प्रथममन्वन्तरस्याधिपोऽभूत्, तदा एष प्रियव्रतवंशानां राज्ञां सर्गः, ततः स्वारोचिषादावुत्तानपादवंश्यानामित्यर्थः । अत एव उत्तानपादवंश्यस्य प्राचेतसदक्षकन्यायामादित्यानां जन्म वैवस्वतमन्वन्तरे प्रोक्तम् । सप्तम्यन्तपाठे प्रथममन्वन्तराधिपे स्वायम्भुवे वर्तमाने स्वायम्भुवोऽशभूतात् तस्मादेव मनोरेष प्रियव्रतादिसर्गः प्रथमं प्रवृत्त इत्यर्थः । यद्वा पूर्वोक्तसर्वसृष्टिनिगमनमेतत् एष इति

Śrīdhara commentary.

Maitreya asks about the sequence because Uttānapāda and others were previously called universal emperors, while Priyavrata and others are now also called universal emperors, and they cannot have ruled at the same time. The descendants of Priyavrata first ruled Bhārata; afterward, the descendants of Uttānapāda ruled it for some period. That period is the one-manvantara span measured by the four ages and seventy-one groups of four ages. In this Varāha kalpa, when Svāyambhuva was lord of the first manvantara, the creation of kings from Priyavrata’s line occurred; in the Svārociṣa and later manvantaras, those from Uttānapāda’s line. This also explains why the birth of the Ādityas from Uttānapāda’s line and Dakṣa’s daughter is placed in the Vaivasvata manvantara. Another reading places the beginning of Priyavrata’s line in the first manvantara as part of Svāyambhuva Manu; alternatively, this passage concludes the whole creation described earlier.
