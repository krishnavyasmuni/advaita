VP 3.10.1

कथितं चातुराश्रम्यं चातुर्वर्ण्यक्रियास्तथा । पुंसः क्रियामहं श्रोतुमिच्छामि द्विजसत्तम
॥१॥

Sagara: You have explained the four āśramas and the practices of the four varṇas. O best of the twice-born, I want to hear about the rites prescribed for a person.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kathitaṃ cāturāśramyaṃ cāturvarṇyakriyāstathā । puṃsaḥ kriyāmahaṃ śrotumicchāmi dvijasattama ॥१॥ — kathitam — has been explained; cātuḥ-āśramyam — the four āśramas; cātuḥ-varṇya-kriyāḥ — the practices of the four varṇas; tathā — likewise; puṃsaḥ kriyām — the rites of a person; śrotum icchāmi — I wish to hear; dvija-sattama — O best of the twice-born.

Śrīdhara

puṃsaḥ ṣoḍaśasaṃskārairvarṇāśramaniveśanam । atastānāha daśame bāhulyena dvijātiṣu । catvāra āśramā eva cāturāśramyaṃ taddharmaḥ kathitaḥ, evañcāturvarṇyakriyā api puṃsaḥ kriyāṃ ṣoḍaśasaṃskārarūpām — In this tenth chapter the duties of persons are described, especially the sixteen saṃskāras by which one is placed in varṇa and āśrama. The four āśramas and their duties have already been taught, and the varṇa rites are also forms of the sixteen saṃskāras.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kathitaṃ cāturāśramyaṃ cāturvarṇyakriyāstathā । puṃsaḥ kriyāmahaṃ śrotumicchāmi dvijasattama ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

puṃsaḥ ṣoḍaśasaṃskārairvarṇāśramaniveśanam । atastānāha daśame bāhulyena dvijātiṣu । catvāra āśramā eva cāturāśramyaṃ taddharmaḥ kathitaḥ, evañcāturvarṇyakriyā api puṃsaḥ kriyāṃ ṣoḍaśasaṃskārarūpām

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पुंसः षोडशसंस्कारैर्वर्णाश्रमनिवेशनम् । अतस्तानाह दशमे बाहुल्येन द्विजातिषु । चत्वार आश्रमा एव चातुराश्रम्यं तद्धर्मः कथितः, एवञ्चातुर्वर्ण्यक्रिया अपि पुंसः क्रियां षोडशसंस्काररूपाम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1).

Śrīdhara explains that this chapter describes the duties of persons, especially the sixteen saṃskāras by which one is placed in varṇa and āśrama. The four āśramas and their duties have already been taught, and the varṇa rites are also forms of the sixteen saṃskāras.

VP 3.10.2

नित्यनैमित्तिकाः काम्याः क्रियाः पुंसामशेषतः । समाख्याहि भृगुश्रेष्ठ सर्वज्ञो ह्यसि मे मतः
॥२॥

Tell me fully about the daily, occasional, and desire-motivated rites of human beings; I regard you as omniscient, O best of Bhṛgu’s line.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nityanaimittikāḥ kāmyāḥ kriyāḥ puṃsāmaśeṣataḥ । samākhyāhi bhṛguśreṣṭha sarvajño hyasi me mataḥ ॥२॥ — nitya-naimittikāḥ — daily and occasional; kāmyāḥ kriyāḥ — desire-motivated rites; puṃsām — of people; aśeṣataḥ — completely; samākhyāhi — explain; bhṛgu-śreṣṭha — O best of Bhṛgu’s line; sarva-jñaḥ — omniscient; asi — you are; me mataḥ — in my view.

Śrīdhara

nityāmaharahaḥ kriyamāṇāṃ pratyavāyaparihāraphalām, naimittikīṃ rāhudarśādau nimitte sati vihitānāmakaraṇāt pratyavāyapradāṃ kāmyāṃ kāmāya hitām agniṣṭomādikām — Daily rites are performed every day and remove the fault of omission; occasional rites are prescribed when their particular occasion arises and omission brings fault; desired rites are performed for a desired result, such as the Agniṣṭoma.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nityanaimittikāḥ kāmyāḥ kriyāḥ puṃsāmaśeṣataḥ । samākhyāhi bhṛguśreṣṭha sarvajño hyasi me mataḥ ॥२॥

Śrīdhara

nityāmaharahaḥ kriyamāṇāṃ pratyavāyaparihāraphalām, naimittikīṃ rāhudarśādau nimitte sati vihitānāmakaraṇāt pratyavāyapradāṃ kāmyāṃ kāmāya hitām agniṣṭomādikām

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नित्यामहरहः क्रियमाणां प्रत्यवायपरिहारफलाम्, नैमित्तिकीं राहुदर्शादौ निमित्ते सति विहितानामकरणात् प्रत्यवायप्रदां काम्यां कामाय हिताम् अग्निष्टोमादिकाम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 2).

Śrīdhara defines daily rites as those performed every day to avoid the fault of omission, occasional rites as those prescribed when a particular occasion arises, and desired rites as those performed for a desired result, such as the Agniṣṭoma.

VP 3.10.3

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नित्यनैमितिकाश्रितम् इति काम्यस्याप्युपलक्षणम्

VP 3.10.5

युग्मांस्तु प्राड्मुखान्विप्रान्भोजयेन्मनुभेश्वर । यथा तृप्तिस्तथा कुर्याद्दैवं पित्र्यं द्विजन्मनाम्
॥५॥

He should feed twice-born men facing east in even numbers, O lord of men, and should perform the divine and ancestral rites so that they are satisfied.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yugmāṃstu prāḍmukhānviprānbhojayenmanubheśvara । yathā tṛptistathā kuryāddaivaṃ pitryaṃ dvijanmanām ॥५॥ — yugmān — even-numbered; prāṅ-mukhān — facing east; viprān — Brāhmaṇas; bhojayet — should feed; manuja-īśvara — O lord of men; yathā tṛptiḥ — as satisfaction; tathā kuryāt — should perform accordingly; daivam pitryam — divine and ancestral rites; dvijanmanām — for the twice-born.

Śrīdhara

dvijanmanāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ yathāvṛtti yathopacāraṃ daivaṃ devasambandhikarma pitryaṃ pitṛsambandhikarma kuryāt । abhyudayaśrāddhoktena viśeṣeṇa pradakṣiṇopacārādinā daivapitryaśrāddhaṃ kuryāt ityarthaḥ — The divine and ancestral rites should be performed by feeding Brāhmaṇas seated in pairs facing east, according to one’s means and proper observance, until they are satisfied.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yugmāṃstu prāḍmukhānviprānbhojayenmanubheśvara । yathā tṛptistathā kuryāddaivaṃ pitryaṃ dvijanmanām ॥५॥

Śrīdhara

dvijanmanāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ yathāvṛtti yathopacāraṃ daivaṃ devasambandhikarma pitryaṃ pitṛsambandhikarma kuryāt । abhyudayaśrāddhoktena viśeṣeṇa pradakṣiṇopacārādinā daivapitryaśrāddhaṃ kuryāt ityarthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

द्विजन्मनां ब्राह्मणानां यथावृत्ति यथोपचारं दैवं देवसम्बन्धिकर्म पित्र्यं पितृसम्बन्धिकर्म कुर्यात् । अभ्युदयश्राद्धोक्तेन विशेषेण प्रदक्षिणोपचारादिना दैवपित्र्यश्राद्धं कुर्यात् इत्यर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 5).

Śrīdhara explains that the divine and ancestral rites are performed for the twice-born according to one’s means and proper observance; the special procedures of an auspicious śrāddha, such as the prescribed circumambulation, are included.
