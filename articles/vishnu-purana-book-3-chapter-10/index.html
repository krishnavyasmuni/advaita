VP 3.10.1

कथितं चातुराश्रम्यं चातुर्वर्ण्यक्रियास्तथा । पुंसः क्रियामहं श्रोतुमिच्छामि द्विजसत्तम
॥१॥

Sagara: You have explained the four āśramas and the practices of the four varṇas. O best of the twice-born, I want to hear about the rites prescribed for a person.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kathitam — has been explained; cātuḥ-āśramyam — the four āśramas; cātuḥ-varṇya-kriyāḥ — the practices of the four varṇas; tathā — likewise; puṃsaḥ kriyām — the rites of a person; śrotum icchāmi — I wish to hear; dvija-sattama — O best of the twice-born.

Śrīdhara

puṃsaḥ ṣoḍaśa-saṃskāraiḥ varṇāśrama-niveśanam — placement of a man in varṇa and āśrama through sixteen rites; ataḥ tān āha daśame bāhulyena dvijātiṣu — therefore he states them in the tenth, mostly for the twice-born; catvāra āśramāḥ eva cātur-āśramyam — the four āśramas are the fourfold āśrama system; tad-dharmaḥ kathitaḥ — its duties are taught; evaṃ cātur-varṇya-kriyā api — likewise the acts of the four varṇas; puṃsaḥ kriyāṃ ṣoḍaśa-saṃskāra-rūpām — and the man's rites in the form of sixteen saṃskāras

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kathitaṃ cāturāśramyaṃ cāturvarṇyakriyāstathā । puṃsaḥ kriyāmahaṃ śrotumicchāmi dvijasattama ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

puṃsaḥ ṣoḍaśasaṃskārairvarṇāśramaniveśanam । atastānāha daśame bāhulyena dvijātiṣu । catvāra āśramā eva cāturāśramyaṃ taddharmaḥ kathitaḥ, evañcāturvarṇyakriyā api puṃsaḥ kriyāṃ ṣoḍaśasaṃskārarūpām

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पुंसः षोडशसंस्कारैर्वर्णाश्रमनिवेशनम् । अतस्तानाह दशमे बाहुल्येन द्विजातिषु । चत्वार आश्रमा एव चातुराश्रम्यं तद्धर्मः कथितः, एवञ्चातुर्वर्ण्यक्रिया अपि पुंसः क्रियां षोडशसंस्काररूपाम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1).

Śrīdhara explains that this chapter describes the duties of persons, especially the sixteen saṃskāras by which one is placed in varṇa and āśrama. The four āśramas and their duties have already been taught, and the varṇa rites are also forms of the sixteen saṃskāras.

VP 3.10.2

नित्यनैमित्तिकाः काम्याः क्रियाः पुंसामशेषतः । समाख्याहि भृगुश्रेष्ठ सर्वज्ञो ह्यसि मे मतः
॥२॥

Tell me fully about the daily, occasional, and desire-motivated rites of human beings; I regard you as omniscient, O best of Bhṛgu’s line.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nitya-naimittikāḥ — daily and occasional; kāmyāḥ kriyāḥ — desire-motivated rites; puṃsām — of people; aśeṣataḥ — completely; samākhyāhi — explain; bhṛgu-śreṣṭha — O best of Bhṛgu’s line; sarva-jñaḥ — omniscient; asi — you are; me mataḥ — in my view.

Śrīdhara

nityāmaharahaḥ kriyamāṇāṃ pratyavāyaparihāraphalām, naimittikīṃ rāhudarśādau nimitte sati vihitānāmakaraṇāt pratyavāyapradāṃ kāmyāṃ kāmāya hitām agniṣṭomādikām — Daily rites are performed every day and remove the fault of omission; occasional rites are prescribed when their particular occasion arises and omission brings fault; desired rites are performed for a desired result, such as the Agniṣṭoma.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nityanaimittikāḥ kāmyāḥ kriyāḥ puṃsāmaśeṣataḥ । samākhyāhi bhṛguśreṣṭha sarvajño hyasi me mataḥ ॥२॥

Śrīdhara

nityāmaharahaḥ kriyamāṇāṃ pratyavāyaparihāraphalām, naimittikīṃ rāhudarśādau nimitte sati vihitānāmakaraṇāt pratyavāyapradāṃ kāmyāṃ kāmāya hitām agniṣṭomādikām

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नित्यामहरहः क्रियमाणां प्रत्यवायपरिहारफलाम्, नैमित्तिकीं राहुदर्शादौ निमित्ते सति विहितानामकरणात् प्रत्यवायप्रदां काम्यां कामाय हिताम् अग्निष्टोमादिकाम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 2).

Śrīdhara defines daily rites as those performed every day to avoid the fault of omission, occasional rites as those prescribed when a particular occasion arises, and desired rites as those performed for a desired result, such as the Agniṣṭoma.

VP 3.10.3

यदेतदुक्तं भवता नित्यनैमित्तिकाश्रयम् । तदहं कथयिष्यांमि शृणुष्वैकमना मम
॥३॥

Śrīdhara

nityanaimitikāśritam iti kāmyasyāpyupalakṣaṇam — The expression “based on daily and occasional rites” also includes desired rites.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yadetaduktaṃ bhavatā nityanaimittikāśrayam । tadahaṃ kathayiṣyāṃmi śṛṇuṣvaikamanā mama ॥३॥

Śrīdhara

nityanaimitikāśritam iti kāmyasyāpyupalakṣaṇam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नित्यनैमितिकाश्रितम् इति काम्यस्याप्युपलक्षणम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 3).

Śrīdhara explains that the expression based on daily and occasional rites also includes desired rites.

VP 3.10.4

जातस्य जातकर्मादिक्रियाकाण्डमशेषतः । पुत्रस्य कुर्वीत पिता श्राद्धं चाभ्युदयात्मकम्
॥४॥

For a newborn son, the father should perform the entire series of rites beginning with the birth ceremony, as well as the śrāddha performed for prosperity.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jātasya — of one born; jāta-karma-ādi — beginning with the birth rite; kriyā-kāṇḍam — series of rites; aśeṣataḥ — completely; putrasya — of the son; kurvīta — should perform; pitā — the father; śrāddham — śrāddha; abhyudaya-ātmakam — intended for prosperity.

Śrīdhara

jātasya putrasya jātakarma ca ādikriyā-kāṇḍam — the Jātakarma and first ritual section for a born son; janmanaḥ prācīnam garbhādhāna-puṃsavana-ādi-kriyā-samūham — the group of rites beginning with conception and Puṃsavana that precede birth; pitā kurvīta iti arthaḥ — the meaning is: the father should perform them; tad uktam — this is said; garbhādhānam ṛtau puṃsaḥ savanam spandanāt purā — conception in season, Puṃsavana before movement, and so on

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jātasya jātakarmādikriyākāṇḍamaśeṣataḥ । putrasya kurvīta pitā śrāddhaṃ cābhyudayātmakam ॥४॥

Śrīdhara

jātasya putrasya jātakarma ca ādikriyākāṇḍañca janmanaḥ prācīnaṃ garbhādhānapuṃsavanādikriyāsamūhaṃ pitā kurvīta ityarthaḥ । taduktaṃ garbhādhānamṛtau puṃsaḥ savanaṃ spandanāt purā’ iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जातस्य पुत्रस्य जातकर्म च आदिक्रियाकाण्डञ्च जन्मनः प्राचीनं गर्भाधानपुंसवनादिक्रियासमूहं पिता कुर्वीत इत्यर्थः । तदुक्तं गर्भाधानमृतौ पुंसः सवनं स्पन्दनात् पुराऽ इति

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 4).

Śrīdhara explains that the father should perform jāta-karma for the newborn son and the preceding rites beginning with garbhādhāna and puṃsavana.

VP 3.10.5

युग्मांस्तु प्राड्मुखान्विप्रान्भोजयेन्मनुभेश्वर । यथा तृप्तिस्तथा कुर्याद्दैवं पित्र्यं द्विजन्मनाम्
॥५॥

He should feed twice-born men facing east in even numbers, O lord of men, and should perform the divine and ancestral rites so that they are satisfied.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yugmān — even-numbered; prāṅ-mukhān — facing east; viprān — Brāhmaṇas; bhojayet — should feed; manuja-īśvara — O lord of men; yathā tṛptiḥ — as satisfaction; tathā kuryāt — should perform accordingly; daivam pitryam — divine and ancestral rites; dvijanmanām — for the twice-born.

Śrīdhara

dvijanmanāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ yathāvṛtti yathopacāraṃ daivaṃ devasambandhikarma pitryaṃ pitṛsambandhikarma kuryāt । abhyudayaśrāddhoktena viśeṣeṇa pradakṣiṇopacārādinā daivapitryaśrāddhaṃ kuryāt ityarthaḥ — The divine and ancestral rites should be performed by feeding Brāhmaṇas seated in pairs facing east, according to one’s means and proper observance, until they are satisfied.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yugmāṃstu prāḍmukhānviprānbhojayenmanubheśvara । yathā tṛptistathā kuryāddaivaṃ pitryaṃ dvijanmanām ॥५॥

Śrīdhara

dvijanmanāṃ brāhmaṇānāṃ yathāvṛtti yathopacāraṃ daivaṃ devasambandhikarma pitryaṃ pitṛsambandhikarma kuryāt । abhyudayaśrāddhoktena viśeṣeṇa pradakṣiṇopacārādinā daivapitryaśrāddhaṃ kuryāt ityarthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

द्विजन्मनां ब्राह्मणानां यथावृत्ति यथोपचारं दैवं देवसम्बन्धिकर्म पित्र्यं पितृसम्बन्धिकर्म कुर्यात् । अभ्युदयश्राद्धोक्तेन विशेषेण प्रदक्षिणोपचारादिना दैवपित्र्यश्राद्धं कुर्यात् इत्यर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 5).

Śrīdhara explains that the divine and ancestral rites are performed for the twice-born according to one’s means and proper observance; the special procedures of an auspicious śrāddha, such as the prescribed circumambulation, are included.

VP 3.10.6

दध्ना युक्तैः सबदरैर्मिश्रान् पिडान्मुदा युतः । नान्दीमुखेभ्यस्तीर्थेन दद्याद्दैवेन पार्थिव
॥६॥

With joy, he should offer to the Nandīmukhas balls mixed with curds, barley, and jujubes, using the prescribed sacred rite, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dadhnā — with curds; yavaiḥ — with barley; sabadaraiḥ — with jujubes; miśrān piṇḍān — mixed balls; mudā-yutaḥ — filled with joy; nāndī-mukhebhyaḥ — to the Nandīmukhas; tīrthena — with the sacred rite; dadyāt — should give; daivena — by the divine/right-hand rite; pārthiva — O king.

Śrīdhara

tadevāha—dadhna iti । daivena karāgreṇa — He further explains the rite: the piṇḍas are mixed with curd and jujube and joyfully offered to the Nandimukha ancestors with the prescribed sacred hand.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dadhnā yuktaiḥ sabadarairmiśrān piḍānmudā yutaḥ । nāndīmukhebhyastīrthena dadyāddaivena pārthiva ॥६॥

Śrīdhara

tadevāha—dadhna iti । daivena karāgreṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तदेवाह—दध्न इति । दैवेन कराग्रेण

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 6).

Śrīdhara further explains that the piṇḍas are mixed with curd and jujube and offered to the Nandimukhas with the prescribed sacred hand.

VP 3.10.7

प्राजापत्येन वा सर्वमुपचारं प्रदक्षिणम् । कुर्वीत तत्तथाशेषवृद्धिकालेषु भूपते
॥७॥

Then, at ceremonies of increase, O king, he should perform every offering and service in the proper order, using the Prajāpati rite.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prājāpatyena — with the Prajāpati rite; vā — or; sarvam upacāram — every service; pradakṣiṇam — in the proper order; kurvīta — should perform; tat tathā — so; aśeṣa-vṛddhi-kāleṣu — at all ceremonies of increase; bhūpate — O king.

Śrīdhara

prājāpatyena kaniṣṭhāmūlena । taduktam— kaniṣṭhādeśinyaṅguṣṭhamūlānyagraṃ karasya ca । prajāpatipitṛ-brahma-devatīrthānyanukramāt । iti । aśeṣavṛddhikāleṣu kanyā putravivāheṣvityādinā vakṣyamāṇeṣu — Śrīdhara identifies prājāpatya with the root of the little finger, cites the traditional order of the sacred places on the hand, and says that this rule applies on occasions of growth and at marriages, among other occasions described below.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prājāpatyena vā sarvamupacāraṃ pradakṣiṇam । kurvīta tattathāśeṣavṛddhikāleṣu bhūpate ॥७॥

Śrīdhara

prājāpatyena kaniṣṭhāmūlena । taduktam— kaniṣṭhādeśinyaṅguṣṭhamūlānyagraṃ karasya ca । prajāpatipitṛ-brahma-devatīrthānyanukramāt । iti । aśeṣavṛddhikāleṣu kanyā putravivāheṣvityādinā vakṣyamāṇeṣu

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्राजापत्येन कनिष्ठामूलेन । तदुक्तम्— कनिष्ठादेशिन्यङ्गुष्ठमूलान्यग्रं करस्य च । प्रजापतिपितृ-ब्रह्म-देवतीर्थान्यनुक्रमात् । इति । अशेषवृद्धिकालेषु कन्या पुत्रविवाहेष्वित्यादिना वक्ष्यमाणेषु

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 7).

Śrīdhara identifies prājāpatya with the root of the little finger, cites the traditional order of the sacred places on the hand, and says that this rule applies on occasions of growth and at marriages, among other occasions described below.

VP 3.10.8

ततश्च नाम कुर्वीत पितैव दशमेहनि । देवपूर्वं नराख्यं हि शर्मवर्मादिसंयुतम्
॥८॥

On the tenth day the father should give the child a name, beginning with a divine name and ending in a human one, joined with Śarman, Varman, and the like.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataḥ — then; nāma kurvīta — should give a name; pitā eva — the father himself; daśame ahani — on the tenth day; deva-pūrvam — beginning with a divine name; nara-ākhyam — a human name; śarma-varma-ādi-saṃyutam — joined with Śarman, Varman, and the like.

Śrīdhara

daśame ahani — on the tenth day; aśauca-anta-upalakṣaṇam — indicating the end of ritual impurity; nāmadheyaṃ daśamyām — the name on the tenth day; devapūrvam — with the family deity’s name first; narākhyam — a name denoting a person; śarma-varma-ādi-saṃyutam — ending in śarman, varman, and the like

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataśca nāma kurvīta pitaiva daśamehani । devapūrvaṃ narākhyaṃ hi śarmavarmādisaṃyutam ॥८॥

Śrīdhara

daśame’hani atīta iti śeṣaḥ । taccāśaucāntopalakṣaṇam । atraiva kālāntaramapyāha yathā— nāmadheyaṃ daśamyāñca kecidicchanti pārthiva । dvādaśyāmathavā rājyāṃ māse pūrṇe tathāpare । iti । devapūrvaṃ kuladevatānāmapūrvakaṃ kuladevatāsambaddhaṃ nāma kuryād iti śaṅkhokteḥ । narākhyaṃ puruṣavācakam । tatra prānte śarmavarmādisaṃyutaṃ yathā somaśarmā indravarmā, candravarmā, candraguptaḥ śivadāsaḥ ityādi

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

दशमेऽहनि अतीत इति शेषः । तच्चाशौचान्तोपलक्षणम् । अत्रैव कालान्तरमप्याह यथा— नामधेयं दशम्याञ्च केचिदिच्छन्ति पार्थिव । द्वादश्यामथवा राज्यां मासे पूर्णे तथापरे । इति । देवपूर्वं कुलदेवतानामपूर्वकं कुलदेवतासम्बद्धं नाम कुर्याद् इति शङ्खोक्तेः । नराख्यं पुरुषवाचकम् । तत्र प्रान्ते शर्मवर्मादिसंयुतं यथा सोमशर्मा इन्द्रवर्मा, चन्द्रवर्मा, चन्द्रगुप्तः शिवदासः इत्यादि

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 8).

Śrīdhara explains that after the impurity has ended, the name may be given on the tenth or twelfth day, or at the end of the month according to different authorities. It should be formed with the family deity’s name first and a masculine ending such as śarman or varman.

VP 3.10.9

शर्मेति ब्राह्यणस्योक्तं वर्मेति क्षत्रसंश्रयम् । गुप्तदासात्मकं नाम प्रशस्तं वैश्यशुद्रयोः
॥९॥

Śarman is prescribed for a Brāhmaṇa, Varman for a Kṣatriya, and a name ending in Gupta or Dāsa is auspicious for a Vaiśya or Śūdra.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śarma — Śarman; brāhmaṇasya uktam — prescribed for a Brāhmaṇa; varma — Varman; kṣatra-saṃśrayam — associated with the Kṣatriya; gupta-dāsa-ātmakam nāma — a name ending in Gupta or Dāsa; praśastam — auspicious; vaiśya-śūdrayoḥ — for Vaiśya and Śūdra.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śarmeti brāhyaṇasyoktaṃ varmeti kṣatrasaṃśrayam । guptadāsātmakaṃ nāma praśastaṃ vaiśyaśudrayoḥ ॥९॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.10.10

नार्थहीनं न चाशस्तं नापशब्दयुतं तथा । नामङ्गल्यं जुगुप्साकं नाम कुर्यात्समाक्षरम्
॥१०॥

He should give a name that is not meaningless, inauspicious, vulgar, harsh-sounding, or suggestive of misfortune, and that has an even number of syllables.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 3.10.24

VP 3.10.25
