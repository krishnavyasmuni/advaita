VP 3.10.1

सगर उवाच— कथितं चातुराश्रम्यं चातुर्वर्ण्य-क्रियास् तथा।
पुंसः क्रियाम् अहं श्रोतुम् इच्छामि द्विज-सत्तम

Sagara: You have explained the four āśramas and the practices of the four varṇas. O best of the twice-born, I want to hear about the rites prescribed for a person.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
kathitam — has been explained;
cātuḥ-āśramyam — the four āśramas;
cātuḥ-varṇya-kriyāḥ — the practices of the four varṇas;
tathā — likewise;
puṃsaḥ kriyām — the rites of a person;
śrotum icchāmi — I wish to hear;
dvija-sattama — O best of the twice-born.
Śrīdhara
puṃsaḥ ṣoḍaśa-saṃskāraiḥ varṇāśrama-niveśanam — placement of a man in varṇa and āśrama through sixteen rites;
ataḥ tān āha daśame bāhulyena dvijātiṣu — therefore he states them in the tenth, mostly for the twice-born;
catvāra āśramāḥ eva cātur-āśramyam — the four āśramas are the fourfold āśrama system;
tad-dharmaḥ kathitaḥ — its duties are taught;
evaṃ cātur-varṇya-kriyā api — likewise the acts of the four varṇas;
puṃsaḥ kriyāṃ ṣoḍaśa-saṃskāra-rūpām — and the man's rites in the form of sixteen saṃskāras
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kathitaṃ cāturāśramyaṃ cāturvarṇyakriyāstathā । puṃsaḥ kriyāmahaṃ śrotumicchāmi dvijasattama ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

puṃsaḥ ṣoḍaśasaṃskārairvarṇāśramaniveśanam । atastānāha daśame bāhulyena dvijātiṣu । catvāra āśramā eva cāturāśramyaṃ taddharmaḥ kathitaḥ, evañcāturvarṇyakriyā api puṃsaḥ kriyāṃ ṣoḍaśasaṃskārarūpām

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पुंसः षोडशसंस्कारैर्वर्णाश्रमनिवेशनम् । अतस्तानाह दशमे बाहुल्येन द्विजातिषु । चत्वार आश्रमा एव चातुराश्रम्यं तद्धर्मः कथितः, एवञ्चातुर्वर्ण्यक्रिया अपि पुंसः क्रियां षोडशसंस्काररूपाम्

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sagara: You have explained the four āśramas and the practices of the four varṇas. O best of the twice-born, I want to hear about the rites prescribed for a person.

VP 3.10.2

नित्य-नैमित्तिकाः काम्याः क्रियाः पुंसाम् अशेषतः।

Tell me fully about the daily, occasional, and desire-motivated rites of human beings; I regard you as omniscient, O best of Bhṛgu’s line.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarva-jñaḥ — omniscient;

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara commentary.

Tell me fully about the daily, occasional, and desire-motivated rites of human beings; I regard you as omniscient, O best of Bhṛgu’s line.
