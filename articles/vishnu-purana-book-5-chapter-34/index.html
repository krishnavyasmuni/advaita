VP 5.34.1

चक्रे कर्म महच्छौरिर्बिभ्राणो मानुषीं तनुम्

मैत्रेयः - चक्रे कमि महच्छौररः वर्वर्भ्ाणो मानुर्षीां तनुम् | स्जगाय शक्रां शर्वं च सर्वािन् देर्वान् च िीिया ॥ १ ॥ Maitreya - Krishna, having taken a human form itself, has performed some great acts. He won over Shankara, Kaartikeya, and all the gods, without any effort. This is the most wonderful thing that He has done, taking on a human form itself.
