VP 5.34.1

श्रीमैत्रेय उवाच
चक्रे कर्म महच्छौरिर्बिभ्राणो मानुषीं तनुम् ।
जिगाय शक्रं शर्वं च सर्वान्देवांश्च लीलया

Maitreya said: Śauri, assuming a human body, performed great deeds; he conquered Indra, Śarva, and all the gods with ease.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
cakre — performed;
karma mahat — great deeds;
śauriḥ — Śauri;
bibh-rāṇaḥ mānuṣīm tanum — assuming a human body;
jigāya — conquered;
śakram śarvam ca — Indra and Śarva;
sarvān devān — all the gods;
līlayā — playfully / with ease;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

cakre karma mahacchaurirbibhrāṇo mānuṣīṃ tanum | jigāya śakraṃ śarvaṃ ca sarvāndevāṃśca līlayā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.34.2

यच्चान्यदकरोत्कर्म दिव्यचेष्टाविघातकृत् ।
तत्कथ्यतां महाभाग परं कौतूहलं हि मे

What other deed did he perform that obstructed the divine activities? O greatly blessed one, tell me; I am exceedingly eager to hear it.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
yat ca anyat — what other;
akarot karma — deed he performed;
divya-ceṣṭā-vighāta-kṛt — obstructing divine activities;
tat kathyatām — let that be told;
mahā-bhāga — O greatly blessed one;
param kautūhalam hi me — I am very eager;
Śrīdhara
divyānām — of the divine ones;
divi bhavānām devānām — of the gods dwelling in heaven;
ceṣṭā — prowess;
tad-vighātakam — obstructing that;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yaccānyadakarotkarma divyaceṣṭāvighātakṛt | tatkathyatāṃ mahābhāga paraṃ kautūhalaṃ hi me

Śrīdhara

yac cānyad iti | divyānāṃ divi bhavānāṃ devānāṃ ceṣṭā parākramaḥ, tad-vighātakam ॥१ ॥

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यच् चान्यद् इति । दिव्यानां दिवि भवानां देवानां चेष्टा पराक्रमः, तद्-विघातकम् ॥१ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Divine activities” means the prowess of the gods who dwell in heaven; this deed obstructs that prowess.

VP 5.34.4

Pauṇḍraka Vāsudeva became [a claimant to being] Vāsudeva on earth, called by people deluded by ignorance, “You have incarnated.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

pauṇḍraka iti | pauṇḍrakaḥ pauṇḍra-deśajaḥ | ajñāna-mohitaiḥ prākṛtair janair vāsudevo’vatīrṇas tvam ity uktaḥ ॥४ ॥

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पौण्ड्रक इति । पौण्ड्रकः पौण्ड्र-देशजः । अज्ञान-मोहितैः प्राकृतैर् जनैर् वासुदेवोऽवतीर्णस् त्वम् इत्य् उक्तः ॥४ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Pauṇḍraka means one born in the Pauṇḍra country. Ordinary people deluded by ignorance told him, “You are Vāsudeva incarnate.”

VP 5.34.5

Transliteration

He came to believe literally, “I alone am Vāsudeva.”

VP 5.34.8

Śrīdhara commentary.

By saying “I shall cast the discus at you,” Kṛṣṇa had Pauṇḍraka mentally in view: “I shall throw the discus and destroy him.”

VP 5.34.9

Śrīdhara commentary.

The next four verses explain this: the messenger is to tell Pauṇḍraka that Kṛṣṇa has understood the real meaning of his words.

“Having come to your city, I shall act so that I will have no further fear from you”—that is, “I shall kill you.”

ययौ शरण्यं जगतां शरणं मधुसूदनम्

Seeing her, the people, their eyes rolling from fear, went to Madhusūdana, the refuge of the worlds and the giver of refuge, O sage.

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

tām iti | trāsena vicalanti locanāni yasya sa tathoktaḥ ॥३४ ॥

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ताम् इति । त्रासेन विचलन्ति लोचनानि यस्य स तथोक्तः ॥३४ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

He is called one whose eyes roll because they tremble from fear.
