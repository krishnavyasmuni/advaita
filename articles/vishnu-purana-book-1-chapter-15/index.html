VP 1.15.1

तपश् चरत्सु पृथिवीं प्रचेतःसु महीरुहाः ।
अरक्ष्यमाणाम् आबब्रुर् बभूवाथ प्रजाक्षयः ॥१॥

SWhen they were deeply immersed in penance like this, the entire world was filled by trees, growing by great proportions. Mankind declined, and nobody could survive because the plants completely filled the world.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tapaś caratsu pṛthivīṃ pracetaḥsu mahīruhāḥ | arakṣyamāṇām ābabrur babhūvātha prajākṣayaḥ || 1 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.15.2

नाशकन् मारुतो वातु वृतं खम् अभवद् द्रुमैः ।
दशवर्षसहस्राणि न शेकुश् चेष्टितं प्रजाः ॥२॥

TThe trees went up to the sky, and air was not able to blow at all. People were not able to move around or do any functioning, for ten thousand years.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nāśakan māruto vātu vṛtaṃ kham abhavad drumaiḥ | daśavarṣasahasrāṇi na śekuś ceṣṭitaṃ prajāḥ || 2 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.15.3

तद् दृष्ट्वा जलनिष्क्रान्ताः सर्वे क्रुद्धाः प्रचेतसः ।
मुखेभ्यो वायुम् अग्निं च तेऽसृजन् जातमन्यवः ॥३॥

SSeeing such a sorry state in the world, the Prachetasaas got very angry, and becoming very angry, they blew air and fire through their mouth.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

pracetaḥsu tapaś caratsu tatpitari ca nādāt tattvajñānaṃ prāpya rājyaṃ tyaktvā vanaṃ gate sati iti draṣṭavyam| tad uktam— prācīnabarhiṣaṃ kṣattakarmasv āsaktacetasam| nārado’dhyātmatattvajñaḥ kṛpāluḥ pratyabodhayat || [bhā.pu. 4.25.3] ityādinā purañjanākhyānena| ato’rakṣyamāṇāṃ pṛthivīṃ karṣaṇādyabhāvād mahīruhā āvṛtavantaḥ — contextual sense: When the Pracetases were engaged in penance, their father had obtained knowledge of the truth, abandoned the kingdom, and gone to the forest; this should be understood. As the Bhāgavata says, Nārada compassionately instructed Prācīnabarhis, whose mind was attached to ritual works, through the story of Purañjana. Thus, because the earth was unprotected and there was no cultivation, the trees covered it.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tad dṛṣṭvā jalaniṣkrāntāḥ sarve kruddhāḥ pracetasaḥ | mukhebhyo vāyum agniṃ ca te’sṛjan jātamanyavaḥ || 3 ||

Śrīdhara

pracetaḥsu tapaś caratsu tatpitari ca nādāt tattvajñānaṃ prāpya rājyaṃ tyaktvā vanaṃ gate sati iti draṣṭavyam| tad uktam— prācīnabarhiṣaṃ kṣattakarmasv āsaktacetasam| nārado’dhyātmatattvajñaḥ kṛpāluḥ pratyabodhayat || [bhā.pu. 4.25.3] ityādinā purañjanākhyānena| ato’rakṣyamāṇāṃ pṛthivīṃ karṣaṇādyabhāvād mahīruhā āvṛtavantaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रचेतःसु तपश् चरत्सु तत्पितरि च नादात् तत्त्वज्ञानं प्राप्य राज्यं त्यक्त्वा वनं गते सति इति द्रष्टव्यम्। तद् उक्तम्— प्राचीनबर्हिषं क्षत्तकर्मस्व् आसक्तचेतसम्। नारदोऽध्यात्मतत्त्वज्ञः कृपालुः प्रत्यबोधयत् ॥ [भा.पु. ४.२५.३] इत्य्आदिना पुरञ्जनाख्यानेन। अतोऽरक्ष्यमाणां पृथिवीं कर्षणाद्य्अभावाद् महीरुहा आवृतवन्तः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

When the Pracetases were engaged in penance, their father had obtained knowledge of the truth, abandoned the kingdom, and gone to the forest; this should be understood. As the Bhāgavata says, Nārada compassionately instructed Prācīnabarhis, whose mind was attached to ritual works, through the story of Purañjana. Thus, because the earth was unprotected and there was no cultivation, the trees covered it.

VP 1.15.4

उन्मूलान् अथ तान् वृक्षान् कृत्वा वायुर् अशोषयत् ।
तान् अग्निर् अदहद् घोरस् तत्राभूद् द्रुमसङ्क्षयः ॥४॥

TThe air that they blew was so powerful that it uprooted all the trees, and the air dried everything. The fire which they blew burnt everything. Then, there was a severe lack of trees.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

vāyor agneḥ sṛṣṭeḥ prayojanam āha—unmūlān iti — contextual sense: He explains the purpose of the creation of air and fire: “they uprooted.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

unmūlān atha tān vṛkṣān kṛtvā vāyur aśoṣayat | tān agnir adahad ghoras tatrābhūd drumasaṅkṣayaḥ || 4 ||

Śrīdhara

vāyor agneḥ sṛṣṭeḥ prayojanam āha—unmūlān iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वायोर् अग्नेः सृष्टेः प्रयोजनम् आह—उन्मूलान् इति

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He explains the purpose of the creation of air and fire: “they uprooted.”

VP 1.15.5

द्रुमक्षयम् अथो दृष्ट्वा किञ्चिच् छिष्टेषु शाखिषु ।
उपागम्याब्रवीद् एतान् राजा सोमः प्रजापतीन् ॥५॥

King Soma, having seen the destruction of the trees and that only a few branches remained, approached the Prajāpatis and spoke.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

drumakṣayam — the destruction of the trees; atho dṛṣṭvā — then, having seen; kiñcit śiṣṭeṣu śākhiṣu — when some branches remained; upāgamya abravīt — having approached, he said; etān prajāpatīn — to these Prajāpatis; rājā somaḥ — King Soma

Śrīdhara

somo vṛkṣāṇāṃ rājā| prajāpatīn ity anena "kandamūlaphalādibhiḥ prajāvṛddhikāriṇāṃ vṛkṣāṇāṃ dāhaḥ prajāpālakānāṃ yuṣmākam anucitaḥ" iti sāmopāyaḥ .sūcitaḥ — contextual sense: Soma is the king of trees. By saying “Prajāpatis” he indicates a conciliatory approach: it is improper for you, protectors of creatures, to burn trees that nourish creatures with roots, fruits and fruits.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

drumakṣayam atho dṛṣṭvā kiñcic chiṣṭeṣu śākhiṣu | upāgamyābravīd etān rājā somaḥ prajāpatīn || 5 ||

Śrīdhara

somo vṛkṣāṇāṃ rājā| prajāpatīn ity anena "kandamūlaphalādibhiḥ prajāvṛddhikāriṇāṃ vṛkṣāṇāṃ dāhaḥ prajāpālakānāṃ yuṣmākam anucitaḥ" iti sāmopāyaḥ .sūcitaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सोमो वृक्षाणां राजा। प्रजापतीन् इत्य् अनेन "कन्दमूलफलादिभिः प्रजावृद्धिकारिणां वृक्षाणां दाहः प्रजापालकानां युष्माकम् अनुचितः" इति सामोपायः .सूचितः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Soma is the king of trees. By saying “Prajāpatis” he indicates a conciliatory approach: it is improper for you, protectors of creatures, to burn trees that nourish creatures with roots, fruits and fruits.

VP 1.15.6

कोपं यच्छत राजानः शृणुध्वं च वचो मम ।
सन्धानं वः करिष्यामि सह क्षितिरुहैर् अहम् ॥६॥

OO great kings, please do control your anger and listen to me. I will make sure that trees and plants are not going to cause more trouble to you. I will make sure that they live in peace with you.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

kopaṃ yacchata niyamayata — contextual sense: “Control your anger” means restrain it.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kopaṃ yacchata rājānaḥ śṛṇudhvaṃ ca vaco mama | sandhānaṃ vaḥ kariṣyāmi saha kṣitiruhair aham || 6 ||

Śrīdhara

kopaṃ yacchata niyamayata

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कोपं यच्छत नियमयत

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Control your anger” means restrain it.

VP 1.15.7

रत्नभूता च कन्येयं वाक्षेयी वरवर्णिनी ।
भविष्यं जानता पूर्वं मया गोभिर् विवर्धिता ॥७॥

II also have this wonderful woman, gem of a woman, one who is born of the trees. I am protecting this gem of a woman, excellent among women. Through my grace, I have nurtured her, knowing the future what is going to happen.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

tapaḥpradānena sandhānaṃ karoti--ratnabhūtā iti| vārkṣeyī vṛkṣajā| bhaviṣyata "iyaṃ yuṣmākaṃ patnī bhaviṣyati, asyāṃ dakṣo janiṣyate, tadvaṃśena ca trilokī pūrayiṣyate" ityādir ajānatā mayā gobhiḥ sudhāmayai raśmibhiḥ saṃvardhitā — contextual sense: He reconciles them by offering penance: “ratna-bhūtā” means precious; “Vārkṣeyī” means born from trees. “She will be your wife; Dakṣa will be born from her, and the three worlds will be filled by his lineage”—not knowing this, I nourished her with my nectar-filled rays.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ratnabhūtā ca kanyeyaṃ vākṣeyī varavarṇinī | bhaviṣyaṃ jānatā pūrvaṃ mayā gobhir vivardhitā || 7 ||

Śrīdhara

tapaḥpradānena sandhānaṃ karoti--ratnabhūtā iti| vārkṣeyī vṛkṣajā| bhaviṣyata "iyaṃ yuṣmākaṃ patnī bhaviṣyati, asyāṃ dakṣo janiṣyate, tadvaṃśena ca trilokī pūrayiṣyate" ityādir ajānatā mayā gobhiḥ sudhāmayai raśmibhiḥ saṃvardhitā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तपःप्रदानेन सन्धानं करोति--रत्नभूता इति। वार्क्षेयी वृक्षजा। भविष्यत "इयं युष्माकं पत्नी भविष्यति, अस्यां दक्षो जनिष्यते, तद्वंशेन च त्रिलोकी पूरयिष्यते" इत्य्आदिर् अजानता मया गोभिः सुधामयै रश्मिभिः संवर्धिता

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He reconciles them by offering penance: “ratna-bhūtā” means precious; “Vārkṣeyī” means born from trees. “She will be your wife; Dakṣa will be born from her, and the three worlds will be filled by his lineage”—not knowing this, I nourished her with my nectar-filled rays.

VP 1.15.8

मारिषा नाम नाम्नैषा वृक्षाणाम् इति निर्मिता ।
भार्या वोऽस्तु महाभागा ध्रुवं वंशविवर्धिनी ॥८॥

SShe is the daughter of the trees, vrukshaas, and is called as Maarishaa. She will become your wife and will continue the progeny, and help you in expanding the mankind.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

nāmnā māriṣā vṛkṣāṇām eṣā kanyā iti nāma| evaṃ hi prasiddha nirmitā niṣpannā ity anvayaḥ — contextual sense: Her name is Māriṣā; the name means “this girl of the trees.” Thus “created” means produced, as is well known.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māriṣā nāma nāmnaiṣā vṛkṣāṇām iti nirmitā | bhāryā vo’stu mahābhāgā dhruvaṃ vaṃśavivardhinī || 8 ||

Śrīdhara

nāmnā māriṣā vṛkṣāṇām eṣā kanyā iti nāma| evaṃ hi prasiddha nirmitā niṣpannā ity anvayaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नाम्ना मारिषा वृक्षाणाम् एषा कन्या इति नाम। एवं हि प्रसिद्ध निर्मिता निष्पन्ना इत्य् अन्वयः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Her name is Māriṣā; the name means “this girl of the trees.” Thus “created” means produced, as is well known.

VP 1.15.9

युष्माकं तेजसोऽर्धेन मम चार्धेन तेजसः ।
अस्याम् उत्पत्स्यते विद्वान् दक्षो नाम प्रजापतिः ॥९॥

WWith half of your tejas, and half of my tejas, in her, one Prajaapati is going to be born, by name Dakṣa.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

yuṣmākaṃ tapomayasya tīvrasya tejaso’rdhena śītair madīyair gobhiḥ saṃvṛddhatvād asyā mama ca tejaso’rdhena saṃyukto’syām utpatsyate — contextual sense: With half of your intense, ascetic radiance and half of my radiance, joined in her—she having grown by my cool rays—Dakṣa will be born.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yuṣmākaṃ tejaso’rdhena mama cārdhena tejasaḥ | asyām utpatsyate vidvān dakṣo nāma prajāpatiḥ || 9 ||

Śrīdhara

yuṣmākaṃ tapomayasya tīvrasya tejaso’rdhena śītair madīyair gobhiḥ saṃvṛddhatvād asyā mama ca tejaso’rdhena saṃyukto’syām utpatsyate

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

युष्माकं तपोमयस्य तीव्रस्य तेजसोऽर्धेन शीतैर् मदीयैर् गोभिः संवृद्धत्वाद् अस्या मम च तेजसोऽर्धेन संयुक्तोऽस्याम् उत्पत्स्यते

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

With half of your intense, ascetic radiance and half of my radiance, joined in her—she having grown by my cool rays—Dakṣa will be born.

VP 1.15.10

मम चांशेन संयुक्तो युष्मत्तेजोमयेन वै ।
तेजसाग्रिसमो भूयः प्रजाः संवर्धयिष्यति ॥१०॥

DDakṣa having my amsha, and also your tejas, both, Maarishaa and Prachetasaas, and Maarishaa was nurtured by Soma also, Dakṣa will have all qualities like fire, veerya, shourya, and will also have all pleasing qualities, which are Soma's gunaas, and he will help in expanding the mankind.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

evaṃ ca saumyena mama aṃśena yuṣmattejomayena cāgninā saṃyukto’gniṣomātmakaḥ san agnisamaḥ apradhṛṣyo bhūtvā prajāḥ saṃvarddhayiṣyatīty arthaḥ — contextual sense: Thus, joined with my gentle lunar portion and the fire consisting of your radiance, he will be of the nature of fire and moon; becoming equal to fire and invincible, he will increase the creatures.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mama cāṃśena saṃyukto yuṣmattejomayena vai | tejasāgrisamo bhūyaḥ prajāḥ saṃvardhayiṣyati || 10 ||

Śrīdhara

evaṃ ca saumyena mama aṃśena yuṣmattejomayena cāgninā saṃyukto’gniṣomātmakaḥ san agnisamaḥ apradhṛṣyo bhūtvā prajāḥ saṃvarddhayiṣyatīty arthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवं च सौम्येन मम अंशेन युष्मत्तेजोमयेन चाग्निना संयुक्तोऽग्निषोमात्मकः सन् अग्निसमः अप्रधृष्यो भूत्वा प्रजाः संवर्द्धयिष्यतीत्य् अर्थः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Thus, joined with my gentle lunar portion and the fire consisting of your radiance, he will be of the nature of fire and moon; becoming equal to fire and invincible, he will increase the creatures.

VP 1.15.11

कण्डुर् नाम मुनिः पूर्वम् आसीद् वेदविदां वरः ।
सुरम्ये गोमतीतीरे स तेपे परमं तपः ॥११॥

TThere was a rishi called Kandu, well versed in the Vedaas, on the pleasant banks of Gomati river, he was performing tapas. Now, in order to tell the Prachetasaas that Maarishaa is born in a good vamsha, he began to tell about the story of Kandu Maharshi.

Word-for-word
