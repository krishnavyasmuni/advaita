VP 1.15.1

तपश् चरत्सु पृथिवीं प्रचेतःसु महीरुहाः ।
अरक्ष्यमाणाम् आबब्रुर् बभूवाथ प्रजाक्षयः ॥१॥

SWhen they were deeply immersed in penance like this, the entire world was filled by trees, growing by great proportions. Mankind declined, and nobody could survive because the plants completely filled the world.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — tapaś — lexical term; caratsu — lexical term; pṛthivīṃ — lexical term; pracetaḥsu — lexical term; mahīruhāḥ — lexical term; arakṣyamāṇām — inflected form; ābabrur — lexical term; babhūvātha — lexical term; prajākṣayaḥ — prajākṣayaḥ (name); sense — SWhen they were deeply immersed in penance like this, the entire world was filled by trees, growing by great proportions. Mankind declined, and nobody could survive because the plants completely filled the world.

Śrīdhara

Words — commentary — pracetaḥsu tapaś caratsu tatpitari ca nādāt tattvajñānaṃ prāpya rājyaṃ tyaktvā vanaṃ gate sati iti draṣṭavyam | tad uktam prācīnabarhiṣaṃ kṣattakarmasv āsaktacetasam | nārado’dhyātmatattvajñaḥ kṛpāluḥ pratyabodhayat || [bhā.pu. 4.25.3] ityādinā purañjanākhyānena | ato’rakṣyamāṇāṃ pṛthivīṃ karṣaṇādyabhāvād mahīruhā āvṛtavantaḥ || 13 ||; sense — When the Pracetases entered their penance, the earth was left unprotected after their father had relinquished the kingdom; without cultivation and care, trees spread over it.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tapaś caratsu pṛthivīṃ pracetaḥsu mahīruhāḥ | arakṣyamāṇām ābabrur babhūvātha prajākṣayaḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara

pracetaḥsu tapaś caratsu tatpitari ca nādāt tattvajñānaṃ prāpya rājyaṃ tyaktvā vanaṃ gate sati iti draṣṭavyam | tad uktam prācīnabarhiṣaṃ kṣattakarmasv āsaktacetasam | nārado’dhyātmatattvajñaḥ kṛpāluḥ pratyabodhayat || [bhā.pu. 4.25.3] ityādinā purañjanākhyānena | ato’rakṣyamāṇāṃ pṛthivīṃ karṣaṇādyabhāvād mahīruhā āvṛtavantaḥ || 13 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रचेतःसु तपश् चरत्सु तत्पितरि च नादात् तत्त्वज्ञानं प्राप्य राज्यं त्यक्त्वा वनं गते सति इति द्रष्टव्यम् । तद् उक्तम् प्राचीनबर्हिषं क्षत्तकर्मस्व् आसक्तचेतसम् । नारदोऽध्यात्मतत्त्वज्ञः कृपालुः प्रत्यबोधयत् ॥ [भा.पु. ४.२५.३] इत्यादिना पुरञ्जनाख्यानेन । अतोऽरक्ष्यमाणां पृथिवीं कर्षणाद्यभावाद् महीरुहा आवृतवन्तः ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

When the Pracetases entered their penance, the earth was left unprotected after their father had relinquished the kingdom; without cultivation and care, trees spread over it.

उन्मूलान् अथ तान् वृक्षान् कृत्वा वायुर् अशोषयत् ।

VP 1.15.5

द्रुमक्षयम् अथो दृष्ट्वा किञ्चिच् छिष्टेषु शाखिषु ।
उपागम्याब्रवीद् एतान् राजा सोमः प्रजापतीन् ॥५॥

SSoma, who is the king of all trees and plants, came and told the Prachetasaas, seeing all the destruction of the trees, and few branches being left over. Sri Engal Aalwaan's Commentary: Drumakshayam iti | Raajaa vrukshaadeenaam |

Sri Engal Aalwaan's Commentary: Drumakshayam iti | Raajaa vrukshaadeenaam |

Śrīdhara

somo vṛkṣāṇāṃ rājā | prajāpatīn ityanena kandamūlaphalādibhiḥ prajāvṛddhikāriṇāṃ vṛkṣāṇāṃ dāhaḥ prajāpālakānāṃ yuṣmākam anucita iti sāmopāyaḥ .sūcitaḥ || 5 ||

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Soma, the king of the trees, tells the Pracetases that destroying trees that sustain the people is improper.

VP 1.15.6

VP 1.15.7

रत्नभूता च कन्येयं वाक्षेयी वरवर्णिनी ।

Meaning of para is the samashti purusha, the collectivity of all the sentients; during pralaya, all sentients are merged into avyakta. Avyaktam akshare leeyate, aksharam tamasi leeyate. For mutually different objects, He maintains their svabhaava, svaroopa, separately without any two getting mixed up. He is also superior to nityaas and muktaas, because He is the cause of the entire world, and nityaas and muktaas cannot create, and cannot involve in srushti; in those aspects, Bhagavaan is superior to them, and also He is the master of all, and everything is shesha bhoota to Him. Paara paarah - first word paara means kaala, He is the resting place, or controller of kaala also; everything is resting in Him, and He is ultimate, where all realities go and stay with Him, being supported by Him, and controlled by Him. Kaala is the controller of everything in this prakruti mandala. Kaala has no control in Parama Pada, where Paramaatman controls everything there. He is also the fulfiller of even vyoma and others, vyoma is said to be vibhu, aakaasha, He is antaryaami of aakaasha also; where aakaasha does not exist, He exists; He is vibhu. Everything is filled with Paramaatman only. He is also the protector of the protectors. But for Hari, who is all protector, nobody else can protect. Without His sankalpa, nobody can even protect anyone else. In this one verse, Kandu Maharshi eulogizes Paramaatman as superior to everything else, svetara samasta vilakshana, He is Parama Sheshi. Starting with everything, avyakta, pradhaana, samashti purushaas, muktaas, nityaas, kaala, aakaasha, everything is controlled by Him, and everything rests in Him. He is the sole creator of this entire world. He is superior to Chaturmukha Brahma and others.

Meaning of para is the samashti purusha, the collectivity of all the sentients; during pralaya, all sentients are merged into avyakta. Avyaktam akshare leeyate, aksharam tamasi leeyate. In a form which cannot be differentiated as to what is pradhaana, what is chetana, they go and stay with Paramaatman, as though with ekee bhaava. How can be chetanaas be told as para, means with respect to avyakta, they can be told as para, they are superior to the avyakta. He is the cause of pradhaana and chetanaas; both of them go and merge into Paramaatman, they go and stay one with Him. Samashti purushaas also go and join unto Paramaatman. He is the One who is the abode of even that during pralaya, everything merges unto Him. Or, one more meaning can be told as all those objects which are mutually different, they don't get mixed up, there are innumerable chetanaas, and every chetana when created will have its own body, whether it is keeta, manushya, deva, gandharva, yaksha, the individual karmaas are all maintained, and there is no mixing up of anybody's karma with anybody else; this is called asaankarya. For mutually different objects, He maintains their svabhaava, svaroopa, separately without any two getting mixed up. He is also superior to nityaas and muktaas, because He is the cause of the entire world, and nityaas and muktaas cannot create, and cannot involve in srushti; in those aspects, Bhagavaan is superior to them, and also He is the master of all, and everything is shesha bhoota to Him. Paara paarah - first word paara means kaala, He is the resting place, or controller of kaala also; everything is resting in Him, and He is ultimate, where all realities go and stay with Him, being supported by Him, and controlled by Him. Kaala is the controller of everything in this prakruti mandala. Kaala has no control in Parama Pada, where Paramaatman controls everything there. He is also the fulfiller of even vyoma and others, vyoma is said to be vibhu, aakaasha, He is antaryaami of aakaasha also; where aakaasha does not exist, He exists; He is vibhu. Everything is filled with Paramaatman only. He is also the protector of the protectors. But for Hari, who is all protector, nobody else can protect. Without His sankalpa, nobody can even protect anyone else. In this one verse, Kandu Maharshi eulogizes Paramaatman as superior to everything else, svetara samasta vilakshana, He is Parama Sheshi. Starting with everything, avyakta, pradhaana, samashti purushaas, muktaas, nityaas, kaala, aakaasha, everything is controlled by Him, and everything rests in Him. He is the sole creator of this entire world. He is superior to Chaturmukha Brahma and others.

VP 1.15.155

समचेता जगत्यस्मिन् यः सर्वेष्व् एव जन्तुषु ।
यथात्मनि तथान्यत्र परं मैत्रगुणान्वितः ॥१५५॥

IIn respect of all beings, he was equally positioned, in this world. With respect to others, he was disposed as though towards himself. He was very friendly with respect to everyone.

VP 1.15.156

धर्मात्मा सत्यशौचादिगुणानाम् आकरस् तथा ।
उपमानम् अशेषाणां साधूनां यः सदाभवत् ॥१५६॥

HHe was a dharmaatmaa, always following the path of dharma. He was the abode of all excellent qualities, satya, shoucha. He was upamaana for all the saadhus, all the time. All the saadhus were compared only with Prahlaada.
