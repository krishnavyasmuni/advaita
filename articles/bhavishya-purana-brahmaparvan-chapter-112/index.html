BP 1.112.1

evaṃ kṛṣṇa sadā bhānurnarairbhaktyā yathāvidhi |

Brahmā said: Thus, O Kṛṣṇa, Bhānu, worshipped by people with devotion and according to the rule, always gives a difficult-to-obtain fruit even when worshipped with water alone.

BP 1.112.11
