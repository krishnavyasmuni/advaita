VP 3.8.1

भगवन्भगवान्देवः संसारविजिगीषुभिः । समाख्याहि जगन्नाथो विष्णुराराध्यते यथा
॥१॥

O Lord, O divine Lord, tell me how Lord Viṣṇu, the master of the universe, is worshipped by those who seek to overcome saṃsāra.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhagavan — O Lord; bhagavān devaḥ — the divine Lord; saṃsāra-vijigīṣubhiḥ — by those seeking to overcome saṃsāra; samākhyāhi — explain; jagannāthaḥ — the Lord of the universe; viṣṇuḥ ārādhyate — Viṣṇu is worshipped; yathā — how.

Śrīdhara

viṣṇorārādhanopāyo nijadharmaprasaṅgataḥ । sagaraurvīyasaṃvādaṃ navādhyāyairnirūpyate । yathā yatprakāreṇa dharmeṇa — The means of worshipping Viṣṇu through one’s own dharma will be explained over nine chapters through the dialogue between Sagara and Aurva, showing how He is worshipped through each form of dharma.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhagavanbhagavāndevaḥ saṃsāravijigīṣubhiḥ । samākhyāhi jagannātho viṣṇurārādhyate yathā ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

viṣṇorārādhanopāyo nijadharmaprasaṅgataḥ । sagaraurvīyasaṃvādaṃ navādhyāyairnirūpyate । yathā yatprakāreṇa dharmeṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विष्णोराराधनोपायो निजधर्मप्रसङ्गतः । सगरौर्वीयसंवादं नवाध्यायैर्निरूप्यते । यथा यत्प्रकारेण धर्मेण

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1).

Śrīdhara explains that the means of worshipping Viṣṇu in relation to one’s own dharma is taught through the dialogue of Sagara and Aurva over nine chapters, showing the manner in which He is worshipped through dharma.

VP 3.8.2

आराधिताच्च गोविन्दादाराधनपरैर्नरैः । यत्प्राप्यते फलं श्रोतुं तच्चेच्छामि महामुने
॥२॥

O great sage, I want to hear what fruit people devoted to worship obtain from Govinda after worshipping Him.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ārādhitāt ca govindāt — from Govinda when worshipped; ārādhana-paraiḥ naraiḥ — by people devoted to worship; yat phalam prāpyate — the fruit that is obtained; śrotum icchāmi — I wish to hear; tat ca — that also; mahā-mune — O great sage.

Śrīdhara

viṣṇorārādhanaparairājñānuṣṭhānatatparaiḥ — By people devoted to worshipping Viṣṇu and intent on carrying out His commands.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ārādhitācca govindādārādhanaparairnaraiḥ । yatprāpyate phalaṃ śrotuṃ taccecchāmi mahāmune ॥२॥

Śrīdhara

viṣṇorārādhanaparairājñānuṣṭhānatatparaiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विष्णोराराधनपरैराज्ञानुष्ठानतत्परैः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 2).

Śrīdhara glosses “devoted to worship” as those who are intent on carrying out the Lord’s commands.

VP 3.8.3

यत्पृच्छति भवानेतत्सगरेण महात्मना । और्वः प्राह यथा पृष्टस्तन्मे निगदतः शृणु
॥३॥

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

svargibandhaṃ brahmalokādipadam — A connection with the heavenly worlds, including Brahmaloka and similar attainments.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yatpṛcchati bhavānetatsagareṇa mahātmanā । aurvaḥ prāha yathā pṛṣṭastanme nigadataḥ śṛṇu ॥३॥

Śrīdhara

svargibandhaṃ brahmalokādipadam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्वर्गिबन्धं ब्रह्मलोकादिपदम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 3).

Śrīdhara indicates that the result in view includes heavenly attainments such as Brahmaloka.

VP 3.8.5

फलं चाराधिते विष्णौ यत्पुंसामभिजायते । स चाह पृष्टो यत्नेन तस्मै तन्मेऽखिलं शृणु

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

phalaṃ cārādhite viṣṇau yat puṃsām abhijāyate । sa cāha pṛṣṭo yatnena tasmai tan me'khilaṃ śṛṇu ॥५॥

VP 3.8.6

भौमं मनोरथं स्वर्गं स्वर्गिवन्द्यं च यत्पदम् । प्राप्नोत्याराधिते विष्णौ निर्वाणमपि चोत्तमम्

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhaumaṃ manorathaṃ svargaṃ svargivandyaṃ ca yat padam । prāpnoty ārādhite viṣṇau nirvāṇam api cottamam ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.8.7

यद्यदिच्छति यावच्च फलमाराधितेऽच्युते । तत्तदाप्नोति राजेन्द्र भूरि स्वल्पमथापि वा
॥७॥

Whatever result a person desires, and in whatever measure, he obtains it by worshipping Acyuta—whether the result is abundant or small, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yad yat icchati — whatever one desires; yāvat ca — and in whatever measure; phalam — result; ārādhite acyute — when Acyuta is worshipped; tat tat āpnoti — one obtains that; bhūri — abundant; svalpam atha api vā — or small, even.

Śrīdhara

yāvat yatsaṅkhyakaṃ bhūri svalpaṃ veti pramāṇataḥ — The desired result is obtained in whatever quantity is intended, whether abundant or small.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yadyadicchati yāvacca phalamārādhite’cyute । tattadāpnoti rājendra bhūri svalpamathāpi vā ॥७॥

Śrīdhara

yāvat yatsaṅkhyakaṃ bhūri svalpaṃ veti pramāṇataḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यावत् यत्सङ्ख्यकं भूरि स्वल्पं वेति प्रमाणतः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 7).

Śrīdhara explains that the result is obtained in the exact measure intended, whether abundant or small.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yattu pṛcchasi bhūpāla kathāmārādhyate hariḥ । tadahaṃ sakalaṃ tubhyaṃ kathayāmi nibodha me ॥८॥

VP 3.8.9

वर्णाश्रमाचारवता पुरुषेणपरः पुमान् । विष्णुराराध्यते पन्था नान्यस्तत्तोषकारकः
॥९॥

The Supreme Person, Viṣṇu, is worshipped by a person who lives by the duties of his varṇa and āśrama; there is no other path that pleases Him.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

varṇa-āśrama-ācāravatā puruṣeṇa — by a person observing varṇa and āśrama conduct; paraḥ pumān viṣṇuḥ — the Supreme Person Viṣṇu; ārādhyate — is worshipped; panthāḥ na anyaḥ — there is no other path; tat-toṣa-kārakaḥ — that brings His satisfaction.

Śrīdhara

varṇāśramācāravatety-adhikāri-viśeṣaṇād vedokta-tad-aviruddha-purāṇāgamādy-uktācāravān eva tatrādhikārī, na vigītācāraḥ । anyaḥ śruty-ukta-dharma-parityāgena tad-vrata-dhāraṇa-śravaṇa-kīrtanādi-rūpaḥ panthā na bhavati — Śrīdhara explains the qualification expressed by “one who follows varṇa and āśrama conduct”: the eligible person follows conduct taught by the Veda and by Purāṇic and Āgamic texts that do not contradict it, not censured conduct. Abandoning scriptural duties while merely adopting vows or devotional practices is not an alternative path.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

varṇāśramācāravatā puruṣeṇaparaḥ pumān । viṣṇurārādhyate panthā nānyastattoṣakārakaḥ ॥९॥

Śrīdhara

varṇāśramācāravatety-adhikāri-viśeṣaṇād vedokta-tad-aviruddha-purāṇāgamādy-uktācāravān eva tatrādhikārī, na vigītācāraḥ । anyaḥ śruty-ukta-dharma-parityāgena tad-vrata-dhāraṇa-śravaṇa-kīrtanādi-rūpaḥ panthā na bhavati

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वर्णाश्रमाचारवतेत्य्-अधिकारि-विशेषणाद् वेदोक्त-तद्-अविरुद्ध-पुराणागमाद्य्-उक्ताचारवान् एव तत्राधिकारी, न विगीताचारः । अन्यः श्रुत्य्-उक्त-धर्म-परित्यागेन तद्-व्रत-धारण-श्रवण-कीर्तनादि-रूपः पन्था न भवति

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 9).

Śrīdhara explains that the qualified worshipper follows conduct taught by the Veda and by Purāṇic and Āgamic texts consistent with it, and is not one of censured conduct. No alternative path exists through abandoning scriptural duties and merely taking up vows or devotional practices.

VP 3.8.10

यजन्यज्ञान्यजत्येनं जपत्येनं जपन्नृप । निघ्नन्नन्यान्हिनस्त्येनं सर्वभूतो यतो हरिः
॥१०॥

One worships Him through sacrifice, through study, and through recitation; because Hari is the Self in every being, one also worships Him—though wrongly—when one harms another.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajann yajñān — offering sacrifices; yajati enam — worships Him; japati enam — worships Him through recitation; japann — while reciting; nighnann anyān — striking down others; hinasti enam — harms Him; sarva-bhūtaḥ — the Self in every being; yataḥ hariḥ — because Hari is [that].

Śrīdhara

yajñān yaṣṭavyān indrādīn — sacrifices to be performed for Indra and the other deities; yajan viṣṇum — worshipping Viṣṇu; yajati — he worships; yaṃ kañcana japan — reciting any [name]; viṣṇum eva japati — he recites Viṣṇu alone; anyam ghnan — striking another; viṣṇum eva hanti — he strikes Viṣṇu alone.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajann yajñān yajaty enaṃ japaty enaṃ japannṛpa । nighnann anyān hinasty enaṃ sarva-bhūto yato hariḥ ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara

yajñān yaṣṭavyān indrādīn yajan viṣṇuṃ yajati, yaṃ kañcana japan viṣṇum eva japati, anyam ghnan viṣṇum eva hanti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यज्ञान् यष्टव्यान् इन्द्रादीन् यजन् विष्णुं यजति, यं कञ्चन जपन् विष्णुमेव जपति, अन्यं घ्नन् विष्णुमेव हन्ति

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 10).

Śrīdhara asks how duties directed toward deities can be worship of Bhagavān. He answers that Hari is the Self of every being. The rites performed for ancestors, gods, Brāhmaṇas, and the sacred fire therefore reach Viṣṇu, the inner Self of all; even worship offered with faith to other deities is worship of Him, though performed without the proper understanding. The sacrificial and other duties included in varṇa-āśrama conduct are thus forms of Viṣṇu’s worship.

VP 3.8.11

तस्मात्सदाचारवता पुरुषेण जनार्दनः । आराध्यते स्ववर्णोक्तधर्मानुष्ठानकारिणा
॥११॥

Therefore, a person of good conduct worships Janārdana by carrying out the duties prescribed for his own varṇa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmāt — therefore; sad-ācāra-vatā puruṣeṇa — by a person of good conduct; janārdanaḥ ārādhyate — Janārdana is worshipped; sva-varṇokta-dharma-anuṣṭhāna-kāriṇā — by one who performs the duties prescribed for his own varṇa.

Śrīdhara

yataḥ pūjya vadhyādisarvasvarūpo viṣṇustasmāt tadājñayā varṇāśramadharmān yāgādīn sadācārāṃśca kurvatā viṣṇurārādhyate toṣyate । sadācāraḥ avigītaśiṣṭācāraḥ — Because Viṣṇu is the form of all that is worshipped and disciplined, He is worshipped and pleased by the person who, according to His command, performs the duties of varṇa and āśrama, sacrifices, and good conduct.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmāt sadācāravatā puruṣeṇa janārdanaḥ । ārādhyate sva-varṇokta-dharmānuṣṭhāna-kāriṇā ॥११॥

Śrīdhara

yataḥ pūjya vadhyādisarvasvarūpo viṣṇustasmāt tadājñayā varṇāśramadharmān yāgādīn sadācārāṃśca kurvatā viṣṇurārādhyate toṣyate । sadācāraḥ avigītaśiṣṭācāraḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यतः पूज्य वध्यादिसर्वस्वरूपो विष्णुस्तस्मात् तदाज्ञया वर्णाश्रमधर्मान् यागादीन् सदाचारांश्च कुर्वता विष्णुराराध्यते तोष्यते । सदाचारः अविगीतशिष्टाचारः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 11).

Śrīdhara explains that Viṣṇu is the form of all that is worshipped and disciplined. Therefore He is worshipped and pleased by one who, according to His command, performs varṇa-āśrama duties, sacrifices, and good conduct. Good conduct is the accepted conduct of the learned that is not censured.

VP 3.8.12

ब्राह्मणः क्षत्रियो वैश्यः शूद्रश्च पृथिवीपते । स्वधर्मतत्परो विष्णुमाराधयति नान्यथा
॥१२॥

O ruler of the earth, a Brāhmaṇa, Kṣatriya, Vaiśya, or Śūdra worships Viṣṇu by being devoted to his own duty, and not otherwise.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyaḥ vaiśyaḥ śūdraḥ ca — Brāhmaṇa, Kṣatriya, Vaiśya, and Śūdra; sva-dharma-tat-paraḥ — devoted to one’s own duty; viṣṇum ārādhayati — worships Viṣṇu; na anyathā — not otherwise.

Śrīdhara

uktamarthamevādhikāridarśanapūrvakaṃ vyatirekamukhena draḍhayati—brāhmaṇa iti — A Brāhmaṇa, Kṣatriya, Vaiśya, or Śūdra worships Viṣṇu by being devoted to the dharma proper to that person’s own station, and not otherwise.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyo vaiśyaḥ śūdraś ca pṛthivīpate । sva-dharma-tat-paraḥ viṣṇum ārādhayati nānyathā ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara

uktamarthamevādhikāridarśanapūrvakaṃ vyatirekamukhena draḍhayati—brāhmaṇa iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उक्तमर्थमेवाधिकारिदर्शनपूर्वकं व्यतिरेकमुखेन द्रढयति—ब्राह्मण इति

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 12).

Śrīdhara reinforces the same point by identifying the qualified persons: Brāhmaṇa, Kṣatriya, Vaiśya, and Śūdra worship Viṣṇu by devoted observance of their own dharma, not otherwise.

VP 3.8.13

परापवादं पैशुन्यमनृतं च न भाषते । अन्योद्वेगकरं वापि तोष्यते तेनकेशवः
॥१३॥

Keśava is pleased by the person who does not speak ill of others, spread malicious gossip, or tell lies, and who does not say anything that disturbs another.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

para-apavādam — speaking ill of others; paiśunyam — malicious gossip; anṛtam — falsehood; na bhāṣate — does not speak; anya-udvega-karam — causing distress to another; vā api — or even; toṣyate tena keśavaḥ — Keśava is pleased by that person.

Śrīdhara

tatra sādhāraṇadharmānāha ṣaḍbhiḥ । parasyāpavāḍhaṃ samakṣanindāṃ, paiśunyaṃ parokṣe rahodoṣakathanam — Śrīdhara now states general duties: do not slander another openly, do not backbite by recounting another’s faults in private, and do not speak falsely or cause distress to others.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

parāpavādaṃ paiśunyamanṛtaṃ ca na bhāṣate । anyodvegakaraṃ vāpi toṣyate tenakeśavaḥ ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 25).

Śrīdhara explains “stone” as a stone and “another’s jewel” as a jewel belonging to someone else; the verse says that the Brāhmaṇa should regard them with equal-mindedness. Union with one’s wife during her fertile period is permitted and praised by scripture, but the statement is not a restrictive command that forbids union at other times. He then distinguishes three kinds of Vedic injunction: a vidhi creates an act not otherwise available; a niyama restricts a choice when the act is available in some ways but not others; and a parisaṃkhyā excludes alternatives when both would otherwise be possible. He illustrates these with the commands to perform Jyotiṣṭoma, to husk rice, and to eat five-clawed animals, as well as the injunction to approach one’s wife in season. In the last case, ordinary desire already supplies the possibility of union, so the scriptural statement serves to exclude other times rather than to create the act. The same logic explains why a mantra about taking the horse’s bridle is applied to the horse and not to a donkey: the contextual sign and the mantra together make the horse-application available, while the other is excluded. Thus the three defects—giving up the direct meaning, inventing another meaning, and failing to block the alternative—do not arise. Śrīdhara concludes that “approach one’s wife in season” is a parisaṃkhyā, since union is otherwise possible in both seasonal and non-seasonal periods. The warning that one who does not approach his wife after she has bathed incurs the sin of foeticide is not contradictory: it applies when desire is present but he abstains through aversion or another fault. The general point is that sexual intercourse, meat, and liquor are objects of ordinary human desire, not acts made obligatory by a command.

दया परदुःखप्रहरणेच्छा, तितिक्षा शीतोष्णादिद्वन्द्वसहिष्णुता, अनभिमानिता आत्मनि श्रेष्ठत्वाद्यभिमानशून्यता, सत्यं यथार्थकथनम्, शौचम् मृज्जलाभ्यां स्मृतं बाह्यं भावशुद्धिस्तथान्तरं तत् द्विविधम् । शरीरं पीड्यते येन सुशुभेनापि कर्मणा । अत्यन्तं तन्न कुर्वीत अनायासः स उच्यते ॥ इत्यनायासः । मङ्गलं शिरः सपुष्पं चरणौ सुपूजितौ इत्युक्तम् । मैत्री सर्वमित्रभावेन वर्त्तनम्, अस्पृहा प्राणयात्रामात्रनिमित्तादन्यत्रानभिलाषः । अत्युष्णात् सघृतादन्नादच्छिद्राच्चैव वाससः । अपरप्रेष्यभावाच्च नूय इच्छन् पतत्यधः ॥ इति । अकार्पण्यं यथाशक्ति दानम् अनसूया गुणेषु दोषारोपाभावः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 37).

Śrīdhara’s explanation identifies these as virtues common to all the varṇas: compassion is the wish to remove another’s suffering; forbearance is endurance of heat, cold, and other opposites; purity is both external cleanliness and inward purity; an unburdensome life avoids needless bodily strain; friendliness means acting as a friend to all; absence of possessiveness means not appropriating what others have left unclaimed; and freedom from stinginess and jealousy completes the list.

VP 3.8.39

In an emergency, a Brāhmaṇa may undertake Kṣatriya work, a Kṣatriya may undertake Vaiśya work, and a Vaiśya may undertake Śūdra work; these alternatives are not prescribed for the other two.

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 39).

Śrīdhara explains that the verse turns to emergency duties: a Brāhmaṇa may take up Kṣatriya work, a Kṣatriya may take up Vaiśya work, and a Vaiśya may take up Śūdra work. The prohibition concerning the two higher groups marks the limits of these alternatives.

VP 3.8.40
