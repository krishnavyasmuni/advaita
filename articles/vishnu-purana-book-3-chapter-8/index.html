VP 3.8.1

भगवन् भगवान् देवः संसार-विजिगीषुभिः।
समाख्याहि जगन्नाथो विष्णुर् आराध्यते यथा॥१॥

O Lord, O divine Lord, tell me how Lord Viṣṇu, the master of the universe, is worshipped by those who seek to overcome saṃsāra.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
भगवन् (bhagavan) — O Lord
भगवान् देवः (bhagavān devaḥ) — the divine Lord
संसार-विजिगीषुभिः (saṃsāra-vijigīṣubhiḥ) — by those seeking to overcome saṃsāra
समाख्याहि (samākhyāhi) — explain
जगन्नाथः (jagannāthaḥ) — the Lord of the universe
विष्णुः आराध्यते (viṣṇuḥ ārādhyate) — Viṣṇu is worshipped
यथा (yathā) — how
Śrīdhara
viṣṇorārādhanopāyo nijadharmaprasaṅgataḥ . sagaraurvīyasaṃvādaṃ navādhyāyairnirūpyate . yathā yatprakāreṇa dharmeṇa — The means of worshipping Viṣṇu through one’s own dharma will be explained over nine chapters through the dialogue between Sagara and Aurva, showing how He is worshipped through each form of dharma.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhagavanbhagavāndevaḥ saṃsāravijigīṣubhiḥ । samākhyāhi jagannātho viṣṇurārādhyate yathā ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

viṣṇorārādhanopāyo nijadharmaprasaṅgataḥ । sagaraurvīyasaṃvādaṃ navādhyāyairnirūpyate । yathā yatprakāreṇa dharmeṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विष्णोराराधनोपायो निजधर्मप्रसङ्गतः । सगरौर्वीयसंवादं नवाध्यायैर्निरूप्यते । यथा यत्प्रकारेण धर्मेण

Śrīdhara commentary.

O Lord, O divine Lord, tell me how Lord Viṣṇu, the master of the universe, is worshipped by those who seek to overcome saṃsāra.

VP 3.8.2

आराधिताच् च गोविन्दाद् आराधन-परैर् नरैः।

O great sage, I want to hear what fruit people devoted to worship obtain from Govinda after worshipping Him.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

श्रोतुम् इच्छामि (śrotum icchāmi) — I wish to hear

Śrīdhara
viṣṇorārādhanaparairājñānuṣṭhānatatparaiḥ — By people devoted to worshipping Viṣṇu and intent on carrying out His commands.

Śrīdhara

viṣṇorārādhanaparairājñānuṣṭhānatatparaiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विष्णोराराधनपरैराज्ञानुष्ठानतत्परैः

VP 3.8.6

प्राप्नोत्य् आराधिते विष्णौ निर्वाणम् अपि चोत्तमम्॥६॥

When Viṣṇu is worshipped, one obtains earthly desires, heaven, and even that exalted state revered by the gods of heaven; one also obtains the highest liberation.

Word-for-word

स्वर्गम् (svargam) — heaven

आराधिते विष्णौ (ārādhite viṣṇau) — when Viṣṇu is worshipped

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.8.7

यद् यद् इच्छति यावच् च फलम् आराधितेऽच्युते।
तत् तद् आप्नोति राजेन्द्र भूरि स्वल्पम् अथापि वा॥७॥

Whatever result a person desires, and in whatever measure, he obtains it by worshipping Acyuta—whether the result is abundant or small, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यद् यत् इच्छति (yad yat icchati) — whatever one desires
यावत् च (yāvat ca) — and in whatever measure
फलम् (phalam) — result
आराधिते अच्युते (ārādhite acyute) — when Acyuta is worshipped
तत् तत् आप्नोति (tat tat āpnoti) — one obtains that
राजेन्द्र (rājendra) — O king

स्वल्पम् अथ अपि वा (svalpam atha api vā) — or small, even
Śrīdhara
yāvat yatsaṅkhyakaṃ bhūri svalpaṃ veti pramāṇataḥ — The desired result is obtained in whatever quantity is intended, whether abundant or small.
Transliteration

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यावत् यत्सङ्ख्यकं भूरि स्वल्पं वेति प्रमाणतः

Whatever result a person desires, and in whatever measure, he obtains it by worshipping Acyuta—whether the result is abundant or small, O king.

VP 3.8.9

वर्णाश्रमाचरवता पुरुषेण परः पुमान्।
विष्णुर् आराध्यते पन्था नान्यत् तत्-तोष-कारणम्॥९॥

The Supreme Person, Viṣṇu, is worshipped by a person who lives by the duties of his varṇa and āśrama; there is no other path that pleases Him.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
वर्ण-आश्रम-आचारवता पुरुषेण (varṇa-āśrama-ācāravatā puruṣeṇa) — by a person observing varṇa and āśrama conduct
परः पुमान् विष्णुः (paraḥ pumān viṣṇuḥ) — the Supreme Person Viṣṇu
आराध्यते (ārādhyate) — is worshipped
पन्थाः न अन्यः (panthāḥ na anyaḥ) — there is no other path
तत्-तोष-कारकः (tat-toṣa-kārakaḥ) — that brings His satisfaction
Śrīdhara
varṇāśramācāravateti — “possessing varṇa-and-āśrama conduct,” thus;
adhikāri-viśeṣaṇāt — from the qualification of the eligible person;
vedokta-tad-aviruddha-purāṇāgamādy-uktācāravān eva — only one possessing conduct taught by the Veda and by non-conflicting Purāṇa, Āgama, and the like;
tatrādhikārī — is eligible there;
na vigītācāraḥ — not one of censured conduct;
anyaḥ — another;
śruty-ukta-dharma-parityāgena — by abandoning scripture-taught dharma;
tad-vrata-dhāraṇa-śravaṇa-kīrtanādi-rūpaḥ — in the form of bearing that vow, hearing, reciting, and the like;
panthā na bhavati — is not a path
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

varṇāśramācāravatā puruṣeṇaparaḥ pumān । viṣṇurārādhyate panthā nānyastattoṣakārakaḥ ॥९॥

Śrīdhara

varṇāśramācāravatety-adhikāri-viśeṣaṇād vedokta-tad-aviruddha-purāṇāgamādy-uktācāravān eva tatrādhikārī, na vigītācāraḥ । anyaḥ śruty-ukta-dharma-parityāgena tad-vrata-dhāraṇa-śravaṇa-kīrtanādi-rūpaḥ panthā na bhavati

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वर्णाश्रमाचारवतेत्य्-अधिकारि-विशेषणाद् वेदोक्त-तद्-अविरुद्ध-पुराणागमाद्य्-उक्ताचारवान् एव तत्राधिकारी, न विगीताचारः । अन्यः श्रुत्य्-उक्त-धर्म-परित्यागेन तद्-व्रत-धारण-श्रवण-कीर्तनादि-रूपः पन्था न भवति

Śrīdhara commentary.

The Supreme Person, Viṣṇu, is worshipped by a person who lives by the duties of his varṇa and āśrama; there is no other path that pleases Him.

VP 3.8.10

यजन् यज्ञान् यजत्य् एनं जपत्य् एनं जपन् नृप।
निघ्नन्न् अन्यान् हिनस्त्य् एनं सर्व-भूतो यतो हरिः॥१०॥

One worships Him through sacrifice, through study, and through recitation; because Hari is the Self in every being, one also worships Him—though wrongly—when one harms another.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
यजन्न् यज्ञान् (yajann yajñān) — offering sacrifices
यजति एनम् (yajati enam) — worships Him
जपति एनम् (japati enam) — worships Him through recitation
जपन्न् (japann) — while reciting
निघ्नन्न् अन्यान् (nighnann anyān) — striking down others
हिनस्ति एनम् (hinasti enam) — harms Him
सर्व-भूतः (sarva-bhūtaḥ) — the Self in every being
यतः हरिः (yataḥ hariḥ) — because Hari is [that]
Śrīdhara
yajñān yaṣṭavyān indrādīn — sacrifices to be performed for Indra and the other deities;
yajan viṣṇum — worshipping Viṣṇu;
yajati — he worships;
yaṃ kañcana japan — reciting any [name];
viṣṇum eva japati — he recites Viṣṇu alone;
anyam ghnan — striking another;
viṣṇum eva hanti — he strikes Viṣṇu alone.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajann yajñān yajaty enaṃ japaty enaṃ japannṛpa । nighnann anyān hinasty enaṃ sarva-bhūto yato hariḥ ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara

yajñān yaṣṭavyān indrādīn yajan viṣṇuṃ yajati, yaṃ kañcana japan viṣṇum eva japati, anyam ghnan viṣṇum eva hanti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यज्ञान् यष्टव्यान् इन्द्रादीन् यजन् विष्णुं यजति, यं कञ्चन जपन् विष्णुमेव जपति, अन्यं घ्नन् विष्णुमेव हन्ति

Śrīdhara commentary.

One worships Him through sacrifice, through study, and through recitation; because Hari is the Self in every being, one also worships Him—though wrongly—when one harms another.

VP 3.8.11

तस्मात् सदाचारवता पुरुषेण जनार्दनः।
आराध्यते स्व-वर्णोक्त-धर्मानुष्ठान-कारिणा॥११॥

Therefore, a person of good conduct worships Janārdana by carrying out the duties prescribed for his own varṇa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore
सद्-आचार-वता पुरुषेण (sad-ācāra-vatā puruṣeṇa) — by a person of good conduct
जनार्दनः आराध्यते (janārdanaḥ ārādhyate) — Janārdana is worshipped
स्व-वर्णोक्त-धर्म-अनुष्ठान-कारिणा (sva-varṇokta-dharma-anuṣṭhāna-kāriṇā) — by one who performs the duties prescribed for his own varṇa
Śrīdhara
yataḥ — because;
pūjya vadhyādi-sarva-svarūpo viṣṇuḥ — Viṣṇu is the form of all that is worshipful, punishable, and so on;
tasmāt — therefore;

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmāt sadācāravatā puruṣeṇa janārdanaḥ । ārādhyate sva-varṇokta-dharmānuṣṭhāna-kāriṇā ॥११॥

Śrīdhara

yataḥ pūjya vadhyādisarvasvarūpo viṣṇustasmāt tadājñayā varṇāśramadharmān yāgādīn sadācārāṃśca kurvatā viṣṇurārādhyate toṣyate । sadācāraḥ avigītaśiṣṭācāraḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यतः पूज्य वध्यादिसर्वस्वरूपो विष्णुस्तस्मात् तदाज्ञया वर्णाश्रमधर्मान् यागादीन् सदाचारांश्च कुर्वता विष्णुराराध्यते तोष्यते । सदाचारः अविगीतशिष्टाचारः

Śrīdhara commentary.

Therefore, a person of good conduct worships Janārdana by carrying out the duties prescribed for his own varṇa.

VP 3.8.12
