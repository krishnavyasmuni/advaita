VP 5.27.1

शम्बरेण हृतो वीरः प्रद्युम्नः स कथं मुने

श्री मैत्रेयः - शांर्रेण हृतो र्वीरः प्रद्युम्नः स कथां मुने | शांर्रः स महार्वीयिः प्रद्युम्नेन कथां हतः ॥ १ ॥ Sri Maitreya - You said that Pradyumna, who was very valiant, was stolen by Shambara. How did that happen? How was Shambara, who was a very powerful demon, killed by Pradyumna?

VP 5.27.3

षष्ठेऽह्नि जातमात्रं तु प्रद्युम्नं सूतिकागृहात्

Sri Paraasharar - On the sixth day after birth, Pradyumna was taken away from the delivery place where he was born. Shambaraasura knew that Pradyumna is going to kill him. So, he just snatched the child and went away.
