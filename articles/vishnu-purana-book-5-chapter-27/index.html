VP 5.27.1

संबरेण हृतो वीरः प्रद्युम्नः स कथं मुने । शंबरः स महावीर्यः प्रद्युम्नेन कथं हतः

Sri Maitreya - You said that Pradyumna, who was very valiant, was stolen by Shambara. How did that happen? How was Shambara, who was a very powerful demon, killed by Pradyumna?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śambareṇa hṛto vīraḥ pradyumnaḥ sa kathaṃ mune . śambaraḥ sa mahāvīryaḥ pradyumnena kathaṃ hataḥ — Sri Maitreya - You said that Pradyumna, who was very valiant, was stolen by Shambara. How did that happen? How was Shambara, who was a very powerful demon, killed by Pradyumna?

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saṃbareṇa hṛto vīraḥ pradyumnaḥ sa kathaṃ mune . śaṃbaraḥ sa mahāvīryaḥ pradyumnena kathaṃ hataḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.27.2

यस्तेनापहृतः पूर्वं स कथं विजघान तम् । एतद्विस्तरतः श्रोतुमिच्छामि सरलं गुरो

When Shambaraasura had taken away the child, how did that child kill Shambaraasura himself? I would like to know this story in detail. Please do tell me about it.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yastenāpahṛtaḥ pūrvaṃ sa kathaṃ vijaghāna tam . etadvistarataḥ śrotumicchāmi saralaṃ guro

No commentary.

VP 5.27.3

षष्ठेऽह्नि जातमात्रं तु प्रद्युम्नं सूतिकागृहात् । ममैष हन्तेति मुने हृतवान्कालशंबरः

Sri Paraasharar - On the sixth day after birth, Pradyumna was taken away from the delivery place where he was born. Shambaraasura knew that Pradyumna is going to kill him. So, he just snatched the child and went away. Shambara was black to look at. He was a fearful demon.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṣaṣṭhe’hni jātamātraṃ tu pradyumnaṃ sūtikāgṛhāt . mamaiṣa hanteti mune hṛtavānakālaśambaraḥ — Sri Paraasharar - On the sixth day after birth, Pradyumna was taken away from the delivery place where he was born. Shambaraasura knew that Pradyumna is going to kill him. So, he just snatched the child and went away. Shambara was black to look at. He was a fearful demon.

Śrīdhara

ṣaṣṭhe’hnīti . kāla-śambaraḥ mṛtyu-tulya-śambaraḥ — Sri Paraasharar - On the sixth day after birth, Pradyumna was taken away from the delivery place where he was born. Shambaraasura knew that Pradyumna is going to kill him. So, he just snatched the child and went away. Shambara was black to look at. He was a fearful demon.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṣaṣṭhe’hni jātamātraṃ tu pradyumnaṃ sūtikāgṛhāt . mamaiṣa hanteti mune hṛtavānkālaśaṃbaraḥ

Śrīdhara

ṣaṣṭhe’hnīti . kāla-śambaraḥ mṛtyu-tulya-śambaraḥ ||३|| hṛtvā cikṣepa caivainaṃ grāhogre lavaṇārṇave .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

षष्ठेऽह्नीति । काल-शम्बरः मृत्यु-तुल्य-शम्बरः ॥३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - On the sixth day after birth, Pradyumna was taken away from the delivery place where he was born. Shambaraasura knew that Pradyumna is going to kill him. So, he just snatched the child and went away. Shambara was black to look at. He was a fearful demon.

VP 5.27.7

तस्य मायावती नाम पत्नी सर्वगृहेश्वरी । कारयामास सूदानामाधिपत्यमनिन्दिता

While they were taking all the fish for preparing food for Shambaraasura, the preparations were overlooked by Maayaavati, who was wife of Shambaraasura. She was not actually the wife, she was a maayaa roopa. She was in charge of the kitchen, and was taking care of the preparations for Shambaraasura.

Word-for-word

She was not actually the wife, she was a maayaa roopa. She was in charge of the kitchen, and was taking care of the preparations for Shambaraasura.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

tasyeti . māyāvatī nāma patnī māyā-rūpeṇa tasya patnīty evaṃ mohotpādanataḥ tat-patnīti vyavahāraḥ . yad vā, nāma-patnī nāma-mātreṇa patnīm ucyate, vastutas tv anindiā pativratā kāmasya bhāryety arthaḥ . etac cāgre sphuṭībhaviṣyati ||७|| dārite matsya-jaṭhare dadṛśe sātiśobhanam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तस्येति । मायावती नाम पत्नी माया-रूपेण तस्य पत्नीत्य् एवं मोहोत्पादनतः तत्-पत्नीति व्यवहारः । यद् वा, नाम-पत्नी नाम-मात्रेण पत्नीम् उच्यते, वस्तुतस् त्व् अनिन्दिआ पतिव्रता कामस्य भार्येत्य् अर्थः । एतच् चाग्रे स्फुटीभविष्यति ॥७॥

She was not actually the wife, she was a maayaa roopa. She was in charge of the kitchen, and was taking care of the preparations for Shambaraasura.
