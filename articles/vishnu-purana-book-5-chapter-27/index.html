VP 5.27.1

शम्बरेण हृतो वीरः प्रद्युम्नः स कथं मुने ।
शम्बरः स महावीर्यः प्रद्युम्नेन कथं हतः

Maitreya asked: “How was the heroic Pradyumna carried away by Śambara, and how was the greatly powerful Śambara killed by Pradyumna?”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śambareṇa hṛto vīraḥ pradyumnaḥ sa kathaṃ mune . śambaraḥ sa mahāvīryaḥ pradyumnena kathaṃ hataḥ

Word-for-word

śambareṇa — by Śambara; hṛtaḥ — carried away; vīraḥ — the heroic one; pradyumnaḥ — Pradyumna; saḥ — he; katham — how; mune — O sage; śambaraḥ — Śambara; saḥ — he; mahā-vīryaḥ — greatly powerful; pradyumnena — by Pradyumna; katham — how; hataḥ — killed.

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.27.2

यस्तेनापहृतः पूर्वं स कथं विजघान तम् ।

VP 5.27.3

षष्ठेऽह्नि जातमात्रं तु प्रद्युम्नं सूतिकागृहात् ।
ममैष हन्तेति मुने हृतवान् कालशम्बरः

On the sixth day after his birth, Pradyumna was carried from the birth chamber by Kāla-Śambara, who thought, “This is the one who will kill me.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṣaṣṭhe ’hni jātamātraṃ tu pradyumnaṃ sūtikā-gṛhāt . mamaiṣa hanteti mune hṛtavān kāla-śambaraḥ

Śrīdhara

ṣaṣṭhe’hnīti . kāla-śambaraḥ mṛtyu-tulya-śambaraḥ ||३|| hṛtvā cikṣepa caivainaṃ grāhogre lavaṇārṇave .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

षष्ठेऽह्नीति । काल-शम्बरः मृत्यु-तुल्य-शम्बरः ॥३॥

Word-for-word

ṣaṣṭhe ahni — on the sixth day; jāta-mātram — just born; tu — indeed; pradyumnam — Pradyumna; sūtikā-gṛhāt — from the birth chamber; mama eṣaḥ hantā — this is my killer; iti — thus; mune — O sage; hṛtavān — carried away; kāla-śambaraḥ — Kāla-Śambara.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - On the sixth day after birth, Pradyumna was taken away from the delivery place where he was born. Shambaraasura knew that Pradyumna is going to kill him. So, he just snatched the child and went away. Shambara was black to look at. He was a fearful demon.

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.27.7

तस्य मायावती नाम पत्नी सर्वगृहेश्वरी ।
कारयामास सूदानामाधिपत्यमनिन्दिता

His wife named Māyāvatī, mistress of the entire household, appointed the blameless one over the cooks.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasya māyāvatī nāma patnī sarva-gṛheśvarī . kārayāmāsa sūdānām ādhipatyam aninditā

Śrīdhara

tasyeti . māyāvatī nāma patnī māyā-rūpeṇa tasya patnīty evaṃ mohotpādanataḥ tat-patnīti vyavahāraḥ . yad vā, nāma-patnī nāma-mātreṇa patnīm ucyate, vastutas tv anindiā pativratā kāmasya bhāryety arthaḥ . etac cāgre sphuṭībhaviṣyati ||७|| dārite matsya-jaṭhare dadṛśe sātiśobhanam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तस्येति । मायावती नाम पत्नी माया-रूपेण तस्य पत्नीत्य् एवं मोहोत्पादनतः तत्-पत्नीति व्यवहारः । यद् वा, नाम-पत्नी नाम-मात्रेण पत्नीम् उच्यते, वस्तुतस् त्व् अनिन्दिआ पतिव्रता कामस्य भार्येत्य् अर्थः । एतच् चाग्रे स्फुटीभविष्यति ॥७॥

Word-for-word

tasya — his; māyāvatī — Māyāvatī; nāma — named; patnī — wife; sarva-gṛha-īśvarī — mistress of the whole household; kārayāmāsa — appointed; sūdānām — of the cooks; ādhipatyam — authority; aninditā — blameless.

Śrīdhara commentary.

While they were taking all the fish for preparing food for Shambaraasura, the preparations were overlooked by Maayaavati, who was wife of Shambaraasura. She was not actually the wife, she was a maayaa roopa. She was in charge of the kitchen, and was taking care of the preparations for Shambaraasura.

VP 5.27.22
