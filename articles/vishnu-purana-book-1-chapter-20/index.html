VP 1.20.1

एवं सञ्चितन्यन् विष्णुम् अभेदेनात्मनो द्विज । तन्मयत्वम् अवाप्याग्र्यं मेने चात्मानम् अच्युतम् ॥१॥

Thus, meditating on Viṣṇu as non-different from his own self, Prahlāda attained the highest identity with Him and regarded himself as Acyuta.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — evaṃ — thus; sañcitanyan — meditating/contemplating; viṣṇum — upon Viṣṇu; abhedena — without difference; ātmanaḥ — from his own self; dvija — O twice-born; tanmayatvam — identity with Him; avāpya — having attained; agryam — highest; mene — he regarded; ca — and; ātmānam — himself; acyutam — Acyuta.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃ sañcitanyan viṣṇum abhedenātmano dvija . tanmayatvam avāpyāgryaṃ mene cātmānam acyutam ||१||

VP 1.20.2

विसस्मार तथात्मानं नान्यत् किञ्चिद् अजानत । अहम् एवाव्ययोऽनन्तः परमात्मेत्यचिन्तयत् ॥२॥

He completely forgot his own individuality and knew nothing else; he thought, “I alone am the imperishable, infinite Supreme Self.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — visasmāra — he forgot; tathā ātmānam — himself likewise; na anyat kiñcit — nothing else; ajānata — he knew; aham eva — I alone; avyayaḥ — imperishable; anantaḥ — infinite; paramātmā iti — “the Supreme Self,” thus; acintayat — he thought.

Śrīdhara

Words — ātmānaṃ prahlādo’ham iti vismṛtavān ||२||; sense — Prahlāda forgot himself, thinking, “I am [the Supreme Self].”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

visasmāra tathātmānaṃ nānyat kiñcid ajānata . aham evāvyayo’nantaḥ paramātmetyacintayat ||२||

Śrīdhara

ātmānaṃ prahlādo’ham iti vismṛtavān ||२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आत्मानं प्रह्लादोऽहम् इति विस्मृतवान् ॥२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Prahlāda forgot himself, thinking, “I am [the Supreme Self].”

VP 1.20.3

तस्य तद्भावनायोगात् क्षीणपापस्य वै क्रमात् । शुद्धेऽन्तःकरणे विष्णुस् तस्थौ ज्ञानमयेऽच्युतः ॥३॥

As the power of that contemplation gradually destroyed his sins, Viṣṇu, the imperishable Lord whose nature is knowledge, stood in his purified inner being.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — tasya — his; tat-bhāvanā-yogāt — through the power of that contemplation; kṣīṇa-pāpasya — whose sin was exhausted; vai — indeed; kramāt — gradually; śuddhe antaḥkaraṇe — in the purified inner organ; viṣṇuḥ — Viṣṇu; tasthau — stood/was present; jñānamayaḥ — knowledge-filled; acyutaḥ — Acyuta.

Śrīdhara

Words — kṣīṇapāpasya dhvastasamastakarmavāsanāmalasya ||३||; sense — “One whose sins are exhausted” means one whose entire impurity of karmic tendencies has been destroyed.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasya tadbhāvanāyogāt kṣīṇapāpasya vai kramāt . śuddhe’ntaḥkaraṇe viṣṇus tasthau jñānamaye’cyutaḥ ||३||

Śrīdhara

kṣīṇapāpasya dhvastasamastakarmavāsanāmalasya ||३||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

क्षीणपापस्य ध्वस्तसमस्तकर्मवासनामलस्य ॥३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“One whose sins are exhausted” means one whose entire impurity of karmic tendencies has been destroyed.

VP 1.20.4

योगप्रभावात् प्रह्लादे जाते विष्णुमयेऽसुरे । चलत्य् उरगबन्धैस् तैर् मैत्रेय त्रुटितं क्षणात् ॥४॥

When, through the power of yoga, the Asura Prahlāda had become filled with Viṣṇu, the serpent bonds broke the moment he moved.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — yoga-prabhāvāt — through the power of yoga; prahlāde jāte — when Prahlāda had become; viṣṇumaye asure — an Asura filled with Viṣṇu; calati — as he moved; uraga-bandhaiḥ taiḥ — by those serpent-bonds; maitreya — O Maitreya; truṭitam — broken; kṣaṇāt — instantly.

Śrīdhara

Words — truṭitaṃ chinnaṃ, bhāve ktaḥ ||४||; sense — “Broken” means severed; the kta suffix is used in the sense of a state.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yogaprabhāvāt prahlāde jāte viṣṇumaye’sure . calaty uragabandhais tair maitreya truṭitaṃ kṣaṇāt ||४||

Śrīdhara

truṭitaṃ chinnaṃ, bhāve ktaḥ ||४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

त्रुटितं छिन्नं, भावे क्तः ॥४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Broken” means severed; the kta suffix is used in the sense of a state.

VP 1.20.5

भ्रान्तग्राहगणः सोर्मिर् ययौ क्षोभं महार्णवः । चचाल च मही सर्वा सशैलवनकानना ॥५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“With a confused host of crocodiles” means an ocean in which the crocodile-host is agitated; “with waves” means accompanied by waves.

VP 1.20.6

VP 1.20.7

तुष्टाव च पुनर् धीमान् अनादिं पुरुषोत्तमम् । एकाग्रमतिर् अव्यग्रो यतवाक् कायमानसः ॥८॥

VP 1.20.9

ओं नमो परमार्थार्थं स्थूलसूक्ष्मक्षराक्षर । व्यक्ताव्यक्त कलातीत सकलेश निरञ्जन ॥९॥

Om! Glory to You, the Supreme Reality—subtle and substantial, mutable and immutable, perceptible and imperceptible, beyond the manifest and unmanifest and beyond the kalās, Lord of all, stainless.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — oṃ — Om; namaḥ — homage; paramārtha-artham — the Supreme Reality/ultimate truth; sthūla-sūkṣma — gross and subtle; kṣara-akṣara — perishable and imperishable; vyakta-avyakta — manifest and unmanifest; kalā-atīta — beyond the kalās; sakala-īśa — Lord of all; nirañjana — stainless.

Śrīdhara

Words — he paramārthajñānarūpa ! he arthadṛśyarūpa ! jñānasvarūpam atyantanirmalaṃ paramārthataḥ . tam evārthasvarūpeṇa bhrāntidarśanataḥ sthitam || ity uktatvāt . sthūlajāgraddṛśyarūpa ! kalātīta niravayava ! sakala sāvayava ! īśa niyāmaka ! nirañjana nirlepa ||९||; sense — “O form of ultimate knowledge! O form of the object seen as reality!” Since the nature of knowledge is utterly pure in ultimate reality, and that same reality is situated as the form of the object through mistaken perception, [the invocation continues:] “O form of the gross object perceived in waking; beyond the kalās, without parts; possessing all parts; Lord, controller; stainless, untouched.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

oṃ namo paramārthārthaṃ sthūlasūkṣmakṣarākṣara . vyaktāvyakta kalātīta sakaleśa nirañjana ||९||

Śrīdhara

he paramārthajñānarūpa ! he arthadṛśyarūpa ! jñānasvarūpam atyantanirmalaṃ paramārthataḥ . tam evārthasvarūpeṇa bhrāntidarśanataḥ sthitam || ity uktatvāt . sthūlajāgraddṛśyarūpa ! kalātīta niravayava ! sakala sāvayava ! īśa niyāmaka ! nirañjana nirlepa ||९||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

हे परमार्थज्ञानरूप ! हे अर्थदृश्यरूप ! ज्ञानस्वरूपम् अत्यन्तनिर्मलं परमार्थतः । तम् एवार्थस्वरूपेण भ्रान्तिदर्शनतः स्थितम् ॥ इत्य् उक्तत्वात् । स्थूलजाग्रद्दृश्यरूप ! कलातीत निरवयव ! सकल सावयव ! ईश नियामक ! निरञ्जन निर्लेप ॥९॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“O form of ultimate knowledge! O form of the object seen as reality!” Since the nature of knowledge is utterly pure in ultimate reality, and that same reality is situated as the form of the object through mistaken perception, [the invocation continues:] “O form of the gross object perceived in waking; beyond the kalās, without parts; possessing all parts; Lord, controller; stainless, untouched.”

VP 1.20.10

गुणाञ्जन गुणाधार निर्गुणात्मन् गुणस्थिर । मूर्तामूर्त महामूर्ते सूक्ष्ममूर्ते स्फुटास्फुट ॥१०॥

You are the subject of attributes and void of attributes, abiding in qualities though they do not abide in You; morphous and amorphous, vast and subtle, manifest and unmanifest.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — guṇa-añjana — coloring/illumining the guṇas; guṇa-ādhāra — support of the guṇas; nirguṇa-ātman — Self beyond the guṇas; guṇa-sthira — steady in the guṇas; mūrta-amūrta — embodied and unembodied; mahā-mūrte — O great form; sūkṣma-mūrte — O subtle form; sphuṭa-asphuṭa — manifest and unmanifest.

Śrīdhara

Words — guṇān anakti svasattāprakāśābhyām anurañjayatīti vā . guṇānām ādhāra ! guṇeṣu sthira ! parasthiteti pāṭhe param avyaktaṃ tatra sthitetyarthaḥ . bhaktānāṃ sphuṭa, anyeṣām asphuṭa ||१०||; sense — “Guṇāñjana” means either one who colors the guṇas through his own existence and illumination, or [with the reading] “situated beyond”: the Supreme Unmanifest is situated there. He is manifest to devotees and unmanifest to others.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

guṇāñjana guṇādhāra nirguṇātman guṇasthira . mūrtāmūrta mahāmūrte sūkṣmamūrte sphuṭāsphuṭa ||१०||

Śrīdhara

guṇān anakti svasattāprakāśābhyām anurañjayatīti vā . guṇānām ādhāra ! guṇeṣu sthira ! parasthiteti pāṭhe param avyaktaṃ tatra sthitetyarthaḥ . bhaktānāṃ sphuṭa, anyeṣām asphuṭa ||१०||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

गुणान् अनक्ति स्वसत्ताप्रकाशाभ्याम् अनुरञ्जयतीति वा । गुणानाम् आधार ! गुणेषु स्थिर ! परस्थितेति पाठे परम् अव्यक्तं तत्र स्थितेत्यर्थः । भक्तानां स्फुट, अन्येषाम् अस्फुट ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Guṇāñjana” means either one who colors the guṇas through his own existence and illumination, or [with the reading] “situated beyond”: the Supreme Unmanifest is situated there. He is manifest to devotees and unmanifest to others.

VP 1.20.11

करालसौम्यरूपात्मन् विद्याविद्यालयाच्युत । सदसद्रूप सद्भाव सदसद्भावभावन ॥११॥

You are terrible and beautiful in form; knowledge and ignorance; being and non-being; the source and sustainer of the existence of both.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — karāla-saumya-rūpa-ātman — Self of terrible and gentle forms; vidyā-avidyā-ālaya — abode of knowledge and ignorance; acyuta — Acyuta; sat-asat-rūpa — form of being and non-being; sat-bhāva — being/existence; sat-asat-bhāva-bhāvana — producer and sustainer of being and non-being.

Śrīdhara

Words — karālasaumyarūpāṇām ātman ! sadasadrūpayoḥ kāryakāraṇayoḥ sadbhāva utpattir yasmāt tāv eva ca sadasadbhāvau bhāvayati pālayatīti tathā ||११||; sense — “O Self of terrible and gentle forms!” The existence of the forms of being and non-being—cause and effect—arises from You; You also bring forth and preserve those two forms, so You are called that.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

karālasaumyarūpātman vidyāvidyālayācyuta . sadasadrūpa sadbhāva sadasadbhāvabhāvana ||११||

Śrīdhara

karālasaumyarūpāṇām ātman ! sadasadrūpayoḥ kāryakāraṇayoḥ sadbhāva utpattir yasmāt tāv eva ca sadasadbhāvau bhāvayati pālayatīti tathā ||११||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

करालसौम्यरूपाणाम् आत्मन् ! सदसद्रूपयोः कार्यकारणयोः सद्भाव उत्पत्तिर् यस्मात् ताव् एव च सदसद्भावौ भावयति पालयतीति तथा ॥११॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“O Self of terrible and gentle forms!” The existence of the forms of being and non-being—cause and effect—arises from You; You also bring forth and preserve those two forms, so You are called that.

VP 1.20.12

नित्यानित्यप्रपञ्चात्मन् निष्प्रपञ्चामलाश्रित । एकानेक नमस् तुभ्यं वासुदेवादिकारण ॥१२॥

Word-for-word

Śrīdhara

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

amalair jñānibhir āśrita paramārthetyādiṣu ādikāraṇetyanteṣu ṣaṭtriṃśat sambodhaneṣu tubhyaṃ nama ityanuṣaṅgena pratyekaṃ vākyasamāptiḥ kartavyā ||१२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Resorted to by stainless knowers.” From “ultimate reality” through “cause of Vāsudeva and the rest,” there are thirty-six vocatives; by supplying “homage to You” with each, each phrase should be completed as a sentence.

VP 1.20.13

यः स्थूलसूक्ष्मः प्रकटः प्रकाशो यः सर्वभूतो न च सर्वभूतः । विश्वं यतश् चैतद् अविश्वहेतोर् नमोऽस्तु तस्मै पुरुषोत्तमाय ॥१३॥

You are gross and subtle, manifest light, present in every being yet not any single being; from You this universe proceeds, though You are distinct from its cause. Glory to You, Purushottama.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

सदा चरणारविन्दस्य फलम् आहप्रह्लादम् इति ।

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He states the fruit of always [resting at] the lotus feet with “Prahlāda...”

Textual basis

Wilson reference: H. H. Wilson, The Vishnu Purāṇa, Book 1, Chapter XX; the verse division follows the Vasuki Sanskrit text. Sanskrit and Śrīdhara Sanskrit commentary: Vasuki source text. Base text: Vasuki mūla-text edition.
