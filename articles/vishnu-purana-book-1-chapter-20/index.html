VP 1.20.1

एवं सञ्चितन्यन् विष्णुम् अभेदेनात्मनो द्विज । तन्मयत्वम् अवाप्याग्र्यं मेने चात्मानम् अच्युतम् ॥१॥ ॥1॥

Thinking of Viṣṇu as non-different from himself, Prahlāda attained supreme absorption and regarded himself as Acyuta.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Key terms — evaṃ, sañcitanyan, viṣṇum, abhedenātmano, dvija, tanmayatvam; direct sense — Thinking of Viṣṇu as non-different from himself, Prahlāda attained supreme absorption and regarded himself as Acyuta.

Śrīdhara

Commentary — No separate Sanskrit gloss is supplied in the source.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃ sañcitanyan viṣṇum abhedenātmano dvija . tanmayatvam avāpyāgryaṃ mene cātmānam acyutam ||१|| ||1||

Śrīdhara

No separate Sanskrit gloss is supplied in the source.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

No separate Sanskrit gloss is supplied in the source.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Prahlāda’s absorption has become complete: he sees Acyuta as his own true Self.

VP 1.20.2

विसस्मार तथात्मानं नान्यत् किञ्चिद् अजानत । अहम् एवाव्ययोऽनन्तः परमात्मेत्यचिन्तयत् ॥२॥ ॥2॥

VP 1.20.3

तस्य तद्भावनायोगात् क्षीणपापस्य वै क्रमात् । शुद्धेऽन्तःकरणे विष्णुस् तस्थौ ज्ञानमयेऽच्युतः ॥३॥ ॥3॥
