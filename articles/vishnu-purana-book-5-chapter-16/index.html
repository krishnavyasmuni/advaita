VP 5.16.1

केशी चापि बलोदग्रः कंसदूतप्रचोदितः।
कृष्णस्य निधनाकाङ्क्षी वृन्दावनमुपागमत्

Sri Paraasharar - Keshi, being very arrogant with power, and being ordered by Kamsa, to go and kill Balaraama and Sri Krishna, came to Brundaavana.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
keśī ca api balodagraḥ — Keśin too, of mighty strength;
kaṃsa-dūta-pracoditaḥ — impelled by Kamsa’s messenger;
kṛṣṇasya nidhanākāṅkṣī — desiring Kṛṣṇa’s death;
vṛndāvanam upāgamat — came to Vṛndāvana
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

keśī cāpi balodagraḥ kaṃsadūtapracoditaḥ . kṛṣṇasya nidhanākāṅkṣī vṛndāvanamupāgamat

Śrīdhara

keśī iti . balodagraḥ balenoddhataḥ kaṃsasya dūtena prcoditaḥ sva-sthānād eva pracoditaḥ, na tu kaṃsenāhūya svayaṃ preṣitaḥ . asura-kāryādi-svārthasya tāvad vilambāsahanād evety arthaḥ ||१|| sa khura-kṣata-bhū-pṛṣṭhaḥ saṭākṣepa-dhutāmbudaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

केशी इति । बलोदग्रः बलेनोद्धतः कंसस्य दूतेन प्र्चोदितः स्व-स्थानाद् एव प्रचोदितः, न तु कंसेनाहूय स्वयं प्रेषितः । असुर-कार्यादि-स्वार्थस्य तावद् विलम्बासहनाद् एवेत्य् अर्थः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - Keshi, being very arrogant with power, and being ordered by Kamsa, to go and kill Balaraama and Sri Krishna, came to Brundaavana.

VP 5.16.2

स खुरक्षतभूपृष्ठःसटाक्षेपधुतांबुदः।
द्रुतविक्रान्तचन्द्रार्कमार्गो गोपानुपाद्रवत्

Keshi was an extremely powerful demon compared to all the others who had come. He was digging the earth with his powerful hoofs. When he shakes his mane, it used to scatter the clouds itself. He was such a powerful and huge demon. With high speed, he would go and cross over the paths of Sun and Moon. He came to torture the gopaas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
sa khura-kṣata-bhū-pṛṣṭhaḥ — he, with earth’s surface wounded by hooves;
saṭā-kṣepa-dhutāmbudaḥ — shaking clouds by tossing his mane;
druta-vikrānta-candrārka-mārgaḥ — with moon-and-sun paths swiftly crossed;
gopān upādravat — attacked the cowherds
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sa khurakṣatabhūpṛṣṭhaḥsaṭākṣepadhutāṃbudaḥ . drutavikrāntacandrārkamārgo gopānupādravat

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.16.3

तस्य ह्रेषित-शब्देन गोपाला दैत्य-वाजिनः।
गोप्यश् च भय-संविग्ना गोविन्दं शरणं ययुः

Having heard Keshi's terrifying neighing sound, heshaarava, of that daitya Keshi, who came in the form of a horse, the gopaalaas, gopis, and all of them, were terrified, and came to Krishna seeking His protection.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gopālāḥ daitya-vājinaḥ — the cowherds, [seeing] the demon-horse;
gopyaḥ ca bhaya-saṃvignāḥ — and the cowherd women, terrified by fear;
govindaṃ śaraṇaṃ yayuḥ — went to Govinda for refuge
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasya deṣitaśabdena gopālā daityavājinaḥ . gopyaśca bhayasaṃvignā govindaṃ śaraṇaṃ yayuḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.16.4

त्राहित्राहीति गोविन्दःश्रुत्वा तेषां ततो वचः।
सतोयजलदध्वानगंभीरमिदमुक्तवान्

Having heard Keshi's terrifying neighing sound, heshaarava, of that daitya Keshi, who came in the form of a horse, the gopaalaas, gopis, and all of them, were terrified, and came to Krishna seeking His protection. They came running to Krishna, saying " Krishna, do protect us, protect us". Having heard that, Krishna, roared with the sounding thunderous voice like the roaring of a water bearing cloud. Like this, with a resounding voice, He told them thus.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
trāhi trāhi iti — “protect, protect”;
govindaḥ śrutvā teṣāṃ vacaḥ — Govinda, hearing their words;
sato’ya-jalada-dhvāna-gambhīram — deep like the sound of a rain-cloud;
idam uktavān — spoke this
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

trāhitrāhīti govindaḥśrutvā teṣāṃ tato vacaḥ . satoyajaladadhvānagaṃbhīramidamuktavān

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.16.5

अलं त्रासेन गोपालाः केशिनः किं भयातुरैः।
भविद्भिर्गोपजाती यैर्वीरवीर्यं विलोप्यते

"O gopaalaas, don't get scared. Why are you getting scared of this Keshi? You should not be afraid at all, you belong to the gopa jaati, you are all very valiant, why do you get scared of this Keshi?" Enough of getting scared of this Keshi. You are making small of the valiance of all the veeraas, of the gopa jaati you belong to. You have to be valiant, not scared. You belong to gopa jaati.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
alaṃ trāseṇa gopālāḥ — enough of fear, O cowherds;
keśinaḥ kim bhayāturaiḥ — why fear-stricken because of Keśin;
bhavadbhiḥ gopa-jātīyaiḥ — by you of cowherd kind;
vīra-vīryaṃ vilopyate — the valor of heroes is being erased
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

alaṃ trāsena gopālāḥ keśinaḥ kiṃ bhayāturaiḥ . bhavidbhirgopajātī yairvīravīryaṃ vilopyate

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.16.6

किम् अनेनाल्प-सारेण ह्रेषिताटोप-कारिणा।
दैतेय-बल-बाह्येन वल्गता दुष्ट-वाजिना

This weak and powerless Keshi, what can he do? Only with his neighing, he is making a lot of loud noise, that's all, nothing else. Why are you scared of this powerless weak Keshi? He is a demon, and is very weak, powerless. He is simply dancing and making noise. Why are you getting scared of this Keshi, who has come in the form of a horse?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
kim anena alpa-sāreṇa — what [is there] in this weak one;
heṣitāṭopa-kāriṇā — making a show of neighing;
daiteya-bala-vāhyena — carrying demonic strength;
valgatā duṣṭa-vājinā — the prancing wicked horse
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kimanenālpasāreṇa haṣitāṭopakāriṇā . daiteyabalavāhyena valgatā duṣṭavājinā

Śrīdhara

kim aneneti . hreṣitam evāṭopaḥ tat-kāriṇā daiteya-bala-bāhyena daityair balād ākramya aśvo’yam āruhyate, ato daitya-hantṝṇām asmākaṃ kim anena bhaved ity arthaḥ . valgatā trikam avanamya gacchatā tad uktaṃ valgitaṃ ca natas trikam iti ||६|| ehy ehi duṣṭa kṛṣṇo’haṃ pūṣṇas tv iva pināka-dhṛk .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

किम् अनेनेति । ह्रेषितम् एवाटोपः तत्-कारिणा दैतेय-बल-बाह्येन दैत्यैर् बलाद् आक्रम्य अश्वोऽयम् आरुह्यते, अतो दैत्य-हन्तॄणाम् अस्माकं किम् अनेन भवेद् इत्य् अर्थः । वल्गता त्रिकम् अवनम्य गच्छता तद् उक्तं वल्गितं च नतस् त्रिकम् इति ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

This weak and powerless Keshi, what can he do? Only with his neighing, he is making a lot of loud noise, that's all, nothing else. Why are you scared of this powerless weak Keshi? He is a demon, and is very weak, powerless. He is simply dancing and making noise. Why are you getting scared of this Keshi, who has come in the form of a horse?

VP 5.16.7

एह्य् एहि दुष्ट कृष्णोऽहं पूष्णस् त्व् इव पिनाक-धृक्।
पातयिष्यामि दशनान् वदनाद् अखिलांस् तव

O evil wicked demon. Come here, come here, I am Krishna standing here. Just like the one holding the trident who did for Pooshan, like that, I am going to break all your teeth out of your mouth. In Daksha yajnya, Veerabhadra carrying a trident, did this to Pooshan, breaking all his teeth, so that they all come out of the mouth. Like that, I am going to do same thing to you, knock the teeth out of your mouth.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ehi ehi duṣṭa — come, come, wicked one;
kṛṣṇaḥ aham — I am Kṛṣṇa;
pūṣṇas tu iva pināka-dhṛk — like the bow-bearing [Śiva] for Pūṣan;
pātayiṣyāmi daśanān — I will knock down the teeth;
vadanāt akhilān tava — all from your mouth
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ehyehi duṣṭa kṛṣṇo’haṃ pūṣṇastviva pinākadhṛt . pātayiṣyāmi daśanānvadanādakhilāṃstava

Śrīdhara

ehy ehīti . pūṣṇo dantān pināka-dhṛt vīrabhadra iva . pūṣṇor iveti pāṭhe pauṣṇaś caruḥ aindrāpauṣṇaś carur ity-ādiṣu pūṣṇa eva ekatama-rūpeṇaindra-sāhitya-rūpeṇa devatā-bheda-rūpa-bhedād dvi-vacanam ||७|| ity uktvāsphoṭyagovindaḥkeśinaḥ sammukhaṃ yayau .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एह्य् एहीति । पूष्णो दन्तान् पिनाक-धृत् वीरभद्र इव । पूष्णोर् इवेति पाठे पौष्णश् चरुः ऐन्द्रापौष्णश् चरुर् इत्य्-आदिषु पूष्ण एव एकतम-रूपेणैन्द्र-साहित्य-रूपेण देवता-भेद-रूप-भेदाद् द्वि-वचनम् ॥७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

O evil wicked demon. Come here, come here, I am Krishna standing here. Just like the one holding the trident who did for Pooshan, like that, I am going to break all your teeth out of your mouth. In Daksha yajnya, Veerabhadra carrying a trident, did this to Pooshan, breaking all his teeth, so that they all come out of the mouth. Like that, I am going to do same thing to you, knock the teeth out of your mouth.

VP 5.16.8

इत्य् उक्त्वास्फोट्यगोविन्दःकेशिनः सम्मुखं ययौ।
विवृतास्यश् च सोऽप्य् एनं दैतेयश् च उपाद्रवत्

Having told this, He moved His arms ferociously, and went in front of Keshi, challenging him. Keshi, the demon in the form of a huge horse, opened his mouth wide, and ran towards Krishna.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

enaṃ daiteya-aśvaḥ upādravat — the demon-horse attacked him
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityuktvāsphoṭya govindaḥ keśinaḥsaṃmukhaṃ yayau . vivṛtāsyaśca so’pyenaṃ daiteyāśva upādravat

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.16.9

बाहुम् आभोगिनं कृत्वा मुखे तस्य जनार्दनः।
प्रवेशयामास तदा केशिनो दुष्ट-वाजिनः

Immediately, Krishna made His arms as large as Keshi's mouth, and put His arm into his face, into the face of the evil horse.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mukhe tasya janārdanaḥ — Janārdana into his mouth;

keśinaḥ duṣṭa-vājinaḥ — of the wicked horse Keśin
Transliteration

VP 5.16.11

कृष्णस्य ववृधे बाहुः केशिदेहगतो द्विज।
विनाशाय यथा व्याधिरासंभूतेरुपेक्षितः

Krishna's arm grew inside Keshi's face, in order to kill him. It was just like a disease which is neglected, right from the beginning, which keeps growing. Just like that, His arms grew inside Keshi's mouth to kill him, to get rid of Keshi.

Krishna's arm grew inside Keshi's face, in order to kill him. It was just like a disease which is neglected, right from the beginning, which keeps growing. Just like that, His arms grew inside Keshi's mouth to kill him, to get rid of Keshi.

VP 5.16.12

VP 5.16.13

Keshi started to stamp the earth with his feet, not being able to bear the pain. He was urinating, and throwing out excreta, because of the fear, and was about to die. He became very quiet, and was perspiring all over, became quiet, not able to put any more effort.

VP 5.16.14

व्यादितास्यमहांरन्ध्रःसोऽसुरः कृष्णबाहुना।

When Krishna tore apart the mouth of Keshi into two parts, and Keshi was killed, and thrown on the earth just like a tree is thrown down by lightning.

Transliteration

Śrīdhara commentary.

When Krishna tore apart the mouth of Keshi into two parts, and Keshi was killed, and thrown on the earth just like a tree is thrown down by lightning.

VP 5.16.15

The two parts of Keshi's body were shining on the ground. Each part had two legs, and half of the back, half tail, one ear, one eye and one nostril. Keshi was torn apart exactly at half.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

dvi-pāde iti . dvau pādau yayoḥ pṛṣṭha-pucche ardha-rūpe yayoḥ . eka-śabdaḥ śravaṇa-padāt prāg yojyaḥ . tathā ca ekaikā śravaṇākṣi-nāsikā yayos te tathokte śakale . eka-karṇākṣi-nāsike iti pāṭhāntaraṃ tu suṣṭhu ||१५|| hatvā tu keśinaṃ kṛṣṇo gopālair muditair vṛtaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

द्वि-पादे इति । द्वौ पादौ ययोः पृष्ठ-पुच्छे अर्ध-रूपे ययोः । एक-शब्दः श्रवण-पदात् प्राग् योज्यः । तथा च एकैका श्रवणाक्षि-नासिका ययोस् ते तथोक्ते शकले । एक-कर्णाक्षि-नासिके इति पाठान्तरं तु सुष्ठु ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The two parts of Keshi's body were shining on the ground. Each part had two legs, and half of the back, half tail, one ear, one eye and one nostril. Keshi was torn apart exactly at half.
