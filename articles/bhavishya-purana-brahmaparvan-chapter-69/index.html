  • Chapter 69 — Dream Omens on the Saptamī Observance

BP 1.69.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
सप्तम्यामुषितो विप्रः स्वप्नदर्शनमुच्यते ।
स्वप्ने दृष्टे च सप्तम्यां पुरुषो नियतव्रतः ॥ 1 ॥

Brahmā said: The dream-vision of a Brāhmaṇa who has fasted on Saptamī is described. When a dream is seen on Saptamī, the disciplined person [should understand its signs].

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; saptamyām uṣitaḥ — having fasted on Saptamī; vipraḥ — a Brāhmaṇa; svapna-darśanam — dream-vision; ucyate — is described; svapne dṛṣṭe — when a dream is seen; ca — and; saptamyām — on Saptamī; puruṣaḥ — a person; niyata-vrataḥ — disciplined in the vow
Transliteration
brahmovāca |
saptamyām uṣito vipraḥ svapnadarśanam ucyate |
svapne dṛṣṭe ca saptamyāṃ puruṣo niyatavrataḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.69.2

समाप्य विधिवत्सर्वं जपहोमादिकं क्रमात् ।
पूजयित्वा दिनेशं तु यथाविभवमात्मनः ॥ 2 ॥

Having completed everything properly, in sequence—recitation, fire-offering, and the rest—he should worship Dineśa according to his means.

Word-for-word
samāpya — having completed; vidhivat — properly; sarvam — everything; japa-homa-ādikam — recitation, fire-offering, and the rest; kramāt — in sequence; pūjayitvā — having worshiped; dineśam — Dineśa; yathā-vibhavam — according to means; ātmanaḥ — of himself
Transliteration
samāpya vidhivat sarvaṃ japahomādikaṃ kramāt |
pūjayitvā dineśaṃ tu yathāvibhavam ātmanaḥ || 2 ||

BP 1.69.3

ततः शयीत शयने देवदेवं विचिन्तयन् ।
सम्प्रसुप्तो यदा पश्येदुदयन्तं दिवाकरम् ॥ 3 ॥

Then he should lie down on his bed, contemplating the God of gods. When, having fallen fully asleep, he sees the Sun rising—

Word-for-word
tataḥ — then; śayīta — he should lie down; śayane — on the bed; deva-devam — God of gods; vicintayan — contemplating; samprasuptaḥ — fully asleep; yadā — when; paśyet — he sees; udayantam — rising; divākaram — the Sun
Transliteration
tataḥ śayīta śayane devadevaṃ vicintayan |
samprasupto yadā paśyed udayantaṃ divākaram || 3 ||

BP 1.69.4

शक्रध्वजं तथा चन्द्रं तस्य सर्वाः समृद्धयः ।
दृश्यं जनं तथा शक्तिं स्रग्विगोवेणुनिस्वनाः ॥ 4 ॥

“[Seeing] Indra’s banner and the Moon brings him every prosperity; [seeing] people and power, garlands, cows, and the sound of flutes [is auspicious].”

Word-for-word
śakra-dhvajam — Indra’s banner; tathā — likewise; candram — the Moon; tasya — for him; sarvāḥ samṛddhayaḥ — all prosperities; dṛśyam janam — people seen; tathā — likewise; śaktim — power; srak — garland; vigaḥ — cow; veṇu-nisvanāḥ — sounds of flutes
Transliteration
śakradhvajaṃ tathā candraṃ tasya sarvāḥ samṛddhayaḥ |
dṛśyaṃ janaṃ tathā śaktiṃ sragvigo veṇunisvanāḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.69.5

श्वेताब्जचामरादर्शकनकासिसुतोद्भवम् ।
रुधिरस्य स्रुतिं सेकं पानं चैश्वर्यकारकम् ॥ 5 ॥

“The white lotus, a fly-whisk, a mirror, gold, a sword, and the birth of a son; the flowing, sprinkling, and drinking of blood are causes of prosperity.”

Word-for-word
śveta-abja — white lotus; cāmara — fly-whisk; ādarśa — mirror; kanaka — gold; asi — sword; suta-udbhavam — birth of a son; rudhirasya srutim — flow of blood; sekam — sprinkling; pānam — drinking; aiśvarya-kārakam — causing prosperity
Transliteration
śvetābjacāmarādarśakanakāsisutodbhavam |
rudhirasya srutiṃ sekaṃ pānaṃ caiśvaryakārakam || 5 ||

BP 1.69.6

श्वेतायाः पञ्चपूताया दर्शनं वृद्धिकारकम् ।
प्रजापतेर्घृताक्तस्य दर्शनं पुत्रदं स्मृतम् ॥ 6 ॥

“Seeing a purified white [one] brings increase; seeing Prajāpati anointed with ghee is remembered as granting a son.”

Word-for-word
śvetāyāḥ — of the white one; pañca-pūtāyāḥ — purified fivefold; darśanam — seeing; vṛddhi-kārakam — causing increase; prajāpateḥ — of Prajāpati; ghṛta-aktasya — anointed with ghee; darśanam — seeing; putra-dam — granting a son; smṛtam — remembered
Transliteration
śvetāyāḥ pañcapūtāyā darśanaṃ vṛddhikārakam |
prajāpater ghṛtāktasya darśanaṃ putradaṃ smṛtam || 6 ||

BP 1.69.7

शस्तवृक्षाभिरोहश्च क्षिप्रमैश्वर्यकारकः ।
दोहने महिषीसिंहीगोधेनूनां स्वके मुखे ॥ 7 ॥

“Climbing auspicious trees quickly brings prosperity; milking buffaloes, lionesses, cows, and cattle with one’s own mouth [is also an omen].”

Word-for-word
śasta-vṛkṣa-abhirohaḥ — climbing auspicious trees; ca — and; kṣipram — quickly; aiśvarya-kārakaḥ — causing prosperity; dohanam — milking; mahiṣī — buffalo; siṃhī — lioness; go-dhenūnām — cows and cattle; svake mukhe — with one’s own mouth
Transliteration
śastavṛkṣābhirohaś ca kṣipram aiśvaryakārakaḥ |
dohanaṃ mahiṣīsiṃhīgodhenūnāṃ svake mukhe || 7 ||

BP 1.69.8

धनुषां च शराणां च नाभौ च द्रुतनिर्गतिः ।
अभिहन्यात्स्वयं खादेत्सिंहान्गा भुजगांस्तथा ॥ 8 ॥

“The swift emergence of bows and arrows from the navel, striking [enemies] oneself, and eating lions, cattle, and serpents likewise [are omens].”

Word-for-word
dhanuṣām — of bows; ca — and; śarāṇām — of arrows; ca — and; nābhau — at the navel; druta-nirgatiḥ — swift emergence; abhihanyāt — should strike; svayam — oneself; khādet — should eat; siṃhān — lions; gāḥ — cattle; bhujagān — serpents; tathā — likewise
Transliteration
dhanuṣāṃ ca śarāṇāṃ ca nābhau ca drutanirgatiḥ |
abhihanyāt svayaṃ khādet siṃhān gā bhujagāṃs tathā || 8 ||

BP 1.69.9

स्वाङ्गशीर्षं हुतवहे तस्य श्रीरग्रतः स्थिता ।
राजते हैमने पात्रे यो भुङ्क्ते पायसं द्विजः ॥ 9 ॥

“If one’s own head is in the sacrificial fire, fortune stands before him. The Brāhmaṇa who eats rice-milk pudding in a golden vessel shines.”

Word-for-word
sva-aṅga-śīrṣam — one’s own head; hutavahe — in the fire; tasya — for him; śrīḥ — fortune; agrataḥ sthitā — standing before; rājate — shines; haimane pātre — in a golden vessel; yaḥ — who; bhuṅkte — eats; pāyasam — rice-milk pudding; dvijaḥ — Brāhmaṇa
Transliteration
svāṅgaśīrṣaṃ hutavahe tasya śrīr agrataḥ sthitā |
rājate haimane pātre yo bhuṅkte pāyasaṃ dvijaḥ || 9 ||

BP 1.69.10

पद्मपत्रे यथा विप्रस्तस्य जन्तोर्बलं भवेत् ।
द्यूते वादेऽथ वा युद्धे विजयॊ हि सुखावहः ॥ 10 ॥

“As [something rests] upon a lotus leaf, so strength comes to that person. In gambling, debate, or battle, victory is pleasant and brings happiness.”

Word-for-word
padma-patre — on a lotus leaf; yathā — as; vipra — O Brāhmaṇa; tasya jantoḥ — of that person; balam — strength; bhavet — becomes; dyūte — in gambling; vāde — in debate; atha vā — or in; yuddhe — battle; vijayaḥ — victory; sukhāvahaḥ — bringing happiness
Transliteration
padmapatre yathā vipras tasya jantor balaṃ bhavet |
dyūte vāde ’tha vā yuddhe vijayo hi sukhāvahaḥ || 10 ||

BP 1.69.11

अग्नेस्तु ग्रसनं विप्र आग्नेयं वृद्धिकारकम् ।
गात्रस्य ज्वलनं विप्र शिरोवेधश्च भूतये ॥ 11 ॥

“The swallowing of fire, O Brāhmaṇa, is an igneous omen causing increase; the burning of the body and piercing of the head, O Brāhmaṇa, are for prosperity.”

Word-for-word
agneḥ — of fire; tu — indeed; grasanam — swallowing; vipra — O Brāhmaṇa; āgneyam — igneous; vṛddhi-kārakam — causing increase; gātrasya — of the body; jvalanam — burning; śiraḥ-vedhaḥ — piercing of the head; ca — and; bhūtaye — for prosperity
Transliteration
agnes tu grasanaṃ vipra āgreyaṃ vṛddhikārakam |
gātrasya jvalanaṃ vipra śirovedhaś ca bhūtaye || 11 ||

BP 1.69.12

माल्याम्बराणां युक्तानां शस्तानां शुक्लपक्षिणाम् ।
सदा लाभं प्रशंसन्ति तथा विष्ठानुलेपनम् ॥ 12 ॥

“The wise praise the constant gain of garlands, garments, auspicious white birds, and likewise an anointing with excrement.”

Word-for-word
mālya-ambarāṇām — of garlands and garments; yuktānām — furnished with; śastānām — auspicious; śukla-pakṣiṇām — white birds; sadā — always; lābham — gain; praśaṃsanti — they praise; tathā — likewise; viṣṭhā-anulepanam — anointing with excrement
Transliteration
mālyāmbarāṇāṃ yuktānāṃ śastānāṃ śuklapakṣiṇām |
sadā lābhaṃ praśaṃsanti tathā viṣṭhānulepanam || 12 ||

BP 1.69.13

स्वाङ्गस्य कर्तने क्षेपे रथयाने प्रजागमः ।
नानाशिरोबाहुता च हस्तानां कुरुते श्रियम् ॥ 13 ॥

“Cutting and casting away one’s own limb, traveling in a chariot, the arrival of people, and having many heads and arms bring prosperity to the hands.”

Word-for-word
sva-aṅgasya — of one’s own limb; kartane — in cutting; kṣepe — in casting away; ratha-yāne — in chariot travel; prajā-gamaḥ — arrival of people; nānā-śiro-bāhutā — many heads and arms; ca — and; hastānām — of hands; kurute — makes; śriyam — prosperity
Transliteration
svāṅgasya kartane kṣepe rathayāne prajāgamaḥ |
nānāśirobāhutā ca hastānāṃ kurute śriyam || 13 ||

BP 1.69.14

अगम्यागमनं चैव शोकमध्ययनं तथा ।
देवद्विजजनाचार्यगुरुवृद्धतपस्विनः ॥ 14 ॥

“Going to an inaccessible place, sorrow, and study, as well as [seeing] gods, Brāhmaṇas, teachers, gurus, elders, and ascetics—”

Word-for-word
agamya-āgamanam — going to an inaccessible place; ca eva — and indeed; śokam — sorrow; adhyayanam — study; tathā — likewise; deva — gods; dvija-jana — Brāhmaṇa people; ācārya — teachers; guru — gurus; vṛddha — elders; tapasvin — ascetics
Transliteration
agamyāgamanaṃ caiva śokam adhyayanaṃ tathā |
devadvijajanācāryaguruvṛddhatapasvinaḥ || 14 ||

BP 1.69.15

यद्यद्वदन्ति तत्सर्वं सत्यमेव हि निर्दिशेत् ।
प्रशस्तदर्शनं चैव अभिषेको नृपश्रियाः ॥ 15 ॥

“Whatever they say should all be declared true. An auspicious vision and an anointing are [omens] of royal prosperity.”

Word-for-word
yat yat — whatever; vadanti — they say; tat sarvam — all that; satyam eva — truly; hi — indeed; nirdiśet — should declare; praśasta-darśanam — auspicious vision; ca eva — and indeed; abhiṣekaḥ — anointing; nṛpa-śriyāḥ — of royal prosperity
Transliteration
yad yad vadanti tat sarvaṃ satyam eva hi nirdiśet |
praśastadarśanaṃ caiva abhiṣeko nṛpaśriyāḥ || 15 ||

BP 1.69.16

स्याद्राज्यं शिरश्छेदेन बहुधा स्फुटितेन तु ।
रुदितं हर्षसम्प्राप्त्यै राज्यं निगडबन्धने ॥ 16 ॥

“Kingship may come through decapitation and through something split open in many ways; weeping is for the attainment of joy, and bondage in fetters [is an omen of] kingdom.”

Word-for-word
syāt — may be; rājyam — kingdom; śiraḥ-chedana — decapitation; bahudhā — in many ways; sphuṭita — split open; tu — indeed; ruditaṃ — weeping; harṣa-samprāptyai — for attainment of joy; rājyam — kingdom; nigaḍa-bandhane — in bondage with fetters
Transliteration
syād rājyaṃ śiraśched ena bahudhā sphuṭitena tu |
ruditaṃ harṣasamprāptyai rājyaṃ nigaḍabandhane || 16 ||

BP 1.69.17

तुरङ्गं वृषभं पद्मं राजानां श्वेतकुञ्जरम् ।
महदैश्वर्यमाप्नोति योऽभीकश्चाधिरोहति ॥ 17 ॥

“Whoever fearlessly mounts a horse, a bull, a lotus, or the white elephant of kings obtains great prosperity.”

Word-for-word
turaṅgam — horse; vṛṣabham — bull; padmam — lotus; rājñām — of kings; śveta-kuñjaram — white elephant; mahat aiśvaryam — great prosperity; āpnoti — obtains; yaḥ — who; abhīkaḥ — fearless; ca adhirohati — mounts
Transliteration
turaṅgaṃ vṛṣabhaṃ padmaṃ rājñāṃ śvetakuñjaram |
mahad aiśvaryam āpnoti yo ’bhīkaś cādhirohati || 17 ||

BP 1.69.18

ग्रसमानो ग्रहांस्तारामहीं च परिवर्तयन् ।
उन्मूलयन्पर्वतांश्च राज्यलाभमवाप्नुयात् ॥ 18 ॥

“One who swallows planets and stars, turns the earth, and uproots mountains may obtain a kingdom.”

Word-for-word
grasamānaḥ — swallowing; grahān — planets; tārāḥ — stars; mahīm — earth; ca — and; parivartayan — turning; unmūlayan — uprooting; parvatān — mountains; ca — and; rājya-lābham — gain of kingdom; avāpnuyāt — may obtain
Transliteration
grasamāno grahāṃs tārā mahīṃ ca parivartayan |
unmūlayan parvatāṃś ca rājyalābham avāpnuyāt || 18 ||

BP 1.69.19

देहान्निष्क्रान्तिरन्त्राणां तैर्वा वृक्षस्य वेष्टनम् ।
पातः समुद्रसरितामैश्वर्याणि सुखानि च ॥ 19 ॥

“The departure of the entrails from the body, or their wrapping around a tree, and the falling of oceans and rivers [bring] prosperity and happiness.”

Word-for-word
dehāt — from the body; niṣkrāntiḥ — departure; antrāṇām — of the entrails; taiḥ — with them; vā — or; vṛkṣasya veṣṭanam — wrapping of a tree; pātaḥ — falling; samudra-saritām — of oceans and rivers; aiśvaryāṇi — prosperities; sukhāni — happiness; ca — and
Transliteration
dehān niṣkrāntir antrāṇāṃ tair vā vṛkṣasya veṣṭanam |
pātaḥ samudrasaritām aiśvaryāṇi sukhāni ca || 19 ||

BP 1.69.20

उदधिं सरितं वापि तीर्त्वा पारं प्रयाति च ।
अद्रिं लङ्घयतेऽपि च भवन्त्यर्थजयायुषः ॥ 20 ॥

“Crossing an ocean or a river and reaching the farther shore, or leaping over a mountain, brings wealth, victory, and long life.”

Word-for-word
udadhiṃ — ocean; saritam — river; vā api — or; tīrtvā — having crossed; pāram — the farther shore; prayāti — goes; ca — and; adrim — mountain; laṅghayate — leaps over; api — also; bhavanti — become; artha-jaya-āyuṣaḥ — wealth, victory, and long life
Transliteration
udadhiṃ saritaṃ vāpi tīrtvā pāraṃ prayāti ca |
adriṃ laṅghayate ’pi ca bhavanty arthajayāyuṣaḥ || 20 ||

BP 1.69.21

उज्ज्वला स्त्री विशेदङ्कमाशीर्वादपराः स्त्रियः ।
भवत्यर्थागमः शीघ्रं कृमिभिर्यदि भक्ष्यते ॥ 21 ॥

“A radiant woman entering one’s lap, and women devoted to blessing, [are auspicious]. Wealth comes quickly if [something] is seen being eaten by worms.”

Word-for-word
ujjvalā strī — radiant woman; viśet — enters; aṅkam — lap; āśīrvāda-parāḥ striyaḥ — women devoted to blessing; bhavati — becomes; artha-āgamaḥ — arrival of wealth; śīghram — quickly; kṛmibhiḥ — by worms; yadi — if; bhakṣyate — is eaten
Transliteration
ujjvalā strī viśed aṅkam āśīrvādaparāḥ striyaḥ |
bhavaty arthāgamaḥ śīghraṃ kṛmibhir yadi bhakṣyate || 21 ||

BP 1.69.22

स्वप्ने स्वप्न इति ज्ञातं दृष्टप्रकथनं तथा ।
मङ्गलानां च सर्वेषां शुभं दर्शनमेव च ॥ 22 ॥

“Knowing in a dream, ‘This is a dream,’ speaking of what was seen, and seeing all auspicious things are themselves auspicious.”

Word-for-word
svapne — in a dream; svapnaḥ iti jñātam — known as a dream; dṛṣṭa-prakathanam — telling what was seen; tathā — likewise; maṅgalānām — of auspicious things; ca — and; sarveṣām — all; śubham darśanam — auspicious seeing; eva — indeed
Transliteration
svapne svapna iti jñātaṃ dṛṣṭaprakathanaṃ tathā |
maṅgalānāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ śubhaṃ darśanam eva ca || 22 ||

BP 1.69.23

संयोगश्चैव माङ्गल्यैरारोग्यधनकारकः ।
ऐश्वर्यराज्यलाभाय यस्मिन्स्वप्न उदाहृतः ॥ 23 ॥

“Union with auspicious things causes health and wealth; such a dream is declared to be for the gain of prosperity and kingdom.”

Word-for-word
saṃyogaḥ — union; ca eva — and indeed; māṅgalyaiḥ — with auspicious things; ārogya-dhana-kārakaḥ — causing health and wealth; aiśvarya-rājya-lābhāya — for gain of prosperity and kingdom; yasmin — in which; svapnaḥ — dream; udāhṛtaḥ — is declared
Transliteration
saṃyogaś caiva māṅgalyair ārogyadhanakārakaḥ |
aiśvaryarājyalābhāya yasmin svapna udāhṛtaḥ || 23 ||

BP 1.69.24

तैर्दृष्टै रोगिणो रोगान्मुच्यन्ते नात्र संशयः ।
न स्वप्नं शोभनं दृष्ट्वा स्वप्यात्प्रातश्च कीर्तयेत् ।
राजभोजकविप्रेभ्यः शुचिभ्यश्च शुचिर्नरः ॥ 24 ॥

“By seeing these, the sick are freed from diseases—of this there is no doubt. Having seen an excellent dream, one should not sleep again; at dawn a pure person should recount it to kings, Bhojakas, Brāhmaṇas, and the pure.”

Word-for-word
taiḥ dṛṣṭaiḥ — by those seen; rogiṇaḥ — the sick; rogāt — from diseases; mucyante — are freed; na atra saṃśayaḥ — there is no doubt; na svapyāt — should not sleep; prātaḥ — at dawn; kīrtayet — should recount; rāja-bhojaka-viprebhyaḥ — to kings, Bhojakas, and Brāhmaṇas; śucibhyaḥ — to the pure; śuciḥ naraḥ — a pure person
Transliteration
tair dṛṣṭai rogiṇo rogān mucyante nātra saṃśayaḥ |
na svapnaṃ śobhanaṃ dṛṣṭvā svapyāt prātaś ca kīrtayet |
rājabhojakaviprebhyaḥ śucibhyaś ca śucir naraḥ || 24 ||

इति श्रीभविष्ये महापुराणे शतार्द्धसाहस्र्यां संहितायां ब्राह्मे पर्वणि सप्तमीकल्पे स्वप्नदर्शनवर्णनं नामैकोनसप्ततिमोऽध्यायः ॥ ६९ ॥

Thus ends the sixty-ninth chapter, named “The Description of Dream-Visions,” in the Brāhma section of the Bhaviṣya Mahāpurāṇa’s Saptamī Kalpa.
