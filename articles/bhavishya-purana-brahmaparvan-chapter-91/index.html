  • Chapter 91 — The Rogahara Observance

BP 1.91.1

brahmovāca |
pūṣṇo bhaved yadā ṛkṣaṃ bhavec ca bhagadaivatam |
vāsaraḥ sa mahān proktaḥ sarvarogabhayāpahaḥ || 1 ||

Brahmā said: When the asterism of Pūṣan occurs and its presiding deity is Bhaga, that day is proclaimed great, removing the fear of every disease.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
pūṣṇaḥ — of Pūṣan;
bhavet — may be, occurs;
yadā — when;
ṛkṣam — asterism;
bhavet — may be, is;
ca — and;
bhaga-daivatam — having Bhaga as its presiding deity;
vāsaraḥ — day;
saḥ — that;
mahān — great;
proktaḥ — proclaimed;
sarva-roga-bhaya-apahaḥ — remover of the fear of every disease.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | pūṣṇo bhaved yadā ṛkṣaṃ bhavec ca bhagadaivatam | vāsaraḥ sa mahān proktaḥ sarvarogabhayāpahaḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.91.2

yo'tra pūjayate bhānuṃ śubhagandhavilepanaiḥ |
sarvarogavinirmukto yāti bhānusamokatām || 2 ||

Whoever worships Bhānu here with auspicious fragrances and unguents, freed from every disease, goes to the world of Bhānu.

Word-for-word
yaḥ — whoever;
atra — here;
pūjayate — worships;
bhānum — Bhānu;
śubha-gandha-vilepanaiḥ — with auspicious fragrances and unguents;
sarva-roga-vinirmuktaḥ — freed from every disease;
yāti — goes;
bhānu-salokataṃ — to the world of Bhānu.
Transliteration
yo'tra pūjayate bhānuṃ śubhagandhavilepanaiḥ | sarvarogavinirmukto yāti bhānusamokatām || 2 ||

BP 1.91.3

arkapatrapuṭe kṛtyā puṣpāṇyarkasya suvrata |
devasya purato rātrau bhaktyā yaḥ sthāpayet budhaḥ || 3 ||

O well-disciplined one, the wise person should make a cup of arka leaves and place arka flowers in it before the deity at night, with devotion.

devasya — of the deity;
purataḥ — before;
rātrau — at night;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
yaḥ — whoever;
sthāpayet — should place;
budhaḥ — the wise person.
Transliteration
arkapatrapuṭe kṛtyā puṣpāṇyarkasya suvrata | devasya purato rātrau bhaktyā yaḥ sthāpayet budhaḥ || 3 ||

BP 1.91.4

pūjayitvārkapuṣpaistu arkamarkapriyaṃ sadā |
prāśayitvārkapuṣpaṃ tu dattvā viprāya dakṣiṇām || 4 ||

Having worshiped with arka flowers the arka tree, always dear to Arka, and having consumed an arka flower, he should give a gift to a Brāhmaṇa.

sadā — always;
prāśayitvā — having consumed;
arka-puṣpam — an arka flower;
tu — indeed;
dattvā — having given;
viprāya — to a Brāhmaṇa;
dakṣiṇām — a gift, sacrificial fee.
Transliteration
pūjayitvārkapuṣpaistu arkamarkapriyaṃ sadā | prāśayitvārkapuṣpaṃ tu dattvā viprāya dakṣiṇām || 4 ||

BP 1.91.5

bhaktyā ca pāyasaṃ vīra rātrau svapiti bhūtale |
anena vidhinā yastu pūjayetatra vai ravim || 5 ||

With devotion, O hero, he eats payasa and at night sleeps upon the ground. Whoever worships Ravi here according to this procedure

BP 1.91.6

sa muktaḥ sarvarogaiṃstu gaccheddinakarālayam |
tasmādapi vrajellokaṃ phuṃkāraravahetinaḥ || 6 ||
