VP 4.3.5

Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 Taih cha gandharva veerya avadhootaih urageshvaraih stooyamaano bhagavaan ashesha deveshah stava shravana unmeelita unnidra pundareeka nayanah nidraa avasaana prabuddhah panipatya abhihitah, bhagavan asmaakam etebhyo gandharvebhyo bhayam utpannam katham upashamam eshyati iti | The serpent lords were insulted by the Gandharva veeraas.
