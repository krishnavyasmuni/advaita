VP 4.22.1

अतश्चेक्ष्वाकवो भविष्याः पार्थिवाः कथ्यन्ते

Sri Paraasharar - The future kings of Ikshvaaku vamsha will be told now. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ataḥ ca — therefore now;
ikṣvākavaḥ bhāvyāḥ pārthivāḥ — the future Ikṣvāku kings;
kathyante — are narrated.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ataścekṣvākavo bhaviṣyāḥ pārthivāḥ kathyante

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.22.2

बृहद्बलस्य पुत्रो बृहत्क्षणः

Bruhadbala will have son Bruhatkshana.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
bṛhadbalasya putraḥ — Bṛhadbala’s son;
bṛhatkṣaṇaḥ — will be Bṛhatkṣaṇa.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bṛhadbalasya putro bṛhatkṣaṇaḥ

Śrīdhara

somavaṃśa-bhaviṣyāṇāṃ rājñāmatra prasaṅgataḥ . dvāviṃśe sūryavaṃśyānāṃ bhaviṣyo vaṃśa ucyate . bṛhadbalasyeti-caturthe’dhyāye arjunatanayenābhimanyunā bhārate yuddhe kṣayamanīyatetyatra ca darsitastasyety arthaḥ ||२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सोमवंश-भविष्याणां राज्ञामत्र प्रसङ्गतः । द्वाविंशे सूर्यवंश्यानां भविष्यो वंश उच्यते । बृहद्बलस्येति-चतुर्थेऽध्याये अर्जुनतनयेनाभिमन्युना भारते युद्धे क्षयमनीयतेत्यत्र च दर्सितस्तस्येत्य् अर्थः ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Bruhadbala will have son Bruhatkshana.

VP 4.22.3

तस्मादुरुक्षयस्तस्माच्च वत्सव्यूहस्ततश्च प्रतिव्योमस्तस्मादपि दिवारकः

Bruhatkshana's son will be Urukshaya, then Vatsavyooha, then Prativyoma, then Divaakara.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tasmāt urukṣayaḥ — from him Urukṣaya;
tasmāt vatsavyūhaḥ — from him Vatsavyūha;
tataḥ prativyomaḥ — then Prativyoma;
tasmāt api divākaraḥ — from him also Divākara.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmādurukṣayastasmācca vatsavyūhastataśca prativyomastasmādapi divārakaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.22.4

तस्मात्सहदेवः सहदेवाद्बृहदश्वस्तत्सूनुर्भानुरथस्तस्य चज प्रतीताश्वस्तस्यापि सुप्रतीकस्ततश्च मरुदेवस्ततः सुनक्षत्रस्तमात्किन्नरः

His son will be Sahadeva, then Bruhadashva, his son will be Bhaanuratha, then Prateetaashva, then Suprateeka, then Marudeva, then Sunakshatra, then Kinnara.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tasmāt sahadevaḥ — from him Sahadeva;
sahadevāt bṛhadaśvaḥ — from Sahadeva Bṛhadaśva;
tat-sūnuḥ bhānurathaḥ — his son Bhānuratha;
tasya ca pratītāśvaḥ — and his Pratītāśva;
tasyāpi supratīkaḥ — of him also Supratīka;
tataḥ marudevaḥ — then Marudeva;
tataḥ sunakṣatraḥ — then Sunakṣatra;
tasmāt kinnaraḥ — from him Kinnara.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmātsahadevaḥ sahadevādbṛhadaśvastatsūnurbhānurathastasya caja pratītāśvastasyāpi supratīkastataśca marudevastataḥ sunakṣatrastamātkinnaraḥ

Śrīdhara

yatra tatra nāmnāṃ vailakṣaṇyaṃ dṛśyate, tatra kalpayugādibhedena vyavasthāpanīyam ||४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यत्र तत्र नाम्नां वैलक्षण्यं दृश्यते, तत्र कल्पयुगादिभेदेन व्यवस्थापनीयम् ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

His son will be Sahadeva, then Bruhadashva, his son will be Bhaanuratha, then Prateetaashva, then Suprateeka, then Marudeva, then Sunakshatra, then Kinnara.

VP 4.22.13

इक्ष्वाकूणामयं वंशःसुमित्रान्तो भविष्यति । यतस्तं प्राप्य राजानं संस्थां प्राप्स्यति वै कलौ

This vamsha of Ikshvaakus will end with Sumitra. When Sumitra comes into power as king, the Ikshvaaku vamsha comes to an end in the Kaliyuga. This completes Chapter 22. Maagadha Kings Now, Chapter 23.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ikṣvākūṇām ayam vaṃśaḥ — this lineage of the Ikṣvākus;
sumitrāntaḥ bhaviṣyati — will end with Sumitra;
yataḥ tam prāpya rājānam — after reaching that king;
saṃsthām prāpsyati vai kalau — will attain its end in the Kali age.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ikṣvākūṇāmayaṃ vaṃśaḥsumitrānto bhaviṣyati . yatastaṃ prāpya rājānaṃ saṃsthāṃ prāpsyati vai kalau

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.
