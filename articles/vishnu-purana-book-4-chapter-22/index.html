VP 4.22.1

चतुर्थांशे द्वाविंशोऽध्यायः अतश्चेक्ष्वाकवो भविष्याः पार्थिवाः कथ्यन्ते

Sri Paraasharar - The future kings of Ikshvaaku vamsha will be told now. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

caturathāṃśe — Sri; davāviṃśo'dhayāyaḥ — The; ataśacekaṣavākavo — of; bhaviṣayāḥ — will; pārathivāḥ — now; kathayanate — Puraana

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

caturathāṃśe davāviṃśo'dhayāyaḥ ataśacekaṣavākavo bhaviṣayāḥ pārathivāḥ kathayanate ||1||

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.22.2

बृहद्बलस्य पुत्रो बृहत्क्षणः

Bruhadbala will have son Bruhatkshana.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

baṛhadabalasaya — Bruhadbala; putaro — will; baṛhatakaṣaṇaḥ — son

Śrīdhara

somavaṃśa-bhaviṣayāṇāṃ — Bruhadbala; rājañāmatara — Bruhadbala; parasaṅagataḥ — Bruhadbala; . — Bruhadbala; davāviṃśe — will; sūrayavaṃśayānāṃ — will; bhaviṣayo — will; vaṃśa — will; ucayate — have; . — have; baṛhadabalasayeti-caturathe'dhayāye — have; arajunatanayenābhimanayunā — have; bhārate — son; yudadhe — son; kaṣayamanīyatetayatara — son; ca — son; darasitasatasayetaya — Bruhatkshana; arathaḥ — Bruhatkshana; ||2|| — Bruhatkshana

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

baṛhadabalasaya putaro baṛhatakaṣaṇaḥ ||2||

Śrīdhara

somavaṃśa-bhaviṣayāṇāṃ rājañāmatara parasaṅagataḥ . davāviṃśe sūrayavaṃśayānāṃ bhaviṣayo vaṃśa ucayate . baṛhadabalasayeti-caturathe'dhayāye arajunatanayenābhimanayunā bhārate yudadhe kaṣayamanīyatetayatara ca darasitasatasayetaya arathaḥ ||2||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सोमवंश-भविष्याणां राज्ञामत्र प्रसङ्गतः । द्वाविंशे सूर्यवंश्यानां भविष्यो वंश उच्यते । बृहद्बलस्येति-चतुर्थेऽध्याये अर्जुनतनयेनाभिमन्युना भारते युद्धे क्षयमनीयतेत्यत्र च दर्सितस्तस्येत्य् अर्थः ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 2).

Bruhadbala will have son Bruhatkshana.

VP 4.22.3

VP 4.22.4

तस्मात्सहदेवः सहदेवाद्बृहदश्वस्तत्सूनुर्भानुरथस्तस्य चज प्रतीताश्वस्तस्यापि सुप्रतीकस्ततश्च मरुदेवस्ततः सुनक्षत्रस्तमात्किन्नरः

His son will be Sahadeva, then Bruhadashva, his son will be Bhaanuratha, then Prateetaashva, then Suprateeka, then Marudeva, then Sunakshatra, then Kinnara.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasamātasahadevaḥ — His; sahadevādabaṛhadaśavasatatasūnurabhānurathasatasaya — be; caja — Bruhadashva; paratītāśavasatasayāpi — will; suparatīkasatataśaca — then; marudevasatataḥ — Suprateeka; sunakaṣatarasatamātakinanaraḥ — then

Śrīdhara

yatara — His; tatara — will; nāmanāṃ — Sahadeva; vailakaṣaṇayaṃ — his; daṛśayate, — will; tatara — then; kalapayugādibhedena — then; vayavasathāpanīyama — Marudeva; ||4|| — Sunakshatra

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasamātasahadevaḥ sahadevādabaṛhadaśavasatatasūnurabhānurathasatasaya caja paratītāśavasatasayāpi suparatīkasatataśaca marudevasatataḥ sunakaṣatarasatamātakinanaraḥ ||4||

Śrīdhara

yatara tatara nāmanāṃ vailakaṣaṇayaṃ daṛśayate, tatara kalapayugādibhedena vayavasathāpanīyama ||4||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यत्र तत्र नाम्नां वैलक्षण्यं दृश्यते, तत्र कल्पयुगादिभेदेन व्यवस्थापनीयम् ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 4).

His son will be Sahadeva, then Bruhadashva, his son will be Bhaanuratha, then Prateetaashva, then Suprateeka, then Marudeva, then Sunakshatra, then Kinnara.
