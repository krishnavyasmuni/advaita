VP 4.22.1

अतश्चेक्ष्वाकवो भविष्याः पार्थिवाः कथ्यन्ते

Sri Paraasharar - The future kings of Ikshvaaku vamsha will be told now. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ataścekṣvākavo bhaviṣyāḥ pārthivāḥ kathyante — Sri Paraasharar - The future kings of Ikshvaaku vamsha will be told now. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ataścekṣvākavo bhaviṣyāḥ pārthivāḥ kathyante

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.22.2

बृहद्बलस्य पुत्रो बृहत्क्षणः

Bruhadbala will have son Bruhatkshana.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bṛhada‍्balasya putro bṛhatkṣaṇaḥ — Bruhadbala will have son Bruhatkshana.

Śrīdhara

somavaṃśa-bhaviṣyāṇāṃ rājñāmatra prasaṅgataḥ . dvāviṃśe sūryavaṃśyānāṃ bhaviṣyo vaṃśa ucyate . bṛhadbalasyeti-caturthe’dhyāye arjunatanayenābhimanyunā bhārate yuddhe kṣayamanīyatetyatra ca darsitastasyety arthaḥ — Bruhadbala will have son Bruhatkshana.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bṛhadbalasya putro bṛhatkṣaṇaḥ

Śrīdhara

somavaṃśa-bhaviṣyāṇāṃ rājñāmatra prasaṅgataḥ . dvāviṃśe sūryavaṃśyānāṃ bhaviṣyo vaṃśa ucyate . bṛhadbalasyeti-caturthe’dhyāye arjunatanayenābhimanyunā bhārate yuddhe kṣayamanīyatetyatra ca darsitastasyety arthaḥ ||२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सोमवंश-भविष्याणां राज्ञामत्र प्रसङ्गतः । द्वाविंशे सूर्यवंश्यानां भविष्यो वंश उच्यते । बृहद्बलस्येति-चतुर्थेऽध्याये अर्जुनतनयेनाभिमन्युना भारते युद्धे क्षयमनीयतेत्यत्र च दर्सितस्तस्येत्य् अर्थः ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Bruhadbala will have son Bruhatkshana.

VP 4.22.3

VP 4.22.4

तस्मात्सहदेवः सहदेवाद्बृहदश्वस्तत्सूनुर्भानुरथस्तस्य चज प्रतीताश्वस्तस्यापि सुप्रतीकस्ततश्च मरुदेवस्ततः सुनक्षत्रस्तमात्किन्नरः

His son will be Sahadeva, then Bruhadashva, his son will be Bhaanuratha, then Prateetaashva, then Suprateeka, then Marudeva, then Sunakshatra, then Kinnara.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmātsahadevaḥ sahadevād‍bṛhadaśvastatsūnurbhānurathastasya ca pratītāśvastasyāpi supratīkastataśca marudevastataḥ sunakṣatrastasmātkinnaraḥ — His son will be Sahadeva, then Bruhadashva, his son will be Bhaanuratha, then Prateetaashva, then Suprateeka, then Marudeva, then Sunakshatra, then Kinnara.

Śrīdhara

yatra tatra nāmnāṃ vailakṣaṇyaṃ dṛśyate, tatra kalpayugādibhedena vyavasthāpanīyam — His son will be Sahadeva, then Bruhadashva, his son will be Bhaanuratha, then Prateetaashva, then Suprateeka, then Marudeva, then Sunakshatra, then Kinnara.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmātsahadevaḥ sahadevādbṛhadaśvastatsūnurbhānurathastasya caja pratītāśvastasyāpi supratīkastataśca marudevastataḥ sunakṣatrastamātkinnaraḥ

Śrīdhara

yatra tatra nāmnāṃ vailakṣaṇyaṃ dṛśyate, tatra kalpayugādibhedena vyavasthāpanīyam ||४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यत्र तत्र नाम्नां वैलक्षण्यं दृश्यते, तत्र कल्पयुगादिभेदेन व्यवस्थापनीयम् ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

His son will be Sahadeva, then Bruhadashva, his son will be Bhaanuratha, then Prateetaashva, then Suprateeka, then Marudeva, then Sunakshatra, then Kinnara.
