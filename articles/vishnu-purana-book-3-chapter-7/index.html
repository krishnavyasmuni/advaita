VP 3.7.1

यथा वत्कथितं सर्वं यत्पृष्टोसिं मया गुरो । श्रोतुमिच्छाम्यहं त्वेकं तद्भवान्प्रब्रवीतु मे

Maitreya said: O teacher, you have told me everything I asked about exactly as it is. I want to hear one more thing; please tell me that.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathāvat — exactly; kathitam sarvam — everything told; yat pṛṣṭaḥ asi — what was asked; śrotum icchāmi — I wish to hear; tat prabravītu — may he tell that

Śrīdhara

vedaśākhāpraṇayanamuktaṃ kādibhedataḥ । atha tāntrikadharmaśca setihāso’tra varṇyate । bhūtalādiprasaṅgānuprasaṅgādanuvarṇyate । sadgatistatsthajantūnāṃ bhagavaddharmasaṅgataḥ । uktamabhinandan śrutismṛtisārabhūtaṃ dharmaṃ pṛcchati—yathāvad iti saptabhiḥ — Atha shrutyaadi ukto dharmo bhagavad aaraadhanatayaa anushtito nishreyasaaya iti etat artham yama bhata samvaadam prastouti yathaavat iti | Maitreyar is asking about whatever dharma is told in the Shrutis, Shrutis and other Upabrahmanaas, that which is performed with the idea that it is worship of the Lord, Bhagavaan; and when the anushthaana is done like this, it leads to Moksha, and this is what he wants to know. Related to that, the dialogue which happens between Yama and his bhata, assistant, is being told here.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathā vatkathitaṃ sarvaṃ yatpṛṣṭosiṃ mayā guro । śrotumicchāmyahaṃ tvekaṃ tadbhavānprabravītu me ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

vedaśākhāpraṇayanamuktaṃ kādibhedataḥ । atha tāntrikadharmaśca setihāso’tra varṇyate । bhūtalādiprasaṅgānuprasaṅgādanuvarṇyate । sadgatistatsthajantūnāṃ bhagavaddharmasaṅgataḥ । uktamabhinandan śrutismṛtisārabhūtaṃ dharmaṃ pṛcchati—yathāvad iti saptabhiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वेदशाखाप्रणयनमुक्तं कादिभेदतः । अथ तान्त्रिकधर्मश्च सेतिहासोऽत्र वर्ण्यते । भूतलादिप्रसङ्गानुप्रसङ्गादनुवर्ण्यते । सद्गतिस्तत्स्थजन्तूनां भगवद्धर्मसङ्गतः । उक्तमभिनन्दन् श्रुतिस्मृतिसारभूतं धर्मं पृच्छति—यथावद् इति सप्तभिः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1).

Atha shrutyaadi ukto dharmo bhagavad aaraadhanatayaa anushtito nishreyasaaya iti etat artham yama bhata samvaadam prastouti yathaavat iti | Maitreyar is asking about whatever dharma is told in the Shrutis, Shrutis and other Upabrahmanaas, that which is performed with the idea that it is worship of the Lord, Bhagavaan; and when the anushthaana is done like this, it leads to Moksha, and this is what he wants to know. Related to that, the dialogue which happens between Yama and his bhata, assistant, is being told here.

VP 3.7.2

सप्तद्वीपानि पातालविधयश्च महामुने । स्पतलोकाश्च येन्तस्था ब्रह्माण्डस्यास्य सर्वतः

O great sage, [you have described] the seven islands, the paths of Pātāla, and the seven worlds situated throughout this Brahmāṇḍa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sapta dvīpāni — seven islands; pātāla-vīthayaḥ — paths of Pātāla; sapta lokāḥ — seven worlds; antaḥ-sthāḥ — situated within; brahmāṇḍasya — of the cosmic egg

Śrīdhara

pātālavīthī bhūvivarapaṅktiḥ । sapta lokāḥ svargādayā । etat saptadvīpādirūpaṃ sarvam — The lower regions are the successive subterranean paths; the seven worlds are the heavens and the rest. All this is the whole form comprising the seven islands and related regions.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saptadvīpāni pātālavidhayaśca mahāmune । spatalokāśca yentasthā brahmāṇḍasyāsya sarvataḥ ॥२॥

Śrīdhara

pātālavīthī bhūvivarapaṅktiḥ । sapta lokāḥ svargādayā । etat saptadvīpādirūpaṃ sarvam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पातालवीथी भूविवरपङ्क्तिः । सप्त लोकाः स्वर्गादया । एतत् सप्तद्वीपादिरूपं सर्वम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 2).

Śrīdhara explains that the lower regions are the successive subterranean paths, while the seven worlds are the heavens and the rest. Together these constitute the whole arrangement beginning with the seven islands.

O sage, this very question was once asked by the great Nakula. Bhīṣma, the grandfather, answered him; hear from me what he said.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ayam eva praśnaḥ — this very question; nakulena — by Nakula; pṛṣṭaḥ — asked; pitāmahaḥ — grandfather; bhīṣmaḥ prāha — Bhīṣma said; śṛṇu — hear

Śrīdhara

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ayameva mune praśro nakulena mahātmanā । pṛṣṭaḥ pitāmahaḥ prāha bhīṣmo yattacchṛṇuṣva me ॥८॥

Śrīdhara

ayameva praśnaḥ praṣṭavyo’rtho nakulena bhīṣmaṃ prati pṛṣṭaḥ, tataśca sa bhīṣmastaṃ prati yat prāha tacca śṛṇuṣva

VP 3.7.9

पुरा ममागतो वत्स सखा कालिङ्गको द्विजः । स मामुवाच प्रष्टो वै मया जातिस्मरो मुनिः

Long ago, my friend, the Brāhmaṇa from Kaliṅga came to me and said, “I questioned a sage who remembered his former births.”

VP 3.7.10

तेनाख्यातमिदं सर्वमित्थं चैतद्भविष्यति । तथा च तदभूद्वत्स यथोक्तं तेन धीमता

That sage told me all this exactly, and said that it would happen in this way. It happened just as that wise one had said.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tena ākhyātam — told by him; idam sarvam — all this; ittham bhaviṣyati — it will happen thus; tathā abhūt — it happened so

Śrīdhara

tena jātismareṇa idamidaṃ vartamānametaditthamevaṃlakṣaṇaṃ bhaviṣyatīti ca kāliṅgasthāne yadākhyātaṃ, tena ca kāliṅgakena mahyaṃ yathoktaṃ, tathaivaṃ tadabhūt, he vatsa nakuletyanvayaḥ — Svasya tam prati asya arthasya prashna-kaaranam tasya satyavaaditvam iti aaha tena iti | Idamittham, idaaneem sa deshaantare vartate, etat cha ittham bhavishyati, iti vyavahitam viprakrushtam cha tena yathaa aakhyaatam tat Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 3 tathaa abhoot | Bheeshmar is telling Nakula - Why did I ask this question to Kaalingaka dvija, because whatever I heard from him happened exactly like what he said, and I have realized that he says the truth. Whatever that Kaalingaka said, what he had heard from the muni, that this will happen like this, that this person will be in some other place at this time, this will happen like this in future, whatever is far away, and whatever is going to happen after a time gap, all that whatever he said, everything happened exactly like what he said.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tenākhyātamidaṃ sarvamitthaṃ caitadbhaviṣyati । tathā ca tadabhūdvatsa yathoktaṃ tena dhīmatā ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara

tena jātismareṇa idamidaṃ vartamānametaditthamevaṃlakṣaṇaṃ bhaviṣyatīti ca kāliṅgasthāne yadākhyātaṃ, tena ca kāliṅgakena mahyaṃ yathoktaṃ, tathaivaṃ tadabhūt, he vatsa nakuletyanvayaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तेन जातिस्मरेण इदमिदं वर्तमानमेतदित्थमेवंलक्षणं भविष्यतीति च कालिङ्गस्थाने यदाख्यातं, तेन च कालिङ्गकेन मह्यं यथोक्तं, तथैवं तदभूत्, हे वत्स नकुलेत्यन्वयः

VP 3.7.11

स पृष्टश्च मया भूयः श्रद्दधानेन वै द्विजः । यद्यदाह न तद्दृष्टमन्यथा हि मया क्वचित्

I questioned him again, O twice-born one, trusting his words; whatever he said, I have never found otherwise.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saḥ pṛṣṭaḥ mayā — he was asked by me; śraddadhānena — trusting; yad yad āha — whatever he said; na dṛṣṭam anyathā — never seen otherwise

Śrīdhara

śraddadhānamanoyuktena sa kāliṅgako dvijo bhūyaḥ punaḥ pṛṣṭaḥ san jātismaravākyaṃ yadyadāha, tadanyathā vyabhicāri mayā na dṛṣṭamityativiśvāsārthamuktam — The Kalinga Brahmana, endowed with a faithful mind, was asked again. Whatever he said about the Jātismara’s words was repeated here to establish complete confidence: I had never seen it prove otherwise.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sa pṛṣṭaśca mayā bhūyaḥ śraddadhānena vai dvijaḥ । yadyadāha na taddṛṣṭamanyathā hi mayā kvacit ॥११॥

Śrīdhara

śraddadhānamanoyuktena sa kāliṅgako dvijo bhūyaḥ punaḥ pṛṣṭaḥ san jātismaravākyaṃ yadyadāha, tadanyathā vyabhicāri mayā na dṛṣṭamityativiśvāsārthamuktam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

श्रद्दधानमनोयुक्तेन स कालिङ्गको द्विजो भूयः पुनः पृष्टः सन् जातिस्मरवाक्यं यद्यदाह, तदन्यथा व्यभिचारि मया न दृष्टमित्यतिविश्वासार्थमुक्तम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 11).

Śrīdhara explains that the Kalinga Brahmana was questioned again with a mind full of faith. Whatever he reported from the Jātismara’s teaching had never, in Bhīṣma’s experience, proved otherwise; this is said to establish complete confidence.

VP 3.7.13

जातिस्मरेण कथितो रहस्यः परमो मम । यमकिङ्करयोर्योभूत्संवादस्तं ब्रवीमि ते

Word-for-word
