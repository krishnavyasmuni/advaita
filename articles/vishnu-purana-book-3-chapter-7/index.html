VP 3.7.1

यथावत् कथितं सर्वं यत् पृष्टोऽसि मया गुरो।
श्रोतुम् इच्छाम्य् अहं त्व् एकं तद् भवान् प्रब्रवीतु मे

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वेदशाखाप्रणयनमुक्तं कादिभेदतः । अथ तान्त्रिकधर्मश्च सेतिहासोऽत्र वर्ण्यते । भूतलादिप्रसङ्गानुप्रसङ्गादनुवर्ण्यते । सद्गतिस्तत्स्थजन्तूनां भगवद्धर्मसङ्गतः । उक्तमभिनन्दन् श्रुतिस्मृतिसारभूतं धर्मं पृच्छति—यथावद् इति सप्तभिः

Commentary by Śrīdhara.

The text describes the arrangement of the Vedic branches according to their divisions. It next describes the Tantric duties together with the histories. It continues the account through related topics beginning with the earth and other things. It describes the good destination of the beings there in connection with dharma of the Lord. Maitreya praises the teaching which is the essence of the Śruti and Smṛti and asks about dharma in the seven verses beginning with yathāvat.

VP 3.7.38

Commentary by Śrīdhara.

They are not able to bring that person under control. Keśava-ālambana means one whose support is Keśava. Keśava-āśraya means one who takes refuge in Keśava.

Textual basis and translation notes

Main Sanskrit and Śrīdhara commentary follow the pinned VishvAsa and Vasuki source collection. The chapter mūla is pinned here. Śrīdhara is pinned here. The English translation by Wilson was checked at Wisdom Library and the Internet Sacred Text Archive.

At 3.7.14 the mūla reads sva-puruṣam. The verse text in the Śrīdhara file reads kha-puruṣam while the commentary explains sva-puruṣam. This page follows the mūla verse and the Śrīdhara commentary. At 3.7.2 the mūla reads pātāla-vīthayaḥ. The verse text in the Śrīdhara file reads pātāla-vidhayaḥ. Śrīdhara explains the phrase as pātāla-vīthī meaning a row of cavities beneath the earth. This page follows the mūla verse and the Śrīdhara gloss. At 3.7.20 the text reads nija-varṇa-dharma and does not include āśrama. Varṇa is kept as varṇa and is not translated as caste. At 3.7.23 the Śrīdhara note reads sita-manasam and may refer to sthita-manasam at 3.7.20.

The page follows the Śrīdhara verse reading at 3.7.3, 3.7.15 to 18, 3.7.22, and 3.7.25 where the mūla file differs. At 3.7.3 the mūla reads sūkṣma-sūkṣmaiḥ while the page follows the Śrīdhara reading sūkṣma-sūkṣmāt. At 3.7.15 and 3.7.18 the mūla has optional gaṇa and vara elements. The page follows the shorter Śrīdhara forms amarārcitena and amaragaṇārcita. At 3.7.16 the mūla reads makuṭa and the page follows mukuṭa. At 3.7.17 the mūla reads punar api yānti while the page follows the Śrīdhara reading punar upayānti. At 3.7.22 the mūla has a transcriptional omission in viṣṇu-bhaktam which is restored in the Śrīdhara reading. At 3.7.25 the mūla reads saumya-rūpaḥ and the page follows the Śrīdhara reading saukhya-rūpaḥ. At 3.7.35 Śrīdhara takes tena as the Jātismara. The mūla commentary takes it as the Yama messenger. The English translation follows Śrīdhara.

At 3.7.1 bhavān prabravītu is a respectful request to Parāśara. The Sanskrit uses third-person agreement with the honorific bhavān but the speaker addresses Parāśara as you so the translation says may you tell me. In the opening note by Śrīdhara the verb pṛcchati means Maitreya asks. Maitreya is the subject. That verb does not refer to Śrīdhara.
