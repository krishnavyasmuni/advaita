VP 3.7.1

यथावत्कथितं सर्वं यत्पृष्टोऽसि मया गुरो । श्रोतुमिच्छाम्यहं त्वेकं तद्भवान्प्रब्रवीतु मे

Maitreya said: O teacher, you have told me everything I asked about exactly as it is. I want to hear one more thing; please tell me that.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathāvat kathitaṃ sarvaṃ yat pṛṣṭo'si mayā guro . śrotum icchāmy ahaṃ tv ekaṃ tad bhavān prabravītu me .1. — exactly; kathitam sarvam — everything told; yat pṛṣṭaḥ asi — what was asked; śrotum icchāmi — I wish to hear; tat prabravītu — may he tell that

Śrīdhara

veda-śākhā-praṇayanam — the exposition of the Vedic branches; kādi-bhedataḥ — according to their divisions; atha tāntrika-dharmaḥ — then the tantric duties; sa-itihāsaḥ atra varṇyate — the historical account is described here; bhūtalādi-prasaṅgāt — through the discussion of the earth and other realms; sad-gatiḥ tat-stha-jantūnām — the good destiny of beings dwelling there; bhagavad-dharma-saṅgataḥ — connected with the Lord’s dharma; dharmaṃ pṛcchati — he asks about the dharma.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathāvat kathitaṃ sarvaṃ yat pṛṣṭo'si mayā guro । śrotum icchāmy ahaṃ tv ekaṃ tad bhavān prabravītu me ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

vedaśākhāpraṇayanamuktaṃ kādibhedataḥ । atha tāntrikadharmaśca setihāso’tra varṇyate । bhūtalādiprasaṅgānuprasaṅgādanuvarṇyate । sadgatistatsthajantūnāṃ bhagavaddharmasaṅgataḥ । uktamabhinandan śrutismṛtisārabhūtaṃ dharmaṃ pṛcchati—yathāvad iti saptabhiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वेदशाखाप्रणयनमुक्तं कादिभेदतः । अथ तान्त्रिकधर्मश्च सेतिहासोऽत्र वर्ण्यते । भूतलादिप्रसङ्गानुप्रसङ्गादनुवर्ण्यते । सद्गतिस्तत्स्थजन्तूनां भगवद्धर्मसङ्गतः । उक्तमभिनन्दन् श्रुतिस्मृतिसारभूतं धर्मं पृच्छति—यथावद् इति सप्तभिः

Śrīdhara commentary.

Maitreya said: O teacher, you have told me everything I asked about exactly as it is. I want to hear one more thing; please tell me that.

VP 3.7.2

सप्त द्वीपानि पातालवीथयश्च महामुने । सप्तलोकाश्च येऽन्तस्था ब्रह्माण्डस्यास्य सर्वतः

O great sage, [you have described] the seven islands, the paths of Pātāla, and the seven worlds situated throughout this Brahmāṇḍa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sapta dvīpāni pātālavidhayaś ca mahāmune . saptalokāś ca ye'ntaḥsthā brahmāṇḍasyāsya sarvataḥ .2. — seven islands; pātāla-vīthayaḥ — paths of Pātāla; sapta lokāḥ — seven worlds; antaḥ-sthāḥ — situated within; brahmāṇḍasya — of the cosmic egg

Śrīdhara

pātālavīthī bhūvivarapaṅktiḥ . sapta lokāḥ svargādayā . etat saptadvīpādirūpaṃ sarvam — The lower regions are the successive subterranean paths; the seven worlds are the heavens and the rest. All this is the whole form comprising the seven islands and related regions.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sapta dvīpāni pātālavidhayaś ca mahāmune । saptalokāś ca ye'ntaḥsthā brahmāṇḍasyāsya sarvataḥ ॥२॥

Śrīdhara

pātālavīthī bhūvivarapaṅktiḥ । sapta lokāḥ svargādayā । etat saptadvīpādirūpaṃ sarvam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पातालवीथी भूविवरपङ्क्तिः । सप्त लोकाः स्वर्गादया । एतत् सप्तद्वीपादिरूपं सर्वम्

Śrīdhara commentary.

O great sage, [you have described] the seven islands, the paths of Pātāla, and the seven worlds situated throughout this Brahmāṇḍa.

VP 3.7.8

VP 3.7.9

Long ago, my friend, the Brāhmaṇa from Kaliṅga came to me and said, “I questioned a sage who remembered his former births.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara
