VP 3.3.1

ज्ञातमेतन्मया त्वत्तो यथा सर्वमिदं जगत् । विष्णुर्विष्णौ विष्णुतश्च न परं विद्यते ततः

I have understood from you that this entire world is Viṣṇu, that it exists in Viṣṇu, and that it arises from Viṣṇu. Nothing beyond that is known.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jñātametanmayā tvatto yathā sarvamidaṃ jagat | viṣṇurviṣṇau viṣṇutaśca na paraṃ vidyate tataḥ ||1|| — jñātam etat mayā — this has been understood by me; tvattaḥ — from you; yathā — how; sarvam idam jagat — this entire world; viṣṇuḥ — Viṣṇu; viṣṇau — in Viṣṇu; viṣṇutaḥ ca — and from Viṣṇu; na param vidyate — nothing further is known; tataḥ — beyond that.

Śrīdhara

sthitihetuprasaṅgena muneḥ praśnānusārataḥ । vedavyāsastṛtīye'tra saprapañco'nuvarṇyate । vedavyastiṃ praṣṭuṃ sarvagranthatātparyamanuvadati—jñātam iti । yathā sarvaṃ jagat viṣṇureva, tataḥ paraṃ na vidyate, yathā ca viṣṇo tiṣṭhati, viṣṇauḥ sakāśāt yathā bhavati, tathā tvatto mayā jñātam । anena sarvo'pi kārakādyartho viṣṇāvupalakṣyate — Because the context is the cause of preservation, the sage’s question is answered here with a detailed account of Veda-vyāsa in the third Book. To ask about the division of the Veda, he restates the purpose of the whole work: “I have understood.” Just as the whole world is Viṣṇu alone, there is nothing higher than him; I have understood from you how he abides in Viṣṇu and how it arises from Viṣṇu. By this, all meanings of the grammatical cases and the rest are indicated as relating to Viṣṇu.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jñātametanmayā tvatto yathā sarvamidaṃ jagat | viṣṇurviṣṇau viṣṇutaśca na paraṃ vidyate tataḥ ||1||

Śrīdhara

sthitihetuprasaṅgena muneḥ praśnānusārataḥ । vedavyāsastṛtīye'tra saprapañco'nuvarṇyate । vedavyastiṃ praṣṭuṃ sarvagranthatātparyamanuvadati—jñātam iti । yathā sarvaṃ jagat viṣṇureva, tataḥ paraṃ na vidyate, yathā ca viṣṇo tiṣṭhati, viṣṇauḥ sakāśāt yathā bhavati, tathā tvatto mayā jñātam । anena sarvo'pi kārakādyartho viṣṇāvupalakṣyate ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्थितिहेतुप्रसङ्गेन मुनेः प्रश्नानुसारतः । वेदव्यासस्तृतीयेऽत्र सप्रपञ्चोऽनुवर्ण्यते । वेदव्यस्तिं प्रष्टुं सर्वग्रन्थतात्पर्यमनुवदति—ज्ञातम् इति । यथा सर्वं जगत् विष्णुरेव, ततः परं न विद्यते, यथा च विष्णो तिष्ठति, विष्णौः सकाशात् यथा भवति, तथा त्वत्तो मया ज्ञातम् । अनेन सर्वोऽपि कारकाद्यर्थो विष्णावुपलक्ष्यते ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1).

Because the context is the cause of preservation, the sage’s question is answered here with a detailed account of Veda-vyāsa in the third Book. To ask about the division of the Veda, he restates the purpose of the whole work: “I have understood.” Just as the whole world is Viṣṇu alone, there is nothing higher than him; I have understood from you how he abides in Viṣṇu and how it arises from Viṣṇu. By this, all meanings of the grammatical cases and the rest are indicated as relating to Viṣṇu.

VP 3.3.2

एतत्तु श्रोतुमिच्छामि व्यस्ता वेदा महात्मना । वेदव्यासस्वरूपेण यथा तेन युगेयुगे

Śrīdhara

yathā vedā vyastā iti prakārapraśnaḥ — It is a question about the manner in which the Vedas were divided.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

etattu śrotumicchāmi vyastā vedā mahātmanā | vedavyāsasvarūpeṇa yathā tena yugeyuge ||2||

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यथा वेदा व्यस्ता इति प्रकारप्रश्नः ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 2).

It is a question about the manner in which the Vedas were divided.

VP 3.3.3

tatra tāvat śākhābhedānāṃ saṅkhyāto nāmataśca vistāro vaktumaśakya ityāha—vedadrumasya iti — Here he says that the extent of the branch divisions, by number and by name, cannot be stated.

VP 3.3.6

VP 3.3.7

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ननु अपर एव वेदव्यासाः, कथं भगवान् वेदभेदान् करोति इत्याशङ्क्य—सर्वे भगवत्तनव इत्याह—यय इति ॥७॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 7).

If the Veda-vyāsas were separate beings, how could Bhagavān divide the Vedas? Anticipating that doubt, he says that all are forms of Bhagavān, beginning with “by whatever [body].”

VP 3.3.8

यस्मिन्मन्वन्तरे व्यासा येये स्युस्तान्निबोध मे । यथा च भेदश्शाखानां व्यासेन क्रियते मुने

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 8).

VP 3.3.11

द्वापरे प्रथमे व्यस्तस्स्वयं वेदस्वयंभुवा। द्वितीये द्वापरे चैव वेदव्यासः प्रजापतिः

इदानीं वाच्यवाचकप्रणवब्रह्मण एव अभेदविवक्षया व्याहृत्यादिरूपताम् आह—यद्रूपम् इति सार्द्धैस्त्रिभिः । वासुदेवस्य यत् प्रणवाख्यं रूपमेतदभेदमपि त्रिधा व्याहृतिरूपेण भूरादिलोकत्रयरूपेण च भेदो यस्य तत् । तत्र हेतुमाह स प्रभुः प्रणवात्मा वासुदेव एव विभिन्नाभिर्बुद्धिभिः भिद्यते भिन्नतया प्रतीयते । दृश्यते भिन्नबुद्धिभिरिति पाठे विषमदृष्टिभिः सर्वभूतेष्वभेदोऽसौ दृश्यते अतः भेदमपि तत्स्वरूपम् । त्रिधा भिद्यते इति त्रिधा भेदं ज्ञानात्मभिन्नं त्रिधा भिन्नन्तु भूराद्यात्मनेत्यर्थः ॥२९॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 29).

Now, intending the non-difference of the expressive and expressed Praṇava-Brahman, he states its form as the sacred utterances and the rest, beginning “its form,” in two and a half verses. The form of Vāsudeva called Praṇava is undivided yet has division in the form of the three utterances and the three worlds Bhūr and the rest. The reason is that that Lord, Vāsudeva who is the Praṇava-Self, is perceived as divided by differing intellects. In the reading “is seen by differing minds,” it is seen as undivided in all beings by those who see without distinction; hence difference too belongs to its form. “Divided threefold” means divided in three as knowledge, or divided threefold in the form of Bhūr and the rest.

VP 3.3.30

VP 3.3.31

स भिद्यते वेदमयस्स्ववेदं करोति वेदैर्बहुभिस्सशाखम् ।शाखाप्रणेता स समस्तशाखाज्ञानस्वरूपो भगवाननन्तः
