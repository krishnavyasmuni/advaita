VP 3.3.1

अथ तृतीयोऽध्यायः मैत्रेयः- ज्ञातमेतन्मया त्वत्तो यथा सर्वमिदं जगत् । विष्णुर्विष्णौ विष्णुतश्च न परं विद्यते ततः ॥१॥

Maitreya: Now, from you, your good self, I have realized that the whole world is Vishnu only; everything is established in Vishnu, and the whole world is created or emanating from Vishnu only. There is nobody who is superior to Him. I have understood all these things.

Initially, He only was present as one Veda; after that, He was present as four Vedaas - Rig, Yajus, Saama, Atharva. In each Veda, He creates many branches. He is only present as Veda Vyaasa in every Dvaapara Yuga. He divided the Vedaas, was told. He only is present as all the shaakhaas. That is why the Vedaas are said to be ananta. He is jnyaana svaroopa and is known as ananta.
