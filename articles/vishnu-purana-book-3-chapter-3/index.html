VP 3.3.1

ज्ञातम् एतन् मया त्वत्तो यथा सर्वम् इदं जगत्।
विष्णुर् विष्णौ विष्णुतश् च न परं विद्यते ततः

I have understood from you that this entire world is Viṣṇu, that it exists in Viṣṇu, and that it arises from Viṣṇu. Nothing beyond that is known.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
jñātam etat mayā — this has been understood by me;
tvattaḥ — from you;
yathā — how;
sarvam idam jagat — this entire world;
viṣṇuḥ — Viṣṇu;
viṣṇau — in Viṣṇu;
viṣṇutaḥ ca — and from Viṣṇu;
na param vidyate — nothing further is known;
tataḥ — beyond that.
Śrīdhara
sthiti-hetu-prasaṅgena — in the context of preservation;
muneḥ praśna-anusārataḥ — according to the sage’s question;
veda-vyāsaḥ tṛtīye saprapañcam anuvartanīyate — Veda Vyāsa is described in detail in the Third Book;
jñātam iti — “I have understood”;
sarvaṃ jagat viṣṇuḥ eva — the whole world is Viṣṇu alone;
viṣṇoḥ sakāśāt yathā bhavati — how it arises from Viṣṇu
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jñātametanmayā tvatto yathā sarvamidaṃ jagat | viṣṇurviṣṇau viṣṇutaśca na paraṃ vidyate tataḥ ||1||

Śrīdhara

sthitihetuprasaṅgena muneḥ praśnānusārataḥ । vedavyāsastṛtīye'tra saprapañco'nuvarṇyate । vedavyastiṃ praṣṭuṃ sarvagranthatātparyamanuvadati—jñātam iti । yathā sarvaṃ jagat viṣṇureva, tataḥ paraṃ na vidyate, yathā ca viṣṇo tiṣṭhati, viṣṇauḥ sakāśāt yathā bhavati, tathā tvatto mayā jñātam । anena sarvo'pi kārakādyartho viṣṇāvupalakṣyate ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्थितिहेतुप्रसङ्गेन मुनेः प्रश्नानुसारतः । वेदव्यासस्तृतीयेऽत्र सप्रपञ्चोऽनुवर्ण्यते । वेदव्यस्तिं प्रष्टुं सर्वग्रन्थतात्पर्यमनुवदति—ज्ञातम् इति । यथा सर्वं जगत् विष्णुरेव, ततः परं न विद्यते, यथा च विष्णो तिष्ठति, विष्णौः सकाशात् यथा भवति, तथा त्वत्तो मया ज्ञातम् । अनेन सर्वोऽपि कारकाद्यर्थो विष्णावुपलक्ष्यते ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Because the context is the cause of preservation, the sage’s question is answered here with a detailed account of Veda-vyāsa in the third Book. To ask about the division of the Veda, he restates the purpose of the whole work: “I have understood.” Just as the whole world is Viṣṇu alone, there is nothing higher than him; I have understood from you how he abides in Viṣṇu and how it arises from Viṣṇu. By this, all meanings of the grammatical cases and the rest are indicated as relating to Viṣṇu.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Here, leaving aside the Vyāsas of past and future Manvantaras as too extensive, he intends to speak of the Vyāsas who have passed in the Vaivasvata Manvantara, one future Vyāsa, and the branch divisions.

प्रणवाख्यवासुदेवस्य सर्वात्मत्वं निवेदयति—स भिद्यते इति । स एव भिद्यते ऋगादिरूपेण बहुभिर्भेदैः सशाखम् अनेकप्रकारकशाखाकं वेदमात्मानमेव तत्तत्प्रणेतृरूपः सन् स एव करोति स एव समस्तशाखारूपश्च । तत्र हेतुभूतविशेषणत्रयम् आह—ज्ञानस्वरूपो भगवाननन्त इति । तेन अनन्तशाखारूपत्वं तत्प्रणेतृत्वञ्चोपपद्यते इति ॥३०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

He declares the all-Self nature of Praṇava-Vāsudeva: “he is divided.” He alone is divided into the form of Ṛg and the rest, through many divisions, making the Veda manifold with branches; becoming the form of each promulgator, he alone makes it, and he alone is the form of all the branches. He states three qualifying features that are the reason: Bhagavān is knowledge in nature and infinite. Therefore his being the form of endless branches and their promulgator is established.
