VP 1.4.1

ब्रह्मा नारायणाख्योऽसौ कल्पादौ भगवान् यथा ।
ससर्ज सर्व-भूतानि तदाचक्ष्व महामुने ॥१॥

Tell me, mighty sage, how, in the commencement of the (present) Kalpa, Nārāyaṇa, who is named Brahmā, created all existent things.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahmā nārāyaṇākhyaḥ asau — Brahmā named Nārāyaṇa; kalpādau — at the beginning of the Kalpa; bhagavān — the blessed Lord; yathā — how; sasarja — created; sarva-bhūtāni — all beings; tat ācakṣva — tell that; mahā-mune — O great sage

Śrīdhara

pūrva-upakṣiptam — previously introduced; brāhma-sarga — Brahmic creation; prasaṅgāntaritam — interrupted by another topic; śrotukāmaḥ — wishing to hear; pṛcchati — asks

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahmā nārāyaṇākhyo’sau kalpādau bhagavān yathā |
sasarja sarva-bhūtāni tadācakṣva mahāmune ||1||

Śrīdhara

pūrvopakṣiptam eva brāhmasargaṃ prasaṅgāntaritaṃ śrotukāmo
maitrayaḥ pṛcchati-brahma iti ||1-2||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पूर्वोपक्षिप्तम् एव ब्राह्मसर्गं प्रसङ्गान्तरितं श्रोतुकामो
मैत्रयः पृच्छति-ब्रह्म इति ॥१-२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The interrupted account of Brahmic creation is requested again, having been introduced earlier and displaced by another topic.

VP 1.4.3

अतीत-कल्पावसाने निशासुप्तोत्थितः प्रभुः ।
सत्त्वोद्रिक्तस् तथा ब्रह्मा शून्यं लोकम् अवैक्षत ॥३॥

At the close of the past (or Pādma) Kalpa, the divine Brahmā, endowed with the quality of goodness, awoke from his night of sleep, and beheld the universe void.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

atīta-kalpa-avasāne — at the end of the past Kalpa; niśā-supta-utthitaḥ — awakened from the night’s sleep; prabhuḥ — the Lord; sattva-udriktaḥ — with sattva predominant; brahmā — Brahmā; śūnyam lokam — the empty universe; avaikṣata — beheld

Śrīdhara

pratijñāta-prajā-sarga — promised creation of progeny; bhūmi-uddhāra — raising of the earth; niśāyāṃ suptam — asleep during the night; sattva-atiśaya — predominance of sattva

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

atīta-kalpāvasāne niśāsuptotthitaḥ prabhuḥ |
sattvodriktas tathā brahmā śūnyaṃ lokam avaikṣata ||3||

Śrīdhara

pratijñātaṃ prajāsargaṃ nirūpayiṣyan prathamaṃ tadādhārabhūtāyā bhūmeruddhāraṃ vaktum āha—atītakalpa ity-ādinā | niśāyāṃ suptaṃ prasvāpaḥ, tasmādutthitaḥ | ata eva tadā sattvodriktaḥ | dṛśyate hi nidrāvasāne prabuddhānām prasannakaraṇatvalakṣaṇaḥ sattvātiśayaḥ ||3||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रतिज्ञातं प्रजासर्गं निरूपयिष्यन् प्रथमं तदाधारभूताया भूमेरुद्धारं वक्तुम् आह—अतीतकल्प इत्य्-आदिना । निशायां सुप्तं प्रस्वापः, तस्मादुत्थितः । अत एव तदा सत्त्वोद्रिक्तः । दृश्यते हि निद्रावसाने प्रबुद्धानाम् प्रसन्नकरणत्वलक्षणः सत्त्वातिशयः ॥३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Before describing the promised creation of progeny, Parāśara first speaks of raising the earth, which is its support. Brahmā has awakened from sleep; hence sattva is predominant, as wakefulness brings clarity to the faculties.

VP 1.4.4

नारायणः परोऽचिन्त्यः परेषाम् अपि स प्रभुः ।
ब्रह्म-स्वरूपी भगवान् अनादिः सर्व-सम्भवः ॥४॥

He, the supreme Nārāyaṇa, the incomprehensible, the sovereign of all creatures, invested with the form of Brahmā, the god without beginning, the creator of all things;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nārāyaṇaḥ — Nārāyaṇa; paraḥ — supreme; acintyaḥ — inconceivable; pareṣām api prabhuḥ — Lord even of the highest; brahma-svarūpī — whose form is Brahmā; bhagavān — the Lord; anādiḥ — without beginning; sarva-sambhavaḥ — source of all

Śrīdhara

sarva-sambhavaḥ — creator of the universe

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nārāyaṇaḥ paro’cintyaḥ pareṣām api sa prabhuḥ |
brahma-svarūpī bhagavān anādiḥ sarva-sambhavaḥ ||4||

Śrīdhara

sarvasambhavaḥ viśvasraṣṭā ||4||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सर्वसम्भवः विश्वस्रष्टा ॥४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Sarva-sambhava means the creator of the universe.

VP 1.4.5

इमं चोदाहरन्त्यत्र श्लोकं नारायणं प्रति ।
ब्रह्मस्वरूपिणं देवं जगतः प्रभवाप्ययम् ॥५॥

of whom, with respect to his name Nārāyaṇa, the god who has the form of Brahmā, the imperishable origin of the world, this verse is repeated,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

imam — this; ca — and; udāharanti — they recite; atra — here; ślokam — verse; nārāyaṇam prati — concerning Nārāyaṇa; brahma-svarūpiṇam devam — the god whose form is Brahmā; jagataḥ prabhava-apyayam — origin and dissolution of the world

Śrīdhara

nārāyaṇa-saṃjñā — the name Nārāyaṇa; brahma-svarūpa — whose form is Brahmā

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

imaṃ codāharanty atra ślokaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ prati |
brahma-svarūpiṇaṃ devaṃ jagataḥ prabhavāpyayam ||5||

Śrīdhara

brahmaṇo nārāyaṇasañjñāṃ vyutpādayati-imañca iti | brahmasvarūpiṇaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ prati brahmasvarūpo nārāyaṇa evetyasminnarthe ||5||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ब्रह्मणो नारायणसञ्ज्ञां व्युत्पादयति-इमञ्च इति । ब्रह्मस्वरूपिणं नारायणं प्रति ब्रह्मस्वरूपो नारायण एवेत्यस्मिन्नर्थे ॥५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The relation between Nārāyaṇa and Brahmā is as follows: the verse is addressed to Nārāyaṇa in the sense that Nārāyaṇa himself has Brahmā’s form.

VP 1.4.6

आपो नारा इति प्रोक्ता आपो वै नर-सूनवः ।
अयनं तस्य ताः पूर्वं तेन नारायणः स्मृतः ॥६॥

The waters are called Nārā, because they were the offspring of Nara (the supreme spirit); and as in them his first (Ayana) progress (in the character of Brahmā) took place, he is thence named Nārāyaṇa (he whose place of moving was the waters).

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

āpaḥ — waters; nārāḥ iti proktāḥ — called Nārā; āpaḥ narasya sūnavaḥ — waters born from Nara; ayanam — abode; tasya — his; tāḥ pūrvam — they first; tena — therefore; nārāyaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ — called Nārāyaṇa

Śrīdhara

naraḥ — the Supreme Self; narāḥ — waters born from him; ayana — abode; pūrvam — first

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

āpo nārā iti proktā āpo vai nara-sūnavaḥ |
ayanaṃ tasya tāḥ pūrvaṃ tena nārāyaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ ||6||

Śrīdhara

naratīti naraḥ proktaḥ paramātmā sanātanaḥ | iti vacanāt naraḥ puruṣottamastasmājjātā nārāḥ | taduktam-

tāsvavātsīt svasṛṣṭāsusahasraparivatsarān |
tena nārāyaṇo nāma yadāpaḥ puruṣodbhavāḥ iti ||6||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नरतीति नरः प्रोक्तः परमात्मा सनातनः । इति वचनात् नरः पुरुषोत्तमस्तस्माज्जाता नाराः । तदुक्तम्-

तास्ववात्सीत् स्वसृष्टासुसहस्रपरिवत्सरान् ।
तेन नारायणो नाम यदापः पुरुषोद्भवाः इति ॥६॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Nara is the eternal Supreme Self; nārāḥ are the waters born from him, and because those waters were his first abode, he is called Nārāyaṇa.

VP 1.4.7

तोयान्तःस्थां महीं ज्ञात्वा जगत्य् एकार्णवीकृते ।
अनुमानात् तदुद्धारं कर्तुकामः प्रजापतिः ॥७॥

He, the lord, concluding that within the waters lay the earth, and being desirous to raise it up, created another form for that purpose;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

toya-antaḥ-sthāṃ mahīm — the earth lying within the waters; jagati eka-arṇavī-kṛte — when the world was made one ocean; anumānāt — by inference; tat-uddhāram — its raising; kartukāmaḥ — desiring to do; prajāpatiḥ — the Lord of beings

Śrīdhara

puṣkara-parṇa — lotus leaf; adhiṣṭhāna-anumāna — inference of a support; uddhāra — raising; anyā tanu — another body

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

toyāntaḥsthāṃ mahīṃ jñātvā jagaty ekārṇavī-kṛte |
anumānāt tad-uddhāraṃ kartu-kāmaḥ prajāpatiḥ ||7||

Śrīdhara

tataḥ kimata āha—toyāntar iti pādenadvābhyām | toyasyopari puṣkaraparṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā tadadhiṣṭānānumānāt toyasyāntarmahīṃ jñātvā taduddhāraṃ kartumicchan nārāyaṇo’nyāṃ tanuṃ taduddhārayogyāmakarodity anvayaḥ ||7||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ततः किमत आह—तोयान्तर् इति पादेनद्वाभ्याम् । तोयस्योपरि पुष्करपर्णं दृष्ट्वा तदधिष्टानानुमानात् तोयस्यान्तर्महीं ज्ञात्वा तदुद्धारं कर्तुमिच्छन् नारायणोऽन्यां तनुं तदुद्धारयोग्यामकरोदित्य् अन्वयः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Seeing the lotus leaf upon the water, Brahmā inferred the earth as its support beneath the waters and wished to raise it; Nārāyaṇa therefore made a body suited to that task.

VP 1.4.8

अकरोत् स्वतनूम् अन्यां कल्पादिषु यथा पुरा ।
मत्स्यकूर्मादिकां तद्वद् वाराहं वपुरास्थितः ॥८॥

and as in preceding Kalpas he had assumed the shape of a fish or a tortoise, so in this he took the figure of a boar.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

akarot — made; sva-tanūm anyām — another body of his own; kalpa-ādiṣu — in earlier Kalpas; yathā purā — as before; matsya-kūrma-ādikām — fish, tortoise, and others; tadvat — likewise; vārāhaṃ vapuḥ — a boar body; āsthitaḥ — having assumed

Śrīdhara

Transliteration

पुरा यथा मत्स्यकूर्मादिकां तनुं कल्पादिषु तत्तत्कार्यार्थमकरोत् तद्वत् । तथा च श्रुतिः—सोऽपश्यत् पुष्करपर्णे तिष्ठन्, सोऽमन्यत अस्ति चैतत् यस्मिन्निदमधितिष्ठति इति । स च वाराहं रूपकृत्वा उपन्यमज्जत् । स पृथिवीमप आर्च्छद् । इत्य्-आदि । तदा च वाराहं वपुरास्थितः सन् तोयं प्रविवेश इत्युत्तरेणान्वयः ॥८॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

As in earlier Kalpas he had assumed fish, tortoise, and other forms for their respective purposes, so he took the form of a boar and entered the waters.

VP 1.4.9

वेद-यज्ञ-मयं रूपम् अशेष-जगतः स्थितौ ।

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The boar form is made of Veda and sacrifice; the world is sustained through Vedic sacrifice, and he entered the waters for its preservation.

VP 1.4.11

निरीक्ष्य तं तदा देवी पाटाल-तलम् आगतम् ।
तुष्टाव प्रणता भूत्वा भक्ति-नम्रा वसुन्धरा ॥११॥

The goddess Earth, beholding him thus descending to the subterrene regions, bowed in devout adoration, and thus glorified the god:

nirīkṣya — having seen; tam — him; tadā — then; devī — the goddess; pātāla-talam āgatam — arrived at Pātāla; tuṣṭāva — praised; praṇatā bhūtvā — having bowed; bhakti-namrā — humbled by devotion; vasundharā — Earth

Śrīdhara

vasundharā — Earth; tad-adhiṣṭhātrī devī — goddess presiding over it; tuṣṭāva — praised

Transliteration

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nimitta-mātraṃ muktvaivaṃ nānyat kiñcid apekṣate |
nīyate tapatāṃ śreṣṭha sva-śaktyā vastu vastutām ||52||

Śrīdhara

etad vivṛṇoti-nimittamātram iti | nimittamātrasamavadhāne sati kāraṇātmanā sthitaṃ sūkṣmaṃ vastu svapariṇāmaśaktyaiva vastutāṃ sthūlarūpatāṃ nīyate | nimittād yadanyat tat sthūlarūpapariṇāmārthaṃ nāpekṣate | yathā dhānnādibījeṣu sūkṣātmanā sthitamaṅkurādi parjanye sati svapariṇāmaśaktyaiva tathā pariṇamate tadvat | ato noktadoṣaprasaṅga ity arthaḥ ||52||

iti śrīdharasvāmikṛtāyāmātmaprakāśabhidhāyāṃ viṣṇu-purāṇa-ṭīkāyāṃ prathamāṃśe caturtho’dhyāyaḥ |

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एतद् विवृणोति-निमित्तमात्रम् इति । निमित्तमात्रसमवधाने सति कारणात्मना स्थितं सूक्ष्मं वस्तु स्वपरिणामशक्त्यैव वस्तुतां स्थूलरूपतां नीयते । निमित्ताद् यदन्यत् तत् स्थूलरूपपरिणामार्थं नापेक्षते । यथा धान्नादिबीजेषु सूक्षात्मना स्थितमङ्कुरादि पर्जन्ये सति स्वपरिणामशक्त्यैव तथा परिणमते तद्वत् । अतो नोक्तदोषप्रसङ्ग इत्य् अर्थः ॥५२॥

इति श्रीधरस्वामिकृतायामात्मप्रकाशभिधायां विष्णु-पुराण-टीकायां प्रथमांशे चतुर्थोऽध्यायः ।

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

When the instrumental cause alone is present, subtle matter becomes gross through its own power, as a seed develops into a sprout when rain is present; no additional cause is required.
