VP 3.4.1

आद्यो वेदश्चतुष्पादः शतसाहस्रसम्मितः । ततो दशगुणः कृत्स्नो यज्ञोऽयं सर्वकामधुक्

At first, the Veda had four divisions and comprised one hundred thousand [units]. From it arose this complete, tenfold sacrifice, the fulfiller of every desire.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ādyaḥ — first; vedaḥ — the Veda; catuṣpādaḥ — fourfold, having four divisions; śatasāhasrasammitaḥ — measured in one hundred thousand; tataḥ — from it, thereafter; daśaguṇaḥ — tenfold; kṛtsnaḥ — complete; yajñaḥ ayam — this sacrifice; sarvakāmadhuk — fulfiller of every desire

Śrīdhara

aṣṭāviṃśe dvāpare — in the twenty-eighth Dvāpara; atra — here; kṛṣṇadvaipāyanena eva — by Kṛṣṇa-Dvaipāyana alone; vedāḥ vyastāḥ iti īryate — it is said that the Vedas were divided; ādyaḥ īśvarāt bhūtaḥ — primordial, originating from Īśvara; catuṣpādaḥ — fourfold; ṛgādi-caturbheda-samūha-rūpaḥ — having the form of the collection of four divisions beginning with Ṛg; śata-sāhasra-sammitaḥ — measured at one hundred thousand; tataḥ pravṛttaḥ ayam yajñaḥ — from it this sacrifice proceeded; daśaguṇaḥ daśavidhaḥ — tenfold, of ten kinds; pañcavidhaḥ — fivefold; prakṛti-vikṛti-bhedena — by the distinction of primary and derivative rites; gṛhyoktaiḥ pañcayajñaiḥ saha — together with the five sacrifices taught in the Gṛhya texts

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ādyo vedaścatuṣpādaḥ śatasāhasrasammitaḥ । tato daśaguṇaḥ kṛtsno yajño’yaṃ sarvakāmadhuk ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

aṣṭāviṃśe dvāpare’tra nānyatrāpi prasaṅgataḥ kṛṣṇadvaipāyanenaiva vedā vyastā itīryate । ādya īśvarād bhūtaścatuṣpāda ṛgādicaturbhedasamūharūpaḥ śatasāhasrasambhita ityarthaḥ । tataḥ pravṛtto’yaṃ kṛtsno jño daśaguṇaḥ daśavidhaḥ, agnihotradarśapaurṇamāsacāturmāsyapaśusomā iti pañcavidhaḥ, sa eva prakṛtivikṛtibhedena daśavidha iti । yadvā gṛhyoktaiḥ pañcayajñaiḥ saha daśavidhatvam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अष्टाविंशे द्वापरेऽत्र नान्यत्रापि प्रसङ्गतः कृष्णद्वैपायनेनैव वेदा व्यस्ता इतीर्यते । आद्य ईश्वराद् भूतश्चतुष्पाद ऋगादिचतुर्भेदसमूहरूपः शतसाहस्रसम्भित इत्यर्थः । ततः प्रवृत्तोऽयं कृत्स्नो ज्ञो दशगुणः दशविधः, अग्निहोत्रदर्शपौर्णमासचातुर्मास्यपशुसोमा इति पञ्चविधः, स एव प्रकृतिविकृतिभेदेन दशविध इति । यद्वा गृह्योक्तैः पञ्चयज्ञैः सह दशविधत्वम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1).

Śrīdhara says that here the division of the Vedas is specifically attributed to Kṛṣṇa-Dvaipāyana in the twenty-eighth Dvāpara. “Primordial” means that the Veda originated from Īśvara and had four divisions beginning with the Ṛgveda; it was said to comprise one hundred thousand. From it this complete sacrifice proceeded. It is tenfold: it is fivefold as Agnihotra, Darśapūrṇamāsa, Cāturmāsya, Paśubandha, and Soma, and the distinction between primary and derivative rites makes it tenfold. Alternatively, it is tenfold when the five sacrifices taught in the Gṛhya texts are included.

VP 3.4.2

ततोऽत्र मत्सुतो व्यासो अष्टाविंशतिमेऽन्तरे । वेदमेकं चतुष्पादं चतुर्द्धा व्यभजत्प्रभुः

Then, in this twenty-eighth Dvāpara, my son Vyāsa—the Lord—divided the one fourfold Veda into four parts.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataḥ atra — then, here; mat-sutaḥ — my son; vyāsaḥ — Vyāsa; aṣṭāviṃśatime antare — in the twenty-eighth interval; vedam ekam catuṣpādam — the one fourfold Veda; caturddhā — into four parts; vyabhajat — divided; prabhuḥ — the Lord

Śrīdhara

atra manvantare — in this Manvantara; aṣṭāviṃśatitame dvāpare — in the twenty-eighth Dvāpara; vedam ekam — the one Veda; ṛg-yajuḥ-sāma-atharva-rūpeṇa — in the form of Ṛg, Yajus, Sāman, and Atharvan; pṛthak vyabhajat — divided separately; prabhuḥ — the Lord, in incarnate form

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tato’tra matsuto vyāso aṣṭāviṃśatime’ntare । vedamekaṃ catuṣpādaṃ caturddhā vyabhajatprabhuḥ ॥२॥

Śrīdhara

atra manvantare aṣṭāviṃśatitame dvāpare catuṣpādamekaṃ santaṃ vedaṃ caturddhā ṛgyajuḥsāmātharvarūpeṇa pṛthag vyabhajata । prabhuḥ īśvaraḥ avatārarūpaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अत्र मन्वन्तरे अष्टाविंशतितमे द्वापरे चतुष्पादमेकं सन्तं वेदं चतुर्द्धा ऋग्यजुःसामाथर्वरूपेण पृथग् व्यभजत । प्रभुः ईश्वरः अवताररूपः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 2).

Here, in this Manvantara and in the twenty-eighth Dvāpara, the Lord—an incarnation of Īśvara—divided the single fourfold Veda separately into the forms of Ṛg, Yajus, Sāman, and Atharvan.

VP 3.4.3

यथा च तेन ते व्यस्ता वेदव्यासेन धीमता । वेदास्तथा समस्तैस्तैर्व्यस्ता व्यासेस्तथा मया

Just as the wise Vedavyāsa divided the Vedas, so too did all those Vyāsas, and so did I.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathā — just as; ca — and; tena — by him; te vedāḥ — those Vedas; vyastāḥ — divided; vedavyāsena dhīmatā — by the wise Vedavyāsa; tathā — so; samastaiḥ taiḥ vyāsaiḥ — by all those Vyāsas; tathā mayā — so by me

Śrīdhara

taiḥ pūrvaiḥ samastaiḥ — by all those former [Vyāsas]; mayā ca — and by me; ṣaḍviṃśe — in the twenty-sixth [Dvāpara]; vedāḥ vyastāḥ — the Vedas were divided

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathā ca tena te vyastā vedavyāsena dhīmatā । vedāstathā samastaistairvyastā vyāsestathā mayā ॥३॥

Śrīdhara

taiḥ pūrvaiḥ samastaiḥ mayā ca ṣaḍviṃśe vedā vyastāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तैः पूर्वैः समस्तैः मया च षड्विंशे वेदा व्यस्ताः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 3).

The Vedas were divided by all those earlier Vyāsas collectively, and by me in the twenty-sixth [Dvāpara].

VP 3.4.4

तदनेनैव वेदानां शाखाभेदान्द्विजोत्तम । चतुर्युगेषु रचितान्समस्तेष्ववधारय

Therefore, O best of the twice-born, understand from this example the divisions of the Veda made in every one of the four yugas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tad — therefore; anena eva — by this very [example]; vedānām śākhābhedān — divisions of the Vedas into branches; dvijottama — O best of the twice-born; caturyugeṣu — in the four yugas; racitān — made; samasteṣu — in all; avadhāraya — understand

Śrīdhara

tat tasmāt — therefore; anena — by this; kṛṣṇadvaipāyana-kṛtena vedavibhāgena — by the division of the Veda made by Kṛṣṇa-Dvaipāyana; dṛṣṭāntena — by the example; caturyugeṣu sarveṣu — in all four yugas; vedavibhāgān — divisions of the Veda; pūrvaiḥ kṛtān — made by earlier [Vyāsas]; avadhāraya — understand

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tadanenaiva vedānāṃ śākhābhedāndvijottama । caturyugeṣu racitānsamasteṣvavadhāraya ॥४॥

Śrīdhara

tat tasmāt anena kṛṣṇadvaipāyanakṛtena vedavibhāgena dṛṣṭāntena caturyugeṣu sarveṣvapi vedavibhāgān pūrvaiḥ kṛtān avadhāraya jānīhi

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत् तस्मात् अनेन कृष्णद्वैपायनकृतेन वेदविभागेन दृष्टान्तेन चतुर्युगेषु सर्वेष्वपि वेदविभागान् पूर्वैः कृतान् अवधारय जानीहि

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 4).

Therefore, using the division of the Veda made by Kṛṣṇa-Dvaipāyana as the example, understand that the divisions of the Veda were made by earlier Vyāsas in all four yugas.

VP 3.4.5

कृष्णद्वैपायनं व्यासं विद्धि नारायणं प्रभुम् । को ह्यन्यो भुवि मैत्रेय महाभारतकृद्भवेत्

Know Kṛṣṇa-Dvaipāyana Vyāsa as the Lord Nārāyaṇa. Who else on earth, O Maitreya, could have composed the Mahābhārata?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṛṣṇadvaipāyanam vyāsam — Kṛṣṇa-Dvaipāyana Vyāsa; viddhi — know; nārāyaṇam prabhum — the Lord Nārāyaṇa; kaḥ anyaḥ — who else; hi — indeed; bhuvi — on earth; maitreya — O Maitreya; mahābhāratakṛt — maker of the Mahābhārata; bhavet — could be

Śrīdhara

tadevam — thus; kṛṣṇadvaipāyana-vyāsasya mukhyatvam uktvā — having stated the pre-eminence of Kṛṣṇa-Dvaipāyana Vyāsa; tatra hetum āha — he states the reason there; ataḥ — therefore; asmin manvantare — in this Manvantara; aṣṭāviṃśe eva dvāpare — in the twenty-eighth Dvāpara alone; bhārata-āvirbhāvaḥ — the appearance of the Bhārata; iti gamyate — is understood

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṛṣṇadvaipāyanaṃ vyāsaṃ viddhi nārāyaṇaṃ prabhum । ko hyanyo bhuvi maitreya mahābhāratakṛdbhavet ॥५॥

Śrīdhara

tadevaṃ kṛṣṇadvaipāyanavyāsasya mukhyatvamuktvā tatra hetum āha—kṛṣṇa iti । ataḥ asmin manvantara aṣṭāviṃśe eva dvāpare bhāratāvirbhāva iti gamyate

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तदेवं कृष्णद्वैपायनव्यासस्य मुख्यत्वमुक्त्वा तत्र हेतुम् आह—कृष्ण इति । अतः अस्मिन् मन्वन्तर अष्टाविंशे एव द्वापरे भारताविर्भाव इति गम्यते

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 5).

Having stated the pre-eminence of Kṛṣṇa-Dvaipāyana Vyāsa, the commentator gives the reason: “Kṛṣṇa...” From this it is understood that, in this Manvantara, the Bhārata appeared in the twenty-eighth Dvāpara.

VP 3.4.6

तेन व्यस्ता यथा वेदा मत्पुत्रेण महात्मना । द्वापरे ह्यत्र मैत्रेय तन्मे शृणु यथातथम्

Listen to me, Maitreya, as I tell you exactly how the Vedas were divided by my great-souled son in this Dvāpara.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tena vyastāḥ — divided by him; yathā — how; vedāḥ — the Vedas; mat-putreṇa — by my son; mahātmanā — by the great-souled one; dvāpare — in the Dvāpara; hi atra — indeed here; maitreya — O Maitreya; tat me śṛṇu — hear that from me; yathātatham — exactly as it was

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tena vyastā yathā vedā matputreṇa mahātmanā । dvāpare hyatra maitreya tanme śṛṇu yathātatham ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.4.7

ब्रह्मणा चोदितो व्यासो वेदान्व्यस्तुं प्रचक्रमे । अथ शिष्यान्स जग्राह चतुरो वेदपारगान्

Urged by Brahmā, Vyāsa began to divide the Vedas. He then accepted four disciples who had mastered the Veda.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahmaṇā coditaḥ — urged by Brahmā; vyāsaḥ — Vyāsa; vedān vyastum — to divide the Vedas; pracakrame — began; atha — then; śiṣyān — disciples; sa jagrāha — he accepted; caturaḥ — four; vedapāragaḥ — accomplished in the Veda

Śrīdhara

vedapāragaḥ — one capable of reaching the far shore of the Veda

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahmaṇā codito vyāso vedānvyastuṃ pracakrame । atha śiṣyānsa jagrāha caturo vedapāragān ॥७॥

Śrīdhara

vedapāragān vedasya pāraṃ gantuṃ samarthān

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वेदपारगान् वेदस्य पारं गन्तुं समर्थान्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 7).

“Accomplished in the Veda” means capable of reaching the far shore of the Veda.

VP 3.4.8

ऋग्वेदपाठकं पैलं जग्राह स महामुनिः । वैशंपायननामानं यजुर्वेदस्य चाग्रहीत्

The great sage took Paila as the reciter of the Ṛgveda and accepted Vaiśampāyana for the Yajurveda.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛgvedapāṭhakam — reciter of the Ṛgveda; pailam — Paila; jagrāha — he accepted; saḥ mahāmuniḥ — that great sage; vaiśaṃpāyananāmānam — named Vaiśampāyana; yajurvedasya — of the Yajurveda; ca — and; agrāhīt — accepted

Śrīdhara

ṛgvedaśrāvakam — reciter/hearer of the Ṛgveda; iti samāsa-stham api — although situated in the compound; śrāvaka-padam — the word śrāvaka; agre anukṛṣya yojyam — should be carried forward and construed; pāragam — accomplished; iti kvacit pāṭhaḥ — some readings have [pāragam]

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛgvedapāṭhakaṃ pailaṃ jagrāha sa mahāmuniḥ । vaiśaṃpāyananāmānaṃ yajurvedasya cāgrahīt ॥८॥

Śrīdhara

ṛgvedaśrāvakamiti-samāsasthamapi śrāvakapadamagre’nukṛṣya yojyaṃ pāragam iti kvacit pāṭhaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ऋग्वेदश्रावकमिति-समासस्थमपि श्रावकपदमग्रेऽनुकृष्य योज्यं पारगम् इति क्वचित् पाठः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 8).

Although the word śrāvaka occurs within the compound Ṛgvedaśrāvaka, it should be carried forward and construed with pāragam; some readings have pāragam in that form.

VP 3.4.9

जैमिनं सामवेदस्य तथैवाथर्ववेदवित् । सुमंतुस्तस्य शिष्योऽभूद्वेदव्यासस्य धीमतः

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jaiminam — Jaimini; sāmavedasya — for the Sāmaveda; tathā eva — likewise; atharvavedavit — knower of the Atharvaveda; sumantuḥ — Sumantu; tasya śiṣyaḥ abhūt — became his disciple; vedavyāsasya dhīmataḥ — of wise Vedavyāsa

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jaiminaṃ sāmavedasya tathaivātharvavedavit । sumaṃtustasya śiṣyo’bhūdvedavyāsasya dhīmataḥ ॥९॥

VP 3.4.10

रोमहर्षणनामानं महाबुद्धिं महामुनिः । सूतं जग्राह शिष्यं स इतिहासपुराणयोः

The great sage took the highly intelligent Sūta Romaharṣaṇa as his disciple in the Itihāsa and Purāṇa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

romaharṣaṇanāmānam — named Romaharṣaṇa; mahābuddhim — highly intelligent; mahāmuniḥ — the great sage; sūtam — a Sūta; jagrāha — accepted; śiṣyam — as a disciple; saḥ — he; itihāsapurāṇayoḥ — in Itihāsa and Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

itihāsa-purāṇayoḥ — of Itihāsa and Purāṇa; ārṣyādi — beginning with the archaic construction; bahudhā ākhyānam — varied narrative; devarṣi-carita-āśrayam — resting on accounts of divine sages; itihāsam iti proktam — called Itihāsa; bhaviṣya-adbhuta-dharma-yuk — connected with future, extraordinary, and dharma accounts; sargādi-pañcalakṣaṇam — having five characteristics beginning with creation; daśalakṣaṇam — having ten characteristics; avatāra-bheda-vivakṣayā — when distinctions among incarnations are intended

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

romaharṣaṇanāmānaṃ mahābuddhiṃ mahāmuniḥ । sūtaṃ jagrāha śiṣyaṃ sa itihāsapurāṇayoḥ ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara

itihāsapurāṇayoriti ārṣyādi bahudhākhyānaṃ devarṣicaritāśrayam । itihāsam iti proktaṃ bhaviṣyādbhutadharmayuk । sargādipañcalakṣaṇaṃ sargādyavatārabhedavivakṣayā daśalakṣaṇañca

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इतिहासपुराणयोरिति आर्ष्यादि बहुधाख्यानं देवर्षिचरिताश्रयम् । इतिहासम् इति प्रोक्तं भविष्याद्भुतधर्मयुक् । सर्गादिपञ्चलक्षणं सर्गाद्यवतारभेदविवक्षया दशलक्षणञ्च

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 10).

The expression “in Itihāsa and Purāṇa” is an archaic construction. These are varied narratives resting on accounts of divine sages. Itihāsa is said to be connected with accounts of past and future dharma. It has five characteristics beginning with creation, and ten when distinctions among creation, incarnations, and the like are intended.

VP 3.4.11

एक आसीद्यजुर्वेदस्तं चतुर्द्धा व्यकल्पयत् । चातुर्होत्रमभूत्तस्मिंस्तेन यज्ञमथाकरोत्

There was originally one Yajurveda; he divided it into four, and from it arose the four-priest arrangement through which he performed the sacrifice.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ekaḥ āsīt — there was one; yajurvedaḥ — Yajurveda; tam — that; caturddhā — into four; vyakalpayat — arranged; cāturhotram abhūt — became the four-priest rite; tasmin — in it; tena — by that; yajñam akarot — he performed the sacrifice

Śrīdhara

yajurvedikādhvaryava-kriyā-bāhulyāt — because Adhvaryu actions predominate in the Yajurveda; yājanāt — because of the act of conducting sacrifice; yajuḥ-prādhānyāt — because Yajus predominates; evaṃ uktam — it is said thus; yajurvede — in the Yajurveda; tena yajñam ayuñjata — with it they yoked the sacrifice; catur-hotṛ-kartṛkam karma — the rite performed by four priests; cāturhotram — is the four-priest rite; caturdhā vibhakte vede — in the Veda divided into four; tena yajñam akarot — by that he performed the sacrifice

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

eka āsīdyajurvedastaṃ caturddhā vyakalpayat । cāturhotramabhūttasmiṃstena yajñamathākarot ॥११॥

Śrīdhara

eka āsīditi yajurvedikādhvaryavakriyābāhulyāt pradhānakarmaṇaśca yājanasya tadvihitatvāt yajuḥprādhānyādevamuktam— yacchiṣṭañca yajurveda tena yajñamayuñjata । yājanāddhi yajurveda iti śāstrasya niścayaḥ ॥ iti vāyukteḥ । hotropalakṣitāścatvāra ṛtvijaścatvāro hotāraḥ । taiḥ anuṣṭheyaṃ karma cāturhotram, tat yasminnabhūt, jātaṃ, tena caturddhā vibhaktena vedena yajñam akarot pravartitavān

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एक आसीदिति यजुर्वेदिकाध्वर्यवक्रियाबाहुल्यात् प्रधानकर्मणश्च याजनस्य तद्विहितत्वात् यजुःप्राधान्यादेवमुक्तम्— यच्छिष्टञ्च यजुर्वेद तेन यज्ञमयुञ्जत । याजनाद्धि यजुर्वेद इति शास्त्रस्य निश्चयः ॥ इति वायुक्तेः । होत्रोपलक्षिताश्चत्वार ऋत्विजश्चत्वारो होतारः । तैः अनुष्ठेयं कर्म चातुर्होत्रम्, तत् यस्मिन्नभूत्, जातं, तेन चतुर्द्धा विभक्तेन वेदेन यज्ञम् अकरोत् प्रवर्तितवान्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 11).

The phrase “there was one” is explained as referring to the Yajurveda: because Adhvaryu actions predominate there, because the principal sacrificial act is prescribed there, and because Yajus predominates, the whole Veda is spoken of in that way. The Vāyu text says: “What remains is the Yajurveda; with it they yoked the sacrifice. The Yajurveda is so called because of sacrificial action.” The four priests, identified through the Hotṛ, are the four officiating priests; the rite performed by them is the Cāturhotra. When that rite arose in the Veda divided into four, he performed the sacrifice through it.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

krauṃco vaitālikastadvadbalākaśca mahāmuniḥ । niruktaśca caturtho’bhūdvedavedāṅgapāragaḥ ॥२४॥

Śrīdhara

tacca saṃhitātritayaṃ krauñcādibhyaḥ adāt । caturtho niruktakṛt nāmeti kecit paṭhanti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तच्च संहितात्रितयं क्रौञ्चादिभ्यः अदात् । चतुर्थो निरुक्तकृत् नामेति केचित् पठन्ति

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 24).

He gave those three Saṃhitās to Krauñca and the others. Some readers take “the fourth, the maker of the Nirukta” as the name of the fourth recipient.

VP 3.4.25

VP 3.4.26

इत्येते बह्वृचाः प्रोक्ताः संहिता यैः प्रवर्त्तिताः

These are the Bahvṛcas, named for having propagated the Saṃhitās.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

iti ete — thus these; bahvṛcāḥ — Bahvṛcas; proktāḥ — are called; saṃhitāḥ — the Saṃhitās; yaiḥ — by whom; pravartitāḥ — were propagated

Śrīdhara

anuśākhāḥ — sub-branches; avāntaraśākhāḥ — intermediate branches; bāskaliḥ pailaśiṣyaḥ — Bāṣkali, Paila’s disciple; tisraḥ saṃhitāḥ cakre — made three Saṃhitās; saḥ eva anyāḥ tisraḥ — he made three others as well; tacchiṣyāḥ — his disciples; kālāyani-pramukhāḥ trayaḥ — three led by Kālāyani

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityete bahvṛcāḥ proktāḥ saṃhitā yaiḥ pravarttitāḥ ॥२६॥

Śrīdhara

anuśākhāḥ avāntaraśākhāḥ । bāskaliḥ pailaśiṣyaḥ tisraḥ saṃhitāścake ityuktaṃ, sa evānyāstisraḥ saṃhitāḥ kṛtavān । apara eva śākalyasatīrtho bāskaliḥ tacchiṣyāḥ kālāyanipramukhāstrayaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अनुशाखाः अवान्तरशाखाः । बास्कलिः पैलशिष्यः तिस्रः संहिताश्चके इत्युक्तं, स एवान्यास्तिस्रः संहिताः कृतवान् । अपर एव शाकल्यसतीर्थो बास्कलिः तच्छिष्याः कालायनिप्रमुखास्त्रयः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 26).

“Sub-branches” means intermediate branches. Bāṣkali, Paila’s disciple, is said to have made three Saṃhitās; he made three additional ones as well. There was another Bāṣkali, Śākalya’s fellow student, whose three disciples were led by Kālāyani.
