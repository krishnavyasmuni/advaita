VP 3.4.1

अथ चतुर्थोऽध्यायः श्रीपराशरः- आद्यो वेदश्चतुष्पादः शतसाहस्रसम्मितः । ततो दशगुणः कृत्स्नो यज्ञोऽयं सर्वकामधुक् ॥१॥

Paraśara: Veda which was present as one unit initially, was divided into four; and then hundreds of thousands of branches afterwards. It has dasha guna. It can bestow any desire of anybody. Whatever you desire, you can get from the Vedaas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

It has dasha guna. It can bestow any desire of anybody. Whatever you desire, you can get from the Vedaas.
