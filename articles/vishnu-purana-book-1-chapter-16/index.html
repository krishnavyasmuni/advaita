VP 1.16.1

कथितो भवता वंशो मानवानां महामुने ।
कारणं चास्य जगतो विष्णुर् एव सनातनः ॥१॥

OO Parāśara, you have told me the lineage of the great descendants of Manu and the generations that arose from it. I understand that Viṣṇu alone is the eternal cause of this universe.

kathito bhavatā vaṃśo mānavānāṃ mahāmune | kāraṇaṃ cāsya jagato viṣṇur eva sanātanaḥ ||१ ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उक्तानुवादपूर्वकं प्रह्लादचरितं पृच्छतिकथित इत्यादिना । मानवानाम् उत्तानपादध्रुवादीनाम् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The passage recalls Svāyambhuva Manu’s lineage and the teaching that Viṣṇu is the eternal cause of the world.

The question seeks the reason Prahlāda’s own relatives tried to kill him despite his devotion and wisdom.
