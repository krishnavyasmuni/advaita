VP 1.16.1

कथितो भवता वंशो मानवानां महा-मुने।
कारणं चास्य जगतो विष्णुर् एव सनातनः॥१॥

OO Parāśara, you have told me the lineage of the great descendants of Manu and the generations that arose from it. I understand that Viṣṇu alone is the eternal cause of this universe.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kathito bhavatā vaṃśo mānavānāṃ mahāmune | kāraṇaṃ cāsya jagato viṣṇur eva sanātanaḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara

uktānuvādapūrvakaṃ prahlādacaritaṃ pṛcchati—kathita ityādinā| mānavānām uttānapādadhruvādīnām

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उक्तानुवादपूर्वकं प्रह्लादचरितं पृच्छति—कथित इत्य्आदिना। मानवानाम् उत्तानपादध्रुवादीनाम्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He asks about Prahlāda’s story after the preceding narration, beginning “what was told”—such as the accounts of human beings, Uttānapāda, Dhruva and others.

VP 1.16.16

तद् एतत् कथ्यतां सर्वं विस्तरान् मुनि-सत्तम।
दैत्येश्वरस्य चरितं श्रोतुम् इच्छाम्य् अशेषतः॥१६॥

PPlease tell me all of this in detail. I want to hear the complete story of the lord of the Daityas, Prahlāda.
