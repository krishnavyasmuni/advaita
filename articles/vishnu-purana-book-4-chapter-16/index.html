VP 4.16.1

इत्येष समाप्ततस्ते यदोर्वंशः कथितः

Sri Paraasharar - I have briefly told you about the Yadu vamsha, thus.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityeṣa samāsataste yadorvaṃśaḥ kathitaḥ — Sri Paraasharar - I have briefly told you about the Yadu vamsha, thus.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityeṣa samāptataste yadorvaṃśaḥ kathitaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.16.2

अथ दुर्वसोर्वशमवधारय

Now, I will tell you about Durvasu's vamsha. Listen carefully.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

atha turvasorvaṃśamavadhāraya — Now, I will tell you about Durvasu's vamsha. Listen carefully.

Śrīdhara

evaṃ yadu-vaṃśaṃ savistaram abhidhāya tad-anujasya turvasor vaṃśam āha—turvasor iti yāvat-samāptiḥ . yayāti-śāpād iti, atra yadyapi rājyanarhatva-rūpa eva śāpaḥ pratīyate, tathāpi turvasuṃ prati viśiṣya santaty-uccheda-lakṣaṇaḥ śāpo’pīty eva jñāyate — Now, I will tell you about Durvasu's vamsha. Listen carefully.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

atha durvasorvaśamavadhāraya

Śrīdhara

evaṃ yadu-vaṃśaṃ savistaram abhidhāya tad-anujasya turvasor vaṃśam āha—turvasor iti yāvat-samāptiḥ . yayāti-śāpād iti, atra yadyapi rājyanarhatva-rūpa eva śāpaḥ pratīyate, tathāpi turvasuṃ prati viśiṣya santaty-uccheda-lakṣaṇaḥ śāpo’pīty eva jñāyate ||२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवं यदु-वंशं सविस्तरम् अभिधाय तद्-अनुजस्य तुर्वसोर् वंशम् आह—तुर्वसोर् इति यावत्-समाप्तिः । ययाति-शापाद् इति, अत्र यद्यपि राज्यनर्हत्व-रूप एव शापः प्रतीयते, तथापि तुर्वसुं प्रति विशिष्य सन्तत्य्-उच्छेद-लक्षणः शापोऽपीत्य् एव ज्ञायते ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Now, I will tell you about Durvasu's vamsha. Listen carefully.

VP 4.16.3

दुर्वसोर्वह्निरात्मजः बह्नेर्भार्गः भार्गाद्भानुः ततश्च त्रयीसानुः तस्माच्च करन्दमः तस्यापि मरुत्तः

Durvasu had Vanhi as the son. Vanhi had Bhaarga. Bhaarga had Bhaanu. Then Trayeesaanu, then Karandama. Then Marut.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

turvasorvahnirātmajaḥ, vahnerbhārgo bhārgādbhānustataśca trayīsānustasmācca karandamastasyāpi maruttaḥ — Durvasu had Vanhi as the son. Vanhi had Bhaarga. Bhaarga had Bhaanu. Then Trayeesaanu, then Karandama. Then Marut.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

durvasorvahnirātmajaḥ bahnerbhārgaḥ bhārgādbhānuḥ tataśca trayīsānuḥ tasmācca karandamaḥ tasyāpi maruttaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.
