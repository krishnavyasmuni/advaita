BP 1.82.1

diṇḍir uvāca |
ye tv āditya-dine brahman pūjayanti divākaram |
snāna-dānādikaṃ teṣāṃ kiṃ phalaṃ syād bravītu me || 1 ||

Diṇḍin said: O Brahman, what fruit belongs to those who worship Divākara on the day of Āditya, with bathing, giving, and the other rites? Tell me.
