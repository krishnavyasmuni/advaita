  • Chapter 68 — The Worship of Sūrya and the Fruits of Offerings

BP 1.68.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
वच्मि ते परमं देवं सर्वदेवैश्च पूजितम् ।
आराधयन्ति यं देवं ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेश्वराः ॥ 1 ॥

Brahmā said: I shall tell you of the supreme Deity, worshiped by all the gods, whom Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Maheśvara worship.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said; vacmi — I shall tell; te — to you; paramam devam — supreme Deity; sarva-devaiḥ — by all gods; ca — and; pūjitam — worshiped; ārādhayanti — worship; yam — whom; brahma-viṣṇu-maheśvarāḥ — Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Maheśvara
Transliteration

vacmi te paramaṃ devaṃ sarvadevaiś ca pūjitam |
ārādhayanti yaṃ devaṃ brahmaviṣṇumaheśvarāḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.68.2

पद्माकृतिं सदा ब्रह्मा नलिनैर्गुग्गुलेन तु ।

BP 1.68.3

कटुतैलप्रदानेन स शत्रूञ्जयते नरः ।

BP 1.68.27

मधूकतैलदानेन सौभाग्यं परमं व्रजेत् ।
सम्पूज्य विधिवद्देवं पुष्पधूपादिभिर्बुधः ॥ 27 ॥

BP 1.68.28

BP 1.68.29
