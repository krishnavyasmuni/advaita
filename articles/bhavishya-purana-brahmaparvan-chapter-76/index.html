  • Chapter 76 — Nārada’s Vision of the Solar Retinue

BP 1.76.1

नारद उवाच ।<ब्र/>कदाचित ्पर्यटंल ्लोकान ्सूर्यलोकं अहं गतः ।<ब्र/>तत्र दृष्टो मया सूर्यः सर्वदेवगणैर ्वृतः ॥ १ ॥

Nārada said: Once, while wandering through the worlds, I went to the world of the Sun. There I saw the Sun surrounded by all the hosts of the gods.

Word-for-word
nāradaḥ uvāca — Nārada said;
kadācit — once;
paryaṭan lokān — wandering through the worlds;
sūrya-lokam — to the world of the Sun;

tatra — there;

sūryaḥ — the Sun;
sarva-deva-gaṇaiḥ — by all the hosts of gods;
vṛtaḥ — surrounded.
Transliteration
nārada uvāca | kadācit paryaṭaṃl lokān sūryalokaṃ ahaṃ gataḥ | tatra dṛṣṭo mayā sūryaḥ sarvadevagaṃair vṛtaḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.76.2

Gandharvas, Apsarases, Nāgas, Yakṣas, and Rākṣasas were there; the Gandharvas sang and the Apsarases danced.

BP 1.76.3

रक्षन्त्य ुद्गतशस्त्रास ्तं यक्षराक्षसपन्नगाः ।<ब्र/>ऋचो यजूंषि सामानि मूर्तिमन्तीह सर्वशः ।<ब्र/>तत्कृतैर ्विविधैः स्तोत्रैः स्तुवन्ति ऋषयो रविम ॥ ३ ॥

The Yakṣas, Rākṣasas, and Nāgas guarded him with weapons raised. The Ṛks, Yajus formulas, and Sāmans were present there in embodied form; with hymns of many kinds fashioned from them, the Ṛṣis praised the Sun.

Word-for-word
rakṣanti — protect;

yakṣa-rākṣasa-pannagāḥ — Yakṣas, Rākṣasas, and Nāgas;
ṛcaḥ — Ṛk verses;
yajūṃṣi — Yajus formulas;
sāmāni — Sāmans;

tat-kṛtaiḥ — made from them;
vividhaiḥ stotraiḥ — with various hymns;
stuvanti — praise;
ṛṣayaḥ — the Ṛṣis;
ravim — the Sun.
Transliteration
rakṣanty udgataśastrās taṃ yakṣarākṣasapannagāḥ | ṛco yajūṃṣi sāmāni mūrtimantīha sarvaśaḥ | tatkṛtair vividhaiḥ stotraiḥ stuvanti ṛṣayo ravim || 3 ||

BP 1.76.4

मूर्तिमत्यः स्थितास ्तत्र तिस्रः संध्याः शुभाननाः ।<ब्र/>गृहीतवज्रनाराचाः परिवार्य रविं स्थिताः ॥ ४ ॥

There stood the three Sandhyās in embodied form, beautiful-faced, holding thunderbolts and iron arrows; surrounding the Sun, they remained stationed there.

Transliteration

BP 1.76.5

अरुणा वर्णतः पूर्वा मध्यमा चेन्दुसन्निभा ।<ब्र/>तृतीया क्ष्माजसंकाशा संध्या चैव प्रकीर्तिता ॥ ५ ॥

The first was red in color, the middle one resembled the moon, and the third Sandhyā was said to have the appearance of the earth.

Word-for-word

varṇataḥ — in color;
pūrvā — the first;
madhyamā — the middle one;
ca indu-sannibhā — and resembling the moon;
tṛtīyā — the third;
kṣmāja-saṃkāśā — resembling the earth;
saṃdhyā — Sandhyā;
ca eva prakīrtitā — was indeed proclaimed.
Transliteration
aruṇā varṇataḥ pūrvā madhyamā cendusannibhā | tṛtīyā kṣmājasaṃkāśā saṃdhyā caiva prakīrtitā || 5 ||

BP 1.76.6

आदित्या वसवो रुद्रा मरुतो ऽथाश्विनौ तथा ।<ब्र/>त्रिसंध्यं पूजयन्त्य र्कं तथान्ये च दिवौकसः ॥ ६ ॥

The Ādityas, Vasus, Rudras, Maruts, and the Aśvins, together with other gods of heaven, worshipped Arka at the three Sandhyās.

rudrāḥ — Rudras;
marutaḥ — Maruts;
atha aśvinau tathā — and likewise the two Aśvins;
tri-saṃdhyam — at the three Sandhyās;
pūjayanti — worship;

divaukasaḥ — gods of heaven.
Transliteration
ādityā vasavo rudrā maruto ’thāśvinau tathā | trisaṃdhyaṃ pūjayanty arkaṃ tathānye ca divaukasaḥ || 6 ||

BP 1.76.7

ईरयञ ्जयशब्दं तु इन्द्रस ्तत्रैव तिष्ठति ।<ब्र/>कविश ्च त्र्यम्बको देवस ्त्रिसंध्यं पूजयन्ति वै ॥ ७ ॥

Indra stood there, uttering cries of victory. Kavi and the god Tryambaka worshipped at the three Sandhyās.

Transliteration

BP 1.76.8

दिनादाव म्बुजाकारं पूजयेद म्बुजासनम ।<ब्र/>चक्ररूपं तु मध्याह्ने घृतार्चिः पूजयेत ्सदा ॥ ८ ॥

At the beginning of the day one should worship him seated upon a lotus, in lotus form; at midday one should always worship him in the form of a discus, as a flame of ghee.

Transliteration

BP 1.76.9

पूजयेत ्सगणं रात्रौ विपुलाज्यस्वरूपिणम ।<ब्र/>रविं भक्त्या सदा देवं कंजार्धकृतशेखरः ॥ ९ ॥
