VP 1.12.1

निशम्य तदशेषेण मैत्रेय नृपतेः सुतः ।
निर्जगाम वनात् तस्मात् प्रणिपत्य स तान् ऋषीन् ॥१॥

O Maitreya, that prince Dhruva, listened to all these things completely, and after that bowed down in reverence, did namaskaara to the ṛṣis, and left that forest.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

niśamya tadaśeṣeṇa maitreya nṛpateḥ sutaḥ | nirjagāma vanāt tasmāt praṇipatya sa tān ṛṣīn || 1 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.12.2

कृतकृत्यम् इवात्मानं मन्यमानस् ततो द्विज ।
मधुसञ्ज्ञं महापुण्यं जगाम यमुनातटम् ॥२॥

He felt very happy and as though one who has done what ought to be done, now that he knows what to do to attain what he desires. On the banks of Yamuna river, there was a very sacred place called Madhu; he went and reached that place.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

tasmāt — therefore; puro-pavanāt — from the grove near the city; nirjagāma — he departed.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṛtakṛtyam ivātmānaṃ manyamānas tato dvija | madhusañjñaṃ mahāpuṇyaṃ jagāma yamunātaṭam || 2 ||

Śrīdhara

tasmāt puropavanād nirjagāma ||2||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तस्मात् पुरोपवनाद् निर्जगाम ॥२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Therefore” means that he departed from the grove near the city.

VP 1.12.3

पुनश् च मधुसञ्ज्ञेन दैत्येनाधिष्ठितं यतः ।
ततो मधुवनं नाम्ना ख्यातम् अत्र महीतले ॥३॥

Earlier, that place was occupied by an asura by name Madhu. So, it was called Madhuvana after him; this is well known in this world.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

punaś ca madhusañjñena daityenādhiṣṭhitaṃ yataḥ | tato madhuvanaṃ nāmnā khyātam atra mahītale || 3 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.12.4

हत्वा च लवणं रक्षोमधुपुत्रं महाबलम् ।
शत्रुघ्नो मथुरां नाम पुरीं तत्र चकार वै ॥४॥

Madhu had a son named Lavana who was very powerful. Shatrughna, brother of Sri Rama, killed him, and built a city there called Mathura.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hatvā ca lavaṇaṃ rakṣomadhuputraṃ mahābalam | śatrughno mathurāṃ nāma purīṃ tatra cakāra vai || 4 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.12.5

यत्र वै देवदेवस्य सान्निध्यं हरिमेधसः ।
सर्वपापहरे तस्मिंस् तपस् तीर्थे चकार सः ॥५॥

In that saanidhya of Deva deva, Lord of the lords, Hari is always present. In that holy place, he did penance, that place itself can destroy all our sins.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara
haratīti hariḥ | sarvaduḥkhahāriṇī yadviṣayā medhā sa harimedhāḥ | nityam asi ca prajāmedhayor iti sāntatvam || 5 ||;
Harih harati pāpaani;
Hari destroys all our sins. That Hari is always there all the time.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yatra vai devadevasya sānnidhyaṃ harimedhasaḥ | sarvapāpahare tasmiṃs tapas tīrthe cakāra saḥ || 5 ||

Śrīdhara

haratīti hariḥ | sarvaduḥkhahāriṇī yadviṣayā medhā sa harimedhāḥ | nityam asi ca prajāmedhayor iti sāntatvam || 5 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

हरतीति हरिः । सर्वदुःखहारिणी यद्विषया मेधा स हरिमेधाः । नित्यम् असि च प्रजामेधयोर् इति सान्तत्वम् ॥५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Harih harati pāpaani; Hari destroys all our sins. That Hari is always there all the time.

VP 1.12.6

मरीचिमुख्यैर् मुनिभिर् यथोद्दिष्टम् अभूत् तथा ।
आत्मन्यशेषदेवेशं स्थितं विष्णुम् अमन्यत ॥६॥

He starts meditating intensely on Viṣṇu, as taught by Marīci and the ṛṣis, and as time passes, he meditates so intensely and concentratedly, he started to meditate with his entire mind, as though Viṣṇu was present in his entire mind. He did dhaarana, focussed completely on Viṣṇu, as Marīci and other ṛṣis wanted him to perform.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

marīci-mukhyaiḥ — headed by Marīci; yathā uddiṣṭam — as instructed, as taught; amanyata — he considered, he meditated.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

marīcimukhyair munibhir yathoddiṣṭam abhūt tathā | ātmanyaśeṣadeveśaṃ sthitaṃ viṣṇum amanyata || 6 ||

Śrīdhara

marīci-mukhyair marīci-pramukhaiḥ | yathā uddiṣṭam upadiṣṭam | amanyata dadhyau ||6||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मरीचि-मुख्यैर् मरीचि-प्रमुखैः । यथा उद्दिष्टम् उपदिष्टम् । अमन्यत दध्यौ ॥६॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Headed by Marīci” means headed by Marīci and the principal sages; “as instructed” means as taught; and amanyata means that he meditated.

VP 1.12.7

अनन्यचेतसस् तस्य ध्यायतो भगवान् हरिः ।
सर्वभूतगतो विप्र सर्वभावगतोऽभवत् ॥७॥

He never thought of anything else other than Viṣṇu, and he started meditating thus. At that time, Hari, who was the object of meditation, who is present in all beings, for his mind and thinking, there was nothing else which was the object of his thinking. Viṣṇu became completely the object of his thoughts.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara
sarvabhāvagataḥ sarvabhāvena viśvarūpeṇa taccittatvaṃ gataḥ prāpto’bhavat || 7 ||;
Viṣṇu became the object of his meditation, completely.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ananyacetasas tasya dhyāyato bhagavān hariḥ | sarvabhūtagato vipra sarvabhāvagato’bhavat || 7 ||

Śrīdhara

sarvabhāvagataḥ sarvabhāvena viśvarūpeṇa taccittatvaṃ gataḥ prāpto’bhavat || 7 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सर्वभावगतः सर्वभावेन विश्वरूपेण तच्चित्तत्वं गतः प्राप्तोऽभवत् ॥७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Viṣṇu became the object of his meditation, completely.

VP 1.12.8

मनस्यवस्थिते तस्य विष्णौ मैत्रेय योगिनः ।
न शशाक धरा भारम् उद्वोढुं भूतधारिणी ॥८॥

When Viṣṇu filled the mind of the yogi completely like that, the earth which supports all beings, could not tolerate the weight of Dhruva because Viṣṇu was completely filled in him.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara
tataś ca tasya bhāram udvoḍhuṃ bhūrnāśaknod ity āhamanasyavasthita iti tribhiḥ || 8 ||;
There are ashta siddhis, eight types of siddhis, which are told for Yogis, animaa, mahimaa, garimaa, laghimaa, praapti, praakaamya, eeshatva, vashitva. Garimaa is told, which means that he became very heavy. Earth was not able to tolerate him due to the meditation on Viṣṇu. Animaa mahimaa chaiva garimaa laghimaa tathaa | Praaptih praakaamyam eeshatvam vashitvam cha ashta siddhayah || Yogis can become very subtle and enter into something, they can become very heavy also.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

manasyavasthite tasya viṣṇau maitreya yoginaḥ | na śaśāka dharā bhāram udvoḍhuṃ bhūtadhāriṇī || 8 ||

Śrīdhara

tataś ca tasya bhāram udvoḍhuṃ bhūrnāśaknod ity āhamanasyavasthita iti tribhiḥ || 8 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ततश् च तस्य भारम् उद्वोढुं भूर्नाशक्नोद् इत्य् आहमनस्यवस्थित इति त्रिभिः ॥८॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

There are ashta siddhis, eight types of siddhis, which are told for Yogis, animaa, mahimaa, garimaa, laghimaa, praapti, praakaamya, eeshatva, vashitva. Garimaa is told, which means that he became very heavy. Earth was not able to tolerate him due to the meditation on Viṣṇu. Animaa mahimaa chaiva garimaa laghimaa tathaa | Praaptih praakaamyam eeshatvam vashitvam cha ashta siddhayah || Yogis can become very subtle and enter into something, they can become very heavy also.

VP 1.12.9

वामपादस्थिते तस्मिन् ननामार्धेन मेदिनी ।
द्वितीयं च ननामार्धं क्षितेर् दक्षिणसंस्थिते ॥९॥

When Dhruva was meditating standing, for sometime he would stand on his left leg, and sometime on his right leg. When he was standing on his left leg, that half of the hemisphere of the earth just bent and swung down, not able to tolerate the weight. When he stood on the right leg, the other half of the earth went down, not able to bear the weight.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
vāmena pādena sthite tasmin ardhena ardhaṃ vāmabhāgato medinī nanāma avanatābhūt | dakṣiṇena pādena sthite tasmin kṣiter dvitīyaṃ dakṣiṇam ardhaṃ nanāma || 9 ||
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vāmapādasthite tasmin nanāmārdhena medinī | dvitīyaṃ ca nanāmārdhaṃ kṣiter dakṣiṇasaṃsthite || 9 ||

Śrīdhara

vāmena pādena sthite tasmin ardhena ardhaṃ vāmabhāgato medinī nanāma avanatābhūt | dakṣiṇena pādena sthite tasmin kṣiter dvitīyaṃ dakṣiṇam ardhaṃ nanāma || 9 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वामेन पादेन स्थिते तस्मिन् अर्धेन अर्धं वामभागतो मेदिनी ननाम अवनताभूत् । दक्षिणेन पादेन स्थिते तस्मिन् क्षितेर् द्वितीयं दक्षिणम् अर्धं ननाम ॥९॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

When he stood on the left foot, half of the earth on the left side bent down. When he stood on the right foot, the second, southern half of the earth bent down.

VP 1.12.10

पादाङ्गुष्ठेन सम्पीड्य यदा स वसुधां स्थितः ।
तदा सा वसुधा विप्र चचाल सह पर्वतैः ॥१०॥

After some time, he stood with half of his thumb of his foot, supporting his entire body. Then, the entire earth, with all the mountains, shook up because of not able to tolerate his weight. That is the power of his tapas, meditation.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
yadā ca pādāṅguṣṭhena vasudhāṃ madhyataḥ sampīḍya ākramya sthitaḥ, tadā samastā vasudhā cacāla || 10 ||
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pādāṅguṣṭhena sampīḍya yadā sa vasudhāṃ sthitaḥ | tadā sā vasudhā vipra cacāla saha parvataiḥ || 10 ||

Śrīdhara

yadā ca pādāṅguṣṭhena vasudhāṃ madhyataḥ sampīḍya ākramya sthitaḥ, tadā samastā vasudhā cacāla || 10 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यदा च पादाङ्गुष्ठेन वसुधां मध्यतः सम्पीड्य आक्रम्य स्थितः, तदा समस्ता वसुधा चचाल ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

When he stood after pressing and treading on the earth at its centre with his great toe, the whole earth trembled.

VP 1.12.11

नद्यो नदाः समुद्राश् च सङ्क्षोभं परमं ययुः ।
तत् क्षोभाद् अमराः क्षोभं परं जग्मुर् महामुने ॥११॥

All the oceans, rivers and streams, were completely disturbed. Because of that, the earth got shaken up and disturbed, the gods could not tolerate that, and were highly disturbed and thrown out of balance.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
nadyādayaś ca kṣobhaṃ prāptāḥ || 11 ||
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nadyo nadāḥ samudrāś ca saṅkṣobhaṃ paramaṃ yayuḥ | tat kṣobhād amarāḥ kṣobhaṃ paraṃ jagmur mahāmune || 11 ||

Śrīdhara

nadyādayaś ca kṣobhaṃ prāptāḥ || 11 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नद्यादयश् च क्षोभं प्राप्ताः ॥११॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The rivers and the rest were also agitated.

VP 1.12.12

यामा नाम तदा देवा मैत्रेय परमाकुलाः ।
इन्द्रेण सह सम्मन्त्र्य ध्यानभङ्गं प्रचक्रमुः ॥१२॥

The Yaama gods got worried and disturbed, they discussed with Indra, and they wanted to disturb or break the meditation, concentration of Dhruva.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara
tato devair anekadhā vighne kriyamāṇe’pi tatsaṃhiter avyutthānalakṣaṇaṃ matidārḍhyam āhayāmā nāmetyādinā | evaṃ sarvāsu māyāsv ityataḥ prāktanena granthena || 12 ||;
In the Svaayambhuva manvantara, there were a class of devaas called Yaamaas.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yāmā nāma tadā devā maitreya paramākulāḥ | indreṇa saha sammantrya dhyānabhaṅgaṃ pracakramuḥ || 12 ||

Śrīdhara

tato devair anekadhā vighne kriyamāṇe’pi tatsaṃhiter avyutthānalakṣaṇaṃ matidārḍhyam āhayāmā nāmetyādinā | evaṃ sarvāsu māyāsv ityataḥ prāktanena granthena || 12 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ततो देवैर् अनेकधा विघ्ने क्रियमाणेऽपि तत्संहितेर् अव्युत्थानलक्षणं मतिदार्ढ्यम् आहयामा नामेत्यादिना । एवं सर्वासु मायास्व् इत्यतः प्राक्तनेन ग्रन्थेन ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

In the Svaayambhuva manvantara, there were a class of devaas called Yaamaas.

VP 1.12.13

कुष्माण्डा विविधै रूपैः सहस्रेण महामुने ।
संहितिभङ्गम् अत्यन्तम् आरब्धाः कर्तुम् आतुराः ॥१३॥

At that time, Kooshmaandaas took different forms, along with Indra, and wanted to somehow break his concentration, meditation, tapas. They started to try all kinds of things to break his meditation.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara
kuṣmāṇḍāḥ kecid upadevāḥ || 13 ||;
Kooshmaandaas are another sub class of gods.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kuṣmāṇḍā vividhai rūpaiḥ sahasreṇa mahāmune | saṃhitibhaṅgam atyantam ārabdhāḥ kartum āturāḥ || 13 ||

Śrīdhara

kuṣmāṇḍāḥ kecid upadevāḥ || 13 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कुष्माण्डाः केचिद् उपदेवाः ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Kooshmaandaas are another sub class of gods.

VP 1.12.14

सुनीतिर् नाम तन्माता सास्रा तत्पुरतः स्थिता ।
पुत्रेति करुणां वाचम् आह मायामयी तदा ॥१४॥

At that time, his mother by name Suneeti, with tears in her eyes, stood in front of him. She called Dhruva as "O my son", with a heart breaking tone. She was a Maayaa Suneeti, not the real Suneeti. Kooshmaandaas took on a form like Suneeti, came crying in front of Dhruva and called him with great sorrow.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
sāsnā āśrumukhī satī || 14 ||
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sunītir nāma tanmātā sāsrā tatpurataḥ sthitā | putreti karuṇāṃ vācam āha māyāmayī tadā || 14 ||

Śrīdhara

sāsnā āśrumukhī satī || 14 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सास्ना आश्रुमुखी सती ॥१४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

She stood before him with a tearful face.

VP 1.12.15

पुत्रकास्मान् निवर्तस्व शरीरव्ययदारुणात् ।
निर्बन्धन्तो मया लब्धो बहुभिस् त्वं मनोरथैः ॥१५॥

You are torturing your body with this fierce tapas, fierce meditation, which is very harsh. Please do retire from this. Stop this meditation. I had so much of desire and so many wishes of you, and because of that, I got you as my son.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
śarīrasya vyayena dāruṇāt tīvrād asmāt taponirbandhāt nivartasva || 15 ||
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

putrakāsmān nivartasva śarīravyayadāruṇāt | nirbandhanto mayā labdho bahubhis tvaṃ manorathaiḥ || 15 ||

Śrīdhara

śarīrasya vyayena dāruṇāt tīvrād asmāt taponirbandhāt nivartasva || 15 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

शरीरस्य व्ययेन दारुणात् तीव्राद् अस्मात् तपोनिर्बन्धात् निवर्तस्व ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Turn back from this terrible and intense insistence on austerity, which consumes the body.

VP 1.12.16

दीनाम् एकां परित्यक्तुम् अनाथां न त्वम् अर्हसि ।
सपत्नीवचनाद् वत्स अगतेस् तं गतिर् मम ॥१६॥

I am under so much grief. I have so much sorrow. You should not be deserting me. I do not have anybody to help me look after me. You should not desert me. Listening to the other wife of the king, you have deserted me. I don't have anyone who can take care of me. You only have to look after me.

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

त्रैलोक्याश्रयतां प्राप्तं यत् परं विष्णुपदाख्यं स्थानम् । स्थिरायति स्थिरा नित्या मुक्तिरूपा आयतिरुत्तरकाले भावि फलं यस्मिस्तथाभूतं स्थानं निवासं प्राप । आयतिस्तु स्त्रियां दैवे प्रभावागामिकालयो इत्यभिधानात् । प्राप्तेति वा पाठः । कथं प्राप्ता वरं श्रेष्ठं विष्णुभक्तं ध्रुवं कुक्षिविवरे गर्भे कृत्वा या वरं स्थानं प्राप्ता, तस्या महिमानं वर्णयितुं कः शक्त इत्य् अर्थः ॥१०१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The supreme station called Viṣṇu’s realm has become the support of the three worlds. “Sthirāyati” means a firm, eternal, liberation-like future fruit: he attained such a dwelling. The word āyati is feminine according to the lexicon; another reading is prāpta. The meaning is: who could describe the glory of Sunīti, who, placing Dhruva—the supreme devotee of Viṣṇu—in her womb, attained that excellent station?
