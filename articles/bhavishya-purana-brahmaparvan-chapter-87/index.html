  • Chapter 87 — The Jayanta Observance

BP 1.87.1

ब्रह्मोवाच ।<ब्र/>जयन्तो ह्युत्तरे ज्ञेयश्चायने गणनायक ।<ब्र/>वारो देवस्य यः स्याद्वै तत्र पूज्यो दिवाकरः ॥ १ ॥

Brahmā said: Jayanta is to be understood in the northern course, O lord of hosts; whatever day of the Lord occurs there, on that day Divākara should be worshiped.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
jayantaḥ — Jayanta;
hi — indeed;
uttare — in the northern [course];
jñeyaḥ — to be known;
ca — and;
ayane — in the course, solstice;
gaṇanāyaka — O lord of hosts;
vāraḥ — day;
devasya — of the deity;
yaḥ — which;
syāt — may be;
vai — indeed;
tatra — there;
pūjyaḥ — to be worshiped;
divākaraḥ — Divākara, the Sun.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | jayanto hyuttare jñeyaścāyane gaṇanāyaka | vāro devasya yaḥ syādvai tatra pūjyo divākaraḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.87.2

पूजितस्तत्र देवेशः सहस्रगुणितं फलम ।<ब्र/>ददाति देवशार्दूल स्नानदानादिकर्मणाम ॥ २ ॥

When the Lord of the gods is worshiped there, he grants a thousandfold fruit of acts such as bathing and giving, O tiger among the gods.

Word-for-word
pūjitaḥ — worshiped;
tatra — there;
deveśaḥ — Lord of the gods;
sahasra-guṇitam — thousandfold;
phalam — fruit;
dadāti — gives;
deva-śārdūla — O tiger among the gods;
snāna-dāna-ādi-karmaṇām — of acts such as bathing and giving.
Transliteration
pūjitastatra deveśaḥ sahasraguṇitaṃ phalam | dadāti devaśārdūla snānadānādikarmaṇām || 2 ||

BP 1.87.3

घृतेन पयसा यत्र स्नानमिक्षुरसेन तु ।<ब्र/>विलेपनं कुङ्कुमं तु प्रशस्तं भास्करे प्रियम ॥ ३ ॥

There, bathing with ghee, milk, and sugarcane juice, and anointing with saffron, is praised and is dear to Bhāskara.

Word-for-word

payasā — with milk;

ikṣu-rasena — with sugarcane juice;
tu — and;
vilepanam — anointing;
kuṅkumam — saffron;
tu — indeed;
praśastam — praised, excellent;
bhāskare — for Bhāskara;
priyam — dear.
Transliteration
ghṛtena payasā yatra snānamikṣurasena tu | vilepanaṃ kuṅkumaṃ tu praśastaṃ bhāskare priyam || 3 ||

BP 1.87.4

धूपक्रिया गुग्गुलेन नैवेद्ये मोदकः स्मृतः ।<ब्र/>इत्थं सम्पूज्य देवेशं कुर्याद्धोमं ततस्तिलैः ।<ब्र/>ब्राह्मणान्भोजयेत्पश्चान्मोदकांस्तिलशष्कुलीः ॥ ४ ॥

The incense rite is with guggulu, and modaka is prescribed for the food offering. Having worshiped the Lord of the gods in this way, one should then perform the fire offering with sesame; afterward one should feed the Brāhmaṇas with modakas and sesame cakes.

Word-for-word
dhūpa-kriyā — incense rite;
guggulena — with guggulu;

modakaḥ — modaka;
smṛtaḥ — prescribed, remembered;
ittham — thus;
sampūjya — having fully worshiped;
deveśam — the Lord of the gods;
kuryāt — should perform;
homam — fire offering;
tataḥ — then;
tilaiḥ — with sesame;
brāhmaṇān — the Brāhmaṇas;
bhojayet — should feed;
paścāt — afterward;
modakān — modakas;
tila-śaṣkulīḥ — sesame cakes.
Transliteration
dhūpakriyā guggulena naivedye modakaḥ smṛtaḥ | itthaṃ sampūjya deveśaṃ kuryāddhomaṃ tatastilaiḥ | brāhmaṇānbhojayetpaścānmodakāṃstilaśaṣkulīḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.87.5

इत्थं यः पूजयेद्भानुं मन्त्रेणैव गणाधिप ।<ब्र/>सहस्रगुणितं तस्य फलं देवो ददाति वै ॥ ५ ॥

BP 1.87.6

स्नानदानजपादीनामुपवासस्य वै विभो ॥ ६ ॥

O Lord, [the fruit] of bathing, giving, recitation, and fasting [is multiplied a thousandfold].

Word-for-word

vibho — O Lord.
Transliteration
snānadānajapādīnāmupavāsasya vai vibho || 6 ||
