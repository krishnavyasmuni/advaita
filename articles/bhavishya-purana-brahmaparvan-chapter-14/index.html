Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 14 — Conduct During Absence and Relations Among Co-Wives

BP 1.14.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
प्रेषिते मण्डनं स्त्रीणां पत्यौ मङ्गलमात्रकम् ।
निष्पादनं च यत्नेन तदारब्धस्य कर्मणः ॥

Brahmā said: When her husband is away, a woman’s adornment should be only what is auspicious; she should carefully complete the work he began.

ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
प्रेषिते पत्यौ (preṣite patyau) — when husband is away;
मण्डनम् स्त्रीणाम् (maṇḍanam strīṇām) — women’s adornment;
मङ्गल-मात्रकम् (maṅgala-mātrakam) — limited to auspicious minimum;
निष्पादनम् च यत्नेन (niṣpādanam ca yatnena) — diligent completion;
तद्-आरब्धस्य कर्मणः (tad-ārabdhasya karmaṇaḥ) — of work begun by him

BP 1.14.2

शय्यामुपार्जनमर्थानां व्ययानां परिहापणम् ॥

She should prepare the beds, acquire the household goods, and reduce expenses.

शय्याम् उपार्जनम् (śayyām upārjanam) — maintenance of sleeping arrangements;
अर्थानाम् (arthānām) — of resources;
व्ययानाम् परिहापणम् (vyayānām parihāpaṇam) — reduction of expenditures

BP 1.14.3

व्रतोपवासतात्पर्यं तद्वार्तापरिमार्गणम् ।
दैवज्ञेक्षणिकप्रश्नो देवानामुपयाचनम् ॥

She should be intent on vows and fasts, seek news of him, ask astrologers and diviners, and make offerings to the gods.

व्रत-उपवास-तात्पर्यम् (vrata-upavāsa-tātparyam) — intent on vows and fasts;
तत्-वार्ता-परिमार्गणम् (tat-vārtā-parimārgaṇam) — seeking news of him;
दैव-ज्ञ-ईक्षणिक-प्रश्नः (daiva-jña-īkṣaṇika-praśnaḥ) — questioning astrologers and diviners;
देवानाम् उपयाचनम् (devānām upayācanam) — supplication to gods

BP 1.14.4

नित्यं तस्यागमाशंसा क्षेमार्थं देवपूजनम् ।
न चात्युज्ज्वलवेषत्वं न सदा तैलधारणम् ॥

She should always hope for his return and worship the gods for his welfare; she should not wear excessively bright clothing or apply oil every day.

नित्यम् तस्य आगम-आशंसा (nityam tasya āgama-āśaṃsā) — continual hope for his return;
क्षेम-अर्थम् देव-पूजनम् (kṣema-artham deva-pūjanam) — worship of gods for welfare;
न अति-उज्ज्वल-वेषत्वम् (na ati-ujjvala-veṣatvam) — not excessively brilliant dress;
न सदा तैल-धारणम् (na sadā taila-dhāraṇam) — not daily application of oil

BP 1.14.5

ज्ञातिवेश्म न गन्तव्यं सकामगमनेन च ।
गुरूणामाज्ञया यावद्भर्तुराप्तजनैः सह ॥

She should not go to a relative’s house just because she wants to; she should go with the elders’ permission and with people trusted by her husband.

ज्ञाति-वेश्म न गन्तव्यम् (jñāti-veśma na gantavyam) — should not go to relative’s house;
स-काम-गमनेन (sa-kāma-gamanena) — from personal desire to go;
गुरूणाम् आज्ञया (gurūṇām ājñayā) — with elders’ permission;
यावत् (yāvat) — only then;
भर्तुः आप्त-जनैः सह (bhartuḥ āpta-janaiḥ saha) — with husband’s trusted people

BP 1.14.6

तत्रापि न चिरं तिष्ठेत्स्नानादीन्वापि नाचरेत् ।
यावदर्थं क्षणं स्थित्वा ततः शीघ्रं समाचरेत् ॥

Even there she should not stay long or perform bathing and the like; staying only as long as needed, she should then return quickly.

तत्र अपि न चिरम् तिष्ठेत् (tatra api na ciram tiṣṭhet) — even there should not remain long;
स्नान-आदीन् वा अपि न आचरेत् (snāna-ādīn vā api na ācaret) — should not perform bathing and others;
यावत्-अर्थम् (yāvat-artham) — as long as needed;
क्षणम् स्थित्वा (kṣaṇam sthitvā) — remaining briefly;
ततः शीघ्रम् समाचरेत् (tataḥ śīghram samācaret) — then return quickly

BP 1.14.7

आगते प्रकृतिस्थैव कृत्वा तात्कालिकं विधिम् ।
मुक्तप्रवासने पथ्ये स्नाते भुक्तवति प्रिये ॥

When her husband returns, she should remain composed and perform the rites for that time. When he has rested from travel, taken suitable food, bathed, and eaten, dear one—

आगते (āgate) — when he returns;
प्रकृति-स्था एव (prakṛti-sthā eva) — remaining naturally composed;
कृत्वा तत्-कालिकम् विधिम् (kṛtvā tat-kālikam vidhim) — performing procedure appropriate to time;
मुक्त-प्रवासने (mukta-pravāsane) — after putting aside travel dress;
पथ्ये (pathye) — after suitable refreshment;
स्नाते (snāte) — bathed;
भुक्तवति प्रिये (bhuktavati priye) — beloved having eaten

BP 1.14.8

आत्मानं समलङ्कृत्य सविशेषं मुदान्विता ।
देवपूजोपहारादीन्दद्यात्प्रागुपपादितान् ॥

—she should adorn herself especially, with joy, and give the offerings for worship of the gods and the other things prepared beforehand.

आत्मानम् समलङ्कृत्य (ātmānam samalaṅkṛtya) — adorning herself;
स-विशेषम् (sa-viśeṣam) — especially;
मुदा-अन्विता (mudā-anvitā) — filled with joy;
देव-पूजा-उपहार-आदीन् (deva-pūjā-upahāra-ādīn) — offerings for divine worship and others;
दद्यात् (dadyāt) — should give;
प्राक् उपपादितान् (prāk upapāditān) — prepared beforehand

BP 1.14.9

कनिष्ठामातृवज्ज्येष्ठां तदपत्यानि चात्मवत् ।
पश्येत्तत्परिवर्गं तु नित्यं स्वपरिवर्गवत् ॥

She should regard the younger wife like a mother, her children as her own, and her whole circle always like her own family.

कनिष्ठा (kaniṣṭhā) — younger wife;
मातृ-वत् ज्येष्ठाम् (mātṛ-vat jyeṣṭhām) — elder like mother;
तत्-अपत्यानि च आत्म-वत् (tat-apatyāni ca ātma-vat) — her children like own;
पश्येत् तत्-परिवर्गम् (paśyet tat-parivargam) — should regard her circle;
नित्यम् स्व-परिवर्ग-वत् (nityam sva-parivarga-vat) — always like own circle

BP 1.14.10

तत्पुरोनासने तिष्ठेत्पतिं नामंत्रयेत च ।
तदभिप्रायतः कुर्यात्प्रवृत्तिं सर्वकर्मसु ॥

She should not sit on a seat before her; she should address her husband and carry out all actions according to his intention.

तत्-पुरः न आसने तिष्ठेत् (tat-puraḥ na āsane tiṣṭhet) — should not sit before her;
पतिम् न आमन्त्रयेत् च (patim na āmantrayet ca) — should not independently summon husband;
तत्-अभिप्रायतः (tat-abhiprāyataḥ) — according to her intention;
कुर्यात् प्रवृत्तिम् (kuryāt pravṛttim) — should proceed;
सर्व-कर्मसु (sarva-karmasu) — in all activities

BP 1.14.11

न संसृजेत तद्द्विष्टैः सख्यं कुर्वीत तत्प्रियैः ।
जनमाप्ततमं तस्य सदाभर्तुश्च मानयेत् ॥

She should not associate with those whom the elder wife dislikes; she should befriend those dear to her and always honor the husband’s most trusted person.

न संसृजेत् तत्-द्विष्टैः (na saṃsṛjet tat-dviṣṭaiḥ) — should not associate with those disliked by her;
सख्यम् कुर्वीत तत्-प्रियैः (sakhyam kurvīta tat-priyaiḥ) — should befriend those dear to her;
जनम् आप्ततमम् तस्याः (janam āptatamam tasyāḥ) — person most trusted by her;
सदा भर्तुः च मानयेत् (sadā bhartuḥ ca mānayet) — and husband should always honour

BP 1.14.12

पैतृकात्समुपानीतं वसुसौगंधिकादिकम् ।
तस्मै निवेद्यात्मतया तदा तदुपयोजयेत् ॥

Whatever wealth, perfumes, or the like she has brought from her father’s house, she should disclose to the elder wife and then use from time to time as her own.

पैतृकात् समुपानीतम् (paitṛkāt samupānītam) — brought from paternal home;
वसु (vasu) — property;
सौगन्धिक-आदिकम् (saugandhika-ādikam) — perfumes and others;
तस्यै निवेद्य (tasyai nivedya) — informing her;
आत्मतया (ātmatayā) — as shared or one’s own;
तदा तदा उपयोजयेत् (tadā tadā upayojayet) — should use at appropriate times

BP 1.14.13

सोपि तत्प्रीतये किंचिदादद्यादल्पमूल्यकम् ।
संगोप्य मातृवत्स्थेयं तत्तथैवोपयोजयेत् ॥

The elder wife should accept something of little value for her pleasure; the younger should remain with her as with a mother and use the rest accordingly.

सा अपि तत्-प्रीतये (sā api tat-prītaye) — she also for affection;
किञ्चित् आददीत (kiñcit ādadīta) — may take something;
अल्प-मूल्यकम् (alpa-mūlyakam) — of small value;
संगोप्य (saṃgopya) — preserving;
मातृ-वत् स्थेयम् (mātṛ-vat stheyam) — should remain maternally;
तत् तथा एव उपयोजयेत् (tat tathā eva upayojayet) — should use it likewise

BP 1.14.14

तत्प्रीत्यर्थं गृहीतं यद्वैलक्ष्यादिनिवृत्तये ।
सविशेषं प्रसंगेन तस्यैतत्प्रतिपादयेत् ॥

Whatever she has received to please the elder wife or remove embarrassment should be presented to her with special care when an occasion arises.

तत्-प्रीति-अर्थम् गृहीतम् यत् (tat-prīti-artham gṛhītam yat) — whatever accepted for her pleasure;
वैलक्ष्य-आदि-निवृत्तये (vailakṣya-ādi-nivṛttaye) — to remove embarrassment and similar;
स-विशेषम् (sa-viśeṣam) — with special consideration;
प्रसङ्गेन (prasaṅgena) — on occasion;
तस्यै एतत् प्रतिपादयेत् (tasyai etat pratipādayet) — should present it to her

BP 1.14.15

स्त्रीणां यदेतत्सापत्न्यं परं मात्सर्यकारणम् ।
तस्मात्तत्परिहर्तव्यं परमोदारचर्यया ॥

Because co-wifehood is a great cause of jealousy among women, that should be avoided through conduct of the highest generosity.

स्त्रीणाम् यत् एतत् सापत्न्यम् (strīṇām yat etat sāpatnyam) — this condition of co-wives among women;
परम् मात्सर्य-कारणम् (param mātsarya-kāraṇam) — great cause of jealousy;
तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore;
तत् परिहर्तव्यम् (tat parihartavyam) — it should be avoided;
परम-उदार-चर्यया (parama-udāra-caryayā) — by exceptionally generous conduct

BP 1.14.16

तथा कल्पितनेपथ्या भर्तुः पर्यायवासरे ।
ह्रियमादयमानेव पतिं गच्छेद्विसर्जिता ॥

Properly dressed for her turn with her husband, she should approach him when sent for, as if overcome by modesty.

तथा कल्पित-नेपथ्या (tathā kalpita-nepathyā) — properly arranged in dress;
भर्तुः पर्याय-वासरे (bhartuḥ paryāya-vāsare) — on rotating night with husband;
ह्रियम् आददाना इव (hriyam ādadānā iva) — displaying modesty;
पतिम् गच्छेत् (patim gacchet) — should go to husband;
विसर्जिता (visarjitā) — when released or permitted

BP 1.14.17

गत्वा रहसि भर्तारं तत्कालोचितसंभ्रमैः ।
तद्भावानुगतैस्तैस्तैः सविशेषमुपाचरेत् ॥

Having gone to her husband in private, she should attend to him especially with attentions suited to that time and following his disposition.

गत्वा रहसि भर्तारम् (gatvā rahasi bhartāram) — going privately to husband;
तत्-काल-उचित-सम्भ्रमैः (tat-kāla-ucita-sambhramaiḥ) — with attentions suited to time;
तत्-भाव-अनुगतैः तैः तैः (tat-bhāva-anugataiḥ taiḥ taiḥ) — responsive to his various moods;
स-विशेषम् उपाचरेत् (sa-viśeṣam upācaret) — should serve especially

BP 1.14.18

प्रतिबुध्य ततः काले सविशेषं त्रपान्विता ।
ज्येष्ठाय वसतिं गच्छेद्विशेषेण तथा पुनः ॥

Awakening at the proper time, she should return to the elder wife’s dwelling, especially with modesty, and do likewise again.

प्रतिबुध्य ततः काले (pratibudhya tataḥ kāle) — awakening at proper time;
स-विशेषम् त्रपा-अन्विता (sa-viśeṣam trapā-anvitā) — especially modest;
ज्येष्ठायाः वसतिम् गच्छेत् (jyeṣṭhāyāḥ vasatim gacchet) — should go to elder’s residence;
विशेषेण तथा पुनः (viśeṣeṇa tathā punaḥ) — likewise especially again

BP 1.14.19

अप्रातिकूल्यं ज्येष्ठाया हितमन्यत्र योषितः ।
ततः शनैस्त्ववच्छिद्य पतिं स्ववशमानयेत् ॥

Without opposing the elder wife and while acting for the good of the other women, she should gradually win her husband over to herself.

अप्रातिकूल्यम् ज्येष्ठायाः (aprātikūlyam jyeṣṭhāyāḥ) — not opposing elder;
हितम् अन्यत्र योषितः (hitam anyatra yoṣitaḥ) — welfare toward other women;
ततः शनैः (tataḥ śanaiḥ) — then gradually;
अवच्छिद्य (avacchidya) — distinguishing herself;
पतिम् स्व-वशम् आनयेत् (patim sva-vaśam ānayet) — may bring husband under her influence

BP 1.14.20

बहिष्पाकादियोगेन चतुःषष्ट्या रहोगतम् ।
ज्येष्ठामतिशयानेव भर्तारमुपरञ्जयेत् ॥

Through separate cooking and the like, and by the sixty-four arts in private, she should please her husband, even surpassing the elder wife.

बहिः-पाक-आदि-योगेन (bahiḥ-pāka-ādi-yogena) — through separate cooking and related means;
चतुः-षष्ट्या (catuḥ-ṣaṣṭyā) — through sixty-four arts;
रहः-गतम् (rahaḥ-gatam) — in privacy;
ज्येष्ठाम् अतिशयाना इव (jyeṣṭhām atiśayānā iva) — as if surpassing elder;
भर्तारम् उपरञ्जयेत् (bhartāram uparañjayet) — should delight husband

BP 1.14.21

प्रागल्भ्यं रहसि स्त्रीणां लज्जाधिक्यं ततोऽन्यदा ।
चित्तज्ञानानुवृत्तिश्च पत्यौ संसेवनं परम् ॥

Boldness in private, greater modesty at other times, understanding and following her husband’s mind, and service to him are supreme for women.

प्रागल्भ्यम् रहसि (prāgalbhyam rahasi) — boldness in privacy;
स्त्रीणाम् (strīṇām) — for women;
लज्जा-आधिक्यम् ततः अन्यदा (lajjā-ādhikyam tataḥ anyadā) — greater modesty at other times;
चित्त-ज्ञान-अनुवृत्तिः (citta-jñāna-anuvṛttiḥ) — understanding and following mind;
पत्यौ संसेवनम् परम् (patyau saṃsevanam param) — devoted service to husband is supreme

BP 1.14.22

एवमाराध्य भर्तारं गृहमाक्रम्य च क्रमात् ।
गौरवं प्रतिपत्तिं वा ज्येष्ठादिषु न हापयेत् ॥

Having thus pleased her husband and gradually taken her place in the household, she should not lessen respect or deference toward the elder wife and the others.

एवम् आराध्य भर्तारम् (evam ārādhya bhartāram) — pleasing husband thus;
गृहम् आक्रम्य च क्रमात् (gṛham ākramya ca kramāt) — resuming household place in order;
गौरवम् (gauravam) — respect;
प्रतिपत्तिम् वा (pratipattim vā) — or courteous treatment;
ज्येष्ठ-आदिषु (jyeṣṭha-ādiṣu) — toward elder and others;
न हापयेत् (na hāpayet) — should not diminish

BP 1.14.23

गृहव्यापारदानेषु पतिं गुढं तधा वदेत् ।
अधिकुर्यादनिच्छन्ती ज्येष्ठैवेनां यथा बलात् ॥

In household affairs and giving, she should speak discreetly to her husband; the elder wife should assign responsibility to her, even if she is unwilling, as if compelling her.

गृह-व्यापार-दानेषु (gṛha-vyāpāra-dāneṣu) — in household business and gifts;
पतिम् गूढम् वदेत् (patim gūḍham vadet) — should speak discreetly to husband;
अधिकुर्यात् (adhikuryāt) — should delegate authority;
अनिच्छन्तीम् (anicchantīm) — though reluctant;
ज्येष्ठा एव एनाम् (jyeṣṭhā eva enām) — elder herself to her;
यथा बलात् (yathā balāt) — firmly as appropriate

BP 1.14.24

सापि विज्ञाय भर्तारं कनिष्ठाकृष्टमानसम् ।
विश्रामं प्रार्थयेदेनामधिकुर्यात्सुतामिव ॥

The elder wife, understanding that her husband’s mind is drawn to the younger, should ask her for relief and appoint her as though she were a daughter.

सा अपि विज्ञाय भर्तारम् (sā api vijñāya bhartāram) — she also recognizing husband;
कनिष्ठा-आकृष्ट-मानसम् (kaniṣṭhā-ākṛṣṭa-mānasam) — mind attracted to younger;
विश्रामम् प्रार्थयेत् एनाम् (viśrāmam prārthayet enām) — should ask her for respite;
अधिकुर्यात् सुताम् इव (adhikuryāt sutām iva) — should entrust like daughter

BP 1.14.25

मत्वा भर्तुरभिप्रेतं रक्षन्ती निजगौरवम् ।
कृतं भर्त्रनुकूलं स्यात्तदिष्टायानुमोदयेत् ॥

Understanding what her husband desires and preserving her own dignity, the elder wife should approve what has been done to please him.

मत्वा भर्तुः अभिप्रेतम् (matvā bhartuḥ abhipretam) — understanding husband’s desire;
रक्षन्ती निज-गौरवम् (rakṣantī nija-gauravam) — preserving own dignity;
कृतम् भर्तृ-अनुकूलम् स्यात् (kṛtam bhartṛ-anukūlam syāt) — what is done in harmony with husband;
तत्-इष्टाय अनुमोदयेत् (tat-iṣṭāya anumodayet) — should approve for his pleasure

BP 1.14.26

स्थानिनो यदभिप्रेतं भृत्यैः किं क्रियतेऽन्यथा ।
क्लिश्यते तत्र मूढात्मा परतन्त्रो वृथा जनः ॥

What can servants do otherwise than the wish of their master? A foolish dependent person suffers there in vain.

स्थानिनः यत् अभिप्रेतम् (sthāninaḥ yat abhipretam) — what authority-holder desires;
भृत्यैः किम् क्रियते अन्यथा (bhṛtyaiḥ kim kriyate anyathā) — what can dependants do otherwise;
क्लिश्यते तत्र (kliśyate tatra) — suffers there;
मूढ-आत्मा (mūḍha-ātmā) — foolish person;
पर-तन्त्रः (para-tantraḥ) — dependent;
वृथा जनः (vṛthā janaḥ) — person in vain

BP 1.14.27

तस्मात्सर्वास्ववस्थासु मनोवाक्कायकर्मभिः ।
हितं स्वाम्यनुकूलत्वं नारीणां तु विशेषतः ॥

Therefore, in every circumstance, acting for the master’s good through mind, speech, body, and deeds is beneficial, especially for women.

तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore;
सर्वासु अवस्थासु (sarvāsu avasthāsu) — in all conditions;
मनः-वाक्-काय-कर्मभिः (manaḥ-vāk-kāya-karmabhiḥ) — by mind, speech, body and action;
हितम् (hitam) — beneficial;
स्वामि-अनुकूलत्वम् (svāmi-anukūlatvam) — conformity with master;
नारीणाम् तु विशेषतः (nārīṇām tu viśeṣataḥ) — especially for women

BP 1.14.28

सापि ज्येष्ठापतिं चैव गृहतन्त्रं च सर्वदा ।
समावर्ज्य गुणैर्धीरा प्रागवस्थां न विस्मरेत् ॥

She too, being composed, should always win over the elder wife, her husband, and the household order through her virtues, and should not forget her former position.

सा अपि (sā api) — she also;
ज्येष्ठा-पतिम् च एव (jyeṣṭhā-patim ca eva) — elder wife and husband;
गृह-तन्त्रम् च (gṛha-tantram ca) — household order;
सर्वदा समावर्ज्य (sarvadā samāvarjya) — continually winning over;
गुणैः (guṇaiḥ) — through virtues;
धीरा (dhīrā) — composed;
प्राक्-अवस्थाम् न विस्मरेत् (prāk-avasthām na vismaret) — should not forget earlier status

BP 1.14.29

न सौभाग्यमदं कुर्यान्न चौद्धत्यादिविक्रियाम् ।
नितरामानतिं गच्छेत्सदानार्यभयादिव ॥

She should not become proud of her good fortune or show arrogance and the like; she should especially remain humble, as if fearing the noble women.

न सौभाग्य-मदम् कुर्यात् (na saubhāgya-madam kuryāt) — should not show pride of good fortune;
न च औद्धत्य-आदि-विक्रियाम् (na ca auddhatya-ādi-vikriyām) — nor haughtiness and similar change;
नितराम् आनतिम् गच्छेत् (nitarām ānatim gacchet) — should remain especially deferential;
सदा नारी-भयात् इव (sadā nārī-bhayāt iva) — as if always mindful of elder woman

BP 1.14.30

यथा योग्यतया पत्यौ सौभाग्यमभिवर्धते ।
स्पर्धयेच्य कुलस्त्रीणां प्रश्रयोपाधिकं तथा ॥

As good fortune with the husband increases through proper conduct, so should rivalry among women of good families be qualified by deference.

यथा योग्यतया (yathā yogyatayā) — as through suitability;
पत्यौ सौभाग्यम् अभिवर्धते (patyau saubhāgyam abhivardhate) — favour with husband increases;
स्पर्धया च (spardhayā ca) — and through rivalry;
कुल-स्त्रीणाम् (kula-strīṇām) — among women of established families;
प्रश्रय-उपाधिकम् तथा (praśraya-upādhikam tathā) — likewise qualified by humility

BP 1.14.31

एवमाराध्य भर्तारं तत्कार्येष्वप्रमादिनी ।
पूज्यानां पूजने नित्यं भृत्यानां भरणेषु च ॥

Having thus pleased her husband and remained attentive to his affairs, she should always honor those worthy of honor and provide for the servants—

एवम् आराध्य भर्तारम् (evam ārādhya bhartāram) — pleasing husband thus;
तत्-कार्येषु अप्रमादिनी (tat-kāryeṣu apramādinī) — attentive in his affairs;
पूज्यानाम् पूजने नित्यम् (pūjyānām pūjane nityam) — continually honouring the honourable;
भृत्यानाम् भरणेषु च (bhṛtyānām bharaṇeṣu ca) — and maintaining servants

BP 1.14.32

गुणानामर्जने नित्यं शीलवत्परिरक्षणे ।
प्रेत्य चेह च निर्द्वन्द्वं सुखमाप्नोत्यनुत्तमम् ॥

—always acquiring virtues and carefully guarding good conduct, she attains unsurpassed happiness, free from conflict, both here and after death.

गुणानाम् अर्जने नित्यम् (guṇānām arjane nityam) — continually acquiring virtues;
शीलवत् परिरक्षणे (śīlavat parirakṣaṇe) — carefully protecting character;
प्रेत्य च इह च (pretya ca iha ca) — after death and here;
निर्द्वन्द्वम् (nirdvandvam) — free from conflict;
सुखम् आप्नोति अनुत्तमम् (sukham āpnoti anuttamam) — obtains unsurpassed happiness
