यद्य् अन्यथा प्रवर्तेयं देवदेव ततो मयि ।
न्याय्यो दण्डनिपातो वै तवैव वचनं यथा

“If I were to act otherwise, O Lord of gods, then punishment falling upon me would be just, according to your own command.”

Śrīdhara translation

“Your own command” means your words in Śruti, Smṛti, and the like, such as: “For the protection of the good, the destruction of evildoers, and the establishment of dharma, I come into being age after age,” and so on.

VP 5.7.81

गते सर्पे परिष्वज्य मृतं पुनर् इवागतम् ।
गोपा मूर्धनिहार्देनसिषिचुर्नेत्रजैर् जलैः

When the serpent had gone, the cowherds embraced Kṛṣṇa, as if he had returned from the dead, and joyfully sprinkled his head with tears.

Śrīdhara translation

“Gone” means the cowherds, beginning with Nanda. They sprinkled him with affection.

VP 5.7.82

कृष्णमक्लिष्टकर्माणमन्ये विस्मितचेतसः ।
तुष्टुवुर्मुदिता गोपा दृष्ट्वा शिवजलां नदीम्

Other cowherds, delighted and astonished, praised Kṛṣṇa, whose deeds are effortless, when they saw the river made pure.

“Kṛṣṇa” refers to the other cowherds of the same age, such as Śrīdāma; they praised him.
