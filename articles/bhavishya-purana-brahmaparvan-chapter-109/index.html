  • Chapter 109 — Mārtaṇḍa Saptamī

BP 1.109.1

brahmovāca |
mārtaṇḍasaptamīṃ kṛṣṇa yathānyāṃ vakṣyāmi te'nagha |
śṛṇuṣvaikamanā vīra gadato me śubhapradām || 1 ||

Brahmā said: I shall tell you of another Mārtaṇḍa Saptamī, O Kṛṣṇa, O sinless one; listen with one-pointed mind, O hero, to me as I speak of the giver of auspiciousness.

vakṣyāmi — I will tell;
te — to you;
anagha — O sinless one;
śṛṇuṣva — listen;
eka-manaḥ — with one mind;
vīra — O hero;
gadataḥ — speaking;

śubha-pradām — bestowing auspiciousness
Transliteration
brahmovāca | mārtaṇḍasaptamīṃ kṛṣṇa yathānyāṃ vakṣyāmi te'nagha | śṛṇuṣvaikamanā vīra gadato me śubhapradām || 1 ||

BP 1.109.2

pauṣe māse site pakṣe saptamyāṃ samupoṣitaḥ || 2 ||

BP 1.109.3

BP 1.109.4

dhūpapuṣpopahārādyairupavāsaiḥ samāhitaḥ |
mārtaṇḍeti japaṃnnāma punastadgatamānasaḥ || 4 ||

BP 1.109.6

pāṣaṇḍādīvikarmasthairālāpaṃ ca vivarjayet |

BP 1.109.7

BP 1.109.13

BP 1.109.14

tasmāttvamādideveśaṃ graheśaṃ bhāskaraṃ ravim |
