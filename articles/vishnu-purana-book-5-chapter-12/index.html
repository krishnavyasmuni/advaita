VP 5.12.1

धृते गोवर्धने शैले परित्राते च गोकुले।
रोचयामास कृष्णस्य दर्शनं पाकशासनः

Sri Paraasharar - When the Govardhana was lifted and held by Krishna, and all the Gokula was completely protected, from the rains, at that time, Indra wanted to see Krishna.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dhṛte govardhane śaile paritrāte ca gokule . rocayāmāsa kṛṣṇasya darśanaṃ pākaśāsanaḥ — Sri Paraasharar - When the Govardhana was lifted and held by Krishna, and all the Gokula was completely protected, from the rains, at that time, Indra wanted to see Krishna.

Śrīdhara

dhṛta iti . rocayāmāsa aicchat — Sri Paraasharar - When the Govardhana was lifted and held by Krishna, and all the Gokula was completely protected, from the rains, at that time, Indra wanted to see Krishna.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dhṛte govardhane śaile paritrāte ca gokule . rocayāmāsa kṛṣṇasya darśanaṃ pākaśāsanaḥ

Śrīdhara

dhṛta iti . rocayāmāsa aicchat ||१|| so’dhiruhya mahā-nāgam airāvatam amitrajit .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

धृत इति । रोचयामास ऐच्छत् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - When the Govardhana was lifted and held by Krishna, and all the Gokula was completely protected, from the rains, at that time, Indra wanted to see Krishna.

VP 5.12.3

चारयन्तं महा-वीर्यं गास्तु२२१गोप-वपुर्-धरम्।
कृत्स्नस्य जगतो गोपं वृतं गोप-कुमारकैः

Krishna was looking after the cows, He was the most valiant of all, having taken the form of a gopa. One who protects all the worlds, is being surrounded by all the gopa boys, He was taking care of the cows. Indra sees this wonderful sight.

One who protects all the worlds, is being surrounded by all the gopa boys, He was taking care of the cows. Indra sees this wonderful sight.

VP 5.12.6

इन्द्र उवाच— कृष्ण कृष्ण शृणुष्वेदं यद्-अर्थमहम् आगतः।
त्वत्-समीपं महा-बाहोनैतच् चिन्त्यं त्वयान्यथा

Indra - Krishna, please listen to me, why I have come. Don't think that I have done with a wrong intention, please don't think like that, please don't mistake me. Do listen to me, why I have come here.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa śṛṇuṣvedaṃ yadarthamahamāgataḥ . tvatsamīpaṃ mahābāho naitaccintyaṃ tvayānyathā — Indra - Krishna, please listen to me, why I have come. Don't think that I have done with a wrong intention, please don't think like that, please don't mistake me. Do listen to me, why I have come here.

Śrīdhara

Don't think that I have done with a wrong intention, please don't think like that, please don't mistake me. Do listen to me, why I have come here.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṛṣṇakṛṣṇa śṛṇuṣvedaṃ yadartha mahamāgataḥ . tvatsamīpaṃ mahābāho naitaccintyaṃ tvayā’nyathā

Śrīdhara

kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇeti . tvat-samīpam aham āgato yad-arthaṃ tavābhiṣekārthaṃ tad idaṃ śṛṇuṣva . anyathā virodha-buddhyeti na cintyam ||६|| bhārāvataraṇārthāya pṛthivyāḥ pṛthivī-tale२२३.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कृष्ण कृष्णेति । त्वत्-समीपम् अहम् आगतो यद्-अर्थं तवाभिषेकार्थं तद् इदं शृणुष्व । अन्यथा विरोध-बुद्ध्येति न चिन्त्यम् ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Indra - Krishna, please listen to me, why I have come. Don't think that I have done with a wrong intention, please don't think like that, please don't mistake me. Do listen to me, why I have come here.

VP 5.12.7

भारावतरणार्थाय पृथिव्याः पृथिवी-तले२२३।
अवतीर्णोऽखिलाधार त्वम् एव परमेश्वर

O Krishna, You are the Supreme Lord. In order to reduce the weight of the evil on this earth, You have incarnated here as a gopa. You are the support of the whole world, the entire world.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

kāryaṃ vijñāpayiṣyan prathamaṃ tāvat kṛṣṇaiśvaryam abhinandati bhārāvataraṇāyety-ādi caturbhiḥ — O Krishna, You are the Supreme Lord. In order to reduce the weight of the evil on this earth, You have incarnated here as a gopa. You are the support of the whole world, the entire world.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhārāvatāraṇārthāya pṛthivyāḥ pṛthivītale . avatīrṇo’khilādhāra tvameva parameśvara

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कार्यं विज्ञापयिष्यन् प्रथमं तावत् कृष्णैश्वर्यम् अभिनन्दति भारावतरणायेत्य्-आदि चतुर्भिः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

O Krishna, You are the Supreme Lord. In order to reduce the weight of the evil on this earth, You have incarnated here as a gopa. You are the support of the whole world, the entire world.

VP 5.12.8

मख-भङ्ग-विरोधेनमया गोकुल-नाशकाः।
समादिष्टा महा-मेघास् तैश् चैतत् कदनं कृतम्

I had only ordered the clouds, in order to protect against the stoppage of that yaaga. In order to destroy the Gokula, I had ordered the clouds here. This is what the clouds did as per my order.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

makhabhaṃgavirodhena mayā gokulanāśakāḥ . samādiṣṭā mahāmeghāstaiścedaṃ kadanaṃ kṛtam — I had only ordered the clouds, in order to protect against the stoppage of that yaaga. In order to destroy the Gokula, I had ordered the clouds here. This is what the clouds did as per my order.

Śrīdhara

mayi gokula-vadhāyodyate’pi tad-rakṣaṇāt ahaṃ toṣito’smīty āha—makhabhaṅgeti . kadanaṃ vimardaḥ — I had only ordered the clouds, in order to protect against the stoppage of that yaaga. In order to destroy the Gokula, I had ordered the clouds here. This is what the clouds did as per my order.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

makhabhaṅgavirodhena mayā gokulānāśakāḥ . samādiṣṭā mahāmeghāstaiścedaṃ kadanaṃ kṛtam

Śrīdhara

mayi gokula-vadhāyodyate’pi tad-rakṣaṇāt ahaṃ toṣito’smīty āha—makhabhaṅgeti . kadanaṃ vimardaḥ ||८|| trātās tāś ca२२४tvayā gāvaḥsamutpāṭya mahīdharam२२५.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मयि गोकुल-वधायोद्यतेऽपि तद्-रक्षणात् अहं तोषितोऽस्मीत्य् आह—मखभङ्गेति । कदनं विमर्दः ॥८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

I had only ordered the clouds, in order to protect against the stoppage of that yaaga. In order to destroy the Gokula, I had ordered the clouds here. This is what the clouds did as per my order.

VP 5.12.11

गोभिश् चचोदितः कृष्ण त्वत्-सकाशम्२२८इहागतः।
त्वया त्राताभिर् अत्य् अर्थं युष्मत्-सत्कार२२९-कारणात्

The cows which are all in Goloka, have requested me, and because of that, I have come here, O Krishna. They have asked me to come and do satkaara to You, to offer worship to You. They have asked me to come and do satkaara to You, to offer worship to You. Because all the cows are protected by You. Though the cows in Goloka were not protected, these cows belong to the same class, and therefore it is told as traatabhih.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gobhiśca coditaḥ kṛṣṇa tvatsakāśamihāgataḥ . tvayā trātābhiratyarthaṃ yuṣmatsatkārakāraṇāt

VP 5.12.12

VP 5.12.13
