Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 15 — Conduct, Care and Traditional Massage

BP 1.15.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
दुर्भगा च पुनर्नित्यमुपवासादितत्परा ।
बाह्येषु पतिकृत्येषु स्याद्विशेषाभियोगिनी ॥

Brahmā said: An unfavoured wife should always be intent on fasting and the like, and should be especially devoted to her husband’s outward affairs.

ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
दुर्भगा (durbhagā) — wife lacking favour;
पुनः नित्यम् (punaḥ nityam) — continually;
उपवास-आदि-तत्परा (upavāsa-ādi-tatparā) — intent on fasts and others;
बाह्येषु पति-कृत्येषु (bāhyeṣu pati-kṛtyeṣu) — in husband’s outward duties;
स्यात् विशेष-अभियोगिनी (syāt viśeṣa-abhiyoginī) — should apply herself especially

BP 1.15.2

न प्रशंसां सपत्नीषु निंदां चापि तथात्मनि ।
असूयां भर्तुरीर्ष्यां वा प्रणयं वापि दर्शयेत् ॥

She should show neither praise of her co-wives, nor self-blame, nor resentment, jealousy toward her husband, or excessive affection.

न प्रशंसाम् सपत्नीषु (na praśaṃsām sapatnīṣu) — not praise regarding co-wives;
निन्दाम् च अपि तथा आत्मनि (nindām ca api tathā ātmani) — nor condemnation of herself;
असूयाम् भर्तुः (asūyām bhartuḥ) — resentment toward husband;
ईर्ष्याम् वा (īrṣyām vā) — or jealousy;
प्रणयम् वा अपि (praṇayam vā api) — or possessive familiarity;
दर्शयेत् (darśayet) — should display

BP 1.15.3

मद्विधा या हि बह्वेतत्तच्चात्त्यंतिकमश्नुते ।
यदस्या युष्मतो यायाद्भार्याशब्दाभिधेयताम् ॥

A woman who is thus devoted attains the highest end, when she comes to be called your wife.

मद्-विधा या हि (mad-vidhā yā hi) — woman of this kind;
बहु एतत् (bahu etat) — much of this;
तत् च आत्यन्तिकम् अश्नुते (tat ca ātyantikam aśnute) — attains that ultimate result;
यत् अस्याः (yat asyāḥ) — whereby for her;
युष्मतः (yuṣmataḥ) — in relation to you;
यायात् (yāyāt) — may come;
भार्या-शब्द-अभिधेयताम् (bhāryā-śabda-abhidheyatām) — designation by word wife

BP 1.15.4

न च निर्भूषणा तिष्ठेन्न चाप्युद्धतभूषणा ।
नान्यदा गंधमाल्यादि ग्राह्यं पत्युपचारतः ॥

She should be neither without ornaments nor adorned excessively; she should take fragrances, garlands, and the like only in serving her husband.

न च निर्भूषणा तिष्ठेत् (na ca nirbhūṣaṇā tiṣṭhet) — should not remain unadorned;
न च अपि उद्धत-भूषणा (na ca api uddhata-bhūṣaṇā) — nor excessively adorned;
न अन्यदा (na anyadā) — not at other time;
गन्ध-माल्य-आदि ग्राह्यम् (gandha-mālya-ādi grāhyam) — fragrance, garlands and others to be taken;
पति-उपचारतः (pati-upacārataḥ) — for service of husband

BP 1.15.5

तन्न्यूनं सर्वशो ग्राह्यं वल्लभाया विशेषतः ।
भूषणं गन्धमाल्यं तु तावत्कालमलक्षितम् ॥

Her ornaments, fragrances, garlands, and the like should in every respect be less than those of the favoured wife, and remain unnoticed for that time.

तत्-न्यूनम् सर्वशः ग्राह्यम् (tat-nyūnam sarvaśaḥ grāhyam) — should take less than that in every respect;
वल्लभायाः विशेषतः (vallabhāyāḥ viśeṣataḥ) — especially than favoured wife;
भूषणम् (bhūṣaṇam) — ornament;
गन्ध-माल्यम् तु (gandha-mālyam tu) — fragrance and garland;
तावत्-कालम् अलक्षितम् (tāvat-kālam alakṣitam) — unobtrusive for that time

BP 1.15.6

सम्बाधानां प्रदेशानां नित्यं स्वेदादिमार्जनम् ।
दन्तनासादिपङ्कानां विगन्धस्य च शोधनम् ॥

She should always wipe perspiration from the concealed parts and clean deposits from the teeth, nose, and the like, and remove bad odour.

सम्बाधानाम् प्रदेशानाम् (sambādhānām pradeśānām) — of confined body regions;
नित्यम् स्वेद-आदि-मार्जनम् (nityam sveda-ādi-mārjanam) — continual cleansing of perspiration and others;
दन्त-नास-आदि-पङ्कानाम् (danta-nāsa-ādi-paṅkānām) — deposits of teeth, nose and others;
विगन्धस्य च (vigandhasya ca) — and unpleasant odour;
शोधनम् (śodhanam) — removal

BP 1.15.7

निमित्तं भर्तुरेतासां यत्किंचिदभिलक्षयेत् ।
नानेन वा तयोर्यत्नं विदध्यादङ्गमार्जने ॥

Whatever sign of her husband’s need for these things she notices, she should accordingly make the effort to cleanse and groom her body.

निमित्तम् भर्तुः (nimittam bhartuḥ) — indication from husband;
एतासाम् (etāsām) — concerning these;
यत् किञ्चित् अभिलक्षयेत् (yat kiñcit abhilakṣayet) — whatever she notices;
अनेन वा (anena vā) — by this;
तयोः यत्नम् विदध्यात् (tayoḥ yatnam vidadhyāt) — should make effort concerning them;
अङ्ग-मार्जने (aṅga-mārjane) — in body cleansing

BP 1.15.8

सर्वासां च सपत्नीनां सर्वत्रानुगता भवेत् ।
वैतसीं वृत्तिमास्थाय वल्लभाया विशेषतः ॥

She should follow all her co-wives in every matter, adopting the yielding manner of a reed, especially toward the favoured wife.

सर्वासाम् च सपत्नीनाम् (sarvāsām ca sapatnīnām) — of all co-wives;
सर्वत्र अनुगता भवेत् (sarvatra anugatā bhavet) — should be accommodating everywhere;
वैतसीम् वृत्तिम् आस्थाय (vaitasīm vṛttim āsthāya) — adopting conduct of a reed;
वल्लभायाः विशेषतः (vallabhāyāḥ viśeṣataḥ) — especially toward favoured wife

BP 1.15.9

अन्यस्या यदनुष्ठेयं यन्न सीदेत्समर्पितम् ।
भर्तुश्चाविदितं यत्नात्तत्कुर्यादविरोधि चेत् ॥

Whatever another wife ought to do, and whatever would otherwise be left undone, she should carefully do, if it does not cause conflict, without her husband’s knowledge.

अन्यस्याः यत् अनुष्ठेयम् (anyasyāḥ yat anuṣṭheyam) — what should be performed by another;
यत् न सीदेत् समर्पितम् (yat na sīdet samarpitam) — so assigned task does not fail;
भर्तुः च अविदितम् (bhartuḥ ca aviditam) — unknown to husband;
यत्नात् तत् कुर्यात् (yatnāt tat kuryāt) — should carefully do it;
अविरोधि चेत् (avirodhi cet) — if not conflicting

BP 1.15.10

कोशवस्त्रान्नताम्बूलगन्धापानौषधादिकम् ।
तत्सर्वमनियुक्तानां दोषवत्वाद्विरुध्यते ॥

The treasury, clothes, food, betel, perfumes, drink, medicines, and the like are improper for those not assigned to them to handle.

कोश (kośa) — treasury;
वस्त्र (vastra) — clothing;
अन्न (anna) — food;
ताम्बूल (tāmbūla) — betel;
गन्ध (gandha) — perfume;
पान (pāna) — drink;
औषध-आदिकम् (auṣadha-ādikam) — medicine and others;
तत् सर्वम् (tat sarvam) — all that;
अनियुक्तानाम् (aniyuktānām) — of unauthorized persons;
दोषवत् (doṣavat) — faulty;
विरुध्यते (virudhyate) — is prohibited

BP 1.15.11

यत्तु मुक्तमनुष्ठेयं गहसम्मार्जनादिकम् ।
स्त्रीणामनधिकारेऽपि प्रायस्तद्विधिरुच्यते ॥

But what is left to be done, such as sweeping the house, is generally prescribed for women, even without authority.

यत् तु मुक्तम् अनुष्ठेयम् (yat tu muktam anuṣṭheyam) — what is left free to perform;
गृह-संमार्जन-आदिकम् (gṛha-saṃmārjana-ādikam) — house sweeping and others;
स्त्रीणाम् अनधिकारे अपि (strīṇām anadhikāre api) — even without women’s authority;
प्रायः तत्-विधिः उच्यते (prāyaḥ tat-vidhiḥ ucyate) — that rule is generally stated

BP 1.15.12

अभ्यङ्गोद्वर्तनं स्नानं भोजनं मण्डनानि च ।
कुर्याद्भर्तुरपत्यानां धात्रीकर्माणि सादरम् ॥

She should respectfully perform the nurse’s duties for her husband’s children: anointing, rubbing with cleansing paste, bathing, feeding, and adorning them.

अभ्यङ्ग (abhyaṅga) — oiling;
उद्वर्तनम् (udvartanam) — rubbing with paste;
स्नानम् (snānam) — bathing;
भोजनम् (bhojanam) — feeding;
मण्डनानि च (maṇḍanāni ca) — and adornment;
कुर्यात् (kuryāt) — should perform;
भर्तुः अपत्यानाम् (bhartuḥ apatyānām) — for husband’s children;
धात्री-कर्माणि (dhātrī-karmāṇi) — nurse’s duties;
स-आदरम् (sa-ādaram) — respectfully

BP 1.15.13

आत्मवत्तान्यपत्यानि साधयत्यनुयोगतः ।
स्वेनाप्यमीषां वित्तेन विदध्यान्मण्डनादिकम् ॥

She should care for the children as her own when entrusted with them; with her own wealth also, she should provide their adornments and the like.

आत्म-वत् तानि अपत्यानि (ātma-vat tāni apatyāni) — those children like own;
साधयति अनुयोगतः (sādhayati anuyogataḥ) — should care for when entrusted;
स्वेन अपि (svena api) — even with her own;
अमीषाम् वित्तेन (amīṣām vittena) — property for them;
विदध्यात् मण्डन-आदिकम् (vidadhyāt maṇḍana-ādikam) — should provide adornment and others

BP 1.15.14

भोगः स्वयमपत्यैर्वा स्त्रीवित्तस्य पतिर्विधा ।
पूर्वे वयस्यभिनन्द्य पश्चिमे चोपयोजनम् ॥

The husband directs the use of a woman’s wealth, whether for himself or her children; it is to be valued in the earlier years and used in the later years.

भोगः स्वयम् अपत्यैः वा (bhogaḥ svayam apatyaiḥ vā) — use by himself or children;
स्त्री-वित्तस्य (strī-vittasya) — of wife’s property;
पतिः विधा (patiḥ vidhā) — husband directs;
पूर्वे वयसि अभिनन्द्यम् (pūrve vayasi abhinandyam) — welcomed in earlier age;
पश्चिमे च उपयोजनम् (paścime ca upayojanam) — and use in later age

BP 1.15.15

उभयोगस्तु वा मा वा कर्मजः पृथगेव सः ।
सद्वृत्ते त्वधिकां ख्यातिं कुर्वीत क्रियया पुनः ॥

Whether the two spouses are joined or not, that result is separately due to past action; through good conduct she should again gain greater renown by her actions.

उभयोः योगः तु (ubhayoḥ yogaḥ tu) — union of both;
वा मा वा (vā mā vā) — whether or not;
कर्म-जः (karma-jaḥ) — born of action;
पृथक् एव सः (pṛthak eva saḥ) — that is separate;
सद्-वृत्ते तु (sad-vṛtte tu) — in good conduct;
अधिकाम् ख्यातिम् कुर्वीत (adhikām khyātim kurvīta) — should create greater reputation;
क्रियया पुनः (kriyayā punaḥ) — through action again

BP 1.15.16

न कापि दुर्भगा नाम सुभगा नाम जातितः ।
व्यवहाराद्भवत्येष निर्देशो रिपुमित्रवत् ॥

No woman is by birth called unfortunate or fortunate; this designation comes from conduct, as do the designations enemy and friend.

न का अपि दुर्भगा नाम (na kā api durbhagā nāma) — no one inherently unfortunate;
सुभगा नाम जातितः (subhagā nāma jātitaḥ) — nor fortunate by birth;
व्यवहारात् भवति (vyavahārāt bhavati) — arises from conduct;
एष निर्देशः (eṣa nirdeśaḥ) — this designation;
रिपु-मित्र-वत् (ripu-mitra-vat) — like enemy and friend

BP 1.15.17

भर्तृचित्तापरिज्ञानादननुष्ठानतोऽपि वा।
वृत्तैर्लोकविरुद्धैश्च यान्ति दुर्भगतां स्त्रियः ॥

Women become unfortunate through not understanding their husband’s mind, through not acting accordingly, or through conduct opposed to people’s ways.

भर्तृ-चित्त-अपरिज्ञानात् (bhartṛ-citta-aparijñānāt) — from not understanding husband’s mind;
अननुष्ठानतः अपि वा (ananuṣṭhānataḥ api vā) — or failure to act;
वृत्तैः लोक-विरुद्धैः च (vṛttaiḥ loka-viruddhaiḥ ca) — by conduct opposed to society;
यान्ति दुर्भगताम् स्त्रियः (yānti durbhagatām striyaḥ) — women become unfavoured

BP 1.15.18

प्रानुकूल्यान्मनोवृत्तैः परोऽपि प्रियतां व्रजेत् ।
प्रातिकूल्यान्निजोप्याशु प्रियः प्रद्वेषतामियात् ॥

Through a favourable disposition, even a stranger becomes dear; through opposition, even one’s own beloved quickly becomes an object of hatred.

प्रानुकूल्यात् मनः-वृत्तैः (prānukūlyāt manaḥ-vṛttaiḥ) — through harmony in mind and conduct;
परः अपि प्रियताम् व्रजेत् (paraḥ api priyatām vrajet) — even outsider becomes dear;
प्रातिकूल्यात् (prātikūlyāt) — through opposition;
निजः अपि आशु प्रियः (nijaḥ api āśu priyaḥ) — even own beloved quickly;
प्रद्वेषताम् इयात् (pradveṣatām iyāt) — becomes hated

BP 1.15.19

तस्मात्सर्वास्ववस्थासु मनोवाक्कायकर्मभिः ।
प्रियं समाचरेन्नित्यं तच्चितानुविधायिनी ॥

Therefore, in every circumstance, she should always act pleasingly through mind, speech, body, and deeds, following his state of mind.

तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore;
सर्वासु अवस्थासु (sarvāsu avasthāsu) — in every circumstance;
मनः-वाक्-काय-कर्मभिः (manaḥ-vāk-kāya-karmabhiḥ) — through mind, speech, body and action;
प्रियम् समाचरेत् नित्यम् (priyam samācaret nityam) — should always do what is pleasing;
तत्-चित्त-अनुविधायिनी (tat-citta-anuvidhāyinī) — conforming to his mind

BP 1.15.20

यामन्यां कामयेत्तासां तं तया संप्रयोजयेत् ।
कुपितां च प्रियां काञ्चिद्यत्नादस्मै प्रसादयेत् ॥

If he desires another of them, she should unite him with that woman; and if any beloved wife is angry, she should carefully reconcile her to him.

याम् अन्याम् कामयेत् (yām anyām kāmayet) — whichever other woman he desires;
तासाम् (tāsām) — among them;
तम् तया सम्प्रयोजयेत् (tam tayā samprayojayet) — should bring him together with her;
कुपिताम् च प्रियाम् काञ्चित् (kupitām ca priyām kāñcit) — and some beloved woman who is angry;
यत्नात् अस्मै प्रसादयेत् (yatnāt asmai prasādayet) — should carefully reconcile to him

BP 1.15.21

तत्पादपरिचर्यायां गोत्रसंवाहने तथा ।
पीडने शिरसश्चैव परं कौशलमभ्यसेत् ॥

She should cultivate great skill in attending to his feet, rubbing his limbs, and pressing his head.

तत्-पाद-परिचर्यायाम् (tat-pāda-paricaryāyām) — in attending his feet;
गात्र-संवाहने (gātra-saṃvāhane) — in massaging limbs;
तथा (tathā) — likewise;
पीडने शिरसः च एव (pīḍane śirasaḥ ca eva) — in pressure to head;
परम् कौशलम् अभ्यसेत् (param kauśalam abhyaset) — should cultivate exceptional skill

BP 1.15.22

पीडनं मृदु मध्यं च गात्रावस्थाविशेषतः ।
मुखगात्रादिभिर्लिङ्गैः प्रयोज्यं तत्सुखावहम् ॥

Pressure should be gentle or medium according to the state of the body; signs from the face, limbs, and the like should guide the pressure that gives comfort.

पीडनम् मृदु मध्यम् च (pīḍanam mṛdu madhyam ca) — pressure gentle or medium;
गात्र-अवस्था-विशेषतः (gātra-avasthā-viśeṣataḥ) — according to body’s condition;
मुख-गात्र-आदिभिः लिङ्गैः (mukha-gātra-ādibhiḥ liṅgaiḥ) — by signs from face, limbs and others;
प्रयोज्यम् (prayojyam) — to be applied;
तत्-सुख-आवहम् (tat-sukha-āvaham) — what brings comfort

BP 1.15.23

बाहूरुकटिपृष्ठेषु स्कंधे शिरसि पादयोः ।
गाढमर्दनमिच्छन्ति प्रायोन्यत्रापि मध्यमम् ॥

Firm massage is generally desired on the arms, thighs, waist, back, shoulders, head, and feet; elsewhere, medium pressure is usually desired.

बाहु (bāhu) — arms;
ऊरु (ūru) — thighs;
कटि (kaṭi) — waist;
पृष्ठेषु (pṛṣṭheṣu) — back;
स्कन्धे (skandhe) — shoulder;
शिरसि (śirasi) — head;
पादयोः (pādayoḥ) — feet;
गाढ-मर्दनम् इच्छन्ति (gāḍha-mardanam icchanti) — firm massage is desired;
प्रायः अन्यत्र अपि मध्यमम् (prāyaḥ anyatra api madhyamam) — generally medium elsewhere

BP 1.15.24

निर्मांसेषु प्रदेशेषु नाभिमूलेषु मर्मसु ।
हृद्गण्डककपोलादाविच्छन्ति मृदुमर्दनम् ॥

Gentle massage is desired on fleshless parts, around the navel and vital points, and from the heart, jaw, and cheeks onward.

निर्मांसेषु प्रदेशेषु (nirmāṃseṣu pradeśeṣu) — in fleshless regions;
नाभि-मूलेषु (nābhi-mūleṣu) — around navel;
मर्मसु (marmasu) — at vital points;
हृत् (hṛt) — heart;
गण्डक (gaṇḍaka) — jaw;
कपोल-आदौ (kapola-ādau) — cheek and others;
इच्छन्ति मृदु-मर्दनम् (icchanti mṛdu-mardanam) — gentle massage is desired

BP 1.15.25

गाढं जाग्रदवस्थायामर्धसुप्तस्य मध्यमम् ।
किञ्चित्सपरिघातं च मृदुसुप्तस्य नेति वा ॥

Massage should be firm while he is awake, medium while he is half-asleep, and light while he is lightly asleep, or not at all.

गाढम् जाग्रत्-अवस्थायाम् (gāḍham jāgrat-avasthāyām) — firm while awake;
अर्ध-सुप्तस्य मध्यमम् (ardha-suptasya madhyamam) — medium when half asleep;
किञ्चित् स-परिघातम् (kiñcit sa-parighātam) — a little with light contact;
मृदु-सुप्तस्य (mṛdu-suptasya) — when gently asleep;
न इति वा (na iti vā) — or none

BP 1.15.26

विरुद्धं सर्वगात्रेषु लोमवस्तु विशेषतः ।
उत्कण्डूयत्यु सोद्धर्षं स्नेहाक्तेषु च मर्द नम् ॥

Rubbing against the hair is improper over the body, especially where there is body hair; scratch vigorously where itching arises, and massage the oiled parts.

विरुद्धम् सर्व-गात्रेषु (viruddham sarva-gātreṣu) — contrary direction on all limbs;
लोम-वस्तु विशेषतः (loma-vastu viśeṣataḥ) — especially hairy surfaces;
उत्कण्डूयति (utkaṇḍūyati) — where it itches;
स-उद्धर्षम् (sa-uddharṣam) — with vigorous scratching;
स्नेह-अक्तेषु च मर्दनम् (sneha-akteṣu ca mardanam) — massage on oiled areas

BP 1.15.27

स्पर्शाद्रोमाञ्चजननं सनखच्छुरितं शनैः ।
पुलकोल्लेखनोपेतं शिरःकंडूश्च पार्श्वयोः ॥

Touch should slowly raise gooseflesh; scratch lightly with the nails, raise the hairs, and scratch the head and sides.

स्पर्शात् रोमाञ्च-जननम् (sparśāt romāñca-jananam) — producing gooseflesh by touch;
स-नख-च्छुरितम् शनैः (sa-nakha-cchuritam śanaiḥ) — slowly traced by nails;
पुलक-उल्लेखन-उपेतम् (pulaka-ullekhana-upetam) — with stroking of raised hairs;
शिरः-कण्डूः (śiraḥ-kaṇḍūḥ) — scratching head;
पार्श्वयोः (pārśvayoḥ) — and sides

BP 1.15.28

तेषु तेषु च गात्रेषु तत्प्रयोज्यं तथातथा ।
निद्रागमाय तत्काले रागसंधुक्षणाय च ॥

On those and those limbs she should apply this in the fitting way, at the time, for bringing sleep and for kindling desire.

तेषु तेषु गात्रेषु (teṣu teṣu gātreṣu) — on those various limbs;
तत् प्रयोज्यम् तथा तथा (tat prayojyam tathā tathā) — should apply accordingly;
निद्रा-आगमाय (nidrā-āgamāya) — to bring sleep;
तत्-काले (tat-kāle) — at that time;
राग-संधुक्षणाय च (rāga-saṃdhukṣaṇāya ca) — and to kindle desire

BP 1.15.29

तिष्ठतश्चोपविष्टस्य जाग्रतः स्वपतोऽपि वा ।
संवाहनं प्रशंसंति यदत्यर्थं सुखावहम् ॥

Massage is praised as very comforting for one standing, seated, awake, or asleep.

तिष्ठतः च (tiṣṭhataḥ ca) — while standing;
उपविष्टस्य (upaviṣṭasya) — seated;
जाग्रतः (jāgrataḥ) — awake;
स्वपतः अपि वा (svapataḥ api vā) — or asleep;
संवाहनम् प्रशंसन्ति (saṃvāhanam praśaṃsanti) — massage is praised;
यत् अत्यर्थम् सुख-आवहम् (yat atyartham sukha-āvaham) — as exceedingly comforting

BP 1.15.30

नैष्पन्द्यं पुलकोद्भेदो गात्राणामक्षिमीलनम् ।
तत्प्रदेशार्पणं किञ्चिद्बोधेद्विकृतिदर्शनम् ॥

Stillness, gooseflesh, closing of the eyes, slight presentation of a body part, and signs of change should be understood.

नैष्पन्द्यम् (naiṣpandyam) — stillness;
पुलक-उद्भेदः (pulaka-udbhedaḥ) — arising of gooseflesh;
गात्राणाम् (gātrāṇām) — of limbs;
अक्षि-मीलनम् (akṣi-mīlanam) — closing eyes;
तत्-प्रदेश-अर्पणम् (tat-pradeśa-arpaṇam) — presenting that region;
किञ्चित् बोधेत् (kiñcit bodhet) — should understand somewhat;
विकृति-दर्शनम् (vikṛti-darśanam) — seeing change

BP 1.15.31

ऊरू मूलादिदेशे च तत्पाणिप्रतिपीडनम् ।
लक्षयेन्निपुणा यत्र तत्रैवाधिकमाचरेत् ॥

At the roots of the thighs and other places, she should notice where he presses her hand; there she should apply greater pressure.

ऊरु-मूल-आदि-देशे (ūru-mūla-ādi-deśe) — at thigh-root and similar region;
तत्-पाणि-प्रतिपीडनम् (tat-pāṇi-pratipīḍanam) — his pressing of her hand;
लक्षयेत् निपुणा (lakṣayet nipuṇā) — skilled woman should observe;
यत्र (yatra) — where;
तत्र एव अधिकम् आचरेत् (tatra eva adhikam ācaret) — should act more there

BP 1.15.32

एवमेव यथोद्दिष्टं स्त्रीवृत्तं यानुतिष्ठति ।
पतिमाराध्य सम्पूर्ण त्रिवर्गं साधिगच्छति ॥

A woman who follows this conduct as stated, having pleased her husband, fully attains the three aims: righteousness, wealth, and desire.

एवम् एव (evam eva) — exactly thus;
यथा-उद्दिष्टम् (yathā-uddiṣṭam) — as set forth;
स्त्री-वृत्तम् (strī-vṛttam) — women’s conduct;
या अनुतिष्ठति (yā anutiṣṭhati) — she who follows;
पतिम् आराध्य (patim ārādhya) — pleasing husband;
सम्पूर्णम् त्रि-वर्गम् (sampūrṇam tri-vargam) — complete three aims;
सा अधिगच्छति (sā adhigacchati) — she attains
