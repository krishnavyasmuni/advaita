BP 1.63.25

अपि नः स कुले धन्यो जायते कुलपावनः ।
भगवान् भक्तिभावेन येन भानुरुपासितः ॥ २५ ॥

Even in our lineage he is born blessed and becomes a purifier of the family—the one by whom the Blessed Bhānu has been worshiped with devotion.

Word-for-word
api — even; naḥ kule — in our family; saḥ — he; dhanyaḥ — blessed; jāyate — is born; kula-pāvanaḥ — purifier of the family; bhagavān bhānuḥ — the Blessed Bhānu; bhakti-bhāvena — with devotional feeling; yena — by whom; upāsitaḥ — worshiped
Transliteration
api naḥ sa kule dhanyo jāyate kulapāvanaḥ |
bhagavān bhaktibhāvena yena bhānur upāsitaḥ || 25 ||

BP 1.63.26

यः कारयति देवार्चां हृदयालम्बनं रवेः ।
स नरो भानुसालोक्यमाप्नोति धुतकल्मषः ॥ २६ ॥

The person who causes an image of the god, a support for the heart, to be made for Ravi reaches the world of Bhānu, his impurities washed away.

Word-for-word
yaḥ — whoever; kārayati — causes to be made; deva-arcām — divine image; hṛdaya-ālambanam — support for the heart; raveḥ — of Ravi; saḥ naraḥ — that person; bhānu-sālokyam — the world of Bhānu; āpnoti — reaches; dhuta-kalmaṣaḥ — with impurities washed away
Transliteration
yaḥ kārayati devārcāṃ hṛdayālambanaṃ raveḥ |
sa naro bhānusālokyam āpnoti dhutakalmaṣaḥ || 26 ||

BP 1.63.27

यस्तु देवालयं भानोर्भक्त्या कारयति ध्रुवम् ।
स सप्त पुरुषान् लोकं भानोर्नयति मानवः ॥ २७ ॥

The human who firmly causes a temple of Bhānu to be made with devotion leads seven generations of persons to the world of Bhānu.

Word-for-word
yaḥ tu — whoever; deva-ālayam — temple; bhānoḥ — of Bhānu; bhaktyā — with devotion; kārayati — causes to be made; dhruvam — firmly; saḥ mānavaḥ — that human; sapta puruṣān — seven generations/persons; lokam bhānoḥ — to the world of Bhānu; nayati — leads
Transliteration
yas tu devālayaṃ bhānor bhaktyā kārayati dhruvam |
sa sapta puruṣān lokaṃ bhānor nayati mānavaḥ || 27 ||

BP 1.63.28

यावन्त्यब्दानि देवार्चा रवेस्तिष्ठति मन्दिरे ।
तावद्वर्षसहस्राणि सूर्यलोके महीयते ॥ २८ ॥

For as many years as the divine image of Ravi remains in the temple, for that many thousands of years the maker is honoured in the world of the Sun.

Word-for-word
yāvanti abdāni — for as many years; deva-arcā — the divine image; raveḥ — of Ravi; tiṣṭhati — remains; mandire — in the temple; tāvat varṣa-sahasrāṇi — that many thousands of years; sūrya-loke — in the world of the Sun; mahīyate — is honoured
Transliteration
yāvanty abdāni devārcā raves tiṣṭhati mandire |
tāvad varṣasahasrāṇi sūryaloke mahīyate || 28 ||

BP 1.63.29

देवार्चा लक्षणोपेता तद्गृहे सन्ततो विधिः ।
निष्कामं च मनो यस्य स यात्यक्षरसाम्यताम् ॥ २९ ॥

When the divine image is furnished with its marks and the rite is constant in that house, the person whose mind is desireless attains likeness with the Imperishable.

Word-for-word
deva-arcā — divine image; lakṣaṇa-upetā — furnished with marks; tat-gṛhe — in that house; santataḥ vidhiḥ — constant rite; niṣkāmam — desireless; ca — and; manaḥ — mind; yasya — whose; saḥ yāti — he goes/attains; akṣara-sāmyatām — likeness with the Imperishable
Transliteration
devārcā lakṣaṇopetā tadgṛhe santato vidhiḥ |
niṣkāmaṃ ca mano yasya sa yāty akṣarasāmyatām || 29 ||

BP 1.63.30

पुष्पाणि च सुगन्धीनि मनोज्ञानि च यः पुमान् ।
प्रयच्छति सहस्रांशोः सदा प्रयतमानसः ॥ ३० ॥

The man who, with a disciplined mind, always offers fragrant and beautiful flowers to the Thousand-rayed One—

Word-for-word
puṣpāṇi — flowers; ca — and; su-gandhīni — fragrant; manojñāni — beautiful/delightful; yaḥ pumān — the man who; prayacchati — offers; sahasra-aṃśoḥ — to the Thousand-rayed One; sadā — always; prayatamānasaḥ — with a disciplined mind
Transliteration
puṣpāṇi ca sugandhīni manojñāni ca yaḥ pumān |
prayacchati sahasrāṃśoḥ sadā prayatamānasaḥ || 30 ||

BP 1.63.31

धूपांश्च तांस्तान् विविधान् गन्धाढ्यं चानुलेपनम् ।
नरः सोऽनुदिनं यज्ञं करोत्याराधनं रवेः ॥ ३१ ॥

That person offers various incenses and fragrant unguents; day after day he makes the sacrifice that is worship of Ravi.

Word-for-word
dhūpān — incenses; ca — and; tān tān vividhān — those various; gandha-āḍhyam — rich in fragrance; ca — and; anulepanam — unguent; naraḥ saḥ — that person; anudinam — daily; yajñam karoti — performs a sacrifice; ārādhanam raveḥ — worship of Ravi
Transliteration
dhūpāṃś ca tāṃs tān vividhān gandhāḍhyaṃ cānulepanam |
naraḥ so’nudinaṃ yajñaṃ karoty ārādhanaṃ raveḥ || 31 ||

BP 1.63.32

यज्ञेशो भगवान् पूषा सदा क्रतुभिरिज्यते ।
बहूपकरणा यज्ञा नानासम्भारविस्तराः ॥ ३२ ॥

The Blessed Pūṣā, Lord of sacrifice, is always worshiped with rituals—sacrifices requiring many implements and an abundance of varied materials.

Word-for-word
yajña-īśaḥ — Lord of sacrifice; bhagavān pūṣā — the Blessed Pūṣā; sadā — always; kratubhiḥ ijyate — is worshiped with rituals; bahu-upakaraṇāḥ — having many implements; yajñāḥ — sacrifices; nānā-sambhāra-vistarāḥ — extensive in varied materials
Transliteration
yajñeśo bhagavān pūṣā sadā kratubhir ijyate |
bahūpakaraṇā yajñā nānāsambhāravistarāḥ || 32 ||

BP 1.63.33

न ते दिण्डिन्नवाप्यन्ते मनुष्यैरल्पसञ्चयैः ।
भक्त्या तु पुरुषैः पूजा कृता दूर्वाङ्कुरैरपि ।
भानोर्ददाति हि फलं सर्वयज्ञैः सुदुर्लभम् ॥ ३३ ॥

These results are not attained by people with little accumulated merit, O Diṇḍi; yet worship performed with devotion, even with shoots of dūrvā grass, gives Bhānu a fruit very difficult to obtain through all sacrifices.

Word-for-word
na te — not these; diṇḍin — O Diṇḍi; avāpyante — are attained; manuṣyaiḥ — by people; alpa-saṃcayaiḥ — with little accumulation; bhaktyā — with devotion; tu — but; puruṣaiḥ — by people; pūjā kṛtā — worship performed; dūrvā-aṅkuraiḥ api — even with shoots of dūrvā; bhānoḥ — of Bhānu; dadāti phalam — gives the fruit; sarva-yajñaiḥ sudurlabham — very difficult through all sacrifices
Transliteration
na te diṇḍinn avāpyante manuṣyair alpa-saṃcayaiḥ |
bhaktyā tu puruṣaiḥ pūjā kṛtā dūrvāṅkurair api |
bhānor dadāti hi phalaṃ sarvayajñaiḥ sudurlabham || 33 ||

BP 1.63.34

यानि पुष्पाणि हृद्यानि धूपगन्धानुलेपनम् ।
दयितं भूषणं यच्च प्रीतये चैव वाससी ॥ ३४ ॥

Whatever flowers are pleasing, incense, fragrance, and unguents, whatever ornament is beloved, and garments for delight—

Word-for-word
yāni — whatever; puṣpāṇi — flowers; hṛdyāni — pleasing to the heart; dhūpa-gandha-anulepanam — incense, fragrance, and unguent; dayitam bhūṣaṇam — beloved ornament; yat ca — whatever; prītaye — for delight; ca eva — and indeed; vāsasī — garments
Transliteration
yāni puṣpāṇi hṛdyāni dhūpagandhānulepanam |
dayitaṃ bhūṣaṇaṃ yac ca prītaye caiva vāsasī || 34 ||

BP 1.63.35

यानि चाभ्यवहार्याणि भक्ष्याणि च फलानि वै ।
प्रयच्छ तानि मार्तण्डे भवेष्ठाश्चैव तन्मनाः ॥ ३५ ॥

Whatever foods are fit to eat, and whatever fruits there are, offer them to Mārtaṇḍa; and remain devoted to him alone, with your mind on him.

Word-for-word
yāni ca — whatever also; abhyavahāryāṇi — fit to be eaten; bhakṣyāṇi — foods; ca — and; phalāni — fruits; vai — indeed; prayaccha — offer; tāni — them; mārtaṇḍe — to Mārtaṇḍa; bhava iṣṭaḥ — remain devoted; ca eva — and indeed; tat-manāḥ — with mind on him
Transliteration
yāni cābhyavahāryāṇi bhakṣyāṇi ca phalāni vai |
prayaccha tāni mārtaṇḍe bhaveṣṭhāś caiva tanmanāḥ || 35 ||

BP 1.63.36

आद्यं तं भुवनाधारं यथाशक्त्या प्रसादय ।
आराध्य याति तं देवं तस्मिन्नेव नरो लयम् ॥ ३६ ॥

Please that primordial one, the support of the worlds, according to your ability. Having worshiped that god, a person goes to him and dissolves in him alone.

Word-for-word
ādyam — primordial; tam — that; bhuvana-ādhāram — support of the worlds; yathā-śaktyā — according to ability; prasādaya — please; ārādhya — having worshiped; yāti — goes; tam devam — to that god; tasmin eva — in him alone; naraḥ — a person; layam — dissolution
Transliteration
ādyaṃ taṃ bhuvanādhāraṃ yathāśaktyā prasādaya |
ārādhya yāti taṃ devaṃ tasminn eva naro layam || 36 ||

BP 1.63.37

पुष्पैस्तीर्थोदकैर्गन्धैर्मधुना सर्पिषा तथा ।
क्षीरेण स्नापयेद्भानुं ग्रहेशं गोपतिं खगम् ॥ ३७ ॥

With flowers, sacred waters, fragrances, honey, and ghee, one should bathe Bhānu—the Lord of the planets, the Lord of the cows, the heavenly bird—with milk.

Word-for-word
puṣpaiḥ — with flowers; tīrtha-udakaiḥ — sacred waters; gandhaiḥ — fragrances; madhunā — with honey; sarpiṣā — with ghee; tathā — likewise; kṣīreṇa — with milk; snāpayet — should bathe; bhānum — Bhānu; graha-īśam — Lord of the planets; go-patim — Lord of the cows; khagam — the sky-going one
Transliteration
puṣpais tīrthodakair gandhair madhunā sarpiṣā tathā |
kṣīreṇa snāpayed bhānuṃ graheśaṃ gopatiṃ khagam || 37 ||

BP 1.63.38

दधिक्षीरह्रदान् पुण्यांस्ततो लोकान् मधुच्युतः ।
प्रयास्यति गणश्रेष्ठ निर्वृत्तिं च विलक्षणाम् ॥ ३८ ॥

Then, O best of the hosts, he will go to holy lakes of curd and milk, to honey-flowing worlds, and to a singular release.

Word-for-word
dadhi-kṣīra-hradān — lakes of curd and milk; puṇyān — holy; tataḥ — then; lokān — worlds; madhu-cyutān — dripping with honey; prayāsyati — will go; gaṇa-śreṣṭha — O best of the hosts; nirvṛttim — release; ca — and; vilakṣaṇām — extraordinary
Transliteration
dadhi-kṣīra-hradān puṇyāṃs tato lokān madhucyutaḥ |
prayāsyati gaṇaśreṣṭha nirvṛttiṃ ca vilakṣaṇām || 38 ||

BP 1.63.39

स्तोत्रैर्गीतैस्तथा वाद्यैर्ब्राह्मणानां च तर्पणैः ।
मनसश्चैव योगेन आराधय दिवाकरम् ॥ ३९ ॥

Worship Divākara with hymns, songs, and instruments, with the satisfaction of Brāhmaṇas, and also through the yoga of the mind.

Word-for-word
stotraiḥ — with hymns; gītaiḥ — with songs; tathā — likewise; vādyaiḥ — with instruments; brāhmaṇānām tarpaṇaiḥ — with satisfaction of Brāhmaṇas; manasaḥ ca eva yogena — and with yoga of the mind; ārādhaya — worship; divākaram — Divākara
Transliteration
stotrair gītaiḥ tathā vādyair brāhmaṇānāṃ ca tarpaṇaiḥ |
manasaś caiva yogena ārādhaya divākaram || 39 ||

BP 1.63.40

आराध्य तं जगन्नाथं मया सर्गः प्रवर्तितः ।
विष्णुश्च पालयेल्लोकांस्तमाराध्य दिवाकरम् ॥ ४० ॥

Having worshiped that Lord of the universe, I set creation in motion; and Viṣṇu protects the worlds after worshiping Divākara.

Word-for-word
ārādhya — having worshiped; tam — that; jagannātham — Lord of the universe; mayā — by me; sargaḥ — creation; pravartitaḥ — was set in motion; viṣṇuḥ ca — and Viṣṇu; pālayet lokān — protects the worlds; tam ārādhya — having worshiped him; divākaram — Divākara
Transliteration
ārādhya taṃ jagannāthaṃ mayā sargaḥ pravartitaḥ |
viṣṇuś ca pālayel lokāṃs tam ārādhya divākaram || 40 ||

BP 1.63.41

रुद्रश्च प्राप्तवान् देवीं भवानीं तत्प्रसादतः ।
दीप्यन्ते ऋषयश्चापि तमाराध्य दिवाकरम् ॥ ४१ ॥

Rudra obtained the goddess Bhavānī through his grace, and the ṛṣis too shine after worshiping Divākara.

Word-for-word
rudraḥ ca — and Rudra; prāptavān — obtained; devīm bhavānīm — the goddess Bhavānī; tat-prasādataḥ — through his grace; dīpyante — shine; ṛṣayaḥ ca api — and the ṛṣis too; tam ārādhya — having worshiped him; divākaram — Divākara
Transliteration
rudraś ca prāptavān devīṃ bhavānīṃ tatprasādataḥ |
dīpyante ṛṣayaś cāpi tam ārādhya divākaram || 41 ||

BP 1.63.42

स त्वमेभिः प्रकारैस्तमुपवासैश्च भास्करम् ।
तोषयाब्दं हि तुष्टोऽसौ भानुर्द्वन्द्वप्रशान्तिदः ॥ ४२ ॥

Therefore you should please Bhāskara by these methods and by fasts for a year; when pleased, Bhānu grants peace from the pairs of opposites.

Word-for-word
saḥ — therefore; tvam — you; ebhiḥ prakāraiḥ — by these methods; tam — him; upavāsaiḥ ca — and by fasts; bhāskaram — Bhāskara; toṣaya — please; abdam — for a year; hi — indeed; tuṣṭaḥ asau — when he is pleased; bhānuḥ — Bhānu; dvaṃdva-praśānti-daḥ — giver of peace from the opposites
Transliteration

toṣayābdaṃ hi tuṣṭo’sau bhānur dvaṃdvapraśāntidaḥ || 42 ||

इति श्रीभविष्ये महापुराणे शतार्द्धसाहस्र्यां संहितायां ब्राह्मे पर्वणि सप्तमीकल्पे ब्रह्मदिण्डिसंवादे आदित्यक्रियायोगवर्णनं नाम त्रिषष्टितमोऽध्यायः ॥ ६३ ॥

Thus ends the sixty-third chapter, named “The Description of Āditya Kriyāyoga,” in the Brāhma section of the Saṃhitā of the Śrī Bhaviṣya Mahāpurāṇa, in the Saptamī Kalpa, the dialogue between Brahmā and Diṇḍi.
