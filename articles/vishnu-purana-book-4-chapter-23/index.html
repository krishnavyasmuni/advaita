VP 4.23.1

मागधानां बार्हद्रथानां भाविनामनुक्रमं कथयिष्यामि

Sri Paraasharar - The descendants of King Bruhadratha, of Magadha desha, who will be kings in future, I will tell about them.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
māgadhānām bārhadrathānām — of the Māgadha Bārhadratha [kings];
bhāvinām anukramam — the succession of future ones;
kathayiṣyāmi — I will narrate.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māgadhānāṃ bārhadrathānāṃ bhāvināmanukramaṃ kathayiṣyāmi

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.23.2

अत्र हि वंशे महाबलपराक्रमा जरांसंधप्रधाना बभूवुः

Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 In this vamsha, powerful and strong kings like Jaraasandha are there.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mahābala-parākramāḥ — very strong and powerful;
jarāsaṃdha-pradhānāḥ — with Jarāsandha foremost;
babhūvuḥ — there were.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

atra hi vaṃśe mahābalaparākramā jarāṃsaṃdhapradhānā babhūvuḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.23.4

सहदेवात्सोमापिस्तस्यानुश्रुतश्रवास्तस्याप्ययुतायुस्ततश्च निरमित्रस्तत्तनयःसुनेत्रस्तस्मादपि बृहत्कर्मा

Word-for-word

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 4.23.13

इत्य् एते बार्हद्रथा भूपतयो वर्ष-सहस्रम् एकं भविष्यन्ति

The descendants of Bruhadratha are going to rule for one thousand years.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
iti ete bārhadrathāḥ bhūpatayaḥ — these Bārhadratha kings;
varṣa-sahasram ekam — one thousand years;
bhaviṣyanti — will be.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityete bārhadrathā bhūpatayo varṣasahasramekaṃ bhaviṣyanti

Śrīdhara

bārhadrathā bṛhadarathavaṃśodbhavāḥ || bṛhadrathasya apatyāni pumāṃsaḥ bārhadrathāḥ teṣām . bṛhadrathaśabdapatyārtheṣṇapratyayaḥ . jarāsādhaḥ jarayā tadākhyayā rākṣasyā sandhā sandhānaṃ bhāgadrayasya melanaṃ yatra saḥ . vṛttametad varṇitamedādhastād . sa eva pradhānaṃ yeṣāṃ te jarāsandhapradhānāḥ ||१३||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

बार्हद्रथा बृहदरथवंशोद्भवाः ॥ बृहद्रथस्य अपत्यानि पुमांसः बार्हद्रथाः तेषाम् । बृहद्रथशब्दपत्यार्थेष्णप्रत्ययः । जरासाधः जरया तदाख्यया राक्षस्या सन्धा सन्धानं भागद्रयस्य मेलनं यत्र सः । वृत्तमेतद् वर्णितमेदाधस्ताद् । स एव प्रधानं येषां ते जरासन्धप्रधानाः ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The descendants of Bruhadratha are going to rule for one thousand years.
