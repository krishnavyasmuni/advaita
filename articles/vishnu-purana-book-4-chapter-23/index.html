VP 4.23.1

मागधानां बार्हद्रथानां भाविनामनुक्रमं कथयिष्यामि

Sri Paraasharar - The descendants of King Bruhadratha, of Magadha desha, who will be kings in future, I will tell about them.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māgadhānāṃ bārhadrathānāṃ bhāvināmanukramaṃ kathayiṣyāmi

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 4.23.13

इत्येते बार्हद्रथा भूपतयो वर्षसहस्रमेकं भविष्यन्ति

The descendants of Bruhadratha are going to rule for one thousand years.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

itīti — The; || — descendants; teṣāṃ — Bruhadratha; varaṣasaṃkhayā — are; tu — to; kalau — rule; savalapāyuṣayasūcanena — one; virakatayarathā — thousand

Śrīdhara

bārahadarathā — The; baṛhadarathavaṃśodabhavāḥ — The; || — The; baṛhadarathasaya — descendants; apatayāni — descendants; pumāṃsaḥ — descendants; bārahadarathāḥ — of; teṣāma — of; . — Bruhadratha; baṛhadarathaśabadapatayāratheṣaṇaparatayayaḥ — Bruhadratha; . — Bruhadratha; jarāsādhaḥ — are; jarayā — are; tadākhayayā — are; rākaṣasayā — going; sanadhā — going; sanadhānaṃ — to; bhāgadarayasaya — to; melanaṃ — to; yatara — rule; saḥ — rule; . — rule; vaṛtatametada — for; varaṇitamedādhasatāda — for; . — one; sa — one; eva — one; paradhānaṃ — thousand; yeṣāṃ — thousand; te — thousand; jarāsanadhaparadhānāḥ — years; ||13|| — years

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityete bārhadrathā bhūpatayo varṣasahasramekaṃ bhaviṣyanti

Śrīdhara

bārhadrathā bṛhadarathavaṃśodbhavāḥ || bṛhadrathasya apatyāni pumāṃsaḥ bārhadrathāḥ teṣām . bṛhadrathaśabdapatyārtheṣṇapratyayaḥ . jarāsādhaḥ jarayā tadākhyayā rākṣasyā sandhā sandhānaṃ bhāgadrayasya melanaṃ yatra saḥ . vṛttametad varṇitamedādhastād . sa eva pradhānaṃ yeṣāṃ te jarāsandhapradhānāḥ ||१३||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

बार्हद्रथा बृहदरथवंशोद्भवाः ॥ बृहद्रथस्य अपत्यानि पुमांसः बार्हद्रथाः तेषाम् । बृहद्रथशब्दपत्यार्थेष्णप्रत्ययः । जरासाधः जरया तदाख्यया राक्षस्या सन्धा सन्धानं भागद्रयस्य मेलनं यत्र सः । वृत्तमेतद् वर्णितमेदाधस्ताद् । स एव प्रधानं येषां ते जरासन्धप्रधानाः ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The descendants of Bruhadratha are going to rule for one thousand years.
