VP 1.13.1

ध्रुवा च्छिष्टिं च भव्यं च भव्याच् छम्भुर् व्यजायत।
शिष्टेर् आधत्त सुच्छाया पञ्च-पुत्रान् अकल्मषान्॥१॥

Dhruva had two sons - Shishti and Bhavya. Bhavya had a son named Shambhu. Shishti's wife Succhaaya gave birth to five sons, who were all without any blemish.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

bhavyāt udbhava-arhāt — from Bhavya, worthy of birth; dhruvāt — from Dhruva; śambhuḥ nāma — one named Śambhu; tad-bhāryām — in his wife; śiṣṭim bhavyam ca janayāmāsa — he begot Śiṣṭi and Bhavya.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dhruvā cchiṣṭiṃ ca bhavyaṃ ca bhavyāc chambhur vyajāyata | śiṣṭer ādhatta succhāyā pañcaputrān akalmaṣān || 1 ||

Śrīdhara

bhavyādudbhavārhāt | dhruvāt śambhurnāma tadbhāryāṃ śiṣṭiṃ bhavyañca janayāmāsety arthaḥ | bhāryā śambhuriti kecit paṭhanti ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भव्यादुद्भवार्हात् । ध्रुवात् शम्भुर्नाम तद्भार्यां शिष्टिं भव्यञ्च जनयामासेत्य् अर्थः । भार्या शम्भुरिति केचित् पठन्ति ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The meaning is that Dhruva begot Śambhu, who begot Śiṣṭi and Bhavya in his wife; some read “Śambhu's wife.”

VP 1.13.2

रिपुं रिपुञ्जयं विप्रं वृकलं वृकतेजसम्।
रिपोर् आधत्त बृहती चाक्षुषं सर्व-तेजसम्॥२॥

Ripu, Ripunjaya, Vipra, Vrukala, Vrukatejas, were the names of those five sons. From Ripu, his wife Bruhatee gave birth to the illustrious Chaakshusha.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ripuṃ ripuñjayaṃ vipraṃ vṛkalaṃ vṛkatejasam | ripor ādhatta bṛhatī cākṣuṣaṃ sarvatejasam || 2 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.3

अजीजनत् पुष्करिण्यां वारुण्यां चाक्षुषो मनुम्।
प्रजापतेर् आत्म-जायां वीरणस्य महात्मनः॥३॥

Chaakshushaas ' wife was Pushkarini, of Varuna kula, Vaaruni, and daughter of the great Varuna Prajaapati.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

vāruṇyām — in one born from Varuṇa's lineage; araṇyasya prajāpateḥ ātma-jāyām — in the daughter of Prajāpati Araṇya; puṣkariṇyām — in Puṣkariṇī; cākṣuṣam manum — Cākṣuṣa Manu; ṣaṣṭham manv-antara-patim — lord of the sixth Manvantara.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ajījanat puṣkariṇyāṃ vāruṇyāṃ cākṣuṣo manum | prajāpater ātmajāyāṃ vīraṇasya mahātmanaḥ || 3 ||

Śrīdhara

vāruṇyāṃ varuṇasantānajātāyāmaraṇyasya prajāpaterātmajāyāṃ puṣkariṇyāṃ cākṣuṣo manaṃ ṣaṣṭhamanvantarapatiṃ janayāmāsetyanvayaḥ ||3||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वारुण्यां वरुणसन्तानजातायामरण्यस्य प्रजापतेरात्मजायां पुष्करिण्यां चाक्षुषो मनं षष्ठमन्वन्तरपतिं जनयामासेत्यन्वयः ॥३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

In Puṣkariṇī, the daughter of Prajāpati Araṇya born in Varuṇa's lineage, Cākṣuṣa Manu, lord of the sixth Manvantara, was begotten.

VP 1.13.4

मनोर् अजायन्त दश नड्वलायां महौजसः।
कन्यायां तपतां श्रेष्ठ वैराजस्य प्रजापतेः॥४॥

From Manu, who was the excellent one among the sages, Nadvalaa, daughter of Vairaaja Prajaapati, had ten illustrious sons.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

manor ajāyanta daśa naḍvalāyāṃ mahaujasaḥ | kanyāyāṃ tapatāṃ śreṣṭha vairājasya prajāpateḥ || 4 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.5

कुरुः पुरुः शतद्युम्नस् तपस्वी सत्यवाञ् छुचिः।
अग्निष्टोमोऽतिरात्रश् च सुधुम्नश् चेति ते नव।
अभिमन्युश् च दशमो नड्वलायां महौजसः॥५॥

Their names are Kuru, Puru, Shatadyumna, Tapasvi, Satyavaan, Shuchi, Agnisthoma, Atiraatra, Sudyumna was ninth, tenth was Abhimanyu. They were very illustrious sons.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kuruḥ puruḥ śatadyumnas tapasvī satyavāñ chuciḥ | agniṣṭomo’tirātraś ca sudhumnaś ceti te nava | abhimanyuś ca daśamo naḍvalāyāṃ mahaujasaḥ || 5 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.6

कुरोर् अजनयत् पुत्रान् षड् आग्नेयी महा-प्रभान्।
अङ्गं सुमनसं ख्यातिं क्रतुम् अङ्गिरसं शिबिम्॥६॥

From Kuru, Aagneyee gave birth to six sons, they were all very illustrious. Anga, Sumanas, Khyaati, Kratu, Angiras, Shibi.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kuror ajanayat putrān ṣaḍ āgneyī mahāprabhān | aṅgaṃ sumanasaṃ khyātiṃ kratum aṅgirasaṃ śibim || 6 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.7

अङ्गात् सुनीथापत्यं वै वेनम् एकम् अजायत।
प्रजार्थम् ऋषयस् तस्य ममन्थुर् दक्षिणं करम्॥७॥

From Anga, Suneetha gave birth to Venaa. Venaa did not have children; in order to continue his progeny, the sages churned his right hand.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

vairājasya prajāpateḥ kanyāyām — in the daughter of Prajāpati Virāja; naḍvalāyām — in Nadvalā; manoh mahau-jasaḥ daśa putrāḥ — ten sons of Manu, possessing great energy; ajāyanta — were born.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aṅgāt sunīthāpatyaṃ vai venam ekam ajāyata | prajārtham ṛṣayas tasya mamanthur dakṣiṇaṃ karam || 7 ||

Śrīdhara

vairājasya prajāpateḥ kanyāyāṃ nadbalāyāṃ manormahaujaso daśa putrā ajāyantetyanvayaḥ ||4-7||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वैराजस्य प्रजापतेः कन्यायां नद्बलायां मनोर्महौजसो दश पुत्रा अजायन्तेत्यन्वयः ॥४-७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The ten sons of Manu were born from Nadvalā, the daughter of the Prajāpati Virāja.

VP 1.13.8

वेनस्य पाणौ मथिते सम्बभूव महामुने।
वैन्यो नाम महीपालो यः पृथुः परिकीर्तितः॥८॥

When they churned his right hand, a king was born, son of Venaa, well known as Pruthu.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

venasya pāṇau mathite sambabhūva mahāmune | vainyo nāma mahīpālo yaḥ pṛthuḥ parikīrtitaḥ || 8 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.9

येन दुग्धा मही पूर्वं प्रजानां हित-कारणात्॥९॥

By Pruthu, the earth was milked, for the good of all people.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yena dugdhā mahī pūrvaṃ prajānāṃ hitakāraṇāt || 9 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.10

किम्-अर्थं मथितः पाणिर् वेनस्य परमर्षिभिः।
यत्र जज्ञे महा-वीर्यः स पृथुर् मुनि-सत्तम॥१०॥

Maitreya asked - Why did the sages churn the right hand of Venaa? Where Pruthu was born, who had great valour.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kimarthaṃ mathitaḥ pāṇir venasya paramarṣibhiḥ | yatra jajñe mahāvīryaḥ sa pṛthur munisattama || 10 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.11

सुनीथा नाम या कन्या मृत्योः प्रथमतोऽभवत्।
अङ्गस्य भार्या सा दत्ता तस्यां वेनो व्यजायत॥११॥

Mrutyu had a daughter by name Suneethaa. She was given as wife of Anga. To them was born Venaa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sunīthā nāma yā kanyā mṛtyoḥ prathamato’bhavat | aṅgasya bhāryā sā dattā tasyāṃ veno vyajāyata || 11 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.12

स मातामह-दोषेण तेन मृत्योः सुतात्मजः।
निसर्गाद् एष मैत्रेय दुष्ट एव व्यजायत॥१२॥

Mrutyu's daughter's son Venaa, by nature itself, was of wicked mind. Because of some sin committed by his maternal grandfather.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sa mātāmahadoṣeṇa tena mṛtyoḥ sutātmajaḥ | nisargād eṣa maitreya duṣṭa eva vyajāyata || 12 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.13

अभिषिक्तो यदा राज्ये स वेनः परमर्षिभिः।
घोषयामास स तदा पृथिव्यां पृथिवी-पतिः॥१३॥

All the ṛṣis gathered and made him the king of that country. The king made a big announcement.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

abhiṣikto yadā rājye sa venaḥ paramarṣibhiḥ | ghoṣayāmāsa sa tadā pṛthivyāṃ pṛthivīpatiḥ || 13 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.14

न यष्टव्यं न दातव्यं न होतव्यं कथञ्चन।
भोक्ता यज्ञस्य कस् त्व् अन्यो ह्य् अहं यज्ञ-पतिः प्रभुः॥१४॥

Nobody should donate, nobody should perform any yajnya, nobody should perform any homa. I am the person who is to be worshipped by all the yajnyaas, I am the yajnya pati. Who else is there other than me? Who else is there who can receive the oblations offered in the yajnya? I am the king and am to be worshipped through Yajnyaas.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

punaruktiḥ — repetition; nadvālāyāṃ mahaujasaḥ — of Mahāujas in Nadvalā; ye daśa — the ten who; te ete — these are they.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na yaṣṭavyaṃ na dātavyaṃ na hotavyaṃ kathañcana | bhoktā yajñasya kas tv anyo hyahaṃ yajñapatiḥ prabhuḥ || 14 ||

Śrīdhara

nadbalāyāṃ mahaujasa iti punaruktistu nadbalāyāṃ mahaujaso ye daśetyanuvādena ta ete iti vidhānārthaḥ ||14||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नद्बलायां महौजस इति पुनरुक्तिस्तु नद्बलायां महौजसो ये दशेत्यनुवादेन त एते इति विधानार्थः ॥१४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The repetition of “in Nadvalā, of Mahāujas” is intended to restate that these ten are the sons of Mahāujas born from Nadvalā.

VP 1.13.15

ततस् तम् ऋषयः पूर्वं सम्पूज्य पृथिवी-पतिम्।
ऊचुः साम-फलं वाक्यं मैत्रेय समुपस्थिताः॥१५॥

All the great sages gathered, and tried to console him, tell him what is right and wrong. To make him understand what is the truth, they told him having made him happy by praising him.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

sāma-kalam — gentle, sweet; nāmanā — by name; kalam vākyam — a soft word; ūcuḥ — they spoke.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatas tam ṛṣayaḥ pūrvaṃ sampūjya pṛthivīpatim | ūcuḥ sāmaphalaṃ vākyaṃ maitreya samupasthitāḥ || 15 ||

Śrīdhara

sāmakalaṃ nāmnā kalaṃ madhuraṃ vākyamūcuḥ ||15||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सामकलं नाम्ना कलं मधुरं वाक्यमूचुः ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Sāma-kalam” means gentle, sweet words.

VP 1.13.16

ऋषय ऊचुः— भो भो राजन् शृणुष्व त्वं यद् वदाम महीपते।
राज्य-देहोपकाराय प्रजानां च हितं परम्॥१६॥

Ṛṣis said - O king, please do listen to what we shall tell you, which will do ultimate good for all the people of this kingdom, and you can also enjoy the kingdom, and also be happy.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

rājya-dehayoḥ upakārāya — for the benefit of the kingdom and the body; kṣemāya — for security and welfare.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bho bho rājan śṛṇuṣva tvaṃ yad vadāma mahīpate | rājyadehopakārāya prajānāṃ ca hitaṃ param || 16 ||

Śrīdhara

rājyadehayor upakārāya kṣemāya ||16||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

राज्यदेहयोर् उपकाराय क्षेमाय ॥१६॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“For the welfare of the kingdom and the body” means for their security and well-being.

VP 1.13.17

दीर्घ-सत्रेण देवेशं सर्व-यज्ञेश्वरं हरिम्।
पूजयिष्याम भद्रं ते तस्यांशस् ते भविष्यति॥१७॥

Let us worship Hari who is to be worshipped by all the yajnyaas. He is Sarva yajneshvara, Lord of all the gods also. Let us perform Deergha Satra yaaga, which goes for a long time, and worship Hari. You will also get a part of that havis (The king is supposed to get one sixth of the havis of the yajnyaas ).

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

dīrgha-satreṇa — by a long sacrifice; sahasra-saṃvatsareṇa — lasting a thousand years; aṃśaḥ — a share; ṣaṣṭhaḥ bhāgaḥ — a sixth part; puṇya-ṣaḍ-bhāgam ādatte — he takes a sixth of the merit.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dīrghasatreṇa deveśaṃ sarvayajñeśvaraṃ harim | pūjayiṣyāma bhadraṃ te tasyāṃśas te bhaviṣyati || 17 ||

Śrīdhara

dīrghasatreṇa sahasrasaṃvatsareṇa | aṃśaḥ ṣaṣṭho bhāgaḥ | puṇyaṣaḍbhāgamādatte nyāyena paripālayan iti smṛteḥ ||17||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

दीर्घसत्रेण सहस्रसंवत्सरेण । अंशः षष्ठो भागः । पुण्यषड्भागमादत्ते न्यायेन परिपालयन् इति स्मृतेः ॥१७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The long sacrifice is a thousand-year sacrifice. The king's share is one-sixth; scripture says that one who rules justly takes a sixth share of the merit.

VP 1.13.18

यज्ञेन यज्ञ-पुरुषो विष्णुः सम्प्रीणितो नृप।
अस्माभिर् भवतः कामान् सर्वान् एव प्रदास्यति॥१८॥

When the Yajnya Purusha, the God who is to be worshipped through the yajnyaas, who is none other than Sri Viṣṇu, if He is pleased through this yajnya, He will bestow all our desires, yours as well as ours.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajñena yajñapuruṣo viṣṇuḥ samprīṇito nṛpa | asmābhir bhavataḥ kāmān sarvān eva pradāsyati || 18 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.19

यज्ञैर् यज्ञेश्वरो येषां राष्ट्रे सम्पूजःय् हरिः।
तेषां सर्वेप्सितावाप्तिं ददाति नृप भूभृताम्॥१९॥

In any kingdom, if the Yajnyeshvara, Viṣṇu is worshipped, through the yajnya, all the king's desires will be fulfilled. He will bestow everything if He is worshipped through yajnyaas.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajñair yajñeśvaro yeṣāṃ rāṣṭre sampūjaḥy hariḥ | teṣāṃ sarvepsitāvāptiṃ dadāti nṛpa bhūbhṛtām || 19 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.20

वेन उवाच— मत्तः कोऽभ्यधिकोऽन्योऽस्ति कश् चाराध्यो ममापरः।
कोऽयं हरिर् इति ख्यातो यो वो यज्ञेश्वरो मतः॥२०॥

Who is there greater than me? Who else is there who is above me? Who else has to be worshipped other than me? You are telling that Hari is Yajnyeshvara, who is that?

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mattaḥ ko’bhyadhiko’nyo’sti kaś cārādhyo mamāparaḥ | ko’yaṃ harir iti khyāto yo vo yajñeśvaro mataḥ || 20 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.21

ब्रह्मा जनार्द्नः शम्भुर् इन्द्रो वायुर् यमो रविः।
हुतभुग् वरुणो धाता पूषा भूमिर् निशाकरः॥२१॥

Brahmā, Janārdana, Śambhu, Indra, Vāyu, Yama, the Sun, Fire, Varuṇa, Dhātā, Pūṣan, Earth, and the Moon.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahmā janārdnaḥ śambhur indro vāyur yamo raviḥ | hutabhug varuṇo dhātā pūṣā bhūmir niśākaraḥ || 21 ||

No commentary.

VP 1.13.22

एते चान्ये च ये देवाः शापानुग्रह-कारिणः।

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The earth was milked by ten classes, each taking its preferred vessel and milk, with a corresponding calf and milker produced by the power of that class. The gods used Indra as calf, Mitra as milker, a golden vessel, and strength as milk; sages used Soma, Bṛhaspati, the metres, and austerity/Brahman; demons used Virocana, Dvimūrdhan, an iron vessel, and magic; Rākṣasas used Sumāli, Jatunābha, a skull, and blood; mountains used Himavān, Meru, stone, and medicinal jewels; Gandharvas used Citraratha, Viśvāvasu, lotus, and fragrance; serpents used Takṣaka, Dhṛtarāṣṭra, a gourd, and poison; Yakṣas used Kubera, Sūkarṇa, clay, and invisibility; ancestors used Yama, Antaka, silver, and svadhā; trees used Plakṣa, Śāla, palāśa wood, and regrowth.
