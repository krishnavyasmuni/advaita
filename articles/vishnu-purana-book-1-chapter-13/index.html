VP 1.13.1

ध्रुवा च्छिष्टिं च भव्यं च भव्याच् छम्भुर् व्यजायत ।
शिष्टेर् आधत्त सुच्छाया पञ्चपुत्रान् अकल्मषान् ॥१॥

Dhruva had two sons - Shishti and Bhavya. Bhavya had a son named Shambhu. Shishti's wife Succhaaya gave birth to five sons, who were all without any blemish.

Words — duḥsvapnopaśamaṃ — lexical term; nṝṇāṃ — lexical term; śṛṇvatām — inflected form; etad — lexical term; uttamam — lexical term; pṛthor — lexical term; janma — birth; prabhāvaś — lexical term; ca — and; karoti — verbal form; satataṃ — lexical term; nṝṇām — inflected form; sense — Those who listen to this story of Pruthu, his greatness, all their bad dreams will be destroyed; they will never get bad dreams. His auspicious birth, his greatness, his qualities, capabilities, whoever recites all the time, they will get all good; for them there will be no bad dreams; all their bad deeds will get destroyed.
