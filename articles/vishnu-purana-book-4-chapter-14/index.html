VP 4.14.4

इत्येते शैनेयाः

They are all belonging to Shini vamsha, they are Shaineyaas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

itayete — They; śaineyāḥ — Shini

Śrīdhara

karamāgatā — They; vaṛṣaṇivaṃśāḥ — They; śaineyādayāśacaturadaṃśa. — They; varaṇayanate — are; śiśupālasaya — are; sāyujayañacātara — are; sūcayate — all; . — all; satayatasaya — all; sapatamaḥ — belonging; putaro — belonging; vaṛṣaṇisatataputarau — to; anamitara-śinī — to; . — to; tatarānamitarasaya — Shini; nighanataḥ — Shini; tasaya — Shini; parasenasatarāditāvitayeko — vamsha; vaṃsa — vamsha; ukataḥ — they; . — they; tatara — they; parasaṅagāgatasayamanatakodāraṇamukatama, — are; idānīmanamitarānujasayānayasaya — are; śineravaśayāḥ — are; śaineyāḥ — Shaineyaas; ||4|| — Shaineyaas

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityete śaineyāḥ

Śrīdhara

kramāgatā vṛṣṇivaṃśāḥ śaineyādyāścaturdaṃśa. varṇyante śiśupālasya sāyujyañcātra sūcyate . satyatasya saptamaḥ putro vṛṣṇistataputrau anamitra-śinī . tatrānamitrasya nighnataḥ tasya prasenasatrāditāvityeko vaṃsa uktaḥ . tatra prasaṅgāgatasyamantakodāraṇamuktam, idānīmanamitrānujasyānyasya śinervaśyāḥ śaineyāḥ ||४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

क्रमागता वृष्णिवंशाः शैनेयाद्याश्चतुर्दंश। वर्ण्यन्ते शिशुपालस्य सायुज्यञ्चात्र सूच्यते । सत्यतस्य सप्तमः पुत्रो वृष्णिस्ततपुत्रौ अनमित्र-शिनी । तत्रानमित्रस्य निघ्नतः तस्य प्रसेनसत्रादितावित्येको वंस उक्तः । तत्र प्रसङ्गागतस्यमन्तकोदारणमुक्तम्, इदानीमनमित्रानुजस्यान्यस्य शिनेर्वश्याः शैनेयाः ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

They are all belonging to Shini vamsha, they are Shaineyaas.

अक्षताः पूर्णा वीर्यादिगु यस्य सः । वीर्यं बलं, शौर्यमुत्साहः पराक्रमः प्रभावः । समाक्रान्ताः त्रैलोक्येश्वरा येन तथाभूतः प्रभावो यस्य सः ॥४८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Again, he was born as Dashaanana, Raavana, who had unparalleled and never decaying valiance, powerful, wealth, very strong asura; and he had captured Indra who was the lord of the three worlds. He was so very powerful.

VP 4.14.49

बहुकालोपभुक्तभगवत्सकाशावाप्तशरीरपातोद्भवपुण्यफलो भगवता राघवरूपिणा सोऽपि निधनमुपपादितः

He enjoyed for a long time. Because he was killed in his previous birth by saakshaat Bhagavaan, he had great punya, he enjoyed for a long time, and finally he was also killed by Bhagavaan who incarnated as Sri Raama.

VP 4.14.53

भगवान् यदि प्रसन्नो यथा अभिलषितं ददाति, तथा अग्रसन्नोपि निघ्नन् दिव्यमनुपमं स्थानं प्रयच्छति

When Bhagavaan is very pleased, He gives whatever is desired by one. At the same time, when He is not prasanna, not pleased, He will kill them, and then also give them a very coveted place.
