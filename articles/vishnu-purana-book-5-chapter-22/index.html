VP 5.22.1

जरासंधसुते कंस उपयेमे महाबलः । अस्तिं प्राप्तिं च मैत्रेय तयोर्भर्तृहणं हरिम्

Sri Paraasharar - Jaraasandha had two daughters, and both of them were married to Kamsa. They were Asti and Praapti. When Kamsa was killed, Jaraasandha got very angry against one who killed their husband.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jarāsandhasute kaṃsa upayeme mahābalaḥ . astiṃ prāptiṃ ca maitreya tayorbhartṛhaṇaṃ harim — Sri Paraasharar - Jaraasandha had two daughters, and both of them were married to Kamsa. They were Asti and Praapti. When Kamsa was killed, Jaraasandha got very angry against one who killed their husband.

Śrīdhara

jarāsandheti . asti-prāpti-sañjñe jarāsandha-sute tayor bhartṛ-haṇaṃ kaṃsa-hantāram — Sri Paraasharar - Jaraasandha had two daughters, and both of them were married to Kamsa. They were Asti and Praapti. When Kamsa was killed, Jaraasandha got very angry against one who killed their husband.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jarāsaṃdhasute kaṃsa upayeme mahābalaḥ . astiṃ prāptiṃ ca maitreya tayorbhartṛhaṇaṃ harim

Śrīdhara

jarāsandheti . asti-prāpti-sañjñe jarāsandha-sute tayor bhartṛ-haṇaṃ kaṃsa-hantāram ||१|| mahā-bala-parīvāro magadhādhipatir balī .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जरासन्धेति । अस्ति-प्राप्ति-सञ्ज्ञे जरासन्ध-सुते तयोर् भर्तृ-हणं कंस-हन्तारम् ॥१॥ महा-बल-परीवारो मगधाधिपतिर् बली ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

( ) Sri Paraasharar - Jaraasandha had two daughters, and both of them were married to Kamsa. They were Asti and Praapti. When Kamsa was killed, Jaraasandha got very angry against one who killed their husband.

VP 5.22.2

महा बलपरीवारो मगधाधिपतिर्बली । हन्तुमभ्याययौ कोपाज्जरासंधःसयादवम्

Śrīdhara

mahā-baleti . magadhādhipatir jarāsandhaḥ . sa-yādavaṃ yādava-sahitam — Taking a huge and powerful army along with him, the Magadha king Jaraasandha, came to kill Krishna and Balaraama, along with all the Yaadavaas.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

महा-बलेति । मगधाधिपतिर् जरासन्धः । स-यादवं यादव-सहितम् ॥२॥ उपेत्य मथुरां सोऽथ रुरोध मगधेश्वरः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Taking a huge and powerful army along with him, the Magadha king Jaraasandha, came to kill Krishna and Balaraama, along with all the Yaadavaas.

VP 5.22.9

जिते तस्मिन्सुदुर्वृत्ते जरासंधे महा मुने । जीवमाने गते कृष्णस्तेनामन्यत नाजितम्

When Jaraasandha was defeated, and Balaraama and Krishna won over, Jaraasandha ran back away from the war field, and because of this, he was not killed. Krishna thought that He has not really won over Jaraasandha.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jite tasminsudurvṛtte jarāsandhe mahāmune . jīvamāne gate kṛṣṇastenāmanyata nājitam — When Jaraasandha was defeated, and Balaraama and Krishna won over, Jaraasandha ran back away from the war field, and because of this, he was not killed. Krishna thought that He has not really won over Jaraasandha.

Śrīdhara

jita iti . taṃ nirjitaṃ nāmanyata punas tad-āgamasyāvaśyambhāvitvāt . pāṭhāntare tenaiva nirjitam ātmānam amanyatety arthaḥ — When Jaraasandha was defeated, and Balaraama and Krishna won over, Jaraasandha ran back away from the war field, and because of this, he was not killed. Krishna thought that He has not really won over Jaraasandha.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jite tasminsudurvṛtte jarāsaṃdhe mahā mune . jīvamāne gate kṛṣṇastenāmanyata nājitam

Śrīdhara

jita iti . taṃ nirjitaṃ nāmanyata punas tad-āgamasyāvaśyambhāvitvāt . pāṭhāntare tenaiva nirjitam ātmānam amanyatety arthaḥ ||९|| punar apy ājagāmātha jarāsandho balānvitaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जित इति । तं निर्जितं नामन्यत पुनस् तद्-आगमस्यावश्यम्भावित्वात् । पाठान्तरे तेनैव निर्जितम् आत्मानम् अमन्यतेत्य् अर्थः ॥९॥ पुनर् अप्य् आजगामाथ जरासन्धो बलान्वितः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

When Jaraasandha was defeated, and Balaraama and Krishna won over, Jaraasandha ran back away from the war field, and because of this, he was not killed. Krishna thought that He has not really won over Jaraasandha.

VP 5.22.13

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara commentary.

Because He is acting like a human, in this incarnation, this is His leelaa, His sport. He was using many kinds of astraas against His enemies.

VP 5.22.15

One who, by mere willing, can cause the dissolution of the whole world, and can also create the whole world by mere willing, for Him, destroying His enemies, is indeed effortless.

Śrīdhara

VP 5.22.16

तथाऽपि यो मनुष्याणां धर्मस्तमनुवर्तते । कुर्वन्बलवता संधिं हीनैर्युद्धं करोत्यसौ

Even in spite of that, He did not want to reveal His divine form, He was behaving just like a human. What He was doing was that, when a powerful enemy came, He would have an agreement with him; and when there were weak people attacking Him, He would fight against them; just like any human being. He did not want to reveal His divine nature.
