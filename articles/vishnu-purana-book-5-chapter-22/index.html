VP 5.22.1

जरासंधसुते कंस उपयेमे महाबलः ।
अस्तिं प्राप्तिं च मैत्रेय तयोर्भर्तृहणं हरिम्

Jarāsandha’s two daughters, Asti and Prāpti, were married by the mighty Kaṃsa, O Maitreya. Hari was the slayer of their husband.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
jarāsaṃdha-sute — the daughters of Jarāsandha;
kaṃsaḥ — Kaṃsa;
upayeme — married;
mahā-balaḥ — the very powerful one;
astim — Asti;
prāptiṃ ca — and Prāpti;
maitreya — O Maitreya;
tayoḥ — of those two;
bhartṛ-haṇam — the slayer of the husband;
harim — Hari.
Śrīdhara
jarāsandha-iti — on “Jarāsandha”;
asti-prāpti-sañjñe — named Asti and Prāpti;
jarāsandha-sute — the daughters of Jarāsandha;
tayoḥ — of those two;
bhartṛ-haṇam — the slayer of the husband;
kaṃsa-hantāram — the slayer of Kaṃsa
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jarāsaṃdhasute kaṃsa upayeme mahābalaḥ . astiṃ prāptiṃ ca maitreya tayor bhartṛhaṇaṃ harim

Śrīdhara

jarāsandheti . asti-prāpti-sañjñe jarāsandha-sute tayor bhartṛ-haṇaṃ kaṃsa-hantāram ||१|| mahā-bala-parīvāro magadhādhipatir balī .

Śrīdhara

jarāsandheti . asti-prāpti-sañjñe jarāsandha-sute tayor bhartṛ-haṇaṃ kaṃsa-hantāram

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जरासन्धेति । अस्ति-प्राप्ति-सञ्ज्ञे जरासन्ध-सुते तयोर् भर्तृ-हणं कंस-हन्तारम् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

On “Jarāsandha”: the daughters of Jarāsandha were named Asti and Prāpti; Hari was the slayer of their husband, Kaṃsa.

Śrīdhara

nanu cakriṇaḥ sannidhi-mātra-māhātm yāt tan na bhavati svayam evāneka-vidhāstra-prayogāt tatrāha — manuṣya-dharma-śīlasyeti . manuṣya-dharma-śīlasya sā līlā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ननु चक्रिणः सन्निधि-मात्र-माहात्म्यात् तन् न भवति स्वयम् एवानेक-विधास्त्र-प्रयोगात् तत्राह—मनुष्य-धर्म-शीलस्येति । मनुष्य-धर्म-शीलस्य सा लीला ॥१४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

On “Whose conduct is human”: one might object that the result is not merely due to the presence of the discus-bearer, since he himself uses many kinds of weapons. The answer is that this is the sport of one whose conduct is human.
