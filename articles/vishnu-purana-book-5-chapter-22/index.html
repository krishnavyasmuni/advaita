VP 5.22.1

जरासंधसुते कंस उपयेमे महाबलः ।
अस्तिं प्राप्तिं च मैत्रेय तयोर्भर्तृहणं हरिम्

Jarāsandha’s two daughters, Asti and Prāpti, were married by the mighty Kaṃsa, O Maitreya. Hari was the slayer of their husband.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jarāsaṃdhasute kaṃsa upayeme mahābalaḥ . astiṃ prāptiṃ ca maitreya tayor bhartṛhaṇaṃ harim

Śrīdhara

jarāsandheti . asti-prāpti-sañjñe jarāsandha-sute tayor bhartṛ-haṇaṃ kaṃsa-hantāram ||१|| mahā-bala-parīvāro magadhādhipatir balī .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जरासन्धेति । अस्ति-प्राप्ति-सञ्ज्ञे जरासन्ध-सुते तयोर् भर्तृ-हणं कंस-हन्तारम् ॥१॥

Word-for-word

jarāsaṃdha-sute — the daughters of Jarāsandha; kaṃsaḥ — Kaṃsa; upayeme — married; mahā-balaḥ — the very powerful one; astim — Asti; prāptiṃ ca — and Prāpti; maitreya — O Maitreya; tayoḥ — of those two; bhartṛ-haṇam — the slayer of the husband; harim — Hari.

Śrīdhara commentary.

( ) Sri Paraasharar - Jaraasandha had two daughters, and both of them were married to Kamsa. They were Asti and Praapti. When Kamsa was killed, Jaraasandha got very angry against one who killed their husband.

VP 5.22.2

महा बलपरीवारो मगधाधिपतिर्बली ।
हन्तुमभ्याययौ कोपाज्जरासंधःसयादवम्

The powerful lord of Magadha, accompanied by a great army, came in anger to kill the Yādavas.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mahā balaparīvāro magadhādhipatir balī . hantum abhyāyayau kopāj jarāsaṃdhaḥ sayādavam

Śrīdhara

mahā-baleti . magadhādhipatir jarāsandhaḥ . sa-yādavaṃ yādava-sahitam ||२|| upetya mathurāṃ so’tha rurodha magadheśvaraḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

महा-बलेति । मगधाधिपतिर् जरासन्धः । स-यादवं यादव-सहितम् ॥२॥

Word-for-word

mahā-bala-parīvāraḥ — having a great army as his retinue; magadhādhipatiḥ — the lord of Magadha; balī — powerful; hantum — to kill; abhyāyayau — came forth; kopāt — in anger; jarāsaṃdhaḥ — Jarāsandha; sa-yādavam — together with the Yādavas.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Taking a huge and powerful army along with him, the Magadha king Jaraasandha, came to kill Krishna and Balaraama, along with all the Yaadavaas.

VP 5.22.3

उपेत्य मथुरां सोऽथ रुरोध मगधेश्वरः ।
अक्षौहिणीभिः सैन्यस्य त्रयोविंशतिभिर्वृतः

The lord of Magadha came to Mathurā and besieged it, surrounded by twenty-three akṣauhiṇīs of troops.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

upetya mathurāṃ so ’tha rurodha magadheśvaraḥ . akṣauhiṇībhiḥ sainyasya trayoviṃśatibhir vṛtaḥ

Śrīdhara

upetyeti . sainyasya trayoviṃśaty akṣauhiṇībhir vṛtaḥ ||३|| niṣkramyālpaparīvārāv ubhau rāma-janārdanau .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उपेत्येति । सैन्यस्य त्रयोविंशत्य् अक्षौहिणीभिर् वृतः ॥३॥

Word-for-word

upetya — having come to; mathurām — Mathurā; saḥ — he; atha — then; rurodha — besieged; magadha-īśvaraḥ — the lord of Magadha; akṣauhiṇībhiḥ — with akṣauhiṇīs; sainyasya — of the army; trayo-viṃśatibhiḥ — with twenty-three; vṛtaḥ — surrounded.

Śrīdhara commentary.

He came with the huge army and attacked the Mathuraa Puri, with 23 Akshouhini sainya he brought along with him.

VP 5.22.4

निष्क्रम्याल्पपरीवारावुभौ रामजनार्दनौ ।
युयुधाते समं तस्य बलिनौ बलिसैनिकैः

Rāma and Janārdana, the two powerful heroes, went out with a small retinue and fought him together with his mighty soldiers.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

niṣkramyālpa-parīvārāv ubhau rāma-janārdanau . yuyudhāte samaṃ tasya balinau bali-sainikaiḥ

Śrīdhara

niṣkramyeti . tasya balibhiḥ sainikaiḥ saha ||४|| tatorāmaś cakṛṣṇaś ca matiṃ cakratur añjasā.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

निष्क्रम्येति । तस्य बलिभिः सैनिकैः सह ॥४॥

Word-for-word

niṣkramya — having gone out; alpa-parīvārau — with a small retinue; ubhau — both; rāma-janārdanau — Rāma and Janārdana; yuyudhāte — fought; samam — together; tasya — with him; balinau — the two powerful ones; bali-sainikaiḥ — with powerful soldiers.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Both Balaraama and Krishna came out of Mathuraa Puri, with very few people. They came and waged a war with Jaraasandha, who had a powerful army.

VP 5.22.5

VP 5.22.8

ततो युद्धे पराजित्य ससैन्यं मगधाधिपम् ।
पुरीं विविशतुर्वीरावुभौ रामजनार्दनौ

VP 5.22.9

जिते तस्मिन्सुदुर्वृत्ते जरासंधे महा मुने ।
जीवमाने गते कृष्णस्तेनामन्यत नाजितम्

When that wicked Jarāsandha had been defeated, O great sage, and had gone away alive, Kṛṣṇa did not regard him as conquered.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jite tasmin sudurvṛtte jarāsaṃdhe mahā mune . jīvamāne gate kṛṣṇas tenāmanyata nājitam

Śrīdhara

jita iti . taṃ nirjitaṃ nāmanyata punas tad-āgamasyāvaśyambhāvitvāt . pāṭhāntare tenaiva nirjitam ātmānam amanyatety arthaḥ ||९|| punar apy ājagāmātha jarāsandho balānvitaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जित इति । तं निर्जितं नामन्यत पुनस् तद्-आगमस्यावश्यम्भावित्वात् । पाठान्तरे तेनैव निर्जितम् आत्मानम् अमन्यतेत्य् अर्थः ॥९॥

Word-for-word

jite — when defeated; tasmin — he; sudurvṛtte — the very wicked one; jarāsaṃdhe — Jarāsandha; mahā mune — O great sage; jīvamāne — while alive; gate — having gone; kṛṣṇaḥ — Kṛṣṇa; tena — by that; amanyata — considered; na ajitam — not conquered.

Śrīdhara commentary.

When Jaraasandha was defeated, and Balaraama and Krishna won over, Jaraasandha ran back away from the war field, and because of this, he was not killed. Krishna thought that He has not really won over Jaraasandha.

VP 5.22.10

VP 5.22.12

VP 5.22.13

न तद्बलं यादवानां विजितं यदनेकशः ।
तत्तु संनिधिमाहात्म्यं विष्णोरंशस्य चक्रिणः

It was not the Yādavas’ army that defeated him repeatedly; rather, it was the majesty of the presence of Viṣṇu, the discus-bearer’s portion.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na tad balaṃ yādavānāṃ vijitaṃ yad anekaśaḥ . tat tu saṃnidhi-māhātmyaṃ viṣṇor aṃśasya cakriṇaḥ

Śrīdhara

neti . na vijitaṃ jarāsandheneti śeṣaḥ ||१३|| manuṣya-dharma-śīlasya līlā sā jagatī-pateḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नेति । न विजितं जरासन्धेनेति शेषः ॥१३॥

Word-for-word

na — not; tat balam — that army; yādavānām — of the Yādavas; vijitam — the victorious one / that which defeated; yat — which; anekaśaḥ — repeatedly; tat tu — rather that; saṃnidhi-māhātmyam — the greatness of the presence; viṣṇoḥ — of Viṣṇu; aṃśasya — of the portion; cakriṇaḥ — of the discus-bearer.

Śrīdhara commentary.

The army of Yaadavaas, which was very small, was not won over by Jaraasandha, though he had a huge army. How did this happen? This was because of the mere presence of Krishna, Vishnu's Amsha, saakshaat Krishna.

VP 5.22.14

मनुष्यधर्मशीलस्य लीला सा जगतीपतेः ।
अस्त्राण्यनेकरूपाणि यदरातिषु मुञ्चति

That is the sport of the Lord of the world, who, while following the conduct of a human, releases weapons of many forms against his enemies.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

manuṣya-dharma-śīlasya līlā sā jagatīpateḥ . astrāṇy aneka-rūpāṇi yad arīṣu muñcati

Śrīdhara

nanu cakriṇaḥ sannidhi-mātra-māhātmyāt tan na bhavati svayam evāneka-vidhāstra-prayogāt tatrāha—manuṣya-dharma-śīlasyeti . manuṣya-dharma-śīlasya sā līlā ||१४|| manasaiva jagat-sṛṣṭiṃ saṃhāraṃ tu karoti yaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ननु चक्रिणः सन्निधि-मात्र-माहात्म्यात् तन् न भवति स्वयम् एवानेक-विधास्त्र-प्रयोगात् तत्राह—मनुष्य-धर्म-शीलस्येति । मनुष्य-धर्म-शीलस्य सा लीला ॥१४॥

Word-for-word

manuṣya-dharma-śīlasya — of one whose conduct is human; līlā — sport; sā — that; jagatī-pateḥ — of the Lord of the world; astrāṇi — weapons; aneka-rūpāṇi — of many forms; yat — because / who; arīṣu — against enemies; muñcati — releases.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Because He is acting like a human, in this incarnation, this is His leelaa, His sport. He was using many kinds of astraas against His enemies.

VP 5.22.15

मनसैव जगत्सृष्टिं संहारं च करोति यः ।
तस्यापिपक्षक्षपणे कियानुद्यमविस्तरः

He who by mind alone creates and dissolves the universe—what exertion is required of him to destroy the hosts of his enemies?

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

manasā eva jagat-sṛṣṭiṃ saṃhāraṃ ca karoti yaḥ . tasyāpi pakṣa-kṣapaṇe kiyān udyama-vistaraḥ

Śrīdhara

līlātvam eva prapañcayati—manasaiveti yāvat samāpti ||१५|| tathāpi ca manuṣyāṇāṃ dharmas tadanuvartate.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

लीलात्वम् एव प्रपञ्चयति—मनसैवेति यावत् समाप्ति ॥१५॥

Word-for-word

manasā eva — by the mind alone; jagat-sṛṣṭim — the creation of the universe; saṃhāram ca — and dissolution; karoti — does; yaḥ — who; tasya api — even for him; pakṣa-kṣapaṇe — in destroying the enemy side; kiyān — how much; udyama-vistaraḥ — extent of exertion.

Śrīdhara commentary.

One who, by mere willing, can cause the dissolution of the whole world, and can also create the whole world by mere willing, for Him, destroying His enemies, is indeed effortless.
