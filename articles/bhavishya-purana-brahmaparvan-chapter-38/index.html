Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 38 — The Fifth Day Serpent Rite in Āśvayuja

BP 1.38.1

॥ ॥ सुमंतुरुवाच ॥ ॥
तथा चाश्वयुजे मासि पञ्चम्यां कुरुनंदन ॥
कृत्वा कुशमयान्नागान्गंधांद्यैः संप्रपूजयेत् ॥ ॥

Sumantu said. Likewise, O descendant of Kuru, in the month of Āśvayuja, on the fifth lunar day, one should make serpents of sacred grass and worship them thoroughly with perfumes and other offerings.

सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
आश्वयुजे मासि पञ्चम्याम् (āśvayuje māsi pañcamyām) — on Pañcamī in Āśvayuja;
कुरुनन्दन (kuru-nandana) — descendant of Kuru;
कृत्वा कुशमयान् नागान् (kṛtvā kuśamayān nāgān) — fashioning Nāgas from kuśa;
गन्धाद्यैः सम्प्रपूजयेत् (gandhādyaiḥ sampra-pūjayet) — thoroughly worship with fragrance and more

BP 1.38.2

घृतोदकाभ्यां पयसा स्नपयित्वा विशांपते ॥
गोधूमैः पयसा स्विन्नैर्भक्ष्यैश्च विविधैस्तथा ॥ ॥

Having bathed them with water mixed with ghee and with milk, O lord of the people, one should offer wheat cooked in milk and various foods.

घृतोदकाभ्याम् पयसा (ghṛtodakābhyām payasā) — with ghee-water and milk;
स्नपयित्वा (snapayitvā) — after bathing;
विशाम्पते (viśāmpate) — ruler of people;
गोधूमैः पयसा स्विन्नैः (godhūmaiḥ payasā svinnaiḥ) — wheat cooked in milk;
भक्ष्यैः विविधैः (bhakṣyaiḥ vividhaiḥ) — various foods

BP 1.38.3

यस्त्वस्यां विधिवन्नागाञ्छुचिर्भक्त्या समन्वितः ॥
पूजयेत्कुरुशार्दूल तस्य शेषादयो नृप ॥ ॥

O tiger of the Kurus, O king, whoever worships the serpents properly on this day, pure and devoted, has Śeṣa and the other serpents.

यः अस्याम् विधिवत् नागान् (yaḥ asyām vidhivat nāgān) — one who duly worships Nāgas on this day;
शुचिः भक्त्या समन्वितः (śuciḥ bhaktyā samanvitaḥ) — pure and devoted;
पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — should worship;
तस्य शेषादयः नागाः (tasya śeṣādayaḥ nāgāḥ) — Śeṣa and other Nāgas toward that person

BP 1.38.4

नागाः प्रीता भवंतीह शांतिमाप्नोति वा विभो ॥
स शांतिलोकमासाद्य मोदते शाश्वतीः समाः ॥ ॥

The serpents are pleased here, and he attains peace, O lord. Having reached the world of peace, he rejoices for everlasting years.

नागाः प्रीताः भवन्ति (nāgāḥ prītāḥ bhavanti) — Nāgas become pleased;
शान्तिम् आप्नोति (śāntim āpnoti) — obtains peace;
शान्तिलोकम् आसाद्य (śānti-lokam āsādya) — reaching world of peace;
मोदते शाश्वतीः समाः (modate śāśvatīḥ samāḥ) — delights for eternal years

BP 1.38.9

इत्येष कथितो वीर पञ्चमीकल्प उत्तमः ।
यत्रायमुच्यते मंत्रः सर्वसर्पनिषेधकः ॥ ॥

Thus, O hero, this excellent rite of the fifth lunar day has been told, in which the formula that wards off all serpents is recited.

इति एषः कथितः (iti eṣaḥ kathitaḥ) — thus this has been told;
पञ्चमीकल्पः उत्तमः (pañcamī-kalpaḥ uttamaḥ) — excellent Pañcamī procedure;
यत्र अयम् उच्यते मन्त्रः (yatra ayam ucyate mantraḥ) — in which this mantra is spoken;
सर्वसर्पनिषेधकः (sarva-sarpa-niṣedhakaḥ) — warding off all snakes;
ॐ कुरुकुल्ले फट् स्वाहा (oṃ kurukulle phaṭ svāhā) — stated mantra
