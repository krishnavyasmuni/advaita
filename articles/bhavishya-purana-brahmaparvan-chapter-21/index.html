Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 21 — Gaurī’s Vow and the Imperishable Third Lunar Day

BP 1.21.1

सुमन्तरुवाच
पतिव्रता पतिप्राणा पतिशुश्रूषणे रता ।
एवंविधापि या प्रोक्ता शुचिः संशोभना सती ॥

Sumantu said: A chaste wife, devoted to her husband as to her life, delighting in serving him, is described as pure, radiant, and faithful.

सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
पति-व्रता (pati-vratā) — faithful wife;
पति-प्राणा (pati-prāṇā) — husband is her life;
पति-शुश्रूषणे रता (pati-śuśrūṣaṇe ratā) — devoted to serving husband;
शुचिः (śuciḥ) — pure;
संशोभना सती (saṃśobhanā satī) — beautiful virtuous woman

BP 1.21.2

सोपवासा तृतीयां तु लवणं परिवर्जयेत् ।
सा गृह्णाति च वै भक्त्या व्रतमामरणान्तिकम् ॥

She should fast on the third lunar day and abstain from salt. With devotion she undertakes this vow until the end of her life.

सा उपवासा (sā upavāsā) — she fasting;
तृतीयाम् (tṛtīyām) — on third day;
लवणम् परिवर्जयेत् (lavaṇam parivarjayet) — should avoid salt;
भक्त्या (bhaktyā) — devotionally;
व्रतम् गृह्णाति (vratam gṛhṇāti) — undertakes vow;
आ-मरण-आन्तिकम् (ā-maraṇa-āntikam) — until death

BP 1.21.3

गौरी ददाति सन्तुष्टा रूपं सौभाग्यमेव च ।
लावण्यं ललितं हृद्यं श्लाघ्यं पुंसां मनोरमम् ॥

When pleased, Gaurī grants beauty and good fortune, grace, charm, loveliness, praiseworthiness, and delightfulness to men.

गौरी सन्तुष्टा (gaurī santuṣṭā) — Gaurī pleased;
ददाति (dadāti) — gives;
रूपम् (rūpam) — beauty;
सौभाग्यम् (saubhāgyam) — marital good fortune;
लावण्यम् (lāvaṇyam) — grace;
ललितम् हृद्यम् (lalitam hṛdyam) — charming delightful;
श्लाघ्यम् (ślāghyam) — praiseworthy;
पुंसाम् मनोरमम् (puṃsām manoramam) — attractive to men

BP 1.21.4

पुंसो मनोरमा नारी भर्त्ता नार्या मनोरमः ।
गौरीव्रतेन भवति राजँल्लवणवर्जनात् ॥

Through Gaurī's vow and abstaining from salt, O king, a woman becomes pleasing to her husband, and her husband pleasing to her.

पुंसः मनोरमा नारी (puṃsaḥ manoramā nārī) — woman delightful to man;
भर्ता नार्याः मनोरमः (bhartā nāryāḥ manoramaḥ) — husband delightful to woman;
गौरी-व्रतेन (gaurī-vratena) — through Gaurī vow;
लवण-वर्जनात् (lavaṇa-varjanāt) — by avoiding salt

BP 1.21.5

इदं व्रतं प्रति विभो धर्मराजस्य शृण्वतः ।
उमया च पुरा प्रोक्तं यद्वाक्यं तन्निबोध मे ॥

O mighty one, concerning this vow, hear from me the words that Umā once spoke while Dharmarāja listened.

इदम् व्रतम् प्रति (idam vratam prati) — concerning this vow;
विभो (vibho) — O lord;
धर्म-राजस्य शृण्वतः (dharma-rājasya śṛṇvataḥ) — while Dharmarāja listened;
उमया पुरा प्रोक्तम् (umayā purā proktam) — formerly spoken by Umā;
तत् वाक्यं निबोध (tat vākyaṃ nibodha) — understand those words

BP 1.21.6

मया व्रतमिदं सृष्टं सौभाग्यकरणं नृणाम् ।
मर्त्ये तु नियता नारी व्रतमेतच्चरिष्यति ॥
सह भर्त्रा स मोदेत यथा भर्ता हरो मम ॥

“I created this vow to bring good fortune to people. A self-controlled mortal woman who observes this vow will rejoice with her husband, just as Hara is my husband.”

मया व्रतम् इदम् सृष्टम् (mayā vratam idam sṛṣṭam) — this vow created by me;
सौभाग्य-करणम् नृणाम् (saubhāgya-karaṇam nṛṇām) — producing good fortune for people;
मर्त्या नियता नारी (martyā niyatā nārī) — disciplined mortal woman;
व्रतम् चरिष्यति (vratam cariṣyati) — performs vow;
सह भर्त्रा मोदेत (saha bhartrā modeta) — rejoices with husband;
यथा भर्ता हरः मम (yathā bhartā haraḥ mama) — as Hara is my husband

BP 1.21.7

या च कन्या न भर्तारं विन्दते शोभना सती ।
सा त्विदं व्रतमुद्दिश्य भवेदक्षारभोजना ॥
मच्चित्ता मन्मनाः कुर्यान्मद्भक्ता मत्परिग्रहा ॥

“A beautiful, chaste maiden who does not find a husband should undertake this vow and eat food without salt. With her mind fixed on me, devoted to me, and given to me, she should perform it.”

कन्या (kanyā) — maiden;
न भर्तारम् विन्दते (na bhartāram vindate) — does not find husband;
व्रतम् उद्दिश्य (vratam uddiśya) — undertaking vow;
अक्षार-भोजना (akṣāra-bhojanā) — eating without alkaline salt;
मत्-चित्ता मत्-मनाः (mat-cittā mat-manāḥ) — consciousness and mind on me;
मत्-भक्ता (mat-bhaktā) — devoted to me;
मत्-परिग्रहा (mat-parigrahā) — under my protection

BP 1.21.8

गौरीं संस्थाप्य सौवर्णीं गन्धालङ्कारभूषिताम् ।
वस्त्रालङ्कारसंवीतां पुष्पमण्डलमण्डिताम् ॥

She should install a golden image of Gaurī, adorned with scents and ornaments, clothed and adorned, and decorated with a circle of flowers.

गौरीम् संस्थाप्य सौवर्णीम् (gaurīm saṃsthāpya sauvarṇīm) — installing golden Gaurī;
गन्ध-अलङ्कार-भूषिताम् (gandha-alaṅkāra-bhūṣitām) — adorned with fragrance and ornaments;
वस्त्र-अलङ्कार-संवीताम् (vastra-alaṅkāra-saṃvītām) — wrapped in garments and jewellery;
पुष्प-मण्डल-मण्डिताम् (puṣpa-maṇḍala-maṇḍitām) — decorated with flower circle

BP 1.21.9

लवणं गुडं घृतं तैलं देव्यै शक्त्या निवेदयेत् ।
कटुखण्डं जीरकं च पत्रशाकं च भारत ॥

She should offer the goddess salt, molasses, ghee, and oil according to her means, and also pieces of pungent spice, cumin, and leafy greens, O Bharata.

लवणम् गुडम् घृतम् तैलम् (lavaṇam guḍam ghṛtam tailam) — salt molasses ghee oil;
देव्यै शक्त्या निवेदयेत् (devyai śaktyā nivedayet) — offer goddess according to means;
कटु-खण्डम् (kaṭu-khaṇḍam) — pungent pieces;
जीरकम् (jīrakam) — cumin;
पत्र-शाकम् (patra-śākam) — leafy greens

BP 1.21.10

गुडघृष्टांस्तथापूपान्खडवेष्टांस्तथा नृप ।
ब्राह्मणे व्रतसम्पन्ने प्रदद्यात्सुबहुश्रुते ॥

O king, she should give molasses-coated cakes and wrapped cakes to a Brahmin who has completed vows and is deeply learned.

गुड-घृष्टान् अपूपान् (guḍa-ghṛṣṭān apūpān) — molasses-coated cakes;
खड-वेष्टान् (khaḍa-veṣṭān) — wrapped delicacies;
प्रदद्यात् (pradadyāt) — should give;
ब्राह्मणे व्रत-सम्पन्ने (brāhmaṇe vrata-sampanne) — to brāhmaṇa accomplished in vows;
सु-बहु-श्रुते (su-bahu-śrute) — very learned

BP 1.21.11

शुक्लपक्षे सदा देया यथा शक्त्या हिरण्मयी ।
धनहीने तु भक्त्या च मधुवृक्षमयी नृप ॥

During the bright fortnight, a golden image should always be given according to one's means. One without wealth may, with devotion, give one made of mahua wood, O king.

शुक्ल-पक्षे सदा देया (śukla-pakṣe sadā deyā) — always given in bright fortnight;
यथा-शक्त्या हिरण्मयी (yathā-śaktyā hiraṇmayī) — golden according to means;
धन-हीने (dhana-hīne) — when without wealth;
भक्त्या (bhaktyā) — devotionally;
मधु-वृक्ष-मयी (madhu-vṛkṣa-mayī) — made of madhūka wood

BP 1.21.12

अर्च्या नित्यं संनिधानात्तत्र गौरी न संशयः ।
अक्षारलवणं रात्रौ भुंक्ते चैव सुवाग्यता ॥

It should be worshipped daily, for Gaurī is undoubtedly present there. At night the fortunate woman eats food without alkali or salt.

अर्च्या नित्यम् (arcyā nityam) — worshipped daily;
सन्निधानात् तत्र गौरी (sannidhānāt tatra gaurī) — Gaurī present there;
न संशयः (na saṃśayaḥ) — no doubt;
अक्षार-लवणम् (akṣāra-lavaṇam) — without alkali and salt;
रात्रौ भुङ्क्ते (rātrau bhuṅkte) — eats at night;
सु-वाग्-यता (su-vāg-yatā) — speech restrained

BP 1.21.13

गौरी सन्निहिता नित्यं भूमौ प्रस्तरशायिनी ।
एवं नियमयुक्तस्य देव्या यत्समुदाहृतम् ॥

Gaurī is always present where she lies on a stone upon the ground. Hear the great result declared by the goddess for one who follows this discipline,

गौरी सन्निहिता नित्यम् (gaurī sannihitā nityam) — Gaurī always present;
भूमौ प्रस्तर-शायिनी (bhūmau prastara-śāyinī) — sleeping on stone on ground;
नियम-युक्तस्य (niyama-yuktasya) — of disciplined observer;
देव्या समुदाहृतम् (devyā samudāhṛtam) — declared by goddess;
शृणु महा-फलम् (śṛṇu mahā-phalam) — hear great fruit

BP 1.21.14

तच्छृणुष्व महाबाहो कथ्यमानं महाफलम् ।
भर्तारं तु लभेत्कन्या यं वाच्छति मनोऽनुगम् ॥

O mighty-armed one: a maiden obtains the husband she desires, the one who follows her heart.

भर्तारम् लभेत् कन्या (bhartāram labhet kanyā) — maiden obtains husband;
यम् वाञ्छति (yam vāñchati) — whom she desires;
मनः-अनुगम् (manaḥ-anugam) — following her heart

BP 1.21.15

सुचिरं सह वै भर्त्रा क्रीडयित्वा इहैव सा ।
सन्ततिं च प्रतिष्ठाप्य सह तेनैव गच्छति ॥

After sporting with that husband here for a long time and establishing offspring, she goes with him.

सु-चिरम् सह भर्त्रा (su-ciram saha bhartrā) — very long with husband;
क्रीडयित्वा इह (krīḍayitvā iha) — enjoying here;
सन्ततिम् प्रतिष्ठाप्य (santatim pratiṣṭhāpya) — establishing progeny;
सह तेन एव गच्छति (saha tena eva gacchati) — departs with him

BP 1.21.16

हेलिलोकं चन्द्रलोकं लोकं चित्रशिखण्डिनः ।
गत्वा याति सदो राजन्वामदेवस्य भारत ॥

Having gone to Heli's world, the Moon's world, and the world of Citraśikhaṇḍin, she goes to the assembly of Vāmadeva, O king, O Bharata.

हेलि-लोकम् चन्द्र-लोकम् (heli-lokam candra-lokam) — realms of Heli and Moon;
लोकम् चित्रशिखण्डिनः (lokam citraśikhaṇḍinaḥ) — Citraśikhaṇḍin’s realm;
गत्वा (gatvā) — having gone;
याति सदः वामदेवस्य (yāti sadaḥ vāmadevasya) — goes to Vāmadeva’s assembly

BP 1.21.17

विधवा तु यदा राजन्देव्या व्रतपरायणा ।
भर्त्तारं नियता नित्यं सदार्चनपरायणा ॥

When a widow is devoted to the goddess's vow, O king, disciplined concerning her husband and always intent upon worship—

विधवा यदा (vidhavā yadā) — when widow;
देव्याः व्रत-परायणा (devyāḥ vrata-parāyaṇā) — devoted to goddess’s vow;
भर्तारम् (bhartāram) — for husband;
नियता नित्यम् (niyatā nityam) — constantly disciplined;
सदा अर्चन-परायणा (sadā arcana-parāyaṇā) — always intent on worship

BP 1.21.18

इह चोत्सृज्य देहं स्वं दृष्ट्वा हरिपुरे प्रियम् ।
आक्षिप्य यमदूतेभ्यः सह भर्त्रा रमेद्दिवि ॥

—leaving her body here, she sees her beloved in Hari's city, draws him away from Yama's messengers, and rejoices in heaven with her husband.

इह उत्सृज्य देहम् स्वम् (iha utsṛjya deham svam) — leaving own body here;
दृष्ट्वा हरि-पुरे प्रियम् (dṛṣṭvā hari-pure priyam) — seeing beloved in Hari’s city;
आक्षिप्य यम-दूतेभ्यः (ākṣipya yama-dūtebhyaḥ) — rescuing from Yama’s messengers;
सह भर्त्रा रमेत् दिवि (saha bhartrā ramet divi) — delights with husband in heaven

BP 1.21.19

वर्षकोटिं दशगुणां रमित्वा सा इहागता ।
भर्त्रा सहैव पूर्वोक्तं लभते फलमीप्सितम् ॥

After enjoying there for ten crores of years, she returns here and, together with her husband, obtains the desired result already described.

वर्ष-कोटिम् दश-गुणाम् (varṣa-koṭim daśa-guṇām) — ten crores of years;
रमित्वा (ramitvā) — enjoying;
इह आगता (iha āgatā) — returning here;
भर्त्रा सह एव (bhartrā saha eva) — together with husband;
पूर्व-उक्तम् फलम् ईप्सितम् लभते (pūrva-uktam phalam īpsitam labhate) — obtains desired result stated before

BP 1.21.20

इन्द्राण्यापि व्रतमिदं पुत्रार्थिन्या नराधिप ।
लब्धः पुत्रो व्रतस्यान्ते जयन्तो नाम नामतः ॥

Indrāṇī also undertook this vow, desiring a son, O king; at the vow's end a son named Jayanta was obtained.

इन्द्राण्या अपि व्रतम् इदम् (indrāṇyā api vratam idam) — this vow also by Indrāṇī;
पुत्र-अर्थिन्या (putra-arthinyā) — desiring son;
व्रत-अन्ते (vrata-ante) — at vow’s end;
लब्धः पुत्रः (labdhaḥ putraḥ) — son obtained;
जयन्तः नाम (jayantaḥ nāma) — named Jayanta

BP 1.21.21

अरुन्धत्या तथा चीर्णं वशिष्ठं प्रति कामतः ।
दृश्यते दिवि चाद्यापि वशिष्ठस्य समीपतः ॥

Arundhatī also observed it out of love for Vasiṣṭha; even today she is seen in heaven beside Vasiṣṭha.

अरुन्धत्या तथा चीर्णम् (arundhatyā tathā cīrṇam) — likewise performed by Arundhatī;
वसिष्ठम् प्रति कामतः (vasiṣṭham prati kāmataḥ) — from love for Vasiṣṭha;
दृश्यते दिवि अद्य अपि (dṛśyate divi adya api) — seen in heaven even today;
वसिष्ठस्य समीपतः (vasiṣṭhasya samīpataḥ) — beside Vasiṣṭha

BP 1.21.22

रोहिण्या लवणत्यागात्सपन्नीगणमर्दनम् ।
लब्धं देव्याः प्रसादेन सौभाग्यमचलं दिवि ॥

By giving up salt, Rohiṇī, through the goddess's favor, obtained the defeat of her co-wives and unshakable good fortune in heaven.

रोहिण्या लवण-त्यागात् (rohiṇyā lavaṇa-tyāgāt) — by Rohiṇī’s salt abandonment;
सपत्नी-गण-मर्दनम् (sapatnī-gaṇa-mardanam) — supremacy over co-wives;
देव्याः प्रसादेन (devyāḥ prasādena) — by goddess’s grace;
सौभाग्यम् अचलम् (saubhāgyam acalam) — unwavering good fortune;
दिवि (divi) — in heaven

BP 1.21.23

इत्येषा तिथिरित्येव तृतीया लोकपूजिता ।
सदा विशेषतः पुण्या वैशाखे मासि या भवेत् ॥

Thus this third lunar day is honored by the world. It is always especially sacred when it occurs in the month of Vaiśākha.

इति एषा तिथिः (iti eṣā tithiḥ) — thus this lunar day;
तृतीया (tṛtīyā) — third;
लोक-पूजिता (loka-pūjitā) — world-worshipped;
सदा पुण्या (sadā puṇyā) — always sacred;
विशेषतः (viśeṣataḥ) — especially;
वैशाखे मासि (vaiśākhe māsi) — in Vaiśākha

BP 1.21.24

पुण्या भाद्रपदे मासि माघेप्येवं न संशयः ।
माघे भाद्रपदे चापि स्त्रीणां धन्या प्रचक्षते ॥

It is sacred in Bhādrapada and likewise in Māgha, no doubt. The third day in Māgha and Bhādrapada is declared fortunate for women.

पुण्या भाद्रपदे (puṇyā bhādrapade) — sacred in Bhādrapada;
माघे अपि एवम् (māghe api evam) — likewise in Māgha;
न संशयः (na saṃśayaḥ) — no doubt;
स्त्रीणाम् धन्या (strīṇām dhanyā) — fortunate for women;
प्रचक्षते (pracakṣate) — proclaimed

BP 1.21.25

साधारणी तु वैशाखे सर्वलोकस्य भारत ।
माघमासे तृतीयायां गुडस्य लवणस्य च ॥
दानं श्रेयस्करं राजन्स्त्रीणां च पुरुषस्य च ॥

O Bharata, in Vaiśākha it is common to all the world. On the third day of Māgha, giving molasses and salt brings welfare to women and men.

साधारणी वैशाखे (sādhāraṇī vaiśākhe) — common to all in Vaiśākha;
सर्व-लोकस्य (sarva-lokasya) — for everyone;
माघ-मासे तृतीयायाम् (māgha-māse tṛtīyāyām) — on Māgha third day;
गुडस्य लवणस्य दानम् (guḍasya lavaṇasya dānam) — gift of molasses and salt;
श्रेयस्-करम् (śreyas-karam) — welfare-producing;
स्त्रीणाम् पुरुषस्य च (strīṇām puruṣasya ca) — for women and men

BP 1.21.26

गुडेन तुष्यते दत्तो लवणेन तु विश्वभूः ।
गुडपूपास्तु दातव्या मासि भाद्रपदे तथा ॥

The recipient is pleased by molasses, and the creator of the universe by salt. In Bhādrapada, molasses cakes should also be given.

गुडेन तुष्यते दत्तः (guḍena tuṣyate dattaḥ) — recipient satisfied by molasses;
लवणेन विश्वभूः (lavaṇena viśvabhūḥ) — Viśvabhū by salt;
गुड-पूपाः दातव्याः (guḍa-pūpāḥ dātavyāḥ) — molasses cakes to be given;
भाद्रपदे मासि (bhādrapade māsi) — in Bhādrapada month

BP 1.21.27

तृतीयायां तु माघस्य वामदेवस्य प्रीतये ।
वारिदानं प्रशस्तं स्यान्मोदकानां च भारत ॥

On Māgha's third day, giving water and sweet dumplings is praised for the pleasure of Vāmadeva, O Bharata.

तृतीयायाम् माघस्य (tṛtīyāyām māghasya) — on Māgha third day;
वामदेवस्य प्रीतये (vāmadevasya prītaye) — for Vāmadeva’s pleasure;
वारि-दानम् प्रशस्तम् (vāri-dānam praśastam) — water gift praised;
मोदकानाम् च (modakānām ca) — and sweetmeats

BP 1.21.28

वैशाखे मासि राजेन्द्र तृतीया चन्दनस्य च ।
वारिणा तुष्यते वेधा मोदकैर्भीम एव हि ॥
दानात्तु चन्दनस्येह कञ्जजो नात्र संशयः ॥

O king of kings, on Vaiśākha's third day the Creator is pleased by water, Bhīma by sweet dumplings, and the lotus-born by a gift of sandalwood, no doubt.

वैशाखे मासि तृतीया (vaiśākhe māsi tṛtīyā) — Vaiśākha third day;
वारिणा तुष्यते वेधाः (vāriṇā tuṣyate vedhāḥ) — Creator pleased by water;
मोदकैः भीमः (modakaiḥ bhīmaḥ) — Bhīma by sweets;
चन्दनस्य दानात् (candanasya dānāt) — by sandalwood gift;
कञ्ज-जः (kañja-jaḥ) — lotus-born;
न संशयः (na saṃśayaḥ) — no doubt

BP 1.21.29

यात्वेषा कुरुशार्दूल वैशाखे मासि वै तिथिः ।
तृतीया साऽक्षया लोके गीर्वाणैरभिनन्दिता ॥

O tiger among the Kurus, this third lunar day in Vaiśākha is called Akṣayā in the world and is praised by the gods.

वैशाखे मासि तिथिः (vaiśākhe māsi tithiḥ) — date in Vaiśākha;
तृतीया सा अक्षया (tṛtīyā sā akṣayā) — that third day is Akṣayā;
लोके (loke) — in world;
गीर्वाणैः अभिनन्दिता (gīrvāṇaiḥ abhinanditā) — welcomed by gods

BP 1.21.30

आगतेयं महाबाहो भूरि चन्द्रं वसुव्रता ।
कलधौतं तथान्नं च घृतं चापि विशेषतः ॥
यद्यद्दत्तं त्वक्षयं स्यात्तेनेयमक्षया स्मृता ॥

When it arrives, O mighty-armed one, abundant silver, food, and especially ghee should be given. Whatever is given becomes imperishable; therefore it is remembered as Akṣayā.

आगता इयम् (āgatā iyam) — this having arrived;
भूरि कलधौतम् (bhūri kaladhautam) — abundant silver;
अन्नम् (annam) — food;
घृतम् विशेषतः (ghṛtam viśeṣataḥ) — especially ghee;
यत् यत् दत्तम् (yat yat dattam) — whatever given;
अक्षयम् स्यात् (akṣayam syāt) — becomes imperishable;
तेन अक्षया स्मृता (tena akṣayā smṛtā) — therefore remembered Akṣayā

BP 1.21.31

यत्किञ्चिद्दीयते दानं स्वल्पं वा यदि वा बहु ।
तत्सर्वमक्षयं स्याद्वै तेनेयमक्षया स्मृता ॥

Whatever gift is given on this day, whether little or much, all of it becomes imperishable; therefore it is remembered as Akṣayā.

यत् किञ्चित् दीयते दानम् (yat kiñcit dīyate dānam) — whatever gift is given;
स्वल्पम् वा बहु वा (svalpam vā bahu vā) — little or much;
तत् सर्वम् अक्षयम् स्यात् (tat sarvam akṣayam syāt) — all becomes imperishable;
तेन इयम् अक्षया स्मृता (tena iyam akṣayā smṛtā) — therefore called Akṣayā

BP 1.21.32

योऽस्यां ददाति करकन्वारिबीजसमन्वितान् ।
स याति पुरुषो वीर लोकं वै हेममालिनः ॥

O hero, a man who gives water-jars supplied with water and seeds on this day goes to the world of the golden-garlanded one.

यः अस्याम् ददाति (yaḥ asyām dadāti) — one who gives on this day;
करकान् (karakān) — water-pots;
वारि-बीज-समन्वितान् (vāri-bīja-samanvitān) — furnished with water and seeds;
सः पुरुषः याति (saḥ puruṣaḥ yāti) — that person goes;
लोकम् हेममालिनः (lokam hemamālinaḥ) — to Hemamālin’s realm

BP 1.21.33

इत्येषा कथिता वीर तृतीया तिथिरुत्तमा ।
यामुपोष्य नरो राजन्नृद्धिं वृद्धिं श्रियं भजेत् ॥

Thus, O hero, the excellent third lunar day is described. By fasting on it, O king, a man partakes of prosperity, increase, and good fortune.

इति एषा कथिता (iti eṣā kathitā) — thus this described;
तृतीया तिथिः उत्तमा (tṛtīyā tithiḥ uttamā) — excellent third day;
याम् उपोष्य (yām upoṣya) — fasting on which;
नरः (naraḥ) — person;
ऋद्धिम् वृद्धिम् श्रियम् भजेत् (ṛddhim vṛddhim śriyam bhajet) — partakes prosperity increase splendour
