VP 3.1.1

कथिता गुरुणा सम्यग्भूसमुद्रादिसंस्थितिः । सूर्यादीनां च संस्थानं ज्योतिषां चातिविस्तरात्

What was fully narrated by you, my teacher, is the arrangement of the earth, oceans, and the rest; also the arrangement of the sun and other luminaries, and of the stars in great detail.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
कथिता गुरुना सम्यक् (kathitā gurunā samyak) — fully narrated by the teacher
भू-समुद्र-आदि-संस्थितिः (bhū-samudra-ādi-saṃsthitiḥ) — arrangement of earth, oceans, and the rest
सूर्य-आदीनाम् च संस्थानम् (sūrya-ādīnām ca saṃsthānam) — arrangement of the sun and others
ज्योतिषाम् च अति-विस्तरात् (jyotiṣām ca ati-vistarāt) — and of the luminaries in great detail
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kathitā guruṇā samyagbhūsamudrādisaṃsthitiḥ | sūryādīnāṃ ca saṃsthānaṃ jyotiṣāṃ cātivistarāt ||1||

Śrīdhara

parānandapadāmbhojaśrīdharaḥ śrīdharo yatiḥ | purāṇe vaiṣṇave vyākhyāt tṛtīyāṃśe yathāmati || aṃśadvayena sṛṣṭyādidvisaptabhuvanoktibhiḥ | adhyāropya niṣiddhaṃ tad rājadvijasaduktibhiḥ || tṛtīyāṃśe manuvyāsadharmādyāḥ sthitihetavaḥ | varṇyante'tra niṣedhāya kauravyapṛtanā yathā || atrāpi prathame'dhyāye saptamanvantarāṇi tu | bhavadbhūtāni kathitānyavatārā harer api || vṛttakīrtanapūrvakaṃ manvantarāṇāṃ svarūpaṃ pṛcchati—kathitā iti caturbhiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

परानन्दपदाम्भोज-श्रीधरः श्रीधरो यतिः । पुराणे वैष्णवे व्याख्यात् तृतीयांशे यथामति ॥ अंशद्वयेन सृष्ट्यादिद्विसप्तभुवनोक्तिभिः । अध्यारोप्य निषिद्धं तद् राजद्विजसदुक्तिभिः ॥ तृतीयांशे मनुव्यासधर्माद्याः स्थितिहेतवः । वर्ण्यन्तेऽत्र निषेधाय कौरव्यपृतना यथा ॥ अत्रापि प्रथमेऽध्याये सप्तमन्वन्तराणि तु । भवद्भूतानि कथितान्यवतारा हरेरपि ॥ वृत्तकीर्तनपूर्वकं मन्वन्तराणां स्वरूपं पृच्छति—कथिता इति चतुर्भिः

Śrīdhara commentary.

What was fully narrated by you, my teacher, is the arrangement of the earth, oceans, and the rest; also the arrangement of the sun and other luminaries, and of the stars in great detail.

VP 3.1.5

अतीतानागतानीह यानि मन्वन्तराणि वै । तान्यहं भवतः सम्यक्कथयामि यथाक्रमम्

I will properly narrate to you, in sequence, the Manvantaras that are past and those that are future.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
अतीत-अनागतानि इह (atīta-anāgatāni iha) — the past and future here
यानि मन्वन्तराणि वै (yāni manvantarāṇi vai) — which indeed are the Manvantaras
तानि अहम् कथयामि (tāni aham kathayāmi) — those I narrate
भवतः सम्यक् यथाक्रमम् (bhavataḥ samyak yathākramam) — to you properly, in sequence
Śrīdhara
iha vārāha-kalpe — in this Varāha Kalpa;
yathākramam — by ‘in sequence’;
krama-prāptam vartamānam api manvantaram — the present Manvantara, which has arrived in the sequence;
jñeyam — is to be understood
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

atītānāgatānīha yāni manvantarāṇi vai | tānyahaṃ bhavataḥ samyakkathayāmi yathākramam || 5 ||

Śrīdhara

iha vārāhakalpe | yathākramamityanena kramaprāptaṃ vartamānamapi manvantaraṃ jñeyam ||5||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इह वाराहकल्पे । यथाक्रममित्यनेन क्रमप्राप्तं वर्तमानमपि मन्वन्तरं ज्ञेयम्

Śrīdhara commentary.

In the Varāha Kalpa. By the expression ‘in sequence,’ the present Manvantara, which has arrived in the sequence, is also to be understood.

No commentary.

VP 3.1.8

स्वायंभुवं तु कथितं कल्पादावन्तरं मया । देवास्तत्रर्षयश्चैव यथावत्कथिता मया

I have already described the Svāyambhuva Manvantara at the beginning of the Kalpa; the gods and sages there were also properly described by me.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
स्वायम्भुवम् तु कथितम् (svāyambhuvam tu kathitam) — the Svāyambhuva has been described
कल्प-आदौ अन्तरम् मया (kalpa-ādau antaram mayā) — by me as the interval at the beginning of the Kalpa
देवाः तत्र ऋषयः च एव (devāḥ tatra ṛṣayaḥ ca eva) — the gods and sages there also
यथावत् कथिताः मया (yathāvat kathitāḥ mayā) — properly described by me
Śrīdhara
kalpasya ādau — at the beginning of the Kalpa;
svāyambhuva-manvantaram tu kathitam — the Svāyambhuva Manvantara was described;
prathamāṃśe — in the first Book;
devāḥ yāma-ākhyāḥ — the gods called Yāmas;
ṛṣayaḥ marīcy-ādayaḥ — the sages beginning with Marīci;
cakārāt indraḥ yajñaḥ — by ‘and,’ Indra Yajña;
manu-putrau priyavrata-uttānapādau — the two sons of Manu, Priyavrata and Uttānapāda;
kathitau — were described
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

svāyaṃbhuvaṃ tu kathitaṃ kalpādāvantaraṃ mayā | devāstatrarṣayaścaiva yathāvatkathitā mayā ||8||

Śrīdhara

kalpasya ādau svāyambhuvamanvantarantu kathitaṃ, prathamāṃśe devā yāmākhyā ṛṣayo marīcyādayaḥ | cakārādindro yajñaḥ | manuputrau priyavratottānapādau kathitau ||8||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कल्पस्य आदौ स्वायम्भुवमन्वन्तरन्तु कथितं, प्रथमांशे देवा यामाख्या ऋषयो मरीच्यादयः । चकारादिन्द्रो यज्ञः । मनुपुत्रौ प्रियव्रतोत्तानपादौ कथितौ

Śrīdhara commentary.

At the beginning of the Kalpa, the Svāyambhuva Manvantara was described. In the first Book, the gods called Yāmas and the sages beginning with Marīci were [described]. By the word ‘and,’ Indra Yajña is included. The two sons of Manu, Priyavrata and Uttānapāda, were described.

VP 3.1.10

अत ऊर्ध्वं प्रवक्ष्यामि मनोस्स्वारोचिषस्य तु । मन्वन्तराधिपान्सम्यग्देवर्षिस्तत्सुतांस्तथा ॥९॥ पारावतास्सतुषिता देवास्स्वारोचिषेऽन्तरे । विपश्चित्तत्र देवेन्द्रो मैत्रेयासीन्महाबलः

Transliteration

Śrīdhara commentary.

The Pārāvatas and Tuṣitas are the two groups of gods; Vipaścit is the Indra of the gods.

VP 3.1.11

अत ऊर्ध्वं प्रवक्ष्यामि मनोस्स्वारोचिषस्य तु । मन्वन्तराधिपान्सम्यग्देवर्षिस्तत्सुतांस्तथा ॥९॥ पारावतास्सतुषिता देवास्स्वारोचिषेऽन्तरे । विपश्चित्तत्र देवेन्द्रो मैत्रेयासीन्महाबलः ॥१०॥ ऊर्जस्स्तम्भस्तथा प्राणो दत्तोग्नी ऋषभस्तथा । निरयश्च परीवांश्च तत्र सप्तर्षयोऽभवन्
