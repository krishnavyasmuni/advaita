  • Chapter 85 — The Kāmadā Observance

BP 1.85.1

brahmovāca |
prāpte mārgaśīrṣe māsi śukla-ṣaṣṭhyāṃ tu yo bhavet |
sa jñeyaḥ kāmado vāraḥ sa iṣṭo bhāskarasya tu || 1 ||

Brahmā said: The day that occurs on the sixth day of the bright fortnight in the month of Mārgaśīrṣa is to be known as the Kāmadā day, beloved of Bhāskara.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
prāpte — having arrived;
mārgaśīrṣe māsi — in the month of Mārgaśīrṣa;
śukla-ṣaṣṭhyām — on the sixth day of the bright fortnight;
tu — indeed;
yaḥ — which;
bhavet — occurs;
saḥ — that;
jñeyaḥ — to be known;
kāmadaḥ — Kāmadā;
vāraḥ — day;
saḥ — that;
iṣṭaḥ — beloved;
bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
tu — indeed.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | prāpte mārgaśīrṣe māsi śukla-ṣaṣṭhyāṃ tu yo bhavet | sa jñeyaḥ kāmado vāraḥ sa iṣṭo bhāskarasya tu || 1 ||

BP 1.85.2

tatra yaḥ pūjayed bhānuṃ bhaktyā śraddhā-samanvitaḥ |
vimuktaḥ sarva-pāpais tu prāpṇute nandanādhipam || 2 ||

Whoever there worships Bhānu with devotion and faith, freed from every sin, attains the Lord of Nandana.

BP 1.85.3

rakta-candana-miśrāṇi karavīrāṇi suvrata |
dhūpaṃ ghṛtāhutiṃ vīra bhāskarasya prayojayet || 3 ||

O well-disciplined one, he should use karavīra flowers mixed with red sandalwood, and, O hero, he should offer incense and oblations of ghee to Bhāskara.

vīra — O hero;
bhāskarasya — for Bhāskara;
prayojayet — should employ, offer.
Transliteration
rakta-candana-miśrāṇi karavīrāṇi suvrata | dhūpaṃ ghṛtāhutiṃ vīra bhāskarasya prayojayet || 3 ||

BP 1.85.4

naivedyaṃ cāpi kṛśaraṃ su-gandhaṃ tīkṣṇam eva ca |
kṛtvopavāsam atha vā naktaṃ tripura-sūdana || 4 ||

He should offer kṛśara as food, fragrant and sharp, and should observe a fast or else the night observance, O destroyer of Tripura.

BP 1.85.5

itthaṃ prapūjito hy atra bhāskaro loka-bhāskaraḥ |
kāmān dadāti sarvān vai ato 'yaṃ kāmadaḥ smṛtaḥ || 5 ||

Bhāskara, the illuminator of the world, being worshiped in this way here, grants every desire; therefore he is remembered as Kāmadā.

ayam — this one;
kāmadaḥ — Kāmadā, giver of desires;

Transliteration
itthaṃ prapūjito hy atra bhāskaro loka-bhāskaraḥ | kāmān dadāti sarvān vai ato 'yaṃ kāmadaḥ smṛtaḥ || 5 ||

BP 1.85.6

sa putraṃ putra-kāmasya dhana-kāmasya vā dhanam |
vidyārthine śubhāṃ vidyām ārogyaṃ rogiṇe vibho || 6 ||

He gives a son to one who desires a son, wealth to one who desires wealth, auspicious learning to one who seeks knowledge, and health to the sick, O Lord.

rogiṇe — to the sick person;
vibho — O Lord.
Transliteration
sa putraṃ putra-kāmasya dhana-kāmasya vā dhanam | vidyārthine śubhāṃ vidyām ārogyaṃ rogiṇe vibho || 6 ||

BP 1.85.7

BP 1.85.8

dadyād yo maṇḍakaṃ cātra gopater gotra-bhūṣaṇaḥ |
gotrāri-tejasā tulyo gopater gopuraṃ vrajet || 8 ||
