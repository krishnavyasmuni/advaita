VP 4.5.1

इक्ष्वाकुतनयो योऽसौ निमिर्नाम्ना, स तु सहस्रं वत्सरं सत्रमारेभ

Sri Paraasharar - Ikshvaaku's son Nimi, started a Satra which goes for 1000 years. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ikṣvākutanayo yo’sau nimirnāma sahasraṃ vatsaraṃ satramārebhe — Sri Paraasharar - Ikshvaaku's son Nimi, started a Satra which goes for 1000 years. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ikṣvākutanayo yo’sau nimirnāmnā, sa tu sahasraṃ vatsaraṃ satramārebha

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.5.4

VP 4.5.6

सोऽपि तत्काल एवान्यैर्गौतमादिभिर्यागमकरोत्

Nimi, meanwhile, invited others like Gautama, and other rishis and started his Satra.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

so’pi tatkāla evāna‍्yairgautamādibhiryāgamakarot — Nimi, meanwhile, invited others like Gautama, and other rishis and started his Satra.

Śrīdhara

maunaṃ sammatilakṣaṇamiti yuktyaiva samanvīpsimanumatamiti matvā tatra gatvā yāgamakarot — Nimi, meanwhile, invited others like Gautama, and other rishis and started his Satra.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मौनं सम्मतिलक्षणमिति युक्त्यैव समन्वीप्सिमनुमतमिति मत्वा तत्र गत्वा यागमकरोत् ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Nimi, meanwhile, invited others like Gautama, and other rishis and started his Satra.

VP 4.5.8

तत्कर्म्मकर्त्तृत्वं च तत्र गौतमस्य दृष्ट्वा स्वपते स तस्मै राज्ञे मामप्रत्याख्यायैव तदनेन गौतमाय कर्म्मान्तरं समर्पितं यस्मात्, तस्मादयं विदेहो भविष्यातीति शापं ददौ

Vasishta comes here and sees that the responsibility to perform that Satra, is with Gautama, who is performing it, and King Nimi was sleeping at that time; that without even informing me, the karma, opportunity to Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 perform this Satra is given to Gautama, without even informing me, and because of this, he will be without a body, he gave a curse.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatkarmakartṛtvaṃ ca gautamasya dṛṣṭvā svapate tasmai rājñe māṃ pratyākhyāyaitadanena gautamāya karmāntaraṃ samarpitaṃ yasmāttasmādayaṃ videho bhaviṣyatīti śāpaṃ dadau — Vasishta comes here and sees that the responsibility to perform that Satra, is with Gautama, who is performing it, and King Nimi was sleeping at that time; that without even informing me, the karma, opportunity to Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 perform this Satra is given to Gautama, without even informing me, and because of this, he will be without a body, he gave a curse.

Śrīdhara

māmapratyāvakhyāyeti pūrva maduttare pratyuramadarttvaṃva yadvā indrayāgāt māmanivāryaiva, etat karmāntaraṃ nimermanuṣṭitapūrva pūrvakarmavat etat karmāntaraṃ madanuṣṭheyaṃ gautamāya samarpitaṃ karmaṇi antaramalpasyāvakāśo datta iti vārthaḥ . śīghradehapātabhayādidaṃ karma ārabdhamityato videha eva bhavatu iti śāpaṃ dadau — Vasishta comes here and sees that the responsibility to perform that Satra, is with Gautama, who is performing it, and King Nimi was sleeping at that time; that without even informing me, the karma, opportunity to Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 perform this Satra is given to Gautama, without even informing me, and because of this, he will be without a body, he gave a curse.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatkarmmakarttṛtvaṃ ca tatra gautamasya dṛṣṭvā svapate sa tasmai rājñe māmapratyākhyāyaiva tadanena gautamāya karmmāntaraṃ samarpitaṃ yasmāt, tasmādayaṃ videho bhaviṣyātīti śāpaṃ dadau

Śrīdhara

māmapratyāvakhyāyeti pūrva maduttare pratyuramadarttvaṃva yadvā indrayāgāt māmanivāryaiva, etat karmāntaraṃ nimermanuṣṭitapūrva pūrvakarmavat etat karmāntaraṃ madanuṣṭheyaṃ gautamāya samarpitaṃ karmaṇi antaramalpasyāvakāśo datta iti vārthaḥ . śīghradehapātabhayādidaṃ karma ārabdhamityato videha eva bhavatu iti śāpaṃ dadau ||८||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मामप्रत्यावख्यायेति पूर्व मदुत्तरे प्रत्युरमदर्त्त्वंव यद्वा इन्द्रयागात् मामनिवार्यैव, एतत् कर्मान्तरं निमेर्मनुष्टितपूर्व पूर्वकर्मवत् एतत् कर्मान्तरं मदनुष्ठेयं गौतमाय समर्पितं कर्मणि अन्तरमल्पस्यावकाशो दत्त इति वार्थः । शीघ्रदेहपातभयादिदं कर्म आरब्धमित्यतो विदेह एव भवतु इति शापं ददौ ॥८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Vasishta comes here and sees that the responsibility to perform that Satra, is with Gautama, who is performing it, and King Nimi was sleeping at that time; that without even informing me, the karma, opportunity to Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 perform this Satra is given to Gautama, without even informing me, and because of this, he will be without a body, he gave a curse.

VP 4.5.10

यस्मान्मामसम्भाष्याजानत एव शयानस्य शापोत्सर्गमसौ दुष्टगुरुश्वकार, तस्मात् तस्यापि देहः पतिष्यतीति प्रतिशापं दत्त्वा देहमत्यजत्

This bad guru, without even talking to me and without my knowledge, when I was sleeping, gave me a curse, and he also cursed Vasishta that his body also will fall, and then he gave up his body.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yasmānmāmasambhāṣyājñānata eva śayānasya śāpotsargamasau duṣṭaguruścakāra tasmāttasyāpi dehaḥ patiṣyatīti śāpaṃ dattvā dehamatyajat — This bad guru, without even talking to me and without my knowledge, when I was sleeping, gave me a curse, and he also cursed Vasishta that his body also will fall, and then he gave up his body.

Śrīdhara

mām asambhāṣyeti māṃ vinaiva gautamadvārā kimiti karma kṛtamiti māṃ pratyanuktvety arthaḥ — This bad guru, without even talking to me and without my knowledge, when I was sleeping, gave me a curse, and he also cursed Vasishta that his body also will fall, and then he gave up his body.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yasmānmāmasambhāṣyājānata eva śayānasya śāpotsargamasau duṣṭaguruśvakāra, tasmāt tasyāpi dehaḥ patiṣyatīti pratiśāpaṃ dattvā dehamatyajat

Śrīdhara

mām asambhāṣyeti māṃ vinaiva gautamadvārā kimiti karma kṛtamiti māṃ pratyanuktvety arthaḥ ||१०||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

माम् असम्भाष्येति मां विनैव गौतमद्वारा किमिति कर्म कृतमिति मां प्रत्यनुक्त्वेत्य् अर्थः ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

This bad guru, without even talking to me and without my knowledge, when I was sleeping, gave me a curse, and he also cursed Vasishta that his body also will fall, and then he gave up his body.

VP 4.5.12

उर्व्वशीदर्शनादुद्भूतबीजप्रपातयोस्तयोः सकाशाद्वशिष्ठो देहमपरं लेभे

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तेजसि वीर्ये तेजो लिङ्गशरीरम्, चेत इति पाठे स एवार्थः । पश्चादुद्भूतो वीर्यप्रपातो वीर्यच्युतिर्ययोस्तादृशयोः ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

When Mitra and Varuna saw Oorvashi, they got excited, and their veerya skalana happened, and because of this, Vasishta got another body, he was born with another body.

VP 4.5.13

निमेरपि तच्छरीरमतिमनोहरगंधतैलादिभरुप संस्क्रियमाणं नैव क्लेदादिकं दोषमवाप, सद्योमृतमिव तस्थौ

Śrīdhara

It was as good as when he was there. The body remained as though he was just dead.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nimerapi taccharīramatimanoharagaṃdhatailādibharupa saṃskriyamāṇaṃ naiva kledādikaṃ doṣamavāpa, sadyomṛtamiva tasthau

Śrīdhara

mṛtasya dehadhāraṇaṃ yajñasamāptiṃ vaṃśapravṛtti coddiśyeti vaktuṃ dehadhāraṇaprakāramāha—nimer iti ||१३||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मृतस्य देहधारणं यज्ञसमाप्तिं वंशप्रवृत्ति चोद्दिश्येति वक्तुं देहधारणप्रकारमाह—निमेर् इति ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Nimi's body was also protected, with wonderful fragrant oil, and was maintained very well, and it never deteriorated in any way. It was as good as when he was there. The body remained as though he was just dead.

VP 4.5.18

तदहमिच्छामि सकललोकलोचनेषु वस्तुम्, न पुनः शरीरग्रहणं कर्त्तुमित्येवमुक्तैर्देवैरसावशेष भूतानां नैत्रेष्ववतारितः

Then the devaas said let it be so, and he got into the eyes of all the beings.

Transliteration
