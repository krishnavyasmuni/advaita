  • Chapter 95 — The Greatness of the Solar Temple

BP 1.95.1

brahmovāca |
tribhiḥ pradakṣiṇāṃ kṛtvā yo namaskurute ravim |
bhūmau gatena śirasā sa yāti paramāṃ gatim || 1 ||

Brahmā said: Whoever circumambulates Ravi three times and bows to him with his head lowered to the ground goes to the highest destination.

Word-for-word
brahmā uvāca — Brahmā said;
tribhiḥ — three times;
pradakṣiṇām kṛtvā — having circumambulated;
yaḥ — whoever;
namaskurute — bows;
ravim — to Ravi;
bhūmau — on the ground;
gatena śirasā — with the head lowered;
saḥ yāti — he goes;
paramām gatim — to the highest destination.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | tribhiḥ pradakṣiṇāṃ kṛtvā yo namaskurute ravim | bhūmau gatena śirasā sa yāti paramāṃ gatim || 1 ||

BP 1.95.2

sopānatko devagṛhamārohedyastu mānavaḥ |
sa yāti narakaṃ ghoraṃ tāmisraṃ nāma nāmataḥ || 2 ||

A person who climbs into the temple of the deity while wearing sandals goes to the terrible hell called Tāmisra.

Word-for-word
sa-upān-atkaḥ — wearing sandals;
deva-gṛham — the temple of the deity;
ārohet — should climb;
yaḥ tu — whoever;
mānavaḥ — person;
saḥ yāti — he goes;
narakam — to hell;
ghoram — terrible;
tāmisram — Tāmisra;
nāma — by name.
Transliteration
sopānatko devagṛhamārohedyastu mānavaḥ | sa yāti narakaṃ ghoraṃ tāmisraṃ nāma nāmataḥ || 2 ||

BP 1.95.3

śleṣmamūtrapurīṣāṇi samutsṛjati yastu vai |
devasyāyatane bhānoḥ sa gacchennarakaṃ kramāt || 3 ||

Whoever releases phlegm, urine, or feces in the temple of Bhānu goes in due course to hell.

Word-for-word
śleṣma — phlegm;
mūtra — urine;
purīṣāṇi — feces;
samutsṛjati — releases;
yaḥ tu — whoever;
vai — indeed;
devasya āyatane — in the temple of the deity;
bhānoḥ — of Bhānu;
saḥ gacchet — he goes;
narakam — to hell;
kramāt — in due course.
Transliteration
śleṣmamūtrapurīṣāṇi samutsṛjati yastu vai | devasyāyatane bhānoḥ sa gacchennarakaṃ kramāt || 3 ||

BP 1.95.4

ghṛtaṃ madhu payastoyaṃ tathekṣurasamuttamam |
snapanārthaṃ tu devasya ye dadatīha mānavāḥ |
sarvakāmānavāpyeha te yānti helimaṇḍalam || 4 ||

Those people who give ghee, honey, milk, water, and excellent sugarcane juice for the bathing of the deity obtain every desire here and go to the orb of Heli.

Word-for-word
ghṛtam — ghee;
madhu — honey;
payaḥ — milk;
toyam — water;
tathā — likewise;
ikṣu-rasa-uttamam — excellent sugarcane juice;
snāpana-artham — for bathing;
devasya — of the deity;
ye dadati — those who give;
sarva-kāmān — every desire;
avāpya — obtaining;
helimaṇḍalam — the orb of Heli;
yānti — they go.
Transliteration
ghṛtaṃ madhu payastoyaṃ tathekṣurasamuttamam | snapanārthaṃ tu devasya ye dadatīha mānavāḥ | sarvakāmānavāpyeha te yānti helimaṇḍalam || 4 ||

BP 1.95.5

snāpyamānaṃ raviṃ bhaktyā ye paśyanti vṛṣadhvaja |
te'śvamedhaphalaṃ prāpya layaṃ yānti vṛṣadhvaje || 5 ||

Those who devotedly behold Ravi while he is being bathed, O bull-bannered one, obtain the fruit of the Aśvamedha and merge into the bull-bannered Lord.

Word-for-word
snāpyamānam — being bathed;
ravim — Ravi;
bhaktyā — with devotion;
ye paśyanti — those who see;
vṛṣadhvaja — O bull-bannered one;
te — they;
aśvamedha-phalam — fruit of the Aśvamedha;
prāpya — obtaining;
layam yānti — go to absorption;
vṛṣadhvaje — in the bull-bannered one.
Transliteration
snāpyamānaṃ raviṃ bhaktyā ye paśyanti vṛṣadhvaja | te'śvamedhaphalaṃ prāpya layaṃ yānti vṛṣadhvaje || 5 ||

BP 1.95.6

snapanaṃ ye ca kurvaṃti bhānorbhaktisamanvitāḥ |
labhante tatphalaṃ bhīma rājasūyāśvamedhayoḥ || 6 ||

Those who perform the bathing of Bhānu with devotion obtain the fruit of the Rājasūya and Aśvamedha, O Bhīma.

Word-for-word

bhakti-samanvitāḥ — endowed with devotion;
labhante — obtain;
tat-phalam — that fruit;
bhīma — O mighty one;
rājasūya-aśvamedhayoḥ — of the Rājasūya and Aśvamedha.
Transliteration
snapanaṃ ye ca kurvaṃti bhānorbhaktisamanvitāḥ | labhante tatphalaṃ bhīma rājasūyāśvamedhayoḥ || 6 ||

BP 1.95.7

yathā na laṅghayetkaścitsnapanaṃ bhāskarasya tu |
tathā kāryaṃ prayatnena laṅghitaṃ hyasukhāvaham || 7 ||

One should take care that no one passes over the bathing of Bhāskara; for when it is passed over, it brings unhappiness.

tathā — thus;
kāryam — it should be done;
prayatnena — with effort;
laṅghitam — passed over;
asukhāvaham — bringing unhappiness.
Transliteration
yathā na laṅghayetkaścitsnapanaṃ bhāskarasya tu | tathā kāryaṃ prayatnena laṅghitaṃ hyasukhāvaham || 7 ||

BP 1.95.8

tāmisraṃ narakaṃ yāti laṅghayecca sa rauravān |
tasmātsarvaprayatnena kāryaṃ snapanamāditaḥ || 8 ||

tasmāt — therefore;
sarva-prayatnena — with every effort;
kāryam — should be performed;
snapanaṃ — bathing;
āditaḥ — from the beginning.
Transliteration
tāmisraṃ narakaṃ yāti laṅghayecca sa rauravān | tasmātsarvaprayatnena kāryaṃ snapanamāditaḥ || 8 ||

BP 1.95.9

ghṛtena snāpayeddevaṃ kañjamāpnoti mānavaḥ |
madhunā priyamāyāti toyenāpi ghṛtaukasam || 9 ||

Bathing the deity with ghee, a person attains the Lotus-born; with honey he reaches the beloved one, and with water he reaches the ghee-dwelling one.

Word-for-word

devam — the deity;
kañjam — the Lotus-born;
āpnoti — attains;
mānavaḥ — person;
madhunā — with honey;
priyam — the beloved;
āyāti — reaches;
to yena api — even with water;
ghṛtaukasam — the ghee-dwelling one.
Transliteration
ghṛtena snāpayeddevaṃ kañjamāpnoti mānavaḥ | madhunā priyamāyāti toyenāpi ghṛtaukasam || 9 ||

BP 1.95.10

ikṣurasena saṃsnāpya payasā kañjaśadhvajam |
evamebhiḥ snāpayedvai ravimīhitamāpnuyāt || 10 ||

Having bathed the lotus-bannered deity with sugarcane juice and milk, and bathing Ravi with these substances, one obtains the desired result.

vai — indeed;
ravim — Ravi;
īhitam — desired result;
āp­nuyāt — should obtain.
Transliteration
ikṣurasena saṃsnāpya payasā kañjaśadhvajam | evamebhiḥ snāpayedvai ravimīhitamāpnuyāt || 10 ||
