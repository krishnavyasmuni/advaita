Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 20 — The Never-Empty Bed Vow, or the Fruit Second-Lunar-Day Vow

BP 1.20.1

शतानीक उवाच
ब्रूहि मे द्विजशार्दूल द्वितीयां फलसंज्ञिताम् ।
यामुपोष्य नरो योषिद्वियोगं नेह गच्छति ॥

Śatānīka said: Tell me, O tiger among Brahmins, of the second lunar day called Phala, by fasting on which a man does not suffer separation from a woman in this world.

शतानीकः उवाच (śatānīkaḥ uvāca) — Śatānīka said;
ब्रूहि मे (brūhi me) — tell me;
द्विज-शार्दूल (dvija-śārdūla) — tiger among brāhmaṇas;
द्वितीयाम् फल-संज्ञिताम् (dvitīyām phala-saṃjñitām) — second day named Phala;
याम् उपोष्य (yām upoṣya) — fasting on which;
नरः योषित् (naraḥ yoṣit) — man or woman;
वियोगम् न गच्छति (viyogam na gacchati) — does not undergo separation

BP 1.20.2

पत्न्या नरो मुनिश्रेष्ठ भार्या च पतिना सह ।
तामहं श्रोतुमिच्छामि विधवा स्त्री न जायते ॥
उपोषितेन येनार्य पत्न्या च सहितो नरः ॥

A man with his wife, O best of sages, and a wife with her husband—I wish to hear of that fast by which a woman does not become a widow and a man remains with his wife.

पत्न्या नरः (patnyā naraḥ) — man with wife;
भार्या पतिना सह (bhāryā patinā saha) — wife with husband;
श्रोतुम् इच्छामि (śrotum icchāmi) — I wish to hear;
विधवा स्त्री न जायते (vidhavā strī na jāyate) — woman does not become widow;
उपोषितेन येन (upoṣitena yena) — by which fast;
पत्न्या सहितः नरः (patnyā sahitaḥ naraḥ) — man together with wife

BP 1.20.3

तन्मे ब्रूहि द्विजश्रेष्ठ श्रेयोऽर्थं नरयोषिताम् ।
येन मे कौतुकं ब्रह्मञ्छ्रुत्वापूर्वं प्रसर्पति ॥

Tell me of it, O best of Brahmins, for the welfare of men and women. Hearing it, my extraordinary curiosity increases, O Brahmin.

तत् मे ब्रूहि (tat me brūhi) — explain that to me;
द्विज-श्रेष्ठ (dvija-śreṣṭha) — best brāhmaṇa;
श्रेयः-अर्थम् (śreyaḥ-artham) — for welfare;
नर-योषिताम् (nara-yoṣitām) — of men and women;
कौतुकम् (kautukam) — curiosity;
श्रुत्वा अपूर्वम् (śrutvā apūrvam) — hearing unprecedented;
प्रसर्पति (prasarpati) — arises

BP 1.20.4

सुमन्तुरुवाच
अशून्यशयनां नाम द्वितीयां शृणु भारत ।
यामुपोष्य न वैधव्यं स्त्री प्रयाति नराधिप ॥
पत्नीवियुक्तश्च नरो न कदाचित्प्रजायते ॥

Sumantu said: Hear, O Bharata, of the second day named Aśūnya-śayanā. A woman who fasts on it does not become a widow, and a man is never separated from his wife.

सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
अशून्य-शयनाम् नाम द्वितीयाम् (aśūnya-śayanām nāma dvitīyām) — second day named Aśūnya-śayana;
शृणु भारत (śṛṇu bhārata) — hear O Bharata;
याम् उपोष्य (yām upoṣya) — fasting on which;
न वैधव्यम् स्त्री प्रयाति (na vaidhavyam strī prayāti) — woman does not attain widowhood;
पत्नी-वियुक्तः नरः न (patnī-viyuktaḥ naraḥ na) — man not separated from wife

BP 1.20.5

शेते जगत्पतिः कृष्णः श्रिया सार्धं यदा नृप ।
अशून्यशयना नाम तदा ग्राह्या हि सा तिथिः ॥

When Kṛṣṇa, lord of the world, lies down with Śrī, O king, that lunar day is to be observed as Aśūnya-śayanā.

शेते जगत्-पतिः कृष्णः (śete jagat-patiḥ kṛṣṇaḥ) — Kṛṣṇa lord of world lies;
श्रिया सार्धम् (śriyā sārdham) — together with Śrī;
यदा (yadā) — when;
अशून्य-शयना नाम (aśūnya-śayanā nāma) — named non-empty bed;
तदा ग्राह्या सा तिथिः (tadā grāhyā sā tithiḥ) — then that date is to be observed

BP 1.20.6

कृष्णपक्षे द्वितीयायां श्रावणे मासि भारत ।
इदमुच्चारयेत्स्नातः प्रणम्य जगतः पतिम् ॥
श्रीवत्सधारिणं देवं भक्त्याभ्यर्च्य श्रिया सह ॥

On the second day of the dark fortnight in Śrāvaṇa, having bathed and bowed to the lord of the world, one should recite this and worship with devotion the god bearing Śrīvatsa together with Śrī.

कृष्ण-पक्षे द्वितीयायाम् (kṛṣṇa-pakṣe dvitīyāyām) — dark fortnight second day;
श्रावणे मासि (śrāvaṇe māsi) — in Śrāvaṇa;
स्नातः (snātaḥ) — bathed;
प्रणम्य जगतः पतिम् (praṇamya jagataḥ patim) — bowing to world’s lord;
श्रीवत्स-धारिणम् देवम् (śrīvatsa-dhāriṇam devam) — god bearing Śrīvatsa;
भक्त्या अभ्यर्च्य श्रिया सह (bhaktyā abhyarcya śriyā saha) — worshipping with devotion together with Śrī;
इदम् उच्चारयेत् (idam uccārayet) — recite this

BP 1.20.7

श्रीवत्सधारिञ्छ्रीकान्त श्रीवत्स श्रीपतेऽव्यय ।
गार्हस्थ्यं मा प्रणाशं मे यातु धर्मार्थकामदम् ॥

“O bearer of Śrīvatsa, beloved of Śrī, O Śrīvatsa, imperishable lord of Śrī, may my household life, which gives dharma, wealth, and desire, never perish.”

श्रीवत्स-धारिन् (śrīvatsa-dhārin) — bearer of Śrīvatsa;
श्री-कान्त (śrī-kānta) — beloved of Śrī;
श्री-पते अव्यय (śrī-pate avyaya) — imperishable Lord of Śrī;
गार्हस्थ्यम् मे (gārhasthyam me) — my household life;
मा प्रणाशम् यातु (mā praṇāśam yātu) — may it not perish;
धर्म-अर्थ-काम-दम् (dharma-artha-kāma-dam) — giving righteousness wealth pleasure

BP 1.20.8

गावश्च मा प्रणश्यन्तु मा प्रणश्यन्तु मे जनाः ॥

“May my cows not perish; may my people not perish.”

गावः च मे मा प्रणश्यन्तु (gāvaḥ ca me mā praṇaśyantu) — may my cattle not perish;
मे जनाः मा प्रणश्यन्तु (me janāḥ mā praṇaśyantu) — may my people not perish

BP 1.20.9

जामयो मा प्रणश्यन्तु मत्तो दाम्पत्यभेदतः ।
लक्ष्म्या वियुज्येऽहं देव न कदाचिद्यथा भवान् ॥

“May my female relatives not perish through separation between spouses caused by me. O god, may I never be separated from Lakṣmī, as you are never separated from her.”

जामयः मे मा प्रणश्यन्तु (jāmayaḥ me mā praṇaśyantu) — may family relations not perish;
मत्तः दाम्पत्य-भेदतः (mattaḥ dāmpatya-bhedataḥ) — through marital division from me;
लक्ष्म्या न वियुज्ये अहम् (lakṣmyā na viyujye aham) — may I not be separated from Lakṣmī;
यथा भवान् (yathā bhavān) — as you are not

BP 1.20.10

तथा कलत्रसम्बन्धो देव मा मे वियुज्यताम् ।
लक्ष्म्या न शून्यं वरद यथा ते शयनं सदा ॥

“O god, may my marriage bond likewise never be severed. O giver of boons, as your bed is never empty of Lakṣmī—”

तथा कलत्र-सम्बन्धः (tathā kalatra-sambandhaḥ) — likewise marital bond;
मा मे वियुज्यताम् (mā me viyujyatām) — may mine not be severed;
वरद (varada) — bestower of boons;
यथा ते शयनम् (yathā te śayanam) — as your bed;
लक्ष्म्या न शून्यम् सदा (lakṣmyā na śūnyam sadā) — is never empty of Lakṣmī

BP 1.20.11

शय्या ममाप्यशून्यास्तु तथा तु मधुसूदन ।
एवं प्रमाद्य पूजां च कृत्वा लक्ष्म्यास्तथा हरेः ॥

“—so may my bed also never be empty, O Madhusūdana.” Having prayed thus, one should worship Lakṣmī and Hari.

शय्या मम अपि अशून्या अस्तु (śayyā mama api aśūnyā astu) — may my bed also not be empty;
मधुसूदन (madhusūdana) — O Madhusūdana;
एवम् प्रार्थ्य (evam prārthya) — praying thus;
पूजाम् कृत्वा (pūjām kṛtvā) — performing worship;
लक्ष्म्याः तथा हरेः (lakṣmyāḥ tathā hareḥ) — of Lakṣmī and Hari

BP 1.20.12

फलानि दद्याच्छायायामभीष्टानि जगत्पतिम् ।
नक्तं प्रणम्यायतने हविर्भुञ्जीत वाग्यतः ॥

One should give the desired fruits in the shade to the lord of the world. At night, bowing at the shrine, one should eat the sacrificial food in silence.

फलानि दद्यात् (phalāni dadyāt) — should offer fruits;
छायायाम् (chāyāyām) — in shade;
अभीष्टानि (abhīṣṭāni) — desired;
जगत्-पतिम् (jagat-patim) — to world’s lord;
नक्तम् (naktam) — at night;
प्रणम्य आयतने (praṇamya āyatane) — bowing in shrine;
हविः भुञ्जीत (haviḥ bhuñjīta) — eat sacrificial food;
वाक्-यतः (vāk-yataḥ) — silently

BP 1.20.13

ब्राह्मणाय द्वितीयेऽह्नि शक्त्या दद्याच्च दक्षिणाम् ॥

On the second day, one should give a gift to a Brahmin according to one's means.

ब्राह्मणाय (brāhmaṇāya) — to brāhmaṇa;
द्वितीये अह्नि (dvitīye ahni) — on second day;
शक्त्या (śaktyā) — according to means;
दद्यात् दक्षिणाम् (dadyāt dakṣiṇām) — give a fee

BP 1.20.14

शतानीक उवाच
कानि तानि अभीष्टानि केशवस्य फलानि तु ।
योज्यानि शयने विप्र देवदेवस्य कथ्यताम् ॥

Śatānīka said: O Brahmin, tell me which fruits are desired by Keśava and should be placed on the bed of the god of gods.

शतानीकः उवाच (śatānīkaḥ uvāca) — Śatānīka said;
कानि फलानि (kāni phalāni) — which fruits;
अभीष्टानि केशवस्य (abhīṣṭāni keśavasya) — dear to Keśava;
योज्यानि शयने (yojyāni śayane) — placed on bed;
देव-देवस्य (deva-devasya) — of God of gods;
कथ्यताम् (kathyatām) — let it be told

BP 1.20.15

किं च दानं द्वितीयेऽह्नि दातव्यं ब्राह्मणस्य तु ।
भक्तैर्नरैर्द्विजश्रेष्ठ देवदेवस्य शक्तितः ॥

And what gift should devoted men give to a Brahmin on the second day, O best of Brahmins, according to their means, for the god of gods?

किम् च दानम् (kim ca dānam) — what gift;
द्वितीये अह्नि (dvitīye ahni) — on second day;
दातव्यम् ब्राह्मणस्य (dātavyam brāhmaṇasya) — to be given to brāhmaṇa;
भक्तैः नरैः (bhaktaiḥ naraiḥ) — by devoted people;
शक्तितः (śaktitaḥ) — according to ability;
देव-देवस्य (deva-devasya) — for God of gods

BP 1.20.16

सुमन्तुरुवाच
यानि तत्र महाबाहो काले सन्ति फलानि तु।
मधुराणि सुतीव्राणि न चापि कटुकानि तु ॥

Sumantu said: O mighty-armed one, whatever fruits are then in season—sweet and very sweet, but not pungent—

सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
यानि फलानि काले सन्ति (yāni phalāni kāle santi) — fruits then in season;
महा-बाहो (mahā-bāho) — O mighty-armed;
मधुराणि (madhurāṇi) — sweet;
न सु-तीव्राणि (na su-tīvrāṇi) — not very sharp;
न कटुकानि (na kaṭukāni) — not bitter

BP 1.20.17

दातव्यानि नृपश्रेष्ठ स्वशक्त्या शयने नृप ।
मधुराणि प्रदत्तानि नरो वल्लभतां यजेत् ॥

—should be placed on the bed according to one's means, O best of kings. By giving sweet fruits, a man becomes beloved.

दातव्यानि (dātavyāni) — should be given;
स्व-शक्त्या (sva-śaktyā) — according to means;
शयने (śayane) — upon bed;
मधुराणि प्रदत्तानि (madhurāṇi pradattāni) — sweet things given;
नरः वल्लभताम् व्रजेत् (naraḥ vallabhatām vrajet) — man becomes beloved

BP 1.20.18

योषिच्च कुरुशार्दूल भर्तुर्वल्लभतामियात् ।
तस्मात्कटुकतीव्राणि स्त्रीलिङ्गानि विवर्जयेत् ॥

A woman also becomes beloved by her husband, O tiger among the Kurus. Therefore she should avoid very pungent and bitter fruits, and fruits of the feminine kind.

योषित् च (yoṣit ca) — woman also;
भर्तुः वल्लभताम् इयात् (bhartuḥ vallabhatām iyāt) — becomes dear to husband;
तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore;
कटुक-तीव्राणि (kaṭuka-tīvrāṇi) — bitter and sharp;
स्त्री-लिङ्गानि (strī-liṅgāni) — feminine-named;
विवर्जयेत् (vivarjayet) — should avoid

BP 1.20.19

खर्जूरमातुलिङ्गानि श्वेतेन शिरसा सह ।
फलानि शयने राजन्यज्ञभागहरस्य तु ॥

Dates, citrons, and fruits with white tops should be placed on the bed of the bearer of the sacrificial share, O king.

खर्जूर-मातुलिङ्गानि (kharjūra-mātuliṅgāni) — dates and citrons;
श्वेतेन शिरसा सह (śvetena śirasā saha) — with white-headed fruits;
फलानि शयने (phalāni śayane) — fruits upon bed;
यज्ञ-भाग-हरस्य (yajña-bhāga-harasya) — of receiver of sacrificial shares

BP 1.20.20

देयानि कुरुशार्दूल स्वशक्त्या मुञ्जकेशिने ।
एतान्येव तु विप्रस्य गाङ्गेयसहितानि तु ॥

They should be offered according to one's means to Muñjakeśin, O tiger among the Kurus. These same fruits should be given to a Brahmin together with gold.

देयानि (deyāni) — should be offered;
स्व-शक्त्या (sva-śaktyā) — according to means;
मुञ्जकेशिने (muñjakeśine) — to Muñjakeśin;
एतानि एव (etāni eva) — these same;
विप्रस्य (viprasya) — to brāhmaṇa;
गाङ्गेय-सहितानि (gāṅgeya-sahitāni) — accompanied by silver

BP 1.20.21

द्वितीयेऽह्नि प्रदेयानि भक्त्या शक्त्या च भारत ।
वासोदानं तथा धान्यफलदानसमन्वितम् ॥
गाङ्गेयस्य विशेषेण धान्यदानं प्रचक्षते ॥

On the second day, they should be given with devotion and according to one's means, O Bharata, together with gifts of clothing, grain, and fruit. In particular, they prescribe a gift of grain together with gold.

द्वितीये अह्नि प्रदेयानि (dvitīye ahni pradeyāni) — to be given on second day;
भक्त्या शक्त्या च (bhaktyā śaktyā ca) — with devotion and ability;
वासो-दानम् (vāso-dānam) — gift of clothing;
धान्य-फल-दान-समन्वितम् (dhānya-phala-dāna-samanvitam) — with grain and fruit;
गाङ्गेयस्य विशेषेण (gāṅgeyasya viśeṣeṇa) — especially with silver;
धान्य-दानम् (dhānya-dānam) — grain gift

BP 1.20.22

एवं करोति यः सम्यङ्नरो मासचतुष्टयम् ।
ततो जन्मत्रयं वीर गृहभङ्गो न जायते ॥

A man who properly does this for four months, O hero, has no breaking of his household for three births.

यः सम्यक् करोति (yaḥ samyak karoti) — one who properly performs;
मास-चतुष्टयम् (māsa-catuṣṭayam) — four months;
ततः जन्म-त्रयम् (tataḥ janma-trayam) — then for three births;
गृह-भङ्गः न जायते (gṛha-bhaṅgaḥ na jāyate) — household rupture does not occur

BP 1.20.23

अशून्यशयनश्चासौ धर्मकामार्थसाधनः ।
भवत्यव्याहतैश्वर्यः पुरुषो नात्र संशयः ॥

He becomes one whose bed is not empty, accomplishing dharma, desire, and wealth; his prosperity is unobstructed, no doubt.

अशून्य-शयनः (aśūnya-śayanaḥ) — bed not empty;
धर्म-काम-अर्थ-साधनः (dharma-kāma-artha-sādhanaḥ) — accomplishing righteousness pleasure prosperity;
अव्याहत-ऐश्वर्यः (avyāhata-aiśvaryaḥ) — unobstructed lordship;
पुरुषः भवति (puruṣaḥ bhavati) — person becomes;
न संशयः (na saṃśayaḥ) — no doubt

BP 1.20.24

नारी च राजन्धर्मज्ञा व्रतमेतद्यथाविधि ।
या करोति न सा शोच्या बन्धुवर्गस्य जायते ॥

O king, a woman who knows dharma and performs this vow according to rule does not become one mourned by her family.

नारी धर्म-ज्ञा (nārī dharma-jñā) — woman knowing dharma;
व्रतम् एतत् यथा-विधि करोति (vratam etat yathā-vidhi karoti) — performs this vow by rule;
न सा शोच्या जायते (na sā śocyā jāyate) — she does not become lamented;
बन्धु-वर्गस्य (bandhu-vargasya) — by family circle

BP 1.20.25

वैधव्यं दुर्भगत्वं च भर्तृत्यागं च सत्तम ।
नाप्नोति जन्म त्रियतमेतच्चीर्त्वा महाव्रतम् ॥

O excellent one, having performed this great vow, she does not experience widowhood, misfortune, or abandonment by her husband for three births.

वैधव्यम् (vaidhavyam) — widowhood;
दुर्भगत्वम् (durbhagatvam) — misfortune;
भर्तृ-त्यागम् (bhartṛ-tyāgam) — husband’s abandonment;
न आप्नोति (na āpnoti) — does not obtain;
जन्म-त्रितयम् (janma-tritayam) — three births;
एतत् महा-व्रतम् चरित्वा (etat mahā-vratam caritvā) — performing this great vow

BP 1.20.26

अदत्त्वा कटुकानीह फलानि कुरुनन्दन ।
खर्जूरमातुलिङ्गानि बृहत्फलशिरांसि च ॥

Do not give pungent fruits here, O delight of the Kurus; [give] dates, citrons, and fruits with large tops.

अदत्त्वा कटुकानि फलानि (adattvā kaṭukāni phalāni) — not giving bitter fruits;
कुरु-नन्दन (kuru-nandana) — O Kuru’s delight;
खर्जूर-मातुलिङ्गानि (kharjūra-mātuliṅgāni) — dates and citrons;
बृहत्-फल-शिरांसि (bṛhat-phala-śirāṃsi) — large prominent-topped fruits

BP 1.20.27

दत्त्वा द्विजेभ्यो राजेन्द्र मधुराणि पराणि च ।
तस्मात्स्वशक्त्या यत्तेन देयानि मधुराणि च ॥

Having given excellent sweet fruits to Brahmins, O king of kings, one should give them according to one's means.

दत्त्वा द्विजेभ्यः (dattvā dvijebhyaḥ) — giving to brāhmaṇas;
मधुराणि पराणि (madhurāṇi parāṇi) — excellent sweet fruits;
तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore;
स्व-शक्त्या (sva-śaktyā) — according to means;
यत्नेन (yatnena) — carefully;
देयानि मधुराणि (deyāni madhurāṇi) — sweet fruits to be given

BP 1.20.28

इत्येषा कथिता कृष्णद्वितीया तिथिरुत्तमा ।
यामुपोष्य नरो राजन्नृद्धिं वृद्धिं तथा व्रजेत् ॥

Thus the excellent second lunar day of the dark fortnight has been told. By fasting on it, O king, a man attains prosperity and increase.

इति एषा कथिता (iti eṣā kathitā) — thus this related;
कृष्ण-द्वितीया (kṛṣṇa-dvitīyā) — dark-fortnight second day;
तिथिः उत्तमा (tithiḥ uttamā) — excellent date;
याम् उपोष्य (yām upoṣya) — fasting on which;
नरः (naraḥ) — person;
ऋद्धिम् वृद्धिम् व्रजेत् (ṛddhim vṛddhim vrajet) — attains prosperity and increase

BP 1.20.29

शतानीक उवाच
भवता कथितेयं वै द्वितीया तिथिरुत्तमा ।
अश्विभ्यां द्विजशार्दूल कथमस्यां जनार्दनः ॥

Śatānīka said: You have told of this excellent second lunar day as belonging to the Aśvins. O tiger among Brahmins, how is Janārdana—

भवता कथिता इयम् (bhavatā kathitā iyam) — this described by you;
द्वितीया तिथिः उत्तमा (dvitīyā tithiḥ uttamā) — excellent second day;
अश्विभ्याम् (aśvibhyām) — belonging to Aśvins;
कथम् अस्याम् जनार्दनः (katham asyām janārdanaḥ) — how on it Janārdana

BP 1.20.30

सम्पूज्यः फलसंज्ञायां कथितः पद्मया सह ।
तदत्र कौतुकं मह्यं सुमहज्जायते द्विज ॥

—said to be worshipped with Padmā on the day named Phala? This raises great curiosity in me, O Brahmin.

सम्पूज्यः (sampūjyaḥ) — to be worshipped;
फल-संज्ञायाम् (phala-saṃjñāyām) — on day named Phala;
पद्मया सह (padmayā saha) — with Padmā;
तत् अत्र कौतुकम् (tat atra kautukam) — curiosity about this;
मह्यत् सु-महत् जायते (mahyat su-mahat jāyate) — very great arises in me

BP 1.20.31

सुमन्तुरुवाच
एवमेतन्न सन्देहो तथा वदसि भारत ।
अश्विनोर्वै तिथिरियं किं तु वाक्यं निबोध मे ॥

Sumantu said: It is as you say, O Bharata; there is no doubt. This lunar day belongs to the Aśvins, but hear my explanation.

सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
एवम् एतत् (evam etat) — it is thus;
न सन्देहः (na sandehaḥ) — no doubt;
तथा वदसि (tathā vadasi) — you speak rightly;
अश्विनोः तिथिः इयम् (aśvinoḥ tithiḥ iyam) — this date of Aśvins;
वाक्यं निबोध मे (vākyaṃ nibodha me) — understand my words

BP 1.20.32

अशून्यशयना दत्ता विष्णोरमिततेजसः ।
अश्विभ्यां कुरु शार्दूल प्रीतये मुञ्जकेशिनः ॥

O tiger among the Kurus, Viṣṇu of immeasurable radiance gave the Aśūnya-śayanā observance to the Aśvins for the pleasure of Muñjakeśin.

अशून्य-शयना (aśūnya-śayanā) — Aśūnya-śayana observance;
दत्ता (dattā) — granted;
विष्णोः अमित-तेजसः (viṣṇoḥ amita-tejasaḥ) — of immeasurably radiant Viṣṇu;
अश्विभ्याम् (aśvibhyām) — to Aśvins;
कुरु-शार्दूल (kuru-śārdūla) — O tiger among Kurus;
प्रीतये मुञ्जकेशिनः (prītaye muñjakeśinaḥ) — for pleasure of Muñjakeśin

BP 1.20.33

तावेव कुरुशार्दूल पूज्येतेऽत्र महीपते ।
नासत्यौ भगवान्विष्णुर्दत्तश्च श्रीर्विभाव्यते ॥

Those two alone are worshipped here, O tiger among the Kurus and lord of the earth: Nāsatya is regarded as Lord Viṣṇu, and Dasra as Śrī.

तौ एव पूज्येते अत्र (tau eva pūjyete atra) — those very two are worshipped here;
कुरु-शार्दूल मही-पते (kuru-śārdūla mahī-pate) — O tiger among Kurus and king;
नासत्यः भगवान् विष्णुः (nāsatyaḥ bhagavān viṣṇuḥ) — Nāsatya as Lord Viṣṇu;
दस्रः च श्रीः विभाव्यते (dasraḥ ca śrīḥ vibhāvyate) — Dasra contemplated as Śrī
