VP 5.29.1

द्वारवत्यां स्थिते कृष्णे शक्रस्त्रिभुवनेश्वरः ।
आजगामाथ मैत्रेय मत्तैरावतपृष्ठगः

Indra, lord of the three worlds, came to Kṛṣṇa in Dvārakā, riding on the back of the intoxicated Airāvata.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
dvāravatyām — in Dvārakā;
sthite kṛṣṇe — when Kṛṣṇa was staying;
śakraḥ — Indra;
tri-bhuvana-īśvaraḥ — lord of the three worlds;
ājagāma — came;
atha — then;
maitreya — O Maitreya;
matta-airāvata-pṛṣṭhagaḥ — riding Airāvata's back;
Śrīdhara
idānīm — now;
bhauma-vadha-prasaṅgena — on the occasion of Bhauma’s killing;
hareḥ sahasraśaḥ bhāryā-lābham — Hari’s gaining thousands of wives;
vaktum — in order to speak;
indrāgamanādikam āha — he speaks of Indra’s arrival and the rest;
dvāravatyām ity ādinā — beginning with “in Dvārakā”;
parimṛśyatām ity antena — ending with “let it be considered”;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dvāravatyāṃ sthite kṛṣṇe śakrastribhuvaneśvaraḥ . ājagāmātha maitreya mattairāvatapṛṣṭhagaḥ

Śrīdhara

idānīṃ bhauma-vadha-prasaṅgena hareḥ sahasraśo bhāryā-lābhaṃ vaktum indrāgamanādikam āha—dvāravatyām ity ādinā, parimṛśyatām (12) ity antena

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इदानीं भौम-वध-प्रसङ्गेन हरेः सहस्रशो भार्या-लाभं वक्तुम् इन्द्रागमनादिकम् आह—द्वारवत्याम् इत्य् आदिना, परिमृश्यताम् (१२) इत्य् अन्तेन ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Now, on the occasion of Bhauma’s killing, the arrival of Indra and the rest is discussed in order to speak of Hari’s gaining thousands of wives, beginning with “in Dvārakā” and ending with “let it be considered” (12).

VP 5.29.6

युष्मद्दोर्दण्डसम्भूतिपरित्राते जगत्त्रये ।
यज्वयज्ञांशसम्प्राप्त्या तृप्तिं यान्ति दिवौकसः

“When the three worlds are protected by the power of your arms, the gods attain satisfaction by receiving the sacrificial shares of the sacrificers.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
yuṣmat-dor-daṇḍa-sambhūti — the power of your arms;
paritrāte jagat-traye — when the three worlds are protected;

tṛptim yānti — attain satisfaction;
divaukasaḥ — the gods;
Śrīdhara
yuṣmad iti — with regard to “your”;
yajvanām — of the sacrificers;
yajñāṃśāḥ — sacrificial portions;
yajña-bhāgāḥ — shares of the sacrifice;
teṣāṃ samprāptyā — through obtaining those;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yuṣmaddordaḍasaṃbhūtiparitrāte jagattraye . yajvayajñāṃśasaṃprāptyā tṛptiṃ yānti divaukasaḥ

Śrīdhara

yuṣmad iti . yajvanāṃ yajñāṃśā yajña-bhāgāḥ teṣāṃ samprāptyā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

युष्मद् इति । यज्वनां यज्ञांशा यज्ञ-भागाः तेषां सम्प्राप्त्या ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Your” means the sacrificial portions of the sacrificers—the shares of the sacrifice; through obtaining those, [the gods are satisfied].

Hearing this, he smiled, thinking: “He now approaches me for the task; once the task is completed, I will fight for the Pārijāta.”

VP 5.29.14

Having thought of the one who had come, he mounted the sky-going Garuḍa, placed Satyabhāmā on him, and went to Prāgjyotiṣa city.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सञ्चिन्त्येति । सञ्चिन्त्यागतम् स्मृति-मात्रेणोपगतं सत्यभामां समारोप्येत्य् अत्र हरिवंशोक्तं कारणं द्रष्टव्यम् । नारदानीते पारिजात-कुसुमे रुक्मिण्यै दत्ते तद् विदित्वा प्रकुपितां सत्यभामां सान्त्वयता भगवतोक्‍तम् ॥१४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Having thought” means: having thought of the one who came by mere recollection. The reason stated in the Harivaṃśa for placing Satyabhāmā [on Garuḍa] should be understood. When the Pārijāta flower brought by Nārada was given to Rukmiṇī, the Lord spoke this while pacifying the angered Satyabhāmā.
