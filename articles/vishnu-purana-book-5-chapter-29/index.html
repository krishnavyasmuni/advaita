VP 5.29.1

द्वारवत्यां स्थिते कृष्णे शक्रस्त्रिभुवनेश्वरः । आजगामाथ मैत्रेय मत्तैरावतपृष्ठगः

Sri Paraasharar - When Krishna was in Dwaaravati, the king of all the gods, Indra, came mounting on his Airaavata, to see Krishna. Bhouma is Bhoomi's son Narakaasura. In order to narrate the story of Narakaasura vadha, and also about another thousand wives which Krishna gets here, that story is being narrated in the beginning of this chapter.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dvāravatyāṃ sthite kṛṣṇe śakrastribhuvaneśvaraḥ . ājagāmātha maitreya mattairāvatapṛṣṭhagaḥ — Sri Paraasharar - When Krishna was in Dwaaravati, the king of all the gods, Indra, came mounting on his Airaavata, to see Krishna. Bhouma is Bhoomi's son Narakaasura. In order to narrate the story of Narakaasura vadha, and also about another thousand wives which Krishna gets here, that story is being narrated in the beginning of this chapter.

Śrīdhara

idānīṃ bhauma-vadha-prasaṅgena hareḥ sahasraśo bhāryā-lābhaṃ vaktum indrāgamanādikam āha—dvāravatyām ity ādinā, parimṛśyatām (१२) ity antena — Sri Paraasharar - When Krishna was in Dwaaravati, the king of all the gods, Indra, came mounting on his Airaavata, to see Krishna. Bhouma is Bhoomi's son Narakaasura. In order to narrate the story of Narakaasura vadha, and also about another thousand wives which Krishna gets here, that story is being narrated in the beginning of this chapter.

Śrīdhara

idānīṃ bhauma-vadha-prasaṅgena hareḥ sahasraśo bhāryā-lābhaṃ vaktum indrāgamanādikam āha—dvāravatyām ity ādinā, parimṛśyatām (१२) ity antena ||१|| praviśya dvārakāṃ so’thasametya hariṇā tadā५९९.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इदानीं भौम-वध-प्रसङ्गेन हरेः सहस्रशो भार्या-लाभं वक्तुम् इन्द्रागमनादिकम् आह—द्वारवत्याम् इत्य् आदिना, परिमृश्यताम् (१२) इत्य् अन्तेन ॥१॥ प्रविश्य द्वारकां सोऽथसमेत्य हरिणा तदा५९९।

Śrīdhara commentary.

श्री पराशरः - द्र्वारर्वत्याां स्स्थते कृषणे शक्रः त्रत्रभुर्वनेश्र्वरः | आजगामाथ मैत्रेय मथैरार्वतपृषठगः ॥ १ ॥

You also pervade all the objects individually, and You only get everything done. You are the act also. You are the doer also. You are the creator also, You are everything. You are the inner self of all beings. How is it possible to even praise You?

Śrīdhara

tvam iti . tvaṃ kartā utpādakaḥ . vikartā viśeṣeṇa vṛddhy-ādīnāṃ kartā poṣakaḥ prabhavaty asmād iti prabhavaḥ . upādāna-kāraṇam apiyanty asminn iti apyayaḥ laya-sthānam . ata eva jagad-rūpaś tvam . atas tava prabhāvādikaṃ mayā kathaṃ stūyate stotuṃ śakyate — You are the all pervader. You also pervade all the objects individually, and You only get everything done. You are the act also. You are the doer also. You are the creator also, You are everything. You are the inner self of all beings. How is it possible to even praise You?

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

त्वम् इति । त्वं कर्ता उत्पादकः । विकर्ता विशेषेण वृद्ध्य्-आदीनां कर्ता पोषकः प्रभवत्य् अस्माद् इति प्रभवः । उपादान-कारणम् अपियन्त्य् अस्मिन्न् इति अप्ययः लय-स्थानम् । अत एव जगद्-रूपश् त्वम् । अतस् तव प्रभावादिकं मया कथं स्तूयते स्तोतुं शक्यते ॥२६॥ व्यापि-व्याप्यङ्क्रिया-कर्ता६११कार्यं च भगवान्यथा६१२। सर्व-भूतात्म-भूतस्य६१३स्तूयतेतवकिन्तथा६१४॥२७॥ विष्णु-चित्तः — *न व्याख्यातम्.* श्रीधरः — अतश् च स्तोतुं न कश्यम् इत्य् आह—व्यापीति द्वाभ्याम् ॥२७॥ परमात्मा च भूतात्मा त्वम् आत्मा चाव्ययो भवान् ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

व्यापी व्याप्यां क्रक्रयाकताि कायं च भगर्वान् यथा | सर्विभूतात्म भूतस्य स्तूयते तर्व क्रकां तथा ॥ २७ ॥ You are the all pervader.
