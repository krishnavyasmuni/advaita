चतुदिम्श्रान् गजान्श्च अग्र्यान् र्षट्सहस्रान् च दृषटर्वान् | काांभोजानाां तथा अश्र्वानाां तनयुतान्येकवर्वांस्श्तम् ॥ ३२ ॥ He saw excellent elephants, and each elephant having four tusks; He saw 6000 such elephants. He saw 21 lakh horses, brought from Kaambhoja desha, which were of excellent breed.
