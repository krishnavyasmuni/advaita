VP 5.9.1

तस्मिन्रासभदैतेये सानुगे विनिपातिते । सौम्यं तद्गोपगोपीनां रम्यं तालवनं बभौ

Sri Paraasharar - When the demon in the form of the donkey, along with his followers, were all killed, and that palm grove became beautiful, the gopaas and gopis started to go there and pluck the flowers.

VP 5.9.2

Feeling extremely happy, Vasudeva's children Krishna and Balaraama, having killed the demon Dhenuka, came near a fig tree which was called as Bhaandeera.

VP 5.9.5

VP 5.9.8

ततस्त्वान्दोलिकाभिश्च नियुद्धैश्च महाबलौ । व्यायामं चक्रतुस्तत्र क्षेपणीयैस्तथाऽश्मभिः

VP 5.9.9

VP 5.9.11
