VP 3.9.1

बालः कृतोपनयनो वेदाहरणतत्परः । गुरुगेहे वसेद्भूप ब्रह्मचारी समाहितः
॥१॥

After a boy has undergone the upanayana ceremony, he should devote himself to learning the Veda. He should live in his teacher’s house as a focused brahmacārin, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bālaḥ kṛtopanayano vedāharaṇatatparaḥ । gurugehe vasedbhūpa brahmacārī samāhitaḥ ॥१॥ — bālaḥ kṛta-upanayanaḥ — a boy who has undergone initiation; veda-āharaṇa-tatparaḥ — devoted to learning the Veda; guru-gehe vaset — should live in the teacher’s house; bhūpa — O king; brahmacārī — a brahmacārin; samāhitaḥ — focused.

Śrīdhara

nirucya haritoṣārthān varṇadharmānaśeṣataḥ । tattadāśramadharmāśca navame’tra nirūpitāḥ — Śrīdhara explains that, after fully setting out the duties of varṇa that please Hari, the duties of each āśrama are taught here over nine chapters.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bālaḥ kṛtopanayano vedāharaṇatatparaḥ । gurugehe vasedbhūpa brahmacārī samāhitaḥ ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

nirucya haritoṣārthān varṇadharmānaśeṣataḥ । tattadāśramadharmāśca navame’tra nirūpitāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

निरुच्य हरितोषार्थान् वर्णधर्मानशेषतः । तत्तदाश्रमधर्माश्च नवमेऽत्र निरूपिताः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1).

Śrīdhara explains that, after fully setting out the duties of varṇa that please Hari, the duties of each āśrama are taught here over nine chapters.

VP 3.9.2

शौचा चारव्रतं तत्र कार्यं शुश्रूषणं गुरोः । व्रतानि चरता ग्राह्यो वेदश्च कृतबुद्धिना
॥२॥

There he should maintain purity and proper conduct and serve his teacher. While observing the vows, he should study the Veda with a disciplined mind.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śaucā cāravrataṃ tatra kāryaṃ śuśrūṣaṇaṃ guroḥ । vratāni caratā grāhyo vedaśca kṛtabuddhinā ॥२॥ — śauca-ācāra-vatā — with purity and proper conduct; tatra — there; kāryam — to be performed; śuśrūṣaṇam guroḥ — service of the teacher; vratāni caratā — by one observing vows; grāhyaḥ — to be learned; vedaḥ — the Veda; kṛta-buddhinā — with a disciplined mind.

Śrīdhara

vratāni prājāpatyaṃ tathā saumyamāgneyaṃ vaiśvadaivikam ityādīni madhumāṃsavarjanādīni ca caratā kurvatā vedo grāhyaḥ — The vows include the Prājāpatya, Saumya, Āgneya, and Vaiśvadeva observances, together with abstinence from honey, meat, and similar things. While practising these, the student should learn the Veda.

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

vratāni prājāpatyaṃ tathā saumyamāgneyaṃ vaiśvadaivikam ityādīni madhumāṃsavarjanādīni ca caratā kurvatā vedo grāhyaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

VP 3.9.4

स्थिते तिष्ठेद्वाजेद्याते नीचैरासीत चासति । शिष्यो गुरोर्नृपश्रेष्ठ प्रतिकूलं न संचरेत्
॥४॥

When the teacher stands, the student should stand; when he goes, the student should go; when he sits, the student should sit below him. O best of kings, the student should never act contrary to his teacher.

VP 3.9.5

तेनै वोक्तं पठेद्वेदं नान्यचित्तः पुरःस्थितः । अनुज्ञातश्च भिक्षान्नमश्नीयाद्गुरुणा ततः
॥५॥

Śrīdhara

tenaivoktaḥ paṭhet na tu śīghrādhyāpanāya guruṃ niyuñjīta — He should study the Veda exactly as taught, without appointing the teacher merely for rapid instruction.

Śrīdhara

VP 3.9.6

अवगाहेदपः पूर्वमाचार्येणावगाहितः । समिज्जलादिकं चास्य काल्यं काल्यमुपानयेत्
॥६॥

He should bathe after his teacher has bathed and should bring firewood, water, and the other morning necessities at the proper time.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration

VP 3.9.7

गृहीतग्राह्यवेदश्च ततोऽनुज्ञामवाप्य च । गार्हस्थ्यमाविशेत्प्राज्ञो निष्पन्नगुरुणिष्कृतिः
॥७॥

After mastering the Veda he is meant to learn and obtaining his teacher’s permission, the wise student should enter household life, having repaid his debt to his teacher.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gṛhītagrāhyavedaśca tato’nujñāmavāpya ca । gārhasthyamāviśetprājño niṣpannaguruṇiṣkṛtiḥ ॥७॥ — gṛhīta-grāhya-vedaḥ — having mastered the Veda to be learned; tataḥ — then; anujñām avāpya — having obtained permission; gārhasthyam āviśet — should enter household life; prājñaḥ — the wise; niṣpanna-guru-niṣkṛtiḥ — having discharged the debt to the teacher.

Śrīdhara

gṛhīto grāhyo grahaṇārho vedo yena saḥ । niṣpannaguruniṣkṛtiḥ dattagurudakṣiṇaḥ — Śrīdhara explains gṛhīta-grāhya-vedaḥ as one who has learned the Veda that ought to be learned, including its meaning, and niṣpanna-guru-niṣkṛtiḥ as one who has discharged the teacher’s debt by giving the prescribed guru-dakṣiṇā. With the teacher’s permission, such a student may enter the householder stage.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gṛhītagrāhyavedaśca tato’nujñāmavāpya ca । gārhasthyamāviśetprājño niṣpannaguruṇiṣkṛtiḥ ॥७॥

VP 3.9.8

विधिनावाप्तदारस्तु धनं प्राप्य स्वकर्मणा । गृहस्थकार्यमखिलं कुर्याद्भूपाल शक्तितः
॥८॥

After taking a wife according to the rule and obtaining wealth through his own work, he should perform all the duties of a householder to the best of his ability, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vidhināvāptadārastu dhanaṃ prāpya svakarmaṇā । gṛhasthakāryamakhilaṃ kuryādbhūpāla śaktitaḥ ॥८॥ — vidhinā avāpta-dāraḥ — having obtained a wife according to rule; tu — and; dhanam prāpya — having obtained wealth; sva-karmaṇā — through his own work; gṛhastha-kāryam akhilam — all household duties; kuryāt — should perform; śaktitaḥ — according to ability.

Śrīdhara

vidhinā brāhmārṣādinā, svakarmaṇā yājanādinā — “By the prescribed rule” means the Brāhma and Ārṣa forms and similar forms of marriage; “by his own work” means work such as officiating for others.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vidhināvāptadārastu dhanaṃ prāpya svakarmaṇā । gṛhasthakāryamakhilaṃ kuryādbhūpāla śaktitaḥ ॥८॥

Śrīdhara

vidhinā brāhmārṣādinā, svakarmaṇā yājanādinā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विधिना ब्राह्मार्षादिना, स्वकर्मणा याजनादिना

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 8).

Śrīdhara explains that the prescribed rule refers to the Brāhma, Ārṣa, and similar forms, and that earning wealth through one’s own work includes officiating for others.

VP 3.9.9

निवापेन पितॄनर्चन् यज्ञैर्देवांस्तथातिथीन् । अन्नैर्मुनींश्च स्वाध्यायैरपत्येन प्रजापतिम्
॥९॥

By offerings he should honor the ancestors, by sacrifices the gods, by food the guests and sages, by study the seers, and through offspring Prajāpati.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nivāpena pitṝnarcan yajñairdevāṃstathātithīn । annairmunīṃśca svādhyāyairapatyena prajāpatim ॥९॥ — nivāpena — by offerings; pitṝn arcayan — honoring the ancestors; yajñaiḥ devān — the gods through sacrifices; tathā atithīn — likewise guests; annaiḥ munīn — sages with food; svādhyāyaiḥ — through study; apatyena — through offspring; prajāpatiṃ — Prajāpati.

Śrīdhara

nivāpena piṇḍadānādinā, pitṛniti pitṛyajño darśitaḥ, yajñairdevāniti devayajñaḥ, atithīnannairiti manuṣyayajñaḥ, munīṃśca svādhyāyairiti brahmayajñaḥ, apatyena prajāpatim iti prasaṅgād gṛhasthasyāvaśyakaṃ karttavyamityuktam — The five great offerings are indicated: the ancestral offering through piṇḍa and related rites, the sacrifice to the gods, hospitality to guests, study of the Veda as the offering to the sages, and children as the offering to Prajāpati. These are obligatory duties of the householder.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nivāpena pitṝnarcan yajñairdevāṃstathātithīn । annairmunīṃśca svādhyāyairapatyena prajāpatim ॥९॥

Śrīdhara

nivāpena piṇḍadānādinā, pitṛniti pitṛyajño darśitaḥ, yajñairdevāniti devayajñaḥ, atithīnannairiti manuṣyayajñaḥ, munīṃśca svādhyāyairiti brahmayajñaḥ, apatyena prajāpatim iti prasaṅgād gṛhasthasyāvaśyakaṃ karttavyamityuktam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

निवापेन पिण्डदानादिना, पितृनिति पितृयज्ञो दर्शितः, यज्ञैर्देवानिति देवयज्ञः, अतिथीनन्नैरिति मनुष्ययज्ञः, मुनींश्च स्वाध्यायैरिति ब्रह्मयज्ञः, अपत्येन प्रजापतिम् इति प्रसङ्गाद् गृहस्थस्यावश्यकं कर्त्तव्यमित्युक्तम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 9).

Śrīdhara identifies the offerings to the ancestors, gods, human guests, and sages, and explains that having children for Prajāpati is included as an obligatory duty of the householder.

VP 3.9.10

भूतानि बलिभिश्चैव वात्सल्येनाखिलं जगत् । प्राप्नोति लोकान्पुरुषो निजकर्मसमार्जितान्
॥१०॥

Word-for-word

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

balikarmaṇā bhūtānīti bhūtayajñaḥ । vāksatyena satyavacanena । vātsalyeneti pāṭhe dātṛtvenopakartṛtvena vā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara explains that bali offered to living beings is the bhūtayajña; vāksatya means speaking the truth; and, in the reading vātsalya, the word means affectionate beneficence—giving and helping.

VP 3.9.11

बिक्षाभुजश्च ये केचित्परिव्रड्ब्रह्मचारिणः । तेप्यत्रैव प्रतिष्ठन्ते गार्हस्थ्यं तेन वै परम्
