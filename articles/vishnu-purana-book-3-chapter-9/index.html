VP 3.9.1

बालः कृतोपनयनो वेदाहरणतत्परः । गुरुगेहे वसेद्भूप ब्रह्मचारी समाहितः
॥१॥

After a boy has undergone the upanayana ceremony, he should devote himself to learning the Veda. He should live in his teacher’s house as a focused brahmacārin, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bālaḥ kṛtopanayano vedāharaṇatatparaḥ । gurugehe vasedbhūpa brahmacārī samāhitaḥ ॥१॥ — bālaḥ kṛta-upanayanaḥ — a boy who has undergone initiation; veda-āharaṇa-tatparaḥ — devoted to learning the Veda; guru-gehe vaset — should live in the teacher’s house; bhūpa — O king; brahmacārī — a brahmacārin; samāhitaḥ — focused.

Śrīdhara

nirucya haritoṣārthān varṇadharmānaśeṣataḥ । tattadāśramadharmāśca navame’tra nirūpitāḥ — Śrīdhara explains that, after fully setting out the duties of varṇa that please Hari, the duties of each āśrama are taught here over nine chapters.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bālaḥ kṛtopanayano vedāharaṇatatparaḥ । gurugehe vasedbhūpa brahmacārī samāhitaḥ ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

nirucya haritoṣārthān varṇadharmānaśeṣataḥ । tattadāśramadharmāśca navame’tra nirūpitāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

निरुच्य हरितोषार्थान् वर्णधर्मानशेषतः । तत्तदाश्रमधर्माश्च नवमेऽत्र निरूपिताः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1).

Śrīdhara explains that, after fully setting out the duties of varṇa that please Hari, the duties of each āśrama are taught here over nine chapters.

VP 3.9.2

शौचा चारव्रतं तत्र कार्यं शुश्रूषणं गुरोः । व्रतानि चरता ग्राह्यो वेदश्च कृतबुद्धिना
॥२॥

There he should maintain purity and proper conduct and serve his teacher. While observing the vows, he should study the Veda with a disciplined mind.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śaucā cāravrataṃ tatra kāryaṃ śuśrūṣaṇaṃ guroḥ । vratāni caratā grāhyo vedaśca kṛtabuddhinā ॥२॥ — śauca-ācāra-vatā — with purity and proper conduct; tatra — there; kāryam — to be performed; śuśrūṣaṇam guroḥ — service of the teacher; vratāni caratā — by one observing vows; grāhyaḥ — to be learned; vedaḥ — the Veda; kṛta-buddhinā — with a disciplined mind.

Śrīdhara

vratāni prājāpatyaṃ tathā saumyamāgneyaṃ vaiśvadaivikam ityādīni madhumāṃsavarjanādīni ca caratā kurvatā vedo grāhyaḥ — The vows include the Prājāpatya, Saumya, Āgneya, and Vaiśvadeva observances, together with abstinence from honey, meat, and similar things. While practising these, the student should learn the Veda.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śaucā cāravrataṃ tatra kāryaṃ śuśrūṣaṇaṃ guroḥ । vratāni caratā grāhyo vedaśca kṛtabuddhinā ॥२॥

Śrīdhara

vratāni prājāpatyaṃ tathā saumyamāgneyaṃ vaiśvadaivikam ityādīni madhumāṃsavarjanādīni ca caratā kurvatā vedo grāhyaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

व्रतानि प्राजापत्यं तथा सौम्यमाग्नेयं वैश्वदैविकम् इत्यादीनि मधुमांसवर्जनादीनि च चरता कुर्वता वेदो ग्राह्यः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 2).

Śrīdhara identifies the vows as the Prājāpatya, Saumya, Āgneya, and Vaiśvadeva observances, along with abstinence from honey, meat, and similar things. While practising them, the student should learn the Veda.

VP 3.9.3

उभे संध्ये रविं भूप तथैवाग्निं समाहितः । उपतिष्ठेत्तदा कुर्याद्गुरोरप्यभिवादनम्

VP 3.9.4

स्थिते तिष्ठेद्वाजेद्याते नीचैरासीत चासति । शिष्यो गुरोर्नृपश्रेष्ठ प्रतिकूलं न संचरेत्
॥४॥

When the teacher stands, the student should stand; when he goes, the student should go; when he sits, the student should sit below him. O best of kings, the student should never act contrary to his teacher.

Word-for-word

Śrīdhara

He has to totally concentrate on learning only. And on serving the Guru with all sincerity. And maintaining all the shoucha told in the shaastraas. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 3, Chapter 9, Shloka 3: Ubhe sandhye ravim bhoopa tathaa evaa agnim samaahitah | Upatishthet tathaa kuryaat guroh api abhivaadanam || Both the sandhyaa kaalaas, and also maadhyaahnika, he has to do sandhyaavandana. He also has to do agni kaarya with proper shraddhe. He has to do abhivaadana to Guru. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 3, Chapter 9, Shloka 4: Sthite tishthet vrajet yaate neechaih aaseeta chaasati | Shishyo guroon nrupa shreshtha pratikoolam na sancharet || He has to follow his Guru in every way and serve him. If Guru stands, he has to stand. He has to follow the Guru. If Guru is sitting, he has to sit; he should not sit in a seat which is higher than the Guru. Shishya should never do anything wrong to a Guru. Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 3

VP 3.9.5

तेनै वोक्तं पठेद्वेदं नान्यचित्तः पुरःस्थितः । अनुज्ञातश्च भिक्षान्नमश्नीयाद्गुरुणा ततः
॥५॥

He should recite the Veda only as instructed, with his attention undivided and seated before the teacher; once the teacher gives permission, he may then eat the food obtained by begging.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tenai voktaṃ paṭhedvedaṃ nānyacittaḥ puraḥsthitaḥ । anujñātaśca bhikṣānnamaśnīyādguruṇā tataḥ ॥५॥ — tena eva uktaḥ — instructed by that very teacher; paṭhet vedam — should recite the Veda; na anya-cittaḥ — with no other thought; purasthitaḥ — seated before him; anujñātaḥ — permitted; bhikṣā-annam aśnīyāt — should eat alms-food; gurunā — by the teacher; tataḥ — then.

Śrīdhara

tenaivoktaḥ paṭhet na tu śīghrādhyāpanāya guruṃ niyuñjīta — He should study the Veda exactly as taught, without appointing the teacher merely for rapid instruction.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tenai voktaṃ paṭhedvedaṃ nānyacittaḥ puraḥsthitaḥ । anujñātaśca bhikṣānnamaśnīyādguruṇā tataḥ ॥५॥

Śrīdhara

tenaivoktaḥ paṭhet na tu śīghrādhyāpanāya guruṃ niyuñjīta

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तेनैवोक्तः पठेत् न तु शीघ्राध्यापनाय गुरुं नियुञ्जीत

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 5).

Śrīdhara explains that the student should study the Veda as taught, not engage the teacher merely for rapid instruction.

VP 3.9.6

अवगाहेदपः पूर्वमाचार्येणावगाहितः । समिज्जलादिकं चास्य काल्यं काल्यमुपानयेत्
॥६॥

He should bathe after his teacher has bathed and should bring firewood, water, and the other morning necessities at the proper time.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

avagāhedapaḥ pūrvamācāryeṇāvagāhitaḥ । samijjalādikaṃ cāsya kālyaṃ kālyamupānayet ॥६॥ — avagāhet apaḥ — should bathe in water; pūrvam — first; ācāryeṇa avagāhitāḥ — after the teacher has bathed; samit-jala-ādikam — firewood, water, and the like; asya — for him; kālyam kālyam — every morning; upānayet — should bring.

Śrīdhara

ācāryeṇāvagāhitā apo’vagāhe । kalyaṃ kalyaṃ prātaḥ prātaḥ — He should bathe in the water in which the teacher has bathed and bring fuel, water, and similar items every morning.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ācāryeṇāvagāhitā apo’vagāhe । kalyaṃ kalyaṃ prātaḥ prātaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara explains that the student bathes in the water in which the teacher has bathed and brings the required items each morning.

VP 3.9.7

॥७॥

VP 3.9.8

विधिनावाप्तदारस्तु धनं प्राप्य स्वकर्मणा । गृहस्थकार्यमखिलं कुर्याद्भूपाल शक्तितः
॥८॥

After taking a wife according to the rule and obtaining wealth through his own work, he should perform all the duties of a householder to the best of his ability, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vidhināvāptadārastu dhanaṃ prāpya svakarmaṇā । gṛhasthakāryamakhilaṃ kuryādbhūpāla śaktitaḥ ॥८॥ — vidhinā avāpta-dāraḥ — having obtained a wife according to rule; tu — and; dhanam prāpya — having obtained wealth; sva-karmaṇā — through his own work; gṛhastha-kāryam akhilam — all household duties; kuryāt — should perform; śaktitaḥ — according to ability.

Śrīdhara

vidhinā brāhmārṣādinā, svakarmaṇā yājanādinā — “By the prescribed rule” means the Brāhma and Ārṣa forms and similar forms of marriage; “by his own work” means work such as officiating for others.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vidhināvāptadārastu dhanaṃ prāpya svakarmaṇā । gṛhasthakāryamakhilaṃ kuryādbhūpāla śaktitaḥ ॥८॥

Śrīdhara

vidhinā brāhmārṣādinā, svakarmaṇā yājanādinā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विधिना ब्राह्मार्षादिना, स्वकर्मणा याजनादिना

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 8).
