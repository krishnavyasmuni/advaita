VP 3.9.1

और्व उवाच— बालः कृतोपनयनो वेदाहरण-तत्परः।
गुरु-गेहे वसेद् भूप ब्रह्मचारी समाहितः॥१॥

After a boy has undergone the upanayana ceremony, he should devote himself to learning the Veda. He should live in his teacher’s house as a focused brahmacārin, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bālaḥ kṛta-upanayanaḥ — a boy who has undergone initiation; veda-āharaṇa-tatparaḥ — devoted to learning the Veda; guru-gehe vaset — should live in the teacher’s house; bhūpa — O king; brahmacārī — a brahmacārin; samāhitaḥ — focused.

Śrīdhara

nirucya haritoṣārthān varṇadharmānaśeṣataḥ . tattadāśramadharmāśca navame’tra nirūpitāḥ — After a boy has undergone the upanayana ceremony, he should devote himself to learning the Veda. He should live in his teacher’s house as a focused brahmacārin, O king.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bālaḥ kṛtopanayano vedāharaṇatatparaḥ । gurugehe vasedbhūpa brahmacārī samāhitaḥ ॥१॥

Śrīdhara

nirucya haritoṣārthān varṇadharmānaśeṣataḥ । tattadāśramadharmāśca navame’tra nirūpitāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

निरुच्य हरितोषार्थान् वर्णधर्मानशेषतः । तत्तदाश्रमधर्माश्च नवमेऽत्र निरूपिताः

Śrīdhara commentary.

After a boy has undergone the upanayana ceremony, he should devote himself to learning the Veda. He should live in his teacher’s house as a focused brahmacārin, O king.

VP 3.9.2

शौचाचार-व्रतं तत्र कार्यं शुश्रूषणं गुरोः।
व्रतानि चरता ग्राह्यो वेदश् च कृत-बुद्धिना॥२॥

There he should maintain purity and proper conduct and serve his teacher. While observing the vows, he should study the Veda with a disciplined mind.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śauca-ācāra-vatā — with purity and proper conduct; tatra — there; kāryam — to be performed; śuśrūṣaṇam guroḥ — service of the teacher; vratāni caratā — by one observing vows; grāhyaḥ — to be learned; vedaḥ — the Veda; kṛta-buddhinā — with a disciplined mind.

Śrīdhara

vratāni prājāpatyaṃ tathā saumyamāgneyaṃ vaiśvadaivikam ityādīni madhumāṃsavarjanādīni ca caratā kurvatā vedo grāhyaḥ — The vows include the Prājāpatya, Saumya, Āgneya, and Vaiśvadeva observances, together with abstinence from honey, meat, and similar things. While practising these, the student should learn the Veda.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śaucā cāravrataṃ tatra kāryaṃ śuśrūṣaṇaṃ guroḥ । vratāni caratā grāhyo vedaśca kṛtabuddhinā ॥२॥

Śrīdhara

vratāni prājāpatyaṃ tathā saumyamāgneyaṃ vaiśvadaivikam ityādīni madhumāṃsavarjanādīni ca caratā kurvatā vedo grāhyaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

व्रतानि प्राजापत्यं तथा सौम्यमाग्नेयं वैश्वदैविकम् इत्यादीनि मधुमांसवर्जनादीनि च चरता कुर्वता वेदो ग्राह्यः

Śrīdhara commentary.

There he should maintain purity and proper conduct and serve his teacher. While observing the vows, he should study the Veda with a disciplined mind.

VP 3.9.3

उभे सन्ध्ये रविं भूप तथैवाग्निं समाहितः।
उपतिष्ठेत् तदा कुर्याद् गुरोर् अप्य् अभिवादनम्॥३॥

At both twilights he should stand before the sun and the sacred fire with concentration; he should also offer reverence to his teacher.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ubhe sandhye — at both twilights; ravim — the sun; tathā agnim — likewise the fire; samāhitaḥ — with concentration; upatiṣṭhet — should attend; tathā kuryāt — should likewise perform; guroḥ abhivādanam — reverence to the teacher.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ubhe saṃdhye raviṃ bhūpa tathaivāgniṃ samāhitaḥ । upatiṣṭhettadā kuryādgurorapyabhivādanam ॥३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.9.4

स्थिते तिष्ठेद् व्रजेद् याते नीचैर् आसीत चासति।
संहितो गुरोर् नृप-श्रेष्ठ प्रतिकूलं न सञ्चरेत्

When the teacher stands, the student should stand; when he goes, the student should go; when he sits, the student should sit below him. O best of kings, the student should never act contrary to his teacher.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sthite — when he stands; tiṣṭhet — should stand; vrajati yāte — when he goes; nīcaiḥ āsīta — should sit below; asati — when he sits; śiṣyaḥ guroḥ — the teacher’s student; na sañcaret pratikūlam — should not act contrary.

Śrīdhara

sthite — when standing; ūrdhībhūte gurau — when the guru is elevated; śiṣyas tiṣṭhet — the student should stand; ūrdhībhavet — should be elevated; tathā āsati — likewise when he is standing; āsīne — when [the guru is] seated; nīcaiḥ hīnabhāvena āsīta — he should sit below, with humble disposition; pratikūlam etad viparītaṃ na kuryāt — he should not do this contrary opposite

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sthite tiṣṭhedvājedyāte nīcairāsīta cāsati । śiṣyo gurornṛpaśreṣṭha pratikūlaṃ na saṃcaret ॥४॥

Śrīdhara

sthite ūrdhībhūte gurau śiṣyastiṣṭhet ūrdhībhavet, tathā āsati āsīne nīcaiḥ hīnabhāvenāsīta, pratikūlametadviparītaṃ na kuryāt

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्थिते ऊर्धीभूते गुरौ शिष्यस्तिष्ठेत् ऊर्धीभवेत्, तथा आसति आसीने नीचैः हीनभावेनासीत, प्रतिकूलमेतद्विपरीतं न कुर्यात्

Śrīdhara commentary.

When the teacher stands, the student should stand; when he goes, the student should go; when he sits, the student should sit below him. O best of kings, the student should never act contrary to his teacher.

VP 3.9.5

तेनैवोक्तं पठेद् वेदं नान्य-चित्तः पुरः-स्थितः।
अनुज्ञातश् च भिक्षान्नम् अश्नीयाद् गुरुणा ततः

He should recite the Veda only as instructed, with his attention undivided and seated before the teacher; once the teacher gives permission, he may then eat the food obtained by begging.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tena eva uktaḥ — instructed by that very teacher; paṭhet vedam — should recite the Veda; na anya-cittaḥ — with no other thought; purasthitaḥ — seated before him; anujñātaḥ — permitted; bhikṣā-annam aśnīyāt — should eat alms-food; gurunā — by the teacher; tataḥ — then.

Śrīdhara

tenaivoktaḥ paṭhet na tu śīghrādhyāpanāya guruṃ niyuñjīta — He should study the Veda exactly as taught, without appointing the teacher merely for rapid instruction.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tenai voktaṃ paṭhedvedaṃ nānyacittaḥ puraḥsthitaḥ । anujñātaśca bhikṣānnamaśnīyādguruṇā tataḥ ॥५॥

Śrīdhara

tenaivoktaḥ paṭhet na tu śīghrādhyāpanāya guruṃ niyuñjīta

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तेनैवोक्तः पठेत् न तु शीघ्राध्यापनाय गुरुं नियुञ्जीत

Śrīdhara commentary.

He should recite the Veda only as instructed, with his attention undivided and seated before the teacher; once the teacher gives permission, he may then eat the food obtained by begging.

VP 3.9.6

अवगाहेद् अपः पूर्वम् आचार्येणावगाहिताः।
समिज्-जलादिकं चास्य कल्यं कल्यम् उपानयेत्

He should bathe after his teacher has bathed and should bring firewood, water, and the other morning necessities at the proper time.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

avagāhet apaḥ — should bathe in water; pūrvam — first; ācāryeṇa avagāhitāḥ — after the teacher has bathed; samit-jala-ādikam — firewood, water, and the like; asya — for him; kālyam kālyam — every morning; upānayet — should bring.

Śrīdhara

ācāryeṇāvagāhitā apo’vagāhe . kalyaṃ kalyaṃ prātaḥ prātaḥ — He should bathe in the water in which the teacher has bathed and bring fuel, water, and similar items every morning.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

avagāhedapaḥ pūrvamācāryeṇāvagāhitaḥ । samijjalādikaṃ cāsya kālyaṃ kālyamupānayet ॥६॥

Śrīdhara

ācāryeṇāvagāhitā apo’vagāhe । kalyaṃ kalyaṃ prātaḥ prātaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आचार्येणावगाहिता अपोऽवगाहे । कल्यं कल्यं प्रातः प्रातः

Śrīdhara commentary.

He should bathe after his teacher has bathed and should bring firewood, water, and the other morning necessities at the proper time.

VP 3.9.7

गृहीत-ग्राह्य-वेदश् च ततोऽनुज्ञाम् अवाप्य च।
गार्हस्थ्यम् आविशेत् प्राज्ञो निष्पन्न-गुरु-निष्कृतिः

After mastering the Veda he is meant to learn and obtaining his teacher’s permission, the wise student should enter household life, having repaid his debt to his teacher.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gṛhīta-grāhya-vedaḥ — having mastered the Veda to be learned; tataḥ — then; anujñām avāpya — having obtained permission; gārhasthyam āviśet — should enter household life; prājñaḥ — the wise; niṣpanna-guru-niṣkṛtiḥ — having discharged the debt to the teacher.

Śrīdhara

gṛhīto grāhyo grahaṇārho vedo yena saḥ . niṣpannaguruniṣkṛtiḥ dattagurudakṣiṇaḥ — After mastering the Veda he is meant to learn and obtaining his teacher’s permission, the wise student should enter household life, having repaid his debt to his teacher.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gṛhītagrāhyavedaśca tato’nujñāmavāpya ca । gārhasthyamāviśetprājño niṣpannaguruṇiṣkṛtiḥ ॥७॥

Śrīdhara

gṛhīto grāhyo grahaṇārho vedo yena saḥ । niṣpannaguruniṣkṛtiḥ dattagurudakṣiṇaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

गृहीतो ग्राह्यो ग्रहणार्हो वेदो येन सः । निष्पन्नगुरुनिष्कृतिः दत्तगुरुदक्षिणः

Śrīdhara commentary.

After mastering the Veda he is meant to learn and obtaining his teacher’s permission, the wise student should enter household life, having repaid his debt to his teacher.

VP 3.9.8

विधिनावाप्त-दारस् तु धनं प्राप्य स्व-कर्मणा।
गृहस्थ-कार्यम् अखिलं कुर्याद् भूपाल शक्तितः॥८॥

After taking a wife according to the rule and obtaining wealth through his own work, he should perform all the duties of a householder to the best of his ability, O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vidhinā avāpta-dāraḥ — having obtained a wife according to rule; tu — and; dhanam prāpya — having obtained wealth; sva-karmaṇā — through his own work; gṛhastha-kāryam akhilam — all household duties; kuryāt — should perform; śaktitaḥ — according to ability.

Śrīdhara

vidhinā brāhmārṣādinā, svakarmaṇā yājanādinā — “By the prescribed rule” means the Brāhma and Ārṣa forms and similar forms of marriage; “by his own work” means work such as officiating for others.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vidhināvāptadārastu dhanaṃ prāpya svakarmaṇā । gṛhasthakāryamakhilaṃ kuryādbhūpāla śaktitaḥ ॥८॥

Śrīdhara

vidhinā brāhmārṣādinā, svakarmaṇā yājanādinā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विधिना ब्राह्मार्षादिना, स्वकर्मणा याजनादिना

Śrīdhara commentary.

After taking a wife according to the rule and obtaining wealth through his own work, he should perform all the duties of a householder to the best of his ability, O king.

VP 3.9.9

निवापेन पितॄन् अर्चन् यज्ञैर् देवांस् तथातिथीन्।
अन्नैर् मुनींश् चस्वाध्यायैर् अपत्येन प्रजापतिम्

By offerings he should honor the ancestors, by sacrifices the gods, by food the guests and sages, by study the seers, and through offspring Prajāpati.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nivāpena — by offerings; pitṝn arcayan — honoring the ancestors; yajñaiḥ devān — the gods through sacrifices; tathā atithīn — likewise guests; annaiḥ munīn — sages with food; svādhyāyaiḥ — through study; apatyena — through offspring; prajāpatiṃ — Prajāpati.

Śrīdhara

nivāpena piṇḍa-dāna-ādinā pitṝn iti pitṛ-yajñaḥ darśitaḥ — “the ancestors by offerings” shows the ancestor sacrifice; yajñaiḥ devān iti deva-yajñaḥ — “the gods by sacrifices” is the god sacrifice; atithīn annaiḥ iti manuṣya-yajñaḥ — “guests by food” is the human sacrifice; munīn ca svādhyāyaiḥ iti brahma-yajñaḥ — “sages by study” is the Brahma sacrifice; apatyena prajā-patim iti — “Prajāpati by offspring”; prasaṅgāt gṛhasthasya āvaśyakam kartavyam iti uktam — thus, incidentally, the householder's obligatory duties are stated

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nivāpena pitṝnarcan yajñairdevāṃstathātithīn । annairmunīṃśca svādhyāyairapatyena prajāpatim ॥९॥

Śrīdhara

nivāpena piṇḍadānādinā, pitṛniti pitṛyajño darśitaḥ, yajñairdevāniti devayajñaḥ, atithīnannairiti manuṣyayajñaḥ, munīṃśca svādhyāyairiti brahmayajñaḥ, apatyena prajāpatim iti prasaṅgād gṛhasthasyāvaśyakaṃ karttavyamityuktam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

निवापेन पिण्डदानादिना, पितृनिति पितृयज्ञो दर्शितः, यज्ञैर्देवानिति देवयज्ञः, अतिथीनन्नैरिति मनुष्ययज्ञः, मुनींश्च स्वाध्यायैरिति ब्रह्मयज्ञः, अपत्येन प्रजापतिम् इति प्रसङ्गाद् गृहस्थस्यावश्यकं कर्त्तव्यमित्युक्तम्

Śrīdhara commentary.

By offerings he should honor the ancestors, by sacrifices the gods, by food the guests and sages, by study the seers, and through offspring Prajāpati.

VP 3.9.10

भूतानि बलिभिश् चैव वैराग्य्लक्षणेनाखिलं जगत्।
प्राप्नोति लोकान् पुरुषो निज-कर्म-समर्जितान्॥१०॥

Through offerings to beings and kindness toward the whole world, a person obtains the worlds earned by his own actions.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhūtāni balibhiḥ — beings through offerings; ca eva — and indeed; vātsalyena — through kindness; akhilam jagat — the whole world; prāpnoti lokān — obtains worlds; nija-karma-samārjitān — earned by one’s own actions.

Śrīdhara

balikarmaṇā bhūtānīti bhūtayajñaḥ . vāksatyena satyavacanena . vātsalyeneti pāṭhe dātṛtvenopakartṛtvena vā — Through offerings to beings and kindness toward the whole world, a person obtains the worlds earned by his own actions.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhūtāni balibhiścaiva vātsalyenākhilaṃ jagat । prāpnoti lokānpuruṣo nijakarmasamārjitān ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara

balikarmaṇā bhūtānīti bhūtayajñaḥ । vāksatyena satyavacanena । vātsalyeneti pāṭhe dātṛtvenopakartṛtvena vā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

बलिकर्मणा भूतानीति भूतयज्ञः । वाक्सत्येन सत्यवचनेन । वात्सल्येनेति पाठे दातृत्वेनोपकर्तृत्वेन वा

Śrīdhara commentary.

Through offerings to beings and kindness toward the whole world, a person obtains the worlds earned by his own actions.

VP 3.9.11

भिक्षा-भुजश् च ये केचित् परिव्राड् ब्रह्मचारिणः।
तेऽप्य् अत्रैव प्रतिष्ठन्ते गार्हस्थ्यं तेन वै परम्॥११॥

Those who live on alms, including wandering brahmacārins, also depend on the householder; for that reason household life is the foremost support.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhikṣā-bhujaḥ — alms-eaters; ye kecit — whoever; parivrāj-brahmacāriṇaḥ — wandering brahmacārins; te api — they too; atra eva pratiṣṭhante — depend here; gārhasthyam — household life; tena vai param — therefore foremost.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bikṣābhujaśca ye kecitparivraḍbrahmacāriṇaḥ । tepyatraiva pratiṣṭhante gārhasthyaṃ tena vai param ॥११॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.9.12

वेदाहरण-कार्याय तीर्थ-स्नानाय च प्रभो।
अटन्ति वसुधां विप्राः पृथिवी-दर्शनाय च॥१२॥

For the sake of learning the Veda, bathing at holy places, and seeing the earth, Brāhmaṇas travel across the land, O Lord.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

veda-āharaṇa-kāryāya — for the purpose of learning the Veda; tīrtha-snāna-āya — for bathing at holy places; ca — and; prabho — O Lord; aṭanti vasudhām — travel over the earth; viprāḥ — Brāhmaṇas; pṛthivī-darśanāya — for seeing the earth.

Śrīdhara

aṭanti bhrāmyanti — “They wander” means that they travel from place to place.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vedāharaṇakāryāya tīrthasnānāya ca prabho । aṭanti vasudhāṃ viprāḥ pṛthivīdarśanāya ca ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara

aṭanti bhrāmyanti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अटन्ति भ्राम्यन्ति

Śrīdhara commentary.

For the sake of learning the Veda, bathing at holy places, and seeing the earth, Brāhmaṇas travel across the land, O Lord.

VP 3.9.13

अनिकेता ह्य् अनाहारा यत्र सायं-गृहाश् च ये।
तेषां गृहस्थः सर्वेषां प्रतिष्ठा योनिर् एव च

Those without a home, those who do not cook and eat, and those whose home is wherever evening finds them—all of them have the householder as their support and source.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aniketāḥ — without homes; hi anāhārāḥ — not taking cooked food; yatra sāyam gṛhāḥ — whose home is where evening comes; ca ye — and those who are; teṣām — of them; gṛhasthaḥ — the householder; pratiṣṭhā — support; yoniḥ — source.

Śrīdhara

VP 3.9.29

VP 3.9.32

कृत्वाग्निहोत्रं स्व-शरीर-संस्थं शरीरम् अग्निं स्व-मुखे जुहोति।
विप्रस् तु भैक्ष्योपहितैर् हविर्भिश् चिताग्निकानां व्रजति स्म लोकान्॥३२॥

Having established the sacred fire in his own body, the twice-born man offers food obtained by begging into the fire in his mouth and reaches the worlds attained by those whose fires have been kindled.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṛtvā agnihotram — having established the fire offering; sva-śarīra-saṃstham — situated in his own body; śārīram agnim — the bodily fire; sva-mukhe juhoti — offers into his mouth; vipraḥ — the twice-born man; bhaikṣa-upahitaiḥ havirbhiḥ — with oblations supplied by alms; citāgnikānām lokān — the worlds of those whose fires are kindled; vrajati — reaches.

Śrīdhara

bhikṣānna-bhakṣaṇa eva upāsanām āha — he teaches the meditation in eating alms-food; agnihotraṃ pūrvam hūyamānam vahniṃ śarīra-saṃstham kṛtvā — having made the previously offered agnihotra fire abide in the body; prājāpatya-iṣṭy-anantaram ātmani agnīn ādhāya — after the Prajāpatya sacrifice, placing the fires within oneself; bhikṣā-rūpaiḥ havirbhiḥ svamukhe kuṇḍa-sthānīye — with alms-food as oblations, in one's mouth as the fire-pit; śārīram prāṇādi-yuktaṃ jāṭharam agnim uddiśya — directed to the bodily gastric fire joined with the vital breaths; citāgninā jāṭharaḥ agniḥ bhuṅkte na aham — “the fire consumes, not I”; citi-caitanyena agninā anusandhānena yaḥ juhoti — one who offers with contemplation of the fire of consciousness; saḥ lokān vrajati — he reaches the worlds; yadvā loko brahma eva — or “world” is Brahman; bahutvam avivakṣitam — plurality is not intended; citāgninā iti pāṭhe — in the reading citāgninā

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṛtvāgnihotraṃsvaśarīrasaṃsthaṃ śārīramagniṃ svamukhe juhoti । viprastu bhaikṣyopahitairhavirbhiścitāgnikānāṃ vrahati sma lokān ॥३२॥

Śrīdhara

bhikṣānnabhakṣaṇa evopāsanām āha—kṛtva iti । agnihotraṃ pūrvaṃ hūyamānaṃ vahniṃ śarīrasaṃsthaṃ kṛtvā prājāpatyeṣṭyanantaramātmanyagnīnādhāya bhikṣārūpairhavirbhiḥ svamukhe kuṇḍasthānīye śārīraṃ prāṇādiyuktaṃ jāṭharamagnimuddiśya citāgninā jāṭharo’gnirbhuṅkte nāham iti citā caitanyenāgninā anusandhānena yo juhoti, sa lokānarthādagnihotriṇāṃ vrajatītyupāsanāphaloktiḥ । yadvā lokyate iti loko brahmaiva, bahutvamavivakṣitam । citāgnineti pāṭhe

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भिक्षान्नभक्षण एवोपासनाम् आह—कृत्व इति । अग्निहोत्रं पूर्वं हूयमानं वह्निं शरीरसंस्थं कृत्वा प्राजापत्येष्ट्यनन्तरमात्मन्यग्नीनाधाय भिक्षारूपैर्हविर्भिः स्वमुखे कुण्डस्थानीये शारीरं प्राणादियुक्तं जाठरमग्निमुद्दिश्य चिताग्निना जाठरोऽग्निर्भुङ्क्ते नाहम् इति चिता चैतन्येनाग्निना अनुसन्धानेन यो जुहोति, स लोकानर्थादग्निहोत्रिणां व्रजतीत्युपासनाफलोक्तिः । यद्वा लोक्यते इति लोको ब्रह्मैव, बहुत्वमविवक्षितम् । चिताग्निनेति पाठे

Śrīdhara commentary.

Having established the sacred fire in his own body, the twice-born man offers food obtained by begging into the fire in his mouth and reaches the worlds attained by those whose fires have been kindled.

VP 3.9.33

मोक्षाश्रमं यश् चरते यथोक्तं शुचिः सुखं कल्पित-बुद्धि-युक्तः।

Transliteration

Śrīdhara commentary.

The twice-born man who follows the path of liberation as taught, pure and firm in understanding, becomes tranquil like a smokeless flame and reaches Brahmaloka.
