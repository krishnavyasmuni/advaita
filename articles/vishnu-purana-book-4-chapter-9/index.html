VP 4.9.1

रजेस्तु पञ्च पुत्रशतान्यतुलबलपराक्रमसाराण्यासन्

Sri Paraasharar - Raji had 500 sons, who were all extremely powerful, and had paraakrama, bala, and known for their stability.

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.9.2

देवासुरसंग्रामारम्भे च परस्परवधेप्सवो देवाश्चासुराश्च ब्रह्माणमुपेत्य पप्रच्छुः

There was a war starting between devaas and asuraas, at the beginning, each wanted to destroy the other; both devaas and asuraas go to Chaturmukha Brahma and ask him.

रजेः पुत्रशतञ्चैन्द्रात् पातितं वर्ण्यते । रम्भोऽनपत्यो नवमे क्षत्रवृद्धान्वयः पुनः । आयुःपुत्रस्य क्षत्रवृद्धस्य वंशमुक्त्वा तद्भ्रातु रजेर्वंशमाह—रजेर् इति । रजेः पुत्रशतपञ्चकस्य पुत्रा नामवन्निति वक्तुं कथां प्रस्तौति—देवासुर इति ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 2).

There was a war starting between devaas and asuraas, at the beginning, each wanted to destroy the other; both devaas and asuraas go to Chaturmukha Brahma and ask him.

VP 4.9.8

न वयमन्यथा वदिष्यामोऽन्यथा करिष्यामोऽस्माकमिन्द्रः प्रह्लादस्तदर्थमेवायमुद्यम इत्युक्त्वा गतेश्वसुरेषु देवैरप्यसाववनिपतिरेवमेवोक्तस्तेनापि च तथैवोक्ते देवैरिन्द्रस्त्वं भविष्यसीति समन्विच्छितम्

They say that "We don't say something and do something else". They say that "Our Indra is only Prahlaada, and for that reason only, I am fighting the gods, so that Prahlaada can become the Indra". They did not accept Raji's offer that he wanted to become Indra. They went back being disappointed. After that, the gods approached Raji, and asked him the same thing - "Can you please help us and fight on our side, we want to win over the daityaas". The gods agreed to his condition. "We agree to your condition that if we win over the daityaas, you will be the Indra".
