VP 1.6.1

अर्वाक् स्रोतास् तु कथितो भवता यस् तु मानुषः ।
ब्रह्मन् विस्तरतो ब्रूहि ब्रह्मा तम् असृजद् यथा ॥१॥

Thou hast briefly noticed, illustrious sage, the creation termed Arvāksrotas, or that of mankind: now explain to me more fully how Brahmā accomplished it;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

arvāk-srotas — human creation; kathitaḥ — described; vistārataḥ — fully; brūhi — tell; brahmā — Brahmā; tam — it; asṛjat — created; yathā — how

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

arvāk srotās tu kathito bhavatā yas tu mānuṣaḥ |
brahman vistarato brūhi brahmā tam asṛjad yathā || 1 ||

VP 1.6.2

यथा च वर्णान् असृजद् यद् गुणांश् च प्रजापतिः ।
यच् च तेषां स्मृतं कर्म विप्रादीनां तद् उच्यताम् ॥२॥

how he created the four different castes; what duties he assigned to the Brahmans and the rest.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathā — how; varṇān — castes; asṛjat — created; guṇān — qualities; prajāpatiḥ — the Prajāpati; karma — duties; vipra-ādīnām — of Brahmans and the rest; ucyatām — let it be told

Śrīdhara

vistaratō brūhi — explain fully; yathā ca — how

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathā ca varṇān asṛjad yad guṇāṃś ca prajāpatiḥ |
yac ca teṣāṃ smṛtaṃ karma viprādīnāṃ tad ucyatām || 2 ||

Śrīdhara

vistarato brūhīti yad uktaṃ, tad eva vivṛṇoti–yathā ca iti || 2 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विस्तरतो ब्रूहीति यद् उक्तं, तद् एव विवृणोति–यथा च इति ॥२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Explain fully” is taken up here: “how [the castes were created]...”

VP 1.6.3

सत्याभिध्यायिनः पूर्वं सिसृक्षोर् ब्रह्मणो जगत् ।
अजायन्त द्विज-श्रेष्ठ सत्त्वोद्रिक्ता मुखात् प्रजाः ॥३॥

Formerly, O best of Brahmans, when the truth-meditating Brahmā was desirous of creating the world, there sprang from his mouth beings especially endowed with the quality of goodness;

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

satya-abhi-dhyāyinaḥ — truth-meditating; pūrvam — formerly; sisṛkṣoḥ — desirous to create; brahmaṇaḥ — of Brahmā; jagat — the world; ajāyanta — sprang forth; sattva-udriktāḥ — predominant in goodness; mukhāt — from the mouth; prajāḥ — beings

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

satyābhidhyāyinaḥ pūrvaṃ sisṛkṣor brahmaṇo jagat |

VP 1.6.4

वक्षसो रजसोद्रिक्तास् तथा वै ब्रह्मणोऽभवन् ।
रजसा तमसा चैव समुद्रिक्तास् तथोरुतः ॥४॥

others sprang from his breast, pervaded by the quality of foulness; others from his thighs, in whom foulness and darkness prevailed;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vakṣasaḥ — from the breast; rajasa-udriktāḥ — predominant in foulness; tathā — likewise; uru-taḥ — from the thighs; rajasā — foulness; tamasā — darkness; samudriktāḥ — predominant

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vakṣaso rajasodriktās tathā vai brahmaṇo’bhavan |
rajasā tamasā caiva samudriktās tathorutaḥ || 4 ||

VP 1.6.5

पद्भ्याम् अन्याः प्रजा ब्रह्मा ससर्ज द्विज-सत्तम ।
पादोरु-वक्षः-स्थलतो मुखतश् च समुद्गताः ॥५॥

and others from his feet, in whom the quality of darkness predominated.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

padbhyām — from the feet; anyāḥ prajāḥ — other beings; brahmā sasṛja — Brahmā created; pāda-uru-vakṣaḥ-sthalataḥ — from feet, thighs, and breast; mukhataḥ — from the mouth; samudgatāḥ — arisen

Śrīdhara

caturvidhāḥ prajāḥ — four kinds of beings; mukhādibhyaḥ sṛṣṭāḥ — created from the mouth and other limbs; prātilomyena sambandhaḥ — the reverse correspondence

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

padbhyām anyāḥ prajā brahmā sasarja dvija-sattama |
pādoru-vakṣaḥ-sthalato mukhataś ca samudgatāḥ || 5 ||

Śrīdhara

tāścaturvidhā mukhādibhyaḥ sṛṣṭāḥ prajāḥ | idaṃ-prasiddhaṃ cāturvaṇyaṃ-catvāro varṇāḥ || etad eva vibhāgena spaṣṭayati-brāhmaṇā iti | pādoru-vakṣaḥ-sthalato mukhataś ceti brāhmaṇādibhiḥ prātilomyena sambandhaḥ || 5 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ताश्चतुर्विधा मुखादिभ्यः सृष्टाः प्रजाः । इदं-प्रसिद्धं चातुर्वण्यं-चत्वारो वर्णाः ॥ एतद् एव विभागेन स्पष्टयति-ब्राह्मणा इति । पादोरु-वक्षः-स्थलतो मुखतश् चेति ब्राह्मणादिभिः प्रातिलोम्येन सम्बन्धः ॥५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

These are the four classes of beings created from Brahmā’s mouth and other limbs. The later enumeration connects the feet, thighs, breast, and mouth with the castes in reverse order.

VP 1.6.6

ब्राह्मणाः क्षत्रिया वैश्याः शुद्राश् च द्विज-सत्तम ।
पादोरु-वक्षः-स्थलतो मुखतश् च समुद्गताः ॥६॥

These were, in succession, beings of the several castes—Brahmans, Kshetriyas, Vaisyas, and Śúdras—produced from the mouth, the breast, the thighs, and the feet of Brahmā.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brāhmaṇāḥ — Brahmans; kṣatriyāḥ — Kshetriyas; vaiśyāḥ — Vaisyas; śūdrāḥ — Śúdras; pāda-uru-vakṣaḥ-sthalataḥ — from feet, thighs, and breast; mukhataḥ — from the mouth; samudgatāḥ — arisen

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyāḥ śudrāś ca dvija-sattama |
pādoru-vakṣaḥ-sthalato mukhataś ca samudgatāḥ || 6 ||

VP 1.6.7

यज्ञ-निष्पत्तये सर्वम् एतद् ब्रह्मा चकार वै ।
चातुर्वर्ण्यं महाभाग यज्ञ-साधनम् उत्तमम् ॥७॥

These he created for the performance of sacrifices, the four castes being the fit instruments of their celebration.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajña-niṣpattaye — for accomplishing sacrifice; sarvam etat — all these; brahmā cakāra — Brahmā made; cāturvarṇyam — the fourfold order; yajña-sādhanam — instrument of sacrifice; uttamam — excellent

Śrīdhara

yajña-niṣpatti — accomplishment of sacrifice; cāturvarṇyam — the fourfold order; yajña-sādhanam — instrument of sacrifice; śūdrasya adhikāraḥ — the Śūdra’s participation

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajña-niṣpattaye sarvam etad brahmā cakāra vai |
cāturvarṇyaṃ mahābhāga yajña-sādhanam uttamam || 7 ||

Śrīdhara

tatsṛṣṭeḥ prayojanam āha—yajñaniṣpattaye sarvametad yajñasādhanabhūtam uttamaṃ cāturvarāyaṃ cakāra ity anvayaḥ | yadyapi adhyayanādyabhāvāt na karmāṇi syāt iti sūtre jaiminiḥ śūdrasya adhyayanādyabhāvād yajñādhikāraṃ na manyate, tathāpi dvijaśuśrūṣādvāreṇa tatsādhanatvāt namaskāreṇa mantreṇa pañca yajñān na hāpayet ity-ādismṛteḥ pākayajñādiṣu sākṣādanadhikārānna virodhaḥ || 7 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत्सृष्टेः प्रयोजनम् आह—यज्ञनिष्पत्तये सर्वमेतद् यज्ञसाधनभूतम् उत्तमं चातुर्वरायं चकार इत्य् अन्वयः । यद्यपि अध्ययनाद्यभावात् न कर्माणि स्यात् इति सूत्रे जैमिनिः शूद्रस्य अध्ययनाद्यभावाद् यज्ञाधिकारं न मन्यते, तथापि द्विजशुश्रूषाद्वारेण तत्साधनत्वात् नमस्कारेण मन्त्रेण पञ्च यज्ञान् न हापयेत् इत्य्-आदिस्मृतेः पाकयज्ञादिषु साक्षादनधिकारान्न विरोधः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Brahmā created the four castes as the superior instruments for accomplishing sacrifice. He notes that Śūdras participate in sacrificial purposes through service to the twice-born and in forms of sacrifice permitted by sacred tradition, so there is no contradiction.

VP 1.6.8

यज्ञैर् आप्यायिता देवा वृष्ट्य्-उत्सर्गेण वै प्रजाः ।
आप्याययन्ते धर्मज्ञ यज्ञाः कल्याण-हेतवः ॥८॥

By sacrifices, O thou who knowest the truth, the gods are nourished; and by the rain which they bestow, mankind are supported:

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajñaiḥ — by sacrifices; āpyāyitāḥ — nourished; devāḥ — the gods; vṛṣṭi-utsargeṇa — by the release of rain; prajāḥ — people; āpyāyayante — are nourished; kalyāṇa-hetavaḥ — causes of welfare

Śrīdhara

āpyāyitāḥ — nourished; vṛṣṭy-utsarga — release of rain; agni-aupahuti — oblation in fire; kalyāṇa-hetavaḥ — causes of welfare

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajñair āpyāyitā devā vṛṣṭy-utsargeṇa vai prajāḥ |
āpyāyayante dharmajña yajñāḥ kalyāṇa-hetavaḥ || 8 ||

Śrīdhara

yajñaniṣpādanasya upayogam āha—yajñair iti | āpyāyitāstarpitā devā vṛṣṭayutsargeṇa tāḥ yajñakartīḥ prajāḥ tarpayanti | agnau prāstāhutiḥ samyag ādityamupatiṣṭhate | ādityājjāyate vṛṣṭirvṛṣṭerannaṃ tataḥ prajāḥ iti smṛteḥ ato yajñāḥ kalyāṇahetavaḥ || 8 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यज्ञनिष्पादनस्य उपयोगम् आह—यज्ञैर् इति । आप्यायितास्तर्पिता देवा वृष्टयुत्सर्गेण ताः यज्ञकर्तीः प्रजाः तर्पयन्ति । अग्नौ प्रास्ताहुतिः सम्यग् आदित्यमुपतिष्ठते । आदित्याज्जायते वृष्टिर्वृष्टेरन्नं ततः प्रजाः इति स्मृतेः अतो यज्ञाः कल्याणहेतवः ॥८॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The benefit of sacrifice is this: the gods are nourished and release rain; through the rain, the people who perform sacrifice are nourished. The remembered sequence is: oblation placed in fire reaches the sun; rain arises from the sun; food from rain; and beings from food. Therefore sacrifices are causes of welfare.

VP 1.6.9

निष्पाद्यन्ते नरैस् तैस् तु स्व-धर्माभिरतैः सदा ।
विशुद्धाचरणोपेतैः सद्भिः सन्-मार्ग-गामिभिः ॥९॥

and thus sacrifices, the source of happiness, are performed by pious men, attached to their duties, attentive to prescribed obligations, and walking in the paths of virtue.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

niṣpādyante — are performed; naraiḥ — by men; sva-dharma-abhirataiḥ — devoted to their own duties; viśuddha-ācaraṇa-upetaiḥ — endowed with pure conduct; sadbhiḥ — by good people; san-mārga-gāmi-bhiḥ — walking in the good path

Śrīdhara

sva-dharma — one’s own Vedic duty; viśuddha-ācaraṇa — prescribed pure conduct; san-mārga-gāmin — one walking the good path

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

niṣpādyante narais tais tu sva-dharmābhirataiḥ sadā |
viśuddhācaraṇopetaiḥ sadbhiḥ san-mārga-gāmibhiḥ || 9 ||

Śrīdhara

teṣu śreṣṭhānadhikāriṇo darśayati niṣpādyanta iti | svadharmeṣu śrauteṣu abhirataiḥ viśuddhācaraṇaṃ smṛtyādivihitaṃ tadupetaiḥ | pāṭhāntare viruddhācaraṇāt apetaiḥ nivṛttaiḥ sadbhirvidvadbhiḥ sanmārgagāmibhirmanvādisamānācārānuvartibhiḥ || 9 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तेषु श्रेष्ठानधिकारिणो दर्शयति निष्पाद्यन्त इति । स्वधर्मेषु श्रौतेषु अभिरतैः विशुद्धाचरणं स्मृत्यादिविहितं तदुपेतैः । पाठान्तरे विरुद्धाचरणात् अपेतैः निवृत्तैः सद्भिर्विद्वद्भिः सन्मार्गगामिभिर्मन्वादिसमानाचारानुवर्तिभिः ॥९॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Those best qualified to perform sacrifice are people devoted to their Vedic duties, following purity prescribed by sacred tradition, learned, and walking the good path.

VP 1.6.10

स्वर्गापवर्गौ मानुष्यात् प्राप्नुवन्ति नरा मुने ।
यच् चाभिरुचितं स्थानं तद्यान्ति मनुजा द्विज ॥१०॥

Men acquire (by them) heavenly fruition, or final felicity; they go, after death, to whatever sphere they aspire to, as the consequence of their human nature.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

svarga-apavargau — heaven and liberation; mānuṣyāt — from human birth; prāpnuvanti — attain; narāḥ — men; yad abhirucitam sthānam — the desired realm; yānti — they go

Śrīdhara

mānuṣya — human birth; dharmādhikāra — eligibility for dharma; abhirucita-sthāna — desired realm

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

svargāpavargau mānuṣyāt prāpnuvanti narā mune |
yac cābhirucitaṃ sthānaṃ tadyānti manujā dvija || 10 ||

Śrīdhara

ato dharmādhikāratvānmānuṣyaṃ stauti—svargāpavargāv iti | yathābhirucitaṃ sthānaṃ satyalokādi || 10 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अतो धर्माधिकारत्वान्मानुष्यं स्तौति—स्वर्गापवर्गाव् इति । यथाभिरुचितं स्थानं सत्यलोकादि ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Human birth is praised because it grants eligibility for dharma. The desired destinations include realms such as Satyaloka.

VP 1.6.11

प्रजास् ता ब्रह्मणा सृष्टाश् चातुर्वर्ण्य-व्यवस्थिताः ।
सम्यक्-श्रद्धा-समाचार-प्रवणा मुनि-सत्तम ॥११॥

The beings who were created by Brahmā, of these four castes, were at first endowed with righteousness and perfect faith;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prajāḥ — beings; brahmaṇā sṛṣṭāḥ — created by Brahmā; cāturvarṇya-vyavasthitāḥ — established in the fourfold order; śraddhā-samācāra-pravaṇāḥ — devoted to faith and conduct

Śrīdhara

cāturvarṇya-vyavasthiti — fourfold order; śraddhā — faith; samācāra — conduct

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prajās tā brahmaṇā sṛṣṭāś cāturvarṇya-vyavasthitāḥ |
samyak-śraddhā-samācāra-pravaṇā muni-sattama || 11 ||

Śrīdhara

tad evaṃ cāturvarṇyavyavasthitaye tāḥ prajāḥ sṛṣṭāḥ samyak śraddhayā yaḥ samācārastasmin pravaṇāḥ || 11 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तद् एवं चातुर्वर्ण्यव्यवस्थितये ताः प्रजाः सृष्टाः सम्यक् श्रद्धया यः समाचारस्तस्मिन् प्रवणाः ॥११॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

These beings were created within the fourfold order to be devoted to proper faith and conduct.

VP 1.6.12

यथेच्छा-वास-निरता सर्व-बाधा-विवर्जिताः ।
शुद्धान्तः-करणाः शुद्धाः कर्मानुष्ठान-निर्मलाः ॥१२॥

they abode wherever they pleased, unchecked by any impediment; their hearts were free from guile; they were pure, made free from soil, by observance of sacred institutes.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathā-icchā-vāsa-niratāḥ — dwelling wherever they wished; sarva-bādhā-vivarjitāḥ — free from every affliction; śuddha-antaḥkaraṇāḥ — pure inwardly; śuddhāḥ — pure; karma-anuṣṭhāna-nirmalāḥ — cleansed by sacred practice

Śrīdhara

yathēcchā-vāsa — dwelling as one wishes; sarva-bādha-vivarjita — free from all affliction; śuddha — pure and noble

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathecchā-vāsa-niratā sarva-bādhā-vivarjitāḥ |
śuddhāntaḥ-karaṇāḥ śuddhāḥ karmānuṣṭhāna-nirmalāḥ || 12 ||

Śrīdhara

cāturvarṇyavyavasthāphalam āha—yatheccha iti dvābhyām | yathecchayā āvāsaḥ girikandarādisthānaṃ tasmin niratāḥ | yataḥ sarvabādhābhiḥ śītoṣṇādipīḍābhirvivarjitāḥ | śuddhā satkulīnāḥ || 12 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

चातुर्वर्ण्यव्यवस्थाफलम् आह—यथेच्छ इति द्वाभ्याम् । यथेच्छया आवासः गिरिकन्दरादिस्थानं तस्मिन् निरताः । यतः सर्वबाधाभिः शीतोष्णादिपीडाभिर्विवर्जिताः । शुद्धा सत्कुलीनाः ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The result of the fourfold order was that they lived wherever they wished, in places such as mountain caves, free from suffering such as heat and cold; they were pure and noble.
