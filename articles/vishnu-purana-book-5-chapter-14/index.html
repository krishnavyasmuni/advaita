VP 5.14.1

प्रदोषाग्रो कदाचित्तु रासासक्ते जनार्दने । त्रासयन्समदो गौष्ठमरिष्टसमुपागमत्

Sri Paraasharar - In the Pradosha kaala, when Krishna was enjoying in sporting with the gopis in the Raasamandala, Arishta, a ferocious demon in the form of a bull, causing terror to everyone. He came near all the cows.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

He came near all the cows.

Śrīdhara

He came near all the cows.

Śrīdhara commentary.

श्री पराशरः - प्रदोर्षाग्रे कदाथचत् तु रासासक्ते जनादिने | त्रासयन्समदो गोषठम् अररषटः समुपागमत् ॥ १ ॥ Sri Paraasharar - In the Pradosha kaala, when Krishna was enjoying in sporting with the gopis in the Raasamandala, Arishta, a ferocious demon in the form of a bull, causing terror to everyone. He came near all the cows.

VP 5.14.2

* na vyākhyātam.* — All the gopaas were extremely pleased, by this wonderful act of Krishna, and they started praising Krishna, just like, when Indra killed the demon Jambha, all the gods were so happy and they praised Indra. In the same way, all the gopaas were feeling so happy and joyous that they praised Krishna for this wonderful act of killing Arishta demon, who came in the form of a ferocious bull.
