VP 5.14.1

प्रदोषाग्रे कदाचित्तु रासासक्ते जनार्दने।
त्रासयन्समदो गोष्ठमरिष्टस्समुपागमत्

Sri Paraasharar - In the Pradosha kaala, when Krishna was enjoying in sporting with the gopis in the Raasamandala, Arishta, a ferocious demon in the form of a bull, causing terror to everyone. He came near all the cows.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pradoṣāgre — at the beginning of evening twilight; kadācit tu — once indeed; rāsāsakte janārdane — while Janārdana was absorbed in the Rāsa [dance]; trāsayan — terrifying; samadaḥ — arrogant; goṣṭham — the cowherd settlement; ariṣṭaḥ samupāgamat — Ariṣṭa came near

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pradoṣāgre kadācit tu rāsāsakte janārdane | trāsayan samado goṣṭham ariṣṭas samupāgamat

Śrīdhara

pradoṣāgra iti . pradoṣāgre sandhyāvasāne . ariṣṭo nāma vṛṣabhākir daityaḥ samado goṣṭhaṃ trasayan samupāgataḥ ||१|| satoya-toyada-cchāyastīkṣṇa-śṛṅgo’rka-locanaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रदोषाग्र इति । प्रदोषाग्रे सन्ध्यावसाने । अरिष्टो नाम वृषभाकिर् दैत्यः समदो गोष्ठं त्रसयन् समुपागतः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - In the Pradosha kaala, when Krishna was enjoying in sporting with the gopis in the Raasamandala, Arishta, a ferocious demon in the form of a bull, causing terror to everyone. He came near all the cows.

VP 5.14.2

सतोयतोयदच्छायस्तीक्ष्णशृङ्गोऽर्कलोचनः।
खुराग्रपातैरत्यर्थं दारयन्धरणीतलम्

He was as though the shade of a dark cloud, filled with water. His horns were very sharp. His eyes were bright, shining like the Sun. It was as though, with the tips of his hooves, he was tearing the earth itself.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saḥ — he; toyatoyadacchāyaḥ — having the hue of a rain-cloud; tīkṣṇaśṛṅgaḥ — sharp-horned; arka-locanaḥ — sun-eyed; khurāgra-pātaiḥ — with the strikes of hoof-tips; atyartham — greatly; dārayan — tearing; dharaṇī-talam — the earth’s surface

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sa toyatoyadacchāyas tīkṣṇaśṛṅgo ’rkalocanaḥ | khurāgrapātair atyarthaṃ dārayan dharaṇī-talam

Śrīdhara

sa toyeti . arkāv iva locane yw saḥ ||२|| lelihānaḥ sa-niṣpeṣañjihvayauṣṭhau punaḥ punaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स तोयेति । अर्काव् इव लोचने यः सः ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

He was as though the shade of a dark cloud, filled with water. His horns were very sharp. His eyes were bright, shining like the Sun. It was as though, with the tips of his hooves, he was tearing the earth itself.

VP 5.14.3

लेलिहानस्सनिष्पेषं जिह्वयोष्ठौ पुनः पुनः।
संरम्भाविद्धलाङ्गूलः कठिनस्कन्धबन्धनः

His tongue was licking his lips, making a lot of sound. His tail was curved with excitement. His neck was very hard and powerful.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

lelihānaḥ — licking; sa-niṣpeṣam — with crushing sound; jihvā-oṣṭhau punaḥ punaḥ — tongue and lips again and again; saṃrambhāviddha-lāṅgūlaḥ — with tail impelled by fury; kaṭhina-skandha-bandhanaḥ — hard-shouldered and firm at the neck

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

lelihānas saniṣpeṣaṃ jihvayoṣṭhau punaḥ punaḥ | saṃrambhāviddhalāṅgūlaḥ kaṭhina-skandha-bandhanaḥ

Śrīdhara

lelihāna iti . sa-niṣpeṣaṃ sa-saṅgharṣaṃ yathā bhy evam oṣṭau jihvayā lelihānaḥ saṃrambhād āviddham unnamitaṃ lāṅgūlaṃ yena saḥ . kaṭhinaṃ skandha-bandhanaṃ yw saḥ ||३|| udagra-kakud-ābhoga-pramāṇoduratikramaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

लेलिहान इति । स-निष्पेषं स-सङ्घर्षं यथा भ्य् एवम् ओष्टौ जिह्वया लेलिहानः संरम्भाद् आविद्धम् उन्नमितं लाङ्गूलं येन सः । कठिनं स्कन्ध-बन्धनं यः सः ॥३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

His tongue was licking his lips, making a lot of sound. His tail was curved with excitement. His neck was very hard and powerful.

VP 5.14.4

उदग्रककुदाभोगप्रमाणो दुरतिक्रमः।
विण्मूत्रलिप्तपृष्ठाङ्गो गवामुद्वेगकारकः

He had a huge raised hump on his back. The back side of his body was dirty with urine and cowdung. All the cows were terrified just by looking at him.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

udagra-kakudābhoga-pramāṇaḥ — of immense raised-hump measure; duratikramaḥ — difficult to approach; viṇ-mūtra-lipta-pṛṣṭhāṅgaḥ — with hind-body smeared with feces and urine; gavām udvega-kārakaḥ — causing distress to the cows

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

udagra-kakudābhoga-pramāṇo duratikramaḥ | viṇ-mūtra-lipta-pṛṣṭhāṅgo gavām udvega-kārakaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.14.5

प्रलम्बकण्ठोऽतिमुखस्तरुखाताङ्कताननः।
पातयन्स गवां गर्भान्दैत्यो वृषभरूपधृक्

This demon, who took the form of a bull, his neck was long and hanging down. His face was having the marks, scars of butting against the trees. At the very look at him, the cows which were pregnant aborted, being terrified.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pralamba-kaṇṭhaḥ — long-necked; atimukhaḥ — broad-faced; taru-khātāṅkita-ānanaḥ — with face marked by striking trees; pātayan sa gavāṃ garbhān — causing the cows’ embryos to fall; daityaḥ vṛṣabha-rūpa-dhṛk — the demon bearing a bull’s form

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pralamba-kaṇṭho ’timukhas taru-khātāṅkitānanaḥ | pātayan sa gavāṃ garbhān daityo vṛṣabha-rūpa-dhṛk

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.14.6

सूदयंस्तापसानुग्रो वनानटति यस्सदा

Terrifying and torturing all the tapasvis, sages in the forest, this Arishta, was roaming around everywhere.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sūdayan — tormenting; tāpasān — ascetics; ugraḥ — fierce; vanān aṭati — roams the forests; yaḥ sadā — he who always

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sūdayaṃs tāpasān ugro vanān aṭati yas sadā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.14.7

ततस्तमतिघोराक्षमवेक्ष्यातिभयातुराः।
गोपा गोपस्त्रियश्चैव कृष्ण कृष्णेति चुक्रुशुः

Having seen this most terrifying bull, everyone was terrified. All the gopaas and gopis cried for help - " Krishna Krishna".

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataḥ tam atighorākṣam avekṣya — then seeing him, with exceedingly terrible eyes; ati-bhayāturāḥ — extremely fear-stricken; gopāḥ — cowherd men; gopa-striyaḥ ca eva — and cowherd women indeed; kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa iti cukruśuḥ — cried out “Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatas tam atighorākṣam avekṣyātibhayāturāḥ | gopā gopa-striyaś caiva kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇeti cukruśuḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.14.8

सिंहनादं ततश्चक्रे तलशब्दं च केशवः।
तच्छब्दश्रवणाच्चासौ दामोदरमुपाययौ

Immediately, Krishna clapped His hands, and then roared like a lion. Having heard that sound, Arishta started to run towards Krishna.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

siṃha-nādam tataḥ cakre — then made a lion-roar; tala-śabdam ca keśavaḥ — and a hand-clap sound, Keśava; tat-śabda-śravaṇāt ca asau — and on hearing that sound he; dāmodaram upāyayau — approached Dāmodara

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

siṃhanādaṃ tatas cakre tala-śabdaṃ ca keśavaḥ | tac-chabda-śravaṇāc cāsau dāmodaram upāyayau

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.14.9

अग्रन्यस्तविषाणाग्रः कृष्णकुक्षिकृतेक्षणः।
अभ्यधावत दुष्टात्मा कृष्णं वृषभदानवः

This demon, who was in the form of a Vrushabha, a bull, pointing his sharp horns in the front, and fixing his eyes on Krishna's stomach, bending and pointing his horns towards Krishna, the wicked demon came running towards Krishna.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

agra-nyasta-viṣāṇāgraḥ — with horn-tips placed forward; kṛṣṇa-kukṣikṛtekṣaṇaḥ — with gaze fixed on Kṛṣṇa’s belly; abhyadhāvat — ran toward; duṣṭātmā — wicked-souled; kṛṣṇaṃ vṛṣabha-dānavaḥ — Kṛṣṇa, the bull-demon

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

agra-nyasta-viṣāṇāgraḥ kṛṣṇa-kukṣikṛtekṣaṇaḥ | abhyadhāvad duṣṭātmā kṛṣṇaṃ vṛṣabha-dānavaḥ

Śrīdhara

agreti . agre purato nyaste viṣāṇāgre yena kṛṣṇw kukṣau kam īkṣaṇaṃ yena ||९|| āyāntaṃ daitya-vṛṣabhandṛṣṭvā kṛṣṇo mahā-balaḥ.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अग्रेति । अग्रे पुरतो न्यस्ते विषाणाग्रे येन कृष्णस्य कुक्षौ कम् ईक्षणं येन ॥९॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

This demon, who was in the form of a Vrushabha, a bull, pointing his sharp horns in the front, and fixing his eyes on Krishna's stomach, bending and pointing his horns towards Krishna, the wicked demon came running towards Krishna.

VP 5.14.10

आयान्तं दैत्यवृषभं दृष्ट्वा कृष्णो महाबलः।
न चचाल तदा स्थानादवज्ञास्मितलीलया

On seeing this demon running towards Him, Krishna who was most powerful, did not even move from His place. He was smiling playfully and showing no respect or fear for the bull.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

āyāntam daitya-vṛṣabham dṛṣṭvā — seeing the approaching bull-demon; kṛṣṇaḥ mahā-balaḥ — mighty Kṛṣṇa; na cacāla — did not move; tadā sthānāt — from his place then; avajñā-smita-līlayā — with a playful smile of disregard

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

āyāntaṃ daitya-vṛṣabhaṃ dṛṣṭvā kṛṣṇo mahā-balaḥ | na cacāla tadā sthānād avajñā-smita-līlayā

Śrīdhara

āyāntam iti . avajñayā smitaṃ saiva līlā, tathā ||१०|| āsannaṃ caiva jagrāha grāhavan madhusūdanaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आयान्तम् इति । अवज्ञया स्मितं सैव लीला, तथा ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

On seeing this demon running towards Him, Krishna who was most powerful, did not even move from His place. He was smiling playfully and showing no respect or fear for the bull.

VP 5.14.11

आसन्नं चैव जग्राह ग्राहवन्मधुसूदनः।
जघान जानुना कुक्षौ विषाणग्रहणाचलम्

When he came nearby, He held the bull, as though a crocodile catches a small animal. Holding his horns, Krishna kicked him in his stomach, with His legs.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

āsannam ca eva jagrāha — when near, seized; grāhavat — like a crocodile; madhusūdanaḥ — Madhusūdana; jaghāna jānunā kukṣau — struck in the belly with the knee; viṣāṇa-grahaṇa-acalam — the one immovable by grasping the horns

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

āsannaṃ caiva jagrāha grāhavan madhusūdanaḥ | jaghāna jānunā kukṣau viṣāṇa-grahaṇācalam

Śrīdhara

āsannam itii . viṣāṇa-grahaṇād acalaṃ santaṃ jaghāna ||११|| tasya darpa-balambhaṅktvāgṛhītasya viṣāṇayoḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

आसन्नम् इति । विषाण-ग्रहणाद् अचलं सन्तं जघान ॥११॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

When he came nearby, He held the bull, as though a crocodile catches a small animal. Holding his horns, Krishna kicked him in his stomach, with His legs.

VP 5.14.12

तस्य दर्पबलं भङ्क्त्वा गृहीतस्य विषाणयोः।
अपीडयदरिष्टस्य कण्ठं क्लिन्नमिवाम्बरम्

Having held his horns, Krishna broke his pride, by hitting him powerfully, and humbled him immediately. He wrung the neck of Arishta, as though wringing a wet cloth and removing its water, in no time.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasya darpa-balaṃ bhaṅktvā gṛhītasya viṣāṇayoḥ | apīḍayad ariṣṭasya kaṇṭhaṃ klinnam ivāmbaram

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.14.13

उत्पाट्य शृङ्गमेकं तु तेनैवाताडयत्ततः।
ममार स महादैत्यो मुखाच्छोणितमुद्वमन्

He plucked one horn from that bull, and started hitting that bull with that horn. Immediately, that demon vomited blood from his face, and fell dead.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

utpāṭya śṛṅgam ekaṃ tu tenaivātāḍayat tataḥ | mamāra sa mahā-daityo mukhāc choṇitam udvaman

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.14.14

तुष्टुवुर्निहते तस्मिन्दैत्ये गोपा जनार्दनम्।
जंभे हते सहस्राक्षं पुरा देवगणा यथा

All the gopaas were extremely pleased, by this wonderful act of Krishna, and they started praising Krishna, just like, when Indra killed the demon Jambha, all the gods were so happy and they praised Indra. In the same way, all the gopaas were feeling so happy and joyous that they praised Krishna for this wonderful act of killing Arishta demon, who came in the form of a ferocious bull. This completes Chapter Fourteen. We just concluded Chapter 14 of Amsha 5. Where the killing of demon Arishta, who came in the form of a ferocious bull, was narrated by Sri Paraasharar.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tuṣṭuvuḥ nihate tasmin daitye — when the demon was slain, praised; gopāḥ janārdanam — the cowherds [praised] Janārdana; jambhe hate — when Jambha was slain; sahasrākṣam — the thousand-eyed one; purā deva-gaṇā yathā — as formerly the gods [did]

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tuṣṭuvur nihate tasmin daitye gopā janārdanam | jambhe hate sahasrākṣaṃ purā deva-gaṇā yathā

Śrīdhara

* na vyākhyātam.* || iti śrīdhara-svāmi-viracitātma-prakāśākhya-śrī-viṣṇu-mahāpurāṇa-vyākhyāyāṃ pañcamāṃśe dvādaśo’dhyāyaḥ || iti śrī-viṣṇu-purāṇe pañcame’ṃśecaturdaśo’dhyāyaḥ ||१४|| —o)०(o—

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

* न व्याख्यातम्.* ॥ इति श्रीधर-स्वामि-विरचितात्म-प्रकाशाख्य-श्री-विष्णु-महापुराण-व्याख्यायां पञ्चमांशे द्वादशोऽध्यायः ॥ इति श्री-विष्णु-पुराणे पञ्चमेऽंशेचतुर्दशोऽध्यायः ॥१४॥ —ओ)०(ओ—

Śrīdhara commentary.

All the gopaas were extremely pleased, by this wonderful act of Krishna, and they started praising Krishna, just like, when Indra killed the demon Jambha, all the gods were so happy and they praised Indra. In the same way, all the gopaas were feeling so happy and joyous that they praised Krishna for this wonderful act of killing Arishta demon, who came in the form of a ferocious bull. This completes Chapter Fourteen. We just concluded Chapter 14 of Amsha 5. Where the killing of demon Arishta, who came in the form of a ferocious bull, was narrated by Sri Paraasharar.
