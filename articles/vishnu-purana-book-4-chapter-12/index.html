davādaśe karoṣaṭara-sañajañasaya yadu-putarasaya sanatatiḥ vaṃśa-kaṛda avaraṇayate yatara jayāmaghaḥ satarī-jitāgaraṇīḥ . caturadaśa mahānati ratanāni yasaya saḥ . ratanāni cātara sava-sava-jāti-śareṣaṭhāni dharama-saṃhitokatāni— cakaraṃ ratho maṇiḥ khaḍagaśa carama ratanaṃ ca pañacamama . ketura nidhiśa ca sapataiva parāṇa-hīnāni cakaṣate || bhārayā purohitaśa caiva senānī rathakaṛca ca yaḥ . patayaśavau kalabhāśa ceti parāṇinaḥ sapata kīratitāḥ || caturadaśeti ratanāni saraveṣāṃ cakaravaratināma . jayāmaghasaya banadhayā-pateḥ aputarasaya bhārayā-vaśayatavama eva putara-paradama āsīda iti vakaṣayana kathāma āha—atarādayāpi iti yāvata-samāpati . **adhiṣaṭhānaṃ** nivāsama ||16||

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 16).

All the enemies and kings ran away in all directions, having lost their wives and children, their relatives, wealth, treasure, etc.
