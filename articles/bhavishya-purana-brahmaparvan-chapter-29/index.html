Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 29 — Gaṇeśa Worship and Its Mantras

BP 1.29.1

शतानीक उवाच
गकाराक्षरदेवस्य गणेशस्य महात्मनः ।
आराधनविधिं ब्रूहि साङ्गं मन्त्रसमन्वितम् ॥

Śatānīka said. Tell me the full procedure with its parts and formulas for worshipping the great Gaṇeśa, the god of the syllable ga.

शतानीक उवाच (śatānīka uvāca) — Śatānīka said;
गकाराक्षरदेवस्य (gakārākṣara-devasya) — of the god whose syllable is ga;
गणेशस्य महात्मनः (gaṇeśasya mahātmanaḥ) — of great Gaṇeśa;
आराधनविधिम् (ārādhana-vidhim) — method of worship;
ब्रूहि (brūhi) — tell;
साङ्गम् मन्त्रसमन्वितम् (sāṅgam mantra-samanvitam) — complete and accompanied by mantras

BP 1.29.2

सुमन्तुरुवाच
न तिथिर्न च नक्षत्रं नोपवासो विधीयते ।
यथेष्टं चेष्टतः सिद्धिः सदा भवति कामिका ॥

Sumantu said. No lunar day, constellation, or fast is prescribed. Accomplishment in accordance with one's desire always comes from acting as one wishes.

सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
न तिथिः न नक्षत्रम् (na tithiḥ na nakṣatram) — neither lunar date nor constellation;
न उपवासः विधीयते (na upavāsaḥ vidhīyate) — no fast is prescribed;
यथेष्टम् चेष्टतः (yatheṣṭam ceṣṭataḥ) — acting whenever desired;
सिद्धिः कामिका (siddhiḥ kāmikā) — desired accomplishment;
सदा भवति (sadā bhavati) — always occurs

BP 1.29.3

श्वेतार्कमूलं सङ्गृह्य कुर्याद्गणपतिं बुधः ।
अङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं तु पद्मासनगतं तथा ॥

A wise person should gather the root of the white crown flower and make Gaṇeśa from it, the size of a thumb joint and seated in the lotus posture.

श्वेतार्कमूलम् सङ्गृह्य (śvetārka-mūlam saṅgṛhya) — gathering white arka root;
कुर्यात् गणपतिम् (kuryāt gaṇapatim) — should fashion Gaṇapati;
बुधः (budhaḥ) — wise person;
अङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रम् (aṅguṣṭha-parva-mātram) — one thumb-joint in size;
पद्मासनगतम् (padmāsana-gatam) — seated in lotus posture

BP 1.29.4

चतुर्भुजं त्रिनेत्रं च सर्वाभरणभूषितम् ।
नागयज्ञोपवीताङ्गं शशाङ्ककृतशेखरम् ॥

He should have four arms and three eyes, be adorned with every ornament, wear a serpent as his sacred thread, and bear the moon upon his crest.

चतुर्भुजम् त्रिनेत्रम् (catur-bhujam tri-netram) — four-armed and three-eyed;
सर्वाभरणभूषितम् (sarvābharaṇa-bhūṣitam) — adorned with every ornament;
नागयज्ञोपवीताङ्गम् (nāga-yajñopavīta-aṅgam) — wearing serpent sacred thread;
शशाङ्ककृतशेखरम् (śaśāṅka-kṛta-śekharam) — moon made his crest

BP 1.29.5

दन्तं सव्ये करे दद्याद्द्वितीये चाक्षसूत्रकम् ।
तृतीये परशुं दद्याच्चतुर्थे मोदकं न्यसेत् ॥

Place a tusk in his left hand, a rosary in the second, an axe in the third, and a sweet cake in the fourth.

दन्तम् सव्ये करे दद्यात् (dantam savye kare dadyāt) — place tusk in left hand;
द्वितीये अक्षसूत्रकम् (dvitīye akṣa-sūtrakam) — rosary in second;
तृतीये परशुम् (tṛtīye paraśum) — axe in third;
चतुर्थे मोदकम् (caturthe modakam) — sweetmeat in fourth;
न्यसेत् (nyaset) — should place

BP 1.29.6

कुङ्कुमं चन्दनं चापि समालम्भनमुच्यते ।
वासोभिर्भूषणै रक्तैर्माल्यैश्चाराधयेद्गणम् ॥

Saffron and sandalwood are prescribed for anointing. Worship the Lord of Hosts with red garments, ornaments, and garlands.

कुङ्कुमम् चन्दनम् च (kuṅkumam candanam ca) — saffron and sandalwood;
समालम्भनम् (samālambhanam) — anointing;
वासोभिः भूषणैः रक्तैः (vāsobhiḥ bhūṣaṇaiḥ raktaiḥ) — with red clothes and ornaments;
माल्यैः (mālyaiḥ) — with garlands;
आराधयेत् गणम् (ārādhayet gaṇam) — should worship Gaṇa

BP 1.29.7

धूपेन च सुगन्धेन मोदकैश्चापि पूजयेत् ।
एवं पूज्याग्रतस्तस्य भोजयेद्ब्राह्मणं बुधः ॥

Worship him with fragrant incense and sweet cakes. Having worshipped him in this way, the wise person should feed a Brahmin before him.

धूपेन सुगन्धेन (dhūpena sugandhena) — with fragrant incense;
मोदकैः च (modakaiḥ ca) — and sweetmeats;
पूजयेत् (pūjayet) — should worship;
एवम् पूज्य (evam pūjya) — worshipping thus;
अग्रतः तस्य (agrataḥ tasya) — before him;
भोजयेत् ब्राह्मणम् (bhojayet brāhmaṇam) — should feed a brāhmaṇa

BP 1.29.8

वामनं कुब्जकं चापि भोजयेत्पुरतो द्विजम् ।
आशीर्वादं ततस्तस्मात्प्राप्य सिद्धिमवाप्नुयात् ॥

One should also feed a short or hunchbacked Brahmin before the deity. Then, receiving his blessing, one attains accomplishment.

वामनम् कुब्जकम् च (vāmanam kubjakam ca) — a short or hunchbacked person;
भोजयेत् पुरतः द्विजम् (bhojayet purataḥ dvijam) — should feed the brāhmaṇa before him;
आशीर्वादम् प्राप्य (āśīrvādam prāpya) — receiving blessing;
सिद्धिम् अवाप्नुयात् (siddhim avāpnuyāt) — should attain success

BP 1.29.9

भक्त्या कुरुकुलश्रेष्ठ शृणु मन्त्रपदानि वै ।
गं स्वाहा मूलमन्त्रोऽयं प्रणवेन समन्वितः ॥

Listen with devotion, best of the Kuru line, to the formula words. The seed sound gaṃ, with svāhā and Om, is the root formula.

भक्त्या शृणु (bhaktyā śṛṇu) — listen with devotion;
कुरुकुलश्रेष्ठ (kuru-kula-śreṣṭha) — best of Kuru line;
मन्त्रपदानि (mantra-padāni) — mantra syllables;
गं स्वाहा (gaṃ svāhā) — gaṃ svāhā;
मूलमन्त्रः अयम् (mūla-mantraḥ ayam) — this root mantra;
प्रणवेन समन्वितः (praṇavena samanvitaḥ) — joined with oṃ

BP 1.29.10

गां नमो हदयं ज्ञेयं गीं शिरः परिकीर्तितम् ।
शिखा च गूं नमो ज्ञेयो गैं नमः कवचं स्मृतम् ॥

The seed sound gāṃ with salutation is the heart, gīṃ is declared the head, gūṃ with salutation is the crown, and gaiṃ with salutation is the armor.

गां नमः हृदयम् (gāṃ namaḥ hṛdayam) — gāṃ namaḥ is the heart;
गीं शिरः (gīṃ śiraḥ) — gīṃ is the head;
गूं नमः शिखा (gūṃ namaḥ śikhā) — gūṃ namaḥ is the topknot;
गैं नमः कवचम् (gaiṃ namaḥ kavacam) — gaiṃ namaḥ is the armour

BP 1.29.11

गौं नमो नेत्रमुद्दिष्टं गः फट् कामास्त्रमुच्यते ।
आगच्छोल्कामुखायेति मन्त्र आवाहने ह्ययम् ॥

Gauṃ with salutation is assigned to the eyes, and gaḥ phaṭ is called the weapon of desire. Come, O Flame Faced One: this is the formula for invocation.

गौं नमः नेत्रम् (gauṃ namaḥ netram) — gauṃ namaḥ for the eyes;
गः फट् (gaḥ phaṭ) — gaḥ phaṭ;
कामास्त्रम् (kāmāstram) — desire-weapon;
आगच्छ उल्कामुखाय (āgaccha ulkāmukhāya) — come, O Ulkāmukha;
मन्त्रः आवाहने (mantraḥ āvāhane) — mantra for invocation

BP 1.29.12

गं गणेशाय नमो गन्धमन्त्रः प्रकीर्तितः ।
पुष्पोल्काय नमः पुष्पमन्त्र एष प्रकीर्तितः ॥

Gaṃ, homage to Gaṇeśa, is declared the fragrance formula. Homage to the Flower Flame is declared the flower formula.

गं गणेशाय नमः (gaṃ gaṇeśāya namaḥ) — homage to Gaṇeśa;
गन्धमन्त्रः (gandha-mantraḥ) — fragrance mantra;
पुष्पोल्काय नमः (puṣpolkāya namaḥ) — homage to Puṣpolka;
पुष्पमन्त्रः (puṣpa-mantraḥ) — flower mantra

BP 1.29.13

धूपोल्काय नमो धूपमन्त्र एष प्रकीर्तितः ।
दीपोल्काय नमो दीपमन्त्र एष प्रकीर्तितः ॥

Homage to the Incense Flame is the incense formula. Homage to the Lamp Flame is the lamp formula.

धूपोल्काय नमः (dhūpolkāya namaḥ) — homage to Dhūpolka;
धूपमन्त्रः (dhūpa-mantraḥ) — incense mantra;
दीपोल्काय नमः (dīpolkāya namaḥ) — homage to Dīpolka;
दीपमन्त्रः (dīpa-mantraḥ) — lamp mantra

BP 1.29.14

ॐ गं महोल्काय नमो बलिमन्त्रः प्रकीर्तितः ।
ओं संसिद्धोल्काय नमो मन्त्रश्चायं विसर्जने ॥

Om gaṃ, homage to the Great Flame, is declared the offering formula. Om, homage to the Perfected Flame, is the formula for dismissal.

ॐ गं महोल्काय नमः (oṃ gaṃ maholkāya namaḥ) — homage to Maholka;
बलिमन्त्रः (bali-mantraḥ) — offering mantra;
ओं संसिद्धोल्काय नमः (oṃ saṃsiddholkāya namaḥ) — homage to Saṃsiddholka;
विसर्जने मन्त्रः (visarjane mantraḥ) — dismissal mantra

BP 1.29.15

ओं महाकर्णाय विद्महे वक्रतुण्डाय धीमहि ।
तन्नो दन्तिः प्रचोदयात् गायत्री जपः पूर्वतः ॥

We know the Great Eared One. We meditate upon the Curved Trunked One. May the Tusked One inspire us. This Gāyatrī prayer is to be recited first.

महाकर्णाय विद्महे (mahākarṇāya vidmahe) — we know the Great-Eared One;
वक्रतुण्डाय धीमहि (vakratuṇḍāya dhīmahi) — we meditate on the Curved-Tusked One;
तन्नः दन्तिः प्रचोदयात् (tannaḥ dantiḥ pracodayāt) — may the Tusked One inspire us;
गायत्री जपः पूर्वतः (gāyatrī japaḥ pūrvataḥ) — Gāyatrī recitation first

BP 1.29.16

महागणपतये वीर स्वाहा दक्षिणतः सदा ।
महोल्काय पश्चिमतः कूश्माण्डायोत्तरेण तु ॥
एकदन्तत्रिपुरान्तकाय आग्नेय्यां वीर निर्दिशेत् ॥

Svāhā to the Great Lord of Hosts in the south. To the Great Flame in the west, to Kūṣmāṇḍa in the north, and to the One Tusked Destroyer of Tripura in the southeast, O hero.

महागणपतये स्वाहा (mahāgaṇapataye svāhā) — svāhā to Mahāgaṇapati;
दक्षिणतः (dakṣiṇataḥ) — in south;
महोल्काय पश्चिमतः (maholkāya paścimataḥ) — to Maholka in west;
कूश्माण्डाय उत्तरेण (kūṣmāṇḍāya uttareṇa) — to Kūṣmāṇḍa in north;
एकदन्तत्रिपुरान्तकाय आग्नेय्याम् (ekadanta-tripurāntakāya āgneyyām) — to Ekadanta Tripurāntaka in southeast

BP 1.29.17

ओं शिवदत्त विकटहरहास प्राणाय स्वा नैर्ऋत्याम् ।
तुलम्बनात्यचलदन्तकाय स्वाहा वायव्याम् ॥

In the southwest, Om svāhā to Śivadatta, the Formidable One, the Laughing One, the Life. In the northwest, svāhā to the Long Hanging, Unmoving Tusked One.

नैर्ऋत्याम् (nairṛtyām) — in southwest;
शिवदत्त विकट हरहास प्राणाय स्वाहा (śivadatta vikaṭa harahāsa prāṇāya svāhā) — stated mantra;
वायव्याम् (vāyavyām) — in northwest;
तुलम्बनात्यचलदन्तकाय स्वाहा (tulambanātyacala-dantakāya svāhā) — stated mantra

BP 1.29.18

पद्मदंष्ट्राय नरायेति ऐशान्यां होमयेद्बुधः ।
हुं फट् हुँ फट् हस्ततालध्वनिर्हसनकूर्दनः ॥

The wise should offer in the northeast to the Lotus Tusked One and to Nara. Huṃ phaṭ, huṃ phaṭ. The sound of clapping hands, laughing, and leaping is the gesture of the god.

पद्मदंष्ट्राय नराय (padmadaṃṣṭrāya narāya) — to Padmadaṃṣṭra and Nara;
ऐशान्याम् होमयेत् (aiśānyām homayet) — should offer in northeast;
हुं फट् (huṃ phaṭ) — huṃ phaṭ;
हस्ततालध्वनिः (hasta-tāla-dhvaniḥ) — sound of hand-clapping;
हसनकूर्दनः (hasana-kūrdanaḥ) — laughing and leaping

BP 1.29.19

मृदनर्तनगणपतिर्देवस्य मुद्रां ततो होमं समाचरेत् ।
न यदा वश्या भवति ॥
कृष्णतिलाहुतिमष्टसहस्रं जुहुयात्त्रिरात्रेण राजा वश्यो भवति ॥

Then show the gesture of the Dancing Gaṇapati and perform the fire offering. If the person does not come under control, pour eight thousand oblations of black sesame over three nights, and a king comes under control.

नर्तनगणपतिः देवस्य मुद्रा (nartana-gaṇapatiḥ devasya mudrā) — dancing Gaṇapati gesture;
ततः होमम् समाचरेत् (tataḥ homam samācaret) — then perform fire offering;
न यदा वश्या भवति (na yadā vaśyā bhavati) — when not brought under influence;
कृष्णतिलाहुतिम् अष्टसहस्रम् (kṛṣṇa-tila-āhutim aṣṭa-sahasram) — eight thousand black-sesame oblations;
त्रिरात्रेण (tri-rātreṇa) — over three nights;
राजा वश्यः भवति (rājā vaśyaḥ bhavati) — king becomes compliant

BP 1.29.20

तिलयवहोमेन सर्वे जनपदा वश्या भवन्ति ।
अति रूपवती कन्या गच्छन्तमनु(?)च्छति ॥

By offering sesame and barley, all the inhabitants of a region come under control. An exceedingly beautiful maiden follows the one who departs.

तिलयवहोमेन (tila-yava-homena) — by sesame and barley offering;
सर्वे जनपदाः (sarve janapadāḥ) — all inhabitants;
वश्याः भवन्ति (vaśyāḥ bhavanti) — become compliant;
अतिरूपवती कन्या (ati-rūpavatī kanyā) — surpassingly beautiful maiden;
गच्छन्तम् अनुगच्छति (gacchantam anugacchati) — follows one departing

BP 1.29.21

चणतन्दुलहोमेनाजितो भवेत् ।
निम्बपत्रसमैस्तैलैर्विद्वेषणं करोति ॥
सोमग्रहणे उदकमध्ये अवतीर्य अष्टसहस्रं जपेत् ।
सङ्ग्रामे अपराजितो भवति ॥

By an offering of chickpeas and rice, one becomes unconquered. Oils mixed with neem leaves produce enmity. During a lunar eclipse, enter the water and recite eight thousand times. One becomes undefeated in battle.

चणतन्दुलहोमेन (caṇa-taṇḍula-homena) — by chickpea and rice offering;
अजितः भवेत् (ajitaḥ bhavet) — becomes unconquered;
निम्बपत्रसमैः तैलैः (nimba-patra-samaiḥ tailaiḥ) — with neem leaves and oils;
विद्वेषणम् करोति (vidveṣaṇam karoti) — produces enmity;
सोमग्रहणे (soma-grahaṇe) — during lunar eclipse;
उदकमध्ये अवतीर्य (udaka-madhye avatīrya) — entering water;
अष्टसहस्रम् जपेत् (aṣṭa-sahasram japet) — recite eight thousand times;
सङ्ग्रामे अपराजितः (saṅgrāme aparājitaḥ) — undefeated in battle

BP 1.29.22


आदित्याभिमुखो भूत्वा अष्टसहस्रं जपेत् ।
आदित्यो वरदो भवति ॥

Facing the Sun, recite eight thousand times. The Sun becomes a giver of boons.

ॐ लम्बराज्ञे नमः (oṃ lambarājñe namaḥ) — homage to Lambarājña;
आदित्याभिमुखः भूत्वा (ādityābhimukhaḥ bhūtvā) — facing the sun;
अष्टसहस्रम् जपेत् (aṣṭa-sahasram japet) — recite eight thousand times;
आदित्यः वरदः भवति (ādityaḥ varadaḥ bhavati) — Sun grants a boon

BP 1.29.23

शुक्लचतुर्थ्यामुपोष्य गन्धपुष्पादिभिरर्चनं कृत्वा तिलतन्दुलाञ्जुहुयात् ।
शिरसा धारयंस्तैरपराजितो भवति ॥

After fasting on the bright fourth day and worshipping with fragrance, flowers, and the rest, offer sesame and rice. Carrying them upon the head, one becomes undefeated.

शुक्लचतुर्थ्याम् उपोष्य (śukla-caturthyām upoṣya) — fasting on bright fourth;
गन्धपुष्पादिभिः अर्चनम् कृत्वा (gandha-puṣpādibhiḥ arcanam kṛtvā) — worshipping with fragrance and flowers;
तिलतन्दुलान् जुहुयात् (tila-taṇḍulān juhuyāt) — should offer sesame and rice;
शिरसा धारयन् (śirasā dhārayan) — bearing on head;
अपराजितः भवति (aparājitaḥ bhavati) — becomes undefeated

BP 1.29.24

अपामार्गसमिद्भिरग्निं प्रज्वाल्य एकविंशत्याहुतीर्यो जुहुयात् ।
त्रिरात्राच्छत्रुं व्यापादयति ॥

Having kindled a fire with prickly chaff flower sticks, whoever offers twenty one oblations destroys an enemy within three nights.

अपामार्गसमिद्भिः (apāmārga-samidbhiḥ) — with apāmārga sticks;
अग्निम् प्रज्वाल्य (agnim prajvālya) — kindling fire;
एकविंशत्याहुतीः (ekaviṃśaty-āhutīḥ) — twenty-one oblations;
यः जुहुयात् (yaḥ juhuyāt) — whoever offers;
त्रिरात्रात् (tri-rātrāt) — within three nights;
शत्रुम् व्यापादयति (śatrum vyāpādayati) — destroys enemy

BP 1.29.25

अथोत्तरेण मन्त्रं व्याख्यास्ये ।
वृक्षमूले कज्जलं सङ्गृह्य सप्तभिर्मन्त्रितं कृत्वा नेत्राण्यञ्जयेद्यं पश्यति स वशी भवति ॥

Now I shall explain the next formula. Gather lampblack at the root of a tree, consecrate it with seven recitations, and apply it to the eyes. Whoever one sees comes under one's control.

अथ उत्तरेण मन्त्रम् व्याख्यास्ये (atha uttareṇa mantram vyākhyāsye) — now I explain subsequent mantra;
वृक्षमूले कज्जलम् सङ्गृह्य (vṛkṣa-mūle kajjalam saṅgṛhya) — collecting lampblack at tree root;
सप्तभिः मन्त्रितम् कृत्वा (saptabhiḥ mantritam kṛtvā) — consecrating with seven recitations;
नेत्राणि अञ्जयेत् (netrāṇi añjayet) — should anoint eyes;
यम् पश्यति सः वशी भवति (yam paśyati saḥ vaśī bhavati) — whom one sees becomes compliant

BP 1.29.26

पुष्पं फलं मूलं चाष्टसहस्राभिमन्त्रितं कृत्वा यस्मै ददाति स वश्यो भवति ॥

Having consecrated a flower, fruit, or root with eight thousand recitations, give it to someone. That person comes under one's control.

पुष्पम् फलम् मूलम् च (puṣpam phalam mūlam ca) — flower fruit and root;
अष्टसहस्राभिमन्त्रितम् कृत्वा (aṣṭa-sahasrābhimantritam kṛtvā) — consecrating with eight thousand recitations;
यस्मै ददाति (yasmai dadāti) — to whomever one gives;
सः वश्यः भवति (saḥ vaśyaḥ bhavati) — he becomes compliant

BP 1.29.27

यत्किञ्चिन्मूलमन्त्रेण करोति तत्सिध्यति ।
सर्वे ग्रहाः सुप्रीता भवन्ति ॥
नगरद्वारं गत्वा अष्टसहस्रं द्वारं निरूपयेत् ॥

Whatever one does with the root formula succeeds. All the planets become pleased. Having gone to the city gate, one should direct eight thousand recitations toward the gate.

यत्किञ्चित् मूलमन्त्रेण करोति (yatkiñcit mūla-mantreṇa karoti) — whatever one does with root mantra;
तत् सिध्यति (tat sidhyati) — that succeeds;
सर्वे ग्रहाः सुप्रीताः (sarve grahāḥ suprītāḥ) — all planets are gracious;
नगरद्वारम् गत्वा (nagara-dvāram gatvā) — going to city gate;
अष्टसहस्रम् निरूपयेत् (aṣṭa-sahasram nirūpayet) — should direct eight thousand recitations

BP 1.29.28

पुरं द्वारेण गृह्यते प्राङ्मुखो यजति स उच्चाटयति ।
सम्मुखो जपति चोरान्विद्रावयति ॥

The city is taken through its gate. Worshipping while facing east drives a person away. Reciting face to face drives thieves away.

पुरम् द्वारेण गृह्यते (puram dvāreṇa gṛhyate) — city is taken through gate;
प्राङ्मुखः यजति (prāṅmukhaḥ yajati) — worships facing east;
सः उच्चाटयति (saḥ uccāṭayati) — he drives away;
सम्मुखः जपति (sammukhaḥ japati) — recites face-to-face;
चोरान् विद्रावयति (corān vidrāvayati) — disperses thieves

BP 1.29.29

तृणानि लूनयति । काष्ठानि च्छेदयति ॥

He plucks grass. He cuts wood.

तृणानि लूनयति (tṛṇāni lūnayati) — cuts grasses;
काष्ठानि छेदयति (kāṣṭhāni chedayati) — cleaves wood

BP 1.29.30

गजराजेन युद्धयति । जलमध्ये सप्तरात्रं जपेत् । अकाले वर्षयति । कूपतडागाञ्छोषयति ॥
प्रतिमां नृत्ययति । आकर्षयति । स्तम्भयति । योजनशतात्स्त्रीपुरुषानाकर्षयति ॥

He makes the king of elephants fight. Reciting for seven nights in water, he makes rain fall out of season and dries up wells and ponds. He makes an image dance, draws things toward himself, and makes them stand still. He draws women and men from a hundred leagues away.

गजराजेन युद्धयति (gaja-rājena yuddhayati) — makes elephant-king fight;
जलमध्ये सप्तरात्रम् जपेत् (jala-madhye sapta-rātram japet) — recite seven nights in water;
अकाले वर्षयति (akāle varṣayati) — causes untimely rain;
कूपतडागान् शोषयति (kūpa-taḍāgān śoṣayati) — dries wells and ponds;
प्रतिमाम् नृत्ययति (pratimām nṛtyayati) — makes image dance;
आकर्षयति स्तम्भयति (ākarṣayati stambhayati) — attracts and immobilizes;
योजनशतात् स्त्रीपुरुषान् आकर्षयति (yojana-śatāt strī-puruṣān ākarṣayati) — attracts people from one hundred yojanas

BP 1.29.31

गोरोचनां च सहस्राभिमन्त्रितां कृत्वा हस्ते बद्ध्वा योजनशतसहस्रं गत्वा पुनरागच्छति ॥

Having consecrated cow's yellow pigment with a thousand recitations and tied it to the hand, one travels a hundred thousand leagues and returns.

गोरोचनाम् सहस्राभिमन्त्रिताम् कृत्वा (gorocanām sahasrābhimantritām kṛtvā) — consecrating gorocanā with one thousand recitations;
हस्ते बद्ध्वा (haste baddhvā) — binding on hand;
योजनशतसहस्रम् गत्वा (yojana-śata-sahasram gatvā) — travelling one hundred thousand yojanas;
पुनः आगच्छति (punaḥ āgacchati) — returns again

BP 1.29.32

अथ मारयितुकामः खदिरकीलकं कृत्वा स्त्रीपुरुषं विचिन्त्य हदये निखनयेत् । क्षणादेव म्रियते ॥

If one desires to kill, make a peg of catechu wood, think upon the woman or man, and drive it into the heart. The person dies at once.

अथ मारयितुकामः (atha mārayitu-kāmaḥ) — desiring to kill;
खदिरकीलकम् कृत्वा (khadira-kīlakam kṛtvā) — fashioning khadira peg;
स्त्रीपुरुषम् विचिन्त्य (strī-puruṣam vicintya) — visualizing woman or man;
हृदये निखनयेत् (hṛdaye nikhanayet) — should bury at heart;
क्षणात् एव म्रियते (kṣaṇāt eva mriyate) — dies at once

BP 1.29.33

सर्वपातकविमुक्तो भवति । अग्नितेजाः सर्वेभ्योऽपराजितो भवति ॥

One becomes free of every great sin, radiant with the power of fire, and undefeated by all.

सर्वपातकविमुक्तः (sarva-pātaka-vimuktaḥ) — freed from every sin;
भवति (bhavati) — becomes;
अग्नितेजाः (agni-tejāḥ) — radiant like fire;
सर्वेभ्यः अपराजितः (sarvebhyaḥ aparājitaḥ) — undefeated by all

BP 1.29.34

ॐ वकतुण्डाय स्वाहा । ॐ एकदंष्ट्राय स्वाहा । ॐ कृतकृष्णाय स्वाहा । ॐ गजकर्णाय स्वाहा ॥
ॐ लम्बोदराय स्वाहा । ॐ विकटाय स्वाहा । ॐ धूम्रवर्णाय स्वाहा । ॐ गगनकूजाय स्वाहा ॥
ॐ विनायकाय स्वाहा । ॐ गणपतये स्वाहा । ॐ हस्तिमुखाय स्वाहा ॥

Om, offering to the Curved Trunked One. Om, offering to the One Tusked One. Om, offering to Kṛtakṛṣṇa. Om, offering to the Elephant Eared One. Om, offering to the Big Bellied One. Om, offering to the Formidable One. Om, offering to the Smoke Colored One. Om, offering to the Sky Sounding One. Om, offering to Vināyaka. Om, offering to the Lord of Hosts. Om, offering to the Elephant Faced One.

वक्रतुण्डाय स्वाहा (vakratuṇḍāya svāhā) — svāhā to Vakratuṇḍa;
एकदंष्ट्राय स्वाहा (ekadaṃṣṭrāya svāhā) — to Ekadaṃṣṭra;
कृतकृष्णाय स्वाहा (kṛtakṛṣṇāya svāhā) — to Kṛtakṛṣṇa;
गजकर्णाय स्वाहा (gajakarṇāya svāhā) — to Gajakarṇa;
लम्बोदराय स्वाहा (lambodarāya svāhā) — to Lambodara;
विकटाय स्वाहा (vikaṭāya svāhā) — to Vikaṭa;
धूम्रवर्णाय स्वाहा (dhūmravarṇāya svāhā) — to Dhūmravarṇa;
गगनकूजाय स्वाहा (gaganakūjāya svāhā) — to Gaganakūja;
विनायकाय स्वाहा (vināyakāya svāhā) — to Vināyaka;
गणपतये स्वाहा (gaṇapataye svāhā) — to Gaṇapati;
हस्तिमुखाय स्वाहा (hastimukhāya svāhā) — to Hastimukha
