BP 1.106.1

punaścaitanmahābhāga śrūyatāṃ gadato mama |
proktaṃ khagena devānāṃ tithimāhātmyamuttamam || 1 ||

Brahmā said: Again, O greatly fortunate one, listen to me as I speak of the excellent greatness of the lunar day that was taught by the Bird-bannered Lord to the gods.

BP 1.106.2

Viṣṇu said: Vijayā, Atijayā, and Jayantī are great lunar days, as I have heard from you, O best of the Suras. Tell me of the sin-destroying one.

BP 1.106.3

BP 1.106.5

tasyāṃ sampūjya deveśaṃ citrabhānuṃ jagadgurum |
saptajanmakṛtātpāpānmucyate nātra saṃśayaḥ || 5 ||

Having fully worshipped the Lord of the gods, Citrabhānu, the teacher of the world, on that day, one is freed from sins committed over seven births—of this there is no doubt.
