VP 2.10.1

श्रीपराशर उवाच त्र्यशीतिमण्डलशतं काष्ठयोरन्तरं द्वयोः । आरोहणावरोहाभ्यां भानोरब्देन या गतिः

Sri Paraasharar - Between the two ends - southern and northern, there are 183 mandalaas. He goes up and down. In one year, every day, he moves one mandala at a time; up 183 and down 183.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrīparāśara uvāca tryaśītimaṇḍalaśataṃ kāṣṭhayorantaraṃ dvayoḥ — Sri Paraasharar - Between the two ends - southern and northern, there are 183 mandalaas. He goes up and down. In one year, every day, he moves one mandala at a time; ārohaṇāvarohābhyāṃ bhānorabdena yā gatiḥ — up 183 and down 183.

Śrīdhara

atha pratimāsaṃ sūryarathaparibhramaṇādhikārigaṇān vakṣyannuttarāyaṇadakṣiṇāyanayor anulomapratilomagantavyāni krāntivṛttānyāha—sāśīti iti | uttaradakṣiṇakāṣṭhayor madhye aśītyuttaraṃ maṇḍalaśataṃ bhānorgatirgantavyaṃ vartma ārohaṇāvarohābhyām iti yugākṣakaṭibandhau vāyupāśau dhruvo yadākarṣati tadottarāyaṇe pratyahamabhyantaraikaikamaṇḍalapraveśe rathasyārohaṇaṃ bhavati, yadā tu tau pāśau dhruvaḥ prasārayati dakṣiṇāyane teṣveva maṇḍaleṣu pratilomaṃ bahirekaikamaṇḍalaṃ praviśato rathasyāvarohaṇaṃ bhavati | taduktaṃ mātsye— ākṛṣyate yadā tau tu dhruveṇa samadhiṣṭhitau | tadā so'bhyantaraṃ sūryo bhramate maṇḍalāni tu | dhruveṇa mucyamāne tu punā raśmiyugena tu | tathaiva vākṣataḥ sūryo maṇḍalāni ca iti — On that chariot are located devaas, aadityaas, rishis, gandharvaas, apsaraas, yakshaas, sarpaas and raakshasaas. They are doing various activities there. During each month, the maasaadhishthaana devataas are there. Sri Engal Aalwaan's Commentary: Sa ratha ityaadi | Graamanyo yakshaah |

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

atha pratimāsaṃ sūryarathaparibhramaṇādhikārigaṇān vakṣyannuttarāyaṇadakṣiṇāyanayor anulomapratilomagantavyāni krāntivṛttānyāha—sāśīti iti | uttaradakṣiṇakāṣṭhayor madhye aśītyuttaraṃ maṇḍalaśataṃ bhānorgatirgantavyaṃ vartma ārohaṇāvarohābhyām iti yugākṣakaṭibandhau vāyupāśau dhruvo yadākarṣati tadottarāyaṇe pratyahamabhyantaraikaikamaṇḍalapraveśe rathasyārohaṇaṃ bhavati, yadā tu tau pāśau dhruvaḥ prasārayati dakṣiṇāyane teṣveva maṇḍaleṣu pratilomaṃ bahirekaikamaṇḍalaṃ praviśato rathasyāvarohaṇaṃ bhavati | taduktaṃ mātsye— ākṛṣyate yadā tau tu dhruveṇa samadhiṣṭhitau | tadā so'bhyantaraṃ sūryo bhramate maṇḍalāni tu | dhruveṇa mucyamāne tu punā raśmiyugena tu | tathaiva vākṣataḥ sūryo maṇḍalāni ca iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अथ प्रतिमासं सूर्यरथपरिभ्रमणाधिकारिगणान् वक्ष्यन्नुत्तरायणदक्षिणायनयोर् अनुलोमप्रतिलोमगन्तव्यानि क्रान्तिवृत्तान्याह—साशीति इति । उत्तरदक्षिणकाष्ठयोर् मध्ये अशीत्युत्तरं मण्डलशतं भानोर्गतिर्गन्तव्यं वर्त्म आरोहणावरोहाभ्याम् इति युगाक्षकटिबन्धौ वायुपाशौ ध्रुवो यदाकर्षति तदोत्तरायणे प्रत्यहमभ्यन्तरैकैकमण्डलप्रवेशे रथस्यारोहणं भवति, यदा तु तौ पाशौ ध्रुवः प्रसारयति दक्षिणायने तेष्वेव मण्डलेषु प्रतिलोमं बहिरेकैकमण्डलं प्रविशतो रथस्यावरोहणं भवति । तदुक्तं मात्स्ये— आकृष्यते यदा तौ तु ध्रुवेण समधिष्ठितौ । तदा सोऽभ्यन्तरं सूर्यो भ्रमते मण्डलानि तु । ध्रुवेण मुच्यमाने तु पुना रश्मियुगेन तु । तथैव वाक्षतः सूर्यो मण्डलानि च इति

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges १).

On that chariot are located devaas, aadityaas, rishis, gandharvaas, apsaraas, yakshaas, sarpaas and raakshasaas. They are doing various activities there. During each month, the maasaadhishthaana devataas are there. Sri Engal Aalwaan's Commentary: Sa ratha ityaadi | Graamanyo yakshaah |

VP 2.10.3

धाता क्रतुस्थला चैव पुलस्त्यो बासुकिस्तथा । रथभृद्ग्रामणीर्हेतिस्तुम्बुरुश्चैव सप्तमः

This starts with Chaitra maasa, also called Madhu maasa. In this month, Dhaataa is Aadityaa, Ratusthalaa is the Apsaraa, Pulastya is the Rishi, Vaasuki is the Sarpa, Rathabhrut is the Yaksha, Heti is the Raakshasa gana, and Tumburu is the Gandharva.

वालखित्यास्तथैवैनं परिवार्य समासते

Vaalakhilya Rishis are surrounding, and go along with him.

VP 2.10.23

सोयं सप्तगणः सूर्यमण्डले मुनिसत्तम । हिमोष्णवारिवृष्टीनां हेतुः स्वसमयं गतः

O Maitreyar, all these categories in the orb of the Sun, they are the cause of the snow, warmth during summer, rains, at the right time.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

soyaṃ saptagaṇaḥ sūryamaṇḍale munisattama — O Maitreyar, all these categories in the orb of the Sun, they are the cause of the snow, warmth during summer, rains, at the right time.; himoṣṇavārivṛṣṭīnāṃ hetuḥ svasamayaṃ gataḥ — O Maitreyar, all these categories in the orb of the Sun, they are the cause of the snow, warmth during summer, rains, at the right time.

Śrīdhara

saptānāṃ sādhāraṇaṃ karm āha—so'yam iti | svayam āgata iti pāṭhe svasvakāle hemantagrīṣmādāvāgataḥ san himādivṛṣṭīnāṃ heturityarthaḥ — Vishnu Shakti in the Sun Now, Chapter 11. Here, the various acts of the Sun are going to be told here by Sri Paraasharar.
