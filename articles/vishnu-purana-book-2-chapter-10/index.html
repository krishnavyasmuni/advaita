VP 2.10.1

श्रीपराशर उवाच त्र्यशीतिमण्डलशतं काष्ठयोरन्तरं द्वयोः । आरोहणावरोहाभ्यां भानोरब्देन या गतिः

Sri Paraasharar - Between the two ends - southern and northern, there are 183 mandalaas. He goes up and down. In one year, every day, he moves one mandala at a time; up 183 and down 183.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrīparāśara uvāca tryaśītimaṇḍalaśataṃ kāṣṭhayorantaraṃ dvayoḥ — Sri Paraasharar - Between the two ends - southern and northern, there are 183 mandalaas. He goes up and down. In one year, every day, he moves one mandala at a time; ārohaṇāvarohābhyāṃ bhānorabdena yā gatiḥ — up 183 and down 183.

Śrīdhara

atha pratimāsaṃ sūrya-ratha-paribhramaṇa-adhikāri-gaṇān vakṣyan — now, intending to describe the groups that govern the Sun’s chariot each month; uttarāyaṇa-dakṣiṇāyanayoḥ — of the northward and southward courses; krānti-vṛttāni āha — he describes the paths of declination; aśīti-uttaraṃ maṇḍala-śatam — one hundred and eighty circles; bhānoḥ gatiḥ — are the Sun’s course; ārohaṇa-avarohābhyām — through ascent and descent

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrīparāśara uvāca tryaśītimaṇḍalaśataṃ kāṣṭhayorantaraṃ dvayoḥ | ārohaṇāvarohābhyāṃ bhānorabdena yā gatiḥ ||1||

atha pratimāsaṃ sūryarathaparibhramaṇādhikārigaṇān vakṣyannuttarāyaṇadakṣiṇāyanayor anulomapratilomagantavyāni krāntivṛttānyāha—sāśīti iti | uttaradakṣiṇakāṣṭhayor madhye aśītyuttaraṃ maṇḍalaśataṃ bhānorgatirgantavyaṃ vartma ārohaṇāvarohābhyām iti yugākṣakaṭibandhau vāyupāśau dhruvo yadākarṣati tadottarāyaṇe pratyahamabhyantaraikaikamaṇḍalapraveśe rathasyārohaṇaṃ bhavati, yadā tu tau pāśau dhruvaḥ prasārayati dakṣiṇāyane teṣveva maṇḍaleṣu pratilomaṃ bahirekaikamaṇḍalaṃ praviśato rathasyāvarohaṇaṃ bhavati | taduktaṃ mātsye— ākṛṣyate yadā tau tu dhruveṇa samadhiṣṭhitau | tadā so'bhyantaraṃ sūryo bhramate maṇḍalāni tu | dhruveṇa mucyamāne tu punā raśmiyugena tu | tathaiva vākṣataḥ sūryo maṇḍalāni ca iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अथ प्रतिमासं सूर्यरथपरिभ्रमणाधिकारिगणान् वक्ष्यन्नुत्तरायणदक्षिणायनयोर् अनुलोमप्रतिलोमगन्तव्यानि क्रान्तिवृत्तान्याह—साशीति इति । उत्तरदक्षिणकाष्ठयोर् मध्ये अशीत्युत्तरं मण्डलशतं भानोर्गतिर्गन्तव्यं वर्त्म आरोहणावरोहाभ्याम् इति युगाक्षकटिबन्धौ वायुपाशौ ध्रुवो यदाकर्षति तदोत्तरायणे प्रत्यहमभ्यन्तरैकैकमण्डलप्रवेशे रथस्यारोहणं भवति, यदा तु तौ पाशौ ध्रुवः प्रसारयति दक्षिणायने तेष्वेव मण्डलेषु प्रतिलोमं बहिरेकैकमण्डलं प्रविशतो रथस्यावरोहणं भवति । तदुक्तं मात्स्ये— आकृष्यते यदा तौ तु ध्रुवेण समधिष्ठितौ । तदा सोऽभ्यन्तरं सूर्यो भ्रमते मण्डलानि तु । ध्रुवेण मुच्यमाने तु पुना रश्मियुगेन तु । तथैव वाक्षतः सूर्यो मण्डलानि च इति

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges १).

The commentary explains the Sun’s monthly movement through the northern and southern courses. Dhruva draws the reins inward during the northward course and releases them outward during the southward course, producing the chariot’s ascent and descent.

VP 2.10.2

स रथोधिष्ठितो देवैरादित्यै ऋषिभिस्तथा । गन्धर्वैरप्सरोभिश्च ग्रामणीसर्पराक्षसैः

His car is presided over by divine Ádityas, Rishis, heavenly singers and nymphs, Yakshas, serpents, and Rákshasas (one of each being placed in it in every month).

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sa rathodhiṣṭhito devairādityai ṛṣibhistathā — His car is presided over by divine Ádityas; gandharvairapsarobhiśca grāmaṇīsarparākṣasaiḥ — Rishis, heavenly singers and nymphs

Śrīdhara

devairādityair ādityā eva devās taiḥ, grāmaṇīr yakṣaḥ — The deities are the Ādityas themselves, and the grāmaṇī is a Yakṣa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sa rathodhiṣṭhito devairādityai ṛṣibhistathā | gandharvairapsarobhiśca grāmaṇīsarparākṣasaiḥ ||2||

devairādityair ādityā eva devās taiḥ, grāmaṇīr yakṣaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

देवैरादित्यैरादित्या एव देवास्तैः, ग्रामणीर्यक्षः

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges २).

The deities are the Ādityas themselves, and the grāmaṇī is a Yakṣa.

VP 2.10.3

धाता क्रतुस्थला चैव पुलस्त्यो बासुकिस्तथा । रथभृद्ग्रामणीर्हेतिस्तुम्बुरुश्चैव सप्तमः

This starts with Chaitra maasa, also called Madhu maasa. In this month, Dhaataa is Aadityaa, Ratusthalaa is the Apsaraa, Pulastya is the Rishi, Vaasuki is the Sarpa, Rathabhrut is the Yaksha, Heti is the Raakshasa gana, and Tumburu is the Gandharva.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dhātā kratusthalā caiva pulastyo bāsukistathā — This starts with Chaitra maasa, also called Madhu maasa. In this month; rathabhṛdgrāmaṇīrhetistumburuścaiva saptamaḥ — Dhaataa is Aadityaa

Śrīdhara

ādityādīn pratimāsam sapta sapta anukrāmati — each month he follows seven each, beginning with the Ādityas; dhātā sūryaḥ — Dhātā is the Sun; ratha-kṛt saṃjñaḥ grāmaṇīḥ yakṣaḥ — Rathakṛt by name is the Yakṣa leader; hetī rākṣasaḥ — Hetī is the Rākṣasa

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dhātā kratusthalā caiva pulastyo bāsukistathā | rathabhṛdgrāmaṇīrhetistumburuścaiva saptamaḥ ||3||

vānevādityādīn pratimāsaṃ sapta saptānukrāmati dhāta ityādinā viṣṇuśaktyupavṛṃhitā ityantena | dhātā sūryaḥ rathakṛtsañjño grāmaṇīḥ yakṣaḥ | hetī rākṣasaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वानेवादित्यादीन् प्रतिमासं सप्त सप्तानुक्रामति धात इत्यादिना विष्णुशक्त्युपवृंहिता इत्यन्तेन । धाता सूर्यः रथकृत्सञ्ज्ञो ग्रामणीः यक्षः । हेती राक्षसः

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges ३).

Dhaataa iti | Rathabhrut graamaneeh rathabhrut naama yakshah | Hetih raakshasah | Rathakrut iti cha paathah |

VP 2.10.5

अर्यमा पुलहश्चैव रथौजाः पुञ्चिकस्थला । प्रहेतिः कच्छवीरश्व नारदश्च रथे रवेः

Āryaman, Pulaha, Rathaujā, Puñjikasthalā, Praheti, Kacchavīra, and Nārada are in the Sun’s chariot.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aryamā pulahaś caiva rathaujāḥ puñcikasthalā — Āryaman, Pulaha, Rathaujā, and Puñjikasthalā; prahetiḥ kacchavīraś ca nāradaś ca rathe raveḥ — Praheti, Kacchavīra, and Nārada in the Sun’s chariot

Śrīdhara

Transliteration

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

रथौजा यक्षः, प्रहेतिः राक्षसः । कच्छनीरः सर्पः, माधवे वैशाखे । शुचिसञ्ज्ञ इति ज्यष्ठो विवक्षितः

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges ५–६).

Rathaujā is a Yakṣa, Praheti a Rākṣasa, and Kacchanīra a serpent; Mādhava means Vaiśākha, and by Śuci Jyeṣṭha is intended.

VP 2.10.7

मित्रोत्रिस्तक्षको रक्षः पौरुषेयोथ मेनका । हाहा रथस्वनश्चैव मैत्रेयैते वसन्ति वै

VP 2.10.8

वरुणो वसिष्ठो रम्भा च सहजन्या हुहूरथः । रथचित्रस्तथा शुक्रे वसन्त्याषाढसंज्ञके

In the next month, Shukra, which is Aashaadha, Varuna is the Aaditya, Vasishta is the Rishi, Huhoo is the Gandharva, Sahajanyaa is the Apsaras, Rathachitra is the Yaksha, Naaga is the Sarpa, Ratha is the raakshasa gana.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

varuṇaḥ sūryaḥ rambhā ca sahajanyā iti pāṭhe — in the reading “Varuṇa, Sūrya, Rambhā, and Sahajanyā”; phālgune vakṣyamāṇa-rambhā-vyāvṛtty-arthaṃ — for distinguishing her in Phālguna from the Rambhā mentioned later; saṃjñāntareṇa tasya viśeṣaṇam — this is her designation by another name; ratha-citraḥ iti yakṣa-sarpayor ekam nāma — Rathacitra is one name for the Yakṣa and the Serpent; nāgaḥ ca sahajanyā iti pāṭhe — in the reading “Nāga and Sahajanyā”; nāga-saṃjñakaḥ sarpaḥ — a serpent called Nāga; āṣāḍha-saṃjñakaḥ — called Āṣāḍha; śukraḥ pūrvokta-śuci-saṃjñaḥ jyaiṣṭhaḥ — Śukra, previously called Śuci, is Jyaiṣṭha

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वरुणः सूर्यः रम्भा च सहजन्येति पाठे फाल्गुने वक्ष्यमाण-रम्भाव्यावृत्त्यर्थं सञ्ज्ञान्तरेण तस्य विशेषणम् । रथचित्र इति यक्षसर्पयोर् एकं नाम । नागश्च सहजन्येति पाठे नागसञ्ज्ञकः सर्पः । आषाढसञ्ज्ञक इति चाख्यानादाषाढ एव शुक्रः पूर्वोक्तशुचिसञ्ज्ञो ज्यैष्ठ इति ज्ञेयम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges ८).

VP 2.10.9

VP 2.10.10

soyaṃ saptagaṇaḥ sūryamaṇḍale munisattama — O Maitreyar, all these categories in the orb of the Sun, they are the cause of the snow, warmth during summer, rains, at the right time.; himoṣṇavārivṛṣṭīnāṃ hetuḥ svasamayaṃ gataḥ — O Maitreyar, all these categories in the orb of the Sun, they are the cause of the snow, warmth during summer, rains, at the right time.

Śrīdhara

saptānāṃ sādhāraṇaṃ karma āha — he states the common action of the seven; saḥ ayam — this one; svayam āgataḥ iti pāṭhe — in the reading “having come himself”; sva-sva-kāle hemanta-grīṣma-ādau āgataḥ san — having arrived in each one's season, winter, summer, and so on; himādi-vṛṣṭīnām hetuḥ — he is the cause of snow and rain

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

soyaṃ saptagaṇaḥ sūryamaṇḍale munisattama | himoṣṇavārivṛṣṭīnāṃ hetuḥ svasamayaṃ gataḥ ||23||

saptānāṃ sādhāraṇaṃ karm āha—so'yam iti | svayam āgata iti pāṭhe svasvakāle hemantagrīṣmādāvāgataḥ san himādivṛṣṭīnāṃ heturityarthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सप्तानां साधारणं कर्म् आह—सोऽयम् इति । स्वयम् आगत इति पाठे स्वस्वकाले हेमन्तग्रीष्मादावागतः सन् हिमादिवृष्टीनां हेतुरित्यर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges २१ [follows verse 23]).

Vishnu Shakti in the Sun Now, Chapter 11. Here, the various acts of the Sun are going to be told here by Sri Paraasharar.
