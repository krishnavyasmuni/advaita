VP 2.10.1

साशीति-मण्डल-शतं काष्ठयोर् अन्तरं द्वयोः ।
आरोहणावरोहाभ्याम् भानोर् अब्देन या गतिः ॥१॥

Between the extreme northern and southern points the sun has to traverse in a year one hundred and eighty degrees, ascending and descending.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sāśīti-maṇḍala-śatam — a hundred and eighty circuits or degrees; kāṣṭhayoḥ — of the two limits; antaram — the interval; dvayoḥ — of the two; ārohaṇāvarohābhyām — by ascending and descending; bhānoḥ — of the sun; abdena — in a year; yā — which; gatiḥ — course or movement.

Śrīdhara

atha — now, then; pratimāsam — each month; sūrya-ratha-paribhramaṇa-adhikāri-gaṇān — the groups of authorities who revolve with the sun’s chariot; vakṣyan — about to explain; uttarāyaṇa-dakṣiṇāyanayoḥ — of the northern and southern courses; anuloma-pratiloma-gantavyāni — to be traversed in direct and reverse order; krānti-vṛttāni — the circles of declination; āha — he says; uttara-dakṣiṇa-kāṣṭhayoḥ madhye — between the northern and southern limits; aśīty-uttaraṃ maṇḍala-śatam — a hundred circuits increased by eighty; bhānoḥ gatiḥ — the sun’s course; ārohaṇa-avarohābhyām — by ascending and descending; yugākṣa-kaṭi-bandhau vāyu-pāśau — the two wind-cords at the yoke and axle; dhruvaḥ yad ākarṣati — when Dhruva draws; uttarāyaṇe — in the northern course; pratyaham — each day; abhyantara-ekaika-maṇḍala-praveśe — by entering one inner circuit at a time; rathasya ārohaṇam — the chariot’s ascent; dakṣiṇāyane — in the southern course; pratilomam — in reverse order; bahir ekaikam maṇḍalam — one outer circuit at a time; rathasya avarohaṇam — the chariot’s descent; tad uktam mātsye — that is said in the Matsya.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sāśīti-maṇḍala-śataṃ kāṣṭhayor antaraṃ dvayoḥ | ārohaṇāvarohābhyām bhānor abdena yā gatiḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara

atha pratimāsaṃ sūryarathaparibhramaṇādhikārigaṇān vakṣyannuttarāyaṇadakṣiṇāyanayor anulomapratilomagantavyāni krāntivṛttānyāha—sāśīti iti | uttaradakṣiṇakāṣṭhayor madhye aśītyuttaraṃ maṇḍalaśataṃ bhānorgatirgantavyaṃ vartma ārohaṇāvarohābhyām iti yugākṣakaṭibandhau vāyupāśau dhruvo yadākarṣati tadottarāyaṇe pratyahamabhyantaraikaikamaṇḍalapraveśe rathasyārohaṇaṃ bhavati, yadā tu tau pāśau dhruvaḥ prasārayati dakṣiṇāyane teṣveva maṇḍaleṣu pratilomaṃ bahirekaikamaṇḍalaṃ praviśato rathasyāvarohaṇaṃ bhavati | taduktaṃ mātsye— ākṛṣyate yadā tau tu dhruveṇa samadhiṣṭhitau | tadā so'bhyantaraṃ sūryo bhramate maṇḍalāni tu | dhruveṇa mucyamāne tu punā raśmiyugena tu | tathaiva vākṣataḥ sūryo maṇḍalāni ca iti || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अथ प्रतिमासं सूर्यरथपरिभ्रमणाधिकारिगणान् वक्ष्यन्नुत्तरायणदक्षिणायनयोर् अनुलोमप्रतिलोमगन्तव्यानि क्रान्तिवृत्तान्याह—साशीति इति । उत्तरदक्षिणकाष्ठयोर् मध्ये अशीत्युत्तरं मण्डलशतं भानोर्गतिर्गन्तव्यं वर्त्म आरोहणावरोहाभ्याम् इति युगाक्षकटिबन्धौ वायुपाशौ ध्रुवो यदाकर्षति तदोत्तरायणे प्रत्यहमभ्यन्तरैकैकमण्डलप्रवेशे रथस्यारोहणं भवति, यदा तु तौ पाशौ ध्रुवः प्रसारयति दक्षिणायने तेष्वेव मण्डलेषु प्रतिलोमं बहिरेकैकमण्डलं प्रविशतो रथस्यावरोहणं भवति । तदुक्तं मात्स्ये—

आकृष्यते यदा तौ तु ध्रुवेण समधिष्ठितौ ।
तदा सोऽभ्यन्तरं सूर्यो भ्रमते मण्डलानि तु ।
ध्रुवेण मुच्यमाने तु पुना रश्मियुगेन तु ।
तथैव वाक्षतः सूर्यो मण्डलानि च इति ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Now, while explaining the groups of authorities who travel in the sun’s chariot each month, he states the circles to be traversed in direct and reverse order during the northern and southern courses, beginning with sāśīti. Between the northern and southern limits there is a path of the sun consisting of one hundred and eighty circuits, traversed by ascending and descending. When Dhruva draws the two wind-cords attached to the yoke and axle, in the northern course the chariot ascends, entering one inner circuit each day. When Dhruva stretches those cords, in the southern course it descends in reverse order, entering one outer circuit each day. This is stated in the Matsya: When the two, presided over by Dhruva, are drawn, the sun travels through the inner circuits; when released by Dhruva, he travels again with the pair of rays through the circuits in the same way from the outside.

VP 2.10.2

स रथोऽधिष्ठितो देवैर् आदित्यैर् ऋषिभिस् तथा ।
गन्धर्वैर् अप्सरोभिश् च ग्रामणी-सर्प-राक्षसैः ॥२॥

His car is presided over by divine Ádityas, Rishis, heavenly singers and nymphs, Yakshas, serpents, and Rákshasas (one of each being placed in it in every month).

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saḥ — that; rathaḥ — chariot; adhiṣṭhitaḥ — presided over or occupied; devaiḥ — by gods; ādityaiḥ — by Ādityas; ṛṣibhiḥ tathā — and by sages; gandharvaiḥ — by Gandharvas; apsarobhiḥ ca — and by Apsarases; grāmaṇī-sarpa-rākṣasaiḥ — by Yakṣas, serpents, and Rākṣasas.

Śrīdhara

devaiḥ ādityaiḥ — by gods who are the Ādityas; ādityā eva devāḥ — the Ādityas themselves are the gods; grāmaṇīḥ yakṣaḥ — the Grāmaṇī is a Yakṣa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sa ratho'dhiṣṭhito devair ādityair ṛṣibhis tathā | gandharvair apsarobhiś ca grāmaṇī-sarpa-rākṣasaiḥ || 2 ||

Śrīdhara

devairādityairādityā eva devāstaiḥ, grāmaṇīryakṣaḥ || 2 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

देवैरादित्यैरादित्या एव देवास्तैः, ग्रामणीर्यक्षः ॥२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The gods are the Ādityas themselves; the Grāmaṇī is a yakṣa.

VP 2.10.3

धाता क्रतु-स्थला चैव पुलस्त्यो वासुकिस् तथा ।
रथ-भृद् ग्रामणीर् हेतिस् तुम्बुरुश् चैव सप्तमः ॥३॥

The Áditya Dhátri, the sage Pulastya, the Gandharba Tumburu, the nymph Kratust́halá, the Yaksha Rathakrit, the serpent Vásuki, and the Rákshas Heti, always reside in the sun's car,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dhātā — Dhāta, an Āditya; kratu-sthalā — Kratu-sthalā, an Apsaras; ca eva — and indeed; pulastyaḥ — Pulastya, a sage; vāsukiḥ tathā — and Vāsuki, a serpent; ratha-bhṛt — Rathakṛt, the chariot-bearer; grāmaṇīḥ — the Grāmaṇī or Yakṣa; hetiḥ — Heti, a Rākṣasa; tumburuḥ ca eva — and Tumburu; saptamaḥ — the seventh.

Śrīdhara

ādityādīn — the Ādityas and the rest; pratimāsam — each month; sapta sapta anukrāmati — he proceeds through seven and seven; dhāta ityādinā — beginning with Dhāta; viṣṇu-śakty-upavṛṃhitā — strengthened by Viṣṇu’s power; dhātā sūryaḥ — Dhāta is the sun; rathakṛt-saṃjñaḥ grāmaṇīḥ yakṣaḥ — Rathakṛt by name is the Yakṣa Grāmaṇī; hetī rākṣasaḥ — Heti is a Rākṣasa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dhātā kratu-sthalā caiva pulastyo vāsukis tathā | ratha-bhṛd grāmaṇīr hetis tumburuś caiva saptamaḥ || 3 ||

Śrīdhara

vānevādityādīn pratimāsaṃ sapta saptānukrāmati dhāta ityādinā viṣṇuśaktyupavṛṃhitā ityantena | dhātā sūryaḥ rathakṛtsañjño grāmaṇīḥ yakṣaḥ | hetī rākṣasaḥ || 3 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वानेवादित्यादीन् प्रतिमासं सप्त सप्तानुक्रामति धात इत्यादिना विष्णुशक्त्युपवृंहिता इत्यन्तेन । धाता सूर्यः रथकृत्सञ्ज्ञो ग्रामणीः यक्षः । हेती राक्षसः ॥३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He proceeds through seven attendants each month, beginning with Dhāta and ending with strengthened by Viṣṇu’s power. Dhāta is the sun; Rathakṛt is the Yakṣa called Grāmaṇī; Heti is a Rākṣasa.

VP 2.10.4

एते वसन्ति वै चैत्रे मधु-मासे सदैव हि ।
मैत्रेय स्यन्दने भानोः सप्त मासाधिकारिणः ॥४॥

in the month of Madhu or Chaitra, as its seven guardians.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ete — these; vasanti — dwell; caitre — in Caitra; madhu-māse — in the month Madhu; sadaiva hi — always indeed; maitreya — O Maitreya; syandane — in the chariot; bhānoḥ — of the sun; sapta — seven; māsādhikāriṇaḥ — month-presiding authorities.

Śrīdhara

caitra eva — Caitra itself; madhu-māsaḥ — is the month Madhu; tasmin — in that.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ete vasanti vai caitre madhu-māse sadaiva hi | maitreya syandane bhānoḥ sapta māsādhikāriṇaḥ || 4 ||

Śrīdhara

caitra eva madhumāsastasmin || 4 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

चैत्र एव मधुमासस्तस्मिन् ॥४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Caitra itself is the month Madhu; they dwell in that month.

VP 2.10.5

अर्यमा पुलहश् चैव रथौजाः पुञ्जिक-स्थला ।
प्रहेतिः कच्छ-वीरश् च नारदश् च रथे रवेः ॥५॥

In Vaiśákh or Mádhava the seven are Áryamat, Pulaha, Náreda, Punjikásthalí, Rathaujas, Kachaníra, and Praheti.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aryamā — Āryamat; pulahaḥ ca eva — and Pulaha; rathaujāḥ — Rathaujas; puñjika-sthalā — Puñjika-sthalā; prahetiḥ — Praheti; kaccha-vīraḥ — Kaccha-vīra or Kacchanīra; ca — and; nāradaḥ — Nārada; rathaḥ — chariot; raveḥ — of the sun.

Śrīdhara

rathaujā yakṣaḥ — Rathaujas is a Yakṣa; prahetiḥ rākṣasaḥ — Praheti is a Rākṣasa; kacchanīraḥ sarpaḥ — Kacchanīra is a serpent.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aryamā pulahaś caiva rathaujāḥ puñjika-sthalā | prahetiḥ kaccha-vīraś ca nāradaś ca rathe raveḥ || 5 ||

Śrīdhara

rathaujā yakṣaḥ, prahetiḥ rākṣasaḥ | kacchanīraḥ sarpaḥ, mādhave vaiśākhe | śucisañjña iti jyaṣṭho vivakṣitaḥ ||5-6||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

रथौजा यक्षः, प्रहेतिः राक्षसः । कच्छनीरः सर्पः, माधवे वैशाखे । शुचिसञ्ज्ञ इति ज्यष्ठो विवक्षितः ॥५-६॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Rathaujas is a Yakṣa; Praheti is a Rākṣasa; Kacchanīra is a serpent.

VP 2.10.6

माधवे निवसन्त्य् एते शुचि-सञ्ज्ञे निबोध मे ॥६॥

In Śuchi or Jyesht́ha they are

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mādhave — in Mādhava; nivasanti — dwell; ete — these; śuci-saṃjñe — in the Śuci-designated month; nibodha me — understand or hear from me.

Śrīdhara

mādhave — in Mādhava; vaiśākhe — in Vaiśākha; śuci-saṃjñaḥ — the designation Śuci; jyaiṣṭhaḥ vivakṣitaḥ — Jyaiṣṭha is intended.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mādhave nivasanty ete śuci-sañjñe nibodha me || 6 ||

Śrīdhara

rathaujā yakṣaḥ, prahetiḥ rākṣasaḥ | kacchanīraḥ sarpaḥ, mādhave vaiśākhe | śucisañjña iti jyaṣṭho vivakṣitaḥ ||5-6||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

रथौजा यक्षः, प्रहेतिः राक्षसः । कच्छनीरः सर्पः, माधवे वैशाखे । शुचिसञ्ज्ञ इति ज्यष्ठो विवक्षितः ॥५-६॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Mādhava means Vaiśākha. The designation Śucisaṃjña is intended to mean Jyaiṣṭha.

VP 2.10.7

मित्रोऽत्रिस् तक्षको रक्षः पौरुषेयोऽथ मेनका ।
हाहा रथस्वनश् चैव मैत्रेयैते वसन्ति वै ॥७॥

Mitra, Atri, Háhá, Mená, Rathaswana, Takshaka, and Paurusheya.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mitraḥ — Mitra; atriḥ — Atri; takṣakaḥ — Takṣaka; rakṣaḥ — the Rākṣasa; pauruṣeyaḥ — Pauruṣeya; atha — then; menakā — Menakā; hāhā — Hāhā; rathasvanaḥ ca eva — and Rathasvana; maitreya — O Maitreya; ete vasanti vai — these dwell indeed.

Śrīdhara

pauruṣeyaḥ rākṣasaḥ — Pauruṣeya is a Rākṣasa; rathasvanaḥ yakṣaḥ — Rathasvana is a Yakṣa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mitro'tris takṣako rakṣaḥ pauruṣeyo'tha menakā | hāhā rathasvanaś caiva maitreyaite vasanti vai || 7 ||

Śrīdhara

pauruṣeyo rākṣasaḥ, rathasvano yakṣaḥ || 7 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पौरुषेयो राक्षसः, रथस्वनो यक्षः ॥७॥

VP 2.10.8

वरुणो वसिष्ठो नागश् च सहजन्या हूहू रथः ।
रथचित्रस् तथा शुक्रे वसन्त्य् आषाढ-सञ्ज्ञके ॥८॥

In the month Śukra or Áshádha they are Varuńa, Vaśisht́ha, Huhu, Sahajanyá, Rathachitra, Nága, and Budha.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

varuṇaḥ — Varuṇa; vasiṣṭhaḥ — Vasiṣṭha; nāgaḥ ca — and Nāga; sahajanyā — Sahajanyā; hūhū — Hūhū; rathaḥ — chariot; rathacitraḥ tathā — and Rathacitra; śukre — in Śukra; vasanti — they dwell; āṣāḍha-saṃjñake — in the Āṣāḍha-designated month.

Śrīdhara

varuṇaḥ sūryaḥ — Varuṇa is the sun; rambhā ca sahajanyā iti pāṭhe — in the reading Rambhā and Sahajanyā; saṃjñāntareṇa — by another designation; rathacitra iti — the name Rathacitra; yakṣa-sarpayoḥ ekam nāma — one name of the Yakṣa and serpent; nāga-saṃjñakaḥ sarpaḥ — a serpent designated Nāga; āṣāḍha-saṃjñaka — Āṣāḍha-designated; śukraḥ āṣāḍhaḥ — Śukra is Āṣāḍha; śuci-saṃjñaḥ jyaiṣṭhaḥ — Śuci-designated means Jyaiṣṭha.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

varuṇo vasiṣṭho nāgaś ca sahajanyā hūhū rathaḥ | rathacitras tathā śukre vasanty āṣāḍha-sañjñake || 8 ||

Śrīdhara

varuṇaḥ sūryaḥ rambhā ca sahajanyeti pāṭhe phālgune vakṣyamāṇa-rambhāvyāvṛttyarthaṃ sañjñāntareṇa tasya viśeṣaṇam | rathacitra iti yakṣasarpayor ekaṃ nāma | nāgaśca sahajanyeti pāṭhe nāgasañjñakaḥ sarpaḥ | āṣāḍhasañjñaka iti cākhyānādāṣāḍha eva śukraḥ pūrvoktaśucisañjño jyaiṣṭha iti jñeyam || 8 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वरुणः सूर्यः रम्भा च सहजन्येति पाठे फाल्गुने वक्ष्यमाण-रम्भाव्यावृत्त्यर्थं सञ्ज्ञान्तरेण तस्य विशेषणम् । रथचित्र इति यक्षसर्पयोर् एकं नाम । नागश्च सहजन्येति पाठे नागसञ्ज्ञकः सर्पः । आषाढसञ्ज्ञक इति चाख्यानादाषाढ एव शुक्रः पूर्वोक्तशुचिसञ्ज्ञो ज्यैष्ठ इति ज्ञेयम् ॥८॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Varuṇa is the sun. In the reading Rambhā and Sahajanyā, the latter designation is used to distinguish her from the Rambhā mentioned below in Phālguna. Rathacitra is one name shared by the Yakṣa and the serpent. In the reading Nāga and Sahajanyā, Nāga is the serpent designated Nāga. Since the text calls it Āṣāḍha-designated, Śukra is Āṣāḍha; the previously mentioned Śuci-designated month should be understood as Jyaiṣṭha.

VP 2.10.9

इन्द्रो विश्वावसुः स्रोत एलापुत्रस् तथाङ्गिराः ।
प्रम्लोचा च नभस्य् एते सर्पिश् चार्के वसन्ति वै ॥९॥

In the month Nabhas (or Srávańa) they are Indra, Angiras, Viswávasu, Pramlochá, Śrotas, and Elapatra (the name of both serpent and Rákshas).

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 2.10.21

वहन्ति पन्नगा यक्षैः क्रियते ऽभिषुसङ्ग्रहः ॥२१॥

the serpent harnesses his steeds, and the Yaksha trims the reins:

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vahanti — they carry or harness; pannagāḥ — serpents; yakṣaiḥ — by the Yakṣas; kriyate — is made or performed; abhīṣu-saṅgrahaḥ — the gathering or adjustment of the reins.

Śrīdhara

vahanti — they carry; vahana-anukūlatayā — in a manner conducive to carrying; ratham sannahyanti — they harness the chariot; nāgāḥ — the serpents; iti śukokteḥ — as Śuka says; abhīṣu-saṅgrahaḥ — the gathering or joining of the reins; raśmi-saṃyojanam — the arrangement of the reins.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vahanti pannagā yakṣaiḥ kriyate 'bhiṣusaṅgrahaḥ || 21 ||

Śrīdhara

vahanti vahanānukūlatayā rathaṃ sannahyantītyarthaḥ, sannahyanti rathaṃ nāgāḥ iti śukokteḥ | abhīṣusaṅgrahaḥ raśmisaṃyojanam | nityasevakānāha—bālakhilyā iti || 22 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वहन्ति वहनानुकूलतया रथं सन्नह्यन्तीत्यर्थः, सन्नह्यन्ति रथं नागाः इति शुकोक्तेः । अभीषुसङ्ग्रहः रश्मिसंयोजनम् । नित्यसेवकानाह—बालखिल्या इति ॥२२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He explains that they carry means they harness the chariot in a manner conducive to carrying it; Śuka says, the serpents harness the chariot. Abhīṣusaṅgraha means the joining or arrangement of the reins.

VP 2.10.23

सो ऽयं सप्त-गणः सूर्य-मण्डले मुनि-सत्तम ।
हिमोष्ण वारि-वृष्टीणां हेतुत्वे समयं गतः ॥२३॥

The whole troop of seven, attached to the sun's car, are the agents in the distribution of cold, heat, and rain, at their respective seasons.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saḥ ayam — this very; sapta-gaṇaḥ — troop of seven; sūrya-maṇḍale — in the sun’s orb; muni-sattama — O best of sages; hima-uṣṇa — cold and heat; vāri-vṛṣṭīnām — of water and rain; hetutve — in the causation; samayam gataḥ — having reached the proper season or time.

Śrīdhara

saptānām — of the seven; sādhāraṇam karma — the common function; āha — he states; svayam āgata iti pāṭhe — with the reading svayam āgata; sva-sva-kāle — at their respective times; hemanta-grīṣmādau — in winter, summer, and so on; himādi-vṛṣṭīnām hetuḥ — the cause of frost and rain.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

so 'yaṃ sapta-gaṇaḥ sūrya-maṇḍale muni-sattama | himoṣṇa vāri-vṛṣṭīṇāṃ hetutve samayaṃ gataḥ || 23 ||

Śrīdhara

saptānāṃ sādhāraṇaṃ karm āha—so'yam iti | svayam āgata iti pāṭhe svasvakāle hemantagrīṣmādāvāgataḥ san himādivṛṣṭīnāṃ heturityarthaḥ || 21 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सप्तानां साधारणं कर्म् आह—सोऽयम् इति । स्वयम् आगत इति पाठे स्वस्वकाले हेमन्तग्रीष्मादावागतः सन् हिमादिवृष्टीनां हेतुरित्यर्थः ॥२१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He states the common function of the seven groups, beginning with this group. With the reading svayam āgata, the meaning is that, having arrived at their respective times—winter, summer, and so on—they are the causes of frost, heat, and rain.
