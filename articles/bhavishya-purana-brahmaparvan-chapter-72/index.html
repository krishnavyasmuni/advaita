  • Chapter 72 — Sāmba, the Solar Temples, and the Cause of His Curse

BP 1.72.1

sumanturuvāca |
itthaṃ brahmavaco yogī śrutvā rājan divākaram |
vyomarūpaṃ samārādhya gataḥ sūryasalokatām || 1 ||

Sumantu said: Thus, having heard Brahmā’s words, the yogin worshipped the Sun, O king, whose form is the sky, and went to the world of Sūrya.

Word-for-word
sumantuḥ uvāca — Sumantu said; ittham — thus; brahma-vacaḥ — Brahmā’s words; yogī — the yogin; śrutvā — having heard; rājan — O king; divākaram — the Sun; vyoma-rūpam — sky-formed; samārādhya — having worshipped; gataḥ — went; sūrya-salokatām — to the world of Sūrya.
Transliteration
sumantur uvāca | itthaṃ brahmavaco yogī śrutvā rājan divākaram | vyomarūpaṃ samārādhya gataḥ sūryasalokatām || 1 ||

BP 1.72.2

tathā tvamapi rājendra pūjayitvā vibhāvasum |
gamiṣyasi paraṃ sthānaṃ devānāmapi durlabham || 2 ||

In the same way, O king of kings, having worshipped Vibhāvasu, you will go to the supreme place, difficult even for the gods to attain.

Word-for-word
tathā — in the same way; tvam — you; api — also; rājendra — O king of kings; pūjayitvā — having worshipped; vibhāvasum — Vibhāvasu, the Sun; gamiṣyasi — you will go; param sthānam — supreme place; devānām api — even for the gods; durlabham — difficult to attain.
Transliteration
tathā tvam api rājendra pūjayitvā vibhāvasum | gamiṣyasi paraṃ sthānaṃ devānām api durlabham || 2 ||

BP 1.72.3

Śatānīka said: O great sage, where is the first station of Ravi in Jambūdvīpa, where that Ravi receives the worship prescribed by the ordinances?

BP 1.72.4

BP 1.72.6

candrabhāgātaṭe nāmnā puraṃ yat sāmbasaṃjñitam |

BP 1.72.12

BP 1.72.13
