VP 3.14.1

ब्रह्मेन्द्ररुद्रनासत्यसूर्याग्निवसुमारुतान् । विश्वेदेवान्पितृगणान्वयांसि मनुजान्पशून्

A person who performs śrāddha with faith pleases the whole world: Brahmā, Indra, Rudra, the Nāsatyas, the Sun, Fire, the Vasus, the Maruts, the Viśvedevas, the ancestors, birds, humans, and animals.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahmendra-rudra-nāsatya-sūryāgni-vasu-mārutān — Brahmā, Indra, Rudra, the Nāsatyas, the Sun, Fire, the Vasus, and the Maruts; viśvedevān-pitṛ-gaṇān-vayāṃsi-manujān-paśūn — the Viśvedevas, the hosts of ancestors, birds, humans, and animals

Śrīdhara

श्राद्धं तत्फलकालांश्च नित्यकाम्यादिभेदतः — śrāddhas, their fruits and times, according to the distinction of obligatory and desired; चतुर्दशे तु कल्पांश्च तत्फलान्यन्ववर्णयत् — in the fourteenth he describes rites and their fruits; तत्र तावत् उत्तरक्रियाणां कालान् वर्णयिष्यन् — intending to describe the times of the subsequent rites; श्राद्धप्रशंसाम् आह ब्रह्म इति — he praises śrāddha, beginning with “Brahman”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahmendrarudranāsatyasūryāgnivasumārutān | viśvedevānpitṛgaṇānvayāṃsi manujānpaśūn ||1||

śrāddhaṃ tatphalakālāṃśca nityakāmyādibhedataḥ | caturdaśe tu kalpāṃśca tatphalānyanvavarṇayat | tatra tāvaduttarakriyāṇāṃ kālān varṇayiṣyan śrāddhapraśaṃsām āha—brahma iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

श्राद्धं तत्फलकालांश्च नित्यकाम्यादिभेदतः । चतुर्दशे तु कल्पांश्च तत्फलान्यन्ववर्णयत् । तत्र तावदुत्तरक्रियाणां कालान् वर्णयिष्यन् श्राद्धप्रशंसाम् आह—ब्रह्म इति

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 1).

He describes śrāddha, its times, and its fruits according to distinctions such as obligatory and desired rites. In the fourteenth chapter he has described the procedures and their fruits. Now, while about to describe the times for the subsequent rites, he praises śrāddha, beginning with “Brahma.”

VP 3.14.2

सरीसृपानृषिगणान्यच्चान्यद्भूतसंज्ञितम् । श्राद्धं श्रद्धान्वितः कुर्वन्प्रीणयत्यखिलं जगत्

Reptiles, hosts of sages, and every other being are pleased. A person who performs śrāddha with faith thereby pleases the entire world.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarīsṛpān-ṛṣi-gaṇān-yac-ca-anyad-bhūta-saṃjñitam — reptiles, hosts of ṛṣis, and whatever else is designated a being; śrāddhaṃ śraddhānvitaḥ kurvan-prīṇayati akhilaṃ jagat — performing śrāddha with faith, he pleases the entire world

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarīsṛpānṛṣigaṇānyaccānyadbhūtasaṃjñitam | śrāddhaṃ śraddhānvitaḥ kurvanprīṇayatyakhilaṃ jagat ||2||

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.14.3

मासिमास्यसिते पक्षे पञ्चदश्यां नरेश्वर । तथाऽष्टकासु कुर्वीत काम्यान्कालाञ्छृणुष्व मे

O king, one should perform desired śrāddha every month on the fifteenth day of the dark fortnight and likewise on the Aṣṭakās. Hear from me the appropriate times.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māsi māsi asite pakṣe pañcadaśyāṃ-nareśvara — each month, in the dark fortnight, on the fifteenth day, O king; tathā aṣṭakāsu kurvīta kāmyān-kālān śṛṇuṣva me — and likewise on the Aṣṭakās he should perform [it]; hear from me the desired times

Śrīdhara

प्रतिमासं कृष्णपक्षे पञ्चदश्यां दर्शश्राद्धं कुर्वीतेत्यर्थः । अष्टका आग्रहायण्यादूर्ध्वं तिस्रः कृष्णाष्टम्यस्तासु — Śrāddha is to be performed every month on the fifteenth day of the dark fortnight. The Aṣṭakās are the three dark-fortnight eighth days beginning after Āgrahāyaṇa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māsimāsyasite pakṣe pañcadaśyāṃ nareśvara | tathā'ṣṭakāsu kurvīta kāmyānkālāñchṛṇuṣva me ||3||

pratimāsaṃ kṛṣṇapakṣe pañcadaśyāṃ darśaśrāddhaṃ kurvītetyarthaḥ | aṣṭakā āgrahāyaṇyādūrdhvaṃ tisraḥ kṛṣṇāṣṭamyastāsu

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रतिमासं कृष्णपक्षे पञ्चदश्यां दर्शश्राद्धं कुर्वीतेत्यर्थः । अष्टका आग्रहायण्यादूर्ध्वं तिस्रः कृष्णाष्टम्यस्तासु

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 3).

Śrāddha is to be performed every month on the fifteenth day of the dark fortnight. The Aṣṭakās are the three dark-fortnight eighth days beginning after Āgrahāyaṇa.

VP 3.14.4

श्राद्धार्हमागतं द्रव्यं विशिष्टमथ वा द्विजम् । श्राद्धं कुर्वीत विज्ञाय व्यतीपातेऽयते तथा

Having recognized suitable material that has arrived, or a distinguished Brāhmaṇa who has arrived, one should perform śrāddha at Vyatīpāta and likewise at the ayanas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrāddhārham-āgataṃ-dravyaṃ-viśiṣṭam atha vā dvijam — material suitable for śrāddha that has arrived, or a distinguished Brāhmaṇa; śrāddhaṃ kurvīta vijñāya vyatīpāte ayane tathā — having recognized [such a circumstance], he should perform śrāddha at Vyatīpāta and likewise at an ayana

Śrīdhara

श्राद्धार्हं द्रव्यं विशिष्टञ्च द्विजमागतं विज्ञायेत्यन्वयः — The syntactical connection is: having recognized suitable material for śrāddha and a distinguished Brāhmaṇa who has arrived.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrāddhārhamāgataṃ dravyaṃ viśiṣṭamatha vā dvijam | śrāddhaṃ kurvīta vijñāya vyatīpāte'yate tathā ||4||

śrāddhārhaṃ dravyaṃ viśiṣṭañca dvijamāgataṃ vijñāyetyanvayaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

श्राद्धार्हं द्रव्यं विशिष्टञ्च द्विजमागतं विज्ञायेत्यन्वयः

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 4).

The syntactical connection is: having recognized suitable material for śrāddha and a distinguished Brāhmaṇa who has arrived.

VP 3.14.5

विषुवे चापि संप्राप्ते ग्रहणे शशिसूर्ययोः । समस्तेष्वेव भूपाल राशिष्वर्के च गच्छति

One should also perform it when an equinox arrives, during an eclipse of the Moon or Sun, and whenever the Sun enters any of the zodiacal signs.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

viṣuve ca api saṃprāpte — when an equinox too has arrived; grahaṇe śaśi-sūryayoḥ — at an eclipse of the Moon or Sun; samasteṣu eva rāśiṣu arke ca gacchati — when the Sun enters all the zodiacal signs

Śrīdhara

समस्तेषु राशिषु अर्के गच्छति सति इति सर्वसङ्क्रान्तीनां श्राद्धकालत्वस्योक्तत्वात् अयने विषुवे चेति पृथगुक्तिः फलातिरेकार्था — Since the Sun’s entry into all the zodiacal signs has already been stated as a time for śrāddha, the separate mention of the ayanas and the equinox is intended to indicate an additional fruit.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

viṣuve cāpi saṃprāpte grahaṇe śaśisūryayoḥ | samasteṣveva bhūpāla rāśiṣvarke ca gacchati ||5||

samasteṣu rāśiṣu arke gacchati sati iti sarvasaṅkrāntīnāṃ śrāddhakālatvasyoktatvāt ayane viṣuve ceti pṛthaguktiḥ phalātirekārthā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

समस्तेषु राशिषु अर्के गच्छति सति इति सर्वसङ्क्रान्तीनां श्राद्धकालत्वस्योक्तत्वात् अयने विषुवे चेति पृथगुक्तिः फलातिरेकार्था

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 5).

Since the Sun’s entry into all the zodiacal signs has already been stated as a time for śrāddha, the separate mention of the ayanas and the equinox is intended to indicate an additional fruit.

VP 3.14.6

नक्षत्रग्रहपीडासु दुष्टस्वप्नावलोकने । इच्छाश्राद्धानि कुर्वीत नवसस्यागमे तथा

One should perform desired śrāddhas when the stars or planets are afflicted, when a bad dream is seen, and likewise when new grain arrives.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nakṣatra-graha-pīḍāsu — when the stars and planets are afflicted; duṣṭa-svapna-avalokane — when a bad dream is seen; icchā-śrāddhāni kurvīta — he should perform desired śrāddhas; nava-sasya-āgame tathā — and likewise when new grain arrives

Śrīdhara

इच्छाश्राद्धानि काम्यानि । नवशस्यागम इति—एतच्च व्रीहियवव्यतिरिक्तविषयम्, तयोर्नित्यश्राद्धानिमित्तत्वात् — Desired śrāddhas are optional rites. “When new grain arrives” applies to grain other than rice and barley, since those two are causes for the obligatory śrāddha.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nakṣatragrahapīḍāsu duṣṭasvapnāvalokane | icchāśrāddhāni kurvīta navasasyāgame tathā ||6||

icchāśrāddhāni kāmyāni | navaśasyāgama iti—etacca vrīhiyavavyatiriktaviṣayam, tayornityaśrāddhānimittatvāt

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इच्छाश्राद्धानि काम्यानि । नवशस्यागम इति—एतच्च व्रीहियवव्यतिरिक्तविषयम्, तयोर्नित्यश्राद्धानिमित्तत्वात्

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 6).

Desired śrāddhas are optional rites. “When new grain arrives” applies to grain other than rice and barley, since those two are causes for the obligatory śrāddha.

VP 3.14.7

अमावास्या यदा मैत्रविशाखास्वातियोगिनी । श्राद्धैः पितृगणस्तृप्तिं तथाप्नोत्यष्टवार्षिकीम्

When the new-moon day is joined with Maitra, Viśākhā, or Svāti, the ancestral hosts obtain through śrāddha satisfaction lasting eight years.

amāvāsyā yadā maitraviśākhāsvātiyoginī | śrāddhaiḥ pitṛgaṇastṛptiṃ tathāpnotyaṣṭavārṣikīm ||7||

maitramanurādhā

मैत्रमनुराधा

VP 3.14.8

अमावास्या यदा पुष्ये रौद्रेऽथर्क्षे पुनर्वसौ । द्वादशाब्दं तदा तृप्तिं प्रयान्ति पितरोऽर्चिताः

rodramārdrā

रोद्रमार्द्रा

VP 3.14.9

वासवाजैकपादर्क्षे पितृणां तृप्तिमिच्छताम् । वारुणे वाऽप्यमावास्या देवानामपि दुर्लभा

When the new moon is in Vāsava, Ajaikapād, or Vāruṇa, it is difficult even for the gods to obtain for those who desire the satisfaction of the ancestors.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vāsava-ajai-ka-pād-ṛkṣe — when the asterism is Vāsava or Ajaikapād; pitṛṇāṃ tṛptim icchatām — for those desiring the ancestors’ satisfaction; vāruṇe vā api amāvāsyā devānām api durlabhā — or, when it is Vāruṇa, the new-moon day is difficult even for the gods to obtain

Śrīdhara

वासवं ज्येष्ठा — Vāsava is Jyeṣṭhā; अजैकपात् पूर्वभाद्रपदा — Ajaikapāt is Pūrvabhādrapadā; वारुणं शतभिषा — Vāruṇa is Śatabhiṣā; एतेषु नक्षत्रेषु अमावास्या पितॄणां देवानां च तृप्तिं कर्तुम् इच्छतां पुंसां दुर्लभा — for those wishing to satisfy ancestors and gods, new moon in these constellations is difficult to obtain; इच्छते इति pāṭhe sa eva arthaḥ — in the reading icchate, the same meaning

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vāsavājaikapādarkṣe pitṛṇāṃ tṛptimicchatām | vāruṇe vā'pyamāvāsyā devānāmapi durlabhā ||9||

vāsavaṃ jyeṣṭhā, ajaikapāt pūrvabhādrapadā, vāruṇaṃ śatabhiṣā, eteṣu nakṣatreṣvamāvāsyā pitṝṇāṃ devatānāñca tṛptiṃ kartumicchatāṃ puṃsā durlabhā | icchateti pāṭhe sa evārthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वासवं ज्येष्ठा, अजैकपात् पूर्वभाद्रपदा, वारुणं शतभिषा, एतेषु नक्षत्रेष्वमावास्या पितॄणां देवतानाञ्च तृप्तिं कर्तुमिच्छतां पुंसा दुर्लभा । इच्छतेति पाठे स एवार्थः

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 9).

Vāsava means Jyeṣṭhā; Ajaikapāt means Pūrvabhādrapadā; Vāruṇa means Śatabhiṣā. A new-moon day joined with these asterisms is difficult for a person wishing to bring satisfaction to the ancestors and the deities. With the reading “icchate,” the meaning is the same.

VP 3.14.10

नवस्वृक्षेष्वमावास्या यदेतेष्ववनीपते । तदा हि तृप्तिदं श्राद्धं पितृणां शृणु चापरम्

When the new moon occurs in these nine asterisms, the śrāddha gives satisfaction to the ancestors. Hear also another [such occasion], O king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

navasu ṛkṣeṣu amāvāsyā yadā eteṣu avanīpate — when the new moon is in these nine asterisms, O king; tadā hi tṛptidaṃ śrāddhaṃ pitṛṇāṃ — then the śrāddha is indeed satisfaction-giving for the ancestors; śṛṇu ca aparam — and hear another [case]

Śrīdhara

nanu amāvāsya-śrāddhatvāt nityatā eva — but because it is a new-moon śrāddha, it is surely obligatory; tat katham eteṣu phala-śrutiḥ — then how is there a statement of fruit in these?; ataḥ āha navaṣu iti — therefore he says “in the nine”; go-dohena āpaḥ praṇayet paśu-kāmasya iti vat — as in “he should draw water by milking a cow for one desiring cattle”; nakṣatra-guṇa-yogāt guṇa-phala-vidhiḥ — from conjunction with the qualities of a constellation comes a rule of qualities and fruits; nityāt pṛthak etāni śrāddhāni iti vā — or these śrāddhas are separate from the obligatory one

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

navasvṛkṣeṣvamāvāsyā yadeteṣvavanīpate | tadā hi tṛptidaṃ śrāddhaṃ pitṛṇāṃ śṛṇu cāparam ||10||

nanvamāvasyāśrāddhatvānnityataiva, tat kathameteṣu phalaśrutirityata āha—navasv iti | godohenāpaḥ praṇayeta paśukāmasya itivat nakṣatraguṇayogād guṇaphalavidhirityarthaḥ | nityāt pṛthagetāni śraddhānīti vā

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नन्वमावस्याश्राद्धत्वान्नित्यतैव, तत् कथमेतेषु फलश्रुतिरित्यत आह—नवस्व् इति । गोदोहेनापः प्रणयेत पशुकामस्य इतिवत् नक्षत्रगुणयोगाद् गुणफलविधिरित्यर्थः । नित्यात् पृथगेतानि श्रद्धानीति वा

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 10).

Since the new-moon śrāddha is obligatory, one may ask why a statement of fruit is given for these occasions. The answer is “among the nine”: a fruit is prescribed through the quality of the asterism, as in the rule, “Let him draw water by milking for one desiring cattle”; alternatively, these śrāddhas are distinct from the obligatory one.

VP 3.14.11

तत्राप्यसामर्थ्ययुतः कराग्राग्रस्थितांस्तिलान् । प्रणम्य द्विजमुख्याय कस्मैचिद्भूप दास्यति

VP 3.14.28

VP 3.14.30

न मेऽस्ति वित्तं न धनं च नान्यच्छ्राद्धोपयोग्यं स्वपितृन्नतोऽस्मि । तृप्यन्तु भक्त्या पितरो मयैतौ कृतौ भुजौ वर्त्मनि मारुतस्य

“I have no wealth, no treasure, and nothing else suitable for śrāddha. I bow to my ancestors. May the ancestors be satisfied through devotion; I have placed these two arms on the path of the wind.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

VP 3.14.31

इत्येतत्पितृभिर्गीतं भावाभावप्रयोजनम् । यः करोति कृतं तेन श्राद्धं भवति पार्थिव
