VP 5.4.1

कंसस्ततोद्विग्नमनाः प्राह सर्वान्महासुरान्।
प्रलंबकेशिप्रमुखानाहूयासुरपुङ्गवान्

Sri Paraasharar - Kamsa, being very agitated in his mind, because he did not know what to do, he called all his other asuraas, powerful asuraas who were with him - Pralamba, Keshi, etc. and told them thus -

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kaṃsastatodvignamanāḥ prāha sarvānmahāsurān . pralaṃbakeśipramukhānāhūyāsurapuṅgavān

Śrīdhara

kaṃsa iti . tatodvigna-manāḥ tataṃ vitatam udvignam udvegaḥ vitatodvega-yukta-citta ity arthaḥ . tadeti pāṭhaḥ sugamaḥ ||१|| kaṃsa uvāca— he pralamba mahā-bāho keśin dhenuka pūtane .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कंस इति । ततोद्विग्न-मनाः ततं विततम् उद्विग्नम् उद्वेगः विततोद्वेग-युक्त-चित्त इत्य् अर्थः । तदेति पाठः सुगमः ॥१॥

कंस उवाच—

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - Kamsa, being very agitated in his mind, because he did not know what to do, he called all his other asuraas, powerful asuraas who were with him - Pralamba, Keshi, etc. and told them thus -

VP 5.4.2

कंस उवाच
हे प्रलंब महाबाहो केशिन् धेनुक पूतने।
अरिष्टाद्यास्तथैवान्ये श्रूयतां वचनं मम

Kamsa - O Pralamba, Keshi, Dhenuka, Pootanaa, Arishta, and others, listen to what I am telling, very carefully.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

he pralaṃba mahābāho keśin dhenuka pūtane . ariṣṭādyāstathaivānye śrūyatāṃ vacanaṃ mama

Śrīdhara

he pralambeti . yuṣmābhir ariṣṭādyaiś ca mama vacaḥ śrūyatām ||२|| māṃ hantum amarair yatnaḥ kṛtaḥ kila durātmabhiḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

हे प्रलम्बेति । युष्माभिर् अरिष्टाद्यैश् च मम वचः श्रूयताम् ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Kamsa - O Pralamba, Keshi, Dhenuka, Pootanaa, Arishta, and others, listen to what I am telling, very carefully.

VP 5.4.3

मां हन्तुममरैर्यत्नः कृतः किल दुरात्मभिः।
मद्वीर्यतापितान्वीरो न त्वेतान्गणयाम्यहम्

Pralamba and others, listen to what I am telling. All the gods, the bad ones, are making all kinds of efforts to kill me. They have been troubled with my power, and strength. I don't care at all for them, as I am all powerful and valiant.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

māṃ hantumamarairyatnaḥ kṛtaḥ kila durātmabhiḥ . madvīryatāpitānvīro na tvetāngaṇayāmyaham

Śrīdhara

mām iti . kileti prasiddhau ||३|| kim indreṇālpa-vīryeṇa kiṃ hareṇaika-cāriṇā .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

माम् इति । किलेति प्रसिद्धौ ॥३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

All the gods, the bad ones, are making all kinds of efforts to kill me. They have been troubled with my power, and strength. I don't care at all for them, as I am all powerful and valiant.

VP 5.4.4

किम् इन्द्रेणाल्प-वीर्येण किं हरेणैक-चारिणा।
हरिणा वापि किं साध्यं छिद्रेष्व् असुर-घातिना

What can Indra do, who is very weak, does not have any power. What can Rudra do, who roams alone? Even Hari, what can He do, who finds some fault and kills the demons all the time, what can He do to me? I am so powerful.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kimindreṇālpavīryeṇa kiṃ hareṇaikacāriṇā . hariṇā vāpi kiṃ sādhyaṃ chidreṣvasuraghātinā

Śrīdhara

*na vyākhyātam *||१७|| || iti śrīdhara-svāmi-viracitātma-prakāśākhya-śrī-viṣṇu-mahāpurāṇa-vyākhyāyāṃ pañcamāṃśe caturtho’dhyāyaḥ || iti śrī-viṣṇu-purāṇe pañcame’ṃśe caturtho’dhyāyaḥ ||४|| —o)०(o—

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

*न व्याख्यातम् *॥१७॥ ॥ इति श्रीधर-स्वामि-विरचितात्म-प्रकाशाख्य-श्री-विष्णु-महापुराण-व्याख्यायां पञ्चमांशे चतुर्थोऽध्यायः ॥ इति श्री-विष्णु-पुराणे पञ्चमेऽंशे चतुर्थोऽध्यायः ॥४॥ —ओ)०(ओ—

Śrīdhara commentary.

What can Indra do, who is very weak, does not have any power. What can Rudra do, who roams alone? Even Hari, what can He do, who finds some fault and kills the demons all the time, what can He do to me? I am so powerful.

VP 5.4.5

किम् आदित्यैः किं वसुभिर् अल्प-वीर्यैः किम् अग्निभिः।
किं वान्यैर् अमरैः सर्वैर् मद्-बाहु-बल-निर्जितैः

What can the Dvaadasha Aadityaas, the Ashta Vasus do? All these are weak, they don't have any power. What can all the fires do? Even other gods, what can they do to me? They cannot even harm me. I have won over all of them with my strength and powers.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kimādityaiḥ kiṃ vasubhiralpavīryaiḥ kimagnibhiḥ . kiṃvā’nyairamaraiḥ sarvairmadbāhubalanirjitaiḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.4.6

किं न दृष्टोऽमरपतिर्मया संयुगमेत्य सः।
पृष्ठेनैव वहन्बाणानपगच्छन्नवक्षसा

Amarapati Indra wanted to wage a war with me, he came, and turned his back and ran away, carrying the arrows in his back itself.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kiṃ na dṛṣṭo’marapatirmayā saṃyugametya saḥ . pṛṣṭhenaiva vahanbāṇānapagacchannavakṣasā

Śrīdhara

kiṃ na dṛṣṭa iti . pṛṣṭhenaiva vahan palāyamānaḥ ||६|| mad-rāṣṭre vāritā vṛṣṭir yadā śakreṇa kiṃ tadā .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

किं न दृष्ट इति । पृष्ठेनैव वहन् पलायमानः ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Amarapati Indra wanted to wage a war with me, he came, and turned his back and ran away, carrying the arrows in his back itself.

VP 5.4.7

मद्राष्ट्रे वारिता वृष्टिर्यदा शक्रेण किं तदा।
मद्बाणभिन्नैर्जलदैरापोऽमुक्ता यथेप्सिताः

When Shakra, Indra, prevented rains in my land, country, at that time, I used my arrows and tore apart all the clouds, and we had rains as much as we wanted. I am so powerful, and the devaas cannot do anything to me.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

madrāṣṭre vāritā vṛṣṭiryadā śakreṇa kiṃ tadā . madbāṇabhinnairjaladairāpo’muktā yathepsitāḥ

Śrīdhara

mad-rāṣtra iti . kim āpo āmuktā iti cchedaḥ . nāpo muktā iti vā pāṭhaḥ ||७|| kim urvyām avanī-pālā mad-bāhu-bala-bhīravaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मद्-राष्त्र इति । किम् आपो आमुक्ता इति च्छेदः । नापो मुक्ता इति वा पाठः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

When Shakra, Indra, prevented rains in my land, country, at that time, I used my arrows and tore apart all the clouds, and we had rains as much as we wanted. I am so powerful, and the devaas cannot do anything to me.

VP 5.4.8

किम् उर्व्याम् अवनी-पाला मद्-बाहु-बल-भीरवः।
ते८८सर्वे सन्नतिं याता जरासन्धम् ऋते गुरुम्

All the kings on this earth, what can they do? They are already very scared of my powers. They all bow down to me, and except my father in law, Jaraasandha.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kimurvyāmavanīpālā madbāhubalabhīravaḥ . te sarve’sannatiṃ yātā jarāsaṃdhamṛte guram

Śrīdhara

kim ūrvyām iti . guruṃ śvaśuraṃ jarāsandham ṛte kiṃ sarve sannatiṃ na yātāḥ . te sarve sannatim iti pāṭhe asannatim iti pada-cchedaḥ ||८|| amareṣu mamāvajñā jāyate daitya-puṅgavāḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

किम् ऊर्व्याम् इति । गुरुं श्वशुरं जरासन्धम् ऋते किं सर्वे सन्नतिं न याताः । ते सर्वे सन्नतिम् इति पाठे असन्नतिम् इति पद-च्छेदः ॥८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

All the kings on this earth, what can they do? They are already very scared of my powers. They all bow down to me, and except my father in law, Jaraasandha.

VP 5.4.9

अमरेषु ममावज्ञा जायते दैत्यपुङ्गवाः।
हास्यं मे जायते वीरास्तेषु यत्नपरेष्वपि

I just don't care of the gods, I neglect all the gods, O daityaas. I laugh at all the efforts that they are making. Whatever efforts they are putting, looking at that, I laugh at them. What they are doing is laughable.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

amareṣu mamāvajñā jāyate daityapuṅgavāḥ . hāsyaṃ me jāyate vīrāsteṣu yatnapareṣvapi

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.4.10

तथापि खलु दुष्टानां तेषाम् अप्य् अधिकं मया।
अपकाराय दैत्येन्द्रा यतनीयं दुरात्मनाम्

Even then, because they are all bad people, we have to put some effort to trouble, harm them. We have to put effort to trouble them.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tathā’pi khalu duṣṭānāṃ teṣāmapyadhikaṃ mayā . apakārāya daityendrā yatanīyaṃ durātmanām

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.4.11

तद्ये यशस्विनः केचित्पृथिव्यां ये च यज्वनः।
कार्यो देवापकाराय तेषां सर्वात्मना वधः

In order to ridicule all the gods, we have to kill all those who are very successful, those who are performing yaaga, in this world.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tadye yaśasvinaḥ kecitpṛthivyāṃ ye ca yajvanaḥ . kāryo devāpakārāya teṣāṃ sarvātmanā vadhaḥ

Śrīdhara

tad iti . yaśasvino dāna-niṣṭhāḥ devāpakārāyeti teṣāṃ vadho havir-dātṝṇām abhāvāt devānāṃ vṛttinā śāt upakāraḥ syāt . tasmād itaḥ pradānaṃ devā upajīvanti iti śruteḥ ||११|| utpannaś cāpi me mṛtyur bhūta-pūrvaḥ sa vai kila .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तद् इति । यशस्विनो दान-निष्ठाः देवापकारायेति तेषां वधो हविर्-दातॄणाम् अभावात् देवानां वृत्तिना शात् उपकारः स्यात् । तस्माद् इतः प्रदानं देवा उपजीवन्ति इति श्रुतेः ॥११॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

In order to ridicule all the gods, we have to kill all those who are very successful, those who are performing yaaga, in this world.

VP 5.4.12

उत्पन्नश्चापि मे मृत्युर्भूतपूर्वःस वै किल।
इत्येतद्दारिका प्राह देवकीगर्भसंभवा

And the child which was born to Devaki, that one who is going to kill me, is already born; and even earlier, he had killed me. He is now born again is what the child told me.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

utpannaścāpi me mṛtyurbhūtapūrvaḥsa vai kila . ityetaddārikā prāha devakīgarbhasaṃbhavā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.4.13

तस्माद्बालेषु च परो यत्नः कार्यो महीतले।
यत्रोद्रिक्तं बलं बाले स हन्तव्यः प्रयत्नतः

So, we have to have an eye on all the children also, and wherever you see some child having some extraordinary powers, you have to kill that child also.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmādbāleṣu ca paro yatnaḥ kāryo mahītale . yatrodriktaṃ balaṃ bāle sa hantavyaḥ prayatnataḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.4.14

इत्य् आज्ञाप्यासुरान् कंसः प्रविश्याशु गृहं९० ततः।
मुमोच वसुदेवं च देवकीं च निरोधतः

Having ordered all the demons who were there with him, he quickly entered into his house, and he freed Vasudeva and Devaki from imprisonment.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityājñāpyāsurānkasaḥ praviśyāśu gṛhaṃ tataḥ . mumoca vasudevaṃ ca devakīṃ ca nirodhataḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.4.15

युवयोर्घातिता गर्भा वृथैवैते मयाऽधुना।
कोऽप्यन्य एव नाशाय मम गर्भःसमुद्यतः

Kamsa continued - I have troubled you unnecessarily, and this has served no purpose. I have killed all your children. Someone else, I don't know who, is already born in order to kill me.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yuvayorghātitā garbhā vṛthaivaite mayā’dhunā . ko’pyanya eva nāśāya mama garbhaḥsamudyataḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.4.16

तद् अलं परितापेन नूनं तद्-भाविनो हि ते।
अर्भका युवयोर् दोषाच् चायुषो यद् वियोजिताः

Don't feel bad because all your children were destined to die like that. They had some defect, and because of their karmaas, they were destined to be killed like that.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tadalaṃ paritāpena nunaṃ tadbhāvino hi te . arbhakā yuvayordoṣāccāyuṣo yadviyojitāḥ

Śrīdhara

tad iti . tat-sadyo-maraṇaṃ bhāvi yeṣāṃ te tad-bhāvinaḥ ||१६|| śrī-parāśara uvāca— ity āśvāsya vimucyaiva९२ kaṃsas tau pariśaṅkitaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तद् इति । तत्-सद्यो-मरणं भावि येषां ते तद्-भाविनः ॥१६॥

श्री-पराशर उवाच—

Śrīdhara commentary.

Don't feel bad because all your children were destined to die like that. They had some defect, and because of their karmaas, they were destined to be killed like that.

VP 5.4.17

इत्य् आश्वास्य विमुच्यैव९२ कंसस् तौ परिशङ्कितः।
अन्तर्-गृहं द्विज-श्रेष्ठ प्रविवेश ततः स्वकम्

Sri Paraasharar - Having told them like that, and still having doubts about who is born to kill him, he was always troubled in his mind, he entered into his house after that. This completes Chapter Four. We just saw Chapter 4 of Amsha 5, where Kamsa recalls the words of Durgaa that the person who kills him is already born, so he calls all the asuraas Pralamba, Dhenuka, Keshi, Pootanaa, and others, and tells them to be cautions and watch out, and says that he is not scared of Indra or Hari. He is so arrogant with his powers that nobody can do any harm to him. He says that even then, we have to be very careful and cautious, because the gods are always trying to kill him. Any child, if seen with extraordinary powers, just kill that child, he instructs them. Then he frees Devaki and Vasudeva and then retires. The story of Krishna continues now, the Krishnaavataara.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityāśvāsya vimuktvā ca kaṃsastau pariśaṅkitaḥ . antargṛhaṃ dvijaśreṣṭha praviveśa tataḥ svakam

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.
