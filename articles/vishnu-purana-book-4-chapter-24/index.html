VP 4.24.1

चतुर्थांशे चतुर्विंशोध्यायः योऽयं रिपुञ्जयो नाम बार्हद्रथोऽन्त्यः तस्यामात्यो मुनिको नाम भविष्यति

Sri Paraasharar - Ripunjaya, the last king of the descendants of Bruhadratha, will have a minister by name Munika.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

caturathāṃśe — Sri; caturaviṃśodhayāyaḥ — Paraasharar; yo'yaṃ — Ripunjaya; ripuñajayo — last; nāma — of; bārahadaratho'natayaḥ — descendants; tasayāmātayo — Bruhadratha; muniko — have; nāma — minister; bhaviṣayati — name

Śrīdhara

caturaviṃśe — Sri; kaleradharamasaṅakhaye — Sri; kaṣudarāsatathā — Paraasharar; naṛpāḥ — -; . — Ripunjaya; bhūmeragītāvaśikaṣā — the; ca — the; vairāgayāya — last; nirūpitāḥ — king; . — of; idānīnatu — the; tadavaṃśayāneva — descendants; bhūbhujaḥ — descendants; kālenālapabalapauruṣāna — of; malīmasāna — Bruhadratha; dasayuparāyāṃśacāha—yo'yama — will; itaya-ādinā — have; sarave — a; paṛthivayāṃ — a; bhūbhujo — minister; bhaviṣayanati — by; itayanatena — name; ||1|| — Munika

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

caturathāṃśe caturaviṃśodhayāyaḥ yo'yaṃ ripuñajayo nāma bārahadaratho'natayaḥ tasayāmātayo muniko nāma bhaviṣayati ||1||

Śrīdhara

caturaviṃśe kaleradharamasaṅakhaye kaṣudarāsatathā naṛpāḥ . bhūmeragītāvaśikaṣā ca vairāgayāya nirūpitāḥ . idānīnatu tadavaṃśayāneva bhūbhujaḥ kālenālapabalapauruṣāna malīmasāna dasayuparāyāṃśacāha—yo'yama itaya-ādinā sarave paṛthivayāṃ bhūbhujo bhaviṣayanati itayanatena ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

चतुर्विंशे कलेर्धर्मसङ्ख्ये क्षुद्रास्तथा नृपाः । भूमेर्गीतावशिक्षा च वैराग्याय निरूपिताः । इदानीन्तु तद्वंश्यानेव भूभुजः कालेनाल्पबलपौरुषान् मलीमसान् दस्युप्रायांश्चाह—योऽयम् इत्य्-आदिना सर्वे पृथिव्यां भूभुजो भविष्यन्ति इत्यन्तेन ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - Ripunjaya, the last king of the descendants of Bruhadratha, will have a minister by name Munika.
