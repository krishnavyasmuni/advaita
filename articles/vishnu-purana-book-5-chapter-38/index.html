VP 5.38.1

अर्जुनोऽपि तदाऽन्वीक्ष्य रामकृष्णकलेवरे । संस्कारं लंभयामास तथाऽन्येषामनुक्रमात्

Sri Paraasharar - Arjuna saw the bodies of Krishna and Balaraama, and he wanted to do samskaara, and as well as for others also.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

arjuno’pi tadānviṣya rāmakṛṣṇakalevare . saṃskāraṃ lambhayāmāsa tathānyeṣāmanukramāt — Sri Paraasharar - Arjuna saw the bodies of Krishna and Balaraama, and he wanted to do samskaara, and as well as for others also.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

arjuno’pi tadā’nvīkṣya rāmakṛṣṇakalevare . saṃskāraṃ laṃbhayāmāsa tathā’nyeṣāmanukramāt

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.38.3

रेवती चापि रामस्य देहमाश्लिष्य सत्तमा । विवेश ज्वलितं वह्निं तत्संगाह्लादशीतलम्

Revati also embraced Balaraama's deha, and entered into the burning fire and it became very cool because of the happiness which she attained, because of sanga of Balaraama.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

revatī cāpi rāmasya dehamāśliṣya sattamā . viveśa jvalitaṃ vahniṃ tatsaṅgāhlādaśītalam — Revati also embraced Balaraama's deha, and entered into the burning fire and it became very cool because of the happiness which she attained, because of sanga of Balaraama.

Śrīdhara

revatīti . tat-saṅgena ya āhlādaḥ tena śītala-prāyam — Revati also embraced Balaraama's deha, and entered into the burning fire and it became very cool because of the happiness which she attained, because of sanga of Balaraama.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

revatī cāpi rāmasya dehamāśliṣya sattamā . viveśa jvalitaṃ vahniṃ tatsaṃgāhlādaśītalam

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

रेवतीति । तत्-सङ्गेन य आह्लादः तेन शीतल-प्रायम् ॥३॥ उग्रसेनस् तु तच् छ्रुत्वा तथैवानकदुन्दुभिः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Revati also embraced Balaraama's deha, and entered into the burning fire and it became very cool because of the happiness which she attained, because of sanga of Balaraama.

VP 5.38.5

ततोऽर्जुनः प्रेतकार्यं कृत्वा तेषां यथाविधि । निश्चक्राम जनं सर्वं गृहीत्वा वज्रमेव च

Arjuna did the preta kaarya for all of them, as prescribed in the shaastraas, and then, taking all the people of Dwaarakaa along with him, and also along with Vajra, who was supposed to be the future king, left Dwaarakaa puri.

Word-for-word

Śrīdhara

śravaṇādi-phalaṃ prakaṭayati—yaś ceti — Arjuna did the preta kaarya for all of them, as prescribed in the shaastraas, and then, taking all the people of Dwaarakaa along with him, and also along with Vajra, who was supposed to be the future king, left Dwaarakaa puri.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

śravaṇādi-phalaṃ prakaṭayati—yaś ceti ||९४|| || iti śrīdhara-svāmi-viracitātma-prakāśākhya-śrī-viṣṇu-mahāpurāṇa-vyākhyāyāṃ pañcamāṃśe aṣṭātriṃśo’dhyāyaḥ || iti śrī-viṣṇu-purāṇe pañcame’ṃśe aṣṭatriṃśo’dhyāyaḥ ||३८|| —o)०(o— iti śrī-parāśara-muni-viracite śrī-viṣṇu-puratva-nirṇāyake śrīmati-viṣṇu-mahā-purāṇe pañcamo’ṃśaḥ samāptaḥ || ५ ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

श्रवणादि-फलं प्रकटयति—यश् चेति ॥९४॥ ॥ इति श्रीधर-स्वामि-विरचितात्म-प्रकाशाख्य-श्री-विष्णु-महापुराण-व्याख्यायां पञ्चमांशे अष्टात्रिंशोऽध्यायः ॥ इति श्री-विष्णु-पुराणे पञ्चमेऽंशे अष्टत्रिंशोऽध्यायः ॥३८॥ —ओ)०(ओ— इति श्री-पराशर-मुनि-विरचिते श्री-विष्णु-पुरत्व-निर्णायके श्रीमति-विष्णु-महा-पुराणे पञ्चमोऽंशः समाप्तः ॥ ५ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Arjuna did the preta kaarya for all of them, as prescribed in the shaastraas, and then, taking all the people of Dwaarakaa along with him, and also along with Vajra, who was supposed to be the future king, left Dwaarakaa puri.

VP 5.38.9

प्लावयामास तां शून्यां द्वारकां च महोदधिः । वासुदेवगृहं त्वेकं न प्लावयति सागरः
