VP 5.15.1

ककुद्मति हतेऽरिष्टे धेनुके विनिपातिते।
प्रलंबे निधनं नीते धृते गोवर्धनाचले

Sri Paraasharar - Demon Arishta, who came in the form of a ferocious bull, was killed. Dhenuka was killed. Pralamba was killed. Krishna held up the Govardhana mountain like an umbrella, in one hand.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
kakudmati hate ariṣṭe — when Ariṣṭa was slain;
dhenuke vinipātite — when Dhenuka was struck down;
pralambe nidhanaṃ nīte — when Pralamba was brought to death;
dhṛte govardhanācale — when Govardhana mountain was held
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kakudmati hate’riṣṭe dhenuke vinipātite . pralaṃbe nidhanaṃ nīte dhṛte govardhanācale

Śrīdhara

vasato gokule citraṃ karma kṛṣṇasya varṇitam . athāha mathurākṛtyaṃ prastotuṃ nāradāgamam || tad evaṃ gokule rāsa-krīḍā-vinodaiḥ kaṃsa-vadhād-deva-kāryaṃ hariṇā vismṛtam ivālakṣya tat-smaraṇa-prasaṅgāya nāradāgamanam āha—kakudmati iti . kakudmati vṛṣabhākṛtau daiteye ||१|| damite kāliye nāge bhagne tuṅga-druma-dvaye .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वसतो गोकुले चित्रं कर्म कृष्णस्य वर्णितम् ।
अथाह मथुराकृत्यं प्रस्तोतुं नारदागमम् ॥
तद् एवं गोकुले रास-क्रीडा-विनोदैः कंस-वधाद्-देव-कार्यं हरिणा विस्मृतम् इवालक्ष्य तत्-स्मरण-प्रसङ्गाय नारदागमनम् आह—ककुद्मति इति । ककुद्मति वृषभाकृतौ दैतेये ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - Demon Arishta, who came in the form of a ferocious bull, was killed. Dhenuka was killed. Pralamba was killed. Krishna held up the Govardhana mountain like an umbrella, in one hand.

VP 5.15.2

दमिते कालिये नागे भग्नेतुङ्गद्रुमद्वये।
हतायां पूतनायां च शकटे परिवर्तिते

The Kaaliya serpent was subdued. Yamalaarjuna, who were in the form of two trees, were liberated from their curse. Pootanaa was killed, and Shakataasura was also killed. All these are the leelaa cheshtitaas of Sri Krishna. All these were done.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
damite kāliye nāge — when the serpent Kāliya was subdued;
bhagne tuṅga-druma-dvaye — when the two lofty trees were broken;
hatāyāṃ pūtanāyāṃ ca — when Pūtanā too was slain;
śakaṭe parivartite — when the cart was overturned
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

damite kāliye nāge bhagnetuṅgadrumadvaye . hatāyāṃ pūtanāyāṃ ca śakaṭe parivartite

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.15.3

कंसाय नारदः प्राह यथावृत्तमनुक्रमात्।
यशोदादेवकीगर्भपरिवृत्ताद्यशेषतः

Naarada goes to Kamsa and narrates all these things right from the beginning. Right from how Yashoda's garbha was brought and kept in Devaki's womb, and how Devaki's garbha was transferred to Yashoda, everything Naarada relates to Kamsa in order.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathāvṛttam anukramāt — what occurred, in sequence;
yaśodā-devakī-garbha-parivṛtty-ādi — beginning with the exchange of Yaśodā’s and Devakī’s embryos;
aśeṣataḥ — completely
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kaṃsāya nāradaḥ prāha yathāvṛttamanukramāt . yaśodādevakīgarbhaparivṛttādyaśeṣataḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.15.6

यावन्न बलमारूढौ रामकृष्णौ सुबालकौ।
तावदेव मया वध्यावसाध्यौ रूढयौवनौ

Having come to know all these events, Kamsa thought that before Sri Krishna and Balaraama become powerful, before they attain their youth, he has to get rid of them, kill them. Otherwise, it will be impossible to kill them, once they attain youth, Kamsa thought.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
yāvan na balam ārūḍhau — until they have attained strength;
rāma-kṛṣṇau su-bālakau — Rāma and Kṛṣṇa, very young;
tāvad eva mayā vadhyau — until then they must be slain by me;
asādhyau rūḍha-yauvanau — having reached youth, they would be unconquerable
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yāvanna balamārūḍhau rāmakṛṣṇau subālakau . tāvadeva mayā vadhyāvasādhyau rūḍhayauvanau

Śrīdhara

kaṃsasya manoratha-prakāram āha—yāvad ity-ādi ṣaḍbhiḥ ||६|| cāṇūro’tra mahā-vīryo muṣṭikaś ca mahā-balaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कंसस्य मनोरथ-प्रकारम् आह—यावद् इत्य्-आदि षड्भिः ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Having come to know all these events, Kamsa thought that before Sri Krishna and Balaraama become powerful, before they attain their youth, he has to get rid of them, kill them. Otherwise, it will be impossible to kill them, once they attain youth, Kamsa thought.

VP 5.15.13

कंस उवाच
भो भो दानपते वाक्यं क्रियतां प्रीतये मम।
इतः स्यन्दनमारुह्य गम्यतां नन्दगोकुलम्

Kamsa - O Daanapati Akroora, I am going to tell you, and it is going to please me. You please follow my orders, do as I say. Please go to Nandagokula, taking your chariot. Akroora was known as Daanapati, as he was in possession of the Syamantaka Mani, he was doing a lot of daana, charity.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gamyatāṃ nanda-gokulam — go to Nanda’s Gokula
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bho bho dānapate vākyaṃ kriyatāṃ prītaye mama . itaḥ syandanamāruhya gamyatāṃ nandagokulam

Śrīdhara

svābhiprāyam āvedayan kaṃso’krūram ādiśati—bho bho ity-ādi daśabhiḥ||१३|| vasudeva-sutau tatra viṣṇor aṃśa-samudbhavau .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्वाभिप्रायम् आवेदयन् कंसोऽक्रूरम् आदिशति—भो भो इत्य्-आदि दशभिः॥१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Kamsa - O Daanapati Akroora, I am going to tell you, and it is going to please me. You please follow my orders, do as I say. Please go to Nandagokula, taking your chariot. Akroora was known as Daanapati, as he was in possession of the Syamantaka Mani, he was doing a lot of daana, charity.
