VP 5.15.2

दमिते कालिये नागे भग्नेतुङ्गद्रुमद्वये । हतायां पूतनायां च शकटे परिवर्तिते

दलमते कालिये नागे भ्ने तुङ्गद्ुमद्र्वये | हतायाां पूतनायां च शकटे पररर्वततिते ॥ २ ॥ The Kaaliya serpent was subdued. Yamalaarjuna, who were in the form of two trees, were liberated from their curse. Pootanaa was killed, and Shakataasura was also killed. All these are the leelaa cheshtitaas of Sri Krishna. All these were done.
