VP 2.3.1

श्रीपराशर उवाच— उत्तरं यत् समुद्रस्य हिमाद्रेश् चैव दक्षिणम् । वर्षं तद् भारतं नाम भारती यत्र सन्ततिः

To the north of the ocean and to the south of Himavat lies the region called Bhārata, where the descendants of Bharata dwell.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrīparāśara uvāca— uttaraṃ yat samudrasya himādreś caiva dakṣiṇam । varṣaṃ tad bhārataṃ nāma bhāratī yatra santatiḥ — To the north of the ocean and to the south of Himavat lies the region called Bhārata, where the descendants of Bharata dwell.

Śrīdhara

jambūdvīpasya saṃsthānam uktaṃ varṣa-vibhāgataḥ, tatrāpi bhārataṃ śreṣṭham āha | tatra ca puṃ-vapuḥ | bhāratī bharatasya santatiḥ — Uttaram iti | Samudrasya puraatanasya na tu sagara suta khaatasya | Bhaaratee bharatasya raajnyah sambandhinee | Samudra is existing for a long time; it is not the one formed by the digging by Sagara's sons. Bhaaratee means related to King Bharata.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

VP 2.3.2

नव-योजन-साहस्रो विस्तारोऽस्य महामुने । कर्म-भूमिर् इयं स्वर्गम् अपवर्गं च गच्छताम्

Its extent is nine thousand yojanas, O great sage; it is the land of action for those who seek heaven and liberation.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nava-yojana-sāhasro vistāro’sya mahāmune । karma-bhūmir iyaṃ svargam apavargaṃ ca gacchatām — Its extent is nine thousand yojanas, O great sage; it is the land of action for those who seek heaven and liberation.

Śrīdhara

atraiva śreyaḥ-sādhanam anuṣṭheyam anyatra tad asambhavād iti darśayann āha—karma-bhūmir iti | svargam apavargaṃ ca gacchatāṃ puṃsām ubhaya-sādhana-bhūtānāṃ pravṛtta-nivṛtta-karmaṇāṃ bhūmir iyaṃ kimputtamādi-varṣāṣṭake puṣkara-dvīpe ca karmaṇa evābhāvāt | plakṣādi-dvīpa-pañcake kevalaṃ nivṛttam eva karma, nānyat | vakṣyati hi— yathokta-karma-kartṛtvāt svādhikāra-kṣayāya hi | tatra te tu kuśa-dvīpe brahma-bhūtaṃ janārdanam | yajantaḥ kṣapayanty agram adhikāraṃ phala-pradam ॥ \[vi.pu. 2.4.40\] iti — Nava yojana iti | Vistaarah dakshina uttaratah; aayaamah tu hjimavat paarshve himavataa samah, dakshinato dakshinatah kinchit kinchit nyoonah | 9000 yojanaas is the spread from south to north. At the bottom of the Himavaan mountain, the breadth is the same as Himavaan mountain. As you go down towards south, it gets lesser and lesser; so it is tapering down.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nava-yojana-sāhasro vistāro’sya mahāmune । karma-bhūmir iyaṃ svargam apavargaṃ ca gacchatām

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अत्रैव श्रेयः-साधनम् अनुष्ठेयम् अन्यत्र तद् असम्भवाद् इति दर्शयन्न् आह—कर्म-भूमिर् इति । स्वर्गम् अपवर्गं च गच्छतां पुंसाम् उभय-साधन-भूतानां प्रवृत्त-निवृत्त-कर्मणां भूमिर् इयं किम्पुत्तमादि-वर्षाष्टके पुष्कर-द्वीपे च कर्मण एवाभावात् । प्लक्षादि-द्वीप-पञ्चके केवलं निवृत्तम् एव कर्म, नान्यत् । वक्ष्यति हि— यथोक्त-कर्म-कर्तृत्वात् स्वाधिकार-क्षयाय हि । तत्र ते तु कुश-द्वीपे ब्रह्म-भूतं जनार्दनम् । यजन्तः क्षपयन्त्य् अग्रम् अधिकारं फल-प्रदम् ॥ \[वि.पु. २.४.४०\] इति

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges २).

Nava yojana iti | Vistaarah dakshina uttaratah; aayaamah tu hjimavat paarshve himavataa samah, dakshinato dakshinatah kinchit kinchit nyoonah | 9000 yojanaas is the spread from south to north. At the bottom of the Himavaan mountain, the breadth is the same as Himavaan mountain. As you go down towards south, it gets lesser and lesser; so it is tapering down.

VP 2.3.3

महेन्द्रो मलयः सह्यः शुक्तिमान् ऋक्ष-पर्वतः । विन्ध्यश् च पारिपात्रश् च सप्तात्र कुल-पर्वताः

Mahendra, Malaya, Sahya, Śuktimān, Ṛkṣa, Vindhya, and Pāriyātra are the seven family mountains here.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mahendro malayaḥ sahyaḥ śuktimān ṛkṣa-parvataḥ । vindhyaś ca pāripātraś ca saptātra kula-parvatāḥ — Mahendra, Malaya, Sahya, Śuktimān, Ṛkṣa, Vindhya, and Pāriyātra are the seven family mountains here.

Śrīdhara

samasta-puruṣārtha-sādhana-gaṅgādi-puṇya-nadīnāṃ janma-bhūmayaḥ śailāś cātraiva santīty āha—mahendra iti — samasta-puruṣārtha-sādhana — means of all human aims; gaṅgādi-puṇya-nadīnām — of sacred rivers beginning with the Gaṅgā; janma-bhūmayaḥ — birthplaces; śailāḥ — mountains; ca atra eva santi — are here indeed; iti āha — he says; Mahendra iti — “Mahendra and so on.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

mahendro malayaḥ sahyaḥ śuktimān ṛkṣa-parvataḥ । vindhyaś ca pāripātraś ca saptātra kula-parvatāḥ

samasta-puruṣārtha-sādhana-gaṅgādi-puṇya-nadīnāṃ janma-bhūmayaḥ śailāś cātraiva santīty āha—mahendra iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

समस्त-पुरुषार्थ-साधन-गङ्गादि-पुण्य-नदीनां जन्म-भूमयः शैलाश् चात्रैव सन्तीत्य् आह—महेन्द्र इति

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges ३).

He says that the mountains here are the birthplaces of the sacred rivers, such as the Gaṅgā, which accomplish all the aims of human life: “Mahendra and the rest.”

VP 2.3.4

अतः सम्प्राप्यते स्वर्गो मुक्तिम् अस्मात् प्रयान्ति वै । तिर्यक्त्वं नरं चापि यान्त्य् अतः पुरुषा मुने

From it heaven is attained; from it they proceed to liberation; from it, O sage, people also go to animal existence and to human birth.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ataḥ samprāpyate svargo muktim asmāt prayānti vai । tiryaktvaṃ naraṃ cāpi yānty ataḥ puruṣā mune — From it heaven is attained; from it they proceed to liberation; from it, O sage, people also go to animal existence and to human birth.

Śrīdhara

tasmāt sarvaṃ śubhāśubham atreti anenaiva karmaṇā prāpyata ity āha—ata iti | ato'smād varṣāt svarga-lokaḥ samprāpyate | krama-muktiṃ cāsmāt prayānti — Ata iti | Ato muktim prayaanti cha iti vachanam asmin bhaarata varshe muktih syaat iti etaavat vaktum; na tu anyatra nisheddhum; karma bhoomeh anyatra api brahma vidyaa saadhyasya mokshasya upapatteh | Sootra kaarena Taduparyapi ityaadinaa sootrena devaadeenaam api brahma vidyaa adhikaarasya uktatvaat | In this Bhaarata varsha, people can perform suitable karmaas and attain liberation. It is said that they can attain moksha from Bhaarata varsha. Does not mean that anyone who has to attain moksha has to be born here, and then only attain moksha. It does not negate people attaining moksha in other places, but this is told to highlight the greatness of Bhaarata varsha. Even in other places which are not said to be karma bhoomi, in order to attain moksha, one has to practice Brahma vidyaa; this Brahma vidyaa can be practices even in other places. Even devataas are eligible for adopting Brahma vidyaa, and can attain moksha; this is established clearly in the Brahma Sutraas, as told by Sootra kaara Baadaraayana. People in other lokaas can also attain moksha, it is told; because they have a desire to attain, they have the capability to do Brahma vidyaa, so it is possible for them also; they also have adhikaara for Brahma vidyaa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmāt sarvaṃ śubhāśubham atreti anenaiva karmaṇā prāpyata ity āha—ata iti | ato'smād varṣāt svarga-lokaḥ samprāpyate | krama-muktiṃ cāsmāt prayānti

VP 2.3.5

इतः स्वर्गश् च मोक्षश् च मध्यश् चान्तश् च गम्यते । न खल्व् अन्यत्र मर्त्यानां कर्म भूमौ विधीयते

From here heaven, liberation, the intermediate world, and the lower world are reached; for mortals, action is not enjoined elsewhere as it is in the land of action.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

itaḥ svargaś ca mokṣaś ca madhyaś cāntaś ca gamyate । na khalv anyatra martyānāṃ karma bhūmau vidhīyate — From here heaven, liberation, the intermediate world, and the lower world are reached; for mortals, action is not enjoined elsewhere as it is in the land of action.

Śrīdhara

svargaś ca bhauma ilāvṛtādi । mokṣaś ca sadyo-muktiḥ । madhyamāntarīkṣa-lokāḥ । antaḥ pātālādir ita eva gamyate, nānyataḥ । tat-sādhanasyātraiva sulabhatvāt । yasmād anyatra bhūmau varṣāntarādau śāstreṇa karma na vidhīyate — svargaḥ — heaven; bhauma-ilāvṛtādi — earthly worlds beginning with Ilāvṛta; mokṣaḥ — liberation; sadyo-muktiḥ — immediate liberation; madhya — intermediate; antarīkṣa-lokāḥ — atmospheric worlds; antaḥ — lower worlds; pātālādiḥ — Pātāla and the rest; ita eva — from here alone; gamyate — is reached; na anyataḥ — not elsewhere; tat-sādhanasya — of its means; atra eva sulabhatvāt — because they are readily available here; anyatra bhūmau karma na vidhīyate — action is not enjoined elsewhere on earth.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

itaḥ svargaś ca mokṣaś ca madhyaś cāntaś ca gamyate । na khalv anyatra martyānāṃ karma bhūmau vidhīyate

svargaś ca bhauma ilāvṛtādi । mokṣaś ca sadyo-muktiḥ । madhyamāntarīkṣa-lokāḥ । antaḥ pātālādir ita eva gamyate, nānyataḥ । tat-sādhanasyātraiva sulabhatvāt । yasmād anyatra bhūmau varṣāntarādau śāstreṇa karma na vidhīyate

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्वर्गश् च भौम इलावृतादि । मोक्षश् च सद्यो-मुक्तिः । मध्यमान्तरीक्ष-लोकाः । अन्तः पातालादिर् इत एव गम्यते, नान्यतः । तत्-साधनस्यात्रैव सुलभत्वात् । यस्माद् अन्यत्र भूमौ वर्षान्तरादौ शास्त्रेण कर्म न विधीयते

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges ५).

He explains that heaven, the earthly worlds beginning with Ilāvṛta, liberation, the intermediate worlds, and the lower worlds are reached from here, because their means are readily available here; elsewhere, scripture does not enjoin action in the other regions.

VP 2.3.7

अयं तु नवमस् तेषां द्वीपः सागर-संवृतः । योजनानां सहस्रं तु द्वीपोऽयं दक्षिणोत्तरात्

The ninth among them is this island, surrounded by the ocean; it extends a thousand yojanas from south to north.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ayaṃ tu navamas teṣāṃ dvīpaḥ sāgara-saṃvṛtaḥ । yojanānāṃ sahasraṃ tu dvīpo’yaṃ dakṣiṇottarāt — The ninth among them is this island, surrounded by the ocean; it extends a thousand yojanas from south to north.

Śrīdhara

ayaṃ tv iti । samudra-prānta-vartīti dvīpaḥ samudreṇaikī-bhūtena sāgareṇa sagara-suta-khātena saṃvṛtaḥ iti jñeyam । sāmānyataḥ sagara-suta-khāta-sāgara-saṃvṛtatvaṃ sahasra-yojanāntaraḥ pratyekam ṛte ilāvṛtam anyeṣām api asty eva । yad āha vāyuḥ— bhāratasyāsya varṣasya nava-bhedān nibodhata । parvatāntaritā jñeyās te tv agamyāḥ parasparam ॥ iti — ayam tu dvīpaḥ — this island; samudra-prānta-vartī — situated at the ocean’s edge; sāgara-saṃvṛtaḥ — surrounded by the ocean; sagara-suta-khātaḥ — dug by Sagara’s sons; sahasra-yojanāntaraḥ — separated by a thousand yojanas; parvatāntaritāḥ — separated by mountains; agamyāḥ parasparam — inaccessible to one another.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ayaṃ tu navamas teṣāṃ dvīpaḥ sāgara-saṃvṛtaḥ । yojanānāṃ sahasraṃ tu dvīpo’yaṃ dakṣiṇottarāt

ayaṃ tv iti । samudra-prānta-vartīti dvīpaḥ samudreṇaikī-bhūtena sāgareṇa sagara-suta-khātena saṃvṛtaḥ iti jñeyam । sāmānyataḥ sagara-suta-khāta-sāgara-saṃvṛtatvaṃ sahasra-yojanāntaraḥ pratyekam ṛte ilāvṛtam anyeṣām api asty eva । yad āha vāyuḥ— bhāratasyāsya varṣasya nava-bhedān nibodhata । parvatāntaritā jñeyās te tv agamyāḥ parasparam ॥ iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अयं त्व् इति । समुद्र-प्रान्त-वर्तीति द्वीपः समुद्रेणैकी-भूतेन सागरेण सगर-सुत-खातेन संवृतः इति ज्ञेयम् । सामान्यतः सगर-सुत-खात-सागर-संवृतत्वं सहस्र-योजनान्तरः प्रत्येकम् ऋते इलावृतम् अन्येषाम् अपि अस्त्य् एव । यद् आह वायुः— भारतस्यास्य वर्षस्य नव-भेदान् निबोधत । पर्वतान्तरिता ज्ञेयास् ते त्व् अगम्याः परस्परम् ॥ इति

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges ७–१२).

“This island” means Bhārata, situated at the ocean’s edge and surrounded by the ocean dug by Sagara’s sons. In general the other divisions too are separated by that ocean at intervals of a thousand yojanas, except Ilāvṛta; as the Vāyu says, the nine divisions are separated by mountains and cannot reach one another.

VP 2.3.8

पूर्वे किराता यस्य स्युः पश्चिमे यवनाः स्थिताः । ब्राह्मणाः क्षत्रिया वैश्या मध्ये शूद्राश् च भागशः

At its eastern end are the Kirātas and at its western end the Yavanas; in the middle are Brāhmaṇas, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas, and Śūdras, in their respective portions.

स्वर्ग-देवत्व-प्राप्तेर् अपि अत्र मनुष्यत्वं दुर्लभम् इत्य् अस्मिन्न् अर्थे देव-गीतं प्रमाणयति—गायन्तीति । स्वर्गश् चापवर्गश् च आस्पदं स्थानं तस्य मार्ग-भूते भारताख्ये भूमि-भागे ये पुरुषा भवन्ति, ते तु सुरत्वात् सुरत्वं प्राप्तान् अस्मान् अपि अपेक्ष्य भूयोऽधिकं धन्याः सुकृतिन इत्य् अर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges २४).

To establish that human birth here is rare even after attaining divine status, he cites the song of the gods: those who are born as humans in Bhārata, the land that is the path to heaven and liberation, are more blessed and meritorious than even those who have attained divinity.

VP 2.3.25
