Book 2, Chapter 3

VP 2.3.1

श्रीपराशर उवाच उत्तरं यत्समुद्रस्य हिमाद्रेश्चैव दक्षिणम् । वर्षं तद्भारतं नाम भारती यत्र सन्ततिः

Sri Paraasharar - To the north of the ocean, and to the south of the Himavaan parvata, the place, varsha is called Bhaarata varsha. All are Bhaarateeyaas.

All are Bhaarateeyaas.; varṣaṃ tadbhārataṃ nāma bhāratī yatra santatiḥ — Sri Paraasharar - To the north of the ocean, and to the south of the Himavaan parvata, the place, varsha is called Bhaarata varsha. VP 2.3.2

All the time, in Jamboo Dveepa, the purushaas worship Yajnya Purusha, Bhagavaan. All the people worship Vishnu as Yajnya Purusha Himself, performing various yajnyaas. In other dveepaas, He is worshipped in different forms. Veda says ' Yajnya vai vishnuh'.
