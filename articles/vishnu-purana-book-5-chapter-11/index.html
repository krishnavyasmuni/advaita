VP 5.11.1

मखे प्रतिहते शक्रो मैत्रेयातिरुषान्वितः

श्री पराशरः - मखे प्रततहते शक्रो मैत्रेय अततरुर्षा अस्न्र्वतः | सांर्वतिकां नाम गणां तोयदानामथाब्रर्वीत् ॥ १ ॥ Sri Paraasharar - When Indra saw that his yaaga was stopped, and they did the worship of Govardhana giri, instead of performing the yaaga to worship him, he was extremely angry. Then he immediately called a group of clouds called Samvartaka, and told thus.
