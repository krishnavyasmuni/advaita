VP 1.17.1

मैत्रेय श्रूयतां सम्यक् चरितं तस्य धीमतः । प्रह्लादस्य सदोदारचरितस्य महात्मनः ॥१॥

Listen, Maitreya, to the story of the wise and magnanimous Prahlāda, whose adventures are ever interesting and instructive.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreya — O Maitreya; śrūyatām — let it be heard; caritam — story; dhīmataḥ Prahlādasya — of wise Prahlāda; sad-udāra-caritasya — of noble character; mahātmanaḥ — of the great soul

Śrīdhara

bhagavad-dveṣaḥ — hatred of Bhagavān; tad-bhakta-sva-putra-dveṣe api kāraṇam — also the cause of hatred toward His devotee son; Hiraṇyakaśipoḥ vṛttāntam — the account of Hiraṇyakaśipu

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreya śrūyatāṃ samyak caritaṃ tasya dhīmataḥ. prahlādasya sadodāracaritasya mahātmanaḥ||1||

Śrīdhara

bhagavaddveṣa eva tadbhaktasvaputradveṣe’pi kāraṇam iti vakṣyan hiraṇyakaśipor vṛttāntam āhaditeḥ putra ityādinā||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भगवद्द्वेष एव तद्भक्तस्वपुत्रद्वेषेऽपि कारणम् इति वक्ष्यन् हिरण्यकशिपोर् वृत्तान्तम् आहदितेः पुत्र इत्यादिना ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Hatred of Bhagavān is also the cause of hatred toward His devotee son. The account of Hiraṇyakaśipu begins with “the son of Diti” and the following words.

VP 1.17.2

दितेः पुत्रो महावीर्यो हिरण्यकशिपुः पुरा । त्रैलोक्यं वशम् आनिन्ये ब्रह्मणो वरदर्पितः ॥२॥

Hiraṇyakaśipu, the son of Diti, had formerly brought the three worlds under his authority, confiding in a boon bestowed upon him by Brahmā.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Diteḥ putraḥ — son of Diti; mahāvīryaḥ — of great prowess; Hiraṇyakaśipuḥ — Hiraṇyakaśipu; trai-lokyam — the three worlds; vaśam āninye — brought under control; vara-darpitaḥ — made arrogant by a boon

Śrīdhara

tvad-visṛṣṭebhyaḥ bhūtebhyaḥ — from beings created by you; mṛtyuḥ mā bhūt — may there be no death; anekavara-prārthanā — request for many boons; vara-darpitaḥ — made arrogant by the boons

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

diteḥ putro mahāvīryo hiraṇyakaśipuḥ purā. trailokyaṃ vaśam āninye brahmaṇo varadarpitaḥ||2||

Śrīdhara

bhūtebhyas tvadvisṛṣṭebhyo mṛtyur mābhūn mama prabho ityādyanekavaraprārthane kṛte sati tathaiva brahmaṇā dattair varair darpitaḥ san||2||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भूतेभ्यस् त्वद्विसृष्टेभ्यो मृत्युर् माभून् मम प्रभो इत्याद्यनेकवरप्रार्थने कृते सति तथैव ब्रह्मणा दत्तैर् वरैर् दर्पितः सन् ॥२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Hiraṇyakaśipu became arrogant after asking for many boons, including the request, “May death not come to me from the beings created by you.”

VP 1.17.3

इन्द्रत्वम् अकरोद् दैत्यः स चासीत् सविता स्वयम् । वायुर् अग्निर् अपां नाथः सोमश् चाभन् महासुरः ॥३॥

He had usurped the sovereignty of Indra, and exercised of himself the functions of the sun, of air, of the lord of waters, of fire, and of the moon.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Indratvam akarot — he assumed Indra's office; daityaḥ — the Daitya; savitā svayam — the sun himself; vāyuḥ — air; agniḥ — fire; apām nāthaḥ — lord of waters; Somaḥ — the moon

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

indratvam akarod daityaḥ sa cāsīt savitā svayam. vāyur agnir apāṃ nāthaḥ somaś cābhan mahāsuraḥ||3||

VP 1.17.4

धनानाम् अधिपः सोऽभूत् स एवासीत् स्वयं यमः । यज्ञभागान् अशेषांस् तु स स्वयं बुभुजेऽसुरः ॥४॥

He himself was the god of riches; he was the judge of the dead; and he appropriated to himself, without reserve, all that was offered in sacrifice to the gods.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dhanānām adhipaḥ — lord of riches; Yamaḥ svayam — Yama himself; yajñabhāgān aśeṣān — all sacrificial shares; svayam bubhuje — he consumed himself

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dhanānām adhipaḥ so’bhūt sa evāsīt svayaṃ yamaḥ. yajñabhāgān aśeṣāṃs tu sa svayaṃ bubhuje’suraḥ||4||

VP 1.17.5

देवाः स्वर्गं परित्यज्य तत्त्रासान् मुनिसत्तम । विचेरुर् अवनौ सर्वे बिभ्राणा मानुषीं तनुम् ॥५॥

The deities therefore, flying from their seats in heaven, wandered, through fear of the Daitya, upon the earth, disguised in mortal shapes.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

devāḥ — the gods; svargam parityajya — abandoning heaven; tad-bhayāt — from fear of him; avanau viceruḥ — wandered on earth; mānuṣīṃ tanum bibhrāṇāḥ — assuming human bodies

Śrīdhara

tat-trāsāt — from fear of him; Hiraṇyakaśipoḥ bhayāt — from fear of Hiraṇyakaśipu

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

devāḥ svargaṃ parityajya tattrāsān munisattama. vicerur avanau sarve bibhrāṇā mānuṣīṃ tanum||5||

Śrīdhara

tattrāsāt hiraṇyakaśipor bhayāt||5||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत्त्रासात् हिरण्यकशिपोर् भयात् ॥५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The gods wandered in human form because they were afraid of Hiraṇyakaśipu.

VP 1.17.6

जित्वा त्रिभुवनं सर्वं त्रैलोक्यैश्वर्यदर्पितः । उपगीयमानो गन्धर्वैर् बुभुजे विषयान् प्रियान् ॥६॥

Having conquered the three worlds, he was inflated with pride, and, eulogized by the Gandharvas, enjoyed whatever he desired.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jitvā tribhuvanam — having conquered the three worlds; trailokyaiśvarya-darpitaḥ — inflated with the pride of their sovereignty; upagīyamānaḥ — being praised; viṣayān bubhuje — enjoyed objects of sense

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jitvā tribhuvanaṃ sarvaṃ trailokyaiśvaryadarpitaḥ. upagīyamāno gandharvair bubhuje viṣayān priyān||6||

VP 1.17.7

पानासक्तं महात्मानं हिरण्यकशिपुं तदा । सपासां चक्रिरे सर्वे सिद्धगन्धर्वपन्नगाः ॥७॥

The Gandharvas, the Siddhas, and the snake-gods all attended upon the mighty Hiraṇyakaśipu, as he sat at the banquet.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pānāsaktam — devoted to drinking; Hiraṇyakaśipum — Hiraṇyakaśipu; mahātmānam — the mighty one; siddha-gandharva-pannagāḥ — Siddhas, Gandharvas, and snake-gods; sapāsaṃ cakrire — attended upon him

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pānāsaktaṃ mahātmānaṃ hiraṇyakaśipuṃ tadā. sapāsāṃ cakrire sarve siddhagandharvapannagāḥ||7||

VP 1.17.8

अवादयञ् जगुश् चान्ये जयशब्दान् अथापरे । दैत्यराजस्य पुरतश् चक्रुः सिद्धा मुदान्विताः ॥८॥

The Siddhas delighted stood before him, some playing on musical instruments, some singing songs in his praise, and others shouting cries of victory.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

avādayan — played instruments; jaguḥ — sang; jaya-śabdān — cries of victory; daitya-rājasya purataḥ — before the Daitya king; mudānvitāḥ — filled with joy

Śrīdhara

avādayan — they played instruments; vādyādīni — instruments and the rest; jaguḥ gītam — they sang songs

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

avādayañ jaguś cānye jayaśabdān athāpare. daityarājasya purataś cakruḥ siddhā mudānvitāḥ||8||

Śrīdhara

avādayan vādyādīni jagur gītam||8||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अवादयन् वाद्यादीनि जगुर् गीतम् ॥८॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Some played instruments and others sang songs.

VP 1.17.9

तथा प्रनृत्याप्सरसि स्फटिकाभ्रमयेऽसुरः । पपौ पानं मुदा युक्तः प्रासादे सुमनोहरे ॥९॥

The nymphs of heaven danced gracefully in the crystal palace, where the Asura with pleasure quaffed the inebriating cup.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

apsarasaḥ pranṛtyuḥ — the nymphs danced; sphaṭikābhramaye prāsāde — in the crystal palace; asuraḥ — the Asura; pānam papau — drank; mudā yuktaḥ — filled with pleasure

Śrīdhara

prakṛṣṭaṃ nṛtyam — excellent dancing; apsarasaḥ — the nymphs; sphaṭika-śilābhiḥ abhraka-śilābhiḥ racitaḥ — made of crystal and mica slabs; pānam — drink, liquor and the like

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tathā pranṛtyāpsarasi sphaṭikābhramaye’suraḥ. papau pānaṃ mudā yuktaḥ prāsāde sumanohare||9||

Śrīdhara

prakṛṣṭaṃ nṛtyaṃ yāsāṃ tā apsaraso yasmin tasmin prāsāde sphaṭikābhramaye sphaṭikaśilābhiḥ abhrakaśilābhiś ca racite. pīyata iti pānaṃ madirādi||9||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रकृष्टं नृत्यं यासां ता अप्सरसो यस्मिन् तस्मिन् प्रासादे स्फटिकाभ्रमये स्फटिकशिलाभिः अभ्रकशिलाभिश् च रचिते । पीयत इति पानं मदिरादि ॥९॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The Apsarases whose dancing was excellent danced in the crystal palace, made of crystal and mica slabs. “Drink” means liquor and the like.

VP 1.17.10

तस्य पुत्रो महाभागः प्रह्लादो नाम नामतः । पपाठ बालपाठ्यानि गुरुगेहे गतोऽर्भकः ॥१०॥

The illustrious son of the Daitya king, Prahlāda, being yet a boy, resided in the dwelling of his preceptor, where he read such writings as are studied in early years.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasya putraḥ — his son; mahābhāgaḥ — illustrious; Prahlādaḥ — Prahlāda; guru-gehe gataḥ — gone to the preceptor's house; bāla-pāṭhyāni apāṭhat — read lessons for a boy

Śrīdhara

tad evaṃ — thus; paramaiśvaryam uktvā — having stated the supreme sovereignty; putra-dveṣa-kāraṇam — the cause of hatred toward the son; tasya putraḥ ity ādinā — by “his son” and the rest

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasya putro mahābhāgaḥ prahlādo nāma nāmataḥ. papāṭha bālapāṭhyāni gurugehe gato’rbhakaḥ||10||

Śrīdhara

tad evaṃ tasya paramaiśvaryam uktvā putradveṣakāraṇaṃ vaktum āhatasya putra ityādinā||10||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तद् एवं तस्य परमैश्वर्यम् उक्त्वा पुत्रद्वेषकारणं वक्तुम् आहतस्य पुत्र इत्यादिना ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

After stating his supreme sovereignty, the account turns to the cause of his hatred toward his son, beginning with “his son.”

VP 1.17.11

एकदा तु स धर्मात्मा जगाम गुरुणा सह । पानासक्तस्य पुरतः पितुर् दैत्यपतेस् तदा ॥११॥

On one occasion he came, accompanied by his teacher, to the court of his father, and bowed before his feet as he was drinking.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ekadā — on one occasion; dharma-ātmā — the righteous one; guruṇā saha — with his teacher; pituḥ purataḥ — before his father; pāna-āsaktasya — who was absorbed in drinking

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ekadā tu sa dharmātmā jagāma guruṇā saha. pānāsaktasya purataḥ pitur daityapates tadā||11||

VP 1.17.12

पादप्रणामावनतं तम् उत्थाप्य पिता सुतम् । हिरण्यकशिपुः प्राह प्रह्लादम् अमितौजसम् ॥१२॥

Hiraṇyakaśipu desired his prostrate son to rise, and said to him:

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pāda-praṇāma-avanatam — bowed in reverence at his feet; utthāpya — having raised; pitā sutam — the father his son; prāha — spoke

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pādapraṇāmāvanataṃ tam utthāpya pitā sutam. hiraṇyakaśipuḥ prāha prahlādam amitaujasam||12||

VP 1.17.13

पठ्यतां भवता वत्स सारभूतं सुभाषितम् । कालेनैतावता यत ते सदोद्युक्तेन शिक्षितम् ॥१३॥

“Repeat, boy, in substance, and agreeably, what during the period of your studies you have acquired.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

paṭhyatām — let it be recited; bhavatā — by you; vatsa — O child; sārabhūtam — essential; subhāṣitam — good teaching; śikṣitam — learned

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

paṭhyatāṃ bhavatā vatsa sārabhūtaṃ subhāṣitam. kālenaitāvatā yata te sadodyuktena śikṣitam||13||

VP 1.17.14

श्रूयतां तात वक्ष्यामि सारभूतं तवाज्ञया । समाहितमना भूत्वा यन् मे चेतस्यवस्थितम् ॥१४॥

“Hear, sire,” replied Prahlāda, “what in obedience to your commands I will repeat, the substance of all I have learned: listen attentively to that which wholly occupies my thoughts.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrūyatām — listen; tāta — O father; vakṣyāmi — I shall speak; tavājñayā — at your command; samāhita-manāḥ — with mind collected; cetasi avasthitam — abiding in the mind

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śrūyatāṃ tāta vakṣyāmi sārabhūtaṃ tavājñayā. samāhitamanā bhūtvā yan me cetasyavasthitam||14||

VP 1.17.15

अनादिमध्यान्तम् अजम् अवृद्धिक्षयम् अच्युतम् । प्रणतोऽस्मि महात्मानं सर्वकारणकारणम् ॥१५॥

I have learned to adore him who is without beginning, middle, or end, increase or diminution; the imperishable lord of the world, the universal cause of causes.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

anādi-madhya-antam — without beginning, middle, or end; ajam — unborn; avṛddhi-kṣayam — free from growth and decay; Acyutam — imperishable; sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam — cause of all causes; praṇataḥ asmi — I bow

Śrīdhara

sarva-kāraṇānāṃ kāraṇam — cause of all causes; antayati — brings to an end; antaḥ — ender or dissolver; santanyate anena — through Him it is sustained; sthiti-kartā — maker of preservation; pāṭhāntaram — variant reading

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

anādimadhyāntam ajam avṛddhikṣayam acyutam. praṇato’smi mahātmānaṃ sarvakāraṇakāraṇam||15||

Śrīdhara

tatra hetuḥsarvakāraṇāṇāṃ kāraṇam. praṇato’smyantasantānām iti pāṭhe, antayati ityantaḥ saṃhartā, santanyate’neneti santānaḥ sthitikartā, tam. pāṭhāntaraṃ sugamam||15||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत्र हेतुःसर्वकारणाणां कारणम् । प्रणतोऽस्म्यन्तसन्तानाम् इति पाठे, अन्तयति इत्यन्तः संहर्ता, सन्तन्यतेऽनेनेति सन्तानः स्थितिकर्ता, तम् । पाठान्तरं सुगमम् ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Cause of all causes” gives the reason. In the reading “I bow to the one who brings all to an end,” the ender is the dissolver and the one through whom everything is sustained is the agent of preservation. The alternate reading is easier.

VP 1.17.16

एवं निशम्य दैत्येन्द्रः क्रोधसंरक्तलोचनः । विलोक्य तद्गुरुं प्राह स्फुरिताधरपल्लवः ॥१६॥

On hearing these words, the sovereign of the Daityas, his eyes red with wrath and lip swollen with indignation, turned to the preceptor of his son, and said:

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃ niśamya — having heard thus; daityendraḥ — king of the Daityas; krodha-saṃrakta-locanaḥ — eyes reddened by anger; vilokya tad-gurum — looking at that teacher; prāha — spoke

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃ niśamya daityendraḥ krodhasaṃraktalocanaḥ. vilokya tadguruṃ prāha sphuritādharapallavaḥ||16||

VP 1.17.17

ब्रह्मबन्धो किम् एतत् ते विपक्षस्तुतिसंहितम् । असारं ग्राहितो बालो माम् अवज्ञाय दुर्मते ॥१७॥

“Vile Brahman, what is this preposterous commendation of my foe, that, in disrespect to me, you have taught this boy to utter?”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brahma-bandho — O Brahman's kinsman; brāhmaṇa-adhama — lowest of Brahmans; vipakṣa-stuti-saṃhitam — joined with praise of the enemy; asāram — worthless; grāhitaḥ — taught

Śrīdhara

brahma-bandho — O kinsman of Brahmans; brāhmaṇa-adhama — lowest of Brahmans; vipakṣa-stutyā saṃhitam — joined with praise of the enemy; kim etat grāhitaḥ — what is this that has been taught
