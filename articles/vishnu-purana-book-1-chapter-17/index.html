VP 1.17.1

मैत्रेय श्रूयतां सम्यक् चरितं तस्य धीमतः । प्रह्लादस्य सदोदारचरितस्य महात्मनः ॥१॥ ॥1॥

The most benevolent, great devotee, Prahlaada, please do listen to me, I will tell you his story.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Key terms — maitreya, śrūyatāṃ, samyak, caritaṃ, tasya, dhīmataḥ; direct sense — The most benevolent, great devotee, Prahlaada, please do listen to me, I will tell you his story.

Śrīdhara

Commentary terms — bhagavaddveṣa eva tadbhaktasvaputradveṣe’pi kāraṇam iti vakṣyan hiraṇyakaśipor vṛttāntam āhaditeḥ putra ityādinā ||१||; direct note — The account opens with Prahlāda, whose noble character and devotion are now to be described.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreya śrūyatāṃ samyak caritaṃ tasya dhīmataḥ . prahlādasya sadodāracaritasya mahātmanaḥ ||१|| ||1||

Śrīdhara

bhagavaddveṣa eva tadbhaktasvaputradveṣe’pi kāraṇam iti vakṣyan hiraṇyakaśipor vṛttāntam āhaditeḥ putra ityādinā ||१|| ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

भगवद्द्वेष एव तद्भक्तस्वपुत्रद्वेषेऽपि कारणम् इति वक्ष्यन् हिरण्यकशिपोर् वृत्तान्तम् आहदितेः पुत्र इत्यादिना ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The account opens with Prahlāda, whose noble character and devotion are now to be described.

VP 1.17.2

दितेः पुत्रो महावीर्यो हिरण्यकशिपुः पुरा । त्रैलोक्यं वशम् आनिन्ये ब्रह्मणो वरदर्पितः ॥२॥ ॥2॥

Diti's son, Hiranyakashipu, was there long back, was very valourous. He became arrogant with the boons received from Chaturmukha Brahma. He started to bring under his control all the three worlds.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Key terms — diteḥ, putro, mahāvīryo, hiraṇyakaśipuḥ, purā, trailokyaṃ; direct sense — Diti's son, Hiranyakashipu, was there long back, was very valourous. He became arrogant with the boons received from Chaturmukha Brahma. He started to bring under his control all the three worlds.

Śrīdhara

Commentary terms — bhūtebhyas tvadvisṛṣṭebhyo mṛtyur mābhūn mama prabho ityādyanekavaraprārthane kṛte sati tathaiva brahmaṇā dattair varair darpitaḥ san ||२||; direct note — Hiraṇyakaśipu is introduced as the powerful ruler who, strengthened by Brahmā’s boon, brought the three worlds under his control.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Hiraṇyakaśipu is introduced as the powerful ruler who, strengthened by Brahmā’s boon, brought the three worlds under his control.

VP 1.17.9
