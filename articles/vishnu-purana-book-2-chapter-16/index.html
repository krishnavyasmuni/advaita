VP 2.16.1

ऋभुर्वर्षसहस्रे तु समतीते नरेश्वर ।
निदाघज्ञानदानाय तदेव नगरं ययौ ॥
१॥

After a thousand years had passed, Ribhu went to that same city, O king, to give Nidāgha knowledge.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛbhuḥ varṣa-sahasre — Ribhu, after a thousand years; tu samatīte — when they had passed; nareśvara — O king; Nidāgha-jñāna-dānāya — for giving knowledge to Nidāgha; tat eva nagaram yayau — went to that same city

Śrīdhara

varṣa-sahasre samatīte — after a thousand years had passed; Nidāgha-jñāna-dānāya — for giving knowledge to Nidāgha; ātma-aikya-jñānasya — of knowledge of the unity of the self; cira-kāla-bahūpadeśa-apekṣayā — because it requires much instruction over a long time; durlabhatām darśayati — it shows the difficulty

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛbhurvarṣasahasre tu samatīte nareśvara | nidāghajñānadānāya tadeva nagaraṃ yayau ||1||

Śrīdhara

vaṣasahasre samatīte nidāghajñānadānāya yayāviti vadannātmaikyajñānasya cirakālabahūpadeśāpekṣitayā durlabhatāṃ darśayati ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वषसहस्रे समतीते निदाघज्ञानदानाय ययाविति वदन्नात्मैक्यज्ञानस्य चिरकालबहूपदेशापेक्षितया दुर्लभतां दर्शयति ॥
१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

After a thousand years have passed, when it is said that he went to give Nidāgha knowledge, the difficulty of knowledge of the unity of the self is shown: it requires instruction over a long time.

VP 2.16.2

नगरस्य बहिस्सोऽथ निदाघं ददृशे मुनिः । महाबलपरीवारे पुरं विशति पार्थिवे

Outside the city the sage saw Nidāgha, while the king, accompanied by a mighty retinue, was entering the city.

VP 2.16.3

दूरे स्थितं महामार्ग जनसम्मर्दवर्जकम् । क्षुत्क्षामकण्ठमायान्तमरण्यात्ससमित्कुशम्

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dūre sthitam mahā-bhāgam — standing far away, the great one; jana-sammarda-varjjakam — avoiding the crowd; kṣut-kṣāma-kaṇṭham — his throat emaciated by hunger; āyāntam araṇyāt — coming from the forest; sa-samit-kuśam — with fuel and kuśa grass

Śrīdhara

aspṛśya-sparśa-ādi-śaṅkayā — through fear of contact with untouchables and the like; jana-sammarda-varjjakam — avoiding the crowd; kṣudhā kṣāmaḥ — emaciated through hunger; kṛśaḥ kaṇṭhaḥ yasya — whose throat is thin

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dūre sthitaṃ mahābhāgaṃ janasammardavarjakam | kṣutkṣāmakaṇṭhamāyāntamaraṇyāt sasamitkuśam ||3||

Śrīdhara

aspṛśyasparśādiśaṅkayā janasammardavarjjakam | kṣudhā kṣāma.h kṛśaḥ kaṇṭho yasya tam ||3||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अस्पृश्यस्पर्शादिशङ्कया जनसम्मर्दवर्ज्जकम् ।
क्षुधा क्षाम.ह् कृशः कण्ठो यस्य तम् ॥
३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Because of fear of contact with untouchables and the like, [he is] avoiding the crowd. His throat is shrivelled by hunger: him whose throat is emaciated.

VP 2.16.4

VP 2.16.13

१३॥

“I am above as the king is; you are below as the elephant is. For your understanding, O Brahman, I have shown an example.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

upari aham yathā rājā — I am above as the king; tvam adhaḥ kuñjaraḥ yathā — you are below as the elephant; avabodhāya te brahman — for your understanding, O Brahman; dṛṣṭāntaḥ darśitaḥ mayā — an example has been shown by me

Śrīdhara

tad-upari āroha-aparādham — the fault of climbing above that; pariharati — he removes; dṛṣṭāntaḥ darśitaḥ iti — by saying ‘the example is shown’

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

tadupari ārohāparādhaṃ pariharati dṛṣṭānto darśita iti ||13||

१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

By saying ‘the example is shown,’ the fault of taking ‘above’ from the action of climbing is removed.

VP 2.16.14

त्वं राजेव द्विजश्रेष्ठ स्थितोऽहं गजवद्यदि ।

“If you are situated as the king and I am as the elephant, then tell me exactly which you are and which I am.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tvam rājā iva dvija-śreṣṭha sthitaḥ — you standing as the king, O best of Brahmans; aham gaja-vat yadi — I as the elephant, if; tat eva tvam mama ācakṣva — tell me exactly which you are; katamaḥ tvam aham tathā — which are you and which am I

Śrīdhara

tad eva tvam samācakṣva — tell me precisely which you are; katamaḥ tvam aham tathā — which are you and which am I; sākṣepa-uktyā — by a terse statement; na tvam tvam — you are not you; na ca anye anye — nor are others others; na aham api aham — nor am I even I

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tvaṃ rājeva dvijaśreṣṭha sthito’haṃ gajavadyadi | tadeva tvaṃ mamācakṣva katamastvamahaṃ tathā ||14||

Śrīdhara

tadeva tvaṃ samācakṣva katamastvamahaṃ tatha iti sākṣepoktayā na tvaṃ tvaṃ, na cānye’nye, nāhamapyaham iti ||14||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तदेव त्वं समाचक्ष्व कतमस्त्वमहं तथ इति साक्षेपोक्तया न त्वं त्वं, न चान्येऽन्ये, नाहमप्यहम् इति ॥
१४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

‘Tell me precisely which you are and which I am’: by this terse statement, the earlier teaching ‘you are not you, others are not others, nor am I even I’ is recalled.

VP 2.16.15

इत्युक्तस्सत्वरं तस्य प्रगृह्य चरणावुभौ । निदाघस्त्वाह भगवानाचार्यस्त्वमृभुर्ध्रुवम्

Thus addressed, Nidāgha quickly grasped both his feet and said, “Surely, O Blessed One, you are Ribhu, my teacher.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

iti uktaḥ — thus addressed; satvaram tasya caraṇau ubhau pragṛhya — quickly grasping both his feet; Nidāghaḥ uvāca — Nidāgha said; bhagavān ācāryaḥ tvam ṛbhuḥ dhruvam — surely you, Ribhu, are the blessed teacher

Śrīdhara

pūrvam yat uktam — what was said previously; tvam aham ity-ādi bhedaḥ — the distinction beginning ‘you and I’; mithyā iti — is false; tat eva smāritaḥ — that very point is recalled; caraṇau pragṛhya — having grasped the feet; dhruvam mama ācāryaḥ — surely my teacher

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

pūrvaṃ yaduktaṃ tvamaham ity-ādi bhedo mithyeti tadeva smāritaḥ tasyā caraṇau pragṛhyamāpayannidāgha āha dhruvaṃ mamācārya iti ||15||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पूर्वं यदुक्तं त्वमहम् इत्य्-आदि भेदो मिथ्येति तदेव स्मारितः तस्या चरणौ प्रगृह्यमापयन्निदाघ आह ध्रुवं ममाचार्य इति ॥
१५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.
