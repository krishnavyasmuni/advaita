VP 2.16.1

ब्राह्मण उवाच— ऋभुर् वर्ष-सहस्रे तु समतीते नरेश्वर।
निदाघ-ज्ञान-दानाय तद् एव नगरं ययौ

After a thousand years had passed, Ribhu went to that same city, O king, to give Nidāgha knowledge.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ṛbhuḥ varṣa-sahasre — Ribhu, after a thousand years;
tu samatīte — when they had passed;
nareśvara — O king;
Nidāgha-jñāna-dānāya — for giving knowledge to Nidāgha;
tat eva nagaram yayau — went to that same city
Śrīdhara
varṣa-sahasre samatīte — after a thousand years had passed;
Nidāgha-jñāna-dānāya — for giving knowledge to Nidāgha;
ātma-aikya-jñānasya — of knowledge of the unity of the self;
cira-kāla-bahūpadeśa-apekṣayā — because it requires much instruction over a long time;
durlabhatām darśayati — it shows the difficulty
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛbhurvarṣasahasre tu samatīte nareśvara | nidāghajñānadānāya tadeva nagaraṃ yayau ||1||

Śrīdhara

vaṣasahasre samatīte nidāghajñānadānāya yayāviti vadannātmaikyajñānasya cirakālabahūpadeśāpekṣitayā durlabhatāṃ darśayati ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वषसहस्रे समतीते निदाघज्ञानदानाय ययाविति वदन्नात्मैक्यज्ञानस्य चिरकालबहूपदेशापेक्षितया दुर्लभतां दर्शयति ॥
१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

After a thousand years have passed, when it is said that he went to give Nidāgha knowledge, the difficulty of knowledge of the unity of the self is shown: it requires instruction over a long time.

VP 2.16.2

VP 2.16.3

दूरे स्थितं महाभागं जन-सम्मर्द-वर्जकम्।
क्षुत्-क्षाम-कण्ठम् आयान्तम् अरण्यात् स-समित्-कुशम्

Seeing Nidāgha standing far away, avoiding the crowd, his throat shrivelled by hunger, coming from the forest with fuel and kuśa grass.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
dūre sthitam mahā-bhāgam — standing far away, the great one;
jana-sammarda-varjjakam — avoiding the crowd;
kṣut-kṣāma-kaṇṭham — his throat emaciated by hunger;
āyāntam araṇyāt — coming from the forest;
sa-samit-kuśam — with fuel and kuśa grass
Śrīdhara
aspṛśya-sparśa-ādi-śaṅkayā — through fear of contact with untouchables and the like;
jana-sammarda-varjjakam — avoiding the crowd;
kṣudhā kṣāmaḥ — emaciated through hunger;
kṛśaḥ kaṇṭhaḥ yasya — whose throat is thin
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dūre sthitaṃ mahābhāgaṃ janasammardavarjakam | kṣutkṣāmakaṇṭhamāyāntamaraṇyāt sasamitkuśam ||3||

Śrīdhara

aspṛśyasparśādiśaṅkayā janasammardavarjjakam | kṣudhā kṣāma.h kṛśaḥ kaṇṭho yasya tam ||3||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अस्पृश्यस्पर्शादिशङ्कया जनसम्मर्दवर्ज्जकम् ।
क्षुधा क्षाम.ह् कृशः कण्ठो यस्य तम् ॥
३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Because of fear of contact with untouchables and the like, [he is] avoiding the crowd. His throat is shrivelled by hunger: him whose throat is emaciated.

VP 2.16.13

उपर्य् अहं यथा राजा त्वम् अधः कुञ्जरो यथा।
अवबोधाय ते ब्रह्मन् दृष्टान्तो दर्शितो मया

“I am above as the king is; you are below as the elephant is. For your understanding, O Brahman, I have shown an example.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
upari aham yathā rājā — I am above as the king;
tvam adhaḥ kuñjaraḥ yathā — you are below as the elephant;
avabodhāya te brahman — for your understanding, O Brahman;
dṛṣṭāntaḥ darśitaḥ mayā — an example has been shown by me
Śrīdhara
tad-upari āroha-aparādham — the fault of climbing above that;
pariharati — he removes;
dṛṣṭāntaḥ darśitaḥ iti — by saying ‘the example is shown’
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

uparyahaṃ yathā rājā tvamadhaḥ kuñjaro yathā | avabodhāya te brahmandṛṣṭānto darśito mayā ||13||

Śrīdhara

tadupari ārohāparādhaṃ pariharati dṛṣṭānto darśita iti ||13||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तदुपरि आरोहापराधं परिहरति दृष्टान्तो दर्शित इति ॥
१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

By saying ‘the example is shown,’ the fault of taking ‘above’ from the action of climbing is removed.

VP 2.16.14

ऋभुर् उवाच— त्वं राजेव द्विज-श्रेष्ठ स्थितोऽहं गजवद् यदि।
तद् एतत् त्वं समाचक्ष्व कतमस् त्वम् अहं तथा

“If you are situated as the king and I am as the elephant, then tell me exactly which you are and which I am.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tvam rājā iva dvija-śreṣṭha sthitaḥ — you standing as the king, O best of Brahmans;
aham gaja-vat yadi — I as the elephant, if;
tat eva tvam mama ācakṣva — tell me exactly which you are;
katamaḥ tvam aham tathā — which are you and which am I
Śrīdhara
tad eva tvam samācakṣva — tell me precisely which you are;
katamaḥ tvam aham tathā — which are you and which am I;
sākṣepa-uktyā — by a terse statement;
na tvam tvam — you are not you;
na ca anye anye — nor are others others;
na aham api aham — nor am I even I
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tvaṃ rājeva dvijaśreṣṭha sthito’haṃ gajavadyadi | tadeva tvaṃ mamācakṣva katamastvamahaṃ tathā ||14||

Śrīdhara

tadeva tvaṃ samācakṣva katamastvamahaṃ tatha iti sākṣepoktayā na tvaṃ tvaṃ, na cānye’nye, nāhamapyaham iti ||14||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तदेव त्वं समाचक्ष्व कतमस्त्वमहं तथ इति साक्षेपोक्तया न त्वं त्वं, न चान्येऽन्ये, नाहमप्यहम् इति ॥
१४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

‘Tell me precisely which you are and which I am’: by this terse statement, the earlier teaching ‘you are not you, others are not others, nor am I even I’ is recalled.

VP 2.16.15

Śrīdhara

pūrvaṃ yaduktaṃ tvamaham ity-ādi bhedo mithyeti tadeva smāritaḥ tasyā caraṇau pragṛhyamāpayannidāgha āha dhruvaṃ mamācārya iti ||15||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पूर्वं यदुक्तं त्वमहम् इत्य्-आदि भेदो मिथ्येति तदेव स्मारितः तस्या चरणौ प्रगृह्यमापयन्निदाघ आह ध्रुवं ममाचार्य इति ॥
१५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The distinction beginning ‘you and I’ that was previously stated to be false is recalled. Nidāgha, grasping his feet, says, ‘Surely you are my teacher.’

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जातिस्मरणेन आप्तो बोधो येन ।
भरतजन्मस्मरणेन हि तदा ऋषभदेवेन यत् उपदिष्टं ज्ञानं तदेव योगभ्रंशहेतुभूतप्रारब्धकर्मावसाने सत्यस्मिन् द्विज जन्मन्याविभूतम् ।
अतस्तत्रैव जन्मन्यपवर्गं प्राप ॥
२४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The knowledge obtained through remembrance of births is possessed by him. For at that time, the knowledge taught by Ṛṣabhadeva through remembrance of Bharata’s birth became manifest in this Brahman birth, when the operative karma that caused the fall from yoga had ended. Therefore he attained liberation in that very birth.
