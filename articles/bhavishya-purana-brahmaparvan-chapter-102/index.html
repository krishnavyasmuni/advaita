BP 1.102.78

yaḥ kārayati devārciṃ hṛdayālambanaṃ raveḥ |
sa naro bhānusālokyamupaiti gatakalmaṣaḥ || 78 ||

—the person who causes the worship of the deity, making Ravi the support of his heart, attains the world shared with Bhānu, freed from impurity.

Word-for-word
yaḥ — whoever;
kārayati — causes to be performed;
deva-arcim — worship of the deity;
hṛdaya-ālambanam — making the heart its support;
raveḥ — of Ravi;
saḥ naraḥ — that person;
bhānu-sālokyam — the world shared with Bhānu;
upaiti — approaches;
gata-kalmaṣaḥ — freed from impurity.
