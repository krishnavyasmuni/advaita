VP 4.17.1

द्रुह्योस्तु तनयो बभ्रुः

Sri Paraasharar - Druhyu had a son Babhru.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

druhyoḥ tu — but of Druhyu; tanayaḥ — the son; babhrūḥ — was Babhru.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

druhyostu tanayo babhruḥ

Śrīdhara

duhyoḥ saptadaśe vaṃśaḥ saṅkṣepādanuvarṇyate . mlecchānāṃ ya udīcyānāmādhipatyamathākarot . jyeṣṭhānukramavaṃśāt turvasukaniṣṭhasya śarmiṣṭhaputrasya duhyorvaṃśamāha—duhyor iti ||१||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

दुह्योः सप्तदशे वंशः सङ्क्षेपादनुवर्ण्यते । म्लेच्छानां य उदीच्यानामाधिपत्यमथाकरोत् । ज्येष्ठानुक्रमवंशात् तुर्वसुकनिष्ठस्य शर्मिष्ठपुत्रस्य दुह्योर्वंशमाह—दुह्योर् इति ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - Druhyu had a son Babhru.

VP 4.17.2

बभ्रोःसेतुः

Babhru's son was Setu.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

babhroḥ — of Babhru; setuḥ — Setu [was the son].

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

babhroḥsetuḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.17.4

आरब्धस्यात्मजो गान्धारः गान्धारस्य धर्मः धर्मात्घृतः घृतात्दुर्दमः ततः प्रचेताः

Aarabdha had a son Gaandhaara. Then Dharma, then Ghruta, then Durdama, then Prachetas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ārabdhasya ātmajaḥ — Ārabdha’s son; gāndhāraḥ — was Gāndhāra; gāndhārasya dharmaḥ — Dharma was Gāndhāra’s [son]; dharmāt ghṛtaḥ — Ghṛta from Dharma; ghṛtāt durdamaḥ — Durdama from Ghṛta; tataḥ pracetāḥ — then Pracetās.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ārabdhasyātmajo gāndhāraḥ gāndhārasya dharmaḥ dharmātghṛtaḥ ghṛtātdurdamaḥ tataḥ pracetāḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 4.17.5

प्रचेतसः पुत्रः शतधर्मः बहुलानां म्लेछानामुदीच्यानामाधिपत्यमकरोत्

The verse is translated from the Vasuki English cross-reference.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pracetasaḥ putraḥ — Pracetās’ son; śatadharmaḥ — was Śatadharma; bahūnām mlecchānām — of many Mlecchas; udīcyānām — of the northern [peoples]; ādhipatyam akarot — he exercised lordship.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pracetasaḥ putraḥ śatadharmaḥ bahulānāṃ mlechānāmudīcyānāmādhipatyamakarot

Śrīdhara

mlecchānāmiti mlecchādhipatyakathanāt yayātiśāpapariṇāmo mlecchabhāvaḥ sūcitaḥ ||५||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

म्लेच्छानामिति म्लेच्छाधिपत्यकथनात् ययातिशापपरिणामो म्लेच्छभावः सूचितः ॥५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 Prachetas' son Shatadharma was ruling over the mlecchaas, those living in the northern part.
