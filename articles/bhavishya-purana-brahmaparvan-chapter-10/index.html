Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brāhmaparvan — Chapter 10: Women’s Conduct Among Kin and in Public

BP 1.10.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
आराध्यानां हि सर्वेषामयमाराधने विधिः ।
चित्त१ ज्ञानानुवृत्तिश्च हितैषित्वं च सर्वदा ॥ १

The method of honoring all who are to be honored is this: understanding their minds, following their wishes, and always seeking their welfare.

ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
आराध्यानाम् सर्वेषाम् (ārādhyānām sarveṣām) — of all worthy of honour;
अयम् आराधने विधिः (ayam ārādhane vidhiḥ) — this is procedure of service;
चित्त-ज्ञान (citta-jñāna) — understanding mind;
अनुवृत्तिः (anuvṛttiḥ) — acting in harmony;
हितैषित्वम् च सर्वदा (hitaiṣitvam ca sarvadā) — always seeking welfare

BP 1.10.2

कन्या पुनर्भूर्वेश्या च त्रिविधा एव योषितः ।
प्रिया मध्याप्रिया चैव योग्या मध्येतरा तथा ॥ २

Women are of three kinds: maiden, remarried woman, and prostitute; they are also dear, middling, and not dear, and suitable, middling, and not suitable.

कन्या (kanyā) — maiden;
पुनर्भूः (punarbhūḥ) — remarried woman;
वेश्या (veśyā) — courtesan;
त्रि-विधाः एव योषितः (tri-vidhāḥ eva yoṣitaḥ) — women of three kinds;
प्रिया (priyā) — beloved;
मध्या (madhyā) — intermediate;
अप्रिया (apriyā) — not beloved;
योग्या (yogyā) — suitable;
मध्या (madhyā) — intermediate;
इतरा (itarā) — otherwise

BP 1.10.3

समा श्रेष्ठा च नीचा च भूयोपि त्रिविधाः पुनः ।
पूर्ववत्परयोर्वृत्तिरिष्टानुक्त्या प्रियाप्रिये ॥ ३

Again, there are three kinds: equal, superior, and inferior. As stated before, conduct toward the latter two follows what is desired or left unspoken, in affection or dislike.

समा (samā) — equal;
श्रेष्ठा (śreṣṭhā) — superior;
नीचा (nīcā) — inferior;
भूयः अपि त्रि-विधाः (bhūyaḥ api tri-vidhāḥ) — again threefold;
पूर्व-वत् (pūrva-vat) — as before;
परयोः वृत्तिः (parayoḥ vṛttiḥ) — conduct toward latter two;
इष्ट-अनुक्त्या (iṣṭa-anuktyā) — by desired speech or its omission;
प्रिय-अप्रिये (priya-apriye) — affection and dislike

BP 1.10.4

अधमाप्रिययोरत्र पतिपत्न्यादिका मता ।
निषिद्धानां तु भक्ष्यादि तद्धि यत्नाद्विधीयते ॥ ४

Here the inferior and the not-beloved are considered among relations such as husband and wife. Prohibited foods and the like are prescribed with care.

अधम-अप्रिययोः (adhama-apriyayoḥ) — of inferior and unloved;
अत्र (atra) — here;
पति-पत्नी-आदिका (pati-patnī-ādikā) — relations such as husband and wife;
मता (matā) — considered;
निषिद्धानाम् तु (niṣiddhānām tu) — of forbidden things;
भक्ष्य-आदि (bhakṣya-ādi) — foods and others;
तत् हि यत्नात् विधीयते (tat hi yatnāt vidhīyate) — that is carefully regulated

BP 1.10.5

एकद्वित्वबहुत्वाद्या२ ये भेदाः समुदाहृताः ।
ज्येष्ठादिवृत्ते वक्ष्यामस्तानशेषान्द्विजोत्तमाः ॥ ५

O best of the twice-born, all the distinctions stated—one, two, many, and the like—shall be explained completely in the conduct toward the eldest and the rest.

एक-द्वित्व-बहुत्व-आद्याः (eka-dvitva-bahutva-ādyāḥ) — one, two, many and others;
ये भेदाः समुदाहृताः (ye bhedāḥ samudāhṛtāḥ) — distinctions described;
ज्येष्ठ-आदि-वृत्ते (jyeṣṭha-ādi-vṛtte) — in conduct toward eldest and others;
वक्ष्यामः (vakṣyāmaḥ) — we shall explain;
तान् अशेषान् (tān aśeṣān) — all without omission;
द्विज-उत्तमाः (dvija-uttamāḥ) — O best twice-born

BP 1.10.6

वृत्तं च द्विविधं स्त्रीणां बाह्यमाभ्यन्तरं तथा ।
भर्तुरन्यजने बाह्यं तस्याः शारीरमान्तरम् ॥ ६

Women's conduct is twofold, external and internal. The external concerns the husband and other people; the internal concerns her body.

वृत्तम् द्वि-विधम् स्त्रीणाम् (vṛttam dvi-vidham strīṇām) — women’s conduct twofold;
बाह्यम् (bāhyam) — external;
आभ्यन्तरम् तथा (ābhyantaram tathā) — and internal;
भर्तुः अन्य-जने (bhartuḥ anya-jane) — toward husband and other people;
बाह्यम् (bāhyam) — external;
तस्याः शारीरम् आन्तरम् (tasyāḥ śārīram āntaram) — her bodily life is internal

BP 1.10.7

ज्ञातीतरविभागेन तद्बाह्यं द्विविधं पुनः ।
पूज्यं तुल्यं कनिष्ठं च तत्प्रत्येकं पुनस्त्रिधा ॥ ७

That external conduct is again twofold, according to kin and non-kin. Each is again threefold: one to be honored, equal, and younger.

ज्ञाति-इतर-विभागेन (jñāti-itara-vibhāgena) — by division of relatives and others;
तत् बाह्यम् द्वि-विधम् पुनः (tat bāhyam dvi-vidham punaḥ) — external conduct again twofold;
पूज्यम् (pūjyam) — venerable;
तुल्यम् (tulyam) — equal;
कनिष्ठम् (kaniṣṭham) — junior;
तत् प्रत्येकम् पुनः त्रि-धा (tat pratyekam punaḥ tri-dhā) — each again threefold

BP 1.10.8

रहोरतं प्रकाशं च शारीरमपि तत्त्रिधा ।
भर्तुश्चित्तानुकूल्येन प्रयोक्तव्यं यथोचितम् ॥ ८

Secret sexual conduct, open conduct, and bodily conduct—this too is threefold. It should be practiced suitably according to the husband's mind.

रहः-रतम् (rahaḥ-ratam) — private affection;
प्रकाशम् (prakāśam) — public conduct;
शारीरम् अपि (śārīram api) — and physical relation;
तत् त्रि-धा (tat tri-dhā) — that threefold;
भर्तुः चित्त-अनुकूल्येन (bhartuḥ citta-anukūlyena) — in harmony with husband’s mind;
प्रयोक्तव्यम् यथा-उचितम् (prayoktavyam yathā-ucitam) — to be applied appropriately

BP 1.10.9

माता पिता स्वसा भ्राता पितृव्याचार्यमातुलाः ।
सभार्या भगिनी भर्ता भर्तृमातृपितृष्वसा ॥ ९

Mother, father, sister, brother, paternal uncle, teacher, maternal uncle with his wife, sister, husband, and the husband's mother, father, and paternal aunt;

माता (mātā) — mother;
पिता (pitā) — father;
स्वसा (svasā) — sister;
भ्राता (bhrātā) — brother;
पितृव्य (pitṛvya) — paternal uncle;
आचार्य (ācārya) — teacher;
मातुलाः (mātulāḥ) — maternal uncles;
स-भार्याः (sa-bhāryāḥ) — with wives;
भगिनी (bhaginī) — sister;
भर्ता (bhartā) — husband;
भर्तृ-मातृ-पितृ-स्वसा (bhartṛ-mātṛ-pitṛ-svasā) — husband’s mother, father and paternal aunt

BP 1.10.10

धात्री वृद्धाङ्गनादिश्च यस्तत्रास्ता समो जनः ।
प्रथमोढा सपत्नी च स्त्रीणां मान्यतमो गणः ॥.10.१०

A nurse, elderly women, and others, and anyone of equal standing; the first wife and co-wife form a group most worthy of honor among women.

धात्री (dhātrī) — nurse;
वृद्ध-अङ्गना-आदिः (vṛddha-aṅganā-ādiḥ) — elderly woman and others;
यः तत्र आस्ता समः जनः (yaḥ tatra āstā samaḥ janaḥ) — whoever there is of equal standing;
प्रथम-ऊढा सपत्नी (prathama-ūḍhā sapatnī) — co-wife married first;
स्त्रीणाम् मान्यतमः गणः (strīṇām mānyatamaḥ gaṇaḥ) — group most deserving respect among women

BP 1.10.11

एषामेव त्वपत्यादिभगिनीभ्रातरस्तथा ।
कनिष्ठा भर्तुरित्यादिभार्याश्चाप्तसमो मतः ॥१

Their children, sisters, and brothers, and the younger relatives of the husband and the like, as well as the wives of close equals, are regarded as belonging to the next group.

एषाम् एव तु अपत्य-आदि (eṣām eva tu apatya-ādi) — their children and others;
भगिनी-भ्रातरः तथा (bhaginī-bhrātaraḥ tathā) — sisters and brothers likewise;
कनिष्ठाः भर्तुः इति-आदि (kaniṣṭhāḥ bhartuḥ iti-ādi) — husband’s juniors and others;
भार्याः (bhāryāḥ) — wives;
आप्त-समः मतः (āpta-samaḥ mataḥ) — regarded as comparable to trusted persons

BP 1.10.12

हीनोऽन्यः शासनीयस्तु तत्र तावन्न विद्यते ।
योग्यता सुतसौभाग्यैर्न यावत्स्यात्प्रतिष्ठिता ॥२

A person of lower standing may be instructed; standing is not fixed there until competence, a son, and good fortune are established.

हीनः अन्यः (hīnaḥ anyaḥ) — another of lower standing;
शासनीयः तु (śāsanīyaḥ tu) — may be instructed;
तत्र तावत् न विद्यते (tatra tāvat na vidyate) — not fixed there until;
योग्यता (yogyatā) — competence;
सुत (suta) — son;
सौभाग्यैः (saubhāgyaiḥ) — good fortune;
न यावत् स्यात् प्रतिष्ठिता (na yāvat syāt pratiṣṭhitā) — until established

BP 1.10.13

यत्रापि गुरु भर्तॄणामानुकूल्येन सर्वदा ।
वृत्तिः प्रशस्यते स्त्रीणां पूजाचाराविरोधिनी५ ॥३

Even where the husband has a superior, a woman's conduct is always praised when it follows the husband's wishes and does not oppose the honor and observances due to that superior.

यत्र अपि गुरुः भर्तॄणाम् (yatra api guruḥ bhartṝṇām) — even where superior of husbands;
आनुकूल्येन सर्वदा (ānukūlyena sarvadā) — always through conformity;
वृत्तिः प्रशस्यते स्त्रीणाम् (vṛttiḥ praśasyate strīṇām) — women’s conduct is praised;
पूजा-आचार-अविरोधिनी (pūjā-ācāra-avirodhinī) — not opposing worship and proper observance

BP 1.10.14

देवरैः पतिमित्रैश्च परिहासक्रियोचितैः ।
विविक्तदेशावस्थानं वर्जयेदिति नर्म च ॥४

She should avoid staying in a secluded place and joking with brothers-in-law and the husband's friends, even those fit for joking.

देवरैः (devaraiḥ) — with brothers-in-law;
पति-मित्रैः च (pati-mitraiḥ ca) — and husband’s friends;
परिहास-क्रिया-उचितैः (parihāsa-kriyā-ucitaiḥ) — with whom joking may be appropriate;
विविक्त-देश-अवस्थानम् (vivikta-deśa-avasthānam) — remaining in secluded place;
वर्जयेत् (varjayet) — should avoid;
इति नर्म च (iti narma ca) — and intimate joking

BP 1.10.15

प्रायशो हि कुलस्त्रीणां शीलविध्वंसहेतवः ।
दुष्टयोगो रहो नित्यं स्वातन्त्र्यमतिनर्मता ॥५

For women of good families, bad association, constant seclusion, independence, and excessive joking are generally causes of the destruction of conduct.

प्रायशः हि (prāyaśaḥ hi) — frequently indeed;
कुल-स्त्रीणाम् (kula-strīṇām) — of women of established families;
शील-विध्वंस-हेतवः (śīla-vidhvaṃsa-hetavaḥ) — causes destroying character;
दुष्ट-योगः (duṣṭa-yogaḥ) — corrupt company;
रहः नित्यम् (rahaḥ nityam) — continual privacy;
स्वातन्त्र्यम् (svātantryam) — independence;
अति-नर्मता (ati-narmatā) — excessive joking

BP 1.10.16

दुष्टसङ्गे त्वरा स्त्रीणां युवभिर्नर्म नोचितम् ।
निर्भेषता स्वतन्त्राणां साफल्यं रहसि यजेत् ॥६

Women should not hurry into bad company or joke with young men; fearlessness among those acting independently finds its success in secret.

दुष्ट-सङ्गे त्वरा (duṣṭa-saṅge tvarā) — haste in corrupt company;
स्त्रीणाम् (strīṇām) — of women;
युवभिः नर्म न उचितम् (yuvabhiḥ narma na ucitam) — joking with young men is not proper;
निर्भेषता (nirbheṣatā) — fearlessness;
स्वतन्त्राणाम् (svatantrāṇām) — of unrestricted persons;
साफल्यम् रहसि (sāphalyam rahasi) — finds fulfilment in seclusion

BP 1.10.17

पुंसो दुष्टेङ्गिताकारान्दुष्टभावप्रयोजितान् ।
भ्रातृवत्पितृवच्चैतान्पश्यती परिवर्जयेत् ॥७

She should avoid men whose gestures and appearances are prompted by corrupt intentions, even when she sees them as brothers or fathers.

पुंसः दुष्ट-इङ्गित-आकारान् (puṃsaḥ duṣṭa-iṅgita-ākārān) — corrupt gestures and appearances of men;
दुष्ट-भाव-प्रयोजितान् (duṣṭa-bhāva-prayojitān) — prompted by corrupt intention;
भ्रातृ-वत् पितृ-वत् च (bhrātṛ-vat pitṛ-vat ca) — like brother or father;
एतान् पश्यती (etān paśyatī) — observing these;
परिवर्जयेत् (parivarjayet) — should avoid

BP 1.10.18

पुंसोऽ न्याग्रहमालापस्मितविप्रेक्षितानि च ।
करान्तरेण द्रव्याणां निबन्धं ग्रहणार्पणम् ॥८

She should avoid a man's insistence, conversation, smiles, and sidelong glances, and the obligation involved in taking or giving objects from hand to hand.

पुंसः अन्य-आग्रहम् (puṃsaḥ anya-āgraham) — a man’s persistent demand;
आलाप (ālāpa) — conversation;
स्मित (smita) — smile;
विप्रेक्षितानि (viprekṣitāni) — suggestive glances;
कर-अन्तरेण (kara-antareṇa) — directly hand to hand;
द्रव्याणाम् (dravyāṇām) — of objects;
निबन्धम् (nibandham) — binding obligation;
ग्रहण-अर्पणम् (grahaṇa-arpaṇam) — taking and giving

BP 1.10.19

द्वारप्रदेशावस्थानं राजमार्गावलोकनम् ।
प्रेक्षोद्यानादिशीलत्वं निरुष्याद्देशमालयम् ॥९

She should make her home in a place removed from staying by doorways, watching the royal road, and frequenting spectacles, gardens, and the like.

द्वार-प्रदेश-अवस्थानम् (dvāra-pradeśa-avasthānam) — lingering at doorway;
राज-मार्ग-अवलोकनम् (rāja-mārga-avalokanam) — watching royal road;
प्रेक्षा-उद्यान-आदि-शीलत्वम् (prekṣā-udyāna-ādi-śīlatvam) — habit of spectacles, gardens and others;
निरुन्ध्यात् (nirundhyāt) — should prevent;
देशम् आलयम् (deśam ālayam) — locality and residence

BP 1.10.20

बहूनां दर्शने स्थानं दृष्टिवाक्कायचापलम् ।
ष्ठीवनत्वं ससीत्कारमुच्चैर्हसितजल्पितम् ॥.10.२०

She should avoid being where many can see her, restlessness of eyes, speech, and body, spitting, hissing, loud laughter, and loud talk.

बहूनाम् दर्शने स्थानम् (bahūnām darśane sthānam) — standing in view of many;
दृष्टि-वाक्-काय-चापलम् (dṛṣṭi-vāk-kāya-cāpalam) — restlessness of gaze, speech and body;
ष्ठीवनत्वम् (ṣṭhīvanatvam) — spitting;
स-सीत्कारम् (sa-sītkāram) — with hissing;
उच्चैः हसित-जल्पितम् (uccaiḥ hasita-jalpitam) — loud laughing and speaking

BP 1.10.21

साङ्गत्यं लिङ्गिदुष्टस्त्रीभिघृणीक्षणिकादिभिः ।
मन्त्रमण्डलदीक्षायां सक्तिः संवसनेषु च ॥१

Association with religious mendicants, corrupt women, spiteful people, fortune-tellers, and the like; attachment to ritual circles, initiations, and living together.

साङ्गत्यम् (sāṅgatyam) — association;
लिङ्गि (liṅgi) — sectarian mendicants;
दुष्ट-स्त्रीभिः (duṣṭa-strībhiḥ) — with corrupt women;
घृणी (ghṛṇī) — spiteful people;
ईक्षणिक-आदिभिः (īkṣaṇika-ādibhiḥ) — fortune-tellers and others;
मन्त्र-मण्डल-दीक्षायाम् सक्तिः (mantra-maṇḍala-dīkṣāyām saktiḥ) — attachment to ritual-circle initiation;
संवसनेषु च (saṃvasaneṣu ca) — and shared residences

BP 1.10.22

इत्येवमादिदुर्वृत्तं प्रायोदुष्टजनोचितम् ।
वर्जयेत्परिरक्षन्ती कुलत्रितयवाच्यताम् ॥२

She should avoid such bad conduct and the like, generally proper to wicked people, while guarding the good name of the three families.

इति एवम्-आदि दुर्वृत्तम् (iti evam-ādi durvṛttam) — bad conduct such as this;
प्रायः दुष्ट-जन-उचितम् (prāyaḥ duṣṭa-jana-ucitam) — generally associated with corrupt people;
वर्जयेत् (varjayet) — should avoid;
परिरक्षन्ती (parirakṣantī) — protecting;
कुल-त्रितय-वाच्यताम् (kula-tritaya-vācyatām) — reputation of three families
