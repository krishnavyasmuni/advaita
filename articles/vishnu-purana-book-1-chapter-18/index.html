VP 1.18.1

तस्यैवं दानवाश् चेष्टां दृष्ट्वा दैत्यपतेर् भयात् । आचचक्षुः स चोवाच सूदान् आहूय सत्वरः ॥१॥ ॥1॥

Seeing the demons’ efforts, and fearing their lord, they reported everything. He summoned the cooks at once and spoke.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Key terms — tasyaivaṃ, dānavāś, ceṣṭāṃ, dṛṣṭvā, daityapater, bhayāt; direct sense — Seeing the demons’ efforts, and fearing their lord, they reported everything. He summoned the cooks at once and spoke.

Śrīdhara

Commentary terms — ceṣṭām upadeśarūpām ||१||; direct note — The failed attempts against Prahlāda are reported to Hiraṇyakaśipu, who turns to the cooks for another method.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasyaivaṃ dānavāś ceṣṭāṃ dṛṣṭvā daityapater bhayāt . ācacakṣuḥ sa covāca sūdān āhūya satvaraḥ ||१|| ||1||

Śrīdhara

ceṣṭām upadeśarūpām ||१|| ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

चेष्टाम् उपदेशरूपाम् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The failed attempts against Prahlāda are reported to Hiraṇyakaśipu, who turns to the cooks for another method.
