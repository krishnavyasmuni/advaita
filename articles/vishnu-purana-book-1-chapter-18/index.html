VP 1.18.1

तस्यैवं दानवाश् चेष्टां दृष्ट्वा दैत्यपतेर् भयात् । आचचक्षुः स चोवाच सूदान् आहूय सत्वरः ॥१॥

The Dānavas, observing the conduct of Prahlāda, reported it to the king, lest they should incur his displeasure.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — tasya — of him; evam — thus; dānavāḥ — the Dānavas; ceṣṭām — conduct or efforts; dṛṣṭvā — seeing; daityapateḥ bhayāt — from fear of the Daitya lord; ācacakṣuḥ — they reported; saḥ — he; ca — and; uvāca — spoke; sūdān āhūya — having summoned the cooks; satvaram — swiftly.

Śrīdhara

Words — ceṣṭām upadeśarūpām; sense — “Conduct” means instruction, that is, Prahlāda’s teaching.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasyaivaṃ dānavāś ceṣṭāṃ dṛṣṭvā daityapater bhayāt . ācacakṣuḥ sa covāca sūdān āhūya satvaraḥ ||१||

Śrīdhara

ceṣṭām upadeśarūpām ||१||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

चेष्टाम् उपदेशरूपाम् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Conduct” means instruction, that is, Prahlāda’s teaching.

VP 1.18.2

हे सूदा मम पुत्रोऽसाव् अन्येषाम् अपि दुर्मतिः । कुमार्गदेशिको दृष्टो हन्यताम् अविलम्बितम् ॥२॥

He sent for his cooks, and said to them, “My vile and unprincipled son is now teaching others his impious doctrines: be quick, and put an end to him.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — he sūdāḥ — O cooks; mama putraḥ asau — this son of mine; anyeṣām api — of others also; durmatiḥ — evil-minded; kumārgadeśikaḥ — teacher of the wrong path; dṛṣṭaḥ — is seen or has become; hanyatām — let him be killed; avilambitam — without delay.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

he sūdā mama putro’sāv anyeṣām api durmatiḥ . kumārgadeśiko dṛṣṭo hanyatām avilambitam ||२||

VP 1.18.3

हालाहलं विषं तस्य सर्वभक्ष्येषु दीयताम् । प्रविज्ञातम् असौ पापो हन्यतां मा विचार्यताम् ॥३॥

Let deadly poison be mixed up with all his viands, without his knowledge. Hesitate not, but destroy the wretch without delay.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — hālāhalam viṣam — deadly poison; tasya — for him; sarvabhakṣyeṣu — in all foods; dīyatām — let it be put; pravijñātam — without his knowledge; asau pāpaḥ — this wicked one; hanyatām — let him be killed; mā vicāryatām — let there be no hesitation.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hālāhalaṃ viṣaṃ tasya sarvabhakṣyeṣu dīyatām . pravijñātam asau pāpo hanyatāṃ mā vicāryatām ||३||

VP 1.18.4

ते तथैव ततश् चक्रुः प्रह्लादस्य महात्मने । विषदानं यथाज्ञप्तं पित्रा तस्य महात्मनः ॥४॥

Accordingly they did so, and administered poison to the virtuous Prahlāda, as his father had commanded them.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — te — they; tathaiva — just so; tataḥ — then; cakruḥ — did; prahlādasya mahātmane — to the noble Prahlāda; viṣadānam — the giving of poison; yathājñaptam — as commanded; pitrā — by the father; tasya — his.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

te tathaiva tataś cakruḥ prahlādasya mahātmane . viṣadānaṃ yathājñaptaṃ pitrā tasya mahātmanaḥ ||४||

VP 1.18.5

हालाहलं विषं घोरम् अनन्तोच्चारणेन सः । अभिमन्त्र्य सहान्नेन मैत्रेय बुभुजे तदा ॥५॥

Prahlāda, repeating the name of the imperishable, ate and digested the food in which the deadly poison had been infused, O Maitreya,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — hālāhalam viṣam ghoram — terrible deadly poison; anantoccāraṇena — by uttering Ananta’s name; saḥ — he; abhimantrya — consecrating or invoking by mantra; saha annena — together with food; maitreya — O Maitreya; bubhuje — ate; tadā — then.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hālāhalaṃ viṣaṃ ghoram anantoccāraṇena saḥ . abhimantrya sahānnena maitreya bubhuje tadā ||५||

VP 1.18.6

अविकारं स तद् भुक्त्वा प्रह्लादः स्वस्थमानसः । अनन्तख्यातिनिर्वीर्यं जरयामास तद्विषम् ॥६॥

and suffered no harm from it, either in body or mind, for it had been rendered innocuous by the name of the eternal.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — avikāram — without change or effect; saḥ — he; tat bhuktvā — having eaten that; prahlādaḥ — Prahlāda; svasthamānasaḥ — tranquil in mind; anantakhyātinirvīryam — made powerless by the name of Ananta; jarayāmāsa — digested; tat viṣam — that poison.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

avikāraṃ sa tad bhuktvā prahlādaḥ svasthamānasaḥ . anantakhyātinirvīryaṃ jarayāmāsa tadviṣam ||६||

VP 1.18.7

ततस् तदा भयत्रस्ता जीर्णं दृष्ट्वा महद् विषम् । दैत्येश्वरम् उपागम्य प्रणिपत्येदम् अब्रुवन् ॥७॥

Beholding the strong poison digested, those who had prepared the food were filled with dismay, and hastened to the king, and fell down before him, and said,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — tataḥ tadā — then; bhayatrastāḥ — terrified with fear; jīrṇam dṛṣṭvā — seeing it digested; mahat viṣam — the great poison; daityeśvaram upāgamya — approaching the Daitya lord; praṇipatya — bowing down; idam abruvan — they said this.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatas tadā bhayatrastā jīrṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā mahad viṣam . daityeśvaram upāgamya praṇipatyedam abruvan ||७||

VP 1.18.8

दैत्यराज विषं दत्तम् अस्माभिर् अतिभीषणम् । जीर्णं तेन सहान्नेन प्रह्लादेन सुतेन ते ॥८॥

“King of the Daityas, the fearful poison given by us to your son has been digested by him along with his food, as if it were innocent.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — daityarāja — O king of the Daityas; viṣam — poison; dattam — given; asmābhiḥ — by us; atibhīṣaṇam — exceedingly fearful; jīrṇam — digested; tena — by him; saha annena — along with food; prahlādena — by Prahlāda; sutena te — your son.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

daityarāja viṣaṃ dattam asmābhir atibhīṣaṇam . jīrṇaṃ tena sahānnena prahlādena sutena te ||८||

VP 1.18.9

त्वर्यतां त्वर्यतां हे हे सद्यो दैत्यपुरोहिताः । कृत्यां तस्य विनाशाय उत्पादयत मा चिरात् ॥९॥

Hiraṇyakaśipu, on hearing this, exclaimed, “Hasten, hasten, ministrant priests of the Daitya race! instantly perform the rites that will effect his destruction!”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — tvaryatām — let there be haste; tvaryatām — let there be haste; he he — O, O; sadyaḥ — immediately; daityapurohitāḥ — priests of the Daityas; kṛtyām — a magical destructive being; tasya vināśāya — for his destruction; utpādayata — produce; mā cirāt — without delay.

Śrīdhara

Words — sarvasmād brahmaśaktir adhiketi matvā brāhmaṇān niyuṅktetvaryatām iti; sense — Thinking that Brahmanical power is superior to every other power, he commands the Brahmans, saying, “Hurry.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tvaryatāṃ tvaryatāṃ he he sadyo daityapurohitāḥ . kṛtyāṃ tasya vināśāya utpādayata mā cirāt ||९||

Śrīdhara

sarvasmād brahmaśaktir adhiketi matvā brāhmaṇān niyuṅktetvaryatām iti ||९||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सर्वस्माद् ब्रह्मशक्तिर् अधिकेति मत्वा ब्राह्मणान् नियुङ्क्तेत्वर्यताम् इति ॥९॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Thinking that Brahmanical power is superior to every other power, he commands the Brahmans, saying, “Hurry.”

VP 1.18.10

सकाशम् आगम्य ततः प्रह्लादस्य पुरोहिताः । सामपूर्वम् अथोचुस् ते प्रह्लादं विनयान्वितम् ॥१०॥

Then the priests went to Prahlāda, and, having repeated the hymns of the Sāma-Veda, said to him, as he respectfully hearkened,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — sakaśam āgamya — having come near; tataḥ — then; prahlādasya — of Prahlāda; purohitāḥ — the priests; sāmapūrvam — beginning with Sāman hymns; atha — then; ūcuḥ — said; te — they; prahlādam — to Prahlāda; vinayānvitam — respectfully or with humility.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sakāśam āgamya tataḥ prahlādasya purohitāḥ . sāmapūrvam athocus te prahlādaṃ vinayānvitam ||१०||

VP 1.18.11

जातस् त्रैलोक्यविख्यात आयुष्मन् ब्रह्मणः कुले । दैत्यराजस्य तनयो हिरण्यकशिपोर् भवान् ॥११॥

“Thou hast been born, prince, in the family of Brahmā, celebrated in the three worlds, the son of Hiraṇyakaśipu, the king of the Daityas;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — jātaḥ — born; trailokyavikhyāta — renowned in the three worlds; āyuṣman — long-lived prince; brahmaṇaḥ kule — in the family of Brahmā; daityarājasya tanayaḥ — son of the Daitya king; hiraṇyakaśipoḥ bhavān — you are Hiraṇyakaśipu’s.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jātas trailokyavikhyāta āyuṣman brahmaṇaḥ kule . daityarājasya tanayo hiraṇyakaśipor bhavān ||११||

VP 1.18.12

किं देवैः किम् अनन्तेन किम् अन्येन तवाश्रयः । पिता ते सर्वलोकानां त्वं तथैव भविष्यसि ॥१२॥

why shouldest thou acknowledge dependence upon the gods? why upon the eternal? Thy father is the stay of all the worlds, as thou thyself in turn shalt be.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — kim devaiḥ — why [depend] on the gods?; kim anantena — why [depend] on Ananta, the eternal?; kim anyena tava āśrayaḥ — why seek refuge in another?; pitā te sarvalokānām — your father is the support of all the worlds; tvam tathaiva bhaviṣyasi — you too will become likewise.

Śrīdhara

Words — te pitā sarvalokānām āśrayaḥ; sense — He is the support of all the worlds—your father.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kiṃ devaiḥ kim anantena kim anyena tavāśrayaḥ . pitā te sarvalokānāṃ tvaṃ tathaiva bhaviṣyasi ||१२||

Śrīdhara

te pitā sarvalokānām āśrayaḥ ||१२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ते पिता सर्वलोकानाम् आश्रयः ॥१२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He is the support of all the worlds—your father.

VP 1.18.13

तस्मात् परित्यजैनां त्वं विपक्षस्तवसंहिताम् । वाचं पिता समस्तानां गुरूणां परमो गुरुः ॥१३॥

Desist, then, from celebrating the praises of an enemy; and remember, that of all venerable preceptors, a father is most venerable.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — tasmāt — therefore; parityaja — abandon; enām — this; tvam — you; vipakṣastavasaṃhitām vācam — speech consisting of praise of the enemy; pitā — a father; samastānām gurūṇām — of all teachers; paramaḥ guruḥ — the highest teacher.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasmāt parityajaināṃ tvaṃ vipakṣastavasaṃhitām . vācaṃ pitā samastānāṃ gurūṇāṃ paramo guruḥ ||१३||

VP 1.18.14

एवम् एतन् महाभागाः श्लाघ्यम् एतन् महाकुलम् । मरीचेः सकलेऽप्यस्मिन् त्रैलोक्ये कोऽन्यथा वदेत् ॥१४॥

Prahlāda replied to them, “Illustrious Brahmans, it is true that the family of Marīchi is renowned in the three worlds; this cannot be denied:

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — evam — thus; etat — this; mahābhāgāḥ — illustrious Brahmans; ślāghyam — praiseworthy; etat mahākulam — this great family; marīceḥ — of Marīci; sakale api asmin trailokye — in all these three worlds; kaḥ anyathā vadet — who would say otherwise?

Śrīdhara

Words — brahmaṇaḥ marīceḥ kule; sense — “In the family of Brahmā” means in Marīci’s family.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evam etan mahābhāgāḥ ślāghyam etan mahākulam . marīceḥ sakale’pyasmin trailokye ko’nyathā vadet ||१४||

Śrīdhara

brahmaṇaḥ marīceḥ kule ||१४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ब्रह्मणः मरीचेः कुले ॥१४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“In the family of Brahmā” means in Marīci’s family.

VP 1.18.15

पिता च मम सर्वस्मिन् जगत्य् उत्कृष्टचेष्टितः । एतद् अप्यवगच्छामि सत्यम् अत्रापि नानृतम् ॥१५॥

and I also admit, what is equally indisputable, that my father is mighty over the universe.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — pitā ca mama — and my father; sarvasmin jagati — in the whole world; utkṛṣṭaceṣṭitaḥ — eminent in conduct or power; etat api avagacchāmi — I understand this too; satyam — true; atra api na anṛtam — here also there is no falsehood.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pitā ca mama sarvasmin jagaty utkṛṣṭaceṣṭitaḥ . etad apyavagacchāmi satyam atrāpi nānṛtam ||१५||

VP 1.18.16

गुरुऊणाम् अपि सर्वेषां पिता परमको गुरुः । यद् उक्तं भ्रान्तिर् अत्रापि स्वल्पापि न हि विद्‎‎यते ॥१६॥

There is no error, not the least, in what you have said, “that a father is the most venerable of all holy teachers:” he is a venerable instructor, no doubt, and is ever to be devoutly reverenced.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — gurūṇām api sarveṣām — of all teachers too; pitā — a father; paramakaḥ guruḥ — the highest teacher; yat uktam — what was said; bhrāntiḥ — error; atra api — here too; svalpā api na hi vidyate — there is not even the slightest.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

guruūṇām api sarveṣāṃ pitā paramako guruḥ . yad uktaṃ bhrāntir atrāpi svalpāpi na hi vid‎‎yate ||१६||

VP 1.18.17

पिता गुरुर् न सन्देहः पूजनीयः प्रयत्नतः । तत्रापि एवं मनसि मे स्थितम् ॥१७॥

To all these things I have nothing to object; they find a ready assent in my mind:

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — pitā guruḥ — a father is a teacher; na sandehaḥ — without doubt; pūjanīyaḥ — to be honored; prayatnataḥ — carefully; tatra api — in this matter too; evam — thus; manasi me sthitam — it is settled in my mind.

Śrīdhara

Words — nāparādhyāmi nānyathā karomi; sense — I do not offend; I do not act otherwise.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pitā gurur na sandehaḥ pūjanīyaḥ prayatnataḥ . tatrāpi evaṃ manasi me sthitam ||१७||

Śrīdhara

nāparādhyāmi nānyathā karomi ||१७||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नापराध्यामि नान्यथा करोमि ॥१७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

I do not offend; I do not act otherwise.

VP 1.18.18

यद् एतत् किम् अनन्तेनेत्य् उक्तं युष्माभिर् ईदृशम् । को ब्रवीति यथायुक्तं किन्तु नैतद् वचोऽर्थवत् ॥१८॥

but when you say, “Why should I depend upon the eternal?” who can give assent to this as right? the words are void of meaning.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — yat etat — as for this; kim anantena iti — “what need is there of Ananta?”; uktam — was said; yuṣmābhiḥ — by you; īdṛśam — such; kaḥ bravīti — who says; yathāyuktam — properly or reasonably; kintu — but; na etat vacaḥ arthavat — this speech is not meaningful.

Śrīdhara

Words — yathā yair yair doṣair etad ayuktam, tathā sarvān doṣān ko bravīti ? doṣānantyān na ko’pi vaktuṃ śaknotītyarthaḥ . tathāpi kiñcid vakṣyāmīty āhakin tv iti; sense — Who can state all the faults by which this is improper? Since the faults are endless, no one can state them all. Nevertheless, intending to say something, he says “but.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yad etat kim anantenety uktaṃ yuṣmābhir īdṛśam . ko bravīti yathāyuktaṃ kintu naitad vaco’rthavat ||१८||

Śrīdhara

yathā yair yair doṣair etad ayuktam, tathā sarvān doṣān ko bravīti ? doṣānantyān na ko’pi vaktuṃ śaknotītyarthaḥ . tathāpi kiñcid vakṣyāmīty āhakin tv iti ||१८||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यथा यैर् यैर् दोषैर् एतद् अयुक्तम्, तथा सर्वान् दोषान् को ब्रवीति ? दोषानन्त्यान् न कोऽपि वक्तुं शक्नोतीत्यर्थः । तथापि किञ्चिद् वक्ष्यामीत्य् आहकिन्त्व् इति ॥१८॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Who can state all the faults by which this is improper? Since the faults are endless, no one can state them all. Nevertheless, intending to say something, he says “but.”

VP 1.18.19

इत्य् उक्त्वा सोऽभवन् मौनी तेषां गौरवयन्त्रितः । प्रहस्य च पुनः प्राह किम् अनन्तेन साध्व् इति ॥१९॥

Having said thus much, he was silent a while, being restrained by respect to their sacred functions; but he was unable to repress his smiles, and again said,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — iti uktvā — having said this; saḥ abhavan maunī — he became silent; teṣām gauravayantritaḥ — restrained by respect for them; prahasya — smiling; ca punaḥ prāha — and again said; kim anantena sādhu iti — “what need is there of Ananta? excellent.”

Śrīdhara

Words — kim anantena sādhv ityādi punaruktiḥ sopālambhākṣepārthā; sense — The repetition beginning “What need is there of Ananta?” expresses a reproachful objection.

अपाये तत्र पापैश् च पातिता तत्र याजकैः । तान् एव सा जघानाशु कृत्या नाशं जगाम च ॥३४॥
