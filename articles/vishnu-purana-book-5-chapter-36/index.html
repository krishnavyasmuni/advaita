VP 5.36.1

मैत्रेयैतद्बलं तस्य बलस्य बलशालिनः । कृतं यदन्यत्तेनाभूत्तदपि श्रूयतां त्वया

Sri Paraasharar - Balaraama tried to pull Yamunaa river itself along with Hastinaapura city. I told you that wonderful act of Balaraama. The other wonderful act of killing Dvivida Vaanara, also I will tell you.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreyaitadabalaṃ tasya balasya balaśālinaḥ . kṛtaṃ yadanyattenābhūttadapi śrūyatāṃ tvayā — Sri Paraasharar - Balaraama tried to pull Yamunaa river itself along with Hastinaapura city. I told you that wonderful act of Balaraama. The other wonderful act of killing Dvivida Vaanara, also I will tell you.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreyaitadbalaṃ tasya balasya balaśālinaḥ . kṛtaṃ yadanyattenābhūttadapi śrūyatāṃ tvayā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.36.2

नरकस्यासुरेद्रस्य देवपक्षविरोधिनः । सखाऽभवन्महावीर्यो द्विविदो वानरर्षभः

That Narakaasura, who was an enemy of all the gods, had a great friend who was a very powerful Vaanara, called Dvivida.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

narakasyāsurendrasya devapakṣavirodhinaḥ . sakhābhavanmahāvīryo dvivido vānararṣabhaḥ — That Narakaasura, who was an enemy of all the gods, had a great friend who was a very powerful Vaanara, called Dvivida.

Śrīdhara

maitreya śrūyatām iti yad uktaṃ tad eva vivṛṇoti—narakasyeti — That Narakaasura, who was an enemy of all the gods, had a great friend who was a very powerful Vaanara, called Dvivida.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

narakasyāsuredrasya devapakṣavirodhinaḥ . sakhā’bhavanmahāvīryo dvivido vānararṣabhaḥ

Śrīdhara

maitreya śrūyatām iti yad uktaṃ tad eva vivṛṇoti—narakasyeti ||२|| vairānubandhaṃ balavān sa cakāra surān prati .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मैत्रेय श्रूयताम् इति यद् उक्तं तद् एव विवृणोति—नरकस्येति ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

That Narakaasura, who was an enemy of all the gods, had a great friend who was a very powerful Vaanara, called Dvivida.

VP 5.36.4

करिष्ये सर्वदेवानां तस्मादेतत्प्रतीक्रियाम् । यज्ञविध्वंसनं कुर्वन्मर्त्यलोकक्षयं तथा

He decided that he will take revenge against all the gods. That he will destroy all the yaagaas, because the gods get food only when yaagaas are performed, when havis is offered in the yaagaas. If yaagaas are not done, the gods don't get food. So, he thought that he will destroy all the yajnyaas, and then destroy the earth where the people live.

That he will destroy all the yaagaas, because the gods get food only when yaagaas are performed, when havis is offered in the yaagaas. If yaagaas are not done, the gods don't get food. So, he thought that he will destroy all the yajnyaas, and then destroy the earth where the people live.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

No commentary.

VP 5.36.12

उद्गीयमानो विलसल्ललनामौलिमध्यगः । रेमे यदुकुलश्रेष्ठः कुबेर इव नन्दने

Śrīdhara

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

ekadeti . raivatako dvārakāyāḥ pūrvato giriḥ tat-sambandhiny udyāne ||११|| udgīyamāno vilasal-lalanā-mauli-madhyagaḥ . reme yadu-kula-śreṣṭhaḥ kubera ivanandane७४५||१२|| viṣṇu-cittaḥ* : *udgīyamāna iti . lalanā-maulayaḥ vilāsinī-śreṣṭhāḥ aśoka-drumo vā mauliḥ kirīṭe cūḍāyām aśoke keśa-saṃhatau iti nighaṇṭuḥ . tadā lalanopalakṣitāśoka-vanāntasthaḥ ||१२|| śrīdharaḥ — udgīyamāna iti . vilasal-lalanā vilāsinyaḥ tāsāṃ maulayaḥ śreṣṭhāḥ, tāsāṃ madhyagaḥ ||१२||*.* tataḥ sa vānaro’bhyetya gṛhītvā sīriṇo halam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एकदेति । रैवतको द्वारकायाः पूर्वतो गिरिः तत्-सम्बन्धिन्य् उद्याने ॥११॥
रेमे यदु-कुल-श्रेष्ठः कुबेर इवनन्दने७४५॥१२॥
विष्णु-चित्तः* : *उद्गीयमान इति । ललना-मौलयः विलासिनी-श्रेष्ठाः अशोक-द्रुमो वा मौलिः किरीटे चूडायाम् अशोके केश-संहतौ इति निघण्टुः । तदा ललनोपलक्षिताशोक-वनान्तस्थः ॥१२॥
श्रीधरः — उद्गीयमान इति । विलसल्-ललना विलासिन्यः तासां मौलयः श्रेष्ठाः, तासां मध्यगः ॥१२॥*.*

Śrīdhara commentary.

Either it could be the Ashoka tree, or all the women there. He was singing and enjoying with all the women there, and he was shining like Kubera in Nandanavana.

VP 5.36.13
