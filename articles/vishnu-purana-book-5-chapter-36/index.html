VP 5.36.24

एवंविधान्यनेकानि बलदेवस्य धीमतः । कर्माण्यपरिमेयानि शेषस्य धरणीभृतः

Sri Paraasharar - Like this, there are wonderful acts which Balaraama has done, which are incomparable. He has done many such wonderful acts. Who is none other than Shesha, Aadishesha, who is supporting all the worlds. This completes Chapter Thirty Six. ॥
