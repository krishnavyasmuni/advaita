VP 5.36.1

श्रीपराशर उवाच
मैत्रेयैतद्बलं तस्य बलस्य बलशालिनः
कृतं यदन्यत्तेनाभूत्तदपि श्रूयतां त्वया

Maitreya, hear what other deed was performed by that mighty Balabhadra.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tat api śrūyatām — let that also be heard;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreyaitadbalaṃ tasya balasya balaśālinaḥ | kṛtaṃ yadanyattenābhūttadapi śrūyatāṃ tvayā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.36.2

नरकस्यासुरेन्द्रस्य देवपक्षविरोधिनः
सखाभवन्महावीर्यो द्विविदो वानरर्षभः

Dvivida, a great hero and bull among monkeys, became the friend of Naraka, the asura king hostile to the gods.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
narakasya — of Naraka;
asurendrasya — of the asura king;
deva-pakṣa-virodhinaḥ — hostile to the gods;
sakhā abhavat — became a friend;
mahā-vīryaḥ — great hero;
dvividaḥ — Dvivida;
vānara-ṛṣabhaḥ — bull among monkeys;
Śrīdhara
maitreya — O Maitreya;
śrūyatām — let it be heard;
iti yat uktam — what was said;
tat eva vivṛṇoti — he explains that very thing;
narakasya iti — with “narakasya”;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

narakasyāsurendrasya devapakṣavirodhinaḥ | sakhābhavanmahāvīryo dvivido vānararṣabhaḥ

Śrīdhara

maitreya śrūyatām iti yad uktaṃ tad eva vivṛṇoti—narakasyeti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मैत्रेय श्रूयताम् इति यद् उक्तं तद् एव विवृणोति—नरकस्येति ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara says that the words “Maitreya, hear” introduce the explanation of what was just promised; he now explains the verse beginning “of Naraka.”
