VP 5.36.1

मैत्रेयैतद्बलं तस्य बलस्य बलशालिनः।
कृतं यदन्यत्तेनाभूत्तदपि श्रूयतां त्वया

Sri Paraasharar - Balaraama tried to pull Yamunaa river itself along with Hastinaapura city. I told you that wonderful act of Balaraama. The other wonderful act of killing Dvivida Vaanara, also I will tell you.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreya etad balam tasya — Maitreya, this strength of his; balasya balaśālinaḥ — of the mighty Baladeva; kṛtaṃ yad anyat tena abhūt — whatever else was done by him; tad api śrūyatāṃ tvayā — let that too be heard by you

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreyaitadbalaṃ tasya balasya balaśālinaḥ . kṛtaṃ yadanyattenābhūttadapi śrūyatāṃ tvayā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.36.2

नरकस्यासुरेद्रस्य देवपक्षविरोधिनः।
सखाऽभवन्महावीर्यो द्विविदो वानरर्षभः

That Narakaasura, who was an enemy of all the gods, had a great friend who was a very powerful Vaanara, called Dvivida.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

narakasya asurendrasya — of Naraka, lord of the demons; deva-pakṣa-virodhinaḥ — the enemy of the gods; sakhā abhavat — became a friend; mahā-vīryaḥ — of great valor; dvividaḥ vānara-ṛṣabhaḥ — Dvivida, the bull among monkeys

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

narakasyāsuredrasya devapakṣavirodhinaḥ . sakhā’bhavanmahāvīryo dvivido vānararṣabhaḥ

Śrīdhara

maitreya śrūyatām iti yad uktaṃ tad eva vivṛṇoti—narakasyeti ||२|| vairānubandhaṃ balavān sa cakāra surān prati .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मैत्रेय श्रूयताम् इति यद् उक्तं तद् एव विवृणोति—नरकस्येति ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

That Narakaasura, who was an enemy of all the gods, had a great friend who was a very powerful Vaanara, called Dvivida.

VP 5.36.3

वैरानुबन्धं बलवान्स चकार सुरान्प्रति।
नरकं हतवान्कृष्णो देवराजेन चोदितः

This Dvivida came to know that Indra went to Krishna, and told him to kill Narakaasura, so, he got extremely angry with the devaas. Having come to know that Narakaasura was killed by Krishna only because of Indra.

VP 5.36.4

करिष्ये सर्वदेवानां तस्मादेतत्प्रतीक्रियाम्।
यज्ञविध्वंसनं कुर्वन्मर्त्यलोकक्षयं तथा

He decided that he will take revenge against all the gods. That he will destroy all the yaagaas, because the gods get food only when yaagaas are performed, when havis is offered in the yaagaas. If yaagaas are not done, the gods don't get food. So, he thought that he will destroy all the yajnyaas, and then destroy the earth where the people live.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kariṣye sarva-devānāṃ — I will make for all the gods; tasmāt etat pratikriyām — therefore this retaliation; yajña-vidhvaṃsanaṃ kurvan — causing destruction of sacrifices; martya-loka-kṣayaṃ tathā — and destruction of the mortal world likewise

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kariṣye sarvadevānāṃ tasmādetatpratīkriyām . yajñavidhvaṃsanaṃ kurvanmartyalokakṣayaṃ tathā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.36.5

बिभेद साधुमर्यादां क्षयं चक्रे च देहिनाम्

Dvivida who was a very powerful Vaanara, started to destroy all the yajnyaas, being covered by ignorance. He transgressed all the saadhu maryaadaas, and disrespected all the saadhus, and even killed all the people.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

He burnt the Savana and other deshaas, the cities, the smaller cities, the villages. Sometimes, he took the peak of a mountain, and destroyed the city with that, by throwing the peak into the city. The cities were blown into powders.

VP 5.36.12

उद्गीयमानो विलसल्ललनामौलिमध्यगः।
रेमे यदुकुलश्रेष्ठः कुबेर इव नन्दने

Either it could be the Ashoka tree, or all the women there. He was singing and enjoying with all the women there, and he was shining like Kubera in Nandanavana.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

udgīyamānaḥ vilasan — singing and shining; lalana-mouli-madhya-gaḥ — amid the women’s head-ornaments; reme yadu-kula-śreṣṭhaḥ — the foremost of the Yadu family sported; kubera iva mandare — like Kubera on Mandara

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

udgīyamāno vilasallalanāmaulimadhyagaḥ . reme yadukulaśreṣṭhaḥ kubera iva nandane

Śrīdhara

ekadeti . raivatako dvārakāyāḥ pūrvato giriḥ tat-sambandhiny udyāne ||११|| udgīyamāno vilasal-lalanā-mauli-madhyagaḥ . reme yadu-kula-śreṣṭhaḥ kubera ivanandane||१२|| viṣṇu-cittaḥ* : *udgīyamāna iti . lalanā-maulayaḥ vilāsinī-śreṣṭhāḥ aśoka-drumo vā mauliḥ kirīṭe cūḍāyām aśoke keśa-saṃhatau iti nighaṇṭuḥ . tadā lalanopalakṣitāśoka-vanāntasthaḥ ||१२|| śrīdharaḥ — udgīyamāna iti . vilasal-lalanā vilāsinyaḥ tāsāṃ maulayaḥ śreṣṭhāḥ, tāsāṃ madhyagaḥ ||१२||*.* tataḥ sa vānaro’bhyetya gṛhītvā sīriṇo halam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एकदेति । रैवतको द्वारकायाः पूर्वतो गिरिः तत्-सम्बन्धिन्य् उद्याने ॥११॥
रेमे यदु-कुल-श्रेष्ठः कुबेर इवनन्दने७४५॥१२॥
विष्णु-चित्तः* : *उद्गीयमान इति । ललना-मौलयः विलासिनी-श्रेष्ठाः अशोक-द्रुमो वा मौलिः किरीटे चूडायाम् अशोके केश-संहतौ इति निघण्टुः । तदा ललनोपलक्षिताशोक-वनान्तस्थः ॥१२॥
श्रीधरः — उद्गीयमान इति । विलसल्-ललना विलासिन्यः तासां मौलयः श्रेष्ठाः, तासां मध्यगः ॥१२॥*.*

Śrīdhara commentary.

Either it could be the Ashoka tree, or all the women there. He was singing and enjoying with all the women there, and he was shining like Kubera in Nandanavana.

VP 5.36.13

ततःस वानरोऽभ्येत्य गृहीत्वा सीरिणो हलम्।
मुसलं च चकारास्य संमुखं च विडंबनम्

Then, Dvivida came and picked up Balaraama's plough, the halaayudha. And then threw it on Balaraama himself as though he was going to hit him with that. Dvivida made faces, and teased Balaraama with his halaayudha itself.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Then, Dvivida came and picked up Balaraama's plough, the halaayudha. And then threw it on Balaraama himself as though he was going to hit him with that. Dvivida made faces, and teased Balaraama with his halaayudha itself.

VP 5.36.20

पतता तच्छरीरेण गिरेः शृगमशीर्यत।
मैत्रेय शतधा वज्रिवज्रेणेव विदारितम्

Dvivida was so huge, that when Balaraama hit him with his fist on the head, the head fell on the mountain, and the mountain's peak itself broke because of the weight of his head. It was as though Indra hit something with a Vajraayudha, and broke it. The hit from Balaraama's fist was so powerful.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

patatā tat śarīreṇa — by the falling body; gireḥ śṛṅgam aśīryata — the mountain peak was shattered; maitreya śata-dhā — Maitreya, into a hundred pieces; vajri-vajreṇa iva vidāritam — as though torn by Indra’s thunderbolt

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

patatā taccharīreṇa gireḥ śṛgamaśīryata . maitreya śatadhā vajrivajreṇeva vidāritam

Śrīdhara

patateti . aśīryata viśīrṇam abhavat ||२०|| puṣpa-vṛṣṭiṃ tato devā rāmasyopari cikṣipuḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पततेति । अशीर्यत विशीर्णम् अभवत् ॥२०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Dvivida was so huge, that when Balaraama hit him with his fist on the head, the head fell on the mountain, and the mountain's peak itself broke because of the weight of his head. It was as though Indra hit something with a Vajraayudha, and broke it. The hit from Balaraama's fist was so powerful.
