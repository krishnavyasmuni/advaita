VP 5.30.7

प्रणेतर्मनो बुद्धेरिन्द्रियाणां गुणात्मक ।
त्रिगुणातीत निर्द्वन्द्व शुद्धसत्त्व हृदि स्थित

O director of mind, intellect, and senses, whose nature is the guṇas; beyond the three guṇas, free of duality, pure sattva, abiding in the heart.

Śrīdhara translation

“Praṇetā” means “director, agent.” By “whose nature is the guṇas”: tamas makes [you] the source of beings, sattva the director of mind, and rajas the director of intellect and senses. In reality, however, [you are] beyond the three guṇas, therefore free from duality; thus you abide in the hearts of knowers of pure sattva.

VP 5.30.80

सकलभुवनसूतिर्मूर्तिरल्पाल्पसूक्ष्मा विदितसकलवेद्यैर्ज्ञायते यस्य नान्यैः । तमजमकृतमीशं शाश्वतं स्वेच्छयैनं जगदुपकृतिमर्त्यं को विजेतुं समर्थः

“His form, source of all worlds, is minute and subtle; he is known by those who know all that is to be known, and not by others. That unborn, uncreated, eternal Lord, mortal by his own will for the world’s welfare—who is capable of conquering him?”

Śrīdhara translation

“Uncreated” means an Īśa established by himself; his lordship is not caused by karma. For the world’s welfare, by his own will he is mortal, an incarnation among mortals.
