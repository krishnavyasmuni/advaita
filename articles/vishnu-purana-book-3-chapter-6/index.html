VP 3.6.1

सामवेदतरोः शाखा व्यासशिष्यस्य जौमिनिः । क्रमेण येन मैत्रेय विभेदशृणु तन्मम

O Maitreya, hear from me how Jaimini, Vyāsa’s disciple, divided the branches of the Sāmaveda in due order.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sāmavedataroḥ śākhā — branches of the Sāmaveda tree; vyāsasśiṣyaḥ saḥ jaiminiḥ — Jaimini, Vyāsa’s disciple; krameṇa — in due order; bibheda — divided; śṛṇu tat mama — hear that from me

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sāmavedataroḥ śākhā vyāsaśiṣyasya jauminiḥ । krameṇa yena maitreya vibhedaśṛṇu tanmama ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.6.2

सुसंतुस्तस्य पुत्रोभूत्सूत्वानश्चाभवत्सुतः । अधीतवन्तौ चैकैकां संहितां तौ माहमती

Jaimini’s son was Sumantu, and Sumantu’s son was Sutvan. Those two great-minded men each studied one Saṃhitā.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sumantuḥ tasya putraḥ — Sumantu was his son; sukarmā asya api sutaḥ — Sukarman was also his son; adhītavantau — studied; ekām ekām saṃhitām — one Saṃhitā each; tau mahāmatī — those two great-minded men

Śrīdhara

sāmnaḥ atha atharvaṇaḥ śākhābhedāḥ — divisions of the Sāman and Atharvan branches; paurāṇikāḥ — Purāṇic; adhyetāraḥ kartāraḥ — students and makers; tasya jaimineḥ — of that Jaimini; putrapautrau — son and grandson; svakāle — in their respective times; adhītavantau — studied

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

susaṃtustasya putrobhūtsūtvānaścābhavatsutaḥ । adhītavantau caikaikāṃ saṃhitāṃ tau māhamatī ॥२॥

Śrīdhara

sāmno’thātharvaṇaḥ śākhābhedāḥ paurāṇikā api । adhyetāraśca kartārasteṣāṃ ṣaṣṭhe nirūpitāḥ । tasya jaimineḥ । asya jaiminiputrasya sumanto । tau jaimine putrapautrau jaimininā vibhaktāmekaikāṃ saṃhitāṃ sva-svakāle’dhītavantau

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

साम्नोऽथाथर्वणः शाखाभेदाः पौराणिका अपि । अध्येतारश्च कर्तारस्तेषां षष्ठे निरूपिताः । तस्य जैमिनेः । अस्य जैमिनिपुत्रस्य सुमन्तो । तौ जैमिने पुत्रपौत्रौ जैमिनिना विभक्तामेकैकां संहितां स्व-स्वकालेऽधीतवन्तौ

Śrīdhara commentary.

The Purāṇic divisions of the Sāman and Atharvan branches, together with their students and makers, are described in this sixth chapter. “His” refers to Jaimini, and “his son” to Jaimini’s son Sumantu. The son and grandson studied one Saṃhitā each from their respective fathers, at their own times, not simultaneously.

VP 3.6.3

सहस्रसंहिताभेदं सुकर्मातत्सुतस्ततः । चकार तं च तच्छिष्यौ जगृहाते महाव्रतौ

Sukarman, his son, then made a thousand divisions of the Saṃhitā, and his two great-vowed disciples received them.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sahasrasaṃhitābhedam — a thousand divisions of the Saṃhitā; sukarmā tatsutaḥ — Sukarman, his son; tataḥ — then; cakāra — made; tat — that; tacchiṣyau jagṛhāte — his two disciples received; mahāvratau — greatly vowed

Śrīdhara

tatsutaḥ — his son; sumantusutaḥ — Sumantu’s son; tatsaṃhitābhedam — division of that Saṃhitā

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sahasrasaṃhitābhedaṃ sukarmātatsutastataḥ । cakāra taṃ ca tacchiṣyau jagṛhāte mahāvratau ॥३॥

Śrīdhara

tatsutaḥ sumantusutaḥ tatsaṃhitābhedam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत्सुतः सुमन्तुसुतः तत्संहिताभेदम्

Śrīdhara commentary.

“His son” means Sumantu’s son, Sukarman, and “that division of the Saṃhitā” means the division of Sumantu’s Saṃhitā.

VP 3.6.4

हिरण्यनाभः कौसल्यः पौष्पिंजिश्च द्विजोत्तम । उदीच्याःसामगाः शिष्यास्तस्य पञ्चशतं स्मृताः

Hiraṇyanābha, the Kauśalya, and Pauṣpiñji, O best of the twice-born, are remembered as his five hundred northern Sāman-singing disciples.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hiraṇyanābhaḥ — Hiraṇyanābha; kausalyāḥ pauṣpiñjiḥ — Kauśalya and Pauṣpiñji; dvijottama — O best of the twice-born; udīcyāḥ sāmagaḥ — northern Sāman-singers; śiṣyāḥ tasya — his disciples; pañcaśatam — five hundred; smṛtāḥ — are remembered

Śrīdhara

sukarmaṇaḥ śiṣyān āha — he speaks of Sukarman’s disciples; hiraṇyanābha — Hiraṇyanābha; kauśalya — Kauśalya, an epithet; pañcadaśa śiṣyāḥ — fifteen disciples; pañcadaśa saṃhitāḥ — fifteen Saṃhitās; udīcyasāmagaḥ — northern Sāmagas

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hiraṇyanābhaḥ kausalyaḥ pauṣpiṃjiśca dvijottama । udīcyāḥsāmagāḥ śiṣyāstasya pañcaśataṃ smṛtāḥ ॥४॥

Śrīdhara

sukarmaṇaḥ śiṣyāvāha—hiraṇyanāma iti kauśalya iti hiraṇyanābhaviśeṣaṇam । ye pañcadaśa śiṣyāstebhyaḥ pañcadaśa saṃhitā hiraṇyanābhena dattāsta eva udīcyasāmagāḥ smṛtāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सुकर्मणः शिष्यावाह—हिरण्यनाम इति कौशल्य इति हिरण्यनाभविशेषणम् । ये पञ्चदश शिष्यास्तेभ्यः पञ्चदश संहिता हिरण्यनाभेन दत्तास्त एव उदीच्यसामगाः स्मृताः

Śrīdhara commentary.

The commentator now speaks of Sukarman’s disciples. Kauśalya is an epithet of Hiraṇyanābha. The fifteen disciples mentioned in this gloss received fifteen Saṃhitās from Hiraṇyanābha and are remembered as the northern Sāmagas.

VP 3.6.5

हिरण्यनाभात्तावत्यःसंहिता यैर्द्विजोत्तमैः । गृहीतास्तेपि चोच्यन्ते पण्डितैः प्राच्यसामगाः

The Saṃhitās received from Hiraṇyanābha by the excellent Brahmins who learned them are called by the learned the eastern Sāmagas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hiraṇyanābhāt — from Hiraṇyanābha; tāvatyaḥ saṃhitāḥ — that many Saṃhitās; yaiḥ gṛhītāḥ — by whom received; dvijottamaiḥ — by excellent Brahmins; te api — they too; procyante — are called; prācyasāmagaḥ — eastern Sāmagas

Śrīdhara

yaiḥ aparaiḥ dvijottamaiḥ — by the other excellent Brahmins; tāvatyaḥ saṃhitāḥ — that many Saṃhitās; grahītṛṇām saṅkhyayā — according to the number of recipients; hiraṇyanābhāt gṛhītāḥ — received from Hiraṇyanābha; prācyasāmagaḥ — eastern Sāmagas

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hiraṇyanābhāttāvatyaḥsaṃhitā yairdvijottamaiḥ । gṛhītāstepi cocyante paṇḍitaiḥ prācyasāmagāḥ ॥५॥

Śrīdhara

yairaparairdvijottamaistāvatyasteṣāṃ grahītṛṇāṃ saṅkhyayā saṅkhyātāḥ saṃhitā hiraṇyanābhād gṛhītāḥ, te prācyasāmagā ucyanta ityanvayaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यैरपरैर्द्विजोत्तमैस्तावत्यस्तेषां ग्रहीतृणां सङ्ख्यया सङ्ख्याताः संहिता हिरण्यनाभाद् गृहीताः, ते प्राच्यसामगा उच्यन्त इत्यन्वयः

Śrīdhara commentary.

The excellent Brahmins who received the Saṃhitās from Hiraṇyanābha were counted according to the number of recipients; they are called the eastern Sāmagas.

VP 3.6.6

लोकाक्षिर्नैधमिश्चैव कक्षिवांल्लाङ्गलिस्तथा । पौष्पिजिशिष्यास्तद्भेदैः संहिता बहुलीकृताः

Lōkākṣi, Kaumudi, Kakṣīvat, and Lāṅgali were Pauṣpiñji’s disciples; through their subdivisions the Saṃhitās became numerous.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

lokākṣiḥ kaunudīḥ kakṣīvān lāṅgaliḥ — Lōkākṣi, Kaumudi, Kakṣīvat, and Lāṅgali; pauṣpiñjiśiṣyāḥ — Pauṣpiñji’s disciples; tadbhedaiḥ — through their subdivisions; saṃhitāḥ bahulīkṛtāḥ — the Saṃhitās were multiplied

Śrīdhara

pauṣpiñjiśiṣyān āha — he speaks of Pauṣpiñji’s disciples; tadbhedaiḥ — through their divisions; śiṣyapraśiṣyādibhiḥ — through disciples, pupils, and the rest

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

lokākṣirnaidhamiścaiva kakṣivāṃllāṅgalistathā । pauṣpijiśiṣyāstadbhedaiḥ saṃhitā bahulīkṛtāḥ ॥६॥

Śrīdhara

pauṣpiñjiśiṣyānāha—lokākṣir iti । tadbhedaiḥ tacchiṣyapraśiṣyādibhiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पौष्पिञ्जिशिष्यानाह—लोकाक्षिर् इति । तद्भेदैः तच्छिष्यप्रशिष्यादिभिः

Śrīdhara commentary.

The commentator names Pauṣpiñji’s disciples. “Through their subdivisions” means through their disciples, pupils, and the rest.

VP 3.6.7

हिरण्यनाभशिष्यस्तु चतुर्विशतिसंहिताः । प्रोवाच कृतनामासौ शिष्येभ्यश्च महामुनिः

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

hiraṇyanābhasya śiṣyamadhye — among Hiraṇyanābha’s disciples; udīcyasāmaga — northern Sāmaga; kṛtināmā — named Kṛti

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Kṛti was one of Hiraṇyanābha’s disciples among the northern Sāmagas.

VP 3.6.8

taiḥ kṛtināmnaḥ śiṣyaiḥ

VP 3.6.12

Śrīdhara commentary.

Jājali and the others received the three Saṃhitās made by Pathya; they divided their own Saṃhitās into distinct forms.

VP 3.6.13

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 3.6.14

चतुर्थःस्यादाङ्गिरसः शान्तिकल्पश्चपञ्चमः । श्रेष्ठास्त्वथर्वणामेते संहितानां विकल्पकाः

The fourth is Āṅgirasa-kalpa and the fifth Śānti-kalpa. These are the principal variants of the Atharvan Saṃhitās.

उक्तेष्वष्टादशपुराणेषु किमेतत् पुराणं त्वया कथ्यत इत्याह—यदेतदिति मया यदेतत् तव कथ्यते, तदेवानागताख्यानेन पद्मपुराणानन्तरं व्यासेन कृतमित्यर्थः । यथाह—मात्स्ये, वराहकल्पवृत्तान्तमधिकृत्य पराशरः । यान् प्राह धर्मानखिलांस्तदुक्तं वैष्णवं विदुः । इत्येवमेव ब्रह्मादिकथितं व्यासेन निबद्धं ब्रह्मपुराणादि कथ्यते । विष्णुपुराणञ्च क्वचित् दशसाहस्रं क्वचिदष्टसाहस्रम् इत्य्-आदि विकल्पेऽपि अत्र षट् साहस्रमेव व्याख्यायते

Śrīdhara commentary.

Among the eighteen Purāṇas already mentioned, why is this particular Purāṇa being taught? Śrīdhara answers: what is being taught to you by me is the work made by Vyāsa and placed immediately after the Pādma Purāṇa in the account of what follows. Different traditions give different total lengths for the Viṣṇu Purāṇa; here the six-thousand-verse recension is being explained.

The six Vedāṅgas, the four Vedas, Mīmāṃsā, the extensive Nyāya, the Purāṇas, and Dharmaśāstra—these are the fourteen fields of knowledge.

अस्य वैष्णवसञ्ज्ञायां हेतुम् आह—सर्गे च इति

VP 3.6.28

purāṇānāṃ vedoparbṛṃhaṇatvena dharmavedataddhetutvāt dharmavidyāsthāneṣu caturdaśasvantarbhāvam āha—aṅgāni iti । aṅgāni śikṣākalpajyotiśchandoniruktavyākaraṇāni ṣaṭ । caturaścatvāraḥ । dharmaśāstraṃ manvādiproktasmṛtiśāstram

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पुराणानां वेदोपर्बृंहणत्वेन धर्मवेदतद्धेतुत्वात् धर्मविद्यास्थानेषु चतुर्दशस्वन्तर्भावम् आह—अङ्गानि इति । अङ्गानि शिक्षाकल्पज्योतिश्छन्दोनिरुक्तव्याकरणानि षट् । चतुरश्चत्वारः । धर्मशास्त्रं मन्वादिप्रोक्तस्मृतिशास्त्रम्

Śrīdhara commentary.

The four beginning with Āyurveda are fields of knowledge concerned with visible aims. Āyurveda is the eight-limbed medical science taught by Dhanvantari; Dhanurveda concerns the four kinds of weapon use taught by Bhṛgu; Gāndharvaveda concerns dance and song as taught by Bharata; Arthaśāstra is the political science of Bṛhaspati and others. Together with the fourteen previously named, these make eighteen fields of knowledge.
