VP 3.6.1

साम-वेद-तरोः शाखा व्यास-शिष्यः स जैमिनिः।
क्रमेण येन मैत्रेय बिभेद शृणु तन् मम

O Maitreya, hear from me how Jaimini, Vyāsa’s disciple, divided the branches of the Sāmaveda in due order.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
sāmavedataroḥ śākhā — branches of the Sāmaveda tree;
vyāsasśiṣyaḥ saḥ jaiminiḥ — Jaimini, Vyāsa’s disciple;
krameṇa — in due order;
bibheda — divided;
śṛṇu tat mama — hear that from me
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sāmavedataroḥ śākhā vyāsaśiṣyasya jauminiḥ । krameṇa yena maitreya vibhedaśṛṇu tanmama ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.6.2

सुमन्तुस् तस्य पुत्रोऽभूत् सुकर्मास्याप्य् अभूत् सुतः।
अधीतवन्तौ चैकैकां संहितां तौ महा-मती

Jaimini’s son was Sumantu, and Sumantu’s son was Sutvan. Those two great-minded men each studied one Saṃhitā.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
sumantuḥ tasya putraḥ — Sumantu was his son;
sukarmā asya api sutaḥ — Sukarman was also his son;
adhītavantau — studied;
ekām ekām saṃhitām — one Saṃhitā each;
tau mahāmatī — those two great-minded men
Śrīdhara
sāmnaḥ atha atharvaṇaḥ śākhābhedāḥ — divisions of the Sāman and Atharvan branches;
paurāṇikāḥ — Purāṇic;
adhyetāraḥ kartāraḥ — students and makers;
tasya jaimineḥ — of that Jaimini;
putrapautrau — son and grandson;
svakāle — in their respective times;
adhītavantau — studied
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

susaṃtustasya putrobhūtsūtvānaścābhavatsutaḥ । adhītavantau caikaikāṃ saṃhitāṃ tau māhamatī ॥२॥

Śrīdhara

sāmno’thātharvaṇaḥ śākhābhedāḥ paurāṇikā api । adhyetāraśca kartārasteṣāṃ ṣaṣṭhe nirūpitāḥ । tasya jaimineḥ । asya jaiminiputrasya sumanto । tau jaimine putrapautrau jaimininā vibhaktāmekaikāṃ saṃhitāṃ sva-svakāle’dhītavantau

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

साम्नोऽथाथर्वणः शाखाभेदाः पौराणिका अपि । अध्येतारश्च कर्तारस्तेषां षष्ठे निरूपिताः । तस्य जैमिनेः । अस्य जैमिनिपुत्रस्य सुमन्तो । तौ जैमिने पुत्रपौत्रौ जैमिनिना विभक्तामेकैकां संहितां स्व-स्वकालेऽधीतवन्तौ

Śrīdhara commentary.

The Purāṇic divisions of the Sāman and Atharvan branches, together with their students and makers, are described in this sixth chapter. “His” refers to Jaimini, and “his son” to Jaimini’s son Sumantu. The son and grandson studied one Saṃhitā each from their respective fathers, at their own times, not simultaneously.

VP 3.6.3

सहस्र-संहिता-भेदं सुकर्मा तत्-सुतस् ततः।
चकार तं च तच्-छिष्यौ जगृहाते महा-व्रतौ

Sukarman, his son, then made a thousand divisions of the Saṃhitā, and his two great-vowed disciples received them.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
sahasrasaṃhitābhedam — a thousand divisions of the Saṃhitā;
sukarmā tatsutaḥ — Sukarman, his son;
tataḥ — then;
cakāra — made;
tat — that;
tacchiṣyau jagṛhāte — his two disciples received;
mahāvratau — greatly vowed
Śrīdhara
tatsutaḥ — his son;
sumantusutaḥ — Sumantu’s son;
tatsaṃhitābhedam — division of that Saṃhitā
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sahasrasaṃhitābhedaṃ sukarmātatsutastataḥ । cakāra taṃ ca tacchiṣyau jagṛhāte mahāvratau ॥३॥

Śrīdhara

tatsutaḥ sumantusutaḥ tatsaṃhitābhedam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत्सुतः सुमन्तुसुतः तत्संहिताभेदम्

Śrīdhara commentary.

“His son” means Sumantu’s son, Sukarman, and “that division of the Saṃhitā” means the division of Sumantu’s Saṃhitā.

VP 3.6.4

kauśalya — Kauśalya, an epithet;
pañcadaśa śiṣyāḥ — fifteen disciples;

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hiraṇyanābhaḥ kausalyaḥ pauṣpiṃjiśca dvijottama । udīcyāḥsāmagāḥ śiṣyāstasya pañcaśataṃ smṛtāḥ ॥४॥

Śrīdhara

sukarmaṇaḥ śiṣyāvāha—hiraṇyanāma iti kauśalya iti hiraṇyanābhaviśeṣaṇam । ye pañcadaśa śiṣyāstebhyaḥ pañcadaśa saṃhitā hiraṇyanābhena dattāsta eva udīcyasāmagāḥ smṛtāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सुकर्मणः शिष्यावाह—हिरण्यनाम इति कौशल्य इति हिरण्यनाभविशेषणम् । ये पञ्चदश शिष्यास्तेभ्यः पञ्चदश संहिता हिरण्यनाभेन दत्तास्त एव उदीच्यसामगाः स्मृताः

Śrīdhara commentary.

The commentator now speaks of Sukarman’s disciples. Kauśalya is an epithet of Hiraṇyanābha. The fifteen disciples mentioned in this gloss received fifteen Saṃhitās from Hiraṇyanābha and are remembered as the northern Sāmagas.

VP 3.6.5

हिरण्यनाभात् तावत्यः संहिता यैर् द्विजोत्तमैः।

Śrīdhara

yairaparairdvijottamaistāvatyasteṣāṃ grahītṛṇāṃ saṅkhyayā saṅkhyātāḥ saṃhitā hiraṇyanābhād gṛhītāḥ, te prācyasāmagā ucyanta ityanvayaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यैरपरैर्द्विजोत्तमैस्तावत्यस्तेषां ग्रहीतृणां सङ्ख्यया सङ्ख्याताः संहिता हिरण्यनाभाद् गृहीताः, ते प्राच्यसामगा उच्यन्त इत्यन्वयः

Śrīdhara commentary.

The excellent Brahmins who received the Saṃhitās from Hiraṇyanābha were counted according to the number of recipients; they are called the eastern Sāmagas.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Among the eighteen Purāṇas already mentioned, why is this particular Purāṇa being taught?

The four beginning with Āyurveda are fields of knowledge concerned with visible aims. Āyurveda is the eight-limbed medical science taught by Dhanvantari; Dhanurveda concerns the four kinds of weapon use taught by Bhṛgu; Gāndharvaveda concerns dance and song as taught by Bharata; Arthaśāstra is the political science of Bṛhaspati and others. Together with the fourteen previously named, these make eighteen fields of knowledge.
