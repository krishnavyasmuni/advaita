VP 3.6.1

सामवेदतरोः शाखा व्यासशिष्यस्य जौमिनिः । क्रमेण येन मैत्रेय विभेदशृणु तन्मम

VP 3.6.2

In each manvantara, different shrutis are being studied. Based on that, in various parts itself, different shrutis are being practiced, this does not make Vedaas as anitya, because in totality, it is eternal. Different parts are being studied at different times. They are eternal, and various branches, paathaantaraas are seen.
