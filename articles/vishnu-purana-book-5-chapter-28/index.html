VP 5.28.1

चारुदेष्णं सुदेष्णं च चारुदेहं च वीर्यवान् । सुषेणं चारुगुप्तं च भद्रचारुं तथा परम्

Sri Paraasharar - Rukmini had many sons - Chaarudeshna, Sudeshna, Chaarudeha, Veeryavaan, Sushena, Chaarugupta, Bhadrachaaru, ...

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

cārudeṣṇaṃ sudeṣṇaṃ ca cārudehaṃ ca vīryavān . suṣeṇaṃ cāruguptaṃ ca bhadracāruṃ tathā param — Sri Paraasharar - Rukmini had many sons - Chaarudeshna, Sudeshna, Chaarudeha, Veeryavaan, Sushena, Chaarugupta, Bhadrachaaru, ...

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.28.3

VP 5.28.4

देवी जाम्बवती चापि रोहिणी कामरूपिणी । मद्रराजसुता चान्या सुशीला शीलमण्डना

Then Jaambavaan's daughter Jaambavati. Then Rohini, who was very beautiful. Madraraaja's daughter was Susheela, who was with excellent character.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

devī jāmbavatī cāpi rohiṇī kāmarūpiṇī . madrarājasutā cānyā suśīlā śīlamaṇḍanā — Then Jaambavaan's daughter Jaambavati. Then Rohini, who was very beautiful. Madraraaja's daughter was Susheela, who was with excellent character.

Śrīdhara

devīti . jāmbavaty eva rohiṇī-sañjñā . ato na saṅkhyādhikyam . ata eva tasyā ṛkṣa-jātitvāt kāma-rūpiṇīti viśeṣaṇam . kecid rohiṇī nāma paṭṭa-mahiṣī-tulyānyeti prāhuḥ . tathā nopariṣṭhāt tayoḥ santāna-bodhoktir upapatsyate — Then Jaambavaan's daughter Jaambavati. Then Rohini, who was very beautiful. Madraraaja's daughter was Susheela, who was with excellent character.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

devī jāmbavatī cāpi rohiṇī kāmarūpiṇī . madrarājasutā cānyā suśīlā śīlamaṇḍanā

Śrīdhara

devīti . jāmbavaty eva rohiṇī-sañjñā . ato na saṅkhyādhikyam . ata eva tasyā ṛkṣa-jātitvāt kāma-rūpiṇīti viśeṣaṇam . kecid rohiṇī nāma paṭṭa-mahiṣī-tulyānyeti prāhuḥ . tathā nopariṣṭhāt tayoḥ santāna-bodhoktir upapatsyate ||४|| sātrājitī satyabhāmā lakṣmaṇā cāru-hāsinī .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

देवीति । जाम्बवत्य् एव रोहिणी-सञ्ज्ञा । अतो न सङ्ख्याधिक्यम् । अत एव तस्या ऋक्ष-जातित्वात् काम-रूपिणीति विशेषणम् । केचिद् रोहिणी नाम पट्ट-महिषी-तुल्यान्येति प्राहुः । तथा नोपरिष्ठात् तयोः सन्तान-बोधोक्तिर् उपपत्स्यते ॥४॥ सात्राजिती सत्यभामा लक्ष्मणा चारु-हासिनी ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Then Jaambavaan's daughter Jaambavati. Then Rohini, who was very beautiful. Madraraaja's daughter was Susheela, who was with excellent character.

VP 5.28.8

तस्यापि रुक्मिणः पौत्रीं वरयामास केशवः । दौहित्राय ददौ रक्मी तां स्पर्धन्नपि चक्रिणा

And again, Rukmi's granddaughter, Krishna asked for Aniruddha for marriage. Rukmi also agreed to give, though he was always competing with Krishna, and though he did not like Krishna. Aniruddha was also his daughter's son, for Rukmi.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasyāpi rukmiṇaḥ pautrīṃ varayāmāsa keśavaḥ . dauhitrāya dadau rukmī tāṃ sparddhannapi cakriṇā — And again, Rukmi's granddaughter, Krishna asked for Aniruddha for marriage. Rukmi also agreed to give, though he was always competing with Krishna, and though he did not like Krishna. Aniruddha was also his daughter's son, for Rukmi.

Śrīdhara

tasyāpīti . śauriṇā spardhamāno’pi rukmī dadau — And again, Rukmi's granddaughter, Krishna asked for Aniruddha for marriage. Rukmi also agreed to give, though he was always competing with Krishna, and though he did not like Krishna. Aniruddha was also his daughter's son, for Rukmi.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasyāpi rukmiṇaḥ pautrīṃ varayāmāsa keśavaḥ . dauhitrāya dadau rakmī tāṃ spardhannapi cakriṇā

Śrīdhara

tasyāpīti . śauriṇā spardhamāno’pi rukmī dadau ||८|| tasyā vivāhe rāmādyā yādavā hariṇā saha .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तस्यापीति । शौरिणा स्पर्धमानोऽपि रुक्मी ददौ ॥८॥ तस्या विवाहे रामाद्या यादवा हरिणा सह ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

And again, Rukmi's granddaughter, Krishna asked for Aniruddha for marriage. Rukmi also agreed to give, though he was always competing with Krishna, and though he did not like Krishna. Aniruddha was also his daughter's son, for Rukmi.

VP 5.28.9

कल्याणार्थं ततःसर्वे ये चान्ये भूभृतस्तथा । रुक्मिणी नगरं जग्मुर्नाम्ना भोजकटं द्विज

Rukmi was living in the city Bhojakata. For the marriage of Aniruddha with the granddaughter of Rukmi, Balaraama and Krishna, along with the Yaadavaas, came there to witness and participate in the marriage, along with all the other kings.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

For the marriage of Aniruddha with the granddaughter of Rukmi, Balaraama and Krishna, along with the Yaadavaas, came there to witness and participate in the marriage, along with all the other kings.

Śrīdhara

spardhā-phalaṃ vaktum āha—tasyā vivāha ity ādinā yāvat-samāpti — Rukmi was living in the city Bhojakata. For the marriage of Aniruddha with the granddaughter of Rukmi, Balaraama and Krishna, along with the Yaadavaas, came there to witness and participate in the marriage, along with all the other kings.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara commentary.

Rukmi was living in the city Bhojakata. For the marriage of Aniruddha with the granddaughter of Rukmi, Balaraama and Krishna, along with the Yaadavaas, came there to witness and participate in the marriage, along with all the other kings.

VP 5.28.11

अनक्षज्ञो हली द्युते तथाऽस्य व्यसनं महत् । तज्जयामो बलं कस्माद्द्युते नैनं महाबलम्

Balaraama who is very powerful, is ignorant about gambling, but even then, is very much addicted to gambling. So, let us win over Balaraama through gambling. Anyway, we cannot fight with him and win.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

anakṣajño halī dyūte tathāsya vyasanaṃ mahat . na jayāmo balaṃ kasmād‍dyutenainaṃ mahābalam — Balaraama who is very powerful, is ignorant about gambling, but even then, is very much addicted to gambling. So, let us win over Balaraama through gambling. Anyway, we cannot fight with him and win.

Śrīdhara

anakṣajña iti . tathā tathāpi asya dyūte mahad vyasanam asti — Balaraama who is very powerful, is ignorant about gambling, but even then, is very much addicted to gambling. So, let us win over Balaraama through gambling. Anyway, we cannot fight with him and win.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

anakṣajño halī dyute tathā’sya vyasanaṃ mahat . tajjayāmo balaṃ kasmāddyute nainaṃ mahābalam

Śrīdhara

anakṣajña iti . tathā tathāpi asya dyūte mahad vyasanam asti ||११|| parāśara uvāca— tatheti tān āha nṛpān rukmī bala-madānvitaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अनक्षज्ञ इति । तथा तथापि अस्य द्यूते महद् व्यसनम् अस्ति ॥११॥ पराशर उवाच— तथेति तान् आह नृपान् रुक्मी बल-मदान्वितः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Balaraama who is very powerful, is ignorant about gambling, but even then, is very much addicted to gambling. So, let us win over Balaraama through gambling. Anyway, we cannot fight with him and win.

VP 5.28.16

अविद्योऽयं मया द्यूते बलभद्रः पराजितः । भुधैवाक्षावलेपान्धो योऽवमेनेऽक्षकोविदान्
