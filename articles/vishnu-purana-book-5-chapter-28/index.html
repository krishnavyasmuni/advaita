VP 5.28.1

चारुदेष्णं सुदेष्णं च चारुदेहं च वीर्यवान् ।
सुषेणं चारुगुप्तं च भद्रचारुं तथा परम्

Rukmiṇī bore the mighty Cāru­deṣṇa, Sudeṣṇa, Cārudeha, Suṣeṇa, Cārugupta, and Bhadracāru.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

cārudeṣṇaṃ sudeṣṇaṃ ca cārudehaṃ ca vīryavān . suṣeṇaṃ cāruguptaṃ ca bhadracāruṃ tathā param

Word-for-word

cārudeṣṇam — Cārudeṣṇa; sudeṣṇam — Sudeṣṇa; ca — and; cārudeham — Cārudeha; ca — and; vīryavān — the mighty one; suṣeṇam — Suṣeṇa; cāruguptam — Cārugupta; ca — and; bhadracārum — Bhadracāru; tathā — likewise; param — further.

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.28.4

देवी जाम्बवती चापि रोहिणी कामरूपिणी ।
मद्रराजसुता चान्या सुशीला शीलमण्डना

There were also the goddess Jāmbavatī, Rohiṇī of beautiful forms, and another, the daughter of the king of Madra, named Suśīlā, adorned with good character.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

devī jāmbavatī cāpi rohiṇī kāma-rūpiṇī . madra-rāja-sutā cānyā suśīlā śīla-maṇḍanā

Śrīdhara

devīti . jāmbavaty eva rohiṇī-sañjñā . ato na saṅkhyādhikyam . ata eva tasyā ṛkṣa-jātitvāt kāma-rūpiṇīti viśeṣaṇam . kecid rohiṇī nāma paṭṭa-mahiṣī-tulyānyeti prāhuḥ . tathā nopariṣṭhāt tayoḥ santāna-bodhoktir upapatsyate ||४|| sātrājitī satyabhāmā lakṣmaṇā cāru-hāsinī .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

देवीति । जाम्बवत्य् एव रोहिणी-सञ्ज्ञा । अतो न सङ्ख्याधिक्यम् । अत एव तस्या ऋक्ष-जातित्वात् काम-रूपिणीति विशेषणम् । केचिद् रोहिणी नाम पट्ट-महिषी-तुल्यान्येति प्राहुः । तथा नोपरिष्ठात् तयोः सन्तान-बोधोक्तिर् उपपत्स्यते ॥४॥

Word-for-word

devī — goddess; jāmbavatī — Jāmbavatī; ca api — also; rohiṇī — Rohiṇī; kāma-rūpiṇī — beautiful-formed; madra-rāja-sutā — daughter of the king of Madra; ca anyā — and another; suśīlā — Suśīlā; śīla-maṇḍanā — adorned with good character.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Then Jaambavaan's daughter Jaambavati. Then Rohini, who was very beautiful. Madraraaja's daughter was Susheela, who was with excellent character.

satrājitī — daughter of Satrājit; satyabhāmā — Satyabhāmā; lakṣmaṇā — Lakṣmaṇā; cāru-hāsinī — lovely-smiling; ṣoḍaśa — sixteen; āsan — were; sahasrāṇi — thousands; strīṇām — of women; anyāni — other; cakriṇaḥ — of the discus-bearer.

VP 5.28.8

तस्यापि रुक्मिणः पौत्रीं वरयामास केशवः ।
दौहित्राय ददौ रक्मी तां स्पर्धन्नपि चक्रिणा

Keśava sought the granddaughter of Rukmiṇī’s brother, and Rukmī, though jealous of the discus-bearer, gave her to his grandson.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasyāpi rukmiṇaḥ pautrīṃ varayāmāsa keśavaḥ . dauhitrāya dadau r akmī tāṃ spardhann api cakriṇā

Śrīdhara

tasyāpīti . śauriṇā spardhamāno’pi rukmī dadau ||८|| tasyā vivāhe rāmādyā yādavā hariṇā saha .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तस्यापीति । शौरिणा स्पर्धमानोऽपि रुक्मी ददौ ॥८॥

Word-for-word

tasya api — of him too; rukmiṇaḥ — of Rukmī; pautrīm — granddaughter; varayāmāsa — sought in marriage; keśavaḥ — Keśava; dauhitrāya — for the grandson; dadau — gave; rukmī — Rukmī; tām — her; spardhan — being jealous; api — even; cakriṇā — of the discus-bearer.

Śrīdhara commentary.

And again, Rukmi's granddaughter, Krishna asked for Aniruddha for marriage. Rukmi also agreed to give, though he was always competing with Krishna, and though he did not like Krishna. Aniruddha was also his daughter's son, for Rukmi.

VP 5.28.9

तस्या विवाहे रामाद्या यादवा हरिणा सह ।
कल्याणार्थं ततः सर्वे ये चान्ये भूभृतस्तथा ।
रुक्मिणी नगरं जग्मुर्नाम्ना भोजकटं द्विज

At that marriage, Rāma and the Yādavas, together with Hari, and the other kings, went for auspicious purposes to Rukmiṇī’s city, called Bhojakaṭa, O twice-born one.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasyā vivāhe rāmādyā yādavā hariṇā saha . kalyāṇārthaṃ tataḥ sarve ye cānye bhūbhṛtas tathā . rukmiṇī nagaraṃ jagmur nāmnā bhojakaṭaṃ dvija

Śrīdhara

spardhā-phalaṃ vaktum āha—tasyā vivāha ity ādinā yāvat-samāpti ||९|| vivāhe tatra nirvṛtte prādyumnes tu mahātmanaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

स्पर्धा-फलं वक्तुम् आह—तस्या विवाह इत्य् आदिना यावत्-समाप्ति ॥९॥

Word-for-word

tasyāḥ vivāhe — at her marriage; rāma-ādyāḥ — Rāma and others; yādavāḥ — Yādavas; hariṇā saha — together with Hari; kalyāṇārtham — for auspiciousness; tataḥ — then; sarve — all; ye ca anye — and the other; bhūbhṛtaḥ — kings; r ukmiṇī nagaram — Rukmiṇī’s city; jagmuḥ — went; nāmnā — by name; bhojakaṭam — Bhojakaṭa; dvija — O twice-born.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Rukmi was living in the city Bhojakata. For the marriage of Aniruddha with the granddaughter of Rukmi, Balaraama and Krishna, along with the Yaadavaas, came there to witness and participate in the marriage, along with all the other kings.

vivāhe — at the marriage; tatra — there; nirvṛtte — completed; pradyumnasya — of Pradyumna; mahātmanaḥ — great; kaliṅga-rāja-pramukhāḥ — led by the king of Kaliṅga; rukmiṇam — to Rukmī; vākyam — words; abruvan — spoke.

VP 5.28.11

अनक्षज्ञो हली द्यूते तथाऽस्य व्यसनं महत् ।
तज्जयामो बलं कस्माद्द्यूते नैनं महाबलम्

“Halāyudha is unskilled at dice, and he has a great addiction to it. Why should we not defeat Balarāma? How could this mighty one be skilled at dice?”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

anakṣajño halī dyūte tathā asya vyasanaṃ mahat . tad jayāmo balaṃ kasmād dyūte nainaṃ mahā-balam

Śrīdhara

anakṣajña iti . tathā tathāpi asya dyūte mahad vyasanam asti ||११|| parāśara uvāca— tatheti tān āha nṛpān rukmī bala-madānvitaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अनक्षज्ञ इति । तथा तथापि अस्य द्यूते महद् व्यसनम् अस्ति ॥११॥

पराशर उवाच—

Word-for-word

anakṣajñaḥ — unskilled at dice; halī — Halāyudha; dyūte — at dice; tathā — and; asya — his; vyasanam — addiction; mahat — great; tat — that; jayāmaḥ — let us defeat; balam — Bala; kasmāt — how; dyūte — at dice; na — not; enam — him; mahā-balam — the very powerful one.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Balaraama who is very powerful, is ignorant about gambling, but even then, is very much addicted to gambling. So, let us win over Balaraama through gambling. Anyway, we cannot fight with him and win.

VP 5.28.14

ततो दशसहस्राणि निष्काणां पणमाददे ।
बलभद्रोऽजयत्तानि रुक्मी द्युतविदां वरः

Then Balarāma staked ten thousand niṣkas and won them; Rukmī was the foremost among players of dice.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tato daśa-sahasrāṇi niṣkāṇāṃ paṇam ādade . balabhadro ’jayat tāni rukmī dyūta-vidāṃ varaḥ

Śrīdhara

tata iti . balabhadro daśa-sahasrāṇi paṇam ādade . tāni ca rukmy ajayat ||१४|| tato jahāsa svanavat kaliṅgādhipatir dvija .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत इति । बलभद्रो दश-सहस्राणि पणम् आददे । तानि च रुक्म्य् अजयत् ॥१४॥

Word-for-word

tataḥ — then; daśa-sahasrāṇi — ten thousand; niṣkāṇām — niṣkas; paṇam — stake; ādade — took; balabhadraḥ — Balabhadra; ajayat — won; tāni — them; rukmī — Rukmī; dyūta-vidām — of dice-knowers; varaḥ — foremost.

Śrīdhara commentary.

After that, they kept 10000 Nishkaas, as the bet for the third game of gambling. Rukmi won over that also, because Rukmi was an expert in gambling.

VP 5.28.15

tata iti . svanavat sa-śabdaṃ yathā bhavaty tathā jahāsa ||१५|| avidyo’yaṃ mayā dyūte balabhadraḥ parājitaḥ .

tataḥ — then; jahāsa — laughed; svanavat — aloud; kaliṅga-adhipatiḥ — the king of Kaliṅga; dvija — O twice-born; dantān — teeth; vidaśayan — showing; mūḍhaḥ — foolish; rukmī — Rukmī; ca — and; āha — said; mada-uddhataḥ — intoxicated with pride.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Seeing this, the stupid Kalinga Raaja, laughed loudly making a lot of noise, showing all his teeth. Rukmi also was very arrogant, and he said thus -

VP 5.28.16

अविद्योऽयं मया द्यूते बलभद्रः पराजितः ।
बुधैवाक्षावलेपान्धो योऽवमेनेऽक्षकोविदान्

“This Balarāma was defeated by me at dice. He is blinded by contempt for dice-experts, who are wise and whom he has disregarded.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

avidyo ’yaṃ mayā dyūte balabhadraḥ parājitaḥ . budhair evākṣāvalepāndho yo ’vamene ’kṣa-kovidān

Śrīdhara

avidya iti . dyūte avidyaḥ jñāna-śūnyaḥ . mudhā vṛthaiva akṣāvalepāndhaḥ, akṣe avalepena garveṇa andhaḥ mūḍhaḥ ||१६|| dṛṣṭvā kaliṅga-rājaṃ tu prakāśa-daśanānanam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अविद्य इति । द्यूते अविद्यः ज्ञान-शून्यः । मुधा वृथैव अक्षावलेपान्धः, अक्षे अवलेपेन गर्वेण अन्धः मूढः ॥१६॥

Word-for-word

avidyaḥ ayam — this unskilled one; mayā — by me; dyūte — at dice; balabhadraḥ — Balarāma; parājitaḥ — defeated; budhaiḥ — by the wise; eva — indeed; akṣa-avalepa-andhaḥ — blinded by contempt for dice; yaḥ — who; avamene — disregarded; akṣa-kovidān — dice-experts.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Balabhadra was defeated by me and he is ignorant of this gambling. Simply he is boasting. He thinks that he wants to gamble. He used to disrespected all the experts.

VP 5.28.17

दृष्ट्वा कलिङ्गराजानं प्रकाशदशनाननम् ।
रुक्मिणं चापि दुर्वाक्यं कोपं चक्रे हलायुधः

Seeing the king of Kaliṅga baring his teeth and hearing Rukmī’s harsh words, Halāyudha became angry.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dṛṣṭvā kaliṅga-rājānaṃ prakāśa-daśanānanam . rukmiṇaṃ cāpi durvākyaṃ kopaṃ cakre halāyudhaḥ

Śrīdhara

dṛṣṭveti . prakāśa-daśanaṃ vivṛta-dantam ānanaṃ yasya taṃ duṣṭāni vākyāni yasya tam ||१७|| tataḥ kopa-parītātmā niṣka-koṭiṃsamādadhe.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

दृष्ट्वेति । प्रकाश-दशनं विवृत-दन्तम् आननं यस्य तं दुष्टानि वाक्यानि यस्य तम् ॥१७॥

When he saw that Kalinga Raaja, who was showing all his teeth and laughing very loudly, and also seeing Rukmi who was talking ill of him, Balaraama got extremely angry.

VP 5.28.20

Transliteration

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मया जितम् इत्य् अत्र रुक्मी हेतुम् आह—त्वयोक्तोऽयम् इति ॥२०॥

Word-for-word

tvayā uktaḥ — announced by you; ayam — this; glahaḥ — stake; satyam — truly; na — not; mayā — by me; eṣaḥ — this; anumoditaḥ — approved; evam — thus; tvayā — by you; cet — if; vijitam — won; vijitam — won; na — not; mayā — by me; katham — how.

Śrīdhara commentary.

He said - "When I was about to throw the dice, you only told some number. I did not say anything and I did not accept that number, If you say that you have won over, why should I not say that I have won over? I never got a chance, and will not accept what you said". Both have to agree to the bet and the game. If the other one has not agreed, how can you say that you have won?

VP 5.28.22

जितं बलेन धर्मेण रुक्मिणा भाषितं मृषा ।
अनुक्त्वाऽपि वचः किञ्चित्कृतं भवति कर्मणा

“Bala has won by righteousness; what Rukmī has said is false. Even without speaking a word, an act is accomplished through the deed.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jitaṃ balena dharmeṇa rukmiṇā bhāṣitaṃ mṛṣā . anuktvā api vacaḥ kiṃcit kṛtaṃ bhavati karmaṇā

Śrīdhara

jitam iti . anuktvāpi anumoda-vacanam anuktvāpi karmaṇaivākṣa-pātanādi-rūpeṇānumodanaṃ kṛtaṃ bhavati . anyathā anumodanottara-kālīnasyākṣa-pātanādeḥ karmaṇo’sambhavāt ||२२|| tato balaḥ samutthāya kopa-saṃrakta-locanaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जितम् इति । अनुक्त्वापि अनुमोद-वचनम् अनुक्त्वापि कर्मणैवाक्ष-पातनादि-रूपेणानुमोदनं कृतं भवति । अन्यथा अनुमोदनोत्तर-कालीनस्याक्ष-पातनादेः कर्मणोऽसम्भवात् ॥२२॥

Word-for-word

jitam — won; balena — by Bala; dharmeṇa — by righteousness; rukmiṇā — by Rukmī; bhāṣitam — spoken; mṛṣā — falsely; anuktvā api — even without speaking; vacaḥ — a word; kiṃcit — anything; kṛtam — accomplished; bhavati — becomes; karmaṇā — through action.

Śrīdhara commentary.

The voice said that - Balaraama has only won over, and this is the proper dharma. He followed the dharma, and he has won over, and Rukmi is telling lies. Even if he did not say that he had agreed or not, though he did not express, since he threw the dice, he actually played, it means that he had agreed only. Though he did not say whether he accepted the bet or not, he threw the dice anyway. This means that he has accepted the bet. He acted as though he has accepted, which means that he has accepted only. The voice from the skies said this.
