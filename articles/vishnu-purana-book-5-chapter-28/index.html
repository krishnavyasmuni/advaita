र्िेन तनहतां दृषट्र्वा रुस्क्मणां मधुसूदनः | नोर्वाच क्रकांथचत् मैत्रेय रुस्क्मणीर्ियोः भयात् ॥ २७ ॥ Krishna saw that Rukmi was killed, by Balaraama, and even then, He did not open His mouth, because He was scared of Rukmini and also Balaraama.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

balena — Krishna; nihataṃ — that; daṛṣaṭavā — killed; rukamiṇaṃ — and; madhusūdanaḥ — He; . — open; novāca — because; kiñacinamaitareya — scared; rukamiṇībalayorabhayāta — and

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

balena nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā rukmiṇaṃ madhusūdanaḥ
