VP 3.16.1

षोडशोऽध्यायः और्व ः – हविष्यमत्स्यमांसैस्तु शशस्य नकुलस्य च । सौकरच्छागलैणेपरौरवैर्यगवयेन च ॥१॥

Havishya is the grains used by the sages in the forest, milk, soma rasa, shuddha maamsa, saindhava lavana - these are havishya. Fish, deer, rabbit, hog, goat, antelope, some kind of deer, some kind of cow, ... Here, it is told that offering maamsa in shraaddha is supposed to be very good. This is also told in Manu Smruti. There is a Gita Press commentary, the Hindi version, that maamsa is not allowed in the shraaddha. Quoting from Manu Smruti itself, where generally, it is said that " Na himsyaat sarva bhootaani", that one should not do himsaa to any being. But, in yajnya and shraaddha, this is exempted. Nowadays, the grains we use are equivalent for these things. Nowadays, the practice of offering maamsa is not there.
