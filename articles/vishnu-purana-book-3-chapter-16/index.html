VP 3.16.1

हविष्यमत्स्यमांसैस्तु शशस्य नकुलस्य च । सौकरच्छागलैणेपरौरवैर्यगवयेन च

The offerings include havis, fish, meat, rabbit, mongoose, boar, goat, antelope, ruru deer, and gavaya.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

haviṣyamatsyamāṃsaistu śaśasya nakulasya ca — The offerings include havis, fish, meat, rabbit, mongoose, boar, goat, antelope, ruru deer, and gavaya.

Śrīdhara

sadācāraprasaṅgānuprasaṅgenātha ṣoḍaśe | dravyāṇi śrāddhayogyāni tathāyogyāni cādiśat | tatra śrāddhe deyadravyapraśaṃsā haviṣyairvrīhyādibhiḥ matsyādīnāṃ navānāṃ māṃsaśca pratyekamekaikamāsavṛddhyā pitāmahāḥ pitarastṛptiṃ prayānti iti dvayoranvayaḥ | vrīhyādibhirekamāsaṃ matsyamāṃsairdvau māsāvityevaṃ daśamāsāntam matsyāmiṣe māṃsatvopacāraḥ | eṇo hariṇaviśeṣaḥ | ruruḥ pṛṣataḥ tadīyaiḥ | gavayo gosadṛśaḥ paśurāraṇyakaḥ — Havishya iti | Havishyena ekam maasam truptih, matsyena dvou, maamsena treen maasaan | Atra maamso haarinaadi Treen maasaan harinena truptih iti manu ukteh | Chaagalah - ajah, ruruh mahaan krushnasaarah | Havishya is one month. Matsya, if offered, it is two months, they get pleased. Deer, it is for three months. Manu smruti is also quoted here. Chagala is goat. Ruru is another kind of deer. Each one of them, if offered, pitrus will get trupti for one more month each.

Transliteration

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सदाचारप्रसङ्गानुप्रसङ्गेनाथ षोडशे । द्रव्याणि श्राद्धयोग्यानि तथायोग्यानि चादिशत् । तत्र श्राद्धे देयद्रव्यप्रशंसा हविष्यैर्व्रीह्यादिभिः मत्स्यादीनां नवानां मांसश्च प्रत्येकमेकैकमासवृद्ध्या पितामहाः पितरस्तृप्तिं प्रयान्ति इति द्वयोरन्वयः । व्रीह्यादिभिरेकमासं मत्स्यमांसैर्द्वौ मासावित्येवं दशमासान्तम् मत्स्यामिषे मांसत्वोपचारः । एणो हरिणविशेषः । रुरुः पृषतः तदीयैः । गवयो गोसदृशः पशुरारण्यकः

VP 3.16.2

औरभ्रगव्यैश्च तथा मासवृद्ध्या पितामहाः । प्रयान्ति तृप्तिं मांसैस्तु नित्यं वार्ध्रीणसामिपैः

With sheep and products of the cow, the ancestors’ satisfaction increases month by month; with the meat of the Vārddhrīṇasa bird they are perpetually satisfied.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

aurabhragavyaiśca tathā māsavṛddhyā pitāmahāḥ — With sheep and products of the cow, the ancestors’ satisfaction increases month by month; with the meat of the Vārddhrīṇasa bird they are perpetually satisfied.

Śrīdhara

They will be pleased for one year if milk or paayasa is offered. Some people say that gavya is also maamsa. In Aapastamba sookti also, it is told like this. Vaardhreena is a bird which has got white feathers, and read head, and black neck.

urabhro meṣaḥ tadīyaiśca | gavyantu payaḥ pāyasaṃ vā | saṃvatsarantu gavyena payasā pāyasena ca | iti smṛteḥ | māṃsamadhyapāṭhāt māṃsamavetyante, tattu yugāntarīṇamityavadheyam | vādhrīṇasamāṃsaistu nityaṃ sarvakālaṃ tṛpyanti | vādhrīṇasaḥ smṛtiprasiddhaḥ | yathā— tripibantvindriyaṃ kṣīṇaṃ śvetaṃ vṛddhaprajāpatim | vādhrīṇasantu taṃ prāhuryājñikāḥ śrāddhakarmaṇi ॥ iti | jalapāne yasya mukhavat karṇau ca jalaṃ spṛśataḥ sa tripibaḥ | yadvā— kṛṣṇagrīvo raktaśirāḥ śvetapakṣo vihaṅgamaḥ | sa vai vādhrīṇasaḥ prokta ityeṣā naigamī śrutiḥ ॥ iti

VP 3.16.3

खड्गमांसमतीवात्र कालशाकं तथा मधु । शस्तानि कर्मण्यत्यन्ततृप्तिदानि नरेश्वर

Khadga mruga (rhino), some kind of basil, honey, shasta are supposed to be very good for using in shraaddha. They please the pitrus a lot.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

Millet, gram, wheat, rice, sesame, pulse, mustard, are all allowed.

VP 3.16.7

अकृताग्रयणं यच्च धान्यजातं नरेश्वर । राजमाषाणानणूंश्चैव मसूरांश्च विवर्जयेत्

These are what are not allowed. The grains which are brought are to be initially used in yajnya, and then only it is good for other purposes. Lentils - these are not to be used.

VP 3.16.8

अलाबुं गृञ्जनं चैव पलाण्डुं पिण्डमूलकम् । गाम्धारककरंभाणि लवणान्यौपराणि च

Onion and garlic are not to be used. Roots should not be used. Pindamoolaka is a kind of root. Rice with fragrance is not to be used. Fossil salt is not to be used.

VP 3.16.20

गौरी वाऽप्युदहेत्कन्यां नीलं वा वृषमुत्सृजेत् । यजेत वाश्वमेधेन विधिवद्दक्षिणावता

Gouree is a daughter who is ten year old. Will they get their ten year old daughter married? Will they give her in marriage? One which is red in colour, and face and tail are very white, and its nails and horn are normal white, such a bull is said to be a neela vrusha, will someone leave such a bull? Or, will he perform Ashvamedha yaaga, as told in the shaastraas, offering all the dakshinaa to the ritwiks, and others, as told in the shaastraas. The pitrus are expecting this. These are all good to do.
