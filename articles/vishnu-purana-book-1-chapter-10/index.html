VP 1.10.1

कथितं मे त्वया सर्वं यत् पृष्ठोऽसि मया मुने ।
भृगु-सर्गात् प्रभृत्य् एष सर्गो मे कथ्यतां पुनः ॥१॥

Maitreya said: All that I asked you, O sage, you have told me. Now tell me again the creation beginning with the creation of Bhṛgu.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — kathitam — has been told; me — to me; tvayā — by you; sarvam — all; yat — what; pṛṣṭhaḥ asi — has been asked; mayā — by me; mune — O sage; bhṛgu-sargāt — from Bhṛgu’s creation; prabhṛti — beginning with; eṣaḥ — this; sargaḥ — creation; me — to me; kathyatām — let it be told; punaḥ — again

Śrīdhara

Words — commentary — punarbhṛgusargāt prabhṛti bhṛgusutānuvādapūrvakameṣa bhṛrgovaṃśa, kathyatām, anyathā pūrvāparakathāsambandhāpratīterity arthaḥ || 1 ||; sense — The request is to hear the Bhṛgu lineage again in detail, so that the connection between the earlier and later accounts is clear.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kathitaṃ me tvayā sarvaṃ yat pṛṣṭho’si mayā mune | bhṛgu-sargāt prabhṛty eṣa sargo me kathyatāṃ punaḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara

punarbhṛgusargāt prabhṛti bhṛgusutānuvādapūrvakameṣa bhṛrgovaṃśa, kathyatām, anyathā pūrvāparakathāsambandhāpratīterity arthaḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पुनर्भृगुसर्गात् प्रभृति भृगुसुतानुवादपूर्वकमेष भृर्गोवंश, कथ्यताम्, अन्यथा पूर्वापरकथासम्बन्धाप्रतीतेरित्य् अर्थः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The request is to hear the Bhṛgu lineage again in detail, so that the connection between the earlier and later accounts is clear.

VP 1.10.2

भृगोः ख्यात्यां समुत्पन्ना लक्ष्मीर् विष्णु-परिग्रहः ।
तथा धातृ-विधातारौ ख्यात्यां जातौ सुतौ भृगोः ॥२॥

Parāśara said: Lakṣmī was born from Bhṛgu and Khyāti and became Viṣṇu’s consort. Khyāti also bore Bhṛgu the two sons Dhātṛ and Vidhātṛ.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — bhṛgoḥ — of Bhṛgu; khyātyām — in Khyāti, Bhṛgu’s wife; samutpannā — born; lakṣmīḥ — Lakṣmī; viṣṇu-parigrahaḥ — Viṣṇu’s consort; tathā — also; dhātṛ-vidhātārau — Dhātṛ and Vidhātṛ; khyātyām — from Khyāti; jātau — were born; sutau — two sons; bhṛgoḥ — of Bhṛgu

Śrīdhara

Words — commentary — ato bhṛgoḥ sutān anuvadati bhṛgor iti | viṣṇoḥ parigrahaḥ patnī || 2 ||; sense — The verse resumes the sons of Bhṛgu; viṣṇu-parigraha means “Viṣṇu’s wife.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhṛgoḥ khyātyāṃ samutpannā lakṣmīr viṣṇu-parigrahaḥ | tathā dhātṛ-vidhātārau khyātyāṃ jātau sutau bhṛgoḥ || 2 ||

Śrīdhara

ato bhṛgoḥ sutān anuvadati bhṛgor iti | viṣṇoḥ parigrahaḥ patnī || 2 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अतो भृगोः सुतान् अनुवदति भृगोर् इति । विष्णोः परिग्रहः पत्नी ॥२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The verse resumes the sons of Bhṛgu; viṣṇu-parigraha means “Viṣṇu’s wife.”

VP 1.10.3

आयतिर् नियतिश् चैव मेरोः कन्ये महात्मनः ।
भार्ये धातृ-विधात्रोस् ते तयोर् जातौ सुताव् उभौ ॥३॥

Āyati and Niyati were the two daughters of the great Meru; they became the wives of Dhātṛ and Vidhātṛ, and each bore one son.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — āyatiḥ — Āyati; niyatiḥ — Niyati; ca eva — and indeed; meroḥ — of Meru; kanye — daughters; mahātmanaḥ — of the great-souled one; bhārye — wives; dhātṛ-vidhātroḥ — of Dhātṛ and Vidhātṛ; te — they two; tayoḥ — of the two; jātau — were born; sutau — two sons; ubhau — both

Śrīdhara

Words — commentary — tābhyāṃ tayor āyati-niyatyoḥ pratyekaṃ prāṇāś ca mṛkaṇḍaścety evam ubhau sutau jātāvity anvayaḥ | prāṇasya vedaśirā yajñe ity anvayaḥ || 3-4 ||; sense — The two sons are one each: Prāṇa and Mṛkaṇḍu, born respectively to Āyati and Niyati.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

āyatir niyatiś caiva meroḥ kanye mahātmanaḥ | bhārye dhātṛ-vidhātros te tayor jātau sutāv ubhau || 3 ||

Śrīdhara

tābhyāṃ tayor āyati-niyatyoḥ pratyekaṃ prāṇāś ca mṛkaṇḍaścety evam ubhau sutau jātāvity anvayaḥ | prāṇasya vedaśirā yajñe ity anvayaḥ || 3-4 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ताभ्यां तयोर् आयति-नियत्योः प्रत्येकं प्राणाश् च मृकण्डश्चेत्य् एवम् उभौ सुतौ जातावित्य् अन्वयः । प्राणस्य वेदशिरा यज्ञे इत्य् अन्वयः ॥३-४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The two sons are one each: Prāṇa and Mṛkaṇḍu, born respectively to Āyati and Niyati.

VP 1.10.4

प्राणश् चैव मृकण्डुश् च मार्कण्डेयो मृकण्डुतः ।
ततो वेद-शिरा जज्ञे प्राणस्यापि सुतं शृणु ॥४॥

Prāṇa and Mṛkaṇḍu were those sons; Mārkaṇḍeya was born from Mṛkaṇḍu. Now hear of the son born to Prāṇa, Vedaśiras.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — prāṇaḥ — Prāṇa; ca eva — and; mṛkaṇḍuḥ — Mṛkaṇḍu; ca — and; mārkaṇḍeyaḥ — Mārkaṇḍeya; mṛkaṇḍutaḥ — from Mṛkaṇḍu; tataḥ — then, from him; veda-śirāḥ — Vedaśiras; jajñe — was born; prāṇasya — of Prāṇa; api — also; sutam — son; śṛṇu — hear

Śrīdhara

Words — commentary — tābhyāṃ tayor āyati-niyatyoḥ pratyekaṃ prāṇāś ca mṛkaṇḍaścety evam ubhau sutau jātāvity anvayaḥ | prāṇasya vedaśirā yajñe ity anvayaḥ || 3-4 ||; sense — The construction continues from the preceding verse: Mārkaṇḍeya was born from Mṛkaṇḍu, and Vedaśiras from Prāṇa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prāṇaś caiva mṛkaṇḍuś ca mārkaṇḍeyo mṛkaṇḍutaḥ | tato veda-śirā jajñe prāṇasyāpi sutaṃ śṛṇu || 4 ||

Śrīdhara

tābhyāṃ tayor āyati-niyatyoḥ pratyekaṃ prāṇāś ca mṛkaṇḍaścety evam ubhau sutau jātāvity anvayaḥ | prāṇasya vedaśirā yajñe ity anvayaḥ || 3-4 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ताभ्यां तयोर् आयति-नियत्योः प्रत्येकं प्राणाश् च मृकण्डश्चेत्य् एवम् उभौ सुतौ जातावित्य् अन्वयः । प्राणस्य वेदशिरा यज्ञे इत्य् अन्वयः ॥३-४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The construction continues from the preceding verse: Mārkaṇḍeya was born from Mṛkaṇḍu, and Vedaśiras from Prāṇa.

VP 1.10.5

प्राणस्य द्युत्मान् पुत्रो राजवांश् च ततोऽभवत् ।
ततो वंशो महाभाग विस्तरं भार्गवो गतः ॥५॥

Prāṇa’s son was Dyutimat, and from him Rājavān was born. From that point the Bhārgava lineage expanded greatly, O greatly fortunate one.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — prāṇasya — of Prāṇa; dyutmān — Dyutimat; putraḥ — son; rājavān — Rājavān; ca — and; tataḥ — then, from him; abhavat — was born; tataḥ — from that point; vaṃśaḥ — lineage; mahābhāga — O greatly fortunate one; vistaram — expansion; bhārgavaḥ — of Bhṛgu; gataḥ — went, became

Śrīdhara

Words — commentary — tasyaiva putrāntaramāha prāṇasyeti || 5 ||; sense — This verse introduces another son of Prāṇa, Dyutimat, and continues the Bhārgava genealogy through Rājavān.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prāṇasya dyutmān putro rājavāṃś ca tato’bhavat | tato vaṃśo mahābhāga vistaraṃ bhārgavo gataḥ || 5 ||

Śrīdhara

tasyaiva putrāntaramāha prāṇasyeti || 5 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तस्यैव पुत्रान्तरमाह प्राणस्येति ॥५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

This verse introduces another son of Prāṇa, Dyutimat, and continues the Bhārgava genealogy through Rājavān.

VP 1.10.6

पत्नी मरीचेः सम्भूतिः पौर्णमासम् असूयत ।
विरजाः पर्वतश् चैव तस्य पुत्रौ महात्मनः ॥६॥

Marīci’s wife Sambhūti bore Paurṇamāsa. The great-souled Paurṇamāsa had the two sons Virajas and Parvata.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — patnī — wife; marīceḥ — of Marīci; sambhūtiḥ — Sambhūti; paurṇamāsam — Paurṇamāsa; asūyata — bore; virajāḥ — Virajas; parvataḥ — Parvata; ca eva — and indeed; tasya — of him; putrau — two sons; mahātmanaḥ — great-souled

Śrīdhara

Words — commentary — tad evaṃ caturviṃśatidakṣakanyānāṃ madhye śradvādīnāṃ trayodaśānāṃ dharmabhāryāṇāṃ vaṃśāḥ kathitāḥ | anantarañca khyātiḥ satyatha sambhūtirityuddiṣṭānām ekādaśānāṃ bhṛgumarīcyādibhāryāṇāṃ madhye khyātyāṃ bhṛgorvaṃśo nirūpitaḥ | satyāś ca bhavabhāryāyā dakṣakopād aprauḍhāyā evaṃ dehatyāgoktyā tasyā vaṃśo nābhūdity arthaduktam | atha kramaprāptaṃ marīceḥ sambhūtyāṃ jātaṃ vaṃśamāha patnī marīceriti || 6 ||; sense — Śrīdhara situates this genealogy within the descendants of Dakṣa’s daughters. After the line of Khyāti, the account proceeds in order to Sambhūti, Marīci’s wife.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

patnī marīceḥ sambhūtiḥ paurṇamāsam asūyata | virajāḥ parvataś caiva tasya putrau mahātmanaḥ || 6 ||

Śrīdhara

tad evaṃ caturviṃśatidakṣakanyānāṃ madhye śradvādīnāṃ trayodaśānāṃ dharmabhāryāṇāṃ vaṃśāḥ kathitāḥ | anantarañca khyātiḥ satyatha sambhūtirityuddiṣṭānām ekādaśānāṃ bhṛgumarīcyādibhāryāṇāṃ madhye khyātyāṃ bhṛgorvaṃśo nirūpitaḥ | satyāś ca bhavabhāryāyā dakṣakopād aprauḍhāyā evaṃ dehatyāgoktyā tasyā vaṃśo nābhūdity arthaduktam | atha kramaprāptaṃ marīceḥ sambhūtyāṃ jātaṃ vaṃśamāha patnī marīceriti || 6 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तद् एवं चतुर्विंशतिदक्षकन्यानां मध्ये श्रद्वादीनां त्रयोदशानां धर्मभार्याणां वंशाः कथिताः । अनन्तरञ्च ख्यातिः सत्यथ सम्भूतिरित्युद्दिष्टानाम् एकादशानां भृगुमरीच्यादिभार्याणां मध्ये ख्यात्यां भृगोर्वंशो निरूपितः । सत्याश् च भवभार्याया दक्षकोपाद् अप्रौढाया एवं देहत्यागोक्त्या तस्या वंशो नाभूदित्य् अर्थदुक्तम् । अथ क्रमप्राप्तं मरीचेः सम्भूत्यां जातं वंशमाह पत्नी मरीचेरिति ॥६॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Śrīdhara situates this genealogy within the descendants of Dakṣa’s daughters. After the line of Khyāti, the account proceeds in order to Sambhūti, Marīci’s wife.

VP 1.10.7

वंश-सङ्कीर्तने पुत्रान् वदिष्येऽहं ततो द्विज ।
स्मृतिश् चाङ्गिरसः पत्नी प्रसूता कन्यकास् तथा ।
सिनीवाली कुहूश् चैव राका चानुमतिस् तथा ॥७॥

Now, O Brāhmaṇa, I shall tell the sons in the account of the lineages. Smṛti, wife of Aṅgiras, bore daughters: Sinīvālī, Kuhū, Rākā, and Anumati.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — vaṃśa-saṅkīrtane — in the recital of the lineage; putrān — sons; vadiṣye — I shall tell; aham — I; tataḥ — then, thereafter; dvija — O twice-born; smṛtiḥ — Smṛti; ca — and; aṅgīrasaḥ — of Aṅgiras; patnī — wife; prasūtā — bore; kanyakāḥ — daughters; tathā — also; sinīvālī — Sinīvālī; kuhū — Kuhū; rākā — Rākā; anumatiḥ — Anumati

Śrīdhara

Words — commentary — vaṃśasaṅkīrtana iti | marīcer eva kaśyapo’nyaḥ putraḥ, tat putrā vivasvadādayaḥ | ityasminnevāṃśe ante vaṃśakīrtane vakṣye vivasvataḥ śrāddhadevādayaḥ | śrāddhadevasya ikṣvākupramukhā ity-ādi tattat putrān vistarato vaṃśīkīrtane caturthāṃśe vakṣye | atra tu svalpatvāt paurṇamāsānvayamātramuktam itvarthaḥ || 7 ||; sense — The fuller lineages of Kaśyapa, Vivasvat, and Śrāddhadeva are treated in later sections; this passage gives only Paurṇamāsa’s brief line here.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vaṃśa-saṅkīrtane putrān vadiṣye’haṃ tato dvija | smṛtiś cāṅgirasaḥ patnī prasūtā kanyakās tathā | sinīvālī kuhūś caiva rākā cānumatis tathā || 7 ||

Śrīdhara

vaṃśasaṅkīrtana iti | marīcer eva kaśyapo’nyaḥ putraḥ, tat putrā vivasvadādayaḥ | ityasminnevāṃśe ante vaṃśakīrtane vakṣye vivasvataḥ śrāddhadevādayaḥ | śrāddhadevasya ikṣvākupramukhā ity-ādi tattat putrān vistarato vaṃśīkīrtane caturthāṃśe vakṣye | atra tu svalpatvāt paurṇamāsānvayamātramuktam itvarthaḥ || 7 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वंशसङ्कीर्तन इति । मरीचेर् एव कश्यपोऽन्यः पुत्रः, तत् पुत्रा विवस्वदादयः । इत्यस्मिन्नेवांशे अन्ते वंशकीर्तने वक्ष्ये विवस्वतः श्राद्धदेवादयः । श्राद्धदेवस्य इक्ष्वाकुप्रमुखा इत्य्-आदि तत्तत् पुत्रान् विस्तरतो वंशीकीर्तने चतुर्थांशे वक्ष्ये । अत्र तु स्वल्पत्वात् पौर्णमासान्वयमात्रमुक्तम् इत्वर्थः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The fuller lineages of Kaśyapa, Vivasvat, and Śrāddhadeva are treated in later sections; this passage gives only Paurṇamāsa’s brief line here.

VP 1.10.8

अनसूया तथैवात्रेर् जज्ञे निष्कल्मषान् सुतान् ।
सोमं दुर्वाससं चैव दत्तात्रेयं च योगिनम् ॥८॥

Word-for-word

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

VP 1.10.9

प्रीत्यां पुलस्त्य-भार्यायां दत्तोलिस् तत्-सुतोऽभवत् ।
पूर्व-जन्मनि योऽगस्त्यः स्मृतः स्वायम्भुवेऽन्तरे ॥९॥

Prīti, Pulastya’s wife, bore Dattoli, his son who is remembered as Agastya in the former Svāyambhuva Manvantara.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration

VP 1.10.10

कर्दमश् चोर्वरीयांश् च सहिष्णुश् च सुतास् त्रयः ।
क्षमा तु सुषुवे भार्या पुलहस्य प्रजापतेः ॥१०॥

Kṣamā, the wife of the Prajāpati Pulaha, bore three sons: Kardama, Urvarīyān, and Sahiṣṇu.
