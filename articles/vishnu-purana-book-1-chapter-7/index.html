VP 1.7.1

ततोऽभिध्यायतस् तस्य जज्ञिरे मानसाः प्रजाः।
तच्-छरीर-समुत्पन्नैः कार्यैस् तैः करणैः सह।
क्षेत्रज्ञाः समवर्तन्त गात्रेभ्यस् तस्य धीमतः॥१॥

Then, as he continued to meditate, mind-born beings were produced from him, with bodies and faculties arising from his own body; the individual knowers of the field appeared from the limbs of that wise one.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tato’bhidhyāyatas tasya jajñire mānasāḥ prajāḥ | tac-charīra-samutpannaiḥ kāryais taiḥ karaṇaiḥ saha | kṣetrajñāḥ samavartanta gātrebhyas tasya dhīmataḥ ||1||

Śrīdhara

atha yaunasṛṣṭiṃ vakṣyan pūrvoktāyāṃ mānasaśarīrasṛṣṭau kañcid viśeṣaṃ vaktum āha—tata iti tribhiḥ | prāganuktaviśeṣokteḥ tata ityapūrvasṛṣṭivat prastāvaḥ tatraivaṃ śaṅkyate, nanu manasā saṅkalpamātreṇa bhūtopādānaṃ vinā bhaumikānāṃ dehānāṃ kathaṃ janma, kathaṃ vā jaḍāt śarīrāt cetanānāṃ janma iti, tatrāha—taccharīreti | tasya brahmaṇaḥ śarīrāt samutpannaiḥ kāryairdehaiḥ kāraṇaiḥ indriyaiś ca saha manasā nimittabhūtena mānasyaḥ prajā jajñire, brahma-śarīra-stha-bhūtāny eva tad-dehānām apādāna-kāraṇam ity arthaḥ ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अथ यौनसृष्टिं वक्ष्यन् पूर्वोक्तायां मानसशरीरसृष्टौ कञ्चिद् विशेषं वक्तुम् आह—तत इति त्रिभिः । प्रागनुक्तविशेषोक्तेः तत इत्यपूर्वसृष्टिवत् प्रस्तावः तत्रैवं शङ्क्यते, ननु मनसा सङ्कल्पमात्रेण भूतोपादानं विना भौमिकानां देहानां कथं जन्म, कथं वा जडात् शरीरात् चेतनानां जन्म इति, तत्राह—तच्छरीरेति । तस्य ब्रह्मणः शरीरात् समुत्पन्नैः कार्यैर्देहैः कारणैः इन्द्रियैश् च सह मनसा निमित्तभूतेन मानस्यः प्रजा जज्ञिरे, ब्रह्म-शरीर-स्थ-भूतान्य् एव तद्-देहानाम् अपादान-कारणम् इत्य् अर्थः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The sexual creation is now discussed, with a clarification of the earlier mind-and-body creation. The bodies and senses arise from Brahmā’s body, with the mind as the immediate instrument; the elements present in Brahmā’s body are the material cause of those bodies.

VP 1.7.2

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तथा धीमतः चेतनस्य समष्टिजीवस्य तस्य ब्रह्मणो जडेभ्योऽपि गात्रेभ्य उत्पद्यमानैर्देहैः तत्सङ्कल्पमात्रादुद्दुबुद्धसंस्कारा व्यष्टिक्षेत्रज्ञाः समवर्तन्त आविर्भूता इत्य् अर्थः । तान् एवाह ते सर्वे इति ॥२-३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The collective conscious being, Brahmā, is described as intelligent, while individual knowers of the field appear from his otherwise inert limbs through the mere resolve of the collective being and the awakening of their latent impressions.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The purpose of sexual creation is stated: the earlier beings did not increase in the form of sons and grandsons, so Brahmā created Bhṛgu and the other progenitors who would advance that creation.
