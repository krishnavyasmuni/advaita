VP 4.2.1

यावच्च ब्रह्मलोकात्स ककुद्मी रैवतो नाभ्येति तावत्पुण्यजनसंज्ञा राक्षसास्तामस्य पुरीं कुशस्थलीं नजघ्नुः

Sri Paraasharah - Yaavat cha brahma lokaat sa kakudmee raivato naabhyeti taavat Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 punyajana samnjyaa raakshasaah taam asya pureem kushasthaleem nijaghnuh | Sri Paraasharar - When this Kakudmi Raivata went to Brahma loka, Kushasthalee was without a proper ruler; till he returned, the raakshasaas by name Punyajana, destroyed the city of Kushasthalee.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

caturathāṃśedavitīyo'dhayāyaḥ — Sri; yāvacaca — brahma; barahamalokātasa — naabhyeti; kakudamī — Amsha; raivato — taam; nābhayeti — Sri; tāvatapuṇayajanasaṃjañā — Kakudmi; rākaṣasāsatāmasaya — loka; purīṃ — proper; kuśasathalīṃ — the; najaghanuḥ — destroyed

Śrīdhara

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

dhṛṣṭādimanuputrāṇāṃ vaṃśāṃstaccaritāni ca . dvitīye tat prasaṅgena saubharyākhyānamuktavān . raivatasya bhrātṛśatamadhye kintāvat ko’pi rājā nābhavadityāśaṅkyāha—yāvacca iti . puṇyajanasañjñā iti viśeṣaṇaṃ sopālambhaṃ rākṣasajāti viśeṣaparaṃ vā ||१||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharah - Yaavat cha brahma lokaat sa kakudmee raivato naabhyeti taavat Sri Vishnu Puraana, Amsha 4 punyajana samnjyaa raakshasaah taam asya pureem kushasthaleem nijaghnuh | Sri Paraasharar - When this Kakudmi Raivata went to Brahma loka, Kushasthalee was without a proper ruler; till he returned, the raakshasaas by name Punyajana, destroyed the city of Kushasthalee.

Śrīdhara commentary.

This whole world is His form only. He, manifests, having done anupravesha, and created everything. He is present in every object here. One who is devoid of any defects. Who is all pervading.
