VP 3.17.1

इत्याह भगवनौर्वस्सगराय महात्मने । सदाचारं पुरा सम्यङ् मैत्रेय परिपृच्छते

Thus the blessed Aurva spoke to the great Sagara, who had formerly asked properly about right conduct, O Maitreya.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

iti āha bhagavān aurvaḥ sagarāya mahātmane — thus the blessed Aurva spoke to the great Sagara; sadācāram purā samyak maitreya paripṛcchate — who formerly asked properly about right conduct, O Maitreya

Śrīdhara

एवं नवभिरध्यायैस्त्रयीधर्माः प्रपञ्चिताः । व्यतिरेके त्वनर्थाप्तिर्द्वयेनेह निरूप्यते । तत्र सप्तदशे देवैः स्तुतो विष्णरजीजनत् । मायामोहं यतो मग्ना दत्या नग्नाश्च जज्ञिरे — Through nine chapters the duties of the threefold Veda have been elaborated. Their opposite, and the attainment of misfortune that follows it, is explained here in two [sets of] verses. In the seventeenth chapter Viṣṇu, praised by the gods, generated Māyā-moha; through him the Daityas became deluded and were born as nagna.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityāha bhagavanaurvassagarāya mahātmane | sadācāraṃ purā samyaṅ maitreya paripṛcchate ||1||

evaṃ navabhiradhyāyaistrayīdharmāḥ prapañcitāḥ | vyatireke tvanarthāptirdvayeneha nirūpyate | tatra saptadaśe devaiḥ stuto viṣṇarajījanat | māyāmohaṃ yato magnā datyā nagnāśca jajñire

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवं नवभिरध्यायैस्त्रयीधर्माः प्रपञ्चिताः । व्यतिरेके त्वनर्थाप्तिर्द्वयेनेह निरूप्यते । तत्र सप्तदशे देवैः स्तुतो विष्णरजीजनत् । मायामोहं यतो मग्ना दत्या नग्नाश्च जज्ञिरे

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 1).

Through nine chapters the duties of the threefold Veda have been elaborated. Their opposite, and the attainment of misfortune that follows it, is explained here in two [sets of] verses. In the seventeenth chapter Viṣṇu, praised by the gods, generated Māyā-moha; through him the Daityas became deluded and were born as nagna.

VP 3.17.2

VP 3.17.3

षण्डापविद्धप्रमुखा (कथिता भगवंस्त्वया) विदिता भगवन्मया । उदक्याद्याश्च मे सम्यडू नग्नमिच्छामि वेदितुम्

O Blessed One, I know the eunuch, outcast, and others you described, and I also know the menstruating and other persons. I wish to know fully what is meant by nagna.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṣaṇḍha-apaviddha-pramukhāḥ kathitāḥ — the eunuch, outcast, and others have been described; viditā bhagavan mayā — they are known by me, O Blessed One; udakyādayaḥ ca me samyak — and the menstruating and other persons are also known to me fully; nagnam icchāmi veditum — I wish to know the meaning of nagna

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṣaṇḍāpaviddhapramukhā (kathitā bhagavaṃstvayā) viditā bhagavanmayā | udakyādyāśca me samyaḍū nagnamicchāmi veditum ||3||

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.17.4

को नमः किंसमाचारो नग्नसंज्ञां नरो लभेत् । नग्नस्वरूपमिच्छामि यथावत्कथितं त्वया । श्रोतुं धर्मभृतां श्रेष्ठ न ह्यस्त्यविदितं तव

Who is called nagna, and what conduct causes a person to receive that designation? I wish to hear the nature of nagna exactly as you tell it, O best of those who uphold dharma; nothing is unknown to you.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kaḥ naraḥ kiṃ samācāraḥ nagna-saṃjñām labhet — what person, of what conduct, receives the designation nagna; nagna-svarūpam icchāmi yathāvat kathitam tvayā — I wish to hear the nature of nagna properly told by you; śrotum dharma-bhṛtām śreṣṭha — O best of those who uphold dharma; na hi asti aviditam tava — there is nothing unknown to you

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ko namaḥ kiṃsamācāro nagnasaṃjñāṃ naro labhet | nagnasvarūpamicchāmi yathāvatkathitaṃ tvayā | śrotuṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ śreṣṭha na hyastyaviditaṃ tava ||4||

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 3.17.5

ऋग्यजुस्सामसंज्ञेय त्रयी वर्णावृत्तिर्द्विज । एतामुज्झति यो मोहात्स नग्नः पातकी स्मृतः

The threefold Veda, known as Ṛg, Yajus, and Sāman, is the covering of the classes, O twice-born. Whoever abandons it through delusion is remembered as nagna, a sinner.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛg-yajuḥ-sāma-saṃjñeyā trayī — the threefold Veda called Ṛg, Yajus, and Sāman; varṇa-āvṛttiḥ dvija — is the covering of the classes, O twice-born; etām ujjhati yaḥ mohāt — whoever abandons it through delusion; saḥ nagnaḥ pātakī smṛtaḥ — is remembered as nagna, a sinner

Śrīdhara

वर्णानाम् आवृतिः परिधानम् — The covering of the classes is their garment.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ṛgyajussāmasaṃjñeya trayī varṇāvṛttirdvija | etāmujjhati yo mohātsa nagnaḥ pātakī smṛtaḥ ||5||

varṇānām āvṛtiḥ paridhānam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वर्णानाम् आवृतिः परिधानम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 5).

The covering of the classes is their garment.

VP 3.17.6

त्रयी समस्तवर्णानां द्विज संवरणं यतः । नग्नो भवत्युज्झिताया मतस्तस्यां न संशयः

Since the threefold Veda is the covering and protection of all classes, one becomes nagna when it is abandoned; of this there is no doubt.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

trayī samasta-varṇānām dvija saṃvaraṇam yataḥ — since the threefold Veda is the protection of all classes, O twice-born; nagnaḥ bhavati ujjhitāyām — when it is abandoned he becomes nagna; ataḥ tasya na saṃśayaḥ — of this there is no doubt

Śrīdhara

किञ्च यतः सैव संवरणमपि प्रावरणम् उत्तरीयम् इति यावत्, अतस्तत्परित्यागे परिधानोत्तरीयहीन इव नग्नी भवतीत्यर्थः — The threefold Veda is not merely a covering like cloth; it is saṃvaraṇa, that which removes all suffering. Therefore one who abandons it is called nagna; this does not mean merely a digambara.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

trayī samastavarṇānāṃ dvija saṃvaraṇaṃ yataḥ | nagno bhavatyujjhitāyā matastasyāṃ na saṃśayaḥ ||6||

kiñca yataḥ saiva saṃvaraṇamapi prāvaraṇam uttarīyam iti yāvat, atastatparityāge paridhānottarīyahīna iva nagnī bhavatītyarthaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

किञ्च यतः सैव संवरणमपि प्रावरणम् उत्तरीयम् इति यावत्, अतस्तत्परित्यागे परिधानोत्तरीयहीन इव नग्नी भवतीत्यर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 6).

The threefold Veda is not merely a covering like cloth; it is saṃvaraṇa, that which removes all suffering. Therefore one who abandons it is called nagna; this does not mean merely a digambara.

VP 3.17.7

इदं च श्रूयतामन्यद्यद्भीष्माय महात्मने । कथयामास धर्मझो वसिष्ठोऽस्मत्पितामहः

Hear this other account too: our grandfather Vasiṣṭha, the knower of dharma, formerly narrated it to the great Bhīṣma.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

etadeva spaṣṭīkartumitihāsaṃ prastaurti—idañca iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एतदेव स्पष्टीकर्तुमितिहासं प्रस्तौर्ति—इदञ्च इति

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 7).

VP 3.17.9

देवासुरमभूद्युद्धं दिव्यमब्दशतं पुरा । तस्मिन्पराजिता देवा दैत्यैर्ह्रादपुरोगमैः

VP 3.17.11

VP 3.17.12

यतो भूतान्यशेषाणि प्र(सू)भूतानि महात्मनः । यस्मिंश्च लयमेष्यन्ति कस्तं स्तोतुमिहेश्वरः

From whom all beings have been born and into whom they will dissolve—who could praise that Lord here?

Word-for-word

VP 3.17.13

तथाऽप्यरातिविध्वंसध्वस्तवीर्याभयार्थिनः । त्वां स्तोष्यमस्तवोक्तीनां याथा(म्यं)र्थ्यं नैव गोचरे

Nevertheless, our strength has been destroyed by the destruction wrought by our enemies, and we seek fearlessness. We will praise you, although the true extent of your nature is beyond the reach of words.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tathā api arāti-vidhvaṃsa-dhvasta-vīryāḥ — nevertheless, our strength has been destroyed by the destruction wrought by enemies; abhaya-arthinaḥ tvām stoṣyāmaḥ — seeking fearlessness, we will praise you; tava uktīnām yāthārthyam na eva gochare — the true extent of your words is not within reach

Śrīdhara

यद्यपि तव याथार्थ्यं तत्त्वमुक्तीनां वचसां गोचरे विषये नैव वर्त्तते, तथापि अरातिकृतेन ध्वंसेन पराभवेन विध्वस्तं नाशितं वीर्यं प्रभावो येषां ते वयं यस्मात् भवार्थिनः कल्याणार्थिनः स्वमत्यनुरूपं स्तोष्यामः — Although your true nature and the reality of your words are not within the reach of speech, we, whose power has been destroyed by the defeat wrought by our enemies and who seek welfare, will praise you according to our ability.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tathā'pyarātividhvaṃsadhvastavīryābhayārthinaḥ | tvāṃ stoṣyamastavoktīnāṃ yāthā(myaṃ)rthyaṃ naiva gocare ||13||

yadyapi tava yāthārthyaṃ tattvamuktīnāṃ vacasāṃ gocare viṣaye naiva varttate, tathāpi arātikṛtena dhvaṃsena parābhavena vidhvastaṃ nāśitaṃ vīryaṃ prabhāvo yeṣāṃ te vayaṃ yasmāt bhavārthinaḥ kalyāṇārthinaḥ svamatyanurūpaṃ stoṣyāmaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यद्यपि तव याथार्थ्यं तत्त्वमुक्तीनां वचसां गोचरे विषये नैव वर्त्तते, तथापि अरातिकृतेन ध्वंसेन पराभवेन विध्वस्तं नाशितं वीर्यं प्रभावो येषां ते वयं यस्मात् भवार्थिनः कल्याणार्थिनः स्वमत्यनुरूपं स्तोष्यामः

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges 13).

Although your true nature and the reality of your words are not within the reach of speech, we, whose power has been destroyed by the defeat wrought by our enemies and who seek welfare, will praise you according to our ability.

VP 3.17.14

त्वमुर्वी सलिलं वह्निर्वायुराकाशमेव च । समस्तमन्तःकरणं प्रधानं तत्परः पुमान्

You are the earth, water, fire, air, and ether; you are the entire inner organ, primordial matter, and the transcendent Person.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tvam urvī salilam vahniḥ vāyuḥ ākāśam eva ca — you are earth, water, fire, air, and ether; samastam antaḥkaraṇam pradhānam — the entire inner organ and primordial matter; tat-paraḥ pumān — and the transcendent person

Śrīdhara

तत्र प्रथमं विश्वरूपेण सगुणं स्तुवन्ति—त्वमुर्वि इत्यादिना तस्मै सर्वात्मने नमः इत्यन्तेन । समस्तमनोबुद्ध्यहङ्कारचित्ताख्यमन्तःकरणम् । तस्याः प्रकृतेः परः — Here, beginning with “you are the earth,” they praise the Lord in his qualified universal form, up to “homage to that Universal Self.” The entire inner organ consists of mind, intellect, ego, and consciousness; “pradhāna” is that which is beyond primordial matter.

Transliteration
