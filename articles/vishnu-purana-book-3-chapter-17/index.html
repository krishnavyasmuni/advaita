VP 3.17.1

इत्याह भगवनौर्वस्सगराय महात्मने । सदाचारं पुरा सम्यङ् मैत्रेय परिपृच्छते

Sri Paraasharar - Bhagavaan Ourva told to Sagara thus - Whatever you asked about Sadaachaara and all those things, O Maitreya, this is what Ourva Rishi has told to Sagara.

VP 3.17.6

Word-for-word
