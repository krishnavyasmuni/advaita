VP 5.17.1

अक्रूरोऽपि विनिष्क्रम्य स्यन्दनेनाशुगामिना।
कृष्णसंदर्शनाकाङ्क्षी प्रययौ नन्दगोकुलम्

Sri Paraasharar - Akroora left and came to Gokula, in a chariot which moves very fast, eagerly waiting to see Krishna, he comes to Nandagokula.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

akrūro’pi viniṣkramya syandanenāśugāminā . kṛṣṇasaṃdarśanākāṅkṣī prayayau nandagokulam

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.2

चिन्तयामास चाक्रूरो नास्ति धन्यतरो मया।
योऽहमंशावतीर्णस्य मुखं द्रक्ष्यामि चक्रिणः

Akroora thought, while travelling, that "There is no one who is more fortunate than me, because I am going to see Chakri, saakshaat Amsha of Vishnu, who has incarnated here. I am going to see His wonderful face".

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

cintayāmāsa cākrūro nāsti dhanyataro mayā . yo’hamaṃśāvatīrṇasya mukhaṃ drakṣyāmi cakriṇaḥ

Śrīdhara

pathi gacchataḥ akrūrasya manorathān āha—cintayāmāsa ity-ādinā mām akrūreti vakṣyati (५.१७.१२) ity-antena . cintayāmāsa iti mayā sadṛśo dhanyataro nāsti ||२|| adya me saphalaṃ janma suprabhātābhavan niśā .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पथि गच्छतः अक्रूरस्य मनोरथान् आह—चिन्तयामास इत्य्-आदिना माम् अक्रूरेति वक्ष्यति (५.१७.१२) इत्य्-अन्तेन । चिन्तयामास इति मया सदृशो धन्यतरो नास्ति ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Akroora thought, while travelling, that "There is no one who is more fortunate than me, because I am going to see Chakri, saakshaat Amsha of Vishnu, who has incarnated here. I am going to see His wonderful face".

VP 5.17.3

अद्य मे सफलं जन्म सुप्रभाताऽभवन्निशा।
यदुन्निद्राब्जपत्राक्षं विष्णोर्द्रक्ष्याम्यहं मुखम्

Today only, my janma has become fruitful. So long, it was like a night. Now, it is going to be like a dawn for me. He has wonderful eyes like the lotus. I am going to see His face, and this is dawn for me in my life.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

adya me saphalaṃ janma suprabhātā’bhavanniśā . yadunnidrābjapatrākṣaṃ viṣṇordrakṣyāmyahaṃ mukham

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.4

पापं हरति यत्पुंसां स्मृतं संकल्पनामयम्।
तत्पुण्डरीकनयनं विष्णोर्द्रक्ष्याम्यहं मुखम्

Even if someone meditates on Him, and just remembers, that destroys all the sins. That form, that face of Bhagavaan, I am going to see. The form of Krishna is out of His own sankalpa, that He has incarnated here. Aditi, Devaki worshipped Bhagavaan for many many years, and got a boon from Him that He will be born here, as their son. According to this, He takes the form of deva, manushya, as Upendra, Krishna. This is common to even vibhava avataaras. In the Bhagavad Gita, Krishna says that "Whatever form one worships Me in, I will reveal Myself to them in the same form". Taam tathaa eva bhajaami, where bhajaami upaasanaa means darshayaami according to Bhaashyakaarar. Even in the, when one meditates upon Bhagavaan, in their hrudaya, He gives a similar experience to them. Or, He takes different forms out of His own will. In the meditation also, whatever form we see outside, we have to think of the same form inside also, inside our mind.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pāpaṃ harati yatpuṃsāṃ smṛtaṃ saṃkalpanāmayam . tatpuṇḍarīkanayanaṃ viṣṇordrakṣyāmyahaṃ mukham

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.5

विनिर्जग्मुर्यतो वेदा वेदाङ्गान्यखिलानि च।
द्रक्ष्यामि तत्परं धाम धाम्रां भगवतो मुखम्

All the Vedaas came out of His face only, He only did upadesha to Chaturmukha Brahma. He does that in every srushti. The Bhagavaan's face is the resting house of all the Vedaangaas, the tejo padaarthaas. That face of Bhagavaan, I am going to see.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vinirjagmuryato vedā vedāṅgānyakhilāni ca . drakṣyāmi tatparaṃ dhāma dhāmrāṃ bhagavato mukham

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.6

यज्ञेषु यज्ञपुरुषः पुरुषैः पुरुषोत्तमः।
इज्यते योऽखिलाधारस्तं द्रक्ष्यामि जगत्पतिम्

In yajnyaas, He is worshipped as yajnya purusha. By all the other purushaas, He is worshipped as Purushottama. He is the support of the entire world. That Supreme Lord of the world, I am going to see His face.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajñeṣu yajñapuruṣaḥ puruṣaiḥ puruṣottamaḥ . ijyate yo’khilādhārastaṃ drakṣyāmi jagatpatim

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.7

इष्ट्वा यमिन्द्रो यज्ञानां शतेनामरराजताम्।
अवाप तमनन्तादिमहं द्रक्ष्यामि केशवम्

Having worshipped Him with a yaaga, ishti, Indra performed hundred yaagas worshipping Bhagavaan Vishnu, he is called Shatakratu. And he became the lord of the devaas. The beginningless and endless Bhagavaan, I am going to see that Keshava tomorrow.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

iṣṭvā yamindro yajñānāṃ śatenāmararājatām . avāpa tamanantādimahaṃ drakṣyāmi keśavam

Śrīdhara

iṣṭvā iti . nāsty antaś ca ādiś ca yasya tam anantādim ||७|| na brahmā nendra-rudrāśvi-vasv-āditya-marud-gaṇāḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इष्ट्वा इति । नास्त्य् अन्तश् च आदिश् च यस्य तम् अनन्तादिम् ॥७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Having worshipped Him with a yaaga, ishti, Indra performed hundred yaagas worshipping Bhagavaan Vishnu, he is called Shatakratu. And he became the lord of the devaas. The beginningless and endless Bhagavaan, I am going to see that Keshava tomorrow.

VP 5.17.8

न ब्रह्मा नेन्द्र-रुद्राश्वि-वस्व्-आदित्य-मरुद्-गणाः।
यस्य स्वरूपं जानन्ति प्रत्यक्षं याति मे हरिः

Whose svaroopa, even Indra, Rudra, Ashvini devataas, Ashta Vasus, Aadityaas, Marut Ganaas - none of them can know or realize His form, is going to be perceived by me, directly by my eyes. This is going to be pratyaksya to me tomorrow.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na brahma nendrarudrāśvivasvādityamarudgaṇāḥ . yasya svarūpaṃ jānanti pratyakṣaṃ yāti me hariḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.9

सर्वात्मा सर्ववित्सर्वः सर्वभूतेष्ववस्थितः।
योह्यऽचिन्त्योऽव्ययो व्यापी स वक्ष्यति मया सह

He is the inner self of everything, He knows everything. He is all pervading, inside and outside, without any gap. He is not like agni or vaayu, but like aakaasha. It is impossible to think of Him, to know His svaroopa fully. He is avayaya, and all pervading. He is going to converse with me tomorrow.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sarvātmā sarvavitsarvaḥ sarvabhūteṣvavasthitaḥ . yohya’cintyo’vyayo vyāpī sa vakṣyati mayā saha

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.10

मत्स्य कूर्मवराहाश्वसिंहरूपादिभिः स्थितिम्।
चकार जगतो योऽजः सोऽद्य मामालपिष्यति

That One who incarnated as Matsya, Koorma, Varaaha, Hayagreeva, Narasimha, One who did wonderful acts to sustain this world, the beginningless one, is going to converse with me today.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

matsya kūrmavarāhāśvasiṃharūpādibhiḥ sthitim . cakāra jagato yo’jaḥ so’dya māmālapiṣyati

Śrīdhara

matsyeti . aśva-siṃha-rūpādibhiḥ aśvo hayagrīvaḥ . siṃho nṛsiṃhaḥ . mām ālapiṣyatīti akrūreti tātety ābhāṣayiṣyate ||१०|| sāmprataṃ ca jagat-svāmī kāryam ātma-hṛdi sthitam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मत्स्येति । अश्व-सिंह-रूपादिभिः अश्वो हयग्रीवः । सिंहो नृसिंहः । माम् आलपिष्यतीति अक्रूरेति तातेत्य् आभाषयिष्यते ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

That One who incarnated as Matsya, Koorma, Varaaha, Hayagreeva, Narasimha, One who did wonderful acts to sustain this world, the beginningless one, is going to converse with me today.

VP 5.17.11

साम्प्रतं च जगत्-स्वामी कार्यम् आत्म-हृदि स्थितम्।
कर्तुं मनुष्यतां प्राप्तः स्वेच्छा-देह-धृद् अव्ययः

That Supreme Lord of the world, in order to perform His wonderful acts during his avataara, according to His sankalpa, He has come here taking on the form of a human. He takes on whatever form He wants, as per His will.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sāṃprataṃ ca jagatsvāmī kāryamātmahṛdi sthitam . kartuṃ manuṣyatāṃ prāptaḥsvecchādehadhṛdavyayaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.12

योऽनन्तः पृथिवीं धत्ते शेखरस्थितिसंस्थिताम्।
सोऽवतीर्णो जगत्यर्थे माम क्रूरेति वक्ष्यति

That Ananta, who supports the entire earth on His head, the one who has incarnated on this earth for the good of the world, that Balaraama is going to address me as " Akroora".

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yo’nantaḥ pṛthivīṃ dhatte śekharasthitisaṃsthitām . so’vatīrṇo jagatyarthe māma krūreti vakṣyati

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.13

पितृपुत्रसुहृद्भ्रातृमातृबन्धुमयीमिमाम्।
यन्मायां नालमुत्तर्तुं जगत्तस्मै नमोनमः

The prakruti which is made of Sattva, Rajas, Tamas, which is modified in all the forms of father, son, friend, brother, mother, relative, etc. Different people think in different relationships. Nobody can understand all these, or cross over the maaya. For one who surrenders to Him, out of His own grace, one can cross over this maayaa. To that Supreme Lord, I pay obeisance to Him.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pitṛputrasuhṛdbhrātṛmātṛbandhumayīmimām . yanmāyāṃ nālamuttartuṃ jagattasmai namonamaḥ

Śrīdhara

punar māyā-pratibandha-nivṛttaye tam eva praṇamati—pitṛ-putra- ity-ādi-tribhiḥ ||१३|| taraty avidyāṃ vitatāṃ hṛdi yasmin niveśite .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पुनर् माया-प्रतिबन्ध-निवृत्तये तम् एव प्रणमति—पितृ-पुत्र- इत्य्-आदि-त्रिभिः ॥१३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The prakruti which is made of Sattva, Rajas, Tamas, which is modified in all the forms of father, son, friend, brother, mother, relative, etc. Different people think in different relationships. Nobody can understand all these, or cross over the maaya. For one who surrenders to Him, out of His own grace, one can cross over this maayaa. To that Supreme Lord, I pay obeisance to Him.

VP 5.17.14

तरत्य् अविद्यां विततां हृदि यस्मिन् निवेशिते।
योगमायाम् अमेयाय तस्मै विद्यात्मने नमः

If one meditates upon Bhagavaan in their mind, they can cross over all this Avidyaa. He is only Yogamaayaa, He is only Ameya. I pay obeisance to Him. Maayaa is told as Avidyaa here, because, what it does is - it hides the Bhagavat svaroopa from us, we will not be able to realize that He is there inside us, and it also creates wrong understanding; that is why it is told as Avidyaa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

taratyavidyāṃ vitatāṃ hṛdi yasminniveśite . yogamāyāmameyāya tasmai vidyātmane namaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.15

यज्वभिर्यज्ञपुरुषो वासुदेवश्च सात्त्वतैः।
वेदान्तवेदिभिर्विष्णुः प्रोच्यते यो नतोऽस्मि तम्

By those who perform yaagaas, He is worshipped as Yajnya Purusha. By those who are established in performing the Bhagavat shaastraas, Aagamaas, Paancharaatra, He is worshipped as Vaasudeva. Those who are knowledgeable about Vedaanta, they worship Him as Vishnu. That is how He is eulogized by all, and I bow down to Him.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yajvabhiryajñapuruṣo vāsudevaśca sāttvataiḥ . vedāntavedibhirviṣṇuḥ procyate yo nato’smi tam

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.16

यथा यत्र जगद्धाम्नि धातर्येतत्प्रतिष्ठितम्।
सदसत्तेन सत्येन मय्यसौ यातु सौम्याताम्

One who is supporting the whole world, everything is established in Him, He supports everything. That is the truth, and because of that truth, let Him be pleased with me.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathā yatra jagaddhāmni dhātaryetatpratiṣṭhitam . sadasattena satyena mayyasau yātu saumyātām

Śrīdhara

atha kaṃsasya priyo’yam iti mayy asūyām akurvan prasanno bhavatv iti prārthayate—yathā iti . sad-asat kārya-kāraṇātmakaṃ sādhv-asādhu-vṛttopetaṃ vā etaj jagat yatra dhāmni āśraye pratiṣṭhitaṃ tena satyena sarvādhāratayā sarva-sākṣitvena mayi saumya tāṃ prasannatāṃ yātu ||१६|| smṛte sakala-kalyāṇa-bhājanaṃ yatra jāyate .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अथ कंसस्य प्रियोऽयम् इति मय्य् असूयाम् अकुर्वन् प्रसन्नो भवत्व् इति प्रार्थयते—यथा इति । सद्-असत् कार्य-कारणात्मकं साध्व्-असाधु-वृत्तोपेतं वा एतज् जगत् यत्र धाम्नि आश्रये प्रतिष्ठितं तेन सत्येन सर्वाधारतया सर्व-साक्षित्वेन मयि सौम्य तां प्रसन्नतां यातु ॥१६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

One who is supporting the whole world, everything is established in Him, He supports everything. That is the truth, and because of that truth, let Him be pleased with me.

VP 5.17.17

स्मृते सकलकल्याणभाजनं यत्र जायते।
पुरुषस्तमजं नित्यं व्रजामि शरणं हरिम्

On just remembering Him, one will be bestowed with all the auspicious things, that Purusha who is well known in the Purusha Sookta, I take refuge in that Hari. This is how Akroora is praying himself, in his mind, waiting eagerly to get the darshana of Sri Krishna.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

smṛte sakalakalyāṇabhājanaṃ yatra jāyate . puruṣastamajaṃ nityaṃ vrajāmi śaraṇaṃ harim

Śrīdhara

tasmān niḥśaṅkas tam ahaṃ vrajāmīty āha—tasmād iti ||३३|| || iti śrīdhara-svāmi-viracitātma-prakāśākhya-śrī-viṣṇu-mahāpurāṇa-vyākhyāyāṃ pañcamāṃśe dvādaśo’dhyāyaḥ || iti śrī-viṣṇu-purāṇe pañcame’ṃśe saptadaśo’dhyāyaḥ ||१७|| —o)०(o—

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तस्मान् निःशङ्कस् तम् अहं व्रजामीत्य् आह—तस्माद् इति ॥३३॥ ॥ इति श्रीधर-स्वामि-विरचितात्म-प्रकाशाख्य-श्री-विष्णु-महापुराण-व्याख्यायां पञ्चमांशे द्वादशोऽध्यायः ॥ इति श्री-विष्णु-पुराणे पञ्चमेऽंशे सप्तदशोऽध्यायः ॥१७॥ —ओ)०(ओ—

Śrīdhara commentary.

On just remembering Him, one will be bestowed with all the auspicious things, that Purusha who is well known in the Purusha Sookta, I take refuge in that Hari. This is how Akroora is praying himself, in his mind, waiting eagerly to get the darshana of Sri Krishna.

VP 5.17.18

इत्थं सञ्चिन्तयन् विष्णुं भक्ति-नम्रात्म-मानसः।
अक्रूरो गोकुलं प्राप्तः किञ्चित् सूर्ये विराजति

Sri Paraasharar - Akroora's body, mind, was completely filled with devotion to Krishna. Sunset was about to happen. At that time, he reached Gokula, thinking about Bhagavaan and His wonderful svaroopa. Just before sunset, when the Sun was just setting, at that time, Akroora reached Gokula, all the time thinking with great devotion, about Sri Krishna. At Gokula, Akroora sees Krishna's wonderful form and eulogizes Him. How he sees this wonderful form of Krishna, and the darshana that he gets, and things which happen after that, are going to start from here, which we see next.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

itthaṃ saṃcintayanviṣṇuṃ bhaktinamrātmamānasaḥ . akrūro gokulaṃ prāptaḥ kiñcitsūrye virājati

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.19

स ददर्श तदा कृष्णमादावादोहने गवाम्।
वत्समध्यागतं फुल्लनीलोत्पलदलच्छविम्

He then saw Krishna in a place where cows were milked, at the time of milking of the cows, in the evening. Krishna was among the calves. and had the wonderful radiance of the petal of a bloomed Neelotpala, blue lily.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sa dadarśa tadā kṛṣṇamādāvādohane gavām . vatsamadhyāgataṃ phullanīlotpaladalacchavim

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.17.20

प्रफुल्लपद्मपत्राक्षं श्रीवत्सांकितवक्षसम्।
प्रलंबबाहुमायामतुङ्गोरस्थलमुन्नसम्

His eyes were like the petals of a just bloomed lotus. Akroora saw the Srivatsa mark on His chest. Krishna had long arms. Akroora saw His broad and high chest, and high nose. All these wonderful features, Akroora saw in Krishna.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

praphullapadmapatrākṣaṃ śrīvatsāṃkitavakṣasam . pralaṃbabāhumāyāmatuṅgorasthalamunnasam

Śrīdhara

praphulla- iti . āyāmaṃ vistāravat tuṅgam ura-sthalaṃ yasya tam ||२०|| sa-vilāsa-smitādhāraṃ bibhrāṇaṃ mukha-paṅkajam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

VP 5.17.28
