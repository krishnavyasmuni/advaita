VP 3.12.1

देवगोब्राह्मणान्सिद्धान्वृद्धाचार्यांस्तथाऽर्चयेत् । द्विकालं च नमेत्सन्ध्यामग्नीनुपचरेत्तथा
॥१॥

Aurva: One should honor the gods, cows, Brāhmaṇas, perfected beings, elders, and teachers; perform Sandhyā twice daily, including midday, and maintain the sacred fires.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

devagobrāhmaṇānsiddhānvṛddhācāryāṃstathā’rcayet । dvikālaṃ ca nametsandhyāmagnīnupacarettathā ॥१॥ — deva-go-dvija-siddha-vṛddha-ācāryān — gods, cows, twice-born, perfected beings, elders, and teachers; arcayet — should honor; sandhyām — Sandhyā; upāset — should attend; agni-kāryāṇi — fire rites; kuryāt — should perform.

Śrīdhara

brāhma muhūrttamārabhya niśīthāntaṃ nirūpitāḥ । sadācārā dvādaśe tu ta evāniyameritāḥ । prāyeṇa tu gṛhasthānāṃ trivargaparacetasām । dṛṣṭavādṛṣṭapradhānānāṃ saddharmāṇāmihoktayaḥ — Śrīdhara explains that the conduct described from the Brahma-muhūrta through midnight is set out in this twelfth chapter. These are chiefly the good duties of householders devoted to the three aims, with attention to both visible and invisible results.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ब्राह्म मुहूर्त्तमारभ्य निशीथान्तं निरूपिताः । सदाचारा द्वादशे तु त एवानियमेरिताः । प्रायेण तु गृहस्थानां त्रिवर्गपरचेतसाम् । दृष्टवादृष्टप्रधानानां सद्धर्माणामिहोक्तयः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1).

Śrīdhara explains that the conduct described from the Brahma-muhūrta through midnight is set out in this twelfth chapter. These are chiefly the good duties of householders devoted to the three aims, with attention to both visible and invisible results.

VP 3.12.2

सदाऽनुपहते वस्त्रे प्रशस्ताश्च महौषधीः । गारुडानि च रत्नानि बिभृयात्प्रयतो नरः
॥२॥

A person should be using all of these. To basically keep good health.

VP 3.12.11

न श्मश्रु भक्षयेल्लोष्टं न मृद्रीयाद्विचक्षणः । ज्योतींष्यमेध्यशस्तानि नाभिवीक्षेत च प्रभो

The alternative reading also prohibits looking at lights that are harmful to the eyes.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na śmaśru bhakṣayelloṣṭaṃ na mṛdrīyādvicakṣaṇaḥ । jyotīṃṣyamedhyaśastāni nābhivīkṣeta ca prabho ॥११॥

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

न मृद्नीयान्न मर्द्दयेत्, अमेध्योऽशुचिः सन् ज्योतींषि सूर्यादीनि शस्तानि ब्राह्मणादीनि नाभिवीक्षेत । ज्योतींष्यमेध्याशस्तानीति पाठे ज्योतींषि चक्षुःप्रतिकूलानि, अमेध्यानि पुरीषादीनि अशस्तानि अमङ्गलानि

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 11).

Śrīdhara explains “do not crush” as do not knead or break the clods. Depending on the reading, the prohibited lights are either the Sun and other auspicious luminaries while one is impure, or lights that are impure, harmful to the eyes, or inauspicious.

VP 3.12.12

नग्नां परस्त्रियं चैव सूर्यं चास्तमयोदये । न हुंकुर्याच्छवं गन्धं शवगन्धो हि सोमजः
॥१२॥

VP 3.12.41

मैत्रीद्रवान्तःकरणस्तस्य मुक्तिः करे स्थिता
॥४१॥

A person who is always following this sadaachaara, the shaastraas here, a very learned person, and also very obedient, he does not do sin even against sinners; against those who talk very rudely, towards them also he is very nice. His heart melts with friendship. For such a person, liberation is in his very hands itself.

VP 3.12.44

प्रियमुक्तं हितं नैतदिति मत्वा न तद्वदेव । श्रेयस्तत्र हितं वाच्य यद्यप्यत्यन्तमप्रियम्
॥४४॥

Knowing that something is pleasing others, but it is not good for them, one should not speak at all. Instead of telling something which is priya but not hita, one should tell hita even if it is not priya. Even if it very displeasing to others, even then if it is hita, if at all one has to speak, he should speak it out.
