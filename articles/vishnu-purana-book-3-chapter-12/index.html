VP 3.12.1

और्व उवाच— देव-गो-ब्राह्मणान् सिद्धान् वृद्धाचार्यांस् तथार्चयेत्।
द्विकालं च नमेत् सन्ध्याम् अग्नीन् उपचरेत् तथा॥१॥

One should honour the gods, cows, Brāhmaṇas, perfected beings, elders, and teachers; one should salute the Sandhyā twice daily and likewise tend the sacred fires.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

deva-go-brāhmaṇān — gods, cows, and Brāhmaṇas; siddhān — perfected beings; vṛddha-ācāryān — elders and teachers; tathā arcayet — likewise he should honour; dvi-kālam — twice daily; ca — and; namet — he should salute; sandhyām — Sandhyā; agnīn upacaret — he should tend the fires; tathā — likewise

Śrīdhara

brāhma-muhūrta-ārabhya niśītha-antam nirūpitāḥ — described from Brahma-muhūrta through midnight; sad-ācārāḥ dvādaśe tu te eva aniyama-īritāḥ — in the twelfth, those same good practices are stated without fixed rule; prāyeṇa tu gṛhasthānām tri-varga-para-cetasām — mostly for householders whose minds are devoted to the three aims; dṛṣṭa-adṛṣṭa-pradhānānām sad-dharmāṇām iha uktayaḥ — the teachings here concern good duties focused on seen and unseen results

Transliteration

devagobrāhmaṇānsiddhānvṛddhācāryāṃstathā'rcayet | dvikālaṃ ca nametsandhyāmagnīnupacarettathā ||1||

Śrīdhara

brāhma muhūrttamārabhya niśīthāntaṃ nirūpitāḥ । sadācārā dvādaśe tu ta evāniyameritāḥ । prāyeṇa tu gṛhasthānāṃ trivargaparacetasām । dṛṣṭavādṛṣṭapradhānānāṃ saddharmāṇāmihoktayaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ब्राह्म मुहूर्त्तमारभ्य निशीथान्तं निरूपिताः । सदाचारा द्वादशे तु त एवानियमेरिताः । प्रायेण तु गृहस्थानां त्रिवर्गपरचेतसाम् । दृष्टवादृष्टप्रधानानां सद्धर्माणामिहोक्तयः

Śrīdhara commentary.

One should honour the gods, cows, Brāhmaṇas, perfected beings, elders, and teachers; one should salute the Sandhyā twice daily and likewise tend the sacred fires.

प्रियम् उक्तं हितं नैतद् इति मत्वा न तद् वदेत्।
श्रेयस् तद्-रहितं वाच्यं यद्यप्य् अत्यन्त-विप्रियम्॥४४॥

Knowing that what is pleasing is not beneficial, he should not say it; what is beneficial should be spoken, even if it is extremely unpleasant.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

priyam uktam — what is pleasing when spoken; hitam na etat iti matvā — considering that this is not beneficial; na tat vadet — he should not say it; śreyaḥ tat-rahitam — the good, free of that [pleasantness]; vācyam — is to be spoken; yady api atyanta-vipriyam — even if extremely unpleasant

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

priyamuktaṃ hitaṃ naitaditi matvā na tadvadet | śreyastadrahitaṃ vācyaṃ yadyapyatyantavipriyam ||44||

VP 3.12.45

प्राणिनाम् उपकाराय यद् एवेह परत्र च।
कर्मणा मनसा वाचा तद् एव मतिमान् भजेत्॥४५॥

For the welfare of living beings here and hereafter, the wise man should pursue that very good through body, mind, and speech.
