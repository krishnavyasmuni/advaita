VP 1.19.1

हिरण्यकशिपुः श्रुत्वा तां कृत्यां वितथीकृताम् । आहूय पुत्रं पप्रच्छ प्रभावस्यास्य कारणम् ॥१॥ ॥1॥

Hiraṇyakaśipu heard that the Kr̥tyā had been rendered ineffective, summoned his son, and asked what had caused this power.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Key terms — hiraṇyakaśipuḥ, śrutvā, tāṃ, kṛtyāṃ, vitathīkṛtām, āhūya; direct sense — Hiraṇyakaśipu heard that the Kr̥tyā had been rendered ineffective, summoned his son, and asked what had caused this power.

Śrīdhara

Commentary — No separate Sanskrit gloss is supplied in the source.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hiraṇyakaśipuḥ śrutvā tāṃ kṛtyāṃ vitathīkṛtām . āhūya putraṃ papraccha prabhāvasyāsya kāraṇam ||१|| ||1||

Śrīdhara

No separate Sanskrit gloss is supplied in the source.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

No separate Sanskrit gloss is supplied in the source.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The failed Kr̥tyā sends the king back to Prahlāda, whose power he cannot explain.

VP 1.19.5

अन्येषां यो न पापानि चिन्तयत्य् आत्मनो यथा । तस्य पापागमस् तात हेत्वभावान् न विद्यते ॥५॥ ॥5॥

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Key terms — anyeṣāṃ, yo, na, pāpāni, cintayaty, ātmano; direct sense — Father, one who does not think of other beings’ sins as he thinks of his own does not incur sin, because there is no cause for it.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Freedom from sin follows from refusing to wish harm on others.

Because the world is not separate from Him, He is the origin and object of meditation.
