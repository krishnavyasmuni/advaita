VP 1.19.1

हिरण्यकशिपुः श्रुत्वा तां कृत्यां वितथीकृताम् । आहूय पुत्रं पप्रच्छ प्रभावस्यास्य कारणम् ॥१॥

When Hiraṇyakaśipu heard that the powerful incantations of his priests had been defeated, he sent for his son and demanded the secret of his extraordinary might.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — hiraṇyakaśipuḥ — Hiraṇyakaśipu; śrutvā — hearing; tām kṛtyām — that kṛtyā; vitathīkṛtām — made ineffective; āhūya — summoning; putram — his son; papraccha — asked; prabhāvasya asya — of this power; kāraṇam — the cause.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hiraṇyakaśipuḥ śrutvā tāṃ kṛtyāṃ vitathīkṛtām . āhūya putraṃ papraccha prabhāvasyāsya kāraṇam ||१||

VP 1.19.2

प्रह्लाद सुप्रभावोऽसि किम् एतत् ते विचेष्टितम् । एतन् मन्त्रादिजनितम् उताहो सहजं तव ॥२॥

“Prahlāda,” he said, “thou art possessed of marvellous powers; whence are they derived? are they the result of magic rites? or have they accompanied thee from birth?”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — prahlāda — O Prahlāda; suprabhāvaḥ asi — you possess great power; kim etat te viceṣṭitam — what is this action of yours?; etat mantra-ādi-janitam — produced by mantra and the like; uta āho — or rather; sahajaṃ tava — natural to you.

Śrīdhara

Words — sahajaṃ naisargikam; sense — “Natural” means innate.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prahlāda suprabhāvo’si kim etat te viceṣṭitam . etan mantrādijanitam utāho sahajaṃ tava ||२||

Śrīdhara

sahajaṃ naisargikam ||२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सहजं नैसर्गिकम् ॥२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Natural” means innate.

VP 1.19.3

एवं पृष्टस्त् तदा पित्रा प्रह्लादोऽसुरबालकः । प्रणिपत्य पितुः पादाव् इदं वचनम् अब्रवीत् ॥३॥

Prahlāda, thus interrogated, bowed down to his father’s feet and replied,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — evam pṛṣṭaḥ — asked thus; tadā — then; pitrā — by his father; prahlādaḥ asurabālakaḥ — the young Asura Prahlāda; praṇipatya — bowing; pituḥ pādau — his father’s feet; idam vacanam abravīt — spoke these words.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃ pṛṣṭast tadā pitrā prahlādo’surabālakaḥ . praṇipatya pituḥ pādāv idaṃ vacanam abravīt ||३||

VP 1.19.4

न मन्त्रादिकृतं तात न वा नैसर्गिकं मम । प्रभाव एष सामान्यो यस्य यस्याच्युतो हृदि ॥४॥

“Whatever power I possess, father, is neither the result of magic rites, nor is it inseparable from my nature; it is no more than that which is possessed by all in whose hearts Achyuta abides.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — na mantra-ādi-kṛtam — not produced by mantra and the like; tāta — father; na vā naisargikam mama — nor natural to me; prabhāvaḥ eṣaḥ — this power; sāmānyaḥ — common or equal; yasya yasya — of whoever; acyutaḥ hṛdi — Acyuta is in the heart.

Śrīdhara

Words — sāmānyaḥ samānaḥ; sense — “Common” means equal [to all].

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

na mantrādikṛtaṃ tāta na vā naisargikaṃ mama . prabhāva eṣa sāmānyo yasya yasyācyuto hṛdi ||४||

Śrīdhara

sāmānyaḥ samānaḥ ||४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सामान्यः समानः ॥४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Common” means equal [to all].

VP 1.19.5

अन्येषां यो न पापानि चिन्तयत्य् आत्मनो यथा । तस्य पापागमस् तात हेत्वभावान् न विद्यते ॥५॥

He who meditates not of wrong to others, but considers them as himself, is free from the effects of sin, inasmuch as the cause does not exist;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — anyeṣām — of others; yaḥ — who; na cintayati pāpāni — does not think of sins; ātmano yathā — as of himself; tasya pāpāgamaḥ — his coming of sin; tāta — father; hetvabhāvāt — from absence of a cause; na vidyate — does not exist.

Śrīdhara

Words — kiṃ ca, anyeṣāṃ yaḥ pāpāni tatphalāni duḥkhāni na cintayati, tasya pāpāgamo duḥkhāgamo nāsti; sense — Moreover, for one who does not consider the sins of others, their fruits—namely, sufferings—there is no accrual of sin, nor accrual of suffering.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

anyeṣāṃ yo na pāpāni cintayaty ātmano yathā . tasya pāpāgamas tāta hetvabhāvān na vidyate ||५||

Śrīdhara

kiṃ ca, anyeṣāṃ yaḥ pāpāni tatphalāni duḥkhāni na cintayati, tasya pāpāgamo duḥkhāgamo nāsti ||५||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

किं च, अन्येषां यः पापानि तत्फलानि दुःखानि न चिन्तयति, तस्य पापागमो दुःखागमो नास्ति ॥५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Moreover, for one who does not consider the sins of others, their fruits—namely, sufferings—there is no accrual of sin, nor accrual of suffering.

VP 1.19.6

कर्मणा मनसा वाचा परपीडां करोति यः । तद्बीजजन्यं फलति प्रभूतं तस्य चाशुभम् ॥६॥

but he who inflicts pain upon others, in act, thought, or speech, sows the seed of future birth, and the fruit that awaits him after birth is pain.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — karmaṇā manasā vācā — by deed, mind, or speech; parapīḍām karoti — inflicts pain on others; yaḥ — whoever; tad-bīja-janitam — born from that seed; phalati — bears fruit; prabhūtam — abundantly; tasya ca aśubham — and his evil result.

Śrīdhara

Words — yas tat pīḍākāraṇam eva bījaṃ, tasmād janma yasya tad aśubhaṃ pāpaṃ, tasya kartuḥ . atyadhikaṃ phalati duḥkhaphalātmanā ca te; sense — The pain itself is the seed that causes [future suffering]; the sin born from that seed is the agent’s evil. It bears abundant fruit in the form of suffering for that agent.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

karmaṇā manasā vācā parapīḍāṃ karoti yaḥ . tadbījajanyaṃ phalati prabhūtaṃ tasya cāśubham ||६||

Śrīdhara

yas tat pīḍākāraṇam eva bījaṃ, tasmād janma yasya tad aśubhaṃ pāpaṃ, tasya kartuḥ . atyadhikaṃ phalati duḥkhaphalātmanā ca te ||६||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यस् तत् पीडाकारणम् एव बीजं, तस्माद् जन्म यस्य तद् अशुभं पापं, तस्य कर्तुः । अत्यधिकं फलति दुःखफलात्मना च ते ॥६॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The pain itself is the seed that causes [future suffering]; the sin born from that seed is the agent’s evil. It bears abundant fruit in the form of suffering for that agent.

VP 1.19.7

सोऽहं न पापम् इच्छामि न करोमि वदामि वा । चिन्तयन् सर्वभूतस्थम् आत्मन्यपि च केशवम् ॥७॥

I wish no evil to any, and do and speak no offence; for I behold Keśava in all beings, as in my own soul.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — saḥ aham — I; na pāpam icchāmi — do not desire sin; na karomi — do not do; vadāmi vā — or speak it; cintayan — contemplating; sarvabhūtastham — dwelling in all beings; ātmani api — also in myself; keśavam — Keśava.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

so’haṃ na pāpam icchāmi na karomi vadāmi vā . cintayan sarvabhūtastham ātmanyapi ca keśavam ||७||

VP 1.19.8

शारीरं मानसं दुःखं दैवं भूतभवं तथा । सर्वत्र शुभचित्तस्य तस्य मे जा‎यते कुतः ॥८॥

Whence should corporeal or mental suffering or pain, inflicted by elements or the gods, affect me, whose heart is thoroughly purified by him?

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — śārīram — bodily; mānasam — mental; duḥkham — suffering; daivam — divine; bhūtabhavam — produced by elements; tathā — likewise; sarvatra śubhacittasya — whose mind is auspicious everywhere; tasya me — for me; jāyate kutaḥ — how can it arise?

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śārīraṃ mānasaṃ duḥkhaṃ daivaṃ bhūtabhavaṃ tathā . sarvatra śubhacittasya tasya me jā‎yate kutaḥ ||८||

VP 1.19.9

एवं सर्वेषु भूतेषु भक्तिर् अव्यभिचारिणी । कर्तव्या पण्डितैर् ज्ञात्वा सर्वभूतमयं हरिम् ॥९॥

Love, then, for all creatures will be assiduously cherished by all those who are wise in the knowledge that Hari is all things.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — evam — thus; sarveṣu bhūteṣu — in all beings; bhaktiḥ avyabhicāriṇī — unwavering devotion; kartavyā — should be cultivated; paṇḍitaiḥ — by the wise; jñātvā — knowing; sarvabhūtamayam harim — Hari as consisting of all beings.

Śrīdhara

Words — evaṃ sarvabhūtamayaṃ hariṃ jñātvā sarveṣu bhūteṣu tad bhaktiḥ kartavyā; sense — Knowing Hari as consisting of all beings, devotion to Him should be practiced in all beings.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃ sarveṣu bhūteṣu bhaktir avyabhicāriṇī . kartavyā paṇḍitair jñātvā sarvabhūtamayaṃ harim ||९||

Śrīdhara

evaṃ sarvabhūtamayaṃ hariṃ jñātvā sarveṣu bhūteṣu tad bhaktiḥ kartavyā ||९||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवं सर्वभूतमयं हरिं ज्ञात्वा सर्वेषु भूतेषु तद् भक्तिः कर्तव्या ॥९॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Knowing Hari as consisting of all beings, devotion to Him should be practiced in all beings.

VP 1.19.10

इति श्रुत्वा स दैत्येन्द्रः प्रासादशिखरे स्थितः । क्रोधान्धकारितमुखः प्राह दैतेयकिङ्करान् ॥१०॥

When he had thus spoken, the Daitya monarch, his face darkened with fury, commanded his attendants to cast his son from the summit of the palace where he was sitting,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — iti śrutvā — hearing this; saḥ daityendraḥ — the Daitya king; prāsādaśikhare sthitaḥ — standing on the palace roof; krodhāndhakāritamukhaḥ — his face darkened by anger; prāha — spoke; daiteyakinkarān — to the Daitya servants.

Śrīdhara

Words — krodhenāndhakāritaṃ jātāndhakāram iva duṣprekṣyaṃ mukhaṃ yasya saḥ; sense — His face was darkened by anger, as though darkness had arisen—difficult to look upon.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

iti śrutvā sa daityendraḥ prāsādaśikhare sthitaḥ . krodhāndhakāritamukhaḥ prāha daiteyakiṅkarān ||१०||

Śrīdhara

krodhenāndhakāritaṃ jātāndhakāram iva duṣprekṣyaṃ mukhaṃ yasya saḥ ||१०||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

क्रोधेनान्धकारितं जातान्धकारम् इव दुष्प्रेक्ष्यं मुखं यस्य सः ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

His face was darkened by anger, as though darkness had arisen—difficult to look upon.

VP 1.19.11

दुरात्मा क्षित्प्यताम् अस्मात् प्रासादाच् छतयोजनात् । गिरिपृष्ठे पतत्व् अस्मिन् शिलाभिन्नाङ्गसंहतिः ॥११॥

which was many Yojanas in height, down upon the tops of the mountains, where his body should be dashed to pieces against the rocks.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — durātmā — wicked one; kṣipyatām — let him be thrown; asmāt prāsādāt — from this palace; śatayojanāt — a hundred yojanas high; patatu — let him fall; asmin giripṛṣṭhe — upon this mountain rock; śilābhinna-aṅgasaṃhatiḥ — with his body broken by stone.

Śrīdhara

Words — śastraviṣāgnikṛtyādīnāṃ maṇimantrādibhiḥ stambhanaṃ sambhavati, patane tu na kiñcit prabhavati ity āśayena daityān ādiśatidurātmā kṣipyatām iti; sense — Because weapons, poison, fire, kṛtyās, and the like can be neutralized by jewels, mantras, and so forth, but nothing can counteract a fall, he orders the Daityas, saying, “Let the wicked one be thrown.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

durātmā kṣitpyatām asmāt prāsādāc chatayojanāt . giripṛṣṭhe patatv asmin śilābhinnāṅgasaṃhatiḥ ||११||

Śrīdhara

śastraviṣāgnikṛtyādīnāṃ maṇimantrādibhiḥ stambhanaṃ sambhavati, patane tu na kiñcit prabhavati ity āśayena daityān ādiśatidurātmā kṣipyatām iti ||११||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

शस्त्रविषाग्निकृत्यादीनां मणिमन्त्रादिभिः स्तम्भनं सम्भवति, पतने तु न किञ्चित् प्रभवति इत्य् आशयेन दैत्यान् आदिशतिदुरात्मा क्षिप्यताम् इति ॥११॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Because weapons, poison, fire, kṛtyās, and the like can be neutralized by jewels, mantras, and so forth, but nothing can counteract a fall, he orders the Daityas, saying, “Let the wicked one be thrown.”

VP 1.19.12

ततस् त चिक्षिपुः सर्वे बालं दैतेयदानवाः । पपात सोऽप्यधः क्षिप्तो हृदयेनोद्वहन् हरिम् ॥१२॥

Accordingly the Daityas hurled the boy down, and he fell cherishing Hari in his heart.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — tataḥ — then; te sarve daiteyadānavāḥ — all the Daityas and Dānavas; cikṣipuḥ — threw; bālam — the boy; saḥ api — he too; adhaḥ kṣiptaḥ — thrown downward; papāta — fell; hṛdayena udvahan harim — bearing Hari in his heart.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatas ta cikṣipuḥ sarve bālaṃ daiteyadānavāḥ . papāta so’pyadhaḥ kṣipto hṛdayenodvahan harim ||१२||

VP 1.19.13

पतमानं जगद्धात्री जगद्धातरि केशवे । भक्तियुक्तं दधारैनम् उपसङ्गम्य मेदिनी ॥१३॥

Earth, the nurse of all creatures, received him gently on her lap, thus entirely devoted to Keśava, the protector of the world.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — patamānam — falling; jagaddhātrī — Earth, bearer of the world; jagaddhātari keśave — in Keśava, sustainer of the world; bhaktiyuktam enam — him, devoted; upasaṅgamya — approaching; medinī — the Earth; dadhāra — upheld.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

patamānaṃ jagaddhātrī jagaddhātari keśave . bhaktiyuktaṃ dadhārainam upasaṅgamya medinī ||१३||

VP 1.19.14

ततो विलोक्य तं स्वस्थम् अविशीर्णास्थिपञ्जरम् । हिरण्यकशिपुः प्राह शम्बरं मायिनां वरम् ॥१४॥

Beholding him uninjured by the fall, and sound in every bone, Hiraṇyakaśipu addressed himself to Śambara, the mightiest of enchanters,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — tataḥ — then; vilokya — seeing; tam — him; svastham — safe; aviśīrṇa-asthipañjaram — with unbroken skeleton; hiraṇyakaśipuḥ — Hiraṇyakaśipu; prāha — addressed; śambaram — Śambara; māyinām varam — foremost of magicians.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tato vilokya taṃ svastham aviśīrṇāsthipañjaram . hiraṇyakaśipuḥ prāha śambaraṃ māyināṃ varam ||१४||

VP 1.19.15

नास्माभिः शक्‎यते हन्तुम् असौ दुर्बुद्धिबालकः । मायां वेत्ति भवांस् तस्मान् माययैनं निषूदय ॥१५॥

and said to him, “This perverse boy is not to be destroyed by us: do you, who art potent in the arts of delusion, contrive some device for his destruction.”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — na asmābhiḥ śakyate hantum — cannot be killed by us; asau — this; durbuddhi-bālakaḥ — foolish boy; māyām vetti — knows magic; bhavān — you; tasmāt — therefore; māyayā enam niṣūdaya — destroy him by magic.

Śrīdhara

Words — vāstavair upāyair hantum aśakyatvāt tadvadhe māyāvinaṃ niyuṅktenāsmābhir iti . niṣūdaya vināśaya; sense — Because he cannot be killed by ordinary means, he appoints a magician for his death. “Destroy” means “kill.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nāsmābhiḥ śak‎yate hantum asau durbuddhibālakaḥ . māyāṃ vetti bhavāṃs tasmān māyayainaṃ niṣūdaya ||१५||

Śrīdhara

vāstavair upāyair hantum aśakyatvāt tadvadhe māyāvinaṃ niyuṅktenāsmābhir iti . niṣūdaya vināśaya ||१५||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वास्तवैर् उपायैर् हन्तुम् अशक्यत्वात् तद्वधे मायाविनं नियुङ्क्तेनास्माभिर् इति । निषूदय विनाशय ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Because he cannot be killed by ordinary means, he appoints a magician for his death. “Destroy” means “kill.”
