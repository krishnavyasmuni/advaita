VP 5.24.1

इत्थं स्तुतस्तदा तेन मुचुकुन्देन धीमता ।
प्राहेशः सर्वभूतानामनादिनिधनो हरिः

Thus praised by the wise Muchukunda, Hari, the beginningless and endless Lord of all beings, spoke.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

itthaṃ stutas tadā tena mucukundena dhīmatā . prāheśaḥ sarva-bhūtānām anādi-nidhano hariḥ

Word-for-word

ittham — thus; stutaḥ — praised; tadā — then; tena — by him; mucukundena — by Muchukunda; dhīmatā — the wise; prāha — spoke; īśaḥ — the Lord; sarva-bhūtānām — of all beings; anādi-nidhanaḥ — without beginning or end; hariḥ — Hari.

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.24.2

यथाभिवाञ्छितान्दिव्यान्गच्छ लोकान्नराधिप ।
अव्याहतपरैश्वर्यो मत्प्रसादोपबृंहितः

Go, O king, to the divine worlds you desire; your supreme sovereignty will be unobstructed, strengthened by my grace.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yathābhivāñchitān divyān gaccha lokān narādhipa . avyāhata-paraiśvaryaḥ mat-prasādopabṛṃhitaḥ

Śrīdhara

kṛtānāṃ kāmyānāṃ karmaṇāṃ phalam ādau bhuṅkṣva janmāntare mokṣaṃ dadāmīty āha—yathābhivāñchitān ity-ādi dvābhyām ||२||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कृतानां काम्यानां कर्मणां फलम् आदौ भुङ्क्ष्व जन्मान्तरे मोक्षं ददामीत्य् आह—यथाभिवाञ्छितान् इत्य्-आदि द्वाभ्याम् ॥२॥

Word-for-word

yathā-abhivāñchitān — as desired; divyān lokān — divine worlds; gaccha — go; narādhipa — O king; avyāhata — unobstructed; paraiśvaryaḥ — supreme sovereignty; mat-prasāda — by my grace; upabṛṃhitaḥ — strengthened.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Bhagavaan - Whatever divine worlds you are desiring, you will attain all of them, and enjoy there. You will have that kind of prosperity and enjoyment, which is unobstructed. Due to the greatness of My prasaada.

VP 5.24.3

भुक्त्वा दिव्यन्महाभोगान्भविष्यसि महाकुले ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.24.4

इत्युक्तः प्रणिपत्येशं जगतामच्युतं नृपः ।
गुहामुखाद्विनिष्क्रान्तस्स ददर्शाल्पकान्नरान्

Addressed thus, the king bowed to Acyuta, Lord of the worlds, emerged from the cave, and saw a few men.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ity uktaḥ praṇipatya īśaṃ jagatām acyutaṃ nṛpaḥ . guhā-mukhād viniṣkrāntaḥ sa dadarśālpakān narān

Śrīdhara

mama sattva-śuddhy-abhāvād bhagavatā na muktir datteti matvā tapas taptuṃ yayāv ity āha—ity ukta ity-ādi dvābhyām ||४||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मम सत्त्व-शुद्ध्य्-अभावाद् भगवता न मुक्तिर् दत्तेति मत्वा तपस् तप्तुं ययाव् इत्य् आह—इत्य् उक्त इत्य्-आदि द्वाभ्याम् ॥४॥

Word-for-word

iti uktaḥ — thus addressed; praṇipatya — having bowed; īśam — to the Lord; jagatām — of the worlds; acyutam — Acyuta; nṛpaḥ — the king; guhā-mukhāt — from the cave’s mouth; viniṣkrāntaḥ — having emerged; saḥ — he; dadarśa — saw; alpakān narān — a few men.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - Having been told thus, Muchukunda bows down to Krishna, who is the Supreme Lord of the world, Achyuta, he comes out of the cave, and he sees humans who are very mean and interested in small enjoyments.

VP 5.24.8

बलदेवोऽपि मैत्रेय प्रशान्ताखिलविग्रहः ।
ज्ञातिदर्शनसोत्कण्ठः प्रययौ नन्दगोकुलम्

Baladeva too, O Maitreya, with all agitation pacified and longing to see his kinsmen, went to Nanda’s cowherd settlement.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

baladevo ’pi maitreya praśāntākhila-vigrahaḥ . jñāti-darśana-sotkaṇṭhaḥ prayayau nanda-gokulam

Śrīdhara

tathā rāmo’py ekākī gokulaṃ gatvā kālindī-karṣaṇādikaṃ kṛtvā gata iti vakṣyann āha—baladevo’pi ity-ādinā yāvad uttarādhyāya-parisamāpti . baladevo’pi iti . jñātīnāṃ darśane sotkaṇṭhaḥ cāturyavān ||८||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तथा रामोऽप्य् एकाकी गोकुलं गत्वा कालिन्दी-कर्षणादिकं कृत्वा गत इति वक्ष्यन्न् आह—बलदेवोऽपि इत्य्-आदिना यावद् उत्तराध्याय-परिसमाप्ति । बलदेवोऽपि इति । ज्ञातीनां दर्शने सोत्कण्ठः चातुर्यवान् ॥८॥

Word-for-word

baladevaḥ api — Baladeva too; maitreya — O Maitreya; praśānta — pacified; akhila-vigrahaḥ — all agitation; jñāti-darśana — seeing kinsmen; sa-utkaṇṭhaḥ — longing; prayayau — went; nanda-gokulam — to Nanda’s cowherd settlement.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Baladeva, for a long time, had not seen all his relatives, in Nandagokula, and was waiting to see all of them, and wanted to immediately see them, when the war and other things had ended, and the enemies were all killed and destroyed, and all people are safe in Dwaarakaa.

VP 5.24.12

गोप्यः पप्रच्छुरपरा नागरीजनवल्लभम् ।
कच्चिदास्ते सुखं कृष्णश्चलप्रेमलवात्मकः

Other gopīs asked the beloved of the city women, “Does Kṛṣṇa, whose love is but a fickle fragment, dwell happily?”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gopyaḥ papracchur aparā nāgarī-jana-vallabham . kaccid āste sukhaṃ kṛṣṇaś cala-prema-lavātmakaḥ

Śrīdhara

kṛṣṇa-vallabhānāṃ praśnam āha—gopya ity-ādi-saptabhiḥ . gopya iti . cala-prema-lavaḥ ātma-svabhāvo yasya saḥ ||१२|| asmac-ceṣṭām apahasan na kaccit pura-yoṣitām .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कृष्ण-वल्लभानां प्रश्नम् आह—गोप्य इत्य्-आदि-सप्तभिः । गोप्य इति । चल-प्रेम-लवः आत्म-स्वभावो यस्य सः ॥१२॥

Word-for-word

gopyaḥ — the gopīs; papracchuḥ — asked; aparāḥ — other; nāgarī-jana-vallabham — the beloved of city women; kaccit — whether; āste — dwells; sukham — happily; kṛṣṇaḥ — Kṛṣṇa; cala-prema-lava-ātmakaḥ — whose nature is a fickle fragment of love.

Śrīdhara commentary.

The gopaas spoke a lot of things with Him very dearly, and all the gopis were pretending to be angry, out of love towards Him. Some of them were even jealous. Some other gopis who were in love with Krishna, asked Him thus, who is after the women of the city, and who is now very dear to them. "Is Krishna happy there, is He doing well? He has very little love, and that is also not constantly there, and keeps changing. Such Krishna, how is He doing?".

VP 5.24.17

तथापि कच्चिदालापमिहागमनसंश्रयम् ।

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tathāpi kaccid ālāpam ihāgamana-saṃśrayam . karoti kṛṣṇo vaktavyaṃ bhavatā rāma nānṛtam

Śrīdhara

tad evāha—asmad iti . asmākaṃ grāmyāṇāṃ ceṣṭām apahasan, tena saubhāgya-mānaṃ saubhāgya-garvam adhikaṃ kaccin na karoti prāyaśaḥ karotīti serṣyaḥ praśnaḥ ||१३|| kaccit smarati naḥ kṛṣṇo gītānugamanaṃ kalam . apy asau mātaraṃ draṣṭuṃ sakṛd apy āgamiṣyati ||१४|| viṣṇu-cittaḥ : kaccid iti . gītānugamanaṃ gītasyānugamanam apy asāv ity utkaṇṭhā ca-kāro’dhyahāryaḥ ||१४|| śrīdharaḥ — vidagdhā anyā ūcuḥ—kaccid iti . gītānugamanaṃ sva-gītānusaraṇam asmad-gānam kalaṃ madhuraṃ kaccit smarati ity arthaḥ . praṇaya-kopād āhuḥ—apy asau mātaram iti ||१४|| athavā kiṃ tad-ālāpaiḥ kriyantām aparāḥ kathāḥ . yasyāsmābhir vinā tena vināsmākaṃ bhaviṣyati ||१५|| viṣṇu-cittaḥ : athavā iti . athavā iti nirvedaḥ bhaviṣyati jīvanaṃ kāla-yātrā vā idaṃ citta-samādhānaṃ vineti snehānutāpau ||१५|| śrīdharaḥ — sa-nirvedam ūcuḥ— athavā iti . yasya yathā asmābhir vinā tatra sthitiḥ, tathā asmākam api tena vinā atra duḥkhenāpi sthitir bhaviṣyaty evety arthaḥ ||१५|| pitā mātā tathā bhrātā bhartā bandhu-janaś ca kim . santyaktas tat-kṛte’smābhir akṛtajña-dhvajo hi saḥ ||१६|| viṣṇu-cittaḥ : pitā iti . kiṃ kim-arthaṃ santyaktaḥ akṛtajñānāṃ dhvajaḥ pradhānaḥ ||१६|| śrīdharaḥ — śoka-taptā ūcuḥ—pitā mātā iti . akṛtajñeṣu dhvaja iva dṛśyaḥ prasiddha ity arthaḥ ||१६|| tathāpi kaccid ālāpam ihāgamana-saṃśrayam . karoti kṛṣṇo vaktavyaṃ bhavatārāma nānṛtam||१७|| viṣṇu-cittaḥ : tathāpi iti pratyāśā ||१७|| śrīdharaḥ — āśā-baddhā ūcuḥ—tathāpi iti ||१७|| dāmodaro’sau govindaḥ pura-strī-sakta-mānasaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तद् एवाह—अस्मद् इति । अस्माकं ग्राम्याणां चेष्टाम् अपहसन्, तेन सौभाग्य-मानं सौभाग्य-गर्वम् अधिकं कच्चिन् न करोति प्रायशः करोतीति सेर्ष्यः प्रश्नः ॥१३॥
कच्चित् स्मरति नः कृष्णो गीतानुगमनं कलम्५५६ ।
अप्य् असौ मातरं द्रष्टुं सकृद् अप्य् आगमिष्यति ॥१४॥
विष्णु-चित्तः : कच्चिद् इति । गीतानुगमनं गीतस्यानुगमनम् अप्य् असाव् इत्य् उत्कण्ठा च-कारोऽध्यहार्यः ॥१४॥
श्रीधरः — विदग्धा अन्या ऊचुः—कच्चिद् इति । गीतानुगमनं स्व-गीतानुसरणम् अस्मद्-गानम् कलं मधुरं कच्चित् स्मरति इत्य् अर्थः । प्रणय-कोपाद् आहुः—अप्य् असौ मातरम् इति ॥१४॥
अथवा किं तद्-आलापैः क्रियन्ताम् अपराः कथाः ।
यस्यास्माभिर्५५७ विना तेन विनास्माकं भविष्यति ॥१५॥
विष्णु-चित्तः : अथवा इति । अथवा इति निर्वेदः भविष्यति जीवनं काल-यात्रा वा इदं चित्त-समाधानं विनेति स्नेहानुतापौ ॥१५॥
श्रीधरः — स-निर्वेदम् ऊचुः— अथवा इति । यस्य यथा अस्माभिर् विना तत्र स्थितिः, तथा अस्माकम् अपि तेन विना अत्र दुःखेनापि स्थितिर् भविष्यत्य् एवेत्य् अर्थः ॥१५॥
पिता माता तथा भ्राता भर्ता बन्धु-जनश् च किम्५५८ ।
सन्त्यक्तस्५५९ तत्-कृतेऽस्माभिर् अकृतज्ञ-ध्वजो५६० हि सः ॥१६॥
विष्णु-चित्तः : पिता इति । किं किम्-अर्थं सन्त्यक्तः अकृतज्ञानां ध्वजः प्रधानः ॥१६॥
श्रीधरः — शोक-तप्ता ऊचुः—पिता माता इति । अकृतज्ञेषु ध्वज इव दृश्यः प्रसिद्ध इत्य् अर्थः ॥१६॥
करोति कृष्णो वक्तव्यं भवताराम नानृतम्५६२॥१७॥
विष्णु-चित्तः : तथापि इति प्रत्याशा ॥१७॥
श्रीधरः — आशा-बद्धा ऊचुः—तथापि इति ॥१७॥

Word-for-word

tathā api — nevertheless; kaccit — whether; ālāpam — a message; iha-āgamana-saṃśrayam — concerning coming here; karoti — makes / speaks; kṛṣṇaḥ — Kṛṣṇa; vaktavyam — what is to be said; bhavatā — by you; rāma — O Rāma; na anṛtam — not false.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Does He ever talk about coming here at all? O Balaraama, please do tell us, and don't lie. Let us have some hope that He may come sometime to see all.

VP 5.24.18

दामोदरोऽसौ गोविन्दः पुरस्त्रीसक्तमानसः ।
अपेतप्रीतिरस्मासु दुर्दर्शः प्रतिभाति नः

“That Dāmodara, Govinda, whose mind is attached to city women, appears difficult for us to see, his affection for us gone.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dāmodaro ’sau govindaḥ pura-strī-sakta-mānasaḥ . apeta-prītir asmāsu durdarśaḥ pratibhāti naḥ

Śrīdhara

nairāśyāt parasparam ūcuḥ—dāmodara iti . asāv iti rāmaṃ lakṣīkṛtya kṛṣṇākṛṣṭa-cittatayā dāmodaro govinda iti ca tam eva vadanti . asmāsu apeta-prītir ato dūrdarśaḥ pratibhāti iti nairāśyoktiḥ ||१८|| śrī-parāśara uvāca— āmantritaś ca kṛṣṇeti punar dāmodareti ca .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नैराश्यात् परस्परम् ऊचुः—दामोदर इति । असाव् इति रामं लक्षीकृत्य कृष्णाकृष्ट-चित्ततया दामोदरो गोविन्द इति च तम् एव वदन्ति । अस्मासु अपेत-प्रीतिर् अतो दूर्दर्शः प्रतिभाति इति नैराश्योक्तिः ॥१८॥
श्री-पराशर उवाच—

Word-for-word

dāmodaraḥ asau — that Dāmodara; govindaḥ — Govinda; pura-strī — city women; sakta-mānasaḥ — whose mind is attached; apeta-prītiḥ — whose affection has departed; asmāsu — from us; durdarśaḥ — difficult to see; pratibhāti — appears; naḥ — to us.

Śrīdhara commentary.

This Daamodara, Govinda is deeply interested in all the women of the city now. He has lost all love for us. We don't think we are going to see Him at all, any time.
