VP 5.3.2

ततोऽखिलजगत्पद्मबोधायाच्युतभानुना

in order for it to bloom, the Sun in the form of Achyuta rose. To bring enlightenment to the whole world, the Lord rose like the Sun, incarnated Himself. Devaki herself was like a Poorva Sandhyaa which leads to sunrise. Leading to the rising of the Sun of the form of Krishna, to bring enlightenment to the lotus of all the three worlds.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत इति । अखिलं जगद् एव पद्मे तस्य बोधाय विकासाय अच्युत-रूपेण भानुना देवक्य् एव पूर्व-सन्ध्या तस्याम् आविर्भूतम् ॥२॥ तज्-जन्म-दिनम् अत्यर्थम् आह्लाद्य् अमल-दिङ्-मुखम् ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

ततो अर्खि जगत् पद्म र्ोधाय अच्युत भानुना | देर्वक पूर्विसांध्यायाम् आवर्वभूितां महात्मना ॥ २ ॥ This is a wonderful shloka telling about the Krishnaavataara. Krishna Himself is like the Sun. He rose in order to enlighten, or make the lotus of the three worlds, [[37]] of 399 in order for it to bloom, the Sun in the form of Achyuta rose. To bring enlightenment to the whole world, the Lord rose like the Sun, incarnated Himself. Devaki herself was like a Poorva Sandhyaa which leads to sunrise. Leading to the rising of the Sun of the form of Krishna, to bring enlightenment to the lotus of all the three worlds.
