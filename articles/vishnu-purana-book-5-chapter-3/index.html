VP 5.3.1

VP 5.3.2

ततोऽखिलजगत्पद्मबोधायाच्युतभानुना । देवकीपूर्वसंध्यायाम् आविर्भूतं महात्मना

This is a wonderful shloka telling about the Krishnaavataara. Krishna Himself is like the Sun. He rose in order to enlighten, or make the lotus of the three worlds, in order for it to bloom, the Sun in the form of Achyuta rose. To bring enlightenment to the whole world, the Lord rose like the Sun, incarnated Himself. Devaki herself was like a Poorva Sandhyaa which leads to sunrise. Leading to the rising of the Sun of the form of Krishna, to bring enlightenment to the lotus of all the three worlds.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tato’khilajagatpadmabodhāyācyutabhānunā . devakīpūrvasandhyāyāmāvirbhūtaṃ mahātmanā — This is a wonderful shloka telling about the Krishnaavataara. Krishna Himself is like the Sun. He rose in order to enlighten, or make the lotus of the three worlds, in order for it to bloom, the Sun in the form of Achyuta rose. To bring enlightenment to the whole world, the Lord rose like the Sun, incarnated Himself. Devaki herself was like a Poorva Sandhyaa which leads to sunrise. Leading to the rising of the Sun of the form of Krishna, to bring enlightenment to the lotus of all the three worlds.

Śrīdhara

tata iti . akhilaṃ jagad eva padme tasya bodhāya vikāsāya acyuta-rūpeṇa bhānunā devaky eva pūrva-sandhyā tasyām āvirbhūtam — This is a wonderful shloka telling about the Krishnaavataara. Krishna Himself is like the Sun. He rose in order to enlighten, or make the lotus of the three worlds, in order for it to bloom, the Sun in the form of Achyuta rose. To bring enlightenment to the whole world, the Lord rose like the Sun, incarnated Himself. Devaki herself was like a Poorva Sandhyaa which leads to sunrise. Leading to the rising of the Sun of the form of Krishna, to bring enlightenment to the lotus of all the three worlds.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tato’khilajagatpadmabodhāyācyutabhānunā . devakīpūrvasaṃdhyāyām āvirbhūtaṃ mahātmanā

Śrīdhara

tata iti . akhilaṃ jagad eva padme tasya bodhāya vikāsāya acyuta-rūpeṇa bhānunā devaky eva pūrva-sandhyā tasyām āvirbhūtam ||२|| taj-janma-dinam atyartham āhlādy amala-diṅ-mukham .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत इति । अखिलं जगद् एव पद्मे तस्य बोधाय विकासाय अच्युत-रूपेण भानुना देवक्य् एव पूर्व-सन्ध्या तस्याम् आविर्भूतम् ॥२॥ तज्-जन्म-दिनम् अत्यर्थम् आह्लाद्य् अमल-दिङ्-मुखम् ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

This is a wonderful shloka telling about the Krishnaavataara. Krishna Himself is like the Sun. He rose in order to enlighten, or make the lotus of the three worlds, in order for it to bloom, the Sun in the form of Achyuta rose. To bring enlightenment to the whole world, the Lord rose like the Sun, incarnated Himself. Devaki herself was like a Poorva Sandhyaa which leads to sunrise. Leading to the rising of the Sun of the form of Krishna, to bring enlightenment to the lotus of all the three worlds.

VP 5.3.3

तज्जन्मदिनमत्यर्थमाह्लाद्यमलदिङ्मुखम् । बभूव सर्वलोकस्य कौमुदी शशिनो यथा

That day on which Krishnaavataara happened, on which He incarnated on this world, it was most pleasant, and all the directions were without any blemish, were blemishless, pure and clear. Just like the full moon day of the moon, when the moon is fully bright, the whole world was very pleasant.

Just like the full moon day of the moon, when the moon is fully bright, the whole world was very pleasant.

Śrīdhara

Just like the full moon day of the moon, when the moon is fully bright, the whole world was very pleasant.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.3.6

ससृजुः पुष्पवर्षाणि देवा भुव्यन्तरिक्षगाः । जज्वलुश्चाग्नयः शान्ता जायमाने जनार्दने

The gods who were moving around in the antariksha, brought down a rain of flowers in this world, when the Lord incarnated. Fires were all burning when the Lord was born. Fires which were earlier put out because of the fear of Kamsa, all started to burn again brightly, as Lord Krishna incarnated in this world.
