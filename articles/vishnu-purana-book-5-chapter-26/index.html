VP 5.26.1

भीष्मकः कुण्डिने राजा विदर्भविषयेऽभवत् । रुक्मी तस्यभवत्पुत्रो रुक्मिणी च वरानना

Sri Paraasharar - King Bheeshmaka was there in the city of Kundinapura, belonging to the Vidarbha kingdom. He had two children - a son and daughter - Rukmi was the son, and Rukmini was the daughter. The story of how Krishna marries Rukmini is being told in this chapter, which we see next.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhīṣmakaḥ kuṇḍine rājā vidarbhaviṣaye’bhavat . rukmī tasyābhavatputro rukmiṇī ca varānanā — Sri Paraasharar - King Bheeshmaka was there in the city of Kundinapura, belonging to the Vidarbha kingdom. He had two children - a son and daughter - Rukmi was the son, and Rukmini was the daughter. The story of how Krishna marries Rukmini is being told in this chapter, which we see next.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhīṣmakaḥ kuṇḍine rājā vidarbhaviṣaye’bhavat . rukmī tasyabhavatputro rukmiṇī ca varānanā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.2

रुक्मिणीं चकमे कृष्णःसा च तं चारुहीसिनि । न ददौ याचते चैनां रुक्मिद्वेषेण चक्रिणे

Rukmini decided to marry Krishna, and Krishna also was very much interested in marrying Rukmini. Though Krishna asked Rukmi, because Rukmi had hatred towards Krishna, he did not agree to give Rukmini in marriage to Krishna.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

rukmiṇīṃ cakame kṛṣṇassā ca taṃ cāruhāsinī . na dadau yācate caināṃ rukmī dveṣeṇa cakriṇe — Rukmini decided to marry Krishna, and Krishna also was very much interested in marrying Rukmini. Though Krishna asked Rukmi, because Rukmi had hatred towards Krishna, he did not agree to give Rukmini in marriage to Krishna.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

rukmiṇīṃ cakame kṛṣṇaḥsā ca taṃ cāruhīsini . na dadau yācate caināṃ rukmidveṣeṇa cakriṇe

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.3

ददौ च शिशुपालाय जरासन्धप्रचोदितः । भीष्मको रुक्मिणा सार्ध रुक्मिणीमुरुविक्रमः

Bheeshmaka along with Rukmi gave a word to Shishupaala, being told by Jaraasandha ( Bheeshmaka agreed to give Rukmini in marriage to Shishupaala). Shishupaala was Jaraasandha's adopted son. Krishna was hated by all of them, they saw Him as their enemy. Rukmi, who did not like Krishna, had enmity towards Him, agreed to give Rukmini in marriage to Shishupaala, and refused to give her to Krishna.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dadau ca śiśupālāya jarāsandhapracoditaḥ . bhīṣmako rukmiṇā sārdhaṃ rukmiṇīmuruvikramaḥ — Bheeshmaka along with Rukmi gave a word to Shishupaala, being told by Jaraasandha ( Bheeshmaka agreed to give Rukmini in marriage to Shishupaala). Shishupaala was Jaraasandha's adopted son. Krishna was hated by all of them, they saw Him as their enemy. Rukmi, who did not like Krishna, had enmity towards Him, agreed to give Rukmini in marriage to Shishupaala, and refused to give her to Krishna.

Śrīdhara

dadau iti . rukmiṇīṃ ca kanyām ādau cedirājāya dadāv iti dattavān ity arthaḥ — Bheeshmaka along with Rukmi gave a word to Shishupaala, being told by Jaraasandha ( Bheeshmaka agreed to give Rukmini in marriage to Shishupaala). Shishupaala was Jaraasandha's adopted son. Krishna was hated by all of them, they saw Him as their enemy. Rukmi, who did not like Krishna, had enmity towards Him, agreed to give Rukmini in marriage to Shishupaala, and refused to give her to Krishna.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dadau ca śiśupālāya jarāsandhapracoditaḥ . bhīṣmako rukmiṇā sārdha rukmiṇīmuruvikramaḥ

Śrīdhara

dadau iti . rukmiṇīṃ ca kanyām ādau cedirājāya dadāv iti dattavān ity arthaḥ ||३|| vivāhārthaṃ tataḥ sarve jarāsandha-mukhā nṛpāḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ददौ इति । रुक्मिणीं च कन्याम् आदौ चेदिराजाय ददाव् इति दत्तवान् इत्य् अर्थः ॥३॥ विवाहार्थं ततः सर्वे जरासन्ध-मुखा नृपाः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

ददौ च लशशुपािाय जरासांधप्रचोहदतः | भीषमको रुक्मणा साधं रुस्क्मणीमुरुवर्वक्रमः ॥ ३ ॥

VP 5.26.4

विवाहार्यं ततः सर्वे जरासंधमुखा नृपाः । भीष्मकस्य पुरं जग्मुश्शिशुपालप्रियैषिणः

When the marriage was fixed, all of them, Jaraasandha and others who were all dear to Shishupaala, went to Bheeshmaka's city, for the marriage.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vivāhāryaṃ tataḥ sarve jarāsaṃdhamukhā nṛpāḥ . bhīṣmakasya puraṃ jagmuśśiśupālapriyaiṣiṇaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.5

कृष्णोऽपि बलभद्राद्यैर्यदुभिः परिवारितः । प्रययौ कुण्डिनं द्रष्टुं विवाहं चैद्यभूभृतः

VP 5.26.6

श्वो भाविनि विवाहे तु तां कन्यां हृतवान्हरिः । विपक्षभारमासज्य रामादिष्वथ बन्धुषु

On the previous day of the marriage, Krishna escaped with Rukmini, snatched and took her away. When there were so many people who were against Him, in order to take care of all of them, in case there is a fight which may ensue, He left Balaraama and other relatives there.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śvobhāvini vivāhe tu tāṃ kanyāṃ hṛtavāna‍्hariḥ . vipakṣabhāramāsajya rāmādiṣvatha bandhuṣu — On the previous day of the marriage, Krishna escaped with Rukmini, snatched and took her away. When there were so many people who were against Him, in order to take care of all of them, in case there is a fight which may ensue, He left Balaraama and other relatives there.

Śrīdhara

śva iti . purād bahir ambikā-pūjārthaṃ nirgatāṃ tāṃ hṛtavān hariḥ vipakṣa-bhāraṃ vipakṣaiḥ saha kāryaṃ yuddhādi-saṃrambhaṃ rāmādiṣv āsajya samāropya — On the previous day of the marriage, Krishna escaped with Rukmini, snatched and took her away. When there were so many people who were against Him, in order to take care of all of them, in case there is a fight which may ensue, He left Balaraama and other relatives there.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śvo bhāvini vivāhe tu tāṃ kanyāṃ hṛtavānhariḥ . vipakṣabhāramāsajya rāmādiṣvatha bandhuṣu

Śrīdhara

śva iti . purād bahir ambikā-pūjārthaṃ nirgatāṃ tāṃ hṛtavān hariḥ vipakṣa-bhāraṃ vipakṣaiḥ saha kāryaṃ yuddhādi-saṃrambhaṃ rāmādiṣv āsajya samāropya ||६|| tataś ca pauṇḍrakaḥ śrīmān dantavakro vidūrathaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

श्व इति । पुराद् बहिर् अम्बिका-पूजार्थं निर्गतां तां हृतवान् हरिः विपक्ष-भारं विपक्षैः सह कार्यं युद्धादि-संरम्भं रामादिष्व् आसज्य समारोप्य ॥६॥ ततश् च पौण्ड्रकः श्रीमान् दन्तवक्रो विदूरथः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

श्र्वोभावर्वतन वर्वर्वाहे तु ताां कन्याां हृतर्वान् हररः | वर्वपक्षभारमासज्य रामाहदर्षु अथ र्ांधुर्षु ॥ ६ ॥

VP 5.26.7

ततश्च पैण्ड्रकश्र्श्रीमान्दन्तवक्रो विदूरथः । शिशुपालजरासंधसाल्वाद्याश्च महीभृतः

Then Poundraka, Dantavaktra, Vidooratha, Shishupaala, Jaraasandha, Saalva - all of these kings, all ganged up.

VP 5.26.8

कुपितास्ते हरिं हन्तुं चक्रुरुद्योगमुत्तमम् । निर्जिताश्च समागम्य रामाद्यैर्यदुपुङ्गवैः

VP 5.26.9

कुण्डिनं न प्रवेक्ष्यामि ह्यहत्वा युधि केशवम् । कृत्वा प्रतिज्ञां रुक्मी च हन्तुं कृष्णमनुद्रुतः

Rukmi took a vow that he will not enter Kundinapura without killing Krishna. And he went after Krishna.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 5.26.10

हत्वा बलं सनागाश्वं पत्तिस्यन्दनसंकुलम् । निर्जितः पातितश्चोर्व्यां लीलयैव स चक्रिणा

When Rukmi went along with his army, Krishna very easily, along with His army, destroyed the army of Rukmi, along with the elephants, chariots, soldiers, horses. They were all defeated very easily, and Rukmi was made to fall on the ground.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hatvā balaṃ sanāgāśvaṃ pattisyandanasaṅkulam . nirjitaḥ pātitaścorvyāṃ līlayaiva sa cakriṇā — When Rukmi went along with his army, Krishna very easily, along with His army, destroyed the army of Rukmi, along with the elephants, chariots, soldiers, horses. They were all defeated very easily, and Rukmi was made to fall on the ground.

Śrīdhara

hatveti . pātita eva kevalaṃ, na tu hataḥ, rukmiṇī-bhrātṛtvāt — When Rukmi went along with his army, Krishna very easily, along with His army, destroyed the army of Rukmi, along with the elephants, chariots, soldiers, horses. They were all defeated very easily, and Rukmi was made to fall on the ground.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hatvā balaṃ sanāgāśvaṃ pattisyandanasaṃkulam . nirjitaḥ pātitaścorvyāṃ līlayaiva sa cakriṇā

Śrīdhara

hatveti . pātita eva kevalaṃ, na tu hataḥ, rukmiṇī-bhrātṛtvāt ||१०|| nirjitya rukmiṇaṃ samyag upayeme ca rukmiṇīm .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

हत्वेति । पातित एव केवलं, न तु हतः, रुक्मिणी-भ्रातृत्वात् ॥१०॥ निर्जित्य रुक्मिणं सम्यग् उपयेमे च रुक्मिणीम् ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

हत्र्वा र्िां सनागाश्र्वां पवत्तस्यन्दनसांकुिम् | तनस्जितः पातततश्चोव्यां िीिया एर्व स चक्रक्रणा ॥ १० ॥ When Rukmi went along with his army, Krishna very easily, along with His army, destroyed the army of Rukmi, along with the elephants, chariots, soldiers, horses. They were all defeated very easily, and Rukmi was made to fall on the ground.

VP 5.26.11

निर्जित्य रुक्मिणं सम्यगुपयेमे च रुक्मिणीम् । राक्षसेन विवाहेन संप्राप्तां मधुसुदनः

Having won over Rukmi, He married Rukmini as per the Vedic rites, having brought her through the Raakshasa vivaaha, having simply stolen her and escaped with her.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.12

तस्यां जज्ञे च प्रद्युम्नो मदनांशस्सवीर्यवान् । जहार शंबरो यं वै यो जघान च शंबरम्

Rukmini got a son by name Pradyumna, who was the saakshaat amsha of Kaamadeva, Madana. He was very powerful. Shambaraasura immediately snatched and stole away the child. Later on, Paraasharar says briefly that Pradyumna killed Shambaraasura. This completes Chapter Twenty Six. Now, Chapter Twenty Seven.

He was very powerful. Shambaraasura immediately snatched and stole away the child. Later on, Paraasharar says briefly that Pradyumna killed Shambaraasura. This completes Chapter Twenty Six. Now, Chapter Twenty Seven.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasyāṃ jajñe ca pradyumno madanāṃśassavīryavān . jahāra śaṃbaro yaṃ vai yo jaghāna ca śaṃbaram
