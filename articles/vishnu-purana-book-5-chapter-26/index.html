VP 5.26.1

भीष्मकः कुण्डिने राजा विदर्भविषयेऽभवत्

The story of how Krishna marries Rukmini is being told in this chapter, which we see next.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhīṣmakaḥ kuṇḍine rājā vidarbhaviṣaye’bhavat — The story of how Krishna marries Rukmini is being told in this chapter, which we see next.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhīṣmakaḥ kuṇḍine rājā vidarbhaviṣaye’bhavat

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.2

रुक्मिणीं चकमे कृष्णस्सा च तं चारुहासिनी

रुमणीां चकमे कृषणः सा च तां चारुहालसनी | न ददौ याचते चैनाां रुस्क्मद्र्वेर्षण चक्रक्रणे ॥ २ ॥ Rukmini decided to marry Krishna, and Krishna also was very much interested in marrying Rukmini. Though Krishna asked Rukmi, because Rukmi had hatred towards Krishna, he did not agree to give Rukmini in marriage to Krishna.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

rukamiṇīṃ — Rukmini; cakame — to; kaṛṣaṇaḥsā — and; ca — was; taṃ — interested; cāruhīsini — marrying; . — Krishna; na — because; dadau — hatred; yācate — Krishna; caināṃ — not; rukamidaveṣeṇa — give; cakariṇe — marriage

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

rukmiṇīṃ cakame kṛṣṇassā ca taṃ cāruhāsinī

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.3

VP 5.26.5

कृष्णोऽपि बलभद्राद्यैर्यदुभिः परिवारितः

कृषणो अवप र्िभद्ाद्यैः यदुलभः पररर्वाररतः | प्रययौ कुस्र्णिनां द्षटुां वर्वर्वाहां चैद्यभूत्भृतः ॥ ५ ॥ Krishna, along with Balaraama and other Yaadavaas, also went to Kundinapura in order to witness the marriage of Shishupaala.

VP 5.26.6

श्वोभाविनि विवाहे तु तां कन्यां हृतवान‍्हरिः

On the previous day of the marriage, Krishna escaped with Rukmini, snatched and took her away. When there were so many people who were against Him, in order to take care of all of them, in case there is a fight which may ensue, He left Balaraama and other relatives there.

Word-for-word

When there were so many people who were against Him, in order to take care of all of them, in case there is a fight which may ensue, He left Balaraama and other relatives there.

Śrīdhara

śva iti . purād bahir ambikā-pūjārthaṃ nirgatāṃ tāṃ hṛtavān hariḥ vipakṣa-bhāraṃ vipakṣaiḥ saha kāryaṃ yuddhādi-saṃrambhaṃ rāmādiṣv āsajya samāropya ||६|| tataś ca pauṇḍrakaḥ śrīmān dantavakro vidūrathaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

श्व इति । पुराद् बहिर् अम्बिका-पूजार्थं निर्गतां तां हृतवान् हरिः विपक्ष-भारं विपक्षैः सह कार्यं युद्धादि-संरम्भं रामादिष्व् आसज्य समारोप्य ॥६॥ ततश् च पौण्ड्रकः श्रीमान् दन्तवक्रो विदूरथः ।

श्र्वोभावर्वतन वर्वर्वाहे तु ताां कन्याां हृतर्वान् हररः | वर्वपक्षभारमासज्य रामाहदर्षु अथ र्ांधुर्षु ॥ ६ ॥

VP 5.26.10

हत्वा बलं सनागाश्वं पत्तिस्यन्दनसङ्कुलम्

हत्र्वा र्िां सनागाश्र्वां पवत्तस्यन्दनसांकुिम् | तनस्जितः पातततश्चोव्यां िीिया एर्व स चक्रक्रणा ॥ १० ॥ When Rukmi went along with his army, Krishna very easily, along with His army, destroyed the army of Rukmi, along with the elephants, chariots, soldiers, horses. They were all defeated very easily, and Rukmi was made to fall on the ground.

VP 5.26.11

निर्जित्य रुक्मिणं सम्यगुपयेमे च रुक्मिणीम्

तनस्जित्य रुस्क्मणां सम्यक् उपयेमे च रुस्क्मणीम् | राक्षसेन वर्वर्वाहेन सांप्राप्ताां मधुसूदनः ॥ ११ ॥ Having won over Rukmi, He married Rukmini as per the Vedic rites, having brought her through the Raakshasa vivaaha, having simply stolen her and escaped with her.
