VP 5.26.1

भीष्मकः कुण्डिने राजा विदर्भविषयेऽभवत् । रुक्मी तस्यभवत्पुत्रो रुक्मिणी च वरानना

श्री पराशरः - भीषमकः कुस्र्णिने राजा वर्वदभिवर्वर्षये अभर्वत् | रुक्मी तस्य अभत् पुत्रो रुस्क्मणी च र्वरानना ॥ १ ॥ Sri Paraasharar - King Bheeshmaka was there in the city of Kundinapura, belonging to the Vidarbha kingdom. He had two children - a son and daughter - Rukmi was the son, and Rukmini was the daughter. The story of how Krishna marries Rukmini is being told in this chapter, which we see next.

VP 5.26.2

रुक्मिणीं चकमे कृष्णःसा च तं चारुहीसिनि । न ददौ याचते चैनां रुक्मिद्वेषेण चक्रिणे

रुमणीां चकमे कृषणः सा च तां चारुहालसनी | न ददौ याचते चैनाां रुस्क्मद्र्वेर्षण चक्रक्रणे ॥ २ ॥ Rukmini decided to marry Krishna, and Krishna also was very much interested in marrying Rukmini. Though Krishna asked Rukmi, because Rukmi had hatred towards Krishna, he did not agree to give Rukmini in marriage to Krishna.
