VP 5.26.1

भीष्मकः कुण्डिने राजा विदर्भविषयेऽभवत्।
रुक्मी तस्याभवत्पुत्रो रुक्मिणी च वरानना

Sri Paraasharar - King Bheeshmaka was there in the city of Kundinapura, belonging to the Vidarbha kingdom. He had two children - a son and daughter - Rukmi was the son, and Rukmini was the daughter. The story of how Krishna marries Rukmini is being told in this chapter, which we see next.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
bhīṣmakaḥ kuṇḍine rājā — King Bhīṣmaka at Kuṇḍina;
vidarbha-viṣaye ’bhavat — was in the Vidarbha country;
rukmī tasyābhavat putraḥ — Rukmi became his son;
rukmiṇī ca varānanā — and Rukmini, beautiful-faced
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhīṣmakaḥ kuṇḍine rājā vidarbha-viṣaye ’bhavat | rukmī tasyābhavat putro rukmiṇī ca varānanā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.2

रुक्मिणीं चकमे कृष्णस्सा च तं चारुहासिनी।
न ददौ याचते चैनां रुक्मी द्वेषेण चक्रिणे

Rukmini decided to marry Krishna, and Krishna also was very much interested in marrying Rukmini. Though Krishna asked Rukmi, because Rukmi had hatred towards Krishna, he did not agree to give Rukmini in marriage to Krishna.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
rukmiṇīṃ cakame kṛṣṇaḥ — Kṛṣṇa desired Rukmini;
sā ca taṃ cāru-hāsinī — and that lovely-smiling one, him;
na dadau — he did not give;
yācate — when asked;
enām — her;
rukmī dveṣeṇa cakriṇe — Rukmi, through hatred, to the discus-bearer
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

rukmiṇīṃ cakame kṛṣṇas sā ca taṃ cāru-hāsinī | na dadau yācate caināṃ rukmī dveṣeṇa cakriṇe

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.3

ददौ च शिशुपालाय जरासन्धप्रचोदितः।
भीष्मको रुक्मिणा सार्धं रुक्मिणीमुरुविक्रमः

Bheeshmaka along with Rukmi gave a word to Shishupaala, being told by Jaraasandha ( Bheeshmaka agreed to give Rukmini in marriage to Shishupaala). Shishupaala was Jaraasandha's adopted son. Krishna was hated by all of them, they saw Him as their enemy. Rukmi, who did not like Krishna, had enmity towards Him, agreed to give Rukmini in marriage to Shishupaala, and refused to give her to Krishna.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
dadau ca śiśupālāya — and gave to Śiśupāla;
jarāsandha-pracoditaḥ — impelled by Jarāsandha;
bhīṣmakaḥ rukmiṇā sārdham — Bhīṣmaka together with Rukmi;
rukmiṇīm uru-vikramaḥ — Rukmini, of great valor
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dadau ca śiśupālāya jarāsandha-pracoditaḥ | bhīṣmako rukmiṇā sārdhaṃ rukmiṇīm uru-vikramaḥ

Śrīdhara

dadau iti . rukmiṇīṃ ca kanyām ādau cedirājāya dadāv iti dattavān ity arthaḥ ||३|| vivāhārthaṃ tataḥ sarve jarāsandha-mukhā nṛpāḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ददौ इति । रुक्मिणीं च कन्याम् आदौ चेदिराजाय ददाव् इति दत्तवान् इत्य् अर्थः ॥३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Bheeshmaka along with Rukmi gave a word to Shishupaala, being told by Jaraasandha ( Bheeshmaka agreed to give Rukmini in marriage to Shishupaala). Shishupaala was Jaraasandha's adopted son. Krishna was hated by all of them, they saw Him as their enemy. Rukmi, who did not like Krishna, had enmity towards Him, agreed to give Rukmini in marriage to Shishupaala, and refused to give her to Krishna.

VP 5.26.4

विवाहार्थं ततः सर्वे जरासन्धमुखा नृपाः।
भीष्मकस्य पुरं जग्मुश्शिशुपालप्रियैषिणः

When the marriage was fixed, all of them, Jaraasandha and others who were all dear to Shishupaala, went to Bheeshmaka's city, for the marriage.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
vivāhārtham tataḥ sarve — then all, for the marriage;
jarāsandha-mukhāḥ nṛpāḥ — the kings headed by Jarāsandha;
bhīṣmakasya puraṃ jagmuḥ — went to Bhīṣmaka’s city;
śiśupāla-priyaiṣiṇaḥ — seeking Śiśupāla’s welfare
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vivāhārthaṃ tataḥ sarve jarāsandha-mukhā nṛpāḥ | bhīṣmakasya puraṃ jagmuḥ śiśupāla-priyaiṣiṇaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.5

कृष्णोऽपि बलभद्राद्यैर्यदुभिः परिवारितः।
प्रययौ कुण्डिनं द्रष्टुं विवाहं चैद्यभूभृतः

Krishna, along with Balaraama and other Yaadavaas, also went to Kundinapura in order to witness the marriage of Shishupaala.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
kṛṣṇaḥ api balabhadrādyaiḥ — Kṛṣṇa too, with Balarāma and others;
yadubhiḥ parivāritaḥ — surrounded by Yādavas;
prayayau kuṇḍinam — went to Kuṇḍina;
draṣṭuṃ vivāham caidya-bhūbhṛtaḥ — to see the marriage of the Cedi king
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṛṣṇo ’pi balabhadrādyair yadubhiḥ parivāritaḥ | prayayau kuṇḍinaṃ draṣṭuṃ vivāhaṃ caidya-bhūbhṛtaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.6

श्वोभाविनि विवाहे तु तां कन्यां हृतवान्हरिः।
विपक्षभारमासज्य रामादिष्वथ बन्धुषु

On the previous day of the marriage, Krishna escaped with Rukmini, snatched and took her away. When there were so many people who were against Him, in order to take care of all of them, in case there is a fight which may ensue, He left Balaraama and other relatives there.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
śvo-bhāvini vivāhe — on the marriage occurring tomorrow;
tām kanyām hṛtavān hariḥ — Hari carried away that maiden;
vipakṣa-bhāram āsajya — placing the burden of opponents;
rāmādiṣu atha bandhuṣu — on Balarāma and the other relatives
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śvo-bhāvini vivāhe tu tāṃ kanyāṃ hṛtavān hariḥ | vipakṣa-bhāram āsajya rāmādiṣv atha bandhuṣu

Śrīdhara

śva iti . purād bahir ambikā-pūjārthaṃ nirgatāṃ tāṃ hṛtavān hariḥ vipakṣa-bhāraṃ vipakṣaiḥ saha kāryaṃ yuddhādi-saṃrambhaṃ rāmādiṣv āsajya samāropya ||६|| tataś ca pauṇḍrakaḥ śrīmān dantavakro vidūrathaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

श्व इति । पुराद् बहिर् अम्बिका-पूजार्थं निर्गतां तां हृतवान् हरिः विपक्ष-भारं विपक्षैः सह कार्यं युद्धादि-संरम्भं रामादिष्व् आसज्य समारोप्य ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

On the previous day of the marriage, Krishna escaped with Rukmini, snatched and took her away. When there were so many people who were against Him, in order to take care of all of them, in case there is a fight which may ensue, He left Balaraama and other relatives there.

VP 5.26.7

ततश्च पौण्ड्रकश्श्रीमान्दन्तवक्त्रो विदूरथः।
शिशुपालजरासन्धशाल्वाद्याश्च महीभृतः

Then Poundraka, Dantavaktra, Vidooratha, Shishupaala, Jaraasandha, Saalva - all of these kings, all ganged up.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tataḥ ca pauṇḍrakaḥ śrīmān — then the illustrious Pauṇḍraka;
dantavaktraḥ vidūrathaḥ — Dantavaktra and Vidūratha;
śiśupāla-jarāsandha-śālva-ādyāḥ — Śiśupāla, Jarāsandha, Śālva and others;
ca mahībhṛtaḥ — and the kings
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataś ca pauṇḍrakaḥ śrīmān dantavaktraḥ vidūrathaḥ | śiśupāla-jarāsandha-śālvādyāś ca mahībhṛtaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.8

कुपितास्ते हरिं हन्तुं चक्रुरुद्योगमुत्तमम्।
निर्जिताश्च समागम्य रामाद्यैर्यदुपुङ्गवैः

They got very angry and wanted to fight with Balaraama and others. They were then defeated by Balaraama and the other Yaadavaas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
kupitāḥ te hariṃ hantuṃ — enraged, they, to kill Hari;
cakruḥ udyogam uttamam — made great preparation;
nirjitāḥ ca — and were defeated;
samāgamya rāmādyaiḥ yadu-puṅgavaiḥ — by meeting Balarāma and the foremost Yādavas
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kupitās te hariṃ hantuṃ cakrur udyogam uttamam | nirjitāś ca samāgamya rāmādyair yadu-puṅgavaiḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.9

कुण्डिनं न प्रवेक्ष्यामि ह्यहत्वा युधि केशवम्।
कृत्वा प्रतिज्ञां रुक्मी च हन्तुं कृष्णमनुद्रुतः

Rukmi took a vow that he will not enter Kundinapura without killing Krishna. And he went after Krishna.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
kuṇḍinaṃ na pravekṣyāmi — I will not enter Kuṇḍina;
hi ahatvā yudhi keśavam — without killing Keśava in battle;
kṛtvā pratijñām rukmī — having made a vow, Rukmi;
hantuṃ kṛṣṇam anudrutaḥ — pursued Kṛṣṇa to kill him
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kuṇḍinaṃ na pravekṣyāmi hy ahatvā yudhi keśavam | kṛtvā pratijñāṃ rukmī ca hantuṃ kṛṣṇam anudrutaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.10

हत्वा बलं सनागाश्वं पत्तिस्यन्दनसङ्कुलम्।
निर्जितः पातितश्चोर्व्यां लीलयैव स चक्रिणा

When Rukmi went along with his army, Krishna very easily, along with His army, destroyed the army of Rukmi, along with the elephants, chariots, soldiers, horses. They were all defeated very easily, and Rukmi was made to fall on the ground.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
hatvā balam sa-nāgāśvam — having destroyed the army with elephants and horses;
patti-syandana-saṅkulam — crowded with foot-soldiers and chariots;
nirjitaḥ pātitaḥ ca urvyām — defeated and cast down on the earth;
līlayā eva sa cakriṇā — by the discus-bearer merely in play
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

hatvā balaṃ sa-nāgāśvaṃ patti-syandana-saṅkulam | nirjitaḥ pātitaś corvyāṃ līlayaiva sa cakriṇā

Śrīdhara

hatveti . pātita eva kevalaṃ, na tu hataḥ, rukmiṇī-bhrātṛtvāt ||१०|| nirjitya rukmiṇaṃ samyag upayeme ca rukmiṇīm .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

हत्वेति । पातित एव केवलं, न तु हतः, रुक्मिणी-भ्रातृत्वात् ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

When Rukmi went along with his army, Krishna very easily, along with His army, destroyed the army of Rukmi, along with the elephants, chariots, soldiers, horses. They were all defeated very easily, and Rukmi was made to fall on the ground.

VP 5.26.11

निर्जित्य रुक्मिणं सम्यगुपयेमे च रुक्मिणीम्।
राक्षसेन विवाहेन सम्प्राप्तां मधुसूदनः

Having won over Rukmi, He married Rukmini as per the Vedic rites, having brought her through the Raakshasa vivaaha, having simply stolen her and escaped with her.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
nirjitya rukmiṇam samyak — having thoroughly defeated Rukmi;
upayeme ca rukmiṇīm — he married Rukmini;
rākṣasena vivāhena — by a Rākṣasa marriage;
saṃprāptām madhusūdanaḥ — Madhusūdana, who had obtained her
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nirjitya rukmiṇaṃ samyag upayeme ca rukmiṇīm | rākṣasena vivāhena saṃprāptāṃ madhusūdanaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.26.12

तस्यां जज्ञे च प्रद्युम्नो मदनांशः सवीर्यवान्।
जहार शम्बरो यं वै यो जघान च शम्बरम्

Rukmini got a son by name Pradyumna, who was the saakshaat amsha of Kaamadeva, Madana. He was very powerful. Shambaraasura immediately snatched and stole away the child. Later on, Paraasharar says briefly that Pradyumna killed Shambaraasura. This completes Chapter Twenty Six. Now, Chapter Twenty Seven.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tasyām jajñe ca pradyumnaḥ — Pradyumna was born from her;
madanāṃśaḥ sa-vīryavān — a portion of Madana, possessing great valor;
jahāra śambaraḥ yam vai — Śambara indeed carried him away;
yaḥ jaghāna ca śambaram — who later slew Śambara
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasyāṃ jajñe ca pradyumno madanāṃśaḥ sa-vīryavān | jahāra śambaro yaṃ vai yo jaghāna ca śambaram

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.
