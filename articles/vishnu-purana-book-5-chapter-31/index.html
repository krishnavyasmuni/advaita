VP 5.31.1

संस्तुतो भगवानित्थं देवराजेन केशवः ।
प्रहस्य भावगंभीरमुवाचेन्द्रं द्विजोत्तम

Thus praised by Indra, the king of the gods, Keśava laughed and spoke to him with profound feeling, O best of the twice-born.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saṃstuto bhagavān itthaṃ deva-rājena keśavaḥ . prahasya bhāva-gambhīram uvāca indraṃ dvijottama

Word-for-word

saṃstutaḥ — praised; bhagavān — the Blessed Lord; ittham — thus; deva-rājena — by the king of gods; keśavaḥ — Keśava; prahasya — laughing; bhāva-gambhīram — with profound feeling; uvāca — spoke; indram — to Indra; dvija-uttama — O best of the twice-born.

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.31.3

पारिजाततरुश्चायं नीयतामुचितास्पदम् ।
गृहीतोऽयं मया शक्र सत्यावचनकारणात्

“Let this Pārijāta tree be taken to its proper place. I took it, O Śakra, because of my promise to Satyā.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pārijāta-taruḥ ca ayam nīyatām ucitāspadam . gṛhītaḥ ayam mayā śakra satyā-vacana-kāraṇāt

Śrīdhara

pārijāteti . ucitāspadaṃ yathocitaṃ sthānam ||३|| vajraṃ cedaṃ gṛhāṇa tvaṃyad atraprahitaṃ tvayā .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पारिजातेति । उचितास्पदं यथोचितं स्थानम् ॥३॥

Word-for-word

pārijāta-taruḥ — Pārijāta tree; ca — and; ayam — this; nīyatām — let be taken; ucita-āspadam — to the proper place; gṛhītaḥ — taken; ayam — this; mayā — by me; śakra — O Śakra; satya-vacana-kāraṇāt — because of the promise to Satyā.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Please do take this Paarijaata tree back, to its original place. I took it away listening to the words of Satyabhaama, just to please her.

VP 5.31.5

विमोहयसि मामीश मर्त्योऽहमिति किं वदन् ।
जानीमस्त्वां भगवतो न तु सूक्ष्मविदो वयम्

Indra said: “Why do you bewilder me, Lord, saying, ‘I am a mortal’? We know you to be the Blessed Lord, though we do not know your subtle form.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vimohayasi mām īśa martyaḥ aham iti kiṃ vadan . jānīmas tvāṃ bhagavataḥ na tu sūkṣma-vido vayam

Śrīdhara

* na vyākhyātam.* indra uvāca— vimohayasi mām īśa martyo’ham iti kiṃ vadan . jānīmas tvāṃ bhagavatona tu sūkṣma-vidovayam ||५|| viṣṇu-cittaḥ — vimohayasīti . naiva sūkṣma-vidaḥ sūkṣma-rūpaṃ na vidmaḥ ||५|| śrīdharaḥ — vimohayasīti . yathā dṛṣṭa-mātra-rūpaṃ tvāṃ jānīmaḥ, na tu tava sūkṣma-rūpa-vidaḥ ||५|| yo’si so’si jagat-trāṇa-६४५pravṛttau nāthasaṃsthitaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

* न व्याख्यातम्.*

इन्द्र उवाच—


जानीमस् त्वां भगवतोन तु सूक्ष्म-विदो६४४वयम् ॥५॥

विष्णु-चित्तः — विमोहयसीति । नैव सूक्ष्म-विदः सूक्ष्म-रूपं न विद्मः ॥५॥

श्रीधरः — विमोहयसीति । यथा दृष्ट-मात्र-रूपं त्वां जानीमः, न तु तव सूक्ष्म-रूप-विदः ॥५॥

Word-for-word

vimohayasi — you bewilder; mām — me; īśa — O Lord; martyaḥ — mortal; aham — I; iti — thus; kim — why; vadan — saying; jānīmaḥ — we know; tvām — you; bhagavataḥ — the Blessed Lord; na tu — but not; sūkṣma-vidaḥ — knowers of the subtle; vayam — we.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Indra - O Bhagavaan Krishna, You are the Lord. You are causing moha to me, and telling that You are a mere mortal. We know that You are the Supreme Lord. We do not know Your subtle nature. We do know Your real nature, but we know that You are the Supreme Lord. We do not know the subtle form of Yours.

VP 5.31.6

योऽसि सोऽसि जगत्त्राणप्रवृत्तौ नाथ संस्थितः ।
जगतः शल्यनिष्कर्षं करोष्यमुरसूदन

“Whatever you are, you are established, O Lord, for the protection of the universe. You draw out the world’s thorn, O slayer of Mura.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yaḥ asi saḥ asi jagat-trāṇa-pravṛttau nātha saṃsthitaḥ . jagataḥ śalya-niṣkarṣaṃ karoṣy amura-sūdana

Śrīdhara

yo’sīti . śalya-niṣkarṣaṃ kaṇṭakoddhāram ||६|| nīyatāṃ pārijāto’yaṃ kṛṣṇa dvāravatīṃ purīm .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

योऽसीति । शल्य-निष्कर्षं कण्टकोद्धारम् ॥६॥

Word-for-word

yaḥ asi — whatever you are; saḥ asi — that you are; jagat-trāṇa-pravṛttau — in the work of protecting the universe; nātha — O Lord; saṃsthitaḥ — established; jagataḥ — of the world; śalya-niṣkarṣam — drawing out the thorn; karoṣi — you do; amura-sūdana — O slayer of Mura.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Whoever You are, You have incarnated in the same form, in order to protect all the worlds, and in order to destroy the evil in the worlds, You are doing all these divine acts, and are incarnating.

VP 5.31.7

नीयतां पारिजातोऽयं कृष्ण द्वारवतीं पुरीम् ।
मर्त्यलोके त्वया त्यक्ते नायं संस्थास्यते भुवि

“Let this Pārijāta be taken, O Kṛṣṇa, to the city of Dvāravatī. When you leave the mortal world, it will not remain here on earth.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nīyatāṃ pārijāto ayam kṛṣṇa dvāravatīṃ purīm . martya-loke tvayā tyakte na ayam saṃsthāsyate bhuvi

Śrīdhara

nīyatām iti . na ca mamāpy atyanta-kopāya pārijāta-viyoga ity āha—martya-loka iti ||७|| deva deva jagannātha kṛṣṇa viṣṇo mahābhuja .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नीयताम् इति । न च ममाप्य् अत्यन्त-कोपाय पारिजात-वियोग इत्य् आह—मर्त्य-लोक इति ॥७॥

Word-for-word

nīyatām — let be taken; pārijātaḥ — Pārijāta; ayam — this; kṛṣṇa — O Kṛṣṇa; dvāravatīm purīm — to the city of Dvāravatī; martya-loke — in the mortal world; tvayā — by you; tyakte — when left; na — not; ayam — this; saṃsthāsyate — will remain; bhuvi — on earth.

Śrīdhara commentary.

O Krishna, please do take this Paarijaata, to Dwaaravati and put it in Your garden. Once You end Your avataara, after that, this Paarijaata tree will not stay on earth, it will come back to Devaloka.

VP 5.31.9

तथेत्युक्त्वा च देवेन्द्रमाजगाम भुवं हरिः ।
प्रसक्तैः सिद्धगन्धर्वैः स्तूयमानः सुरर्षिभिः

Having said “So be it” to Indra, Hari came to earth, praised by divine sages, Siddhas, and Gandharvas who followed him.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tathā ity uktvā ca deva-indram ājagāma bhuvaṃ hariḥ . prasaktaiḥ siddha-gandharvaiḥ stūyamānaḥ surarṣibhiḥ

Śrīdhara

tathetīti . prasaktair anuṣaktaiḥ anugacchadbhiḥ siddhādibhiḥ stūyamānaḥ ||९|| tataḥ śaṅkham upādhmāya dvārakopari saṃsthitaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तथेतीति । प्रसक्तैर् अनुषक्तैः अनुगच्छद्भिः सिद्धादिभिः स्तूयमानः ॥९॥

Word-for-word

tathā iti — “so be it”; uktvā — having said; ca — and; deva-indram — to Indra; ājagāma — came; bhuvam — to earth; hariḥ — Hari; prasaktaiḥ — accompanied / following; siddha-gandharvaiḥ — by Siddhas and Gandharvas; stūyamānaḥ — being praised; sura-ṛṣibhiḥ — by divine sages.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Sri Paraasharar - After that, Krishna said that Let it be so. And He came back to His place in Dwaaravati, with all the things that He carried from there. The siddhaas, gandharvaas, the gods, rishis were all eulogizing Him.

VP 5.31.12

यमभ्येत्य जनः सर्वो जातिं स्मरति पौर्विकीम् ।
वास्यते यस्य पुष्पोत्थगन्धेनोर्वी त्रियोजनम्

Anyone who approaches it remembers his former birth, and the earth is perfumed for three yojanas by the fragrance arising from its flowers.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yam abhyetya janaḥ sarvaḥ jātiṃ smarati paurvikīm . vāsyate yasya puṣpa-uttha-gandhena urvī tri-yojanam

Śrīdhara

yam iti . paurvikīm pūrva-janma-sambandhinīm ||१२|| tatas te yādavāḥ sarve deha-bandhān amānuṣān .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यम् इति । पौर्विकीम् पूर्व-जन्म-सम्बन्धिनीम् ॥१२॥

Word-for-word

yam — which; abhyetya — approaching; janaḥ — a person; sarvaḥ — everyone; jātim — birth; smarati — remembers; paurvikīm — former; vāsyate — is perfumed; yasya — by whose; puṣpa-uttha-gandhena — fragrance arising from flowers; urvī — the earth; tri-yojanam — three yojanas.

Śrīdhara commentary.

Those who come near the tree, remember all their previous births, this is the power of the Paarijaata tree. The fragrance of the flowers of Paarijaata, spreads across three yojanaas.

VP 5.31.13

ततस्ते यादवाःसर्वे देहबन्धानमानुषान् ।
ददृशुः पादपे तस्मिन् कुर्वन्तो मखदर्शनम्

tata iti . amānuṣān sva-dehāt devākārān dadṛśuḥ, tatra jāti-smaratvāt ||१३|| kiṅkaraiḥ samupānītaṃ hasty-aśvādi tato dhanam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत इति । अमानुषान् स्व-देहात् देवाकारान् ददृशुः, तत्र जाति-स्मरत्वात् ॥१३॥

Word-for-word

tataḥ — then; te — those; yādavāḥ — Yādavas; sarve — all; deha-bandhān — bodily bonds / treasures; amānuṣān — nonhuman; dadṛśuḥ — saw; pādape — at the tree; tasmin — that; kurvantaḥ — performing; mukha-darśanam — welcoming sight.

Śrīdhara commentary.

All the Yaadavaas came there, near the tree, and saw all their previous births. They became aware of all their births, when they saw the Paarijaata tree there.

VP 5.31.19

एकैकमेव ताः कन्या मेनिरे मधुसूदनः ।
ममैव पाणिग्रहणं मैत्रेय कृतवानिति

Each maiden thought, “Madhusūdana has taken my hand alone in marriage, O Maitreya.”

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ekaikam eva tāḥ kanyāḥ menire madhusūdanaḥ . mama eva pāṇi-grahaṇaṃ maitreya kṛtavān iti

Word-for-word

eka-ekam eva — each one alone; tāḥ — those; kanyāḥ — maidens; menire — thought; madhusūdanaḥ — Madhusūdana; mama eva — my alone; pāṇi-grahaṇam — hand-taking in marriage; maitreya — O Maitreya; kṛtavān — made; iti — thus.

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.31.20

निशासु च जगत्स्रष्टा तासां गेहेषु केशवः ।
उवास विप्र सर्वासां विश्वरूपधरो हरिः

At night the Creator of the world, Keśava, dwelt in all their houses, Hari bearing a universal form.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

niśāsu ca jagat-sraṣṭā tāsāṃ geheṣu keśavaḥ . uvāsa vipra sarvāsāṃ viśva-rūpa-dharo hariḥ

niśāsu — at night; ca — and; jagat-sraṣṭā — Creator of the world; tāsām — of them; geheṣu — in the houses; keśavaḥ — Keśava; uvāsa — dwelt; vipra — O brāhmaṇa; sarvāsām — in all; viśva-rūpa-dharaḥ — bearing universal form; hariḥ — Hari.

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.
