VP 5.31.1

संस्तुतो भगवानित्थं देवराजेन केशवः । प्रहस्य भावगंभीरमुवाचेन्द्रं द्विजोत्तम

Sri Paraasharar - Being eulogized by Devendra like this, Bhagavaan Krishna gave a smile, a knowing smile, that He is revealing something secretly to Devendra, that He is the Supreme Lord. Having made that known to Indra, He told thus, putting Indra into delusion -

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saṃstuto bhagavānitthaṃ devarājena keśavaḥ . prahasya bhāvagambhīramuvācendraṃ dvijottama — Sri Paraasharar - Being eulogized by Devendra like this, Bhagavaan Krishna gave a smile, a knowing smile, that He is revealing something secretly to Devendra, that He is the Supreme Lord. Having made that known to Indra, He told thus, putting Indra into delusion -

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

saṃstuto bhagavānitthaṃ devarājena keśavaḥ . prahasya bhāvagaṃbhīramuvācendraṃ dvijottama

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.31.2

देवराजो भवानिन्द्रो वयं मर्त्या जगत्पते । क्षन्तव्यं भवतैवेदमपराधं कृतं मम

Sri Krishna - O Devaraaja, you are the lord Indra, and we are all humans, mere mortals. This is My aparaadha, please do pardon Me. You only have to pardon Me.

VP 5.31.3

पारिजाततरुश्चायं नीयतामुचितास्पदम् । गृहीतोऽयं मया शक्र सत्यावचनकारणात्

Please do take this Paarijaata tree back, to its original place. I took it away listening to the words of Satyabhaama, just to please her.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pārijātataruścāyaṃ nīyatāmucitāspadam . gṛhīto’yaṃ mayā śakra satyāvacanakāraṇāt — Please do take this Paarijaata tree back, to its original place. I took it away listening to the words of Satyabhaama, just to please her.

Śrīdhara

pārijāteti . ucitāspadaṃ yathocitaṃ sthānam — Please do take this Paarijaata tree back, to its original place. I took it away listening to the words of Satyabhaama, just to please her.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pārijātataruścāyaṃ nīyatāmucitāspadam . gṛhīto’yaṃ mayā śakra satyāvacanakāraṇāt

Śrīdhara

pārijāteti . ucitāspadaṃ yathocitaṃ sthānam ||३|| vajraṃ cedaṃ gṛhāṇa tvaṃyad atra६४३prahitaṃ tvayā .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पारिजातेति । उचितास्पदं यथोचितं स्थानम् ॥३॥ वज्रं चेदं गृहाण त्वंयद् अत्र६४३प्रहितं त्वया ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Please do take this Paarijaata tree back, to its original place. I took it away listening to the words of Satyabhaama, just to please her.

VP 5.31.5

विमोहयसि मामीश मर्त्योऽहमिति किं वदन् । जानीमस्त्वां भगवतो न तु सूक्ष्मविदो वयम्

Indra - O Bhagavaan Krishna, You are the Lord. You are causing moha to me, and telling that You are a mere mortal. We know that You are the Supreme Lord. We do not know Your subtle nature. We do know Your real nature, but we know that You are the Supreme Lord. We do not know the subtle form of Yours.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vimohayasi māmīśa martyo’hamiti kiṃ vadan . jānīmastvāṃ bhagavato na tu sūkṣmavido vayam — Indra - O Bhagavaan Krishna, You are the Lord. You are causing moha to me, and telling that You are a mere mortal. We know that You are the Supreme Lord. We do not know Your subtle nature. We do know Your real nature, but we know that You are the Supreme Lord. We do not know the subtle form of Yours.

Śrīdhara

* na vyākhyātam.* indra uvāca— vimohayasi mām īśa martyo’ham iti kiṃ vadan . jānīmas tvāṃ bhagavatona tu sūkṣma-vido644vayam — Indra - O Bhagavaan Krishna, You are the Lord. You are causing moha to me, and telling that You are a mere mortal. We know that You are the Supreme Lord. We do not know Your subtle nature. We do know Your real nature, but we know that You are the Supreme Lord. We do not know the subtle form of Yours.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vimohayasi māmīśa martyo’hamiti kiṃ vadan . jānīmastvāṃ bhagavato na tu sūkṣmavido vayam

Śrīdhara

* na vyākhyātam.* indra uvāca— vimohayasi mām īśa martyo’ham iti kiṃ vadan . jānīmas tvāṃ bhagavatona tu sūkṣma-vido६४४vayam ||५|| viṣṇu-cittaḥ — vimohayasīti . naiva sūkṣma-vidaḥ sūkṣma-rūpaṃ na vidmaḥ ||५|| śrīdharaḥ — vimohayasīti . yathā dṛṣṭa-mātra-rūpaṃ tvāṃ jānīmaḥ, na tu tava sūkṣma-rūpa-vidaḥ ||५|| yo’si so’si jagat-trāṇa-६४५pravṛttau nāthasaṃsthitaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

* न व्याख्यातम्.* इन्द्र उवाच— विमोहयसि माम् ईश मर्त्योऽहम् इति किं वदन् । जानीमस् त्वां भगवतोन तु सूक्ष्म-विदो६४४वयम् ॥५॥ विष्णु-चित्तः — विमोहयसीति । नैव सूक्ष्म-विदः सूक्ष्म-रूपं न विद्मः ॥५॥ श्रीधरः — विमोहयसीति । यथा दृष्ट-मात्र-रूपं त्वां जानीमः, न तु तव सूक्ष्म-रूप-विदः ॥५॥ योऽसि सोऽसि जगत्-त्राण-६४५प्रवृत्तौ नाथसंस्थितः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Indra - O Bhagavaan Krishna, You are the Lord. You are causing moha to me, and telling that You are a mere mortal. We know that You are the Supreme Lord. We do not know Your subtle nature. We do know Your real nature, but we know that You are the Supreme Lord. We do not know the subtle form of Yours.

VP 5.31.6

योऽसि सोऽसि जगत्त्रणप्रवृत्तौ नाथ संस्थितः । जगतः शल्यनिष्कर्षं करोष्यमुरसूदन

Whoever You are, You have incarnated in the same form, in order to protect all the worlds, and in order to destroy the evil in the worlds, You are doing all these divine acts, and are incarnating.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yo’si so’si jagattrāṇapravṛtto nātha saṃsthitaḥ . jagataśśalyaniṣkarṣaṃ karoṣyasurasūdana — Whoever You are, You have incarnated in the same form, in order to protect all the worlds, and in order to destroy the evil in the worlds, You are doing all these divine acts, and are incarnating.

Śrīdhara

yo’sīti . śalya-niṣkarṣaṃ kaṇṭakoddhāram — Whoever You are, You have incarnated in the same form, in order to protect all the worlds, and in order to destroy the evil in the worlds, You are doing all these divine acts, and are incarnating.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yo’si so’si jagattraṇapravṛttau nātha saṃsthitaḥ . jagataḥ śalyaniṣkarṣaṃ karoṣyamurasūdana

Śrīdhara

yo’sīti . śalya-niṣkarṣaṃ kaṇṭakoddhāram ||६|| nīyatāṃ pārijāto’yaṃ kṛṣṇa dvāravatīṃ purīm .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

योऽसीति । शल्य-निष्कर्षं कण्टकोद्धारम् ॥६॥ नीयतां पारिजातोऽयं कृष्ण द्वारवतीं पुरीम् ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Whoever You are, You have incarnated in the same form, in order to protect all the worlds, and in order to destroy the evil in the worlds, You are doing all these divine acts, and are incarnating.

VP 5.31.7

नीयतां पारीजातोऽयं कृष्ण द्वारवतींपुरीम् । मर्त्यलोके त्वया त्यक्ते नायं संस्थास्यते भुवि

O Krishna, please do take this Paarijaata, to Dwaaravati and put it in Your garden. Once You end Your avataara, after that, this Paarijaata tree will not stay on earth, it will come back to Devaloka.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nīyatāṃ pārijāto’yaṃ kṛṣṇa dvāravatīṃ purīm . martyaloke tvayā tyakte nāyaṃ saṃsthāsyate bhuvi — O Krishna, please do take this Paarijaata, to Dwaaravati and put it in Your garden. Once You end Your avataara, after that, this Paarijaata tree will not stay on earth, it will come back to Devaloka.

Śrīdhara

nīyatām iti . na ca mamāpy atyanta-kopāya pārijāta-viyoga ity āha—martya-loka iti — O Krishna, please do take this Paarijaata, to Dwaaravati and put it in Your garden. Once You end Your avataara, after that, this Paarijaata tree will not stay on earth, it will come back to Devaloka.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nīyatāṃ pārījāto’yaṃ kṛṣṇa dvāravatīṃpurīm . martyaloke tvayā tyakte nāyaṃ saṃsthāsyate bhuvi

Śrīdhara

nīyatām iti . na ca mamāpy atyanta-kopāya pārijāta-viyoga ity āha—martya-loka iti ||७|| deva deva jagannātha kṛṣṇa viṣṇo mahābhuja .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नीयताम् इति । न च ममाप्य् अत्यन्त-कोपाय पारिजात-वियोग इत्य् आह—मर्त्य-लोक इति ॥७॥ देव देव जगन्नाथ कृष्ण विष्णो महाभुज ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

O Krishna, please do take this Paarijaata, to Dwaaravati and put it in Your garden. Once You end Your avataara, after that, this Paarijaata tree will not stay on earth, it will come back to Devaloka.

VP 5.31.9

तथेत्युक्त्वा च देवेन्द्रमाजगाम भुवं हरिः । प्रसक्तैः सिद्धगन्धर्वैः स्तूयमानः सुरर्षिभिः

Transliteration

VP 5.31.12

यमभ्येत्य जनः सर्वो जातिं स्मरति पौर्विकीम् । वास्यते यस्य पुष्पोत्थगन्धेनोर्वी त्रियोजनम्

Śrīdhara

Transliteration

Śrīdhara commentary.
