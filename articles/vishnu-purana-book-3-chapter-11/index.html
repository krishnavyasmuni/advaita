VP 3.11.1

गृहस्थस्य सदाचारं श्रोतुमिच्छाम्यहं मुने। लोकादस्मात्परस्माच्च यमातिष्ठन्न हीयते
॥१॥

King Sagara - What are the sadaachaaraas that a gruhasta has to follow, I would like to listen from you. By which he will not get into any difficulty in this loka or para loka. By which he will always have a sadgati, that kind of aachaara, I would like to listen to.

Word-for-word

By which he will not get into any difficulty in this loka or para loka. By which he will always have a sadgati, that kind of aachaara, I would like to listen to.

Transliteration

मृतो नरकमभ्येति हीयतेऽत्रापि चायुषः । परदाररतिः पुंसामिह चामुत्र भीतिदा
॥१२६॥

Going after others wife is said to be a very bad thing. One should not be thinking even in the mind. It is very sinful.
