VP 3.11.1

गृहस्थस्य सदाचारं श्रोतुमिच्छाम्यहं मुने। लोकादस्मात्परस्माच्च यमातिष्ठन्न हीयते

VP 3.11.5

ब्राह्मे मुहूर्ते चोत्थाय मनसा मतिमान्नृप । प्रबुद्धश्चिन्तयेद्धर्ममर्थं चाप्यविरोधिनम्॥५॥

One should wake up in Braahma muhoorta, and should think at that time, about dharma and artha which are not opposed to each other.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brājhe muhūrte cotthāya manasā matimānnṛpa — One should wake up in Braahma muhoorta; prabuddhaścintayeddharmamarthaṃ cāpyavirodhinam — and should think at that time

Śrīdhara

garbhādhānādinā puṃsaḥ saṃskṛtasya kṛtiṃ bruvan । ekādaśe sadācārān prāha śrīharitoṣaṇān । brāhme muhūrte sūryodayāt pūrvaṃ tṛtīye muhūrte । arthañcāsya dharmasyāvirodhinam — Śrīdhara says that after discussing the rites beginning with garbhādhāna, the text now presents conduct that pleases Hari. Brahma-muhūrta is the third muhūrta before sunrise; at that time one should contemplate wealth only when it does not conflict with dharma.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

brāhme muhūrte cotthāya manasā matimānnṛpa | prabuddhaścintayeddharmamarthaṃ cāpyavirodhinam ||5||

Śrīdhara

garbhādhānādinā puṃsaḥ saṃskṛtasya kṛtiṃ bruvan । ekādaśe sadācārān prāha śrīharitoṣaṇān । brāhme muhūrte sūryodayāt pūrvaṃ tṛtīye muhūrte । arthañcāsya dharmasyāvirodhinam

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

गर्भाधानादिना पुंसः संस्कृतस्य कृतिं ब्रुवन् । एकादशे सदाचारान् प्राह श्रीहरितोषणान् । ब्राह्मे मुहूर्ते सूर्योदयात् पूर्वं तृतीये मुहूर्ते । अर्थञ्चास्य धर्मस्याविरोधिनम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 5).

Śrīdhara says that after discussing the rites beginning with garbhādhāna, the text now presents conduct that pleases Hari. Brahma-muhūrta is the third muhūrta before sunrise; at that time one should contemplate wealth only when it does not conflict with dharma.

VP 3.11.6

अपीडया तयोः काममुभयोरपि चिन्तयेत् । दृष्टादृष्टाविनाशाय त्रिवर्गे समदर्शनः
॥६॥

And kaama which is also a purushaartha, which is not opposed to dharma and artha. He should only be thinking about such kaama. All the drushta and adrushta phalaas, those which he can directly see the effects after doing some work, or something which he does not get here, but gets at a later point in time, in order to gain all these fruits, He should dharma, artha, kaama. Artha and kaama should be in line with dharma. He should be equally disposed towards the trivarga. He should not be overdoing some of them.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

apīḍayā tayoḥ kāmamubhayorapi cintayet — And kaama which is also a purushaartha, which is not opposed to dharma and artha.; dṛṣṭādṛṣṭāvināśāya trivarge samadarśanaḥ — He should only be thinking about such kaama.

Śrīdhara

tayordharmārthayodṛṣṭādṛṣṭavināśāya dṛṣṭādṛṣṭavināśanivṛttaye, tādarthye caturthītyatrārthaśabdasya nivṛtterapi vācakatvāt । yadvā vināśamapanetumityarthaḥ । kriyārthopapadasya ca karmaṇi sthānina iti caturthī । yathā raghau vanaṃ gantumityarthe vanāya pītapratibaddhavatsām iti । dṛṣṭādṛṣṭavināśāyeti pāṭhaḥ sugamaḥ । trivarge samadarśitā parasparāvirodhena bhavatviti samadṛṣṭiḥ — He should pursue desire only when it does not injure either dharma or artha, and should view the three aims—dharma, artha, and kāma—with equal regard so as to avoid visible and invisible harm.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

apīḍayā tayoḥ kāmamubhayorapi cintayet | dṛṣṭādṛṣṭāvināśāya trivarge samadarśanaḥ ||6||

Śrīdhara

tayordharmārthayodṛṣṭādṛṣṭavināśāya dṛṣṭādṛṣṭavināśanivṛttaye, tādarthye caturthītyatrārthaśabdasya nivṛtterapi vācakatvāt । yadvā vināśamapanetumityarthaḥ । kriyārthopapadasya ca karmaṇi sthānina iti caturthī । yathā raghau vanaṃ gantumityarthe vanāya pītapratibaddhavatsām iti । dṛṣṭādṛṣṭavināśāyeti pāṭhaḥ sugamaḥ । trivarge samadarśitā parasparāvirodhena bhavatviti samadṛṣṭiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तयोर्धर्मार्थयोदृष्टादृष्टविनाशाय दृष्टादृष्टविनाशनिवृत्तये, तादर्थ्ये चतुर्थीत्यत्रार्थशब्दस्य निवृत्तेरपि वाचकत्वात् । यद्वा विनाशमपनेतुमित्यर्थः । क्रियार्थोपपदस्य च कर्मणि स्थानिन इति चतुर्थी । यथा रघौ वनं गन्तुमित्यर्थे वनाय पीतप्रतिबद्धवत्साम् इति । दृष्टादृष्टविनाशायेति पाठः सुगमः । त्रिवर्गे समदर्शिता परस्पराविरोधेन भवत्विति समदृष्टिः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 6).

Śrīdhara explains that the concern is the non-destruction of dharma and artha by desire. Equal regard among the three aims means that they should be pursued without mutual contradiction.

VP 3.11.7

परित्यजेदर्थकामौ धर्मपीडाकरौ नृप । धर्ममप्यसुखोदर्कं लोकविद्विष्टमेव च
॥७॥

Artha and kaama if they are coming in the way of following dharma, One should completely reject them. Even, dharma, if it is leading to a lot of dukha, or if it is something not accepted in the world, one should not do them.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

parityajedarthakāmau dharmapīḍākarau nṛpa — Artha and kaama if they are coming in the way of following dharma, One should completely reject them.; dharmamapyasukhodarkaṃ lokavidviṣṭameva ca — Even, dharma, if it is leading to a lot of dukha, or if it is something not accepted in the world, one should not do them.

Śrīdhara

dharmamapi iti—vyāghracaurādisamākrāntatīrthagamanādi asukhodarkaṃ duḥkhottaraphalakaṃ, lokavidviṣṭaṃ sautrāmaṇyādau surāgrahādi — A person should abandon wealth and desire when they cause harm to dharma, and even abandon a dharma whose result is suffering or which is rejected by good social practice.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

parityajedarthakāmau dharmapīḍākarau nṛpa | dharmamapyasukhodarkaṃ lokavidviṣṭameva ca ||7||

Śrīdhara

dharmamapi iti—vyāghracaurādisamākrāntatīrthagamanādi asukhodarkaṃ duḥkhottaraphalakaṃ, lokavidviṣṭaṃ sautrāmaṇyādau surāgrahādi

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

धर्ममपि इति—व्याघ्रचौरादिसमाक्रान्ततीर्थगमनादि असुखोदर्कं दुःखोत्तरफलकं, लोकविद्विष्टं सौत्रामण्यादौ सुराग्रहादि

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 7).

Śrīdhara gives examples of dharma that leads to suffering, such as travelling to a place overrun by tigers or thieves, and of conduct rejected by society, such as drinking liquor in the Sautrāmaṇī rite.

VP 3.11.14

उदङ्मुखो दिवा मूत्रं विपरीतमुखो निशि । कुर्वीतानापदि प्राज्ञो मूत्रोत्सर्गं च पार्थिव
॥१४॥

Śrīdhara explains that the verse prohibits even mentally approaching intercourse on the stated festival days, at twilight, or while troubled by the bodily urges of urination or defecation; the surrounding reading also condemns intercourse with another’s wife.
