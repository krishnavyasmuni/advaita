VP 3.13.1

सचेलस्य पितुः स्नानं जाते पुत्रे विधीयते । जातकर्म तथा कुर्याच्छ्राद्धमभ्युदयं च यत्

Ourva - When a person gets a child, (when a child is born), then he should take a bath with the cloth on. He has to do the jaata karma as ordained. And also has to do abhyudaya shraaddha, as told earlier.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sacelasya pituḥ — of the father with his garment; snānaṃ — bathing; jāte putre — when a son is born; vidhīyate — is prescribed; jātakarma — the birth-rite; tathā kuryāt — he should likewise perform; śrāddham abhyudayaṃ — the prosperity-śrāddha; ca yat — and whatever is prescribed

Śrīdhara

trayodaśe'tha śrāddhāni sanimittānyavarṇayat | pretakriyāstathā tāsu yathārhamadhikāriṇaḥ | pituḥ putrajanmakāle sannihitasya — In the thirteenth chapter he describes śrāddhas with their occasions, and the rites for the dead, together with the persons entitled to perform them; this concerns the father present when a son is born.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sacelasya pituḥ snānaṃ jāte putre vidhīyate | jātakarma tathā kuryācchrāddhamabhyudayaṃ ca yat ||1||

trayodaśe'tha śrāddhāni sanimittānyavarṇayat | pretakriyāstathā tāsu yathārhamadhikāriṇaḥ | pituḥ putrajanmakāle sannihitasya

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

त्रयोदशेऽथ श्राद्धानि सनिमित्तान्यवर्णयत् । प्रेतक्रियास्तथा तासु यथार्हमधिकारिणः । पितुः पुत्रजन्मकाले सन्निहितस्य

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1).

In the thirteenth chapter he describes śrāddhas with their occasions, and the rites for the dead, together with the persons entitled to perform them; this concerns the father present when a son is born.

VP 3.13.2

युग्मान्देवांश्च पित्र्यांश्च सम्यक्सव्यक्रमाद्द्विजान् । पूजयेद्भोजयेच्चैव तन्मना नान्यमानसः

In pairs, Gods, Brahmins, and pitrus, in the pradakshina krama, one has to worship; one has to offer food to Brahmins also, being dedicated and focussing on that.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yugmān — in pairs; devāṃś ca — the gods; pitryāṃś ca — the ancestral ones; samyak — properly; savyakramāt — in clockwise order; dvijān — the twice-born; pūjayet — he should worship; bhojayet ca eva — he should also feed; tan-manāḥ — with mind fixed on that; na anyamānasaḥ — not with mind directed elsewhere

Śrīdhara

savyakramāt pradakṣiṇakrameṇa | nānyamānasaḥ anyasmin utpannaputrādau mānasaṃ yasya saḥ śrāddhasamaye tādṛśo bhavedityarthaḥ — Savyakrama means the clockwise or pradakṣiṇa order. Nān-yamānasa means one whose mind is not directed elsewhere—especially toward a newly born child; at the time of the śrāddha he should be attentive in this way.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सव्यक्रमात् प्रदक्षिणक्रमेण । नान्यमानसः अन्यस्मिन् उत्पन्नपुत्रादौ मानसं यस्य सः श्राद्धसमये तादृशो भवेदित्यर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 2).

Savyakrama means the clockwise or pradakṣiṇa order. Nān-yamānasa means one whose mind is not directed elsewhere—especially toward a newly born child; at the time of the śrāddha he should be attentive in this way.

VP 3.13.3

VP 3.13.4

नान्दीमुखः पितृगणस्तेन श्राद्धेन पार्थिव । प्रीयते तत्तु कर्तव्यं पुरुषैस्सर्ववृद्धिषु

In all the vruddhi kaaryaas, used for abhivruddhi, the mangala karmaas, one has to do naandee mukha shraaddha, and the pitru ganaas which are called naandee mukhaas, they will be pleased with this naandee mukha shraaddha. One has to do these naandee mukha shraaddha in all the vruddhi karmaas.

Transliteration

No commentary.

VP 3.13.5

कन्यापुत्रविवाहेषु प्रवेशेषु च वेश्मनः । नामकर्मणि बालानां चूडाकर्मादिके तथा

VP 3.13.8

प्रेतदेहं शुभैः स्नानैस्नापितं स्रग्विभूषितम् । दग्ध्वा ग्रामाद्बहिः स्नात्वा सचेलास्सलिलाशये

For the dead body, one has to give a bath, mangala snaana, and also decorate with garland, take it outside the village and then burn it, go to a water source and take bath with the cloth on.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 8).

VP 3.13.10

प्रविष्टाश्च समं गोभिर्ग्रामं नक्षत्रदर्शने । कटकर्म ततः कुर्युर्भूमौ प्रस्तरशायिनः

They have to re-enter the village, after doing dahana outside the village, when the cows enter, during the evening, during godhooli kaala, and when the stars are visible.

VP 3.13.11

VP 3.13.12

As desired, one can offer food to Brahmins during all the days. When all the relatives and people eat during that time, the preta will get satisfied.

VP 3.13.14

The former rites extend from cremation through the end of impurity, ending with the touching of water, weapons, and the like. Alternatively, “ādāha” denotes the cremation-ground, and the first and last acts are cremation and the touching of water and weapons.

VP 3.13.37

VP 3.13.39

पूर्वाः क्रियास्तु कर्तव्याः पुत्राद्यैरेव चोत्तराः । दौहित्रैर्वा नृपश्रेष्ठ कार्यास्तत्तनयैस्तथा
