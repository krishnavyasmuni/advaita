VP 3.13.1

और्व उवाच— स-चैलस्य पितुः स्नानं जाते पुत्रे विधीयते।
जात-कर्म तदा कुर्याच् छ्राद्धम् अभ्युदये च यत्

When a son is born, the father is prescribed to bathe with his clothes on. He should then perform the birth-rite and the prosperity śrāddha as prescribed.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
sacelasya pituḥ — of the clothed father;
snānaṃ — bathing;
jāte putre — when a son is born;
vidhīyate — is prescribed;
jātakarma — the birth-rite;
tathā kuryāt — he should likewise perform;
śrāddham abhyudayaṃ — the prosperity-śrāddha;
ca yat — and whatever [is prescribed]
Śrīdhara
trayodaśe'tha śrāddhāni sanimittānyavarṇayat | pretakriyāstathā tāsu yathārhamadhikāriṇaḥ | pituḥ putrajanmakāle sannihitasya — In the thirteenth chapter he describes śrāddhas with their occasions, and the rites for the dead, together with the persons entitled to perform them;
this concerns the father present when a son is born.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sacelasya pituḥ snānaṃ jāte putre vidhīyate | jātakarma tathā kuryācchrāddhamabhyudayaṃ ca yat ||1||

trayodaśe'tha śrāddhāni sanimittānyavarṇayat | pretakriyāstathā tāsu yathārhamadhikāriṇaḥ | pituḥ putrajanmakāle sannihitasya

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

त्रयोदशेऽथ श्राद्धानि सनिमित्तान्यवर्णयत् । प्रेतक्रियास्तथा तासु यथार्हमधिकारिणः । पितुः पुत्रजन्मकाले सन्निहितस्य

Śrīdhara commentary.

In the thirteenth chapter he describes śrāddhas with their occasions, and the rites for the dead, together with the persons entitled to perform them; this concerns the father present when a son is born.

VP 3.13.2

युग्मान् देवांश् च पित्र्यांश् च सम्यक् सव्य-क्रमाद् द्विजान्।

Word-for-word

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

VP 3.13.12

आ दाहाद् दाहपूर्वावधिकाश्च ता वार्यायुधादिस्पर्शादिरन्तो यासां ताश्च तथा । यद्वा आ समन्ताद् दह्यन्तेऽस्मिन्नित्यादाहं श्मशानं तेन प्रेतदाहो लक्ष्यते आदाहञ्च वार्यायुधादिस्पर्शञ्च आद्यन्तौ यासां ताः । दाहाद्याशौचान्तभवाः पूर्वाः क्रिया इत्यर्थः

Śrīdhara commentary.

The former rites extend from cremation through the end of impurity, ending with the touching of water, weapons, and the like. Alternatively, “ādāha” denotes the cremation-ground, and the first and last acts are cremation and the touching of water and weapons.

VP 3.13.39

पूर्वाः क्रियाश् कर्तव्याः पुत्राद्यैर् एव चोत्तराः।

The former rites are to be performed by the son and the others; the later rites may be performed by a daughter's sons, O best of kings, and likewise by their descendants.

Śrīdhara

Transliteration

Śrīdhara commentary.

The word “former” includes the middle rites too. After cremation, the paternal or maternal Sapindas, Samanodakas, companions, or the king may perform them; this also includes the middle rites up to Sapindikarana.
