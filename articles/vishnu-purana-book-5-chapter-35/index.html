VP 5.35.1

श्रीमैत्रेय उवाच
भूय एवाहमिच्छामि बलभद्रस्य धीमतः ।
श्रोतुं पराक्रमं ब्रह्मन् तन्ममाख्यातुमर्हसि

Maitreya said: I wish once more to hear of the wise Balabhadra’s prowess. O brāhmaṇa, you should tell me about it.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
bhūyaḥ eva aham icchāmi — I wish again;
balabhadrasya dhīmataḥ — of wise Balabhadra;
śrotum — to hear;
parākramam — the prowess;
tan mama ākhyātum arhasi — you should tell that to me;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhūya evāhamicchāmi balabhadrasya dhīmataḥ | śrotuṃ parākramaṃ brahman tanmamākhyātumarhasi

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.35.2

यमुनाकर्षणादीनि श्रुतानि भगवन्मया ।
तत्कथ्यतां महाभाग यदन्यत्कृतवान्बलः

I have heard of his drawing the Yamunā and such deeds. O greatly blessed one, tell me what else Baladeva did.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
yamunā-ākarṣaṇa-ādīni — drawing the Yamunā and such deeds;
śrutāni — heard;
bhagavan mayā — O blessed one, by me;
tat kathyatām — let that be told;
yat anyat kṛtavān balaḥ — what else Bala did;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yamunākarṣaṇādīni śrutāni bhagavanmayā | tatkathyatāṃ mahābhāga yadanyatkṛtavānbalaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.35.3

श्रीपराशर उवाच
मैत्रेय श्रूयतां कर्म यद्रामेणाभवत्कृतम् ।
अनन्तेनाप्रमेयेन शेषेण धरणीधृता

Parāśara said: Maitreya, hear what was done by Rāma, the immeasurable Ananta, the Śeṣa who supports the earth.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
maitreya — O Maitreya;
śrūyatām — let it be heard;
karma rāmeṇa kṛtam — the deed done by Rāma;
anantena aprameyena — by immeasurable Ananta;
śeṣeṇa dharaṇī-dhṛtā — Śeṣa who supports the earth;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

maitreya śrūyatāṃ karma yadrāmeṇābhavatkṛtam | anantenāprameyena śeṣeṇa dharaṇīdhṛtā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.35.4

सुयोधनस्य तनयां स्वयंवरकृतक्षणाम् ।
बलादादत्तवान्वीरस्साम्बो जाम्बवतीसुतः

The heroic Sāmba, son of Jāmbavatī, forcibly took the daughter of Suyodhana when the occasion for her svayaṃvara had arrived.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
suyodhanasya tanayām — Suyodhana’s daughter;
svayaṃvara-kṛta-kṣaṇām — for whom the svayaṃvara occasion had arrived;
balāt — by force;
ādattavān — took;
vīraḥ sāmbaḥ — the hero Sāmba;
jāmbavatī-sutaḥ — son of Jāmbavatī;
Śrīdhara
suyodhanasya tanayām — Suyodhana’s daughter;
svayaṃvare — in the svayaṃvara;
kṛta-kṣaṇām — having obtained the occasion;
ādattavān — took;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

suyodhanasya tanayāṃ svayaṃvarakṛtakṣaṇām | balādādattavānvīrassāmbo jāmbavatīsutaḥ

Śrīdhara

yathā sakala-bala-janakośādi-sahitāṃ vāraṇāsīṃ viṣṇu-cakraṃ dadāha, tathaiva sarva-sahitaṃ hastināpuraṃ balabhadro gaṅgāyāṃ prakṣeputm udyata iti vaktuṃ tat-prastāvam āha—suyodhanasyety-ādinā viviśur gaja-sāhvayām ity antena | suyodhanasyeti | svayaṃvare kṛtakṣaṇāṃ svayaṃvara-kṛta-kṣaṇāṃ labdhāvasarām ॥४ ॥

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यथा सकल-बल-जनकोशादि-सहितां वारणासीं विष्णु-चक्रं ददाह, तथैव सर्व-सहितं हस्तिनापुरं बलभद्रो गङ्गायां प्रक्षेपुत्म् उद्यत इति वक्तुं तत्-प्रस्तावम् आह—सुयोधनस्येत्य्-आदिना विविशुर् गज-साह्वयाम् इत्य् अन्तेन । सुयोधनस्येति । स्वयंवरे कृतक्षणां स्वयंवर-कृत-क्षणां लब्धावसराम् ॥४ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Śrīdhara explains that the svayaṃvara occasion had arrived; Sāmba took her by force.

VP 5.35.7

तान्निवार्य बलः प्राह मदलोलकलाक्षरम् ।
मोक्ष्यन्ति ते मद्वचनाद्यास्याम्येको हि कौरवान्

Baladeva restrained them and said in a voice unsteady with intoxication: “They will release Sāmba at my word. I alone will go; who are the Kauravas?”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tān nivārya — restraining them;
balaḥ prāha — Bala said;
mada-lola-kala-akṣaram — in a voice unsteady with intoxication;
mokṣyanti te — they will release;
mat-vacanāt — at my word;
yāsyāmi ekaḥ — I alone will go;
kaḥ hi kauravān — who indeed are the Kauravas;
Śrīdhara
madena lolam — made unsteady by intoxication;
kala-akṣaram — the voice;
yathā bhavati — as it becomes;
prāha — he said;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tānnivārya balaḥ prāha madalolakalākṣaram | mokṣyanti te madvacanādyāsyāmyeko hi kauravān

Śrīdhara

tān iti | madena lolaṃ capalaṃ kalākṣaraṃ yathā bhavaty evaṃ prāha ॥७ ॥

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तान् इति । मदेन लोलं चपलं कलाक्षरं यथा भवत्य् एवं प्राह ॥७ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Bala’s voice was made unsteady and playful by intoxication.

VP 5.35.8

श्रीपराशर उवाच
बलदेवस्ततो गत्वा नगरं नागसाह्वयम् ।
बाह्योपवनमध्येऽभून्न विवेश च तत्पुरम्

Then Baladeva went to the city called Nāgasāhvaya. He stayed in the outer garden and did not enter the city.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
baladevaḥ tataḥ gatvā — then Baladeva went;
nagaram nāga-sāhvayam — to the city Nāgasāhvaya;
bāhya-upavana-madhye — in the outer garden;
abhūt — he stayed;
na viveśa — did not enter;
tat puram — that city;
Śrīdhara
tat-puram na viveśa — did not enter that city;
sva-ukti-an-aṅgīkāre — if his statement was not accepted;
raṇe taiḥ yoddhum — to fight them in battle;
gaṅgāyām prakṣeptum icchan — intending to throw it into the Gaṅgā;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

baladevastato gatvā nagaraṃ nāgasāhvayam | bāhyopavanamadhye’bhūnna viveśa ca tatpuram

Śrīdhara

baladeva iti | tat-puraṃ na viveśa svokty-anaṅgīkāre raṇe tair yoddhuṃ tat-puraṃ ca gaṅgāyāṃ parkṣeptum icchan ॥८ ॥

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

बलदेव इति । तत्-पुरं न विवेश स्वोक्त्य्-अनङ्गीकारे रणे तैर् योद्धुं तत्-पुरं च गङ्गायां पर्क्षेप्तुम् इच्छन् ॥८ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Baladeva did not enter the city because, if the Kauravas rejected his words, he intended to fight them and throw the city into the Gaṅgā.

VP 5.35.9

बलमागतमाज्ञाय भूपा दुर्योधनादयः ।
गामर्घ्यमुदकं चैव रामाय प्रत्यवेदयन्

When the kings, Duryodhana and the others, learned that he had arrived, they offered Rāma a cow, the arghya, and water.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
balam āgatam ājñāya — knowing Bala had arrived;
bhūpāḥ — the kings;
duryodhana-ādayaḥ — Duryodhana and others;
gām — a cow;
arghyam udakam ca — arghya and water;
rāmāya — to Rāma;
pratyavedayan — offered;
Śrīdhara
udakam — water;
pādyam — water for the feet;
rāmāya dattavantaḥ — they gave to Rāma;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

balamāgatamājñāya bhūpā duryodhanādayaḥ | gāmarghyamudakaṃ caiva rāmāya pratyavedayan

Śrīdhara

balam iti | udakaṃ ca pādyaṃ ca rāmāya dattavantaḥ ॥९ ॥

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

बलम् इति । उदकं च पाद्यं च रामाय दत्तवन्तः ॥९ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

They offered Rāma water and pādya, the water for washing the feet.

VP 5.35.14

उग्रसेनोऽपि यद्याज्ञां कौरवाणां प्रदास्यति ।
तदलं पाण्डुरैश्छत्रैर्नृपयोग्यैर्विडम्बनैः

“Even if Ugrasena issues an order to the Kauravas, then enough of these pale royal parasols and other royal pretensions—what a mockery!”

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
ugrasenaḥ api — even Ugrasena;
yadi ājñām pradāsyati — if he gives an order;
tad alam — then enough;
pāṇḍuraiḥ chatraiḥ — with pale royal parasols;

Śrīdhara
viḍambitaiḥ — held in imitation;
asmat-anusaraṇena dhṛtaiḥ — held by following us;
nṛpa-arhaiḥ chatraiḥ — parasols fit for kings;
alam — enough / we will take them away;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ugraseno’pi yadyājñāṃ kauravāṇāṃ pradāsyati | tadalaṃ pāṇḍuraiśchatrairnṛpayogyairviḍambanaiḥ

Śrīdhara

ugrasena iti | viḍambitaiḥ asmad-anusaraṇena dhṛtair yamānāṃ nṛpārhaiś chatrair alaṃ tāny apahariṣyāma iti bhāvaḥ ॥१४ ॥

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उग्रसेन इति । विडम्बितैः अस्मद्-अनुसरणेन धृतैर् यमानां नृपार्हैश् छत्रैर् अलं तान्य् अपहरिष्याम इति भावः ॥१४ ॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Their royal parasols were held in imitation of the insignia of kings; he means that the Yādavas would take them away.

VP 5.35.16

प्रणतिर्या कृतास्माकं मान्यानां कुकुरान्धकैः ।

Śrīdhara

yādavaiḥ praṇatiḥ — obeisance by the Yādavas;

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara
