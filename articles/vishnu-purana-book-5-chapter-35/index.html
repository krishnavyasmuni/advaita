VP 5.35.1

भूय एवाहमिच्छामि बलभद्रस्य धीमतः । श्रोतुं पराक्रमं ब्रह्मन् तन्ममाख्यातुमर्हसि

Maitreyar - O Paraasharar, I would like to again the story, the valiant deeds that Balaraama did, who was a very powerful person. Please tell me, narrate to me all the details.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhūya evāhamicchāmi balabhadrasya dhīmataḥ . śrotuṃ parākramaṃ brahman tanmamākhyātumarhasi — Maitreyar - O Paraasharar, I would like to again the story, the valiant deeds that Balaraama did, who was a very powerful person. Please tell me, narrate to me all the details.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhūya evāhamicchāmi balabhadrasya dhīmataḥ . śrotuṃ parākramaṃ brahman tanmamākhyātumarhasi

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

He pulled away the Yamunaa river itself, which I have heard. I want to listen to that story completely, that event, what happened. And also anything else which He did, such wonderful acts, done by Balaraama. I want to listen to them.

VP 5.35.3

All the wonderful acts that Balaraama, who is none other than Ananta, who does not have an equal, whose capability cannot be measured, who is none other than Aadishesha, by whom the entire earth is supported, did.

VP 5.35.4

सुयोधनस्य तनयां स्वयंवरकृतक्षणाम् । बलादादत्तवान्वीरः सांबो जांबवतीसुतः

Duryodhana's daughter was having a Svayamvara. At that time, Jaambavati's son Saamba went and snatched her away by force, and stole her.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

suyodhanasya tanayāṃ svayaṃvarakṛtakṣaṇām . balādādattavānvīrassāmbo jāmbavatīsutaḥ — Duryodhana's daughter was having a Svayamvara. At that time, Jaambavati's son Saamba went and snatched her away by force, and stole her.

Śrīdhara

yathā sakala-bala-janakośādi-sahitāṃ vāraṇāsīṃ viṣṇu-cakraṃ dadāha, tathaiva sarva-sahitaṃ hastināpuraṃ balabhadro gaṅgāyāṃ prakṣeputm udyata iti vaktuṃ tat-prastāvam āha—suyodhanasyety-ādinā viviśur gaja-sāhvayām ity antena . suyodhanasyeti . svayaṃvare kṛtakṣaṇāṃ svayaṃvara-kṛta-kṣaṇāṃ labdhāvasarām — Duryodhana's daughter was having a Svayamvara. At that time, Jaambavati's son Saamba went and snatched her away by force, and stole her.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

suyodhanasya tanayāṃ svayaṃvarakṛtakṣaṇām . balādādattavānvīraḥ sāṃbo jāṃbavatīsutaḥ

Śrīdhara

yathā sakala-bala-janakośādi-sahitāṃ vāraṇāsīṃ viṣṇu-cakraṃ dadāha, tathaiva sarva-sahitaṃ hastināpuraṃ balabhadro gaṅgāyāṃ prakṣeputm udyata iti vaktuṃ tat-prastāvam āha—suyodhanasyety-ādinā viviśur gaja-sāhvayām ity antena . suyodhanasyeti . svayaṃvare kṛtakṣaṇāṃ svayaṃvara-kṛta-kṣaṇāṃ labdhāvasarām ||४|| tataḥ kruddhā mahā-vīryāḥ karṇa-duryodhanādayaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यथा सकल-बल-जनकोशादि-सहितां वारणासीं विष्णु-चक्रं ददाह, तथैव सर्व-सहितं हस्तिनापुरं बलभद्रो गङ्गायां प्रक्षेपुत्म् उद्यत इति वक्तुं तत्-प्रस्तावम् आह—सुयोधनस्येत्य्-आदिना विविशुर् गज-साह्वयाम् इत्य् अन्तेन । सुयोधनस्येति । स्वयंवरे कृतक्षणां स्वयंवर-कृत-क्षणां लब्धावसराम् ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Duryodhana's daughter was having a Svayamvara. At that time, Jaambavati's son Saamba went and snatched her away by force, and stole her.

VP 5.35.7

तान्निवार्य बलः प्राह मदलोलकलाक्षरम् । मोक्ष्यन्ति ते मद्वचनाद्यास्याम्येको हि कौरवान्

Then, Balaraama stopped all of them from planning to go there and fight with the Kouravaas; His eyes were all intoxicated, eyes rolling. He said - "If I just tell them, with my words, they will leave Saamba, and I alone am enough, will go and take care of the Kouravaas. Don't worry". He stops all of them.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

He said - "If I just tell them, with my words, they will leave Saamba, and I alone am enough, will go and take care of the Kouravaas. Don't worry". He stops all of them.

Śrīdhara

tān iti . madena lolaṃ capalaṃ kalākṣaraṃ yathā bhavaty evaṃ prāha — Then, Balaraama stopped all of them from planning to go there and fight with the Kouravaas; His eyes were all intoxicated, eyes rolling. He said - "If I just tell them, with my words, they will leave Saamba, and I alone am enough, will go and take care of the Kouravaas. Don't worry". He stops all of them.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tānnivārya balaḥ prāha madalolakalākṣaram . mokṣyanti te madvacanādyāsyāmyeko hi kauravān

Śrīdhara

tān iti . madena lolaṃ capalaṃ kalākṣaraṃ yathā bhavaty evaṃ prāha ||७|| śrī-parāśara uvāca— baladevas tato gatvā nagaraṃ nāga-sāhvayam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तान् इति । मदेन लोलं चपलं कलाक्षरं यथा भवत्य् एवं प्राह ॥७॥ श्री-पराशर उवाच— बलदेवस् ततो गत्वा नगरं नाग-साह्वयम् ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Then, Balaraama stopped all of them from planning to go there and fight with the Kouravaas; His eyes were all intoxicated, eyes rolling. He said - "If I just tell them, with my words, they will leave Saamba, and I alone am enough, will go and take care of the Kouravaas. Don't worry". He stops all of them.

VP 5.35.8

बलदेवस्ततो दृष्ट्वा नगरं नागसाह्वयम् । बाह्योपवनमध्येऽभून्न विवेश च तत्पुरम्

Sri Paraasharar - Baladeva saw that city, and there was a garden outside itself. He stayed there itself, and did not enter into the city. In case they did not listen to him, he wanted to fight with them. That is why he decided not to enter the city, but he rested outside the city in a garden.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

He stayed there itself, and did not enter into the city. In case they did not listen to him, he wanted to fight with them. That is why he decided not to enter the city, but he rested outside the city in a garden.

Śrīdhara

baladeva iti . tat-puraṃ na viveśa svokty-anaṅgīkāre raṇe tair yoddhuṃ tat-puraṃ ca gaṅgāyāṃ parkṣeptum icchan — Sri Paraasharar - Baladeva saw that city, and there was a garden outside itself. He stayed there itself, and did not enter into the city. In case they did not listen to him, he wanted to fight with them. That is why he decided not to enter the city, but he rested outside the city in a garden.

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

baladeva iti . tat-puraṃ na viveśa svokty-anaṅgīkāre raṇe tair yoddhuṃ tat-puraṃ ca gaṅgāyāṃ parkṣeptum icchan ||८|| balam āgatam ājñāya bhūpā duryodhanādayaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

बलदेव इति । तत्-पुरं न विवेश स्वोक्त्य्-अनङ्गीकारे रणे तैर् योद्धुं तत्-पुरं च गङ्गायां पर्क्षेप्तुम् इच्छन् ॥८॥ बलम् आगतम् आज्ञाय भूपा दुर्योधनादयः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.35.9

VP 5.35.14

We are all deserving to be kings, and we have shveta chatri, and we are all great kings. And what is the purpose of all these things, these will all become laughable. Who is Ugrasena to command us?"

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

ugrasena iti . viḍambitaiḥ asmad-anusaraṇena dhṛtair yamānāṃ nṛpārhaiś chatrair alaṃ tāny apahariṣyāma iti bhāvaḥ ||१४|| tad gaccha bala mā vā tvaṃ sāmbam anyāya-ceṣṭitam .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit
