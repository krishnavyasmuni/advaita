VP 5.6.1

कदाचिच्छकटस्याधः शयानो मधुसूदनः । चिक्षेप चरणावूर्ध्वं स्तन्यार्थी प्ररुरोद ह

Sri Paraasharar - Below the cart, they had made a small bed for Krishna, and He was sleeping there. He felt hungry, and He wanted to be fed with milk. He lifted His small feet and kicked the cart. He cried loudly, asking for milk.

He felt hungry, and He wanted to be fed with milk. He lifted His small feet and kicked the cart. He cried loudly, asking for milk.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kadācicchakaṭasyādhaḥ śayāno madhusūdanaḥ . cikṣepa caraṇāvūrdhvaṃ stanyārthī praruroda ha

No commentary.

VP 5.6.2

तस्य पादप्रहारेण शकटं परिवर्तितम् । विध्वस्तकुंभभाण्डं तद्विपरीतं पपात वै

As soon as child Krishna kicked the cart, with His feet, immediately, that whole cart turned upside down, and all the vessels kept on that, were completely broken, destroyed. The pots which were kept, and instruments kept on the cart were destroyed. A raakshasa by name Mura had come in the form of that cart, Shakata, and nobody knew that. That Shakata was completely shattered into pieces, having been turned upside down. That his name is Mura is told in Harivamsha, he is known as Shakataasura.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasya pādaprahāreṇa śakaṭaṃ parivartitam . vidhvastakumbhabhāṇḍaṃ tadviparītaṃ papāta vai — As soon as child Krishna kicked the cart, with His feet, immediately, that whole cart turned upside down, and all the vessels kept on that, were completely broken, destroyed. The pots which were kept, and instruments kept on the cart were destroyed. A raakshasa by name Mura had come in the form of that cart, Shakata, and nobody knew that. That Shakata was completely shattered into pieces, having been turned upside down. That his name is Mura is told in Harivamsha, he is known as Shakataasura.

Śrīdhara

tasyeti . vidhvasta-kumbha-bhāṇḍaṃ viśīrṇa-kumbhādy-upakaraṇam . kupya-bhāṇḍām iti pāṭhe kupyaṃ svarṇa-rajata-vyatiriktaṃ dravyam — As soon as child Krishna kicked the cart, with His feet, immediately, that whole cart turned upside down, and all the vessels kept on that, were completely broken, destroyed. The pots which were kept, and instruments kept on the cart were destroyed. A raakshasa by name Mura had come in the form of that cart, Shakata, and nobody knew that. That Shakata was completely shattered into pieces, having been turned upside down. That his name is Mura is told in Harivamsha, he is known as Shakataasura.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasya pādaprahāreṇa śakaṭaṃ parivartitam . vidhvastakuṃbhabhāṇḍaṃ tadviparītaṃ papāta vai

Śrīdhara

tasyeti . vidhvasta-kumbha-bhāṇḍaṃ viśīrṇa-kumbhādy-upakaraṇam . kupya-bhāṇḍām iti pāṭhe kupyaṃ svarṇa-rajata-vyatiriktaṃ dravyam ||२|| tato hāhā-kṛtaḥ sarvo gopa-gopī-jano dvija.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तस्येति । विध्वस्त-कुम्भ-भाण्डं विशीर्ण-कुम्भाद्य्-उपकरणम् । कुप्य-भाण्डाम् इति पाठे कुप्यं स्वर्ण-रजत-व्यतिरिक्तं द्रव्यम् ॥२॥ ततो हाहा-कृतः सर्वो गोप-गोपी-जनो द्विज।

Śrīdhara commentary.

As soon as child Krishna kicked the cart, with His feet, immediately, that whole cart turned upside down, and all the vessels kept on that, were completely broken, destroyed. The pots which were kept, and instruments kept on the cart were destroyed. A raakshasa by name Mura had come in the form of that cart, Shakata, and nobody knew that. That Shakata was completely shattered into pieces, having been turned upside down. That his name is Mura is told in Harivamsha, he is known as Shakataasura.

VP 5.6.3

VP 5.6.4

गोपाः केनेति केनेदं शकटं परिवर्तितम् । तत्रैव बालकाः प्रोचुर्बालेनानेन पातितम्

When they came there, they asked - "Who did this, who did this?", "Who was responsible for turning this cart upside down like this?". The kids who were playing around there, they said that this baby who is lying here, did like this - that baby only kicked and did all of this.

Transliteration

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

गोपा इति । केन केनेति सम्भ्रमार्थे द्विरुक्तिः ॥४॥ रुदता दृष्टम् अस्माभिः पाद-विक्षेप-पातितम्१०३।

When they came there, they asked - "Who did this, who did this?", "Who was responsible for turning this cart upside down like this?". The kids who were playing around there, they said that this baby who is lying here, did like this - that baby only kicked and did all of this.

VP 5.6.8

Śrīdhara commentary.

Vasudeva sent Garga Muni to Gokula, secretly. Secretly, without revealing his identity, without anyone knowing, Garga Rishi, did naamakarana samskaara to the boys.
