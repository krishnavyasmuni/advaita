VP 1.9.1

इदं च शृणु मैत्रेय यत् पृष्टोऽहम् इह त्वया ।
श्री-सम्बद्धं मया ह्य् एतच् छ्रुतम् आसीन् मरीचितः ॥१॥

O Maitreya, listen to this also, I am going to tell you more, which is connected to Shridevi, which I heard from Mareechi.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

iadṃ — key term; śṛṇu — listen; maitreya — Maitreya; yat — which/that; pṛṣṭoʼaham — key term; iaha — key term; tvayā — by you

Śrīdhara

tad — key term; eavṃ — key term; maitreyakṛtākṣepaparihārārthṃ — key term; sarvātmatvaprapañcanena — key term; viṣṇoriva — key term; lakṣmyā — key term

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

iadṃ ca śṛṇu maitreya yat pṛṣṭoʼaham iaha tvayā śrīsambaddhṃ mayā hy eatac chrutam āasīn marīcitḥ १

Śrīdhara

tad eavṃ maitreyakṛtākṣepaparihārārthṃ sarvātmatvaprapañcanena viṣṇoriva lakṣmyā aapi nityatvamuktam iadānīm āavirbhāvatirobhāvābhyāṃ taduktṃ virodhṃ parihartumāha iadañcety āadināpūrvṃ bhṛgusutā satītyantena granthena bhṛgusutāpi śrīryathā kṣīrābdhau jātā iaty uacyate iadañca śṛṇu mayāpi eavam eava sandihānena marīceḥ sakāśād eatac śrutamāsīt १

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तद् एवं मैत्रेयकृताक्षेपपरिहारार्थं सर्वात्मत्वप्रपञ्चनेन विष्णोरिव लक्ष्म्या अपि नित्यत्वमुक्तम्, इदानीम् आविर्भावतिरोभावाभ्यां तदुक्तं विरोधं परिहर्तुमाह इदञ्चेत्य् आदिनापूर्वं भृगुसुता सतीत्यन्तेन ग्रन्थेन । भृगुसुतापि श्रीर्यथा क्षीराब्धौ जाता इत्य् उच्यते इदञ्च शृणु । मयापि एवम् एव सन्दिहानेन मरीचेः सकाशाद् एतच् श्रुतमासीत् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Mareechi is the father of Kaashyapa. He told me that story, I will tell you.

VP 1.9.2

दुर्वासाः शङ्करस्यांशश् चचार पृथिवीम् इमाम् ।
स ददर्श स्रजं द्वियाम् ऋषिर् विद्याधरी-करे ॥२॥

Durvaasa Rushi is well known for his anger, is Shankara's amsha, was roaming around in the world. He saw a wonderful divine garland, in the hand of a Vidyaadhara stree.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

durvāsāḥ — Durvāsas; śaṅkarasyāṃśaś — key term; cacāra — wandered; pṛthivīm — earth; iamām — key term; dadarśa — saw; srajṃ — key term

Śrīdhara

tatra — key term; prathamṃ — key term; bhṛgusutāyāḥ — key term; satyāstirodhāne — key term; kāraṇṃ — key term; vaktum — key term

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

durvāsāḥ śaṅkarasyāṃśaś cacāra pṛthivīm iamām sa dadarśa srajṃ dviyām ṛaṣir vidyādharīkare २

Śrīdhara

tatra prathamṃ bhṛgusutāyāḥ satyāstirodhāne kāraṇṃ vaktum āahauanmattavratadhṛgyogino hi jaḍonmattapiśācā iava durvāsā iati śaṅkarasyāṃśa iaty aanena tasya śakraśāpasāmarthyam aaparādhāsahiṣṇutvañcoktam santāno devataruḥ tadīyānāṃ puṣpāṇāṃ srajṃ mālām yasyā gandhena vāsitṃ tad vanṃ vanacāriṇām aatisevyamabhūt २३

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत्र प्रथमं भृगुसुतायाः सत्यास्तिरोधाने कारणं वक्तुम् आह—उन्मत्तव्रतधृग्योगिनो हि जडोन्मत्तपिशाचा इव दुर्वासा इति । शङ्करस्यांश इत्य् अनेन तस्य शक्रशापसामर्थ्यम् अपराधासहिष्णुत्वञ्चोक्तम् । सन्तानो देवतरुः, तदीयानां पुष्पाणां स्रजं मालाम् । यस्या गन्धेन वासितं तद् वनं वनचारिणाम् अतिसेव्यमभूत् ॥२-३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Durvaasa is very intolerant, akshaanti. He curses people for the slightest of mistakes, as well known in the Puraanaas. The cause of the akshaanti is said that he is amsha of Shankara, as Shankara also is said to be like that.

VP 1.9.4

उन्मत्त-व्रत-धृग् विप्रस् तां दृष्ट्वा शोभनां स्रजम् ।
तां ययाचे वरारोहां विद्याधर-वधूं ततः ॥४॥

Durvaasa Rushi was highly attracted by that garland. He was following the vrata of behaving like an unmatta, inebriated. He begged her to give him that garland.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

uanmattavratadhṛg — key term; vipras — key term; tāṃ — key term; dṛṣṭvā — having seen; śobhanāṃ — key term; srajam — garland; yayāce — key term

Śrīdhara

vartante — key term; tāṃ — key term; srajṃ — key term; vidyādharavadhūṃ — key term; yācitavān — key term; iati — key term

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

uanmattavratadhṛg vipras tāṃ dṛṣṭvā śobhanāṃ srajam tāṃ yayāce varārohāṃ vidyādharavadhūṃ tatḥ ४

Śrīdhara

vartante tāṃ srajṃ tāṃ vidyādharavadhūṃ yācitavān iati dvikarmako yācati ४५

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वर्तन्ते । तां स्रजं तां विद्याधरवधूं याचितवान् इति द्विकर्मको याचति ॥४-५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

There is an instance of Sanatkumaara shaapa for Durvaasa, because of which he was as though inebriated. It is also said that yogis behave like this. Yogis when they are in aatma anubhava, behave like inert objects, jada, without realizing anything that is happening around. Or as though they are in an inebriated condition.

First, He is told as kaarana avasthaa, and also kaarya avasthaa. The order of creation is moola prakruti, mahat, ahamkaara, etc. During pralaya, the laya order is that effects go and merge into their causes. In each one of these, they happen only because Paramaatman is antaryaami. It is not that moola prakruti itself becomes the cause of mahat, mahat becomes cause of ahamkaara, etc. Mahat shareeraka Paramaatman only becomes cause of ahamkaara. First, it is told in the srushti order, and then told in the pralaya order. In both of these, Paramaatman only is the cause in every single step.

अन्येषु चावतारेषु विष्णोर् एषा सहायिनी ॥१४४॥

When Bhagavaan incarnated as Sriraama, She became Seeta. And, in His incarnation as Krishna, She manifested as Rukmini. In whatever avataaraas Vishnu is seen, She is always inseparably associated with Him.
