VP 1.9.1

इदं च शृणु मैत्रेय यत् पृष्टोऽहम् इह त्वया ।
श्री-सम्बद्धं मया ह्य् एतच् छ्रुतम् आसीन् मरीचितः ॥१॥

Parāśara said: Hear this also, O Maitreya, concerning Śrī, which I heard from Marīci in answer to your question.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
idam ca — this also;
śṛṇu — hear;
maitreya — O Maitreya;
yat — which;
pṛṣṭaḥ aham — I was asked;
iha tvayā — here by you;
śrī-sambaddham — connected with Śrī;
mayā hi etat śrutam — this indeed was heard by me;
āsīt marīciteḥ — it was from Marīci
Śrīdhara
tad evaṃ ... nityatvam uktam — thus the eternality of Lakṣmī, like that of Viṣṇu, has been stated;
āvirbhāva-tirobhāvābhyāṃ virodhaṃ parihartum āha — to remove the apparent contradiction of manifestation and disappearance, he says;
bhṛgu-sutā satī — being the daughter of Bhṛgu;
bhṛgu-sutā api śrīḥ kṣīrābdhau jātā — Śrī too is said to have been born in the Milk Ocean;
idaṃ ca śṛṇu — hear this also;
marīceḥ sakāśāt etat śrutam — this was heard from Marīci
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

idaṃ ca śṛṇu maitreya yat pṛṣṭo'ham iha tvayā | śrī-sambaddhaṃ mayā hy etac chrutam āsīn marīcitaḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara

tad evaṃ maitreyakṛtākṣepaparihārārthaṃ sarvātmatvaprapañcanena viṣṇoriva lakṣmyā api nityatvamuktam, idānīm āvirbhāvatirobhāvābhyāṃ taduktaṃ virodhaṃ parihartumāha idañcety ādināpūrvaṃ bhṛgusutā satītyantena granthena | bhṛgusutāpi śrīryathā kṣīrābdhau jātā ity ucyate idañca śṛṇu | mayāpi evam eva sandihānena marīceḥ sakāśād etac śrutamāsīt || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तद् एवं मैत्रेयकृताक्षेपपरिहारार्थं सर्वात्मत्वप्रपञ्चनेन विष्णोरिव लक्ष्म्या अपि नित्यत्वमुक्तम्, इदानीम् आविर्भावतिरोभावाभ्यां तदुक्तं विरोधं परिहर्तुमाह इदञ्चेत्य् आदिनापूर्वं भृगुसुता सतीत्यन्तेन ग्रन्थेन । भृगुसुतापि श्रीर्यथा क्षीराब्धौ जाता इत्य् उच्यते इदञ्च शृणु । मयापि एवम् एव सन्दिहानेन मरीचेः सकाशाद् एतच् श्रुतमासीत् ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Marīci is the father of Kāśyapa. From Marīci, this account was heard in the same way.

VP 1.9.2

दुर्वासाः शङ्करस्यांशश् चचार पृथिवीम् इमाम् ।
स ददर्श स्रजं द्वियाम् ऋषिर् विद्याधरी-करे ॥२॥

Durvāsas, a portion of Śaṅkara, wandered over this earth. The sage saw a divine garland in the hand of a Vidyādhara woman.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
durvāsāḥ — Durvāsas;
śaṅkarasya aṃśaḥ — a portion of Śaṅkara;
cacāra — he wandered;
pṛthivīm imām — this earth;
saḥ — he;
dadarśa — saw;
srajam — garland;
divyām — divine;
ṛṣiḥ — the sage;
vidyādharī-kare — in the hand of a Vidyādhara woman
Śrīdhara
tatra prathamaṃ ... kāraṇam āha — first he explains the cause;
unmatta-vrata-dhṛk yogī — a yogin observing the vow of madness;
jaḍa-unmatta-piśāca iva durvāsāḥ — Durvāsas is like an inert, mad, or possessed person;
śaṅkarasya aṃśaḥ — a portion of Śaṅkara;
śakra-śāpa-sāmarthyam — the power to curse Śakra;
santānakaḥ devataruḥ — Santānaka is a celestial tree;
puṣpāṇāṃ srajam mālām — a garland of its flowers;
vanaṃ vanacāriṇām atisevyam — a forest delightful to those who roam there
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

durvāsāḥ śaṅkarasyāṃśaś cacāra pṛthivīm imām | sa dadarśa srajaṃ dviyām ṛṣir vidyādharī-kare || 2 ||

Śrīdhara

tatra prathamaṃ bhṛgusutāyāḥ satyāstirodhāne kāraṇaṃ vaktum āha—unmattavratadhṛgyogino hi jaḍonmattapiśācā iva durvāsā iti | śaṅkarasyāṃśa ity anena tasya śakraśāpasāmarthyam aparādhāsahiṣṇutvañcoktam | santāno devataruḥ, tadīyānāṃ puṣpāṇāṃ srajaṃ mālām | yasyā gandhena vāsitaṃ tad vanaṃ vanacāriṇām atisevyamabhūt || 2-3 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तत्र प्रथमं भृगुसुतायाः सत्यास्तिरोधाने कारणं वक्तुम् आह—उन्मत्तव्रतधृग्योगिनो हि जडोन्मत्तपिशाचा इव दुर्वासा इति । शङ्करस्यांश इत्य् अनेन तस्य शक्रशापसामर्थ्यम् अपराधासहिष्णुत्वञ्चोक्तम् । सन्तानो देवतरुः, तदीयानां पुष्पाणां स्रजं मालाम् । यस्या गन्धेन वासितं तद् वनं वनचारिणाम् अतिसेव्यमभूत् ॥२-३॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Durvāsas is described as observing the vow of madness; yogins may appear like inert, mad, or possessed beings. His being a portion of Śaṅkara explains his power to curse Śakra and his intolerance of offences. Santānaka is a celestial tree whose flowers made the forest fragrant and delightful.

इति सकलविभूत्यवाप्तिहेतुः
स्तुतिर् इयम् इन्द्रमुखोद्गता हि लक्ष्म्याः ।
अनुदिनम् इह पठ्यते नृभिर् यैर्
वसति न तेषु कदाचिद् अप्यलक्ष्मीः ॥१४९॥

This hymn of Lakṣmī, uttered by Indra and the cause of attainment of every prosperity, is recited daily by men; Alakṣmī never dwells in their homes.
