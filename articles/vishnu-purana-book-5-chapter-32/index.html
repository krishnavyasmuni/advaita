VP 5.32.1

प्रद्युम्नाद्या हरेः पुत्रा रुक्मिण्यां कथितास्तव । भानुभौमेरिकाद्यंश्च सत्यभामा व्यजायत

Sri Paraasharar - Earlier, I had told you about Rukmini's children, Pradyumna and others. Satyabhaama had Bhaanu, Bhoumerika, and such sons.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pradyumnādyā hareḥ putrā rukmiṇyāṃ kathitāstava . bhānubhaumerikādyāṃśca satyabhāmā vyajāyata — Sri Paraasharar - Earlier, I had told you about Rukmini's children, Pradyumna and others. Satyabhaama had Bhaanu, Bhoumerika, and such sons.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pradyumnādyā hareḥ putrā rukmiṇyāṃ kathitāstava . bhānubhaumerikādyaṃśca satyabhāmā vyajāyata

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.32.3

तनया भद्रविन्दाद्या नाग्नजित्यां महाबलाः । संग्रामजित्प्रधानास्तु शैब्यायां च हरेस्सुताः

Naagnajiti had Bhadravindhya and others, who were very powerful. Shaibyaa Mitravindaa had Sangraamajit, who was prominent and others.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tanayā bhadravindādyā nāgnajityāṃ mahābalāḥ . saṅgrāmajitpradhānāstu śaivyāyāṃ ca haressutāḥ — Naagnajiti had Bhadravindhya and others, who were very powerful. Shaibyaa Mitravindaa had Sangraamajit, who was prominent and others.

Śrīdhara

pūrvokta-putrānuvāda-pūrvakaṃ hareḥ sarvān putrān āha—pradyumnādyā ity ādi-pañcabhiḥ — Naagnajiti had Bhadravindhya and others, who were very powerful. Shaibyaa Mitravindaa had Sangraamajit, who was prominent and others.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tanayā bhadravindādyā nāgnajityāṃ mahābalāḥ . saṃgrāmajitpradhānāstu śaibyāyāṃ ca haressutāḥ

Śrīdhara

pūrvokta-putrānuvāda-pūrvakaṃ hareḥ sarvān putrān āha—pradyumnādyā ity ādi-pañcabhiḥ ||१|| dīptiman-tāmrapatrākṣādyā rohiṇyāṃ kathitā hareḥ६४९ . babhūvur jāmbavatyāṃ६५० ca sāmbādyā bāhu-śālinaḥ ||२|| *na katareṇa vyākhyātam.* tanayā bhadravindādyā nāgnajityāṃ mahā-balāḥ . saṅgrāmajit-pradhānās tu śaibyāyāṃ ca hareḥ६५१ sutāḥ ||३|| viṣṇu-cittaḥ : tanayā iti . atrāṣṭa-pradhāna-mahiṣī-madhye śaibyānām apraveśaḥ prakhyāta-putravatī-prastāvāt ||३|| śrīdharaḥ — tanayā iti . śaibyāyāṃ mitravindāyām ||३|| vṛkādyās tu sutā mādryāṃ६५२ gātravat pramukhān sutān.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पूर्वोक्त-पुत्रानुवाद-पूर्वकं हरेः सर्वान् पुत्रान् आह—प्रद्युम्नाद्या इत्य् आदि-पञ्चभिः ॥१॥ दीप्तिमन्-ताम्रपत्राक्षाद्या रोहिण्यां कथिता हरेः६४९ । बभूवुर् जाम्बवत्यां६५० च साम्बाद्या बाहु-शालिनः ॥२॥ *न कतरेण व्याख्यातम्.* तनया भद्रविन्दाद्या नाग्नजित्यां महा-बलाः । सङ्ग्रामजित्-प्रधानास् तु शैब्यायां च हरेः६५१ सुताः ॥३॥ विष्णु-चित्तः : तनया इति । अत्राष्ट-प्रधान-महिषी-मध्ये शैब्यानाम् अप्रवेशः प्रख्यात-पुत्रवती-प्रस्तावात् ॥३॥ श्रीधरः — तनया इति । शैब्यायां मित्रविन्दायाम् ॥३॥ वृकाद्यास् तु सुता माद्र्यां६५२ गात्रवत् प्रमुखान् सुतान्।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Naagnajiti had Bhadravindhya and others, who were very powerful. Shaibyaa Mitravindaa had Sangraamajit, who was prominent and others.

VP 5.32.6

प्रद्युम्नः प्रथमस्तेषां सर्वेषां रुक्मिणीसुतः । प्रद्युम्नादनिरुद्धोऽभूद्वज्रस्तस्मादजायत

Pradyumna was the eldest son of Krishna, who was born to Rukmini. Pradyumna had a son by name Aniruddha. Aniruddha had Vajra.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pradyumnaḥ prathamasteṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ rukmiṇīsutaḥ . pradyumnādaniruddho’bhūdvajrastasmādajāyata — Pradyumna was the eldest son of Krishna, who was born to Rukmini. Pradyumna had a son by name Aniruddha. Aniruddha had Vajra.

Śrīdhara

* na vyākhyātam*. pradyumnaḥ prathamas teṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ rukmiṇī-sutaḥ653 . pradyumnād aniruddho’bhūd vajras tasmād ajāyata — Pradyumna was the eldest son of Krishna, who was born to Rukmini. Pradyumna had a son by name Aniruddha. Aniruddha had Vajra.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pradyumnaḥ prathamasteṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ rukmiṇīsutaḥ . pradyumnādaniruddho’bhūdvajrastasmādajāyata

Śrīdhara

* na vyākhyātam*. pradyumnaḥ prathamas teṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ rukmiṇī-sutaḥ६५३ . pradyumnād aniruddho’bhūd vajras tasmād ajāyata ||६|| viṣṇu-cittaḥ : * na vyākhyātam.* śrīdharaḥ — aniruddhasya vivāhāntaraṃ tāvad pradyumnādy-anuvādaḥ . pradyumna iti ||६|| aniruddho raṇe’ruddho baleḥ pautrīṃ mahā-balaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

* न व्याख्यातम्*. प्रद्युम्नः प्रथमस् तेषां सर्वेषां रुक्मिणी-सुतः६५३ । प्रद्युम्नाद् अनिरुद्धोऽभूद् वज्रस् तस्माद् अजायत ॥६॥ विष्णु-चित्तः : * न व्याख्यातम्.* श्रीधरः — अनिरुद्धस्य विवाहान्तरं तावद् प्रद्युम्नाद्य्-अनुवादः । प्रद्युम्न इति ॥६॥ अनिरुद्धो रणेऽरुद्धो बलेः पौत्रीं महा-बलः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Pradyumna was the eldest son of Krishna, who was born to Rukmini. Pradyumna had a son by name Aniruddha. Aniruddha had Vajra.

VP 5.32.8

यत्र युद्धमभूद्धोरं हरिशङ्करयोर्महत् । छिन्नं सहस्रं बाहूनां यत्र बाणस्य चक्रिणा

Word-for-word

Śrīdhara

yatreti . yatra yasmin vivāhe yuddhaṃ samabhūt, yatra yuddhe bānasya bahūnāṃ sahasraṃ cakriṇā cchinnam — Baanaasura had 1000 arms, and during that marriage, there was a fierce fight between Krishna and Rudra. Baanaasura's thousand arms were all cut away by Krishna's Sudarshana Chakra.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yatra yuddhamabhūddhoraṃ hariśaṅkarayormahat . chinnaṃ sahasraṃ bāhūnāṃ yatra bāṇasya cakriṇā

Śrīdhara

yatreti . yatra yasmin vivāhe yuddhaṃ samabhūt, yatra yuddhe bānasya bahūnāṃ sahasraṃ cakriṇā cchinnam ||८|| śrī-maitreya uvāca— kathaṃ yuddham abhūd brahmann uṣārthe hara-kṛṣṇayoḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

यत्रेति । यत्र यस्मिन् विवाहे युद्धं समभूत्, यत्र युद्धे बानस्य बहूनां सहस्रं चक्रिणा च्छिन्नम् ॥८॥ श्री-मैत्रेय उवाच— कथं युद्धम् अभूद् ब्रह्मन्न् उषार्थे हर-कृष्णयोः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Baanaasura had 1000 arms, and during that marriage, there was a fierce fight between Krishna and Rudra. Baanaasura's thousand arms were all cut away by Krishna's Sudarshana Chakra.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उषेति । तद्-आश्रयां स्व-भर्त्रा सह क्रीडा-विषयां स्पृहां चक्रे ॥११॥ ततः सकल-चित्तज्ञा गौरी ताम् आह भामिनीम् । अलम् अत्यर्थ-तापेन६५७ भर्त्रा त्वम् अपि रंस्यसे ॥१२॥ *न कतरेण व्याख्यातम्.* इत्य् उक्ता सा तया चक्रे कदेति मतिम् आत्मनः । को वा भर्ता ममेत्य् आह पुनस् ताम्६५८ आह पार्वती ॥१३॥ विष्णु-चित्तः : * न व्याख्यातम्.* श्रीधरः — इतीति । इत्य् उक्ता तया गौर्या सा उषा भर्त्रा रतिर् आत्मनः कदा स्याद् ? इति मतिं चक्रे ॥१३॥ पार्वत्य् उवाच— वैशाख-शुक्ल-द्वादश्यां स्वप्ने योऽभिभवं तव ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Immediately, Ushaa asked about when will that happen, and who will be her husband. Paarvati told her thus -

VP 5.32.14

सोऽयम् इति । योग-बलाद् आकाश-गामिनी ॥२६॥ चित्रलेखोवाच— अयं कृष्णस्य पौत्रस् ते भर्ता देव्या प्रसादितः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

She said immediately - "He is the one, he is the one". Chitralekhaa had special powers, Chitralekhaa told Baana's daughter Ushaa thus -

VP 5.32.27

अयं कृष्णस्य पौत्रस्ते भर्ता देव्या प्रसादितः । अनिरुद्ध इति ख्यातः प्रख्यातः प्रियदर्शनः

Chitralekhaa - He is none other than Krishna's grandson, who has been graced to be your husband by none other than Paarvati. His name is Aniruddha, and he is very famous. He is most handsome and beautiful to look at.

VP 5.32.28

प्राप्नोषि यदि भर्तारमिमं प्राप्तं त्वयाखिलम् । दुष्प्रवेशा पुरी पूर्वं द्वारका कृष्णपालिता

If you get Aniruddha as your husband, you have got everything that is to be got. But the city of Dwaaraka which is protected by Krishna, is impossible to enter.

VP 5.32.30

अचिरादागमिष्यामि सहस्व विरहं मम । ययौ द्वारवतीं चोषां समाश्वास्य ततः सखीम्

I will come very soon. Bear till I come back. I will bring your husband here itself from there, with my yogic powers. Telling this, Chitralekhaa went to Dwaaravati, having assures Ushaa that she will bring him back here. This completes Chapter Thirty Two. We just completed Chapter 32 of Amsha 5. Where the story of Aniruddha, and Baanaasura's daughter Usha was told. Usha sees Aniruddha in her dream, and her friend Chitralekha draws the paintings, and she is able to identify him. Chitralekha has the yogic powers where she can go unseen, and bring Aniruddha, and she says that she will definitely help. And she leaves, and says that she will return very quickly. She asks Usha to bear the separation till she comes back, bringing Aniruddha. And then she goes to Dwaaravati. This concluded the Chapter Thirty Two.
