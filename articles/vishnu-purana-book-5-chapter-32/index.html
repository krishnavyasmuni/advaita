VP 5.32.1

प्रद्युम्नाद्या हरेः पुत्रा रुक्मिण्यां कथितास्तव ।
भानुभौमेरिकाद्यांश्च सत्यभामा व्यजायत

The sons of Hari, beginning with Pradyumna, born from Rukmiṇī, have been described to you; Satyabhāmā gave birth to Bhānu, Bhaumerika, and the others.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
pradyumnādyāḥ — beginning with Pradyumna;
hareḥ putrāḥ — sons of Hari;
rukmiṇyām — from Rukmiṇī;
kathitāḥ tava — have been described to you;
bhānu-bhaumerikādyāṃś ca — Bhānu, Bhaumerika, and others;
satyabhāmā — Satyabhāmā;
vyajāyata — gave birth;
Śrīdhara
pūrvokta-putrānuvāda-pūrvakam — preceded by the account of the previously mentioned sons;
hareḥ sarvān putrān āha — he speaks of all the sons of Hari;
pradyumnādyāḥ iti — “beginning with Pradyumna”;
ādi-pañcabhiḥ — with the first five verses;
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pradyumnādyā hareḥ putrā rukmiṇyāṃ kathitās tava . bhānubhaumerikādyāṃś ca satyabhāmā vyajāyata

Śrīdhara

pūrvokta-putrānuvāda-pūrvakaṃ hareḥ sarvān putrān āha—pradyumnādyā ity ādi-pañcabhiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पूर्वोक्त-पुत्रानुवाद-पूर्वकं हरेः सर्वान् पुत्रान् आह—प्रद्युम्नाद्या इत्य् आदि-पञ्चभिः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

After recounting the previously mentioned sons, he speaks of all the sons of Hari in the first five verses beginning with “Pradyumna and the others.”

VP 5.32.6

प्रद्युम्नः प्रथमस्तेषां सर्वेषां रुक्मिणीसुतः ।
प्रद्युम्नादनिरुद्धोऽभूद्वज्रस्तस्मादजायत

Pradyumna, the son of Rukmiṇī, was the first of all of them; Aniruddha was born from Pradyumna, and Vajra was born from him.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
pradyumnaḥ — Pradyumna;

Śrīdhara

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

aniruddhasya vivāhāntaraṃ tāvad pradyumnādy-anuvādaḥ . pradyumna iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अनिरुद्धस्य विवाहान्तरं तावद् प्रद्युम्नाद्य्-अनुवादः । प्रद्युम्न इति ॥६॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Until the account of Aniruddha’s marriage, the account of Pradyumna and the others continues; this is the explanation of “Pradyumna.”

Śrīdhara commentary.

“Kṛṣṇa and Rāma” means: seeing Kṛṣṇa and Rāma, who resembled her father-in-law and Pradyumna, she became as if motionless from shame.
