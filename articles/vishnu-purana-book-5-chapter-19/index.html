VP 5.19.1

एवमन्तर्जले विष्णुमभिष्टूय स यादवः । अर्चयामास सर्वेशं धूपपुष्पैर्मनोमयैः

Sri Paraasharar - Having eulogized Bhagavaan under the water like this, Akroora started to worship Him with all the dhoopa, pushpa, which were all in the mind.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evamantarjale viṣṇumabhiṣṭūya sa yādavaḥ . arcayāmāsa sarveśaṃ dhūpapuṣpairmanomayaiḥ — Sri Paraasharar - Having eulogized Bhagavaan under the water like this, Akroora started to worship Him with all the dhoopa, pushpa, which were all in the mind.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evamantarjale viṣṇumabhiṣṭūya sa yādavaḥ . arcayāmāsa sarveśaṃ dhūpapuṣpairmanomayaiḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.19.2

परित्यक्तान्यविषयो मनस्तत्र निवेश्य सः । ब्रह्मभूते चिरं स्थित्वा विरराम समाधितः

Having given up the thought about anything else, focussing his mind completely on the Supreme Form, and the wonderful experience that he was having there, he was meditating and entered into the Samaadhi state and was there for a long time.

VP 5.19.4

ददर्श रामकृष्णौ च यथापुर्वमवस्थितौ । विस्मिताक्षस्तदाऽक्रूरस्तं च कृष्णोऽभ्यभाषत

Akroora, when he came back again and saw Balaraama and Krishna who were sitting there in the chariot as before, was completely filled with surprise, and his eyes widened. Seeing him, Krishna told thus.

Transliteration

VP 5.19.5

नूनं ते दृष्टमाश्चर्यमक्रूर यमुनाजले । विस्मयोत्फुल्लनयनो भवान्संलक्ष्यते यतः

Akroora, when he came back again and saw Balaraama and Krishna who were sitting there in the chariot as before, was completely filled with surprise, and his eyes widened. Seeing him, Krishna told thus. Sri Krishna - I can see the joy in your face. Your eyes are broad, and you are full of surprise; you must have seen some wonderful sight inside the river water.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nūnaṃ te dṛṣṭamāścaryamakrūra yamunājale . vismayotphullanayano bhavānsaṃlakṣyate yataḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.19.6

VP 5.19.7

जगदेतन्महाश्चर्यरूपं यस्य महात्मनः । तेनाश्चर्यपरेणाहं भवता कृष्ण संगतः

This whole world is whose most wonderful form, of such a Supreme Being, of that most wonderful person, O Krishna. You who are such a most wonderful person, I am again united with You.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Śrīdhara

jagad iti . tenāścarya-pareṇa bhagavataiva aham antar-jale cātra ca saṅgato’smi . ato jala eva viśeṣeṇāścaryaṃ kiṃ pṛcchāmīty arthaḥ — This whole world is whose most wonderful form, of such a Supreme Being, of that most wonderful person, O Krishna. You who are such a most wonderful person, I am again united with You.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jagadetanmahāścaryarūpaṃ yasya mahātmanaḥ . tenāścaryapareṇāhaṃ bhavatā kṛṣṇa saṃgataḥ

Śrīdhara

jagad iti . tenāścarya-pareṇa bhagavataiva aham antar-jale cātra ca saṅgato’smi . ato jala eva viśeṣeṇāścaryaṃ kiṃ pṛcchāmīty arthaḥ ||७|| tat kim etena mathurāṃ yāsyāmo३९५ madhusūdana .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जगद् इति । तेनाश्चर्य-परेण भगवतैव अहम् अन्तर्-जले चात्र च सङ्गतोऽस्मि । अतो जल एव विशेषेणाश्चर्यं किं पृच्छामीत्य् अर्थः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

This whole world is whose most wonderful form, of such a Supreme Being, of that most wonderful person, O Krishna. You who are such a most wonderful person, I am again united with You.

VP 5.19.8

तत्किमेतेन मथुरां यास्यामो मधुसूदन । बिभेमि कंसाद्धिग्जन्म परपिण्डोपजीविनाम्

Those who live doing service to others, I hate such a life, and there should be never people like that; they are of no use. I am scared of Kamsa, as he is waiting for me, and I cannot delay any more. What to do with all these things, let us go fast to Mathura. This is not the right place to discuss about tattva, etc. Because I am really delayed and scared of Kamsa to whom I am subordinate. If I am delayed, he will be very angry. Though I perceived You directly with my eyes, I am not able to speak to You, I have to rush and get back to Mathura, being scared of Kamsa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatkimetena mathurāṃ yāsyāmo madhusūdana . bibhemi kaṃsāddhigjanma parapiṇḍopajīvinām — Those who live doing service to others, I hate such a life, and there should be never people like that; they are of no use. I am scared of Kamsa, as he is waiting for me, and I cannot delay any more. What to do with all these things, let us go fast to Mathura. This is not the right place to discuss about tattva, etc. Because I am really delayed and scared of Kamsa to whom I am subordinate. If I am delayed, he will be very angry. Though I perceived You directly with my eyes, I am not able to speak to You, I have to rush and get back to Mathura, being scared of Kamsa.

Śrīdhara

rathe sthitaṃ māṃ jale sthitaḥ tvaṃ kathaṃ dṛṣṭavān ? iti mohayantam evāha—tat kim etena ? iti . parameśvare sākṣād dṛṣṭe’pi, vilambe sati kaṃsaḥ kupyed iti kaṃsād bibhemi . ataḥ para-piṇḍam anyair dattam annam upajīvanti ye, teṣāṃ janma dhik — Those who live doing service to others, I hate such a life, and there should be never people like that; they are of no use. Though I perceived You directly with my eyes, I am not able to speak to You, I have to rush and get back to Mathura, being scared of Kamsa.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatkimetena mathurāṃ yāsyāmo madhusūdana . bibhemi kaṃsāddhigjanma parapiṇḍopajīvinām

Śrīdhara

rathe sthitaṃ māṃ jale sthitaḥ tvaṃ kathaṃ dṛṣṭavān ? iti mohayantam evāha—tat kim etena ? iti . parameśvare sākṣād dṛṣṭe’pi, vilambe sati kaṃsaḥ kupyed iti kaṃsād bibhemi . ataḥ para-piṇḍam anyair dattam annam upajīvanti ye, teṣāṃ janma dhik ||८|| ity uktvā codayāmāsasa३९६ hayān vāta-raṃhasaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

What to do with all these things, let us go fast to Mathura. This is not the right place to discuss about tattva, etc. Because I am really delayed and scared of Kamsa to whom I am subordinate. VP 5.19.9

इत्युक्त्वा चोदयामास स हयान् वातरंहसः । संप्राप्तश्चापि सायाह्ने सोऽक्रूरो मथुरां पुरीम्

Śrīdhara

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.19.10
