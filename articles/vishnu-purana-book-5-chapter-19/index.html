VP 5.19.1

एवम् अन्तर् जले विष्णुम् अभिष्टूय स यादवः।
अर्चयामास सर्वेशं धूप-पुष्पैर् मनो-मयैः

Sri Paraasharar - Having eulogized Bhagavaan under the water like this, Akroora started to worship Him with all the dhoopa, pushpa, which were all in the mind.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
evam antar-jale viṣṇum — thus Viṣṇu in the water;
abhiṣṭūya sa yādavaḥ — having praised, that Yādava;
arcayām āsa sarveśam — worshipped the Lord of all;
dhūpa-puṣpaiḥ manomayaiḥ — with incense and mental flowers
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evamantarjale viṣṇumabhiṣṭūya sa yādavaḥ . arcayāmāsa sarveśaṃ dhūpapuṣpairmanomayaiḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.19.2

परित्यक्तान्यविषयो मनस्तत्र निवेश्य सः।
ब्रह्मभूते चिरं स्थित्वा विरराम समाधितः

Having given up the thought about anything else, focussing his mind completely on the Supreme Form, and the wonderful experience that he was having there, he was meditating and entered into the Samaadhi state and was there for a long time.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
parityakta-anya-viṣayaḥ — abandoning other objects;
manaḥ tatra niveśya — placing the mind there;
brahma-bhūte ciram sthitvā — remaining long in Brahman;
virarāma samādhitaḥ — rested in meditation
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

parityaktānyaviṣayo manastatra niveśya saḥ . brahmabhūte ciraṃ sthitvā virarāma samādhitaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.19.3

कृतकृत्यमिवात्मानं मन्यमानो महामतिः।
आजगाम रथं भूयो निर्गम्य यमुनांभसः

After a long time, he thought that "I have done what I ought to have done", and again, he got up and came back to the chariot having got up from the waters of Yamunaa river.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
kṛta-kṛtyam iva ātmānam — himself as if having done what must be done;
manyamānaḥ mahā-matiḥ — thinking, the great-minded one;
ājagāma ratham bhūyaḥ — came again to the chariot;
nirgamya yamunā-ambhasaḥ — emerging from Yamunā’s water
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṛtakṛtyamivātmānaṃ manyamāno mahāmatiḥ . ājagāma rathaṃ bhūyo nirgamya yamunāṃbhasaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.19.4

ददर्श रामकृष्णौ च यथापुर्वमवस्थितौ।
विस्मिताक्षस्तदाऽक्रूरस्तं च कृष्णोऽभ्यभाषत

Akroora, when he came back again and saw Balaraama and Krishna who were sitting there in the chariot as before, was completely filled with surprise, and his eyes widened. Seeing him, Krishna told thus.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
dadarśa rāma-kṛṣṇau — he saw Rāma and Kṛṣṇa;
yathā-pūrvam avasthitau — standing as before;
vismita-akṣaḥ tadā akrūraḥ — then Akrūra, eyes amazed;
tam ca kṛṣṇaḥ abhyabhāṣata — and Kṛṣṇa addressed him
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dadarśa rāmakṛṣṇau ca yathāpurvamavasthitau . vismitākṣastadā’krūrastaṃ ca kṛṣṇo’bhyabhāṣata

Śrīdhara

dadarśeti . vismite akṣiṇī yasya sa tathābhūt ||४|| śrī-kṛṣṇa uvāca— nūnaṃ te dṛṣṭam āścaryam akrūra yamunā-jale .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ददर्शेति । विस्मिते अक्षिणी यस्य स तथाभूत् ॥४॥

श्री-कृष्ण उवाच—

Śrīdhara commentary.

Akroora, when he came back again and saw Balaraama and Krishna who were sitting there in the chariot as before, was completely filled with surprise, and his eyes widened. Seeing him, Krishna told thus.

VP 5.19.5

श्रीकृष्ण उवाच
नूनं ते दृष्टमाश्चर्यमक्रूर यमुनाजले।
विस्मयोत्फुल्लनयनो भवान्संलक्ष्यते यतः

Akroora, when he came back again and saw Balaraama and Krishna who were sitting there in the chariot as before, was completely filled with surprise, and his eyes widened. Seeing him, Krishna told thus. Sri Krishna - I can see the joy in your face. Your eyes are broad, and you are full of surprise; you must have seen some wonderful sight inside the river water.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
nūnam te dṛṣṭam āścaryam — surely you have seen a wonder;
akrūra yamunā-jale — O Akrūra, in Yamunā’s water;
vismaya-utphulla-nayanaḥ — eyes opened in amazement;
bhavān saṃlakṣyate yataḥ — you are seen to be such
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nūnaṃ te dṛṣṭamāścaryamakrūra yamunājale . vismayotphullanayano bhavānsaṃlakṣyate yataḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.19.6

अक्रूर उवाच
अन्तर्जले यदाश्चर्यं दृष्टं तत्र मयाच्युत।
तदत्रापि हि पश्यामि मूर्तिमत्पुरतः स्थितम्

Akroora - O Krishna, Achyuta. Whatever the wonderful sight that I saw, that wonderful vision of Yours under the water, the same thing, I am seeing here also. And it has taken a real form here. I am seeing in front of my eyes.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
antar-jale yat āścaryam — the wonder in the water;
dṛṣṭam tatra mayā acyuta — seen there by me, O Acyuta;
tat atra api hi paśyāmi — that here too I see;
mūrti-mat purataḥ sthitam — embodied, standing before me
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

antarjale yadāścaryaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ tatra mayācyuta . tadatrāpi hi paśyāmi mūrtimatpurataḥ sthitam

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.19.7

जगद् एतन् महाश्चर्य-रूपं यस्य महात्मनः।
तेनाश्चर्य-परेणाहं भवता कृष्ण सङ्गतः

This whole world is whose most wonderful form, of such a Supreme Being, of that most wonderful person, O Krishna. You who are such a most wonderful person, I am again united with You.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
jagat etat mahā-āścarya-rūpam — this world, of great-wonder form;
yasya mahātmanaḥ — of the great Self;
tena āścarya-pareṇa aham — with that wonder-filled one I;
bhavatā kṛṣṇa saṅgataḥ — have met you, Kṛṣṇa
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

jagadetanmahāścaryarūpaṃ yasya mahātmanaḥ . tenāścaryapareṇāhaṃ bhavatā kṛṣṇa saṃgataḥ

Śrīdhara

jagad iti . tenāścarya-pareṇa bhagavataiva aham antar-jale cātra ca saṅgato’smi . ato jala eva viśeṣeṇāścaryaṃ kiṃ pṛcchāmīty arthaḥ ||७|| tat kim etena mathurāṃ yāsyāmo madhusūdana .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

जगद् इति । तेनाश्चर्य-परेण भगवतैव अहम् अन्तर्-जले चात्र च सङ्गतोऽस्मि । अतो जल एव विशेषेणाश्चर्यं किं पृच्छामीत्य् अर्थः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

This whole world is whose most wonderful form, of such a Supreme Being, of that most wonderful person, O Krishna. You who are such a most wonderful person, I am again united with You.

VP 5.19.8

तत् किम् एतेन मथुरां यास्यामो मधुसूदन।
बिभेमि कंसाद् धिग् जन्म पर-पिण्डोपजीविनाम्

Those who live doing service to others, I hate such a life, and there should be never people like that; they are of no use. I am scared of Kamsa, as he is waiting for me, and I cannot delay any more. What to do with all these things, let us go fast to Mathura. This is not the right place to discuss about tattva, etc. Because I am really delayed and scared of Kamsa to whom I am subordinate. If I am delayed, he will be very angry. Though I perceived You directly with my eyes, I am not able to speak to You, I have to rush and get back to Mathura, being scared of Kamsa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tat kim etena mathurāṃ yāsyāmaḥ — why with this [delay] shall we go to Mathurā;
madhusūdana — O Madhusūdana;
bibhemi kaṃsāt — I fear Kamsa;
dhik janma para-piṇḍa-upajīvinām — fie on the birth of those living on another’s food
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tatkimetena mathurāṃ yāsyāmo madhusūdana . bibhemi kaṃsāddhigjanma parapiṇḍopajīvinām

Śrīdhara

rathe sthitaṃ māṃ jale sthitaḥ tvaṃ kathaṃ dṛṣṭavān ? iti mohayantam evāha—tat kim etena ? iti . parameśvare sākṣād dṛṣṭe’pi, vilambe sati kaṃsaḥ kupyed iti kaṃsād bibhemi . ataḥ para-piṇḍam anyair dattam annam upajīvanti ye, teṣāṃ janma dhik ||८|| ity uktvā codayāmāsasa hayān vāta-raṃhasaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

रथे स्थितं मां जले स्थितः त्वं कथं दृष्टवान् ? इति मोहयन्तम् एवाह—तत् किम् एतेन ? इति । परमेश्वरे साक्षाद् दृष्टेऽपि, विलम्बे सति कंसः कुप्येद् इति कंसाद् बिभेमि । अतः पर-पिण्डम् अन्यैर् दत्तम् अन्नम् उपजीवन्ति ये, तेषां जन्म धिक् ॥८॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Those who live doing service to others, I hate such a life, and there should be never people like that; they are of no use. I am scared of Kamsa, as he is waiting for me, and I cannot delay any more. What to do with all these things, let us go fast to Mathura. This is not the right place to discuss about tattva, etc. Because I am really delayed and scared of Kamsa to whom I am subordinate. If I am delayed, he will be very angry. Though I perceived You directly with my eyes, I am not able to speak to You, I have to rush and get back to Mathura, being scared of Kamsa.

VP 5.19.9

इत्य् उक्त्वा चोदयामासस हयान् वात-रंहसः।
सम्प्राप्तश् चापि सायाह्ने सोऽक्रूरो मथुरां पुरीम्

Having told this, Akroora started to ride the chariot, the horses of which were moving very fast. By evening, he reached Mathura Puri.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
iti uktvā codhayām āsa — saying this, he urged;
sa hayān vāta-raṃhasaḥ — the horses swift as wind;
saṃprāptaḥ api sāyāhne — having arrived in the evening;
saḥ akrūraḥ mathurāṃ purīm — that Akrūra at Mathurā city
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityuktvā codayāmāsa sa hayān vātaraṃhasaḥ . saṃprāptaścāpi sāyāhne so’krūro mathurāṃ purīm

Śrīdhara

itīti . vātasyeva raṃho vego yeṣāṃ tān ||९|| vilokya mathurāṃ kṛṣṇaṃ rāmaṃ cāha sa yādavaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इतीति । वातस्येव रंहो वेगो येषां तान् ॥९॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Having told this, Akroora started to ride the chariot, the horses of which were moving very fast. By evening, he reached Mathura Puri.

VP 5.19.10

विलोक्य मथुरां कृष्णं रामं चाह स यादवः।
पद्य्भां यातं महावीरौ रथेनैको विशाम्यहम्

Having reached Mathura Puri by evening, Akroora told Balaraama and Krishna that he has to go alone in the chariot, and They have to come by walk.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
vilokya mathurāṃ kṛṣṇam — seeing Mathurā, Kṛṣṇa;
rāmaṃ ca āha sa yādavaḥ — and Rāma, that Yādava said;
padbhyāṃ yātam mahā-vīrau — the two great heroes should go on foot;
rathena ekaḥ viśāmy aham — I alone shall enter by chariot
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vilokya mathurāṃ kṛṣṇaṃ rāmaṃ cāha sa yādavaḥ . padybhāṃ yātaṃ mahāvīrau rathenaiko viśāmyaham

Śrīdhara

vilokyeti . kṛṣṇa-māyā-mohitaḥ san kaṃsa-bhayād iva tau śikṣayann āha—padbhyām iti . padbhyāṃ yuvāṃ yātam āgacchatam ||१०|| gantavyaṃ vasudevasya no bhavadbhyāṃ tathā gṛhaṃ.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

विलोक्येति । कृष्ण-माया-मोहितः सन् कंस-भयाद् इव तौ शिक्षयन्न् आह—पद्भ्याम् इति । पद्भ्यां युवां यातम् आगच्छतम् ॥१०॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Having reached Mathura Puri by evening, Akroora told Balaraama and Krishna that he has to go alone in the chariot, and They have to come by walk.

VP 5.19.11

गन्तव्यं वसुदेवस्य नो भवद्भ्यां तथा गृहम्।
युवयोर्हि कृते वृद्धस्स कंसेन निरस्यते

He warned them - "Please do not go to Vasudeva's house; because of You two only, that old man is being troubled by Kamsa".

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

naḥ bhavadbhyām tathā gṛham — our house, by you two likewise;
yuva-yoḥ hi kṛte vṛddhaḥ — for your sake the old one;
sa kaṃsena nirasyate — is rejected by Kamsa
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gantavyaṃ vasudevasya no bhavadbhyāṃ tathā gṛham . yuvayorhi kṛte vṛddhassa kaṃsena nirasyate

Śrīdhara

gantavyam iti . yuvayoḥ kṛte yuṣman-nimittaṃ nirasyate ākṣipyate ||११|| śrī-parāśara uvāca— ity uktvā praviveśātha so’krūro mathurāṃ purīm .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

गन्तव्यम् इति । युवयोः कृते युष्मन्-निमित्तं निरस्यते आक्षिप्यते ॥११॥

श्री-पराशर उवाच—

Śrīdhara commentary.

He warned them - "Please do not go to Vasudeva's house; because of You two only, that old man is being troubled by Kamsa".

VP 5.19.14

VP 5.19.15

कंसस्य रजकस्सोऽथ प्रसादारूढविस्मयः।
बहून्याक्षेपवाक्यानि प्राहोच्चै रामकेशवौ

The washerman started to shout at Balaraama and Krishna, as he was very arrogant, being very close to Kamsa, and Kamsa had been rewarding him with many gifts as he had done Kamsa's work. With this arrogance, he ridiculed Them and shouted at Them with bad words in loud voice.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
kaṃsasya rajakaḥ saḥ atha — then he, Kamsa’s washerman;
prasāda-ārūḍha-vismayaḥ — astonishment mounted by arrogance;
bahūni ākṣepa-vākyāni — many insulting words;
prāha uccaiḥ rāma-keśavau — spoke loudly to Rāma and Keśava
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kaṃsasya rajakasso’tha prasādārūḍhavismayaḥ . bahūnyākṣepavākyāni prāhoccai rāmakeśavau

Śrīdhara

kaṃsasyeti . prasādārūḍha-vismayaḥrājñaḥ prasādena garvam ārūḍhaḥ ||१५|| tatas tala-prahāreṇakṛṣṇas tasya durātmanaḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कंसस्येति । प्रसादारूढ-विस्मयःराज्ञः प्रसादेन गर्वम् आरूढः ॥१५॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

The washerman started to shout at Balaraama and Krishna, as he was very arrogant, being very close to Kamsa, and Kamsa had been rewarding him with many gifts as he had done Kamsa's work. With this arrogance, he ridiculed Them and shouted at Them with bad words in loud voice.

VP 5.19.16

ततस्तलप्रहारेण कृष्णस्तस्य दुरात्मनः।
पातयामास रोषेण रजकस्य शिरो भुवि

Immediately, Krishna hit him on the head, and immediately, that washerman was down and was killed.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tataḥ tala-prahāreṇa — then with a blow of the palm;
kṛṣṇaḥ tasya durātmanaḥ — Kṛṣṇa, of that wicked one;
pātayām āsa roṣeṇa — caused to fall;
rajakasya śiraḥ bhuvi — the washerman’s head to the ground
