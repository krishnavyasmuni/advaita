VP 5.25.1

वने विहरतस् तस्य सह गोपैर् महात्मनः।
मानुष-च्छद्म-रूपस्य शेषस्य धरणी-भृतः

Sri Paraasharar - Balaraama was wandering around in Gokula with all the gopaas. He was taking the form of a human being, He is actually the one who is holding the entire earth, He is Shesha, Aadishesha.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
vane vicarataḥ tasya — while he wandered in the forest;
saha gopaiḥ mahātmanaḥ — with the great-souled cowherds;
mānuṣa-chadma-rūpasya — in human-disguise form;
śeṣasya dharaṇī-dhṛtaḥ — of Śeṣa, bearer of the earth
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vane vicaratastasya saha gopairmahātmanaḥ . mānuṣacchadmarūpasya śeṣasya dharaṇīdhṛtaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.2

निष्पादितोरुकार्यस्य कार्येणोर्वीप्रचारिणः।
उपभोगार्थमत्यर्थं वरुणः प्राह वारुणीम्

With His great act, Balaraama, had executed all the things in order to help the gods. He had incarnated on earth for the sake of the gods, and had done some great acts here. For His enjoyment, Varuna called Vaaruni, Madiraa and told her thus.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
niṣpāditoru-kāryasya — whose great work was completed;
kāryeṇorvī-pracāriṇaḥ — whose work is to move upon the earth;
upabhogārtham atyartham — for abundant enjoyment;
varuṇaḥ prāha vāruṇīm — Varuṇa addressed Vāruṇī
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

niṣpāditorukāryasya kāryeṇorvīpracāriṇaḥ . upabhogārthamatyarthaṃ varuṇaḥ prāha vāruṇīm

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.3

अभीष्टां सर्वदा ह्य् अस्य मदिरे त्वं महौजसः।
अनन्तस्योपभोगाय तस्य गच्छ मुदे शुभे

O Madire, go and give Him all the enjoyments and pleasures, for the enjoyment of Ananta, go happily and please Him. Let Him be pleased.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
abhīṣṭā sarvadā yasya — who is always desired;
madire — O liquor;
tvaṃ mahaujasaḥ — you of great potency;
anantasya upabhogāya — for Ananta’s enjoyment;
tasya gaccha — go to him;
mude śubhe — for auspicious joy
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

abhīṣṭā sarvadā yasya madire tvaṃ mahaujasaḥ . anantasyopabhogāya tasya gaccha mude śubhe

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.4

इत्य् उक्ता वारुणी तेन सन्निधानम् अथाकरोत्।
वृन्दावन-समुत्पन्न-कदम्ब-तरु-कोटरे

Having been told like that, Madiraa comes and lives in all the hollows of the Kadamba trees of Brundaavana.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
iti uktā vāruṇī tena — thus addressed, Vāruṇī by him;
sannidhānam atha akarot — then made her presence;
vṛndāvana-samutpanna — arisen in Vṛndāvana;
kadamba-taru-koṭare — in a hollow of a Kadamba tree
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityuktā vāruṇī tena sannidhānamathākaret . vṛndāvanasamutpannakadaṃ batarukoṭare

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.5

विचरन् बलदेवोऽपि मदिरागन्धमुत्तमम्।
आघ्राय मदिरातर्षमवापाथ वराननः

When Balaraama was wandering around in Vrundaavana, he sensed the fragrance of this Madiraa, which is there in the Kadamba trees hollows. Having sensed this, He liked it very much, and He wanted to drink that Madiraa.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
vicaran baladevaḥ api — wandering, Baladeva too;
madirā-gandham uttamam — the excellent fragrance of liquor;
āghrāya — having smelled;
madirā-tarṣam avāpa — obtained thirst for liquor;
atha varānanaḥ — then, O beautiful-faced one
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vicaran baladevo’pi madirāgandhamuttamam . āghrāya madirātarṣamavāpātha varānanaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.6

ततः कदम्बात् सहसा मद्य-धारां स लाङ्गली।
पतन्तीं वीक्ष्य मैत्रेय प्रययौ परमां मुदम्

Immediately from the Kadamba tree, the Madiraa was flowing incessantly, and He was extremely pleased.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tataḥ kadambāt sahasā — then suddenly from the Kadamba;
madyadhārāṃ sa lāṅgalī — that plough-bearer, a stream of wine;
patantīṃ vīkṣya — seeing as it fell;
maitreya prayayau paramāṃ mudam — Maitreya, went to great joy
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataḥ kadaṃbātsahasā madyadhārāṃ sa lāṅgalī . patantīṃ vīkṣya maitreya prayayau paramāṃ mudam

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.7

पपौ च गोपगोपीभिः समुपेतो मुदाऽन्वितः।
प्रगीयमानो ललितं गीतवाद्यविशारदैः

He went and drank that Madiraa with all the gopaas and gopis, and being very happy and enjoying it. They were all singing all the beautiful songs with all instruments.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
papau ca gopa-gopībhiḥ — and drank with cowherd men and women;
samupetaḥ mudānvitaḥ — accompanied, filled with joy;
pragīyamānaḥ lalitam — while melodiously sung;
gīta-vādya-viśāradaiḥ — by experts in song and instruments
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

papau ca gopagopībhiḥ samupeto mudā’nvitaḥ . pragīyamāno lalitaṃ gītavādyaviśāradaiḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.8

स मत्तोऽत्यन्तघर्मांभःकणिकामौक्तिकोज्ज्वलः।
आगच्छ यमुने स्नातुमिच्छामीत्याह विह्वलः

At that time, He was very intoxicated by the drinking of the Madiraa, and drops of sweat were there on His head, like pearls, because of the heat. He called Yamunaa, "O Yamunaa river, come here, I want to take bath".

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
sa mattaḥ atyanta-gharmāmbhaḥ — he, intoxicated, with drops of perspiration;
kaṇikā-mauktikojjvalaḥ — shining like pearl-drops;
āgaccha yamune snātum — come, Yamunā, to bathe;
icchāmi iti āha vihvalaḥ — I wish, he said, agitated
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sa matto’tyantagharmāṃbhaḥkaṇikāmauktikojjvalaḥ . āgaccha yamune snātumicchāmītyāha vihvalaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.9

तस्य वाचं नदी सा तु मत्तोक्तामवमत्य वै।
नाजगाम ततः क्त्रुद्धो हलं जग्राह लाङ्गली

The Yamunaa river saw that He was intoxicated, and not in His proper senses, so she just neglected what He said. Balaraama got very angry, and took His halaayudha, His great plough.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tasya vācaṃ nadī sā tu — that river, his words;
matta-uktām avamatya vai — disregarding as the speech of an intoxicated one;
na ājagāma — did not come;
tataḥ kruddhaḥ halaṃ jagrāha — then angry, he seized the plough;
lāṅgalī — the plough-bearer
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tasya vācaṃ nadī sā tu mattoktāmavamatya vai . nājagāma tataḥ ktruddho halaṃ jagrāha lāṅgalī

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.10

गृहीत्वा तां हलान्तेन चकर्ष मदविह्वलः।
पापे नायासि नायासि गम्यतामिच्छयाऽन्यतः

"O evil minded Yamunaa, go anywhere if you are capable", and He dragged Yamunaa with the end of His great plough.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
gṛhītvā tām halāntena — taking her with the end of the plough;
cakarṣa madavihvalaḥ — he dragged, bewildered by intoxication;
pāpe na āyāsi — O sinful one, you do not come;
na āyāsi — you do not come;
gamyatām icchayā anyataḥ — go elsewhere as you wish
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

gṛhītvā tāṃ halāntena cakarṣa madavihvalaḥ . pāpe nāyāsi nāyāsi gamyatāmicchayā’nyataḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.11

साकृष्टा सहसा तेन मार्गं संत्यज्य निम्नगा।
यत्रास्ते बलभद्रोऽसौ प्लावयामास तद्वनम्

When Yamunaa was dragged by Balaraama with His halaayudha, she started flowing and flooded all the Brundaavana there. The whole Brundaavana was filled with Yamunaa there.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
sā ākarṣṭā sahasā — she, suddenly dragged;
mārgaṃ santyajya — abandoning her course;
nimnagā — the river;
yatra āste balabhadraḥ asau — where that Balabhadra was;
plāvayām āsa tat vanam — flooded that forest
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sākṛṣṭā sahasā tena mārgaṃ saṃtyajya nimnagā . yatrāste balabhadro’sau plāvayāmāsa tadvanam

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.12

शरीरिणी तदाऽभ्येत्य त्रासविह्वललोचना।
प्रसीदेत्यब्रवीद्रामं मुञ्च मां मुसलायुध

Immediately, she took on a form, and was extremely terrified, and her eyes were all upwards. She pleads to Balaraama "Be pleased and don't trouble me, please leave me, O Musalaayudha".

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
śarīriṇī tadā abhyetya — then, assuming a bodily form, approaching;
trāsa-vihvala-locanā — eyes bewildered with fear;
prasīda iti abravīt rāmam — “be gracious,” she said to Rāma;
muñca māṃ musalāyudha — release me, O club-wielder
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śarīriṇī tadā’bhyetya trāsavihvalalocanā . prasīdetyabravīdrāmaṃ muñca māṃ musalāyudha

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.13

ततस्तस्याः सुवचनमाकर्ण्य स हलायुधः।
सोऽब्रवीदवजानासि मम शौर्यबले नदि।
सोऽहं त्वां हलपातेन नयिष्यामि सहस्रधा

Balaraama was pleased with the nice words of Yamunaa, and said that she was disrespecting and neglecting Him, and she does not know His powers. "I am going to break you into a thousand parts with My halaayudha".

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
saḥ abravīt avajānāsi — he said, you disregard;
mama śaurya-bale nadi — my heroic strength, O river;
saḥ aham tvāṃ halapātena — I shall, with a stroke of the plough;
nayiṣyāmi sahasradhā — carry you into a thousand channels
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

so’bravīdavajānāsi mama śauryabale nadi . so’haṃ tvāṃ halapātena nayiṣyāmi sahasradhā

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.14

इत्य् उक्तयातिसन्त्रसात् तया नद्या प्रसादितः।
भू-भागे प्लाविते तस्मिन्५७६ मुमोच यमुनां बलः

Sri Paraasharar - When she pleaded further and was terrified, Balaraama was also pleased, and He calmed down, and when that whole area was filled with Yamunaa river, He left her at that time.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
iti uktayā ati-saṃtrāsāt — thus addressed, from great fear;
tayā nadyā prasāditaḥ — propitiated by that river;
bhū-bhāge plāvite tasmin — that ground having been flooded;
mumoca yamunāṃ balaḥ — Bala released Yamunā
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ityuktayā’tisaṃtrāsāttayā nadya prasāditaḥ . bhūbhāge plāvite tasminmumoca yamunāṃ balaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.15

ततः स्नातस्य वै कान्तिरजायत महात्मनः

He took bath in that Yamunaa river, wherever He was sitting, as the Yamunaa had come there. Having taken bath, He was radiant.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tataḥ snātasya vai kāntiḥ — then, of him after bathing, radiance;
ajāyata mahātmanaḥ — arose, of the great-souled one
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tataḥ snātasya vai kāntirajāyata mahātmanaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.16

वरुणप्रहितां चास्मै मालामम्लानपङ्कजाम्।
समुद्राभे तथा वस्त्रे नीले लक्ष्मीरयच्छत

Taking a beautiful Karna pushpa, Lakshmi comes there, with one beautiful kundala, earrings, and whatever Varuna had given her, having a lotus garland which never withers, and with wonderful blue clothes which had the radiance of the ocean, Lakshmi came and gave Him all these things. This Lakshmi is different. This is as per the Lakshmi vachana from Harivamsha. Balaraama's Devis are told to be three shaktis - Vaaruni, Kaanti and Lakshmi.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
varuṇa-prahitāṃ ca asmai — sent by Varuṇa to him;
mālāṃ amlāna-paṅkajām — a garland of unfaded lotuses;
samudrābhe tathā vastre — and garments like the ocean in hue;
nīle lakṣmīḥ ayacchat — Lakṣmī gave blue ones
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

varuṇaprahitāṃ cāsmai mālāmamlānapaṅkajām . samudrābhe tathā vastre nīle lakṣmīrayacchata

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.17

कृतावतंसः स तदा चारुकुंडलभूषितः।
नीलांबरधरःस्रग्वी शुशुभे कान्तिसंयुतः

Having decorated Himself with all the beautiful and wonderful kundalaas, the Karna pushpa, the Neelaambara, and the wonderful lotus garland, Balaraama was shining forth with radiance, with great kaanti.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

cāru-kuṇḍala-bhūṣitaḥ — adorned with beautiful earrings;
nīlāmbara-dharaḥ sragvī — wearing blue garments and a garland;
śuśubhe kānti-saṃyutaḥ — he shone, endowed with radiance
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kṛtāvataṃsaḥ sa tadā cārukuṃḍalabhūṣitaḥ . nīlāṃbaradharaḥsragvī śuśubhe kāntisaṃyutaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

VP 5.25.18

VP 5.25.19

रेवतीं नाम तनयां रैवतस्य महीपतेः।
उपयेमे बलस्तस्यां जज्ञाते निशितोल्मुकौ

He then married Revati, who was daughter of King Raivata. Through her, he got two sons, Nishita and Ulmuka. This completed Chapter Twenty Five. The story of Krishna and Rukmini will be told very briefly in the next chapter. We just concluded Chapter 25 of Amsha 5.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
revatīṃ nāma tanayām — the daughter named Revatī;
raivatasya mahīpateḥ — of King Raivata;
upayeme balaḥ tasyām — Bala married her;
jajñāte niṣaṭholmukau — Niṣaṭha and Ulmuka were born
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

revatīṃ nāma tanayāṃ raivatasya mahīpateḥ . upayeme balastasyāṃ jajñāte niśitolmukau

Śrīdhara commentary.
