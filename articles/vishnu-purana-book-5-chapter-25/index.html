VP 5.25.1

वने विचरतस्तस्य सह गोपैर्महात्मनः

श्री पराशरः - र्वने वर्वचरतः तस्य सह गोपैः महात्मनः | मानुर्षच्छद्मरूपस्य शेर्षस्य धरणीधृतः ॥ १ ॥ Sri Paraasharar - Balaraama was wandering around in Gokula with all the gopaas. He was taking the form of a human being, He is actually the one who is holding the entire earth, He is Shesha, Aadishesha.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

atha — -; pañacamāṃśe — -; pañacaviṃśo'dhayāyaḥ — wandering; vane — with; vicaratasatasaya — gopaas; saha — the; gopairamahātamanaḥ — a; . — is; mānuṣacachadamarūpasaya — one; śeṣasaya — the; dharaṇīdhaṛtaḥ — He

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

vane vicaratastasya saha gopairmahātmanaḥ

Śrīdhara commentary.

No commentary.

VP 5.25.2

निष्पादितोरुकार्यस्य कार्येणोर्वीप्रचारिणः

With His great act, Balaraama, had executed all the things in order to help the gods. He had incarnated on earth for the sake of the gods, and had done some great acts here. For His enjoyment, Varuna called Vaaruni, Madiraa and told her thus.

VP 5.25.3

अभीष्टा सर्वदा यस्य मदिरे त्वं महौजसः

अभीषठा सर्विदा यस्य महदरे त्र्वां महौजसः | अनन्तस्य उपभोगाय तस्य गच्च मुदे शुभे ॥ ३ ॥ O Madire, go and give Him all the enjoyments and pleasures, for the enjoyment of Ananta, go happily and please Him. Let Him be pleased.

VP 5.25.5

VP 5.25.8
