Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Book 2, Chapter 9

Planetary system, under the type of a Śiśumára or porpoise. The earth nourished by the sun. Of rain whilst the sun shines. Of rain from clouds. Rain the support, of vegetation, and thence of animal life. Náráyańa the support of all beings.

VP 2.9.1

तारामयं भगवतः शिशुमाराकृति प्रभोः । दिवि रूपं हरेर्यत्तु तस्य पुच्छे स्थितो ध्रुवः

Sri Paraasharar - The entire nakshatra mandala is in the form of an animal called Shimshumaara, which is a kind of whale, mammal which lives in water. This is a form of Bhagavaan Himself, filled with nakshatraas. That form of Hari, Dhruva is located at the tail portion of that. Or also, that Dhruva is supported by Shimshumaara.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tārāmayaṃ bhagavataḥ śiśumārākṛti prabhoḥ — Sri Paraasharar - The entire nakshatra mandala is in the form of an animal called Shimshumaara, which is a kind of whale, mammal which lives in water. This is a form of Bhagavaan Himself, filled with nakshatraas. That form of Hari; divi rūpaṃ hareryattu tasya pucche sthito dhruvaḥ — Dhruva is located at the tail portion of that. Or also, that Dhruva is supported by Shimshumaara.

Śrīdhara

viṣṇupadaṃ dhruvādīnāṃ sarveṣāmāśrayo viṣṇvādikāraṇañcety uktam—yasmin pratiṣṭhito bhāsvān medhībhūtaḥ svayaṃ dhruva ityādinā | tad eva kena prakāreṇetyapekṣāyāṃ tatprakāraviśeṣanirūpaṇārthamadhyāyārambhaḥ | tārāmayam iti | nakṣatrapuñjātmakaṃ śiśumārākṛti, śiśumāraḥ saraṭa-godhādyākāro jalacarasattvaviśeṣaḥ | tasyākṛtirivākṛtiryasya tadbhagavato rūpaṃ tasya pradakṣiṇāvartakuṇḍalībhūtadehasyottambhitapucchasya pucchāgre dhruvaḥ sthita ityarthaḥ — Atha dhruva prasangaat tat dvaaraa sarvaadhaaram taaraa punja kalpita avayavam shimshumaara aakruti bhagavato roopam aaha - taaraamayam iti | While telling about Dhruva loka, through that, the part which is consisting of all the stars, constellations, in the form of Shimshumaara, form of Bhagavaan Himself, is told here.
