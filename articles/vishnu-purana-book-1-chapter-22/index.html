VP 1.22.1

यदाभिषिक्तः स पृथुः पूर्वं राज्ये महर्षिभिः।
ततः क्रमेण राज्यानि ददौ लोक-पितामहः॥१॥

When Prithu was installed in the government of the earth, the great father of the spheres established sovereignties in other parts of the creation.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
yadā abhiṣiktaḥ sa Pṛthuḥ — when that Pṛthu was consecrated;
pūrvaṃ rājye — earlier as king;
tataḥ krameṇa — thereafter in due order;
rājyāni dadau — he assigned the kingdoms;
lokapitāmahaḥ — the grandsire of the world
Śrīdhara
evaṃ tāvad devādīnāṃ sṛṣṭir uktā| idānīṃ tattadadhipakathanamukhena viṣṇor vibhūtīr vaktum āha—yadetyādinā yāvatsamāptiḥ — contextual sense: The creation of the gods and others has now been stated. He now explains Viṣṇu’s manifestations by describing the lords appointed over each class, beginning “whatever.”
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yadābhiṣiktaḥ sa pṛthuḥ pūrvaṃ rājye maharṣibhiḥ. tataḥ krameṇa rājyāni dadau lokapitāmahaḥ||1||

Śrīdhara

evaṃ tāvad devādīnāṃ sṛṣṭir uktā| idānīṃ tattadadhipakathanamukhena viṣṇor vibhūtīr vaktum āha—yadetyādinā yāvatsamāptiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवं तावद् देवादीनां सृष्टिर् उक्ता। इदानीं तत्तद्अधिपकथनमुखेन विष्णोर् विभूतीर् वक्तुम् आह—यदेत्य्आदिना यावत्समाप्तिः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The creation of the gods and others has now been stated. He now explains Viṣṇu’s manifestations by describing the lords appointed over each class, beginning “whatever.”

VP 1.22.2

नक्षत्र-ग्रह-विप्राणां वीरुधां चाप्य् अशेषतः।
सोमं राज्येऽददाद् ब्रह्मा यज्ञानां तपसाम् अपि॥२॥

Soma was appointed monarch of the stars and planets, of Brahmans and of plants, of sacrifices and of penance.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
nakṣatra-graha-viprāṇām — of stars, planets, and Brahmans;
vīrudhām — of plants;
aśeṣataḥ — without remainder, all;
Somam rājye adadāt — appointed Soma to sovereignty;
yajñānām tapasām api — of sacrifices and austerities also
Śrīdhara
nakṣatrādīnāṃ tapo’ntānāṃ rājye somam adadhāt — contextual sense: He appointed Soma to rule the stars and the other bodies, through the sphere of ascetic practice.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

nakṣatragrahaviprāṇāṃ vīrudhāṃ cāpyaśeṣataḥ. somaṃ rājye’dadād brahmā yajñānāṃ tapasām api||2||

Śrīdhara

nakṣatrādīnāṃ tapo’ntānāṃ rājye somam adadhāt

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

नक्षत्रादीनां तपोऽन्तानां राज्ये सोमम् अदधात्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He appointed Soma to rule the stars and the other bodies, through the sphere of ascetic practice.

VP 1.22.3

राज्ञां वैश्रवणं राज्ये जलानां वरुणं तथा।
आदित्यानां पतिं विष्णुं वसूनाम् अथ पावकम्॥३॥

Vaisravaṇa was made king over kings; and Varuṇa, over the waters. Viṣṇu was the chief of the Ādityas; Pāvaka, of the Vasus.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
rājñām — of kings;
Vaiśravaṇam — Vaiśravaṇa;
jalānām Varuṇam — Varuṇa over waters;
Ādityānām patim Viṣṇum — Viṣṇu, lord of the Ādityas;
vasūnām Pāvakam — Pāvaka, lord of the Vasus
Śrīdhara
rājñāṃ rājye vaiśravaṇaṃ kuveram adadhād ity anuṣaṅgaḥ — contextual sense: The implied statement is that he appointed Vaiśravaṇa, Kubera, to rule the kings.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

rājñāṃ vaiśravaṇaṃ rājye jalānāṃ varuṇaṃ tathā. ādityānāṃ patiṃ viṣṇuṃ vasūnām atha pāvakam||3||

Śrīdhara

rājñāṃ rājye vaiśravaṇaṃ kuveram adadhād ity anuṣaṅgaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

राज्ञां राज्ये वैश्रवणं कुवेरम् अदधाद् इत्य् अनुषङ्गः

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The implied statement is that he appointed Vaiśravaṇa, Kubera, to rule the kings.

VP 1.22.4

प्रजापतीनां दक्षं तु वासवं मरुताम् अपि।
दैत्यानां दानवानां च प्रह्लादम् अधिपं ददौ॥४॥

Dakṣa was made chief of the patriarchs; Vāsava, of the winds. To Prahlāda was assigned dominion over the Daityas and Dānavas.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
prajāpatīnām Dakṣam — Dakṣa over the Prajāpatis;
Vāsavam Marutām — Vāsava over the Maruts;
Daityānām Dānavānāṃ ca — of Daityas and Dānavas;
Prahlādam adhipam — Prahlāda as ruler
Śrīdhara
marutāṃ ditiputrāṇāṃ vāsavam adhipaṃ dadau — contextual sense: He made Vāsava, Indra, the lord of the Maruts, the sons of Diti.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prajāpatīnāṃ dakṣaṃ tu vāsavaṃ marutām api. daityānāṃ dānavānāṃ ca prahlādam adhipaṃ dadau||4||

Śrīdhara

marutāṃ ditiputrāṇāṃ vāsavam adhipaṃ dadau

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मरुतां दितिपुत्राणां वासवम् अधिपं ददौ

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He made Vāsava, Indra, the lord of the Maruts, the sons of Diti.

VP 1.22.5

पितृणां धर्म-राजं तं यमं राज्येऽभ्यषेचयत्।
ऐरावतं गजेन्द्राणाम् अशेषाणां पतिं ददौ॥५॥

Yama, the king of justice, was appointed the monarch of the Manes (Pitṛs). Airāvata was made the king of elephants.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

rājye abhyasecayat — consecrated as ruler;
Airāvatam gajendrāṇām — Airāvata over the lordly elephants;
aśeṣāṇām patim — lord of all
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pitṛṇāṃ dharmarājaṃ taṃ yamaṃ rājye’bhyaṣecayat. airāvataṃ gajendrāṇām aśeṣāṇāṃ patiṃ dadau||5||

No commentary.

VP 1.22.6

पतत्रिणां च गरुडं देवानाम् अपि वासवम्।
उच्चैःश्रवसम् अश्वानां वृषभं तु गवाम् अपि॥६॥

Garuḍa became king of birds, and Indra of the gods. Uccaiḥśravas was the chief of horses; Vṛṣabha, of kine.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
patatriṇām Garuḍam — Garuḍa over birds;
devānām Vāsavam — Vāsava over the gods;
Uccaiḥśravasam aśvānām — Uccaiḥśravas over horses;
Vṛṣabham gavām — Vṛṣabha over cattle
Śrīdhara
anyeṣām api devānāṃ vāsavam eva dadau — contextual sense: He made Vāsava the lord of the other gods as well.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

patatriṇāṃ ca garuḍaṃ devānām api vāsavam. uccaiḥśravasam aśvānāṃ vṛṣabhaṃ tu gavām api||6||

Śrīdhara

anyeṣām api devānāṃ vāsavam eva dadau

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अन्येषाम् अपि देवानां वासवम् एव ददौ

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He made Vāsava the lord of the other gods as well.

VP 1.22.7

शेषं तु नाग-राजानां मृगाणां सिंहम् ईश्वरम्।
वनस्पतीनां राजानं प्लक्षम् एवाभ्यषेचयत्॥७॥

Śeṣa became the snake-king; the lion, the monarch of the beasts; and the sovereign of the trees was the holy fig-tree.

mṛgāṇām siṃham īśvaram — the lion, lord of beasts;
vanaspatīnām rājānam — king of trees;
Plakṣam — the plakṣa fig tree
Śrīdhara
siṃham īśvaraṃ patim — contextual sense: He made the lion the lord and master of beasts.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śeṣaṃ tu nāgarājānāṃ mṛgāṇāṃ siṃham īśvaram. vanaspatīnāṃ rājānaṃ plakṣam evābhyaṣecayat||7||

Śrīdhara

siṃham īśvaraṃ patim

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सिंहम् ईश्वरं पतिम्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He made the lion the lord and master of beasts.

VP 1.22.8

एवं विभज्य राज्यानि दिशां पालान् अनन्तरम्।
प्रजापति-पतिर् ब्रह्मा स्थापयामास सर्वतः॥८॥

Having thus fixed the limits of each authority, the great progenitor Brahmā stationed rulers for the protection of the different quarters of the world.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
evaṃ vibhajya rājyāni — having thus divided the kingdoms;
diśāṃ pālān — guardians of the directions;
prajāpatipatiḥ Brahmā — Brahmā, lord of the Prajāpatis;
sthāpayāmāsa sarvataḥ — established everywhere
Śrīdhara
diśāṃ pālān dikpālakān — contextual sense: He appointed the guardians of the directions as the protectors of the quarters.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃ vibhajya rājyāni diśāṃ pālān anantaram. prajāpatipatir brahmā sthāpayāmāsa sarvataḥ||8||

Śrīdhara

diśāṃ pālān dikpālakān

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

दिशां पालान् दिक्पालकान्

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

He appointed the guardians of the directions as the protectors of the quarters.

VP 1.22.9

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

No commentary.

दक्षिणस्यां दिशि तथा कर्दमस्य प्रजापतेः।

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dakṣiṇasyāṃ diśi tathā kardamasya prajāpateḥ. putraṃ śaṅkhapadaṃ nāma rājānaṃ so’bhyaṣecayat||10||

No commentary.

VP 1.22.11

“Acyuta” qualifies Ketumān.

VP 1.22.13

VP 1.22.14

Transliteration

VP 1.22.16

ये तु देवाधिपतयो ये च दैत्य्-आधिपास् तथा।

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 1.22.17

पशूनां ये च पतयः पतयो ये च पक्षिणाम्।
मनुष्याणां च सर्पाणां नागानां चाधिपाश् च ये॥१७॥

The chief amongst beasts, amongst birds, amongst men, and amongst serpents are but portions of the universal Viṣṇu.

pakṣiṇāṃ patayaḥ — lords of birds;
manuṣyāṇām — of human beings;
sarpāṇāṃ nāgānām adhipāḥ — rulers of serpents and Nāgas
