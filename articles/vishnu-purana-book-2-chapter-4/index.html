Book 2, Chapter 4

pañcāśat-koṭi-vistārā seyam urvī mahā-mune | sahaivāṇḍa-kaṭāhena sa-dvīpābdhi-mahīdharā ||6||

Śrīdhara

sahaivāṇḍakaṭāheneti tanmadhyavartinītyarthaḥ ||6||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सहैवाण्डकटाहेनेति तन्मध्यवर्तिनीत्यर्थः ॥९६ ॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

sahaivāṇḍakaṭāheneti tanmadhyavartinītyarthaḥ

VP 2.4.97

सेयं धात्री विधात्री च सर्व-भूत-गुणाधिका ।
आधार-भूता सर्वेषां मैत्रेय जगताम् इति ॥९७॥

the mother and nurse of all creatures, the foundation of all worlds, and the chief of the elements.

Viṣṇu Purāṇa

seyaṃ dhātrī vidhātrī ca sarva-bhūta-guṇādhikā | ādhāra-bhūtā sarveṣāṃ maitreya jagatām iti ||7||

Śrīdhara

dhātrī pālayitrī, vidhātrī janayitrī, ityetaiḥ pālanādibhiḥ sarvebhyo bhūtebhya ākāśādibhyo guṇādhikācety arthaḥ ||7||
