VP 4.13.1

चतुर्थांशे त्रयोदशोऽध्यायः भजनभजमानदिव्यान्धकदेवावृधमहाभोजवृष्णिसंज्ञाःसत्वतस्य पुत्रा बभूवुः

Sri Paraasharar - Satvata's sons are going to be told now. Bhajana, Bhajamaana, Divya, Andhaka, Devaavrudha, Mahaabhoja, Vrushni are the children of Satvata.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

caturathāṃśe — Sri; tarayodaśo'dhayāyaḥ — sons; bhajanabhajamānadivayānadhakadevāvaṛdhamahābhojavaṛṣaṇisaṃjañāḥsatavatasaya — told; putarā — Divya; babhūvuḥ — are

Śrīdhara

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

त्रयोदशे ज्यामघस्य सन्ततौ सत्वतान्वयः स्यमन्तकस्य चाख्यानं प्रसङ्गादनुवर्ण्यते सत्वतस्य भजिनादयः सप्त पुत्रा बभूवुः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 1).

Sri Paraasharar - Satvata's sons are going to be told now. Bhajana, Bhajamaana, Divya, Andhaka, Devaavrudha, Mahaabhoja, Vrushni are the children of Satvata.
