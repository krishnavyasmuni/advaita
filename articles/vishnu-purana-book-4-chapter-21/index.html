VP 4.21.1

VP 4.21.2

योऽयं सांप्रतमवनीपतिः परीक्षित्तस्यापि जनमेजयश्रुतसेनोग्रसेनभीमसेनाश्चत्वारः पुत्रा भविष्यन्ति

Pareekshit is going to have four sons - Janamejaya, Shrutasena, Ugrasena and Bheemasena.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yo'yaṃ — Pareekshit; sāṃparatamavanīpatiḥ — going; parīkaṣitatasayāpi — have; janamejayaśarutasenogarasenabhīmasenāśacatavāraḥ — sons; putarā — Janamejaya; bhaviṣayanati — Ugrasena

Śrīdhara

ekaviṃśe — Pareekshit; purovaṃśo — Pareekshit; bhaviṣaya — is; upavaraṇayate — going; . — going; kaṣemakaṃ — to; parāpaya — have; yaḥ — four; saṃsathāṃ — four; kalāvupagamiṣayati — sons; . — -; kuruputara-parikaṣita — -; ivāsayāpi — Janamejaya; janamejayādisañajañā — Shrutasena; eva — Ugrasena; catavāraḥ — Ugrasena; putarāḥ — and; ||2|| — Bheemasena

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

yo'yaṃ sāṃparatamavanīpatiḥ parīkaṣitatasayāpi janamejayaśarutasenogarasenabhīmasenāśacatavāraḥ putarā bhaviṣayanati ||2||

Śrīdhara

ekaviṃśe purovaṃśo bhaviṣaya upavaraṇayate . kaṣemakaṃ parāpaya yaḥ saṃsathāṃ kalāvupagamiṣayati . kuruputara-parikaṣita ivāsayāpi janamejayādisañajañā eva catavāraḥ putarāḥ ||2||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एकविंशे पुरोवंशो भविष्य उपवर्ण्यते । क्षेमकं प्राप्य यः संस्थां कलावुपगमिष्यति । कुरुपुत्र-परिक्षित इवास्यापि जनमेजयादिसञ्ज्ञा एव चत्वारः पुत्राः ॥२॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 2).

Pareekshit is going to have four sons - Janamejaya, Shrutasena, Ugrasena and Bheemasena.

VP 4.21.4

योऽसौ याज्ञवल्क्याद्वेदमधीत्य कृपादस्त्राण्यवाप्य विषमविषयविरक्तचित्तवृत्तिश्च शौनकोपदेशादात्मज्ञानप्रवीणः परं निर्वाणमवाप्स्यति

Shataaneeka will become a great jnyaani. He will learn the Vedaas from Yaajnyavalkya, he will learn the astra vidyaa from Krupa, his mind will be completely free from all sensual pleasures, he will learn the bhakti jnyaana vidh i from Shounaka, having realized the self, he is going to get moksha. He is going to realize aatma jnyaana, Paramaatma jnyaana and will get mukti.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

|| — Shataaneeka; 1-3 — He; || — Yaajnyavalkya; yo'sau — from; yājañavalakayādavedamadhītaya — completely; kaṛpādasatarāṇayavāpaya — will; viṣamaviṣayavirakatacitatavaṛtatiśaca — i; śaunakopadeśādātamajañānaparavīṇaḥ — self; paraṃ — He; niravāṇamavāpasayati — jnyaana

Śrīdhara

tasaya — Shataaneeka; janamejayasaya — Yaajnyavalkya; . — completely; ātamavijañānaparavaṇasatataparaḥ — i; ||4|| — He

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

|| 1-3 || yo'sau yājañavalakayādavedamadhītaya kaṛpādasatarāṇayavāpaya viṣamaviṣayavirakatacitatavaṛtatiśaca śaunakopadeśādātamajañānaparavīṇaḥ paraṃ niravāṇamavāpasayati ||4||

Śrīdhara

tasaya janamejayasaya . ātamavijañānaparavaṇasatataparaḥ ||4||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तस्य जनमेजयस्य । आत्मविज्ञानप्रवणस्तत्परः ॥४॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 4).

Shataaneeka will become a great jnyaani. He will learn the Vedaas from Yaajnyavalkya, he will learn the astra vidyaa from Krupa, his mind will be completely free from all sensual pleasures, he will learn the bhakti jnyaana vidh i from Shounaka, having realized the self, he is going to get moksha. He is going to realize aatma jnyaana, Paramaatma jnyaana and will get mukti.

VP 4.21.18

ब्रह्मक्षत्रस्य यो योनिर्वंशो देवर्षिसत्कृतः । क्षेमकं प्राप्य राजानं संस्थानं प्राप्यते कलौ ॥ इति

The vamsha which is well honoured by even the Devarshis, and which is the origin of the Brahma and Kshatriya race, that will end in Kaliyuga with Kshemaka as the last king.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

barahamakaṣatarasayeti — The; || — by; saṃsathānaṃ — is; – — Kshatriya; samāpatima — Kaliyuga

Śrīdhara

barahamaṇaḥ — The; barāhamaṇasaya — vamsha; kaṣatarasaya — is; kaṣatariyasaya — well; ca — by; yoniḥ — the; kāraṇaṃ — Devarshis; kaṣatariyaira — which; eva — is; kaiśacita — origin; tapo-viśeṣāta — the; barāhamaṇayaṃ — Brahma; labadhama — Kshatriya; iti — race; pūravaṃ — will; tathokatatavāta — in; . — Kaliyuga; saṃsathāṃ — Kshemaka; samāpatima — as; ||18|| — last

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

barahamakaṣatarasayeti || saṃsathānaṃ – samāpatima ||18||

Śrīdhara

barahamaṇaḥ barāhamaṇasaya kaṣatarasaya kaṣatariyasaya ca yoniḥ kāraṇaṃ kaṣatariyaira eva kaiśacita tapo-viśeṣāta barāhamaṇayaṃ labadhama iti pūravaṃ tathokatatavāta . saṃsathāṃ samāpatima ||18||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

ब्रह्मणः ब्राह्मणस्य क्षत्रस्य क्षत्रियस्य च योनिः कारणं क्षत्रियैर् एव कैश्चित् तपो-विशेषात् ब्राह्मण्यं लब्धम् इति पूर्वं तथोक्तत्वात् । संस्थां समाप्तिम् ॥१८॥

Śrīdhara commentary (source range 18).

The vamsha which is well honoured by even the Devarshis, and which is the origin of the Brahma and Kshatriya race, that will end in Kaliyuga with Kshemaka as the last king.
