VP 2.2.2

यावन्तः सागरा द्वीपास्तथा वर्षाणि पर्वताः । वनानि सरितः पुर्यो देवादीनां तथा मुने

What was the expanse over the oceans, the islands, the countries, the lands, the mountains, the forests, the streams, the cities, of devaas and all others. How the universe was divided, and what were the countries, I would like to know.
