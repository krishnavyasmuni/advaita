VP 2.2.1

कथितो भवता ब्रह्मन्सर्गः स्वायंभुवश्च मे । श्रोतुमिछाम्यहं त्वत्तः सकलं मण्डलं भुवः

Maitreya said—You have told me of the creation of Svāyambhuva, O Brahman; I wish to hear from you the whole expanse of the earth.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

kathito bhavatā brahmansargaḥ svāyaṃbhuvaśca me | śrotumichāmyahaṃ tvattaḥ sakalaṃ maṇḍalaṃ bhuvaḥ — kathitaḥ — told; bhavatā — by you; brahman — O Brahman; sargaḥ — creation; svāyambhuvaḥ ca me — of Svāyambhuva, and for me. śrotum icchāmi — I wish to hear; aham — I; tvattaḥ — from you; sakalam maṇḍalam bhuvaḥ — the whole circle of the earth.

Śrīdhara

uktānuvādena bhuvanakośaṃ saviśeṣaṃ bubhūtsuḥ pṛcchati-kathita iti | svayambhuvo’śatvānmanuratra svayambhuḥ | tadīyaḥ priyavratādisargaḥ kathitaḥ | tatkathanena ca dvīpavarṣādivibhāgoktyā sacitaṃ bhūmaṇḍalādikaṃ śrotumicchāmi — uktānuvādena — by repeating what was stated; bhuvana-kośam — the world-system; saviśeṣam — in detail; bubhūtsuḥ — desiring to know; pṛcchati — he asks; kathitam iti — ‘it was told,’ [is the meaning]. svayambhuvaḥ aṃśatvāt — because of being a portion of Svayambhū; manuḥ atra svayambhuḥ — here ‘Svāyambhuva’ is the Manu; tadīyaḥ priyavratādi-sargaḥ — his creation beginning with Priyavrata; kathitaḥ — was told; tat-kathanena — by that account; dvīpa-varṣādi-vibhāga-uktyā — through stating divisions such as islands and varṣas; sacitam — made known; bhūmaṇḍalādikam — the earth and the rest; śrotum icchāmi — I wish to hear.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

uktānuvādena bhuvanakośaṃ saviśeṣaṃ bubhūtsuḥ pṛcchati-kathita iti | svayambhuvo’śatvānmanuratra svayambhuḥ | tadīyaḥ priyavratādisargaḥ kathitaḥ | tatkathanena ca dvīpavarṣādivibhāgoktyā sacitaṃ bhūmaṇḍalādikaṃ śrotumicchāmi

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

उक्तानुवादेन भुवनकोशं सविशेषं बुभूत्सुः पृच्छति-कथित इति । स्वयम्भुवोऽशत्वान्मनुरत्र स्वयम्भुः । तदीयः प्रियव्रतादिसर्गः कथितः । तत्कथनेन च द्वीपवर्षादिविभागोक्त्या सचितं भूमण्डलादिकं श्रोतुमिच्छामि

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges १).

Śrīdhara takes this as a question repeated from what was already said. Desiring to know the world-system in detail, Maitreya asks, ‘It was told.’ Here ‘Svāyambhuva’ refers to the Manu because he is a portion of Svayambhū. The creation belonging to him, beginning with Priyavrata, was told. By that account, through the statement of divisions such as islands and varṣas, the earth and the rest were made known; ‘I wish to hear [about them].’

VP 2.2.6

Siddhāḥ — Siddhas; aṇimādi-aiśvarya-yuktāḥ — endowed with powers beginning with aṇimā; Cāraṇāḥ — Cāraṇas, divine singers; taiḥ — by them; sevitāḥ — frequented.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तेषां शैलानामन्तरे मध्ये द्रोण्यः दरीविशेषाः सन्ति । सिद्धाः अणिमाद्यैश्वर्ययुक्ताः, चारणा देवगायकाः, तैः सेवितः

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges ४९–५३).

Among those mountains are valleys, special kinds of ravines. Siddhas endowed with powers such as aṇimā, and Cāraṇas, divine singers, frequent them.

VP 2.2.54

स्वस्थाः प्रजा निरातङ्कास्सर्वदुःखविवर्जिताः । दश द्वादशवर्षाणां सहस्राणि स्थिरायुषः

The people are healthy, free from affliction and all suffering; their lifespans remain ten or twelve thousand years.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

svasthāḥ prajā nirātaṅkāssarvaduḥkhavivarjitāḥ | daśa dvādaśavarṣāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi sthirāyuṣaḥ — svasthāḥ prajāḥ — the people are healthy; nirātaṅkāḥ — free from affliction; sarva-duḥkha-vivarjitāḥ — free from all suffering. daśa-dvādaśa-varṣāṇām sahasrāṇi — ten or twelve thousand; sthira-āyuṣaḥ — with steady lifespans.

nirātaṅkāḥ nīrogāḥ | varṣāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi daśa vā dvādaśa vā sthirāyuṣa iti kiloktirilāvṛtādideśabhedavyavasthāyām — nirātaṅkāḥ — free from affliction; nīrogāḥ — free from disease. varṣāṇām sahasrāṇi — thousands of years; daśa vā dvādaśa vā — ten or twelve; sthira-āyuṣaḥ — with steady lifespans. kila uktiḥ — this is a conventional statement; Ilāvṛta-ādi-deśa-bheda-vyavasthāyām — according to distinctions of place such as Ilāvṛta and the rest.

nirātaṅkāḥ nīrogāḥ | varṣāṇāṃ sahasrāṇi daśa vā dvādaśa vā sthirāyuṣa iti kiloktirilāvṛtādideśabhedavyavasthāyām

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

निरातङ्काः नीरोगाः । वर्षाणां सहस्राणि दश वा द्वादश वा स्थिरायुष इति किलोक्तिरिलावृतादिदेशभेदव्यवस्थायाम्

Śrīdhara commentary (source ranges ५४).

‘Free from affliction’ means free from disease. The statement ‘ten or twelve thousand years’ indicates that lifespans are stable according to differences of place, such as Ilāvṛta and the rest.

VP 2.2.55

न तेषु वर्षते देवो भौमान्यम्भांसि तेषु वै । कृतत्रेतादिकं नैव तेषु स्थानेषु कल्पना

Rain does not fall there, for earthly waters are always present; there is no reckoning there of Kṛta, Tretā, and the other ages.

VP 2.2.56
