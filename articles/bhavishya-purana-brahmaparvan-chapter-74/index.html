ye namasyanti cādityaṃ narā bhaktisamanvitāḥ || 27 ||

Therefore he is worthy of worship and reverence in all twelve forms. Those people who salute Āditya with devotion

Word-for-word
tasmāt — therefore;
vandyaḥ — worthy of worship;
namasyaḥ — worthy of reverence;
ca — and;
dvādaśasu api mūrtiṣu — in all twelve forms;
ye — who;
namasyanti — salute;
ca — and;
ādityam — Āditya;
narāḥ — people;
bhakti-samanvitāḥ — endowed with devotion.
Transliteration
tasmād vandyo namasyaś ca dvādaśasv api mūrtiṣu | ye namasyanti cādityaṃ narā bhaktisamanvitāḥ || 27 ||

BP 1.74.28

te yāsyanti paraṃ sthānaṃ tiṣṭhed yatrāmbujeśvaraḥ |
ityevaṃ dvādaśātmānamādityaṃ pūjayettu yaḥ || 28 ||

will go to the supreme place where the Lord of the lotus stands. Whoever thus worships Āditya as the Twelve-Self

Word-for-word
te — they;
yāsyanti — will go;
param sthānam — supreme place;
tiṣṭhet — stands;
yatra — where;
ambuja-īśvaraḥ — Lord of the lotus;
iti evam — thus;
dvādaśātmānam — the Twelve-Self;
ādityam — Āditya;
pūjayet — should worship;
yaḥ — whoever.
Transliteration
te yāsyanti paraṃ sthānaṃ tiṣṭhed yatrāmbujeśvaraḥ | ity evaṃ dvādaśātmānam ādityaṃ pūjayet tu yaḥ || 28 ||

BP 1.74.29

sa muktaḥ sarvapāpebhyo yāti helisalokatām || 29 ||

is freed from all sins and goes to the world of Heliṣa.

Word-for-word
saḥ — he;
muktaḥ — freed;
sarva-pāpebhyaḥ — from all sins;
yāti — goes;
helisa-lokatām — to the world of Heliṣa.
Transliteration
sa muktaḥ sarvapāpebhyo yāti helisalokatām || 29 ||
