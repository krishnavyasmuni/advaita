  • Chapter 84 — The Saumya Observance

BP 1.84.1

ब्रह्मोवाच । नक्षत्रं रोहिणी वीर यदा वारे ऽस्य वै भवेत् । यात्य् असौ सौम्यतां वीर स सौम्यः परिकीर्तितः ॥ १ ॥

Brahmā said: O hero, when the star Rohiṇī occurs on this day, it becomes gentle; that day is proclaimed Saumya, O hero.

Word-for-word

vīra — O hero;
yadā — when;
vāre — on the day;
asya — of this;
vai — indeed;
bhavet — occurs;
yāti — goes, becomes;
asau — this;
saumyatām — gentleness;
vīra — O hero;
saḥ — that;
saumyaḥ — Saumya;
parikīrtitaḥ — proclaimed.
Transliteration
brahmovāca | nakṣatraṃ rohiṇī vīra yadā vāre 'sya vai bhavet | yāty asau saumyatāṃ vīra sa saumyaḥ parikīrtitaḥ || 1 ||

BP 1.84.2

स्नानं दानं जपो होमः पितृ-देवादि-तर्पणम् । अक्षयं स्यान् न सन्देहस् त्व् अत्र वारे महात्मनः ॥ २ ॥

Bathing, giving, recitation, fire offerings, and offerings to the ancestors, gods, and others become inexhaustible on this day; there is no doubt, O great one.

Word-for-word
snānam — bathing;
dānam — giving;
japaḥ — recitation;

akṣayam — inexhaustible;

tu — indeed;
atra — here;
vāre — on the day;
mahātman — O great one.
Transliteration
snānaṃ dānaṃ japo homaḥ pitṛ-devādi-tarpaṇam | akṣayaṃ syān na sandehas tv atra vāre mahātmanaḥ || 2 ||

BP 1.84.3

नक्तं समाश्रितो यो ऽत्र पूजयेद् भास्करं नरः । याति लोकं स देवस्य भास्करस्य न संशयः ॥ ३ ॥

A person who here undertakes the night observance and worships Bhāskara goes to the world of the god Bhāskara; of this there is no doubt.

Word-for-word
naktam — night observance;

bhāskarasya — of Bhāskara;
na saṃśayaḥ — without doubt.
Transliteration
naktaṃ samāśrito yo 'tra pūjayed bhāskaraṃ naraḥ | yāti lokaṃ sa devasya bhāskarasya na saṃśayaḥ || 3 ||

BP 1.84.4

रक्तोत्पलानि वै तत्र तथा रक्तं च चन्दनम् । सु-गन्धश् चापि धूपस् तु नैवेद्यं पायसं तथा । ब्राह्मणाय च दातव्यं भोक्तव्यं चात्मना तथा ॥ ४ ॥

There, red lotuses, red sandalwood, fragrant incense, and milk-rice as the food offering are prescribed. It should also be given to a Brāhmaṇa, and one should likewise eat oneself.

BP 1.84.5

य एवं पूजयेत् सौम्ये चित्रभानुं गवां पतिम् । स विमुक्तः तु पापेभ्यः त्वाष्ट्रीं कान्तिम् अवाप्नुयात् ॥ ५ ॥

Whoever worships Citrabhānu, the Lord of the cows, in this way on Saumya, being freed from sins, attains the radiance of Tvaṣṭṛ’s daughter.
