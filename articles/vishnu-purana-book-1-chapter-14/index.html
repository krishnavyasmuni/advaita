VP 1.14.1

पृथोः पुत्रौ तु धर्मज्ञौ जज्ञातेऽन्तर्द्धिवादिनौ ।
शिखण्डिनी हविर्धानम् अन्तर्धानाद् व्यजायत ॥१॥

Pruthu had two sons, who were knowledgeable about dharma - Antardhaana and Vaadi. Antardhaana's wife Shikhandinee, gave birth to Havirdhaana.

Śrīdhara

antarddhiḥ eva — disappearance itself; antardhānam — becoming invisible; tasmāt — therefore; bhāryā śikhaṇḍinī — the wife Śikhaṇḍinī; havirdhānam putram asūyata — bore Havirdhāna as a son.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

pṛthoḥ putrau tu dharmajñau jajñāte’ntarddhivādinau | śikhaṇḍinī havirdhānam antardhānād vyajāyata || 1 ||

Śrīdhara

antarddhirevāntarddhānaṃ tasmād bhāryā śikhaṇḍinī havirdhānaṃ putramasūyata ||1||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अन्तर्द्धिरेवान्तर्द्धानं तस्माद् भार्या शिखण्डिनी हविर्धानं पुत्रमसूयत ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Disappeared completely” means that he became invisible. Therefore the wife named Śikhaṇḍinī bore Havirdhāna, the son.

VP 1.14.4

प्राचीनाग्राः कुशास् तस्य पृथिव्यां विश्रुता मुने ।
प्राचीनबर्हिर् अभवत् ख्यातो भुवि महाबलः ॥४॥

He was called Praacheenabarhis because all the darbhaas which had praacheena agra, which are used for specific purpose in the yajnyaas, were spread all over the earth. That is why he became famous as Praacheenabarhis. He was of great valour.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prācīnāgrāḥ — prācīnāgrāḥ — (plural); kuśās — contextual sense: which are used for specific purpose in the yajnyaas; tasya — of him; pṛthivyāṃ — contextual sense: were spread all over the earth; viśrutā — contextual sense: That is why he became famous as Praacheenabarhis; mune — O sage; prācīnabarhir — prācīnabarhir (name); abhavat — became; khyāto — contextual sense: He was of great valour; bhuvi — bhuvi — (form); mahābalaḥ — mahābalaḥ — (masculine form); He was called Praacheenabarhis because all the darbhaas which had praacheena agra, which are used for specific purpose in the yajnyaas, were spread all over the earth. That is why he became famous as Praacheenabarhis. He was of great valour.

Śrīdhara

prācīna-agrāḥ kuśāḥ — kuśa grass with tips facing east; sarvatra — everywhere; yajña-anuṣṭhānāt — because of the performance of sacrifices; āstṛtam vasudhā-talam — the earth's surface was spread over.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

prācīnāgrāḥ kuśās tasya pṛthivyāṃ viśrutā mune | prācīnabarhir abhavat khyāto bhuvi mahābalaḥ || 4 ||

Śrīdhara

prācīnāgrāḥ kuśā sarvatra yajñānuṣṭhānāt | taduktaṃ yasyedaṃ devayajanam anuyajñaṃ vitanvataḥ | prācīnāgraiḥ kuśairāsīd āstṛtaṃ vasudhātalam iti ||4||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्राचीनाग्राः कुशा सर्वत्र यज्ञानुष्ठानात् । तदुक्तं यस्येदं देवयजनम् अनुयज्ञं वितन्वतः । प्राचीनाग्रैः कुशैरासीद् आस्तृतं वसुधातलम् इति ॥४॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

The kuśa grass had its tips facing east everywhere because sacrifices were being performed. The cited verse says that the earth was covered with east-pointing kuśa grass while the sacrificial rites were being extended.

VP 1.14.5

समुद्रतनयायां तु कृतदारो महीपतिः ।

Word-for-word

Śrīdhara

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

samudratanayāyāṃ tu kṛtadāro mahīpatiḥ | mahatas tapasaḥ pāre savarṇāyāṃ mahāmate || 5 ||

Śrīdhara

tapasaḥ pāre savarṇāsañjñāyāṃ kṛtadāraḥ | tapastaptvānantaraṃ tāṃ pariṇītavānity arthaḥ | tamasa iti pāṭhe tamaso narakasya pāre nimitte putrotpatyā naraka-tāraṇārtham ity arthaḥ ||5||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

तपसः पारे सवर्णासञ्ज्ञायां कृतदारः । तपस्तप्त्वानन्तरं तां परिणीतवानित्य् अर्थः । तमस इति पाठे तमसो नरकस्य पारे निमित्ते पुत्रोत्पत्या नरक-तारणार्थम् इत्य् अर्थः ॥५॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

After completing austerity beyond Savarṇā, he took her as his wife; that is, he married her after performing austerity. In the reading “beyond darkness,” the meaning is that he produced a son in order to cross the hell called darkness.

VP 1.14.7

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

They practised the same dharma and followed the same religious observances. This is stated to suggest that they had one common wife; they lay submerged in the waters of the ocean.
