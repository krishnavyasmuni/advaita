VP 1.11.1

प्रियव्रतोत्तानपादौ मनोः स्वायम्भुवस्य तु ।
द्वौ पुत्रौ तु महावीर्यौ धर्मज्ञौ कथितौ तव ॥१॥

Parāśara said: I had told you about two sons, who had great valour, who were always following the path of dharma, as two sons of Svāyambhuva Manu, called Priyavrata and Uttānapāda.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

Words — priyavratottānapādau — priyavratottānapādau (name); manoḥ — of Manu; svāyambhuvasya — svāyambhuvasya (name); tu — but, indeed; dvau — two; putrau — sons; mahāvīryau — lexical term; dharmajñau — knowing dharma; kathitau — were spoken of; tava — your, to you; sense — Parāśara said: I had told you about two sons, who had great valour, who were always following the path of dharma, as two sons of Svāyambhuva Manu, called Priyavrata and Uttānapāda.

Śrīdhara

Words — commentary — manoḥ kanyānvayaḥ proktaḥ putravaṃśo’dhunocyate | tatrādhyāyadvayenāha dhruvasya caritaṃ mahat | manordvau putrau dve ca kanye | tatra kanyayor vaṃśa uktaḥ | idānīṃ putradvayānuvādapūrvakamuttānapādavaṃśamāha, prīyavratetyādinā || 1 ||; sense — Manu's daughter's vamsha was told so far. Now Parāśara tells about the vamsha of Manu's sons.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

priyavratottānapādau manoḥ svāyambhuvasya tu | dvau putrau tu mahāvīryau dharmajñau kathitau tava || 1 ||

Śrīdhara

manoḥ kanyānvayaḥ proktaḥ putravaṃśo’dhunocyate | tatrādhyāyadvayenāha dhruvasya caritaṃ mahat | manordvau putrau dve ca kanye | tatra kanyayor vaṃśa uktaḥ | idānīṃ putradvayānuvādapūrvakamuttānapādavaṃśamāha, prīyavratetyādinā || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

मनोः कन्यान्वयः प्रोक्तः पुत्रवंशोऽधुनोच्यते । तत्राध्यायद्वयेनाह ध्रुवस्य चरितं महत् । मनोर्द्वौ पुत्रौ द्वे च कन्ये । तत्र कन्ययोर् वंश उक्तः । इदानीं पुत्रद्वयानुवादपूर्वकमुत्तानपादवंशमाह, प्रीयव्रतेत्यादिना ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Manu's daughter's vamsha was told so far. Now Parāśara tells about the vamsha of Manu's sons.

VP 1.11.2

तयोर् उत्तानपादस्य सुरुच्याम् उत्तमः सुतः ।
अभीष्टायाम् अभूद् ब्रह्मन् पितुर् अत्यन्त-वल्लभः ॥२॥

Uttānapāda had a son named Uttama, who was very dear to him, through Suruci, who was very dear to him. Uttānapāda had two wives Sunīti and Suruci.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Uttānapāda had a son named Uttama, who was very dear to him, through Suruci, who was very dear to him.

VP 1.11.4

राजासन-स्थितस्याङ्कं पितुर् भ्रातरम् आश्रितम् ।

rājāsanasthitasya pituraṅkam utsaṅgamāśritaṃ bhrātaram uttamaṃ dṛṣṭvā, tamaṅkam āroḍhuṃ dhruvo manorathaṃ cakre ity anvayaḥ || 4 ||

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

One day, Dhruva saw his father sitting on the throne, and on his lap, he saw Uttama sitting.

VP 1.11.7

क्रियते किं वृथा वत्स महान् एष मनोरथः ।
अन्य-स्त्री-गर्भ-जातेन ह्य् असम्भूत ममोदरे ॥७॥

O son Dhruva, why are you trying to do this great effort, and having such a desire to go and sit on the lap of your father. You are born to the other woman, and were never born to me.

Word-for-word

Transliteration

Śrīdhara

surucyā duruktim asahamāno dhruvaḥstrījitaṃ pitaraṃ vihāya mātuḥ samīpaṃ gatvā duḥkhitāyāstasyā anumatyā purānnirgatya saptarṣīṇām upadeśena madhuvane bhagavantam ārādhya padaṃ prāpta iti krameṇa kathayiṣyan surucyā garvoktimāha, kriyate kim iti caturbhiḥ || 7 ||

सुरुच्या दुरुक्तिम् असहमानो ध्रुवःस्त्रीजितं पितरं विहाय मातुः समीपं गत्वा दुःखितायास्तस्या अनुमत्या पुरान्निर्गत्य सप्तर्षीणाम् उपदेशेन मधुवने भगवन्तम् आराध्य पदं प्राप्त इति क्रमेण कथयिष्यन् सुरुच्या गर्वोक्तिमाह, क्रियते किम् इति चतुर्भिः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

O son Dhruva, why are you trying to do this great effort, and having such a desire to go and sit on the lap of your father.

VP 1.11.10

Śrīdhara
