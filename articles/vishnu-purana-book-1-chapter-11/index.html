VP 1.11.1

प्रियव्रतोत्तानपादौ मनोः स्वायम्भुवस्य तु ।
द्वौ पुत्रौ तु महावीर्यौ धर्मज्ञौ कथितौ तव ॥१॥

Sri Paraashara - I had told you about two sons, who had great valour, who were always following the path of dharma, as two sons of Svayambhuva Manu, called Priyavrata and Uttaanapaada.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Manu's daughter's vamsha was told so far. Now Paraasharar tells about the vamsha of Manu's sons.
