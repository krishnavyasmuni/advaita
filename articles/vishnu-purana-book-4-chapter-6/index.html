VP 4.6.1

सूर्यस्य वंश्या भगवन्कथिता भवता मम

Maitreyar - O Paraasharar, you told me about Soorya vamsha. The kings who were in the Soma vamsha, I would like to know about all of them also.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sūryasya vaṃśyā bhagavankathitā bhavatā mama — Maitreyar - O Paraasharar, you told me about Soorya vamsha. The kings who were in the Soma vamsha, I would like to know about all of them also.

Śrīdhara

sūrya-vaṃśa-prasaṅgena soma-vaṃśas tad-anvaye . elo’pi varṇyate ṣaṣṭhe tretāgnir yena nirmitaḥ — Maitreyar - O Paraasharar, you told me about Soorya vamsha. The kings who were in the Soma vamsha, I would like to know about all of them also.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

sūryasya vaṃśyā bhagavankathitā bhavatā mama

Śrīdhara

sūrya-vaṃśa-prasaṅgena soma-vaṃśas tad-anvaye . elo’pi varṇyate ṣaṣṭhe tretāgnir yena nirmitaḥ ||१|| kīrtyate sthira-kīrtīnāṃ yeṣām adyāpi santatiḥ .

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

सूर्य-वंश-प्रसङ्गेन सोम-वंशस् तद्-अन्वये । एलोऽपि वर्ण्यते षष्ठे त्रेताग्निर् येन निर्मितः ॥१॥ कीर्त्यते स्थिर-कीर्तीनां येषाम् अद्यापि सन्ततिः ।

Śrīdhara commentary.

Maitreyar - O Paraasharar, you told me about Soorya vamsha. The kings who were in the Soma vamsha, I would like to know about all of them also.

iha home abhisaṃhitavān . agnividhinā’gnyutpādanena ||९३||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इह होमे अभिसंहितवान् । अग्निविधिनाऽग्न्युत्पादनेन ॥९३॥

Śrīdhara commentary.

Having performed many types of yaagaas, using that Agni, doing Agni vidhi, and then he attained gandharva loka, and united with Urvashi forever in the svarga loka.

The Agni which was one earlier, in this Manvantara, it became into three, and from then on, three Agnis were used. These are Gaarhapatya, Aavahaneeya, Daakshinaagni. These are used in the yaagaas.
