VP 2.13.1

भगवन् सम्यग् आख्यातं यत् पृष्टोऽसि मया किल ।
भू-समुद्रादि-सरितां संस्थानं ग्रह-संस्थितिः ॥१॥

Reverend sir, all that I asked of you has been thoroughly explained;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhagavan — O Lord; samyak — thoroughly; ākhyātam — explained; saṃsthānam — arrangement; graha-saṃsthitiḥ — planetary position.

Śrīdhara

evam — thus; sthūle viśvarūpe — in the gross cosmic form; śuddha-matiḥ — purified mind; sūkṣma-rūpam — subtle form; bharata-caritam — Bharata’s story; jñāna-yoga-āśrayam — support of knowledge-yoga.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhagavan samyag ākhyātaṃ yat pṛṣṭo'si mayā kila | bhū-samudrādi-saritāṃ saṃsthānaṃ graha-saṃsthitiḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara

evaṃ tāvaddhareḥ sthūle viśvarūpe nirūpite | tena śuddhamatistasya sūkṣmarūpaṃ bubhutsate | tat kuryādviśati hi yena vāsudevam ityukte tatpraveśopāyabhūtajñānayogāśrayaṃ prathamādhyādhyopakṣiptameva bharatacaritamuktānuvādapūrvakaṃ pṛcchati—bhagavann iti ṣaḍbhiḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवं तावद्धरेः स्थूले विश्वरूपे निरूपिते । तेन शुद्धमतिस्तस्य सूक्ष्मरूपं बुभुत्सते । तत् कुर्याद्विशति हि येन वासुदेवम् इत्युक्ते तत्प्रवेशोपायभूतज्ञानयोगाश्रयं प्रथमाध्याध्योपक्षिप्तमेव भरतचरितमुक्तानुवादपूर्वकं पृच्छति—भगवन्न् इति षड्भिः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

After Hari’s gross cosmic form has been described, the purified mind seeks his subtle form; Maitreya therefore asks for Bharata’s story, which supports the knowledge-yoga leading to Vāsudeva.

VP 2.13.2

विष्ण्व्-आधारं यथा चैतत् त्रैलोक्यं समवस्थितम् ।
परमार्थस् तु ते प्रोक्तो यथा ज्ञानं प्रधानतः ॥२॥

namely, the situation of the earth, oceans, mountains, rivers, and planetary bodies; the system of the three worlds, of which Vishnu is the stay.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

viṣṇu-ādhāram — having Vishnu as support; trailokyam — three worlds; samavasthitam — situated; paramārthaḥ — highest truth; jñānam — knowledge; pradhānataḥ — principally.

Śrīdhara

pradhānataḥ — principally; pradhānam — the principal; tasila-pratyaya — tasil suffix; sarva-vibhaktikatva — applicability to all cases.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

viṣṇv-ādhāraṃ yathā caitat trailokyaṃ samavasthitam | paramārthas tu te prokto yathā jñānaṃ pradhānataḥ || 2 ||

Śrīdhara

pradhānata iti pradhānamityarthaḥ | sārvavibhaktikatvāt tasilpratyayasya || 2 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

प्रधानत इति प्रधानमित्यर्थः । सार्वविभक्तिकत्वात् तसिल्प्रत्ययस्य ॥२॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

“Pradhānataḥ” means “principally”; the suffix tasil can be used with all cases.

VP 2.13.3

Transliteration

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Maitreya asks that Bharata’s story now be told to him, whose mind has been purified through hearing and meditating on Hari’s gross form.

VP 2.13.7

शालग्रामे महाभागो भगवन् न्यस्त-मानसः ।
स उवास चिरं कालं मैत्रेय पृथिवी-पतिः ॥७॥

The illustrious monarch of the earth resided, Maitreya, for a considerable period at Śālagrāma,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śālagrāme — at Śālagrāma; mahā-bhāgaḥ — greatly fortunate; bhagavati — in the Lord; nyasta-mānasaḥ — with mind placed; uvāsa — dwelt.

Śrīdhara

muktiḥ — liberation; uttaratvena — as answer; pūrvam yoga — former yoga; śālagrāme — at Śālagrāma; bhagavati nyasta-mānasa — mind placed in Lord.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śālagrāme mahābhāgo bhagavan nyasta-mānasaḥ | sa uvāsa ciraṃ kālaṃ maitreya pṛthivī-patiḥ || 7 ||

Śrīdhara

kathantu nābhavanmuktir ityasyottaratvena tasmin janmani jātaṃ yogabhraṃśaṃ vaktu tasya prācīnaṃ yogamanuvadati—śālagrāma iti pañcabhiḥ | bhagavati nyastaṃ mānasaṃ yena saḥ || 7 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

कथन्तु नाभवन्मुक्तिर् इत्यस्योत्तरत्वेन तस्मिन् जन्मनि जातं योगभ्रंशं वक्तु तस्य प्राचीनं योगमनुवदति—शालग्राम इति पञ्चभिः । भगवति न्यस्तं मानसं येन सः ॥७॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

To answer how liberation failed, Bharata’s earlier yoga is recounted: his mind had been placed in the Lord.

VP 2.13.8

अहिंसादिष्व् अशेषेषु गुणेषु गुणिनां वरः ।
अवाप परमां काष्ठां मनसश् चापि संयमे ॥८॥

his thoughts being wholly dedicated to god, and his conduct distinguished by kindness and every virtue, until he had effected, in the highest degree, the entire control over his mind.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ahiṃsā-ādiṣu — in non-violence and the rest; guṇeṣu — virtues; avāpa — attained; paramāṃ kāṣṭhām — highest culmination; manasaḥ saṃyame — control of mind.

Śrīdhara

ahiṃsā — non-violence; yama-niyama — restraints and observances; manaḥ-saṃyama — control of mind; samādhi — absorption; paramā kāṣṭhā — highest culmination.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

ahiṃsādiṣv aśeṣeṣu guṇeṣu guṇināṃ varaḥ | avāpa paramāṃ kāṣṭhāṃ manasaś cāpi saṃyame || 8 ||

Śrīdhara

ahiṃsādiṣu yamaniyamādiṣu tatsādhye ca manasaḥ saṃyame samādhau paramāṃ kāṣṭhām utkarṣam avāpa || 8 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

अहिंसादिषु यमनियमादिषु तत्साध्ये च मनसः संयमे समाधौ परमां काष्ठाम् उत्कर्षम् अवाप ॥८॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Through the restraints beginning with non-violence and the control of mind achieved through them, he attained the highest excellence in samādhi.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit
