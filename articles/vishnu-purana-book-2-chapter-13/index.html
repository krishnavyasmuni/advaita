VP 2.13.1

भगवन् सम्यग् आख्यातं यत् पृष्टोऽसि मया किल ।
भू-समुद्रादि-सरितां संस्थानं ग्रह-संस्थितिः ॥१॥

Reverend sir, all that I asked of you has been thoroughly explained;

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhagavan — O Lord; samyak — thoroughly; ākhyātam — explained; saṃsthānam — arrangement; graha-saṃsthitiḥ — planetary position.

Śrīdhara

evam — thus; sthūle viśvarūpe — in the gross cosmic form; śuddha-matiḥ — purified mind; sūkṣma-rūpam — subtle form; bharata-caritam — Bharata’s story; jñāna-yoga-āśrayam — support of knowledge-yoga.

Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

bhagavan samyag ākhyātaṃ yat pṛṣṭo'si mayā kila | bhū-samudrādi-saritāṃ saṃsthānaṃ graha-saṃsthitiḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara

evaṃ tāvaddhareḥ sthūle viśvarūpe nirūpite | tena śuddhamatistasya sūkṣmarūpaṃ bubhutsate | tat kuryādviśati hi yena vāsudevam ityukte tatpraveśopāyabhūtajñānayogāśrayaṃ prathamādhyādhyopakṣiptameva bharatacaritamuktānuvādapūrvakaṃ pṛcchati—bhagavann iti ṣaḍbhiḥ || 1 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवं तावद्धरेः स्थूले विश्वरूपे निरूपिते । तेन शुद्धमतिस्तस्य सूक्ष्मरूपं बुभुत्सते । तत् कुर्याद्विशति हि येन वासुदेवम् इत्युक्ते तत्प्रवेशोपायभूतज्ञानयोगाश्रयं प्रथमाध्याध्योपक्षिप्तमेव भरतचरितमुक्तानुवादपूर्वकं पृच्छति—भगवन्न् इति षड्भिः ॥१॥

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

After Hari’s gross cosmic form has been described, the purified mind seeks his subtle form; Maitreya therefore asks for Bharata’s story, which supports the knowledge-yoga leading to Vāsudeva.

VP 2.13.2

विष्ण्व्-आधारं यथा चैतत् त्रैलोक्यं समवस्थितम् ।

Word-for-word

Transliteration

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

VP 2.13.7

शालग्रामे महाभागो भगवन् न्यस्त-मानसः ।
स उवास चिरं कालं मैत्रेय पृथिवी-पतिः ॥७॥

The illustrious monarch of the earth resided, Maitreya, for a considerable period at Śālagrāma,

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

śālagrāme — at Śālagrāma; mahā-bhāgaḥ — greatly fortunate; bhagavati — in the Lord; nyasta-mānasaḥ — with mind placed; uvāsa — dwelt.

Śrīdhara

kathantu nābhavanmuktir ityasyottaratvena tasmin janmani jātaṃ yogabhraṃśaṃ vaktu tasya prācīnaṃ yogamanuvadati—śālagrāma iti pañcabhiḥ | bhagavati nyastaṃ mānasaṃ yena saḥ || 7 ||

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

To answer how liberation failed, Bharata’s earlier yoga is recounted: his mind had been placed in the Lord.

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Śrīdhara’s Commentary.

Through the restraints beginning with non-violence and the control of mind achieved through them, he attained the highest excellence in samādhi.
