VP 2.13.1

भगवन् सम्यग् आख्यातं यत् पृष्टोऽसि मया किल।
भू-समुद्रादि-सरितां संस्थानं ग्रह-संस्थितिः

Maitreyar asks Paraasharar - O Bhagavan, you have told me completely everything that I wanted to know, whatever I requested you. About the earth, oceans, mountains, their positions, the planetary positions, etc.

Word-for-word
Śrīdhara

evaṃ tāvat hareḥ sthūle viśva-rūpe nirūpite — thus, after Hari's gross cosmic form has been described; tena śuddha-matiḥ tasya sūkṣma-rūpaṃ bubhutsate — with a purified mind, he wishes to know his subtle form; tat kuryāt viśati hi yena vāsudevam — that he should do by which he enters Vāsudeva; tat-praveśa-upāya-bhūta-jñāna-yoga-āśrayam — resting on knowledge and yoga, the means of entry; prathama-adhyāya-upakṣiptam eva bharata-caritam — the account of Bharata introduced in the first chapter; ukta-anuvāda-pūrvakam pṛcchati — he asks after repeating what was said; bhagavan iti ṣaḍbhiḥ — with six verses beginning “O Blessed Lord”

Transliteration

evaṃ tāvaddhareḥ sthūle viśvarūpe nirūpite | tena śuddhamatistasya sūkṣmarūpaṃ bubhutsate | tat kuryādviśati hi yena vāsudevam ityukte tatpraveśopāyabhūtajñānayogāśrayaṃ prathamādhyādhyopakṣiptameva bharatacaritamuktānuvādapūrvakaṃ pṛcchati—bhagavann iti ṣaḍbhiḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवं तावद्धरेः स्थूले विश्वरूपे निरूपिते । तेन शुद्धमतिस्तस्य सूक्ष्मरूपं बुभुत्सते । तत् कुर्याद्विशति हि येन वासुदेवम् इत्युक्ते तत्प्रवेशोपायभूतज्ञानयोगाश्रयं प्रथमाध्याध्योपक्षिप्तमेव भरतचरितमुक्तानुवादपूर्वकं पृच्छति—भगवन्न् इति षड्भिः

Śrīdhara commentary.

Maitreyar asks Paraasharar - O Bhagavan, you have told me completely everything that I wanted to know, whatever I requested you. About the earth, oceans, mountains, their positions, the planetary positions, etc.

VP 2.13.2

How jeevaatman is also Paramaartha, that they never change, also you tole me prominently.

VP 2.13.10

The king Bharata was only uttering the names of Hari, all the time. Even in his dreams, he did not say anything else. He was only doing the japa of Bhagavannaamaas only. Apart from this, he did not think of anything else.
