VP 3.18.1

तपस्य् अभिरतान् सोऽथ मायामोहो महासुरान्।
मैत्रेय ददृशे गत्वा नर्मदा-तीर-संश्रितान्

Māyāmoha went to the great asuras, who were devoted to austerity, and saw them dwelling on the bank of the Narmadā, O Maitreya.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
tapasy-abhiratān — devoted to austerity;
so'tha māyāmoho mahāsurān — then Māyāmoha [the] great asuras;
maitreya dadṛśe — O Maitreya, he saw;
gatvā narmadā-tīra-saṃśritān — having gone [to those] dwelling on the bank of the Narmadā.
Śrīdhara
ihocyante — here are described;
hatā daityāḥ — the Daityas slain;
māyā-moha-vimohitāḥ — deluded by Māyāmoha;
nagna-saṃsarga-doṣa-uktyai — for stating the fault of association with the naked;
śaivyā-śatadhanuḥ-kathāḥ — the stories of Śaivyā and Śatadhanu
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

tapasyabhiratānso'tha māyāmoho mahāsurān | maitreya dadṛśe gatvā narmadātīrasaṃśritān ||1||

ihocyante hatā daityā māyā-moha-vimohitāḥ | nagna-saṃsarga-doṣoktyai śaivyā-śatadhanuḥ-kathāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

इहोच्यन्ते हता दैत्या माया-मोह-विमोहिताः । नग्न-संसर्ग-दोषोक्त्यै शैव्या-शतधनुः-कथाः

Śrīdhara commentary.

Here are described the Daityas slain after being deluded by Māyāmoha; the stories of Śaivyā and Śatadhanu explain the fault of association with the naked.

VP 3.18.3

Śrīdhara

pāratrya means enjoyed in the other world.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

VP 3.18.7

अर्हध्वं धर्मम् एतं च सर्वे यूयं महाबलाः

All of you, O mighty ones, become worthy of this dharma.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
arhadhvam — become worthy;
dharmam etam ca — of this dharma also;
sarve yūyam mahā-balāḥ — all of you, O mighty ones
Śrīdhara
arhaḥ — worthy;
yogyaḥ — fit;
etasmād aparaḥ dharmaḥ na paraḥ na śreṣṭhaḥ — no dharma other than this is higher or superior
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

arhadhvaṃ dharmametaṃ ca sarve yūyaṃ mahābalāḥ ||7||

arhaḥ yogyaḥ | etasmādaparo dharmaḥ na paro na śreṣṭhaḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

Arhat means worthy; no dharma is higher or superior than this.

VP 3.18.8

एवं-प्रकारैर् बहुभिर् युक्ति-दर्शन-चर्चितैः।
मायामोहेन ते दैत्या वेद-मार्गाद् अपाकृताः

Through many such arguments and discussions of reasoning, Māyāmoha turned the Daityas away from the Vedic path.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
evaṃprakārairvahubhiryukti(codana) darśanacarcitaiḥ — with many such methods, discussed through reasoning and doctrine;
māyāmohena te daityā vedamārgādapākṛtāḥ — by Māyāmoha, those Daityas were turned away from the Vedic path
Śrīdhara
evam-prakāraiḥ vākyaiḥ — by statements of these kinds;
yukti-darśanam śuṣka-tarka-vādaḥ — examination by reasoning is dry argumentation;
tena vardhitaiḥ — elaborated by that;
carcitaiḥ iti vā pāṭhaḥ — or the reading is “discussed”;
apākṛtāḥ dūrībhūtāḥ — driven away
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

evaṃprakārairvahubhiryukti(codana) darśanacarcitaiḥ | māyāmohena te daityā vedamārgādapākṛtāḥ ||8||

evam-prakārair vākyaiḥ yukti-darśanaṃ śuṣka-tarka-vādaḥ tena varddhitaiḥ | carcitair iti vā pāṭhaḥ | apākṛtā dūrībhūtāḥ

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

एवम्-प्रकारैर् वाक्यैः युक्ति-दर्शनं शुष्क-तर्क-वादः तेन वर्द्धितैः । चर्चितैर् इति वा पाठः । अपाकृता दूरीभूताः

Śrīdhara commentary.

He explains 'in these ways' as statements elaborated by claims such as 'this does not withstand reasoning'; 'discussed by reasoning' means examined through inference and scripture. In the alternate reading, yukti means fitness, darśana means direct perception, and tarkita means inference. In another reading, the words are discussed through injunction and scripture, using reasoning; thus Māyāmoha explains and overpowers them until they leave the Vedic path.

VP 3.18.9

धर्मायैतद् अधर्माय सद् एतन् न सद् इत्य् अपि।
विमुक्तये त्व् इदं नैतद् विमुक्तिं सम्प्रयच्छति

This is for dharma and for adharma; it is existent and non-existent. This is for liberation, yet it does not grant liberation.

Word-for-word
Viṣṇu Purāṇa
dharmāyaitadadharmāya sadetanna sadityapi — for dharma, for adharma;
existent, not existent also;
vimuktaye tvidaṃ naitadvimuktiṃ saṃprayacchati — for liberation, yet this does not grant liberation
Śrīdhara
veda-mārgāpākaraṇam eva saptadhā darśayati sārdha-dvābhyāṃ—dharmāya iti — Beginning with 'for dharma'
and ending with 'in one way,'
he gives seven examples of the Jaina doctrine of conditional predication (saptabhaṅgī): it may exist, not exist, both exist and not exist, be indescribable, exist and be indescribable, not exist and be indescribable, or both exist and not exist and be indescribable. This is the Ārhata/Jaina anekāntavāda.
Transliteration
Viṣṇu Purāṇa

dharmāyaitadadharmāya sadetanna sadityapi | vimuktaye tvidaṃ naitadvimuktiṃ saṃprayacchati ||9||

veda-mārgāpākaraṇam eva saptadhā darśayati sārdha-dvābhyāṃ—dharmāya iti

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

वेद-मार्गापाकरणम् एव सप्तधा दर्शयति सार्ध-द्वाभ्यां—धर्माय इति

Śrīdhara commentary.

Beginning with 'for dharma' and ending with 'in one way,' he gives seven examples of the Jaina doctrine of conditional predication (saptabhaṅgī): it may exist, not exist, both exist and not exist, be indescribable, exist and be indescribable, not exist and be indescribable, or both exist and not exist and be indescribable. This is the Ārhata/Jaina anekāntavāda.

VP 3.18.12

इत्य् अनेकान्तवादं च माया-मोहेन नैकधा।

Transliteration

By displaying anekāntavāda—whether the doctrine of causes whose fruits vary or the doctrine of difference and non-difference—he made them abandon their own dharma; this is the conclusion.

VP 3.18.15

Transliteration

pāṣaṇḍādayaḥ sarve vedaviruddhā eva yadāhuḥ— bhraṣṭaḥ svadharmāt pāṣaṇḍo vikarmastho niṣiddhakṛt | yasya dharmadhvajo nityaṃ sarādhvaja ivocchritaḥ | pracchannāni ca pāpāni vaiḍālaṃ nāma tadvratam | priyaṃ vakti puro'nyatra vipriyaṃ kurute bhṛśam | tyaktoparodhaceṣṭaśca śaṭho'yaṃ kathito budhaiḥ | sandehakṛdhetubhiśca satkarmasu sahetuka | arvāgdṛṣṭirnaikṛtikaḥ svārthasādhanatatparaḥ | śaṭho mithyāvinītaśca vakavṛttirudāhṛtaḥ | iti | vāṅmātreṇāpi nārcayet | taiḥ sambhāṣaṇamapi na kuryāt

Śrīdhara Sanskrit

पाषण्डादयः सर्वे वेदविरुद्धा एव यदाहुः— भ्रष्टः स्वधर्मात् पाषण्डो विकर्मस्थो निषिद्धकृत् । यस्य धर्मध्वजो नित्यं सराध्वज इवोच्छ्रितः । प्रच्छन्नानि च पापानि वैडालं नाम तद्व्रतम् । प्रियं वक्ति पुरोऽन्यत्र विप्रियं कुरुते भृशम् । त्यक्तोपरोधचेष्टश्च शठोऽयं कथितो बुधैः । सन्देहकृधेतुभिश्च सत्कर्मसु सहेतुक । अर्वाग्दृष्टिर्नैकृतिकः स्वार्थसाधनतत्परः । शठो मिथ्याविनीतश्च वकवृत्तिरुदाहृतः । इति । वाङ्मात्रेणापि नार्चयेत् । तैः सम्भाषणमपि न कुर्यात्

Śrīdhara commentary.

The various classes beginning with the pāṣaṇḍa are all opposed to the Veda. A pāṣaṇḍa is one who has fallen from his own dharma; a vikarmastha performs prohibited acts; a dharmadhvaja carries the signs of dharma without following it; a vaiḍāla-vrata person conceals sins while maintaining a hypocritical observance; a śaṭha speaks pleasantly to someone's face but acts harmfully elsewhere and is known for deceit; a haituka uses false reasons to cast doubt on good actions; a person with a low, crooked outlook is selfish and intent on his own ends; and one with the conduct of a heron displays false dignity. Even with words alone one should not honor such people or converse with them.
