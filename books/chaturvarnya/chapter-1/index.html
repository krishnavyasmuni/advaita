CV Birth-based varṇa

वर्णो द्विजादौ शुक्लादौ इति कोशोक्त्या वर्णशब्दो द्विजादिजातौ रूढः । जातिशब्दस्तु जन्ममूलक एव । यथा सिंहत्वादिजातयः जन्ममूलिका हि ।

By the lexicon statement, “varṇa [is used] for a twice-born person and the like, and for white and the like,” the word varṇa is established in the sense of a jāti beginning with the twice-born. The word jāti, however, is indeed rooted in birth. For example, jātis such as lionhood are indeed rooted in birth.

CV Birth from the Puruṣa

इति मन्त्रेण जन्मना वर्णव्यवस्था निगदव्याख्याता । तथाहि अस्य महाविराड्रूपस्य भगवतो ब्राह्मणः मुखमासीत् मुखाज्जात इत्यर्थः । अस्य बाहू राजन्यः बाहुभ्यां जातत्वात् कृतः । अस्य पुरुषस्य यौ ऊरू तद्रूपो वैश्य आसीत् । अस्य भगवतः पद्भ्यां शूद्रः अजायत ।

By this mantra, the arrangement of the varṇas by birth is explicitly explained. Thus: the Brāhmaṇa was the mouth of this Lord whose form is the Great Cosmic Puruṣa; the meaning is, [he was] born from the mouth. The Rājanya was His arms, made [so] because of being born from the arms. The two thighs of this Puruṣa were the form of the Vaiśya. From the feet of this Lord the Śūdra was born.

CV Objection and reply

नन्वत्र ब्राह्मणो मुखमिति सामानाधिकरण्यव्यपदेशेन ब्राह्मणोऽस्य मुखमिति काल्पनिकमेव ब्राह्मणस्य तन्मुखरूपत्वम् । एवमेव राजन्यादीनामपि सम्बन्धे प्रतिपत्तव्यमिति चेन्न, पद्भ्यां शूद्रोऽजायत इति वाक्यशेषेण शूद्रस्य पादजत्ववत् ब्राह्मणादीनामपि मुखजत्वमेव प्रतिपत्तव्यम् । न चैवं सामानाधिकरण्यमनुपपन्नमिति वाच्यम् । मृद्घटः सुवर्णकुण्डलमित्यादौ तज्जत्वेन सामानाधिकरण्यवत् तदुपपत्तेः । अत एव महर्षिणा शौनकेन ‘तदस्योत्पन्नत्वादिति’ वदता उत्पन्नत्वेनैव ब्राह्मणादीनां मुखादिरूपतोक्ता । ‘स मुखतस्त्रिवृतं निर्मिमीत’ इत्यादि तैत्तिरीयश्रुत्यापि निर्माणोक्त्या जन्मजत्वमेव ब्राह्मणत्वादीनामुक्तम् ।

Now, it may be objected here: because of the expression of co-reference in “the Brāhmaṇa [was] the mouth,” the Brāhmaṇa’s being the mouth of Him may be merely figurative. The same relation should likewise be understood for the Rājanya and the others. This is not so. From the concluding statement, “From His feet the Śūdra was born,” just as the Śūdra’s birth from the feet [is understood], the Brāhmaṇa and the others too must be understood as born from the mouth. Nor should it be said that co-reference is thereby inexplicable: it is established as in “clay-pot” and “gold-earring,” where there is co-reference by being produced from that. Therefore, the great sage Śaunaka, by saying “because it was produced from that,” stated that the Brāhmaṇas and the others take the form of the mouth and the rest precisely through being produced. The Taittirīya Śruti too, by its statement of creation, such as “He fashioned the Trivṛt from the mouth,” has stated the birth-origin of Brāhmaṇa-ness and the rest.

CV Manu

लोकानां तु विवृद्ध्यर्थं मुखबाहूरुपादतः ।
ब्राह्मणं क्षत्रियं वैश्यं शूद्रं च निरवर्तयत् ॥

अत्र कुल्लूकभट्टः — भूरादीनां बाहुल्यार्थं मुखबाहूरुपादेभ्यो ब्राह्मणक्षत्रियवैश्यशूद्रान् यथाक्रमं निर्मितवान् । मुखादिभ्यो ब्राह्मणादिनिर्माणम् । मुखादिभ्यो जायमानानामेव ब्राह्मणत्वादिजातित्वसिद्ध्या जन्मनैव जातिरिति लोकप्रसिद्धः सिद्धान्तोऽप्यत्र सङ्गच्छते । लोके सिंहादीनां जन्मनैव जातित्वस्य प्रसिद्धेः । जातित्वादेवं यथा सिंहादयो न गुणकर्मादिमूलका, एवं ब्राह्मणादयोऽपि ।

For the increase of the worlds, from the mouth, arms, thighs, and feet, he brought forth the Brāhmaṇa, Kṣatriya, Vaiśya, and Śūdra. Here Kullūka Bhaṭṭa [says]: “For the abundance of the worlds beginning with Bhū, he created the Brāhmaṇa, Kṣatriya, Vaiśya, and Śūdra from the mouth, arms, thighs, and feet, in that order. The Brāhmaṇa and the rest were created from the mouth and the rest.” Since the jāti status of Brāhmaṇa-ness and the rest is established for those alone who are born from the mouth and the rest, the well-known conclusion here too fits: jāti is by birth alone. For in the world it is well known that the jāti of lions and the rest is by birth alone. In that way, just as lions and the rest, by virtue of being jāti, are not rooted in qualities, actions, and the like, so too are the Brāhmaṇas and the rest.

CV The Gītā statement

ननु ‘चातुर्वर्ण्यं मया सृष्टं गुणकर्मविभागशः’ इति गीतासु गुणकर्मभिरेव चातुर्वर्ण्यस्य सृष्टिर्ज्ञायत इति चेन्न तात्पर्याज्ञानविजृम्भितत्वात् । तथाहि — घटोत्पत्तौ दण्डादीनामिव चातुर्वर्ण्यसृष्टौ गुणकर्मादीनां निमित्तत्वमुक्तम् । यथोत्पन्ने घटे न दण्डादिभिस्तदवस्थितिः किन्तु घटोत्पत्तेः प्रागेव विद्यमानानां दण्डादीनां घटहेतुत्वम्, तथैव ब्राह्मणादियोनिषु तत्तद्ब्राह्मणादिशरीरप्राप्तौ गुणकर्मादीनां हेतुत्वम् । तेन यैर्गुणैः कर्मभिश्चेश्वरेण ब्राह्मणादिदेहनिर्माणं कृतं तेषामेवात्र चातुर्वर्ण्यनिमित्तत्वमुक्तम् । तच्च सनातनधर्मानुयायिभिः सर्वत्रैव कार्यमात्रस्य गुणकर्ममूलकत्वादिष्यत एव । अत एवेश्वरेण मया वियत्सोमसूर्यादिवत् चातुर्वर्ण्यमित्युक्तम् । यथा वियदादयो न सर्वैर्निर्मातुं पार्यन्ते तथैव ब्राह्मणादयोऽपि । गुणकर्ममूलकचातुर्वर्ण्यव्यवस्थावादिभिस्तु तत्तच्छरीरोत्पत्त्यनन्तरं तत्तद्देहादिगतैर्गुणकर्मभिर्ब्राह्मण्यादिनिर्धारणमिष्यते, तच्च नानेन वाक्येन सिद्ध्यति ।

Now, [it may be objected]: “By the Gītā’s statement, ‘The fourfold varṇa was created by Me according to the division of guṇa and karma,’ it is understood that the fourfold varṇa was created by guṇas and karmas alone.” No, because this arises from ignorance of the intended meaning. Thus: just as sticks and the like are instrumental in the production of a pot, guṇas and karmas and the like are said to be instrumental in the creation of the fourfold varṇa. Just as, when a pot has arisen, its continued state is not by sticks and the like, but the sticks and the like that existed before the pot’s production are causes of the pot; in the same way, when bodies of Brāhmaṇas and the rest are attained by birth in the wombs of Brāhmaṇas and the rest, guṇas and karmas are causes [of that attainment]. Therefore, only those guṇas and karmas by which the Lord made the bodies of Brāhmaṇas and the rest are stated here to be instrumental causes of the fourfold varṇa. And this is indeed accepted by followers of Sanātana Dharma, since every effect everywhere has guṇa and karma as its root. Therefore the Lord said, “The fourfold varṇa [was created] by Me,” as [were] the sky, Moon, Sun, and the rest. Just as the sky and the rest cannot be created by all, so too the Brāhmaṇas and the rest. But proponents of a fourfold-varṇa arrangement rooted in guṇa and karma desire the determination of Brāhmaṇa-ness and the rest by the guṇas and karmas belonging to those respective bodies after the arising of those respective bodies; that is not established by this statement.

ननु (nanu) — now, [it may be objected]
चातुर्वर्ण्यं मया सृष्टं गुणकर्मविभागशः इति गीतासु (cāturvarṇyaṃ mayā sṛṣṭaṃ guṇakarmavibhāgaśa iti gītāsu) — by the Gītā’s statement, “The fourfold varṇa was created by Me according to the division of guṇa and karma”
गुणकर्मभिः एव (guṇakarmabhiḥ eva) — by guṇas and karmas alone
चातुर्वर्ण्यस्य सृष्टिः ज्ञायते (cāturvarṇyasya sṛṣṭir jñāyate) — the creation of the fourfold varṇa is understood
इति चेत् (iti cet) — if it be said
न (na) — no
तात्पर्याज्ञानविजृम्भितत्वात् (tātparyājñānavijṛmbhitatvāt) — because it arises from ignorance of the intended meaning
तथाहि (tathā hi) — thus indeed
घटोत्पत्तौ दण्डादीनाम् इव (ghaṭotpattau daṇḍādīnām iva) — just as sticks and the like [are instrumental] in the production of a pot
चातुर्वर्ण्यसृष्टौ गुणकर्मादीनाम् निमित्तत्वम् उक्तम् (cāturvarṇyasṛṣṭau guṇakarmādīnāṃ nimittatvam uktam) — in the creation of the fourfold varṇa, guṇas and karmas and the like are said to be instrumental causes
यथा उत्पन्ने घटे (yathā utpanne ghaṭe) — just as, when a pot has arisen
न दण्डादिभिः तत्-अवस्थितिः (na daṇḍādibhiḥ tadavasthitiḥ) — its continued state is not by sticks and the like
किन्तु घटोत्पत्तेः प्राक् एव विद्यमानानाम् (kintu ghaṭotpatter prāk eva vidyamānānām) — but of those existing even before the pot’s production
दण्डादीनां घटहेतुत्वम् (daṇḍādīnāṃ ghaṭahetutvam) — the sticks and the like are causes of the pot
तथैव (tathaiva) — in exactly the same way
ब्राह्मणादियोनिषु (brāhmaṇādiyoniṣu) — in the wombs of Brāhmaṇas and the rest
तत्तद्ब्राह्मणादिशरीरप्राप्तौ (tattadbrāhmaṇādiśarīraprāptau) — in the attainment of the respective bodies of Brāhmaṇas and the rest
गुणकर्मादीनां हेतुत्वम् (guṇakarmādīnāṃ hetutvam) — guṇas and karmas and the like are causes
तेन (tena) — therefore
यैः गुणैः कर्मभिः च ईश्वरेण (yaiḥ guṇaiḥ karmabhiś ca īśvareṇa) — by those qualities and actions, by the Lord
ब्राह्मणादिदेहनिर्माणं कृतम् (brāhmaṇādidehanirmāṇaṃ kṛtam) — the bodies of Brāhmaṇas and the rest were made
तेषाम् एव अत्र (teṣām eva atra) — those alone here
चातुर्वर्ण्यनिमित्तत्वम् उक्तम् (cāturvarṇyanimittatvam uktam) — are stated to be instrumental causes of the fourfold varṇa
तत् च सनातनधर्मानुयायिभिः (tac ca sanātanadharmānuyāyibhiḥ) — and that is accepted by followers of Sanātana Dharma
सर्वत्र एव कार्यमात्रस्य (sarvatraiva kāryamātrasya) — since every effect everywhere
गुणकर्ममूलकत्वात् इष्यते एव (guṇakarmamūlakatvād iṣyate eva) — is indeed accepted as rooted in qualities and actions
अत एव ईश्वरेण (ata eva īśvareṇa) — therefore by the Lord
मया (mayā) — by Me
वियत्सोमसूर्यादिवत् चातुर्वर्ण्यम् इति उक्तम् (viyatsomasūryādivat cāturvarṇyam ity uktam) — the fourfold varṇa [was created] as were the sky, Moon, Sun, and the rest, it is said
यथा वियत्-आदयः (yathā viyat-ādayaḥ) — just as the sky and the rest
न सर्वैः निर्मातुं पार्यन्ते (na sarvaiḥ nirmātuṃ pāryante) — cannot be made by all
तथैव ब्राह्मणादयः अपि (tathaiva brāhmaṇādayo api) — so too the Brāhmaṇas and the rest
गुणकर्ममूलकचातुर्वर्ण्यव्यवस्थावादिभिः तु (guṇakarmamūlakacāturvarṇyavyavasthāvādibhiḥ tu) — but by proponents of a fourfold-varṇa arrangement rooted in qualities and actions
तत्तच्छरीरोत्पत्त्यनन्तरम् (tattacchārīrotpattyanantaram) — after the arising of those respective bodies
तत्तद्देहादिगतैः गुणकर्मभिः (tattaddehādigataiḥ guṇakarmabhiḥ) — by the qualities and actions belonging to those respective bodies
ब्राह्मण्यादिनिर्धारणम् इष्यते (brāhmaṇyādinirdhāraṇam iṣyate) — determination of Brāhmaṇa-ness and the rest is desired
तत् च न अनेन वाक्येन सिद्ध्यति (tac ca nānena vākyena siddhyati) — and that is not established by this statement
