Birth-based varṇa

Examines the meanings of varṇa and jāti, birth-based accounts, distinctions among jāti groups, and arguments about birth and action. Read the opening translation.

Livelihood and varṇa

Considers livelihood-based accounts, the grounds and stability of varṇa arrangements, objections and replies, and a marriage question named in the Preface.

Questions about varṇa

Collects further arguments about varṇa and the responses to them.

Eligibility for Vedic study

Considers who may study and teach the Veda, women’s and Śūdras’ access to learning, interpretations of mantras, and arguments attributed to Dayānanda.
