Chāturvarṇya

Full title: Chāturvarṇya Saṃskṛti Vimarśa
Author: Śrī Karapātri Svāmī
Language: Sanskrit

A Sanskrit work on varṇa and related questions in śāstra. This page gives a short English guide to the topics named in the Preface and links to the scan.

Read the Sources

  • Open the Internet Archive scan
  • Open the Sanskrit transcription (work in progress; check against the scan)

Part One — Topics Listed in the Preface

  1. Birth and varṇa: the meanings of varṇa and jāti, arguments about birth, and traditional interpretations.
  2. The two-varṇa position and responses to it.
  3. The one-varṇa position and responses to it.
  4. Livelihood-based accounts of varṇa and related questions.
  5. Other arguments concerning varṇa.
  6. Eligibility for Vedic study, teaching, and the interpretation of Vedic passages.

Part Two — Further Topics Announced in the Preface

  1. Foreign writers’ views on distinctions among jāti groups.
  2. Jāti reform: its meaning and arguments.
  3. The authority of the Vedas.
  4. Gotra and pravara.
  5. The authority of the Purāṇas.

The Preface describes Part Two as forthcoming. This outline records the topics it names; it does not confirm that the linked scan contains that part.

English Translation

This page is an original contents guide, not a translation of the book. No chapter translation is published here yet. Any English rendering should be checked against the scan and should preserve the distinction between the Sanskrit terms varṇa and jāti rather than automatically rendering varṇa as “caste.”
