Advaita Texts

An expanding library of Advaita Vedānta scripture and treatises. Each complete reader presents the Sanskrit, transliteration, English translation, and word-for-word gloss in the same chapter-and-verse reading layout used for the Purāṇas.

Available now: Ātmabodha and the twelve existing Śaṅkara readers. New works are being prepared one text at a time; titles below are marked clearly while their full readers are in progress.
