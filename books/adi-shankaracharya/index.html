Advaita Texts

A growing library of Advaita Vedānta stotrams, short texts, and treatises. Complete readers include the Sanskrit, an IAST control, an English translation, and a word-by-word gloss.

Scope: This directory covers independent works and teaching texts. Bhāṣya commentaries are not included. Readers are completed and published one book at a time.
