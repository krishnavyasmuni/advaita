# Source texts and attribution

The Sanskrit text in these readers is based on Ambuda electronic editions, which mark the transcriptions as dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal. The listed print source for each is *The Works of Sri Sankaracharya*, published by Sri Vani Vilas Press (1910). Ambuda credits Arun Prasad for the machine-readable versions; that credit is retained here. The English translations and word-for-word glosses on this site are original.
